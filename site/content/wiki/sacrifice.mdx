A sacrifice is the only way to raise [[kalis-favour|Kali's favour]], and so to earn her gifts, the
[[kapala|Kapala]] among them. The body has to be one the game leaves lying in the level: most monsters
vanish when they die, so only a few kinds, and the Damsel, can be offered. The altar is a stone table in a
side room, which a level has at most one of ([[kalis-altar|Kali's Altar]]).

## How it works

### The rite

A monster's sacrifice is part of the code most monsters share (`oEnemy`'s step); the Damsel's is in her own
step. On each step, a body is checked in this order <Cite gml="objects/oEnemy/step.gml:130-166" />
<Cite gml="objects/oDamsel/step.gml:311-348" />:

1. **Stunned or dead.** A monster must be stunned or dead; a Damsel must have been thrown by the player, or
   be dead.
2. **Lying still.** Not in the player's hands, and not moving at all, across or down.
3. **On the altar.** The point just under the middle of its feet must be on either half of an altar.
4. **Long enough.** While all three hold, a count of <Stat id="sacrifice.wait" /> runs down, one a step. It
   starts again whenever the body is picked up or moves, so one picked up or knocked off has to lie there for
   the whole count again; one lying still off the altar keeps what's left of it
   <Cite gml="objects/oEnemy/create.gml:33" /> <Cite gml="objects/oEnemy/step.gml:166" />.
5. **The sacrifice.** Once the count is out, the body bursts into flame with a burst of blood and is gone, the
   screen shakes, and the message is "KALI ACCEPTS THE SACRIFICE!" ("YOUR SACRIFICE" for a Damsel). Its worth is
   added to the favour, and Kali's message on how she feels follows, with her gift if it's earned
   ([[kalis-favour#her-gifts]]) <Cite gml="scripts/scrGetFavorMsg.gml:24-111" />.

At a favour of <Stat id="kali.devour" bare /> or less the body is taken all the same, but the message
is "KALI DEVOURS THE SACRIFICE!" and nothing is added <Cite gml="objects/oEnemy/step.gml:144-147" />.

A monster's step only runs while it's near the view, within 20 pixels of its left and top edges and 4 of
its right and bottom, so a monster left on an altar the player has walked away from waits there until he
comes back <Cite gml="objects/oEnemy/step.gml:7-8" />.

### What can be sacrificed

Only a body the game leaves lying in the level counts. Most monsters, such as the [[snake|Snake]] or the
[[bat|Bat]], are removed as soon as their hit points are gone, and some are never stunned. Those that lie
stunned, and those that leave a dead body, are:

| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [[shopkeeper\|Shopkeeper]] | <Stat id="shopkeeper.favor" bare /> | 6 | <Cite gml="objects/oShopkeeper/create.gml:14" /> |
| [[damsel\|Damsel]] | <Stat id="damsel.favor" bare /> (thrown) | <Stat id="damsel.favor" bare /> | <Cite gml="objects/oDamsel/create.gml:18" /> |
| [[hawkman\|Hawkman]] | <Stat id="hawkman.favor" bare /> | 3 | <Cite gml="objects/oHawkman/create.gml:13" /> |
| [[yeti\|Yeti]] | <Stat id="yeti.favor" bare /> | 2 | <Cite gml="objects/oYeti/create.gml:12" /> |
| [[caveman\|Caveman]] | <Stat id="caveman.favor" bare /> | 1 | <Cite gml="objects/oCaveman/create.gml:13" /> |
| [[mantrap\|Mantrap]] | <Stat id="mantrap.favor" bare /> | 1 | <Cite gml="objects/oManTrap/create.gml:12" /> |
| [[vampire\|Vampire]] | <Stat id="vampire.favor" bare /> | 0.5 | <Cite gml="objects/oEnemy/create.gml:32" /> |

A dead monster is worth half a stunned one <Cite gml="objects/oEnemy/step.gml:150-157" />. A Damsel is worth
the same either way: her code has a branch that would make her worth half as much again, but it tests for
a state she's never in <Cite gml="objects/oDamsel/step.gml:331-338" />. The player's own body is never a
sacrifice, as nothing in his code looks for the altar.

### Getting a body there

A stunned monster can be picked up and carried, and set down or thrown onto the altar
([[throwing]]). A [[stomping|stomp]] stuns a Caveman, a Yeti, a Hawkman, a Vampire, an angry Shopkeeper,
and a Mantrap while it's eating or the player wears [[spike-shoes|Spike Shoes]]; a stunned body thrown fast
into any of them but the Shopkeeper stuns it too <Cite gml="objects/oEnemy/step.gml:170-199" />. A stunned monster wakes after a
while: a Yeti after 200 steps, counted down while it's held or on the ground, when it slips out of the
player's hands, so the altar has to be near <Cite quirk="1" row="The rare items' only sources" />. A Damsel is picked up and thrown as an item
is, and lies stunned where she lands.

## Examples

<Clip id="sacrifice/yeti" />

- **A Yeti.** In the Ice Caves, the player stomps a Yeti beside the altar, which stuns it, picks it up and
  drops it on the altar's left half. It comes to rest, lies there for <Stat id="sacrifice.wait" />, and burns:
  the favour goes from 8 to 12, and Kali "SEEMS HAPPY" <Cite clip="sacrifice/yeti" />.
- **A Damsel.** Earlier on the same level, the player carries a Damsel onto the altar and throws her down on
  it: worth 8, she brings the favour from 0 to 8, Kali's first gift <Cite clip="damsel/sacrifice" />.

## Quirks

- **The Kapala comes only from Kali**, so sacrifices are the only way to it, and the rare items' row of
  `docs/quirks.md` §1 spells out the cheapest routes: two Damsels on two levels' altars, or a Damsel and
  stunned monsters on one <Cite quirk="1" row="The rare items' only sources" /> <Cite replay="fidelity/replays/item-seed-11-kapala.txt" />.
