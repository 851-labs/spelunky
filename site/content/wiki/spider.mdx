[[level-generation|Level generation]] hangs Spiders under blocks in [[mines|the Mines]], where each waits for the player to walk
beneath it; elsewhere, Spiders come out of [[jar|Jars]]. Once it has dropped, a Spider hops after the
player in short, high jumps and bites him on contact. With <Stat id="spider.hp" />, it dies to any damage,
whether from the [[whip|Whip]], a [[stomping|stomp]] or a thrown [[rock|Rock]].

## Behaviour

<Clip id="spider/drop" />

A Spider starts out hanging under a block, still. It drops when the player is below it, within 8 pixels of
its middle across and within <Stat id="spider.drop-range" /> of it, or when the block it hangs from is gone
<Cite gml="objects/oSpiderHang/step.gml:17-22" />. The clip shows one dropping as the player walks beneath
it and leaping onto him.

Once down, it falls under gravity and moves only by hopping. It waits 5–20 steps (0.17–0.67 seconds), then
jumps up at 2–5 pixels per step and across at <Stat id="spider.jump-speed" />, toward the player
<Cite gml="objects/oSpider/step.gml:40-44" /> <Cite gml="objects/oSpider/alarm_0.gml:1-13" />. While the
player is within <Stat id="spider.jump-range" />, it jumps at him again each time it lands, with a 1 in 4
chance each time of stopping to wait again instead <Cite gml="objects/oSpider/step.gml:49-64" />. Farther
away, it makes a single hop toward him after each wait <Cite gml="objects/oSpider/step.gml:66-70" />, so it
closes in from any distance, only more slowly.

A Spider that falls into water drowns: it stops, sinks, and is removed 30 steps (1 second) later
<Cite gml="objects/oSpider/step.gml:85-94" /> <Cite gml="objects/oSpider/alarm_1.gml:1" />. It only acts
while it's on screen or just off it <Cite gml="objects/oSpider/step.gml:3-5" />
<Cite gml="objects/oSpiderHang/step.gml:1-3" />.

## Damage

When the player touches a Spider without stomping it, he loses <Stat id="spider.damage" />, is knocked away
from it, and blinks, invincible, for 30 steps (1 second)
<Cite gml="objects/oEnemy/collision_oCharacter.gml:14-25" />. This is the touch code most monsters share; the
Spider has no attack of its own, and the clip above shows a leap that lands on the player taking him from
four hearts to three.

<Cite gml="objects/oSpiderHang/step.gml:9-16" /> <Cite gml="objects/oSpider/step.gml:16-26" />:

A hanging Spider runs its own step event in place of the one most monsters share, so the hazards in that
event (Spear Traps, Spikes, Lava) only reach a Spider once it has dropped, when its step event runs the
shared one first <Cite gml="objects/oSpider/step.gml:1-2" />.

Spiders killed count toward the player's kills, and the screen between levels draws a Spider icon for each
<Cite gml="objects/oSpider/step.gml:19-24" /> <Cite gml="objects/oTransition/alarm_0.gml:137-141" />. One
that Spikes, Lava or a Spear Trap killed doesn't count <Cite gml="objects/oEnemy/step.gml:68" />
<Cite gml="objects/oEnemy/step.gml:101" /> <Cite gml="objects/oEnemy/step.gml:120" />, nor does one that
drowns, which is removed without dying.

## Locations

Level generation makes Spiders only in [[mines|the Mines]], hanging under blocks that have two blocks of
open space below them (with no water there and no monster right under them), outside the starting room and
shops and above the level's bottom 64 pixels. For each such block there's a 1 in 80 chance of a Spider once
the rolls before it have failed: a [[giant-spider|Giant Spider]], on a [[dark-level|dark level]] a lamp or a
[[scarab|Scarab]], and a 1 in 60 chance of a [[bat|Bat]] <Cite gml="scripts/scrEntityGen.gml:45-64" />.

Spiders also appear wherever these are:

- **[[jar|Jars]].** A broken Jar makes a Spider with a 1 in 6 chance once its rolls for gold and gems have
  all failed <Cite gml="objects/oJar/destroy.gml:17-22" /> <Cite gml="objects/oJar/collision_oWhip.gml:9-14" />.
- **[[olmec|Olmec]]'s yellow balls**, which turn into a Bat, Spider, [[snake|Snake]], [[frog|Frog]] or [[fire-frog|Fire Frog]]
  when they hit the ground <Cite gml="objects/oYellowBall/step.gml:4-19" />.
- **[[kalis-altar|Kali's altar]].** The first time the player destroys one, [[kali|Kali]]'s head above it releases six Spiders, and
  it does again on a later one if the level is dark and the [[ghost|Ghost]] is already out <Cite
  gml="objects/oSacAltarLeft/destroy.gml:11-26" /> <Cite gml="objects/oSacAltarLeft/destroy.gml:39-47" />
  <Cite gml="objects/oKaliHead/alarm_0.gml:1-8" />.
- **The [[stars-room|Stars Room]]**, a bonus room off the [[high-scores-room|high scores room]], where a Spider may drop in from the top every
  20 steps, more likely the more [[shopkeeper|Shopkeepers]] the player has killed there
  <Cite gml="objects/oStarsRoom/alarm_11.gml:1-7" /> <Cite gml="objects/oShopkeeper/step.gml:485" />.
- **Custom levels**, as the [[level-editor|level editor]]'s tile `s`, a hanging Spider
  <Cite gml="scripts/scrCreateTileObj.gml:135-138" />.

## Tips

- A hanging Spider only drops for a player below it and within 8 pixels of its middle across, so one can
  be passed on the ledge above it, or beside it, without it dropping.
- Out of range, a Spider still hops toward the player after every wait of 5–20 steps, so it keeps coming.
- Water kills a Spider that falls in, but the kill isn't counted.

## Trivia

- The Spider's code names a walking state, but nothing ever puts it in it: a Spider only ever waits, hops
  or drowns <Cite gml="objects/oSpider/create.gml:17-22" />.
- A commented-out block in its step event would have reversed it at walls
  <Cite gml="objects/oSpider/step.gml:74-79" />, and the Jungle's generation has a commented-out line
  that would have hung Spiders there too <Cite gml="scripts/scrEntityGen.gml:273" />.
