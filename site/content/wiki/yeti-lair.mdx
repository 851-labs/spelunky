A Yeti lair is built by a room script of its own, with floors and ceilings where the rest of
[[ice-caves|the Ice Caves]] are platforms in the open, and nearly all of its blocks are ice. [[level-generation|Level generation]] makes the [[yeti-king|Yeti King]] nowhere else: it
waits in a room of its own with four [[yeti|Yetis]]. There are more [[spikes|Spikes]] and thin
ice than in the Ice Caves' other levels, and fewer [[ufo|UFOs]], and the level starts with the message "IT
SMELLS LIKE WET FUR IN HERE!"

## Generation

An Ice Caves level after the [[moai|Moai]]'s that isn't the [[mothership]]'s is a Yeti lair one time in 6
<Cite gml="scripts/scrLevelGen.gml:234-237" /> <Cite gml="scripts/scrClearGlobals.gml:58" />. The lair's
roll is the last branch of the Moai's, which every Ice Caves level takes until the Moai is made, so a level
before the Moai's, or the Moai's own, is never a lair, and lairs only come on levels 10, 11 and 12
<Cite quirk="1" />. It starts with "IT SMELLS LIKE
WET FUR IN HERE!" <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/alarm_0.gml:40-45" />
<Cite gml="scripts/scrInitLevel.gml:206-220" />. Like the rest of the Ice Caves, it's never dark
<Cite gml="scripts/scrInitLevel.gml:169" />.

<Clip id="yeti-lair/arrival" />

In the clip, a lair loads with its message, and the player runs right from the entrance and drops into the
Yeti King's room, where he whips a Yeti and the Yeti King starts to yell <Cite clip="yeti-lair/arrival" />.

The level is laid out as [[mines#generation|the Mines']] are, four rooms by four on a path from the top row
to the bottom, but its rooms come from the lair's own script rather than the Ice Caves'
<Cite gml="scripts/scrInitLevel.gml:158-162" />. Most of its layouts have a floor of blocks along the
bottom, and some a ceiling, where the Ice Caves' other layouts are mostly platforms in open air
<Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGenYeti.gml:52-216" />. Five blocks in six are ice and one in six the area's dark
rock, where the Ice Caves' other levels have one block of ice in ten
<Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGenYeti.gml:301-310" /> <Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGen3.gml:295-304" />.

### The Yeti King's room

Until the Yeti King has been placed, each room of the second kind is its room with a chance that grows
with the row. Every one rolls 1 in 10; in the second row, a 1 in 3 roll follows when that fails, and in the
third a 1 in 2 roll and then the 1 in 3; in the bottom row it's certain
<Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGenYeti.gml:101-110" />. So a lair has one Yeti King at most. It has none if every
such room above the bottom row fails its rolls and every room of the bottom row is the exit's, the one the
path drops into, or a side room. Its room has a ceiling of ice, some of its blocks left out at random, over an open hall where the
Yeti King stands with two Yetis on each side of it <Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGenYeti.gml:127" />
<Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGenYeti.gml:443-450" />.

## Hazards

- **[[spikes|Spikes]].** The Ice Caves' other levels have Spikes only under the Golden Idol's ledge. The
  lair has rows of them in the bottom row's box side room and under some layouts' thin ice, a floor of them in one of
  the rooms the path drops into, and some on the ground of the outcrops that other layouts add, each such
  tile one time in 3 <Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGenYeti.gml:96" /> <Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGenYeti.gml:125" /> <Cite
  gml="scripts/scrRoomGenYeti.gml:145" /> <Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGenYeti.gml:241-262" /> <Cite
  gml="scripts/scrRoomGenYeti.gml:317" /> <Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGenYeti.gml:423-426" />.
- **Thin ice**, which melts under the player in 30 steps (1 second), as in the rest of the Ice Caves. One of
  the layouts of the path's rooms has a floor of nothing else <Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGenYeti.gml:124" />
  <Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGenYeti.gml:311-314" /> <Cite gml="objects/oThinIce/step.gml:1-13" />.
- **[[spring-trap|Spring Traps]]**, fewer than elsewhere: generation puts them only on dark rock without
  a vein of gold, and dark rock is one block in six here <Cite gml="scripts/scrEntityGen.gml:369" />
  <Cite gml="objects/oDark/create.gml:3-8" />.
- **The Yeti King**, whose touch takes 2 hearts and whose yell shakes blocks of ice loose from the floor
  ([[yeti-king|its page]]). In the clip below, it yells at the crouching player, then his walk into it costs
  him 2 hearts <Cite clip="yeti-king/swat" />.

## Items and shops

Shops come as on [[ice-caves#items-and-shops|any Ice Caves level]], in a side room beside the path, on a
floor of smooth dark blocks <Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGenYeti.gml:149-181" /> <Cite
gml="scripts/scrRoomGenYeti.gml:382-387" />. Treasure is scattered over the blocks as in [[mines#treasure|the Mines]] <Cite gml="scripts/scrEntityGen.gml:373-376" />. The dark rock
hides gems, [[big-sapphire|Big Sapphires]] most often, and items as the Ice Caves' does, but the ice hides
none: its roll for an item is commented
out, and the frozen Caveman is all it can hold <Cite gml="objects/oDark/create.gml:3-15" />
<Cite gml="objects/oIce/create.gml:3-4" />. A lair has no Golden Idol and no Kali's altar (above).

## Trivia

- The lair's room script is a copy of the Mines', and keeps the Mines' code for things no lair has: the
  [[snake-pit|snake pit]]'s rooms, with Snakes and [[big-ruby|Big Rubies]], the idol room's altar, Golden Idol and giant tiki head,
  chests, ladders and push blocks <Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGenYeti.gml:182-195" /> <Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGenYeti.gml:315-348" />
  <Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGenYeti.gml:427-434" /> <Cite quirk="1" />. Its idol room's layout is commented out,
  and the line that marks the idol as made follows a roll that can only give 1, which is why the box side room
  only comes in the bottom row <Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGenYeti.gml:82-98" /> <Cite quirk="1" />.
- The Ice Caves' own room script has a tile for a Yeti that none of its layouts uses; the lair's Yetis and
  the Ice Caves' come from the lair's script and level generation <Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGen3.gml:404-407" />
  <Cite quirk="1" />.
