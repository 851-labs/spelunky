Hawkmen live in [[temple|the Temple]]. A Hawkman behaves much like a [[caveman|Caveman]], wandering until it
sees the player and then charging, but it leaps as it runs, and its touch is a throw: it grabs the player
and flings him away, and he's hurt when he lands. Like a Caveman, it survives most single hits, which stun
it instead.

## Behaviour

A Hawkman stands still for a while, then sets off walking in a random direction, turning around at walls
<Cite gml="objects/oHawkman/step.gml:46-67" /> <Cite gml="objects/oHawkman/step.gml:79-85" />. Its code sets
its speed to <Stat id="hawkman.walk-speed" />, but, like a Caveman, it covers 4 pixels every 3 steps (40 per
second): friction takes 0.1 off before it moves, and the game moves it in whole pixels
<Cite gml="objects/oHawkman/step.gml:87-106" /> <Cite gml="objects/oHawkman/step.gml:204-206" />. It stops
dead at the edge of a ledge, and with a 1 in 100 chance each step, to stand for 20–50 steps (0.67–1.67
seconds) <Cite gml="objects/oHawkman/step.gml:87-113" />. Standing beside a wall, it hops
<Cite gml="objects/oHawkman/step.gml:48-55" />.

Every 6 steps while it stands or walks, a Hawkman sends an invisible sight check flying ahead of it, as a
Caveman does <Cite gml="objects/oHawkman/step.gml:68-77" /> <Cite gml="objects/oHawkman/step.gml:115-124" />.
When one reaches the player, every Caveman and Hawkman within 100 pixels of him that isn't stunned starts
charging, whichever sent it and whichever way it faces <Cite gml="objects/oEnemySight/collision_oCharacter.gml:1-17" />.

<Clip id="hawkman/throw" />

A charging Hawkman runs at <Stat id="hawkman.charge-speed" /> (<Stat id="hawkman.charge-speed"
as="per-second" />) the way it faces, turning only at walls, and leaps every time it lands, unless there's
a block right above it <Cite gml="objects/oHawkman/step.gml:126-141" />. It keeps charging until something
stuns it. The leaps often carry it clean over the player, as in the last clip on this page.

**The throw.** When the player touches a Hawkman that isn't stunned, unless he's invincible from a recent
hit, and there's no block right above it, it grabs him and flings him back the way he came, at
<Stat id="hawkman.throw-speed" /> sideways and 6 upward. He's stunned in the air and drops whatever he's
holding <Cite gml="objects/oHawkman/collision_oCharacter.gml:42-76" />. The first wall or floor he hits costs
him <Stat id="hawkman.throw-damage" />, unless the fall is long enough to hurt him by itself
<Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:1815-1842" />. The clip shows a Hawkman spotting the player, charging,
and throwing him across the room, where he loses a heart as he lands; then it charges again
<Cite clip="hawkman/throw" />.

A Hawkman only acts while it's on screen or just off it <Cite gml="objects/oHawkman/step.gml:3-5" />.

When its hit points run out, a Hawkman dies and leaves its body, which, like a stunned Hawkman, can be picked
up and thrown <Cite gml="objects/oHawkman/step.gml:39-42" /> <Cite gml="objects/oHawkman/step.gml:176-193" />.
Hawkmen killed count toward the player's kills, and the screen between levels draws a Hawkman for each
<Cite gml="objects/oHawkman/step.gml:178-185" /> <Cite gml="objects/oTransition/alarm_0.gml:227-232" />. One
that Spikes, Lava or a Spear Trap killed doesn't count <Cite gml="objects/oEnemy/step.gml:68" />
<Cite gml="objects/oEnemy/step.gml:87" /> <Cite gml="objects/oEnemy/step.gml:120" />.

A stunned or dead Hawkman left lying still on Kali's altar for 20 steps is sacrificed to her, adding
<Stat id="hawkman.favor" /> to her favour if it's stunned and half that if it's dead, unless her favour is
already −8 or less <Cite gml="objects/oEnemy/step.gml:130-167" />.

## Locations

Level generation makes Hawkmen only in [[temple|the Temple]], on top of blocks with open space above them,
outside the starting room: a 1 in 40 chance for each such block, once the rolls for a
[[tomb-lord|Tomb Lord]] and a [[caveman|Caveman]] have failed <Cite gml="scripts/scrEntityGen.gml:452-466" />.

In [[olmecs-lair|Olmec's Lair]], a Hawkman bows before [[olmec|Olmec]]. When Olmec cracks open, it's flung
away, stunned for 300 steps, with 1 hit point <Cite gml="objects/oOlmec/alarm_1.gml:9-21" />.

Custom levels can have them, as the [[level-editor|level editor]]'s tile `H`
<Cite gml="scripts/scrCreateTileObj.gml:217-220" />.

## Tips

- In the open, a Hawkman's touch throws the player rather than hurting him, but he drops what he's
  holding: an item can end up out of reach.
- A charging Hawkman leaps as it runs, so it can be ducked under rather than outrun: it often clears the
  player.
- One bullet kills a Hawkman. The Whip can't hurt a stunned one, so with the Whip alone it takes four hits,
  each after it gets back up.
- A stunned Hawkman is worth <Stat id="hawkman.favor" /> to [[kali|Kali]], more than a stunned Caveman.
