Every shop but the Black Market's Ankh shop has a sign over its entrance, a board painted with what the shop
sells, so the player can tell a [[general-store|General Store]] from a [[weapon-shop|Weapon Shop]] before he walks
in. It's solid, and what breaks blocks breaks it.

## How it works

<Clip id="shop-sign/bomb-shop" />

As a shop's room is laid out, its sign tile makes the sign, and the shop's kind picks its picture
<Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGen.gml:437-447" />:

| Shop | The sign shows |
| --- | --- |
| [[general-store\|General Store]] | A sack |
| [[bomb-shop\|Bomb Shop]] | A bomb |
| [[weapon-shop\|Weapon Shop]] | A pistol |
| [[clothing-shop\|Clothing Shop]] | A shirt |
| [[rare-shop\|Rare Shop]] | A magnifying glass |
| [[dice-house\|Dice House]] | A die |
| [[kissing-parlor\|Kissing Parlor]] | A pair of lips |

The clip shows a Bomb Shop's: the player drops down beside its sign, a bomb on a board over the entrance, and
walks in, and the shopkeeper welcomes him <Cite clip="shop-sign/bomb-shop" />.

A [[bomb|Bomb]], a [[mattock|Mattock]] or a rolling [[boulder|Boulder]] breaks a sign, and its destroy event, its
own, makes rubble and nothing else <Cite gml="objects/oSign/destroy.gml:1-9" />. Breaking it doesn't by itself anger
the [[shopkeeper|Shopkeeper]]: unlike the shop's walls and floor, the sign isn't marked as part of the shop, and
its destroy event doesn't run the check the walls' does <Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGen.gml:413-426" />
<Cite gml="objects/oSolid/destroy.gml:1-7" />. A lit bomb in the shop within 96 pixels of him angers him anyway,
before it goes off <Cite gml="objects/oBomb/step.gml:6-17" /> ([[bomb-angers-every-shopkeeper]]).

## Locations

Every area's room script makes shop signs the same way: [[mines|the Mines]]'
<Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGen.gml:437-447" />, [[jungle|the Jungle]]'s <Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGen2.gml:452-462" />,
[[ice-caves|the Ice Caves]]' <Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGen3.gml:379-389" />, [[temple|the Temple]]'s
<Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGen4.gml:481-491" />, [[black-market|the Black Market]]'s, which has no Kissing Parlor
<Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGenMarket.gml:347-356" />, and a [[yeti-lair|Yeti lair]]'s
<Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGenYeti.gml:398-408" />. The [[level-editor|level editor]] has no tile for it. The Black Market's
[[ankh-shop|Ankh shop]], a room open only from above, has no sign <Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGenMarket.gml:112-116" />.
