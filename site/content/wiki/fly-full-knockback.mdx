A [[tomb-lord|Tomb Lord]]'s flies hurt monsters as well as the player, and knock them back. The code means a heavy
monster to be knocked back half as far, but every monster is heavy, and the lines after the halving set the full
knockback again, so every monster the flies hit, a [[caveman|Caveman]] or a [[hawkman|Hawkman]], is sent flying.

## What happens

A Tomb Lord spits its flies at the player, and a caveman stands between them. The first fly to hit it knocks it
back at the fly's 4 pixels a step and 4 up, and the next at 5 <Cite clip="quirks/fly-full-knockback" />.

<Clip id="quirks/fly-full-knockback" />

## Why

When a fly touches a monster other than a Tomb Lord, it picks the knockback by whether the monster is heavy, then
sets the light one again for both:

```gml objects/oFly/collision_oEnemy.gml:1-14
if (other.type != "Tomb Lord")
{
    if (other.heavy)
    {
        other.xVel = xVel*0.5;
        other.yVel = -2;
    }
    else
    {
        other.xVel = xVel;
        other.yVel = -4;
    }
    other.xVel = xVel;
    other.yVel = -4;
```

Every monster is heavy: the monsters' shared create event sets `heavy`, and none of them clears it
<Cite gml="objects/oEnemy/create.gml:15" />. So the heavy branch is the one that runs, and the two lines after it
undo it: every monster a fly hits gets the fly's own speed, 3 to 5 pixels a step, and 4 pixels a step up
<Cite quirk="1" />. The fly then takes 2 of its hit points and stuns it
<Cite gml="objects/oFly/collision_oEnemy.gml:16-26" />.

## Reproducing it

Stand in front of a Tomb Lord with a Caveman or a Hawkman between you, so its flies hit the monster first. It
happens in the Temple wherever the three meet. The replay of the clip stages it: the player stands on a pillar in
the Tomb Lord's line, and a caveman stands below, between them. The first fly to hit the caveman (frame 272)
knocks it back at 4 pixels a step and 4 up, not 2 and 2, and takes 2 of its 3 hit points; the next (279) at 5 and
4 up <Cite replay="fidelity/replays/enemy-tomb-lord-fly-knockback.txt" />.

## In the port

The spelunky.ai port reproduces it exactly: it's in docs/quirks.md's list of the 1.1 source's bugs
<Cite quirk="1" />, and the oracle's fingerprint of the replay pins both knockbacks. No option turns it off.
