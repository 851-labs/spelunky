The [[crystal-skull|Crystal Skull]] is a kind of [[golden-idol|Golden Idol]] in the code: a child of the idol's
object, with its own sprite and value <Cite gml="objects/oCrystalSkull/create.gml:3-4" />. Two places count an idol for the loot shown between levels, the level's exit and a
sale to a [[shopkeeper|Shopkeeper]], and only the exit tells the Skull apart.

## What happens

On a cemetery level, the player sets the Crystal Skull down in Lazlo's
[[kissing-parlor|Kissing Parlor]], and the Shopkeeper buys it. Between levels, the loot shows it as a golden
idol <Cite clip="quirks/crystal-skull-sold-as-idol" />.

<Clip id="quirks/crystal-skull-sold-as-idol" />

## Why

An idol set down on a shop's floor near a Shopkeeper who's standing calmly is sold: it pays its value and
counts one more idol, whatever its sprite <Cite gml="objects/oGoldIdol/step.gml:9-27" />:

```gml objects/oGoldIdol/step.gml:16-19
        global.collect += value*(global.levelType+1);
        global.collectCounter += 20;
        if (global.collectCounter > 100) global.collectCounter = 100;
        global.idols += 1;
```

Carried out of the level instead, it's counted by the player's step, which tells the two apart by the sprite
<Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:2079-2080" />:

```gml objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:2079-2080
            if (holdItem.sprite_index == sCrystalSkull) global.skulls += 1;
            else global.idols += 1;
```

Between levels, the transition room draws a loot icon for each count, a golden idol for each of `idols` and
a Crystal Skull for each of `skulls`, so the sold Skull is drawn as an idol
<Cite gml="objects/oTransition/alarm_0.gml:102-113" /> <Cite quirk="1" />. The money is the Skull's own.

## Reproducing it

Carry the Crystal Skull from the Jungle's [[restless-dead|graveyard level]] to a shop on the same level, and set it down on the
shop's floor near the Shopkeeper while he's calm. The replay pins it: seed 213's Skull sold in Lazlo's
Kissing Parlor counts as an idol, the transition room draws the golden idol's icon for it, and the count of
Skulls stays 0 <Cite replay="fidelity/replays/item-seed-213-crystal-skull-sold.txt" />.

## In the port

The spelunky.ai port reproduces it exactly: it's in docs/quirks.md's list of the 1.1 source's bugs
<Cite quirk="1" />, and the oracle's fingerprint of the replay pins the counts. No option turns it off.
