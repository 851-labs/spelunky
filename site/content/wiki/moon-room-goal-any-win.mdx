The [[high-scores-room|high scores room]] has a column of trophies, and climbing up beside them shows
a panel for each secret room: its goal if it's unlocked, LOCKED. if not. The [[moon-room|Moon Room]]'s panel tests the best time
against 600,000, a number of milliseconds, but the time is kept in seconds. So after any win the panel shows the
Moon Room's goal, while its door needs a win in 10:00 or less.

## What happens

With one win timed at 11:40, the high scores room has the time's silver trophy but no Moon Room door. The player
climbs the ledges beside the trophies: the Changing Room and the Stars Room read LOCKED., but beside the trophy the
Moon Room's goal shows as if it were unlocked <Cite clip="quirks/moon-room-goal-any-win" />.

<Clip id="quirks/moon-room-goal-any-win" />

## Why

When a game ends, `scrUpdateHighscores` turns the clock's milliseconds into seconds before it keeps the best time:

```gml scripts/scrUpdateHighscores.gml:54-55
// convert time from 1000/sec to sec to prevent overflow
global.time = floor(global.time / 1000);
```

The high scores room reads it back as `tTime`, in seconds, and makes the Moon Room's door only for 600 or less
<Cite gml="objects/oHighscores/create.gml:20" /> <Cite gml="objects/oHighscores/create.gml:61-65" />. But the
panel's limit is kept in milliseconds, `moonGold = 600000` <Cite gml="objects/oHighscores/create.gml:16" />, and
it's what the draw event tests while the player is at the Moon Room's height:

```gml objects/oHighscores/draw.gml:41-47
else if (oPlayer1.y < 80+16)
{
    draw_set_color(c_yellow);
    draw_text(168, 96, "MOON ROOM");
    draw_set_color(c_white);
    if (tTime > 0 and tTime <= moonGold)
    {
```

Any win under 600,000 seconds, nearly a week on the clock, passes, and the panel shows SHOOT THE MOVING TARGETS
WITH YOUR BOW AND ARROWS! rather than LOCKED. The Sun Room's limit, `sunGold`, is in dollars and the Stars Room's,
`starsGold`, in kills, and both match their doors <Cite gml="objects/oHighscores/create.gml:15-17" />
<Cite quirk="1" />.

## Reproducing it

Win a game slower than 10:00, without a shortcut, so that it's the best time, then go into the high scores room
and climb the ledges beside the trophies to the time's trophy. Most players' first wins are slower than that. The
replay starts with one win at 11:40: the room has the silver trophy and no Moon Room door. The player climbs the
trophy column ledge by ledge, past the Changing Room's and the Stars Room's LOCKED. panels, and beside the trophy
the Moon Room's goal shows <Cite replay="fidelity/replays/flow-highscores-moon-room-slow-win.txt" />.

## In the port

The spelunky.ai port reproduces it exactly: it's in docs/quirks.md's list of the 1.1 source's bugs
<Cite quirk="1" />, and the oracle's fingerprint of the replay pins the panel's text. No option turns it off.
