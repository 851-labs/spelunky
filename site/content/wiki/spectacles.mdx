Wearing them, the player sees through [[block|blocks]] to what's buried in them: the gems and items that level
generation hides inside some blocks show in front of the blocks instead of behind them. [[clothing-shop|Clothing
Shops]] and [[rare-shop|Rare Shops]] sell them, and [[crate|Crates]], buried blocks and [[kali|Kali]] give them away.
They're worn from the moment they're picked up or paid for until the game ends.

## Effect

<Clip id="spectacles/reveal" />

Picking up a free pair, paying for one or carrying one out of a shop puts it on: the message "YOU GOT
SPECTACLES! YOUR EYESIGHT SEEMS IMPROVED..." shows, the pair disappears, and an icon of it joins the row under
the hearts <Cite gml="scripts/scrStealItem.gml:126-136" /> <Cite gml="scripts/scrDrawHUD.gml:125-129" />.
They stay on until a new game starts, which clears them <Cite gml="scripts/scrClearGlobals.gml:89" />
<Cite gml="objects/oTransition/create.gml:20-24" />.

What they change is the order things are drawn in. Every item is drawn at depth 51 rather than 101, set
when it's made and again on each step it's on screen, and every treasure on screen at 0 rather than 101,
which puts both in front of the blocks <Cite gml="objects/oItem/create.gml:39-40" />
<Cite gml="objects/oItem/step.gml:1-6" /> <Cite gml="objects/oTreasure/step.gml:63-67" />. The
[[dice-house|Dice House]]'s dice are drawn in front too <Cite gml="objects/oDice/step.gml:93-94" />. So
treasure and items buried inside blocks, which are otherwise hidden behind them, show through. The clip
shows a pair picked up in a cave of bricks: on that step, a pair of [[climbing-gloves|Climbing Gloves]] and
four big gems in the bricks around the player appear <Cite clip="spectacles/reveal" />. A thing off the
screen changes when it comes into view, as items and treasure only act on screen
<Cite gml="objects/oTreasure/step.gml:1-5" />.

Every block of rock away from the level's edges that isn't gold and holds no gem has a 1 in 1200 chance of
hiding an item, one of nineteen at random <Cite gml="objects/oBrick/create.gml:7-16" />
<Cite gml="scripts/scrGenerateItem.gml:63-89" />; ice hides none, its roll being commented out
<Cite gml="objects/oIce/create.gml:4" />. Kali's
first gift, when the player's favour with her reaches 8 at one of her [[kalis-altar|altars]], is one of eight pieces of
equipment the player hasn't got, the Spectacles among them <Cite gml="scripts/scrGetFavorMsg.gml:67-108" />.
In custom levels, they're the [[level-editor|level editor]]'s tile `%`
<Cite gml="scripts/scrCreateTileObj.gml:375-380" />.

Carrying a pair out of a shop without paying puts it on and angers the [[shopkeeper|Shopkeeper]], as with
any ware; whether it goes on at once depends on whether the player or the pair leaves the shop first
([[stolen-ware-unequipped]]) <Cite quirk="1" row="Whether stealing a ware equips it depends on which leaves the shop's room first." /> <Cite replay="fidelity/replays/item-seed-77-spectacles.txt" />.

## Interactions

- Seeing a buried item doesn't free it: the player can't pick up an item whose position is inside a block
  <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:1206-1209" />. A [[bomb|Bomb]] or a [[mattock|Mattock]] digs it out.
- The [[udjat-eye|Udjat Eye]] puts treasure in front of the blocks too, but not items
  <Cite gml="objects/oTreasure/step.gml:63" />.
- A shop can stock a pair the player is already wearing, as it only checks the level for one; buying it
  again changes nothing ([[owned-equipment-for-sale]]) <Cite gml="scripts/scrShopItemsGen.gml:79-82" />
  <Cite gml="scripts/scrStealItem.gml:126-128" /> <Cite quirk="1" row="Shops stock equipment the player already has." />.

## Tips

- Gems and items buried in a block show only while the Spectacles are on, so they show where a Bomb is worth
  using.
- Kali never gives a second pair: her gift skips the equipment the player already has.
