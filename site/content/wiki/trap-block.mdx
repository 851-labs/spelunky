A trap block is a tan stone block carved with a skull, a solid block like any other until a
[[golden-idol|golden idol]] standing on it is lifted. Level generation builds them in two places: the
floor of the [[jungle|Jungle]]'s idol room, over a pool of water, and the top of the
[[sacrifice-pit|Sacrifice Pit]], a [[temple|Temple]] level, over a drop into lava. Lifting the idol takes
them away, and the player falls with it.

## How it works

### The Jungle's idol room

<Clip id="trap-block/crumble" />

The Jungle's idol room has the idol on a floor of six trap blocks across the middle of the room, an open
row below them and a pool of water below that <Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGen2.gml:109-114" />. Each block
is given a delay when it's made: 40 steps less its distance in pixels from the idol, but not below 0,
which comes to 0 for the outer two, 16 for the next two and 32 for the two under the idol
<Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGen2.gml:477-482" /> <Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGen2.gml:395-398" />.

When the player lifts the idol, every trap block within 90 pixels of him starts its delay
<Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:1240-1248" />. Each counts its delay down a step at a time, then
crumbles into rubble with a thump that shakes the screen <Cite gml="objects/oTrapBlock/step.gml:3-7" />
<Cite gml="objects/oTrapBlock/destroy.gml:1-14" />. So the floor goes from the outside in: the outer two on
the step he lifts it, the next two 16 steps later, and the two under him 32 steps later, and he drops into
the water with the idol. The clip shows the player dropping into a Jungle idol room, lifting the idol, and
falling into the pool unhurt as the floor crumbles away <Cite clip="trap-block/crumble" />.

On a [[restless-dead|graveyard level]], the room holds the [[crystal-skull|Crystal Skull]] instead, with no
trap blocks: lifting it brings a ghost <Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGen2.gml:111" />
<Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:1234-1239" />.

### The Sacrifice Pit

<Clip id="trap-block/pit" />

One Temple level in 8 is a Sacrifice Pit: a column of four rooms, other than the start's, is a pit from the
top of the level to the bottom <Cite gml="scripts/scrLevelGen.gml:71-86" />
<Cite gml="scripts/scrClearGlobals.gml:60" />. At its top, a [[damsel|Damsel]] and a golden idol stand on four trap
blocks; below them, the pit drops through the two rooms beneath into a pool of [[lava]] in the bottom room,
with big rubies in the blocks under it <Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGen4.gml:193-204" /> <Cite
gml="scripts/scrRoomGen4.gml:399-409" /> <Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGen4.gml:328-337" /> <Cite
gml="scripts/scrRoomGen4.gml:497-500" />.

In the Temple, lifting an idol brings down the [[ceiling-trap|Ceiling Trap]] if the level has one. A
Sacrifice Pit level has no idol room, so it has none, and the player's code takes its other branch: every
trap block within 90 pixels of him is destroyed at once
<Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:1250-1279" /> <Cite gml="scripts/scrLevelGen.gml:85" />. The
blocks crumble into rubble on the step he lifts it, and he, the idol and the Damsel fall down the pit. The
clip shows the player lifting the idol beside the Damsel and falling the length of the pit into the lava,
where he dies <Cite clip="trap-block/pit" /> <Cite replay="fidelity/replays/trap-sacrifice-pit.txt" />.

## Damage

The blocks hurt nothing; what's below them does.

| Where | The fall | |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The Jungle's idol room | Into water, unhurt | <Cite clip="trap-block/crumble" /> |
| The Sacrifice Pit | Into lava, which kills the player and the Damsel | <Cite clip="trap-block/pit" /> |

off <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:1222" />.

## Avoiding

- **Knock the idol off first.** The trap is set off by lifting an idol that hasn't left the ground; an idol
  knocked off its perch, by a [[bomb|Bomb]]'s blast, say, turns its trigger off, and lifting it then sets
  nothing off <Cite gml="objects/oGoldIdol/step.gml:29-33" />.
- **Destroy them.** A trap block is a block, and a Bomb destroys it like one, with rubble but no thump
  <Cite gml="objects/oExplosion/collision_oSolid.gml:1-15" /> <Cite gml="objects/oTrapBlock/destroy.gml:1-14" />.
- **In the Jungle**, the fall is harmless: it ends in water.

## Interactions

- **The [[ceiling-trap|Ceiling Trap]]** takes the trap blocks' place in the Temple: with one on the level,
  lifting an idol brings it down and leaves the trap blocks alone
  <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:1252-1266" />.
- **The idol's trigger** stays on after a lift in the Jungle, so lifting the idol again before it has left
  the ground runs the trap blocks' code again <Cite quirk="1" />.

## Trivia

- The [[black-market|Black Market]]'s room code can make a trap block, from a tile none of its rooms uses
  <Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGenMarket.gml:369-372" /> <Cite quirk="1" />.
- The Temple's tile for them rolls 1 in 1, a roll that always passes but still draws a random number, four
  on every Sacrifice Pit level <Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGen4.gml:406-409" /> <Cite quirk="1" />.
- The Jungle's branch still holds the Temple's way of taking the blocks, commented out: they were once
  destroyed at once there too <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:1243-1247" />.
- In the Temple's branch, the thump and the screen's shake are inside the loop over the trap blocks, but
  outside the test for their distance, so they play once for every trap block on the level, near or not
  <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:1269-1278" />. Lifting the Sacrifice Pit's idol plays them four times
  on one step, which sounds and looks like once: each play restarts the same thump, and the shake only goes
  up to the same 10 <Cite quirk="1" /> <Cite replay="fidelity/replays/trap-sacrifice-pit.txt" />.
