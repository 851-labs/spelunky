The [[pitchers-mitt|Pitcher's Mitt]] throws harder and lighter: 6 pixels a step faster, with a sixth of the
usual gravity. Most items forget the lighter gravity a step later. A [[jar|Jar]], a [[skull|Skull]] or a die
never does, and floats for the rest of its life. An [[arrow|arrow]]'s own light gravity goes the same way as
most items' Mitt gravity.

## What happens

Wearing a Pitcher's Mitt, the player throws a [[rock|Rock]] straight ahead: it drops to the floor within a few
steps and stops short. He throws a Jar the same way: it keeps the Mitt's light gravity and flies flat across
the room <Cite clip="quirks/mitt-gravity-one-step" />.

<Clip id="quirks/mitt-gravity-one-step" />

## Why

A throw from the hands with the Mitt, not crouching, speeds the item up and sets its gravity, `myGrav`, to 0.1:

```gml scripts/scrUseItem.gml:667-676
            if (global.hasMitt and not scrPlayerIsDucking())
            {
                if (holdItem.xVel < 0) holdItem.xVel -= 6;
                else holdItem.xVel += 6;
            
                if (not kUp and not kDown) holdItem.yVel = -0.4;
                else if (kDown) holdItem.yVel = 6;
                
                holdItem.myGrav = 0.1;
            }
```

But the step most items run (`oItem`'s) sets `myGrav` back to 0.6 on every step the item isn't in lava, after
that step's gravity is added:

```gml objects/oItem/step.gml:160-170
        if (collision_rectangle(x-3, y-3, x+3, y+3, oLava, 0, 0))
        {
            myGrav = 0;
            xVel = 0;
            yVel = 0;
            y += 0.05;
        }
        else
        {
            myGrav = 0.6;
        }
```

So a Rock falls at 0.1 for one step and at 0.6 from the next. A Jar, a Skull and a die run steps of their own
instead, which set `myGrav` only in lava, and they keep the 0.1 for good <Cite
gml="objects/oJar/step.gml:27-30" /> <Cite gml="objects/oJar/step.gml:81-86" /> <Cite
gml="objects/oSkull/step.gml:79-84" /> <Cite gml="objects/oDice/step.gml:94-99" />. Monsters and the
Damsel set their gravity on every step too, and lose it within a step <Cite gml="objects/oEnemy/step.gml:42" />
<Cite gml="objects/oEnemy/step.gml:54" /> <Cite gml="objects/oDamsel/step.gml:42" /> <Cite
gml="objects/oDamsel/step.gml:47" />. An arrow is made with a gravity of 0.2
<Cite gml="objects/oArrow/create.gml:6" />, but its step runs `oItem`'s first
<Cite gml="objects/oArrow/step.gml:1" />, so every arrow, a Bow's or an [[arrow-trap|Arrow Trap]]'s, falls at
0.2 for one step and at 0.6 after <Cite quirk="1" />.

## Reproducing it

Wear the Mitt and throw anything level from the ground: a Rock lands sooner than it would without it. Throw a
Jar or a Skull the same way to see it float, or a die in the [[dice-house|Dice House]]. The replay of the clip's
staged scene, on a custom level, throws a Rock that falls at 0.1 for one step and 0.6 from the next, and is on
the floor 4 steps after it leaves the hands; then a Jar, which still has 0.1 after it lands
<Cite replay="fidelity/replays/item-mitt-gravity-one-step.txt" />.

## In the port

The spelunky.ai port reproduces it exactly: it's in docs/quirks.md's list of the 1.1 source's bugs
<Cite quirk="1" />, and the oracle's fingerprint of the replay pins both items' falls. No option turns it off.
