Wearing them, the player jumps from the ground half as hard again, which takes him nearly three blocks up
instead of under two: high enough to catch a ledge that's out of reach without them. Only jumps from the
ground get the boost. [[clothing-shop|Clothing Shops]], [[rare-shop|Rare Shops]] and the
[[dice-house|Dice House]] offer them, and [[crate|Crates]], blocks and [[kali|Kali]] give them away. They're worn from
the moment they're picked up or paid for until the game ends.

## Effect

<Clip id="spring-shoes/jump" />

Picking up a free pair, paying for one or carrying one out of a shop puts it on: the message "YOU GOT SPRING
SHOES! YOU FEEL BOUNCY." shows, the pair disappears, and an icon of it joins the row under the hearts
<Cite gml="scripts/scrStealItem.gml:171-181" /> <Cite gml="scripts/scrDrawHUD.gml:140-144" />. They stay on
until a new game starts, which clears them <Cite gml="scripts/scrClearGlobals.gml:92" />
<Cite gml="objects/oTransition/create.gml:20-24" />.

A jump from the ground starts with an upward push of 4 (twice the jump's base push of 2), and the shoes make
it <Stat id="spring-shoes.jump" /> that, 6 <Cite gml="scripts/characterStepEvent.gml:338-357" />
<Cite gml="scripts/characterCreateEvent.gml:67" />, which is also the most the push can be
<Cite gml="scripts/characterStepEvent.gml:882-883" />. Held for its full length, a jump without them rises
about 26 pixels; with them, about 46, nearly three blocks. The clip shows the difference: the player jumps at
a ledge three blocks up and falls well short of it, then, in the shoes, catches its edge, climbs onto it, and
jumps on the spot nearly three blocks high <Cite clip="spring-shoes/jump" />.

The higher jump doesn't hurt when he lands. A full jump with the shoes falls for 10 steps back to the ground
it left, and a fall costs hearts only after more than 16 steps
<Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:254-289" /> <Cite clip="spring-shoes/jump" />.

The Dice House puts its prize up from the same set of rare items, and a roll of seven gives it away and
puts up another <Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGen.gml:392-397" /> <Cite gml="objects/oShopkeeper/step.gml:173-200" />.
Every block of rock away from the level's edges that isn't gold and holds no gem has a 1 in 1200 chance of
hiding an item, one of nineteen at random <Cite gml="objects/oBrick/create.gml:7-16" />

[[level-editor|level editor]]'s tile `~` <Cite gml="scripts/scrCreateTileObj.gml:339-344" />.

any ware; whether it goes on at once depends on whether the player or the pair leaves the shop first

## Interactions

- Jumps that don't start on the ground get no boost: out of a [[web]], out of water, or up from a wall or ledge
  the player is hanging on <Cite gml="scripts/characterStepEvent.gml:294-319" />
  <Cite gml="scripts/characterStepEvent.gml:507-523" />. So [[climbing-gloves|Climbing Gloves]] climb no
  faster with them.

## Tips

- A ledge three blocks up, out of reach of an ordinary jump, can be caught and climbed with them.
- Jumping on flat ground in them is safe: the fall back takes too few steps to hurt.
