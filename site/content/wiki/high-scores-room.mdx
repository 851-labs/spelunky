It's one room, built in the room editor, a screen wide: a column of ledges at the left where the trophies
stand, a board at the right that shows the records, and a floor with a [[push-block|block]] and a button that clears them.
Each record good enough earns a trophy, and each gold trophy a door: three to the bonus rooms, the secret
challenges, and one to the Changing Room. Nothing there can hurt the player for long, and a door leads back
to the [[title-screen|title screen]].

## Generation

the doorway back <Cite room="rTitle" /> <Cite room="rHighscores" />

Coming in sets the level back to 1 and clears the bonus rooms' scores for the next challenge
<Cite gml="objects/oHighscores/create.gml:1-13" />. The room can be paused ([[controls#pausing]])
<Cite gml="objects/oHighscores/create.gml:1" />. In the clip, the player comes in from the title screen with
three gold trophies, climbs the column of ledges beside them, and goes into the top door <Cite clip="high-scores-room/climb" />.

### The room

The player's hearts are set back to the start's on every step, 4, or 2 for the
[[tunnel-man|Tunnel Man]], so nothing there kills him <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:2-7" />; his bombs
and ropes can't be used outside a level <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:944-948" />.

The board shows what's level with him. On the floor it's the records, the high scores: TOP DEFILERS, the
most money, kills and saves in a game, and the best time once the game has been won; then STATISTICS, the
plays, deaths and wins <Cite gml="objects/oHighscores/draw.gml:113-148" />. A record the last game beat has
NEW beside it <Cite gml="objects/oHighscores/create.gml:120-123" />. Up the column of ledges it's SECRET
CHALLENGES: each bonus room's best score, and the goal of the room whose door is level with him, or LOCKED.
if its trophy isn't gold <Cite gml="objects/oHighscores/draw.gml:1-112" />. [[scoring#the-high-scores]] has
how the records are kept.

### The trophies and doors

Each record earns a bronze, silver or gold trophy, at a score [[scoring#numbers]] lists, on the column of
ledges, from the top: money, time, kills and saves <Cite gml="objects/oHighscores/create.gml:32-102" />. A gold
one has a door under it:

| Gold trophy | Door | Board | |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Money | [[sun-room\|Sun Room]], the damsel challenge | KEEP YOURSELF AND THE DAMSEL ALIVE FOR AS LONG AS POSSIBLE! | <Cite gml="objects/oHighscores/create.gml:32-39" /> <Cite gml="objects/oHighscores/draw.gml:14-40" /> |
| Time | [[moon-room\|Moon Room]], the archery challenge | SHOOT THE MOVING TARGETS WITH YOUR BOW AND ARROWS! | <Cite gml="objects/oHighscores/create.gml:50-66" /> <Cite gml="objects/oHighscores/draw.gml:41-66" /> |
| Kills | [[stars-room\|Stars Room]], the shotgun challenge | KILL AS MANY ANGRY SHOPKEEPS AS YOU CAN BEFORE THEY GET YOU! | <Cite gml="objects/oHighscores/create.gml:68-84" /> <Cite gml="objects/oHighscores/draw.gml:67-92" /> |
| Saves | The Changing Room | LADY IN RED... | <Cite gml="objects/oHighscores/create.gml:86-102" /> <Cite gml="objects/oHighscores/draw.gml:93-110" /> |

The Moon Room's goal shows on the board for any win, however slow, though its door needs a time of 10:00 or
less ([[moon-room-goal-any-win]]) <Cite quirk="1" row="The Moon Room's goal shows for any winning time." />.

<Clip id="spelunker/change" />

The Changing Room isn't a room: going through its door turns the player into the [[damsel|Damsel]], with 4
hearts, 4 bombs and 4 ropes, and going through again turns her back into the [[spelunker|Spelunker]]
([[spelunker#playing-as-someone-else]]) <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/other_animation_end.gml:173-191" />. In
the clip, the Spelunker goes in and comes out as the Damsel <Cite clip="spelunker/change" />. With all four
gold trophies, the title screen has a door that does the same for the Tunnel Man
([[title-screen#the-room]]).

### Resetting

<Clip id="scoring/reset" />

A block on the floor clears everything. Pushed past the middle of the room, it brings up THIS WILL CLEAR
EVERYTHING! <Cite gml="objects/oHighscores/draw.gml:150-159" />. Pushed onto the button at the floor's end,
it takes the trophies and doors away, and 18 steps later every record, statistic and bonus room's best, and
the [[tunnel-man-shortcuts|Tunnel Man's shortcuts]], go back to the start, with a thump that shakes the room <Cite gml="objects/oButtonHighscore/step.gml:1-32" /> <Cite gml="scripts/scrResetHighscores.gml:24-36" />
<Cite gml="objects/oHighscores/step.gml:1-17" />. The clip shows it with all four gold trophies <Cite clip="scoring/reset" />.

### Leaving

The door the player came in by leads back to the title screen, where he comes out of its SCORES door <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/other_animation_end.gml:110-114" /> <Cite gml="objects/oTitle/create.gml:72-80" /> <Cite replay="fidelity/replays/title-doors.txt" />.

## Hazards

Only the reset block. It stops against the sign just past the button, and a player who keeps pushing creeps
into it and is crushed: he vanishes in blood, but the room isn't a level, so there's no game over, and his
hearts are back the next step ([[push-block-crush]]) <Cite quirk="1" row="Pushing a block into a wall crushes the player." /> <Cite replay="fidelity/replays/flow-highscores-reset-crush.txt" />.

## Secrets

The doors under the gold trophies: the [[sun-room|Sun Room]], the [[moon-room|Moon Room]] and the
[[stars-room|Stars Room]], each a challenge with a best score of its own, and the Changing Room
([The trophies and doors](#the-trophies-and-doors)).

## Trivia

- The board has a message for the [[tunnel-man|Tunnel Man]], "NO MATTOCKS ALLOWED! THIS MEANS YOU!", in place
  of each bonus room's goal, but the test that would show it is `if (false)`, with `(global.isTunnelMan)`
  commented out beside it <Cite gml="objects/oHighscores/draw.gml:21-27" />.
- The three bonus rooms' bests are kept as one number in the high score table, two digits each, and each
  room's score is cut to 99 before it's kept <Cite gml="scripts/scrUpdateHighscores.gml:41-43" /> <Cite gml="scripts/scrUpdateHighscores.gml:117" /> <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:1910-1912" />.
- The room's code still has values for testing every trophy, and a Q key that would clear the table, both
  commented out <Cite gml="objects/oHighscores/create.gml:24-30" /> <Cite gml="objects/oHighscores/keypress_Q.gml:1-8" />.
