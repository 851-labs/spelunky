Each area is built of its own rock: [[mines|the Mines]]' brown earth, [[jungle|the Jungle]]'s mossy earth,
[[ice-caves|the Ice Caves]]' dark blue rock (and [[ice|Ice]]), [[temple|the Temple]]'s stone bricks. Grey stone
blocks are mixed in, and around every level runs a border of blocks that can't be destroyed. A block is solid:
nothing walks or falls through it. Most break to a [[bomb|Bomb]] or a [[mattock|Mattock]], and as a level is
made, each block of rock rolls for a vein of gold or a hidden gem.

### Gold and gems

As each block of the Mines', the Jungle's, the Ice Caves' or the Temple's rock is made, it rolls 1 to 100: below
20, a small vein of gold shows in it; from 20 to 29, a big one. A block with neither, if it's in a level and off
the level's edge, rolls for a big gem inside it, hidden, then for an item, each roll only once the ones before
it fail <Cite gml="objects/oBrick/create.gml:7-16" />:

| [[mines\|The Mines]] | 19 in 100 | 10 in 100 | 1 in 100 | 1 in 120 | 1 in 140 | 1 in 1,200 | <Cite gml="objects/oBrick/create.gml:7-16" /> |

Broken, a small vein lets out three [[gold-chunk|Gold Chunks]], and a big one three Gold Chunks and a
[[gold-nugget|Gold Nugget]]; a gem or item inside simply drops out <Cite gml="objects/oBrick/destroy.gml:9-29" />.
The first clip shows it: a [[boulder|Boulder]] rolls along a corridor of the Mines, breaking the bricks in its
way, and the gold-veined ones spill gold <Cite clip="block/boulder" />. Stone blocks, smooth earth and hard
blocks hold nothing.

### Breaking them

- **[[bomb|Bombs]]** break every block their blast touches, unless it's one that can't be destroyed, as long as
  the blast is near the view (or anywhere in the Tutorial) <Cite gml="objects/oExplosion/collision_oSolid.gml:1-15" />
  ([[explosions-break-blocks-off-screen]]).
- **The [[mattock|Mattock]]** breaks a block a swing.
- **A [[boulder|Boulder]]** rolling fast enough breaks the blocks in its way <Cite gml="objects/oBoulder/collision_oSolid.gml:1-25" />.

Whatever breaks a block, its destruction takes [[spikes|Spikes]] and a tiki torch on top of it with it, brings
down a lamp hanging under it as a [[lamp|Lamp]], and lets water around it drain
<Cite gml="objects/oSolid/destroy.gml:9-35" />.

### Blocks that can't be destroyed

A block marked so ignores bombs and the Mattock: the border around every level
<Cite gml="scripts/scrInitLevel.gml:66-128" />, the block a level's exit stands on
<Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGen.gml:345-357" />, the one under the gold door
<Cite gml="scripts/scrEntityGen.gml:400-406" /> and the hard blocks <Cite gml="objects/oHardBlock/create.gml:3" />.

## Interactions

- **Shops.** A shop's floor and walls are marked as the shop's: breaking one angers its
  [[shopkeeper|Shopkeeper]] <Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGen.gml:413-426" /> <Cite gml="objects/oSolid/destroy.gml:1-7" />.
- **[[spectacles|Spectacles]]** and the [[udjat-eye|Udjat Eye]] draw the treasure hidden in the rock in front of
  it, so it shows <Cite gml="objects/oTreasure/step.gml:63-66" />.
- **Crushing.** A player whose middle ends up inside a block is crushed, losing every heart
  <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:1490-1511" />.

## Tips

- A block showing gold is worth a bomb or a swing of the Mattock: about one in five of the rock blocks has
  some.
- The deeper areas hide more gems in their rock: an Ice Caves block is two and a half times as likely to hold a
  Big Sapphire as a Mines one.

## Trivia

- In custom levels, the [[level-editor|level editor]]'s rock tiles set each block's picture back to plain
  earth after it's made, so no block of a custom level shows or holds gold; its gems and items still roll
  <Cite gml="scripts/scrCreateTileObj.gml:36-68" />.
- One brick in ten in the Mines is drawn with a second picture <Cite gml="objects/oBrick/create.gml:3-5" />.
