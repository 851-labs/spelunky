The lair is a long hall of temple stone, built by hand in the room editor rather than generated, where
[[olmec|Olmec]] sits asleep in stone with three [[caveman|Cavemen]] and a [[hawkman|Hawkman]] bowing before
him. Below its floor are rock and then a sea of [[lava|Lava]], and the way out is through it: Olmec can only
be killed by the lava, and only his death opens the door to the game's ending. Level generation adds the rock
and the treasure on the ledges above the hall, but no monsters, traps or shops.

## Generation

Olmec's Lair is level 16, the last <Cite gml="scripts/scrInitLevel.gml:29" />. Taking level 15's exit, in
[[temple|the Temple]] or the [[city-of-gold|City of Gold]], leads to the screen between levels and from it to
the lair, a room of its own <Cite gml="objects/oTransition/step.gml:26" />. The first clip shows the Temple's
way in: the player crosses level 15 and takes its exit, and the lair loads <Cite clip="temple/to-olmec" />.

<Clip id="temple/to-olmec" />

The room editor laid out the hall: its two doors, one at each end, on a floor of temple stone two blocks
thick; Olmec in the middle with his worshippers; and, far below, lava six blocks deep on a floor of solid
lava <Cite room="rOlmec" />. The player arrives at the left door and can't move while the view pans right to
Olmec and he wakes ([[olmec#awakening|Olmec's awakening]]) <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/create.gml:102" />
<Cite gml="objects/oOlmec/step.gml:51-62" /> <Cite gml="objects/oOlmec/alarm_5.gml:1-8" />.

Level generation adds walls of temple stone that can't be destroyed on the left, right and top
<Cite gml="scripts/scrInitLevel.gml:95-105" />, and fills sixteen rooms of 10 tiles by 8 from the lair's own
room script. It picks a room's layout by its height alone, not by a path to an exit, as the areas' scripts
do <Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGen5.gml:48-72" />:

| Rooms | Layout | |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The twelve above the hall, three rows of four | Open air with one or two 5-by-3 chunks, each a ledge with treasure on it, or nothing, 2 times in 8 | <Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGen5.gml:49-60" /> <Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGen5.gml:106-117" /> |
| The four under the hall's floor, a row | Solid stone, most with a patch of tiles that are each a block 1 time in 2 | <Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGen5.gml:61-72" /> <Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGen5.gml:148-155" /> |

Every block is temple stone, or, one time in ten, a stone block <Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGen5.gml:140-155" />.
Under the rock is a gap three blocks tall, then the lava <Cite room="rOlmec" />, so [[olmec|Olmec]]'s slams,
each of which breaks the blocks he lands on, dig him down through the floor and the rock until he falls
in.

The lair is never a special level: the roll for a [[dark-level|dark level]] is skipped on level 16
<Cite gml="scripts/scrInitLevel.gml:167-174" />, and nothing else is rolled for it. The [[ghost|Ghost]] never comes,
however long the player stays <Cite gml="objects/oGame/step.gml:23-47" />.

### The way out

The lair has no exit at first. When Olmec dies, the door at the right end of the hall becomes one, with a
block of stone that can't be destroyed put under it if the floor there is gone
<Cite gml="objects/oFinalBoss/step.gml:1-19" />. Walking through it leads to the game's ending rather than to
another level <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:741" /> <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/other_animation_end.gml:132-136" />.
The player keeps what he holds unless it's heavy: a rope or an unlit bomb goes back into his supply, a lit
bomb is let go, and anything else goes with him, and the ending draws him holding it if it's one of the items it has a picture for, such as a
[[rock|Rock]], a [[skull|Skull]] or the [[sceptre|Sceptre]]
<Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:682-725" /> <Cite gml="objects/oPDummy/draw.gml:18-38" />. In the
second clip, Olmec, sunk in the lava, dies, and the door opens; the player climbs out of the shaft his slams
dug on four ropes, runs along the hall and goes through the door to the ending <Cite clip="olmecs-lair/exit" />.

## Inhabitants

Level generation makes no monsters in the lair: its code for monsters, traps and scattered treasure covers
the four areas only <Cite gml="scripts/scrEntityGen.gml:30-525" />. The lair's monsters are the room
editor's and Olmec's:

| Monster | Where | Chance | |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [[olmec\|Olmec]] | In the middle of the hall, asleep in stone | Always | <Cite room="rOlmec" /> |
| [[hawkman\|Hawkman]] | One, bowing before Olmec; flung away, stunned, as he wakes | Always | <Cite room="rOlmec" /> <Cite gml="objects/oOlmec/alarm_1.gml:9-21" /> |
| [[caveman\|Caveman]] | Three, bowing before Olmec; they charge as he first hops | Always | <Cite room="rOlmec" /> <Cite gml="objects/oOlmec/alarm_6.gml:1-7" /> |
| [[bat\|Bat]], [[spider\|Spider]], [[snake\|Snake]], [[frog\|Frog]] or [[fire-frog\|Fire Frog]] | Where one of Olmec's yellow balls lands | Three balls, 1 time in 2 after a slam | <Cite gml="objects/oOlmec/step.gml:167-176" /> <Cite gml="objects/oYellowBall/step.gml:4-20" /> |
| [[magma-man\|Magma Man]] | Out of the lava's spurts | 1 spurt in 8 is a blob of magma | <Cite gml="objects/oLava/step.gml:6-15" /> |

No [[shopkeeper|Shopkeeper]] waits at the lair for a thief or a murderer, as one does at other levels'
exits: he's put at an exit as the level is made, and the lair has none until Olmec dies
<Cite gml="scripts/scrInitLevel.gml:245-257" /> <Cite room="rOlmec" />.

## Hazards

- **[[olmec|Olmec]]**, whose only attack is to slam down on the player ([[olmec#damage|Damage]]).
- **[[lava|Lava]]**, below the rock. One surface tile in four spurts flames while the player is within 240
  pixels, every 100–300 steps, and 1 spurt in 8 throws a blob of magma instead <Cite
  gml="scripts/scrSetupWalls.gml:398-414" /> <Cite gml="objects/oLava/create.gml:1-5" />
  <Cite gml="objects/oLava/step.gml:1-16" />.
- **Push blocks.** One stone block in four with temple stone under it, and no lava beside it, becomes a
  push block, as in the Temple <Cite gml="scripts/scrSetupWalls.gml:457-471" />.

## Items and shops

The lair has no shops: its room script doesn't make them <Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGen5.gml:48-72" />. Nor
is treasure scattered over its blocks, as it is in every area <Cite gml="scripts/scrEntityGen.gml:30-525" />.
Its treasure is on the ledges above the hall, where each treasure tile of a chunk rolls, each roll made once
the ones before it fail <Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGen5.gml:177-190" />:

| Treasure | Chance | |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [[chest\|Chest]] | 1 in 15 | <Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGen5.gml:179" /> |
| [[gold-bars\|Gold Bars]] | 1 in 6 | <Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGen5.gml:180" /> |
| Big emerald | 1 in 6 | <Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGen5.gml:181" /> |
| Big sapphire | 1 in 8 | <Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGen5.gml:182" /> |
| Big ruby | 1 in 10 | <Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGen5.gml:183" /> |
| [[crate\|Crate]] | 1 in 10 | <Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGen5.gml:184" /> |
| A stone block | 1 in 10 | <Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGen5.gml:185" /> |
| A block of temple stone | When every roll before it fails | <Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGen5.gml:186-189" /> |

The stone holds more, as the Temple's does: about one block in five shows a vein of gold, which breaks into
three [[gold-chunk|Gold Chunks]], and one in ten a rich vein, which adds a [[gold-nugget|Gold Nugget]]. Of the
rest, 1 in 60 holds a big sapphire, and rarer ones a big emerald, a big ruby, or an item
<Cite gml="objects/oTemple/create.gml:5-16" /> <Cite gml="objects/oTemple/destroy.gml:12-32" />. With seed 1, a
[[compass|Compass]] is buried in a block by the left wall, and Olmec's slams dig it out
<Cite replay="fidelity/replays/item-seed-1-compass-olmec-lair.txt" />.

## Trivia

- The room script's header calls the lair "the final boss level (which is only partially randomized)"
  <Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGen5.gml:1-5" />.
- The room script has code, like the Temple's, for tiles none of its layouts or chunks has: an entrance or
  exit, vines, Spikes, push blocks, a chest, an idol, ceiling traps, doors, water, and a Thwomp trap, which
  no other code makes, so the game never has one <Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGen5.gml:156-176" />
  <Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGen5.gml:191-212" /> <Cite quirk="1" />.
- The lair's exit is a different kind of door from a level's exit, the kind the title screen's doors are,
  which is why the player's step has a branch for what he holds there, as its comment explains: "oXEnd is
  the child of oXStart, for some reason, that's why this is here" <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:682" />.
- Any crush in the lair is counted against Olmec, even one by the block put under the exit
  <Cite quirk="1" /> <Cite replay="fidelity/replays/olmec-door-block-crush-seed-1.txt" />.
