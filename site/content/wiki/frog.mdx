[[level-generation|Level generation]] puts Frogs on blocks all over [[jungle|the Jungle]], under water as well as on land. A Frog
doesn't move until the player comes within <Stat id="frog.wake-range" />; then it jumps at him again and
again, each jump carrying it several blocks, and bites him if it lands on him. With <Stat id="frog.hp" />, it
dies to any damage, whether from the [[whip|Whip]], a [[stomping|stomp]] or a thrown [[rock|Rock]].

## Behaviour

<Clip id="frog/jump" />

A Frog sits still until the player is within <Stat id="frog.wake-range" /> of it, counting from the edges of
the two, and then croaks and starts jumping <Cite gml="objects/oFrog/step.gml:38-47" />. Each jump starts from
the ground at 2–4 pixels per step upward and <Stat id="frog.jump-speed" />
(<Stat id="frog.jump-speed" as="per-second" />) across, toward the player, and it keeps that speed across
until it lands <Cite gml="objects/oFrog/step.gml:58-78" />. A wall it hits sends it back the other way at 1
pixel per step, and a ceiling stops it rising <Cite gml="objects/oFrog/step.gml:25-33" />
<Cite gml="objects/oFrog/step.gml:85-86" />.

Gravity adds 0.2 pixels per step to its fall every step, which brings it down again about 20, 30 or 40 steps
after a jump at 2, 3 or 4 upward, so a jump carries it about 60, 90 or 120 pixels across, four to seven blocks
<Cite gml="objects/oFrog/create.gml:8-9" />. In the clip, its first jump, at 3 upward, carries it 93 pixels
and 23 high, clean over the player's head; its second lands on him <Cite clip="frog/jump" />.

When it lands, it stops dead and rests for <Stat id="frog.rest" /> (<Stat id="frog.rest" as="seconds" />); if
the player is still in range when the rest is over, it jumps again, and if not, it sits where it landed
<Cite gml="objects/oFrog/step.gml:48-57" /> <Cite gml="objects/oFrog/step.gml:40-47" />. It never walks:
jumping is its only way of moving.

Water doesn't trouble a Frog: its gravity in water is the same 0.2 as in air, so it jumps and falls there as
it does on land, and there's no drowning in its code <Cite gml="objects/oFrog/create.gml:8-9" />
<Cite gml="objects/oEnemy/create.gml:18" /> <Cite gml="objects/oEnemy/step.gml:34-55" />. Like most monsters,
it only acts while it's on screen or just off it <Cite gml="objects/oFrog/step.gml:3-4" />.

## Damage

When the player touches a Frog without stomping it, he loses <Stat id="frog.damage" />, is knocked away from
it, and blinks, invincible, for 30 steps (1 second)
<Cite gml="objects/oEnemy/collision_oCharacter.gml:14-25" />. This is the touch code most monsters share; the
Frog has no attack of its own, and the clip above shows a jump landing on the player and taking him from four
hearts to three.

## Locations

Level generation makes Frogs only in [[jungle|the Jungle]], levels 5–8, on top of blocks with open space above
them, outside the starting room and shops, and not on a [[restless-dead|graveyard level]]
<Cite gml="scripts/scrInitLevel.gml:26" /> <Cite gml="scripts/scrEntityGen.gml:262-296" />:

- **On land**, a 1 in 30 chance for each such block, once the rolls for a [[mantrap|Mantrap]], a
  [[caveman|Caveman]] and a [[fire-frog|Fire Frog]] have failed
  <Cite gml="scripts/scrEntityGen.gml:285-292" />.
- **Under water**, where the block has water above it, a 1 in 30 chance once the roll for a Fire Frog has
  failed <Cite gml="scripts/scrEntityGen.gml:294-295" />.

Frogs also come from:

- **Fire Frogs.** A Fire Frog that lands in water turns into a Frog
  <Cite gml="objects/oEnemy/step.gml:44-49" />.
- **[[olmec|Olmec]]'s yellow balls**, which turn into a [[bat|Bat]], [[spider|Spider]], [[snake|Snake]], Frog
  or Fire Frog when they hit the ground <Cite gml="objects/oYellowBall/step.gml:4-19" />.
- **Custom levels**, as the [[level-editor|level editor]]'s tile `f`
  <Cite gml="scripts/scrCreateTileObj.gml:156-159" />.

## Tips

- A Frog doesn't move until the player is within <Stat id="frog.wake-range" />, so one can be left sitting by
  keeping that far away.
- Each jump keeps its speed across until the Frog lands, so a player who steps aside while it's in the air is
  out of its way.
- A Frog rests after every landing, which is the moment to whip it.

## Trivia

- The Frog's code names a walking state and a drowned state, but nothing ever puts a Frog in either: it only
  sits, jumps and lands <Cite gml="objects/oFrog/create.gml:22-28" />.
- A commented-out line beside its range check would have woken it at 48 pixels rather than 64
  <Cite gml="objects/oFrog/step.gml:45" />.
