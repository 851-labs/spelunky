Wearing it, the player sees the gems and gold buried in blocks, and on the right [[jungle|Jungle]] level
it tells him he's near the [[black-market|Black Market]]'s entrance, hidden in a block, by beeping faster as
he closes in. It comes from a locked chest on one of the [[mines|Mines]]' levels 2 to 4, opened with a key
the same level hides. It's worn from the moment it's picked up until the game ends.

## Effect

<Clip id="udjat-eye/chest" />

Picking it up puts it on: the message "YOU GOT THE UDJAT EYE! YOU FEEL AWAKENED." shows, the Eye disappears,
and an icon of it joins the row under the hearts <Cite gml="scripts/scrStealItem.gml:24-34" />
<Cite gml="scripts/scrDrawHUD.gml:100-105" />. It stays on until a new game starts, which clears it
<Cite gml="scripts/scrClearGlobals.gml:84" /> <Cite gml="objects/oTransition/create.gml:20-24" />.

### Buried treasure

Every treasure on the screen is drawn at depth 0 rather than 101 while the player wears it, which puts it in
front of the blocks, so gems and gold buried inside blocks show through them
<Cite gml="objects/oTreasure/step.gml:63-67" />. Items stay hidden: unlike the [[spectacles|Spectacles]],
the Eye doesn't change how they're drawn <Cite gml="objects/oItem/step.gml:5-6" />. In the clip, on the step
the player picks the Eye up, a [[big-sapphire|Big Sapphire]] and a [[big-ruby|Big Ruby]] buried in the rock to his right appear
<Cite clip="udjat-eye/chest" />.

### The Black Market

<Clip id="udjat-eye/market" />

One of the Jungle's levels 5 to 7 hides the entrance to the Black Market: level 5 one time in 3, level 6 one
time in 2, and otherwise level 7, once a game <Cite gml="scripts/scrInitLevel.gml:186-192" />. It's a door
set inside a block, which has to be dug or bombed out to reach it
<Cite gml="scripts/scrEntityGen.gml:207-225" /> <Cite gml="scripts/scrEntityGen.gml:303-319" />. On that
level, the Eye blinks and beeps. Its next blink comes after as many steps as the player is pixels from the
entrance, and no fewer than 4, and sooner if he gets closer in the meantime; each blink switches its icon
and plays one of two sounds in turn <Cite gml="objects/oGame/step.gml:3-12" />
<Cite gml="objects/oGame/alarm_2.gml:1-9" />. The timer runs whether or not the player wears the Eye: in a replay
of a level 5 with the entrance, a blink with the player about 116 pixels away sets the next for 115 steps
later, which comes after 107 as he walks closer, and beside the entrance it blinks every 4 steps
<Cite replay="fidelity/replays/city-of-gold-seed-9.txt" />. It's only set again while the entrance is active,
which, like most of a level, it isn't once it's far outside the view, so far from it the Eye falls silent
after its next blink <Cite gml="objects/oLevel/step.gml:75-94" />. An entrance to the player's right or
below him comes back on before the view nears it, as the region the game keeps active around him stretches
far that way <Cite quirk="1" />, and the blink then waits for as long as he closes in faster than a pixel
a step. In the clip, the player wears the Eye into a level 7 that hides the entrance at the other end of
the level's top from where he comes in: the Eye stays silent as he runs across, blinks for the first time
beside the entrance, and then every 4 steps <Cite clip="udjat-eye/market" />. On a level without the
entrance, the Eye is silent. Walking into the entrance takes the player to the Black Market as the next level
<Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:879" />.

## Obtaining

| Source | Chance | |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A locked chest | Made on level 2 one time in 3, on level 3 one time in 2, and otherwise on level 4 | <Cite gml="scripts/scrInitLevel.gml:178-184" /> |

On the level that makes it, the chest goes in a nook in the rock, rolled for each nook as the level fills
them, or, failing that, beside the exit <Cite gml="scripts/scrTreasureGen.gml:47-64" />
<Cite gml="scripts/scrEntityGen.gml:76-106" />. One of the level's treasures not inside a block becomes its
key, picked at random <Cite gml="scripts/scrEntityGen.gml:108-141" />. Once the key is made, no later level
makes another chest, opened or not <Cite gml="scripts/scrEntityGen.gml:143" />. The chest opens only to the
key held by the player and touching it: the key is used up, and the Eye pops out with a puff of smoke
<Cite gml="objects/oLockedChest/collision_oKey.gml:1-21" />. Picking it up then puts it on. The first clip
shows a key carried down to its chest <Cite clip="udjat-eye/chest" />.

The key can be anywhere in the level, and sometimes in a pocket of rock that nothing leads into ([[udjat-key-sealed]]), the chest
too, which has to be broken open, with a [[bomb|Bomb]] or the [[mattock|Mattock]] <Cite quirk="1" />. In the second clip, two bombs have opened such a pocket; an
[[arrow-trap|Arrow Trap]] fires as the player drops in, and he crawls the key under low rock to the chest
<Cite clip="udjat-eye/sealed-key" />.

<Clip id="udjat-eye/sealed-key" />

No shop sells the Eye, no Crate or block holds one, and the [[level-editor|level editor]] has no tile for it.

## Interactions

- The [[spectacles|Spectacles]] put treasure in front of the blocks the same way, and buried items too
  <Cite gml="objects/oTreasure/step.gml:63" />.
- The Black Market sells the [[ankh|Ankh]], so the Eye is the first step toward it
  <Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGenMarket.gml:361-364" />.

## Tips

- The beeping only starts on the level with the entrance, so a beep on a Jungle level is the sign that the
  Black Market is on it; the faster it comes, the nearer the hidden door. A silent Eye doesn't rule it out:
  far from the door, it can stay quiet until the door is close <Cite clip="udjat-eye/market" />.
- The chest's key is always one of the level's loose treasures, so a level with a chest is worth searching
  for a key lying where a gem or gold would be.
