[[level-generation|Level generation]] puts Yetis on the ground all over [[ice-caves|the Ice Caves]]. A Yeti wanders like a
[[caveman|Caveman]] but never charges, and what it does to the player depends on the ceiling: in the open it
picks him up and throws him, and under a ceiling one block above it, it bites. With <Stat id="yeti.hp" />,
it survives most hits, which stun it, and the [[whip|Whip]] can't hurt it at all.

## Behaviour

A Yeti sets off walking in a random direction as soon as it's made, turning around at walls
<Cite gml="objects/oYeti/step.gml:59-64" /> <Cite gml="objects/oYeti/step.gml:78-82" />. Its code sets its
speed to <Stat id="yeti.walk-speed" />, but it covers 4 pixels every 3 steps (40 per second), as a Caveman
does: friction takes 0.1 off before it moves, and the game moves it in whole pixels <Cite
gml="objects/oYeti/step.gml:94-113" /> <Cite gml="objects/oYeti/step.gml:204-206" /> <Cite
clip="yeti/stomp" />. It stops to stand for 20–50 steps (0.67–1.67 seconds), with a 1 in 100 chance
each step and when it reaches the edge of a ledge, then sets off again in a random direction
<Cite gml="objects/oYeti/step.gml:88-113" />. At a ledge it stops dead, where a Caveman slides on
([[caveman-ledge-slide]]). Standing beside a wall, it hops <Cite gml="objects/oYeti/step.gml:45-52" />.

Every 6 steps while it stands or walks, a Yeti sends an invisible sight check ahead of it, as a Caveman
does <Cite gml="objects/oYeti/step.gml:65-74" /> <Cite gml="objects/oYeti/step.gml:115-124" />. When one
reaches the player, it sets off the Cavemen and [[hawkman|Hawkmen]] within 100 pixels of him
([[caveman-sight-alerts-others]]), but not Yetis: a Yeti never charges at the player ([[yeti-never-charges]]) <Cite
gml="objects/oEnemySight/collision_oCharacter.gml:1-17" />.

A Yeti only acts while it's on screen or just off it
<Cite gml="objects/oEnemy/step.gml:7-11" /> <Cite gml="objects/oYeti/step.gml:3" />.

## Damage

<Clip id="yeti/throw" />

When the player touches a Yeti that isn't stunned, without landing on it, and isn't invincible, the Yeti
grabs him, unless there's a block right above it. It stops, turns toward him, and throws him back over its
head at <Stat id="yeti.throw-speed" /> sideways and 6 pixels per step upward, stunned, and he drops whatever
he's holding <Cite gml="objects/oYeti/collision_oCharacter.gml:42-77" />. The throw itself costs nothing, but
the first wall or floor he hits while stunned costs him <Stat id="yeti.throw-damage" />, or a fall long enough to
hurt him on its own does instead <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:1815-1842" />
<Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:254-257" />. The clip shows a Yeti throwing the player, who loses a
heart where he lands.

<Clip id="yeti/ceiling" />

With a block right above it, a Yeti can't lift the player, and the touch costs him
<Stat id="yeti.damage" /> instead, knocks him away and makes him invincible for 30 steps (1 second)
<Cite gml="objects/oYeti/collision_oCharacter.gml:23-41" />. The clip shows it in a tunnel one block high.
A stunned or dead Yeti does nothing to him.

## Defeating

<Clip id="yeti/stomp" />

Most hits stun a Yeti as well as hurting it: it's knocked back and lies on the ground for
<Stat id="yeti.stun-time" /> (<Stat id="yeti.stun-time" as="seconds" />), counted down while it's on the
ground or carried, and then gets up if it has hit points left, slipping out of the player's hands if he's
holding it <Cite gml="objects/oYeti/step.gml:137-175" />. The clip shows a stomp stunning a Yeti, which gets
up about eight seconds later and walks on. A stomp, a [[throwing|thrown item]] and a stunned monster thrown at it can't
hurt a Yeti that's already stunned:

The Whip's crack and its wind-up only push a Yeti back: they hurt it only when the hit punctures, which a
Machete's and a Mattock's do and the Whip's doesn't <Cite gml="objects/oYeti/collision_oWhipPre.gml:1-31" />
<Cite gml="objects/oWhip/create.gml:5" /> <Cite gml="objects/oSlash/create.gml:6" />.

When its hit points run out, a Yeti dies and leaves its body, which can be picked up and thrown
<Cite gml="objects/oYeti/step.gml:21-24" /> <Cite gml="objects/oYeti/step.gml:176-193" />. Yetis killed count
toward the player's kills, and the screen between levels draws a Yeti icon for each
<Cite gml="objects/oYeti/step.gml:178-188" /> <Cite gml="objects/oTransition/alarm_0.gml:197-201" />. One that
Spikes, Lava or a Spear Trap killed doesn't count <Cite gml="objects/oEnemy/step.gml:68" />
<Cite gml="objects/oEnemy/step.gml:87" /> <Cite gml="objects/oEnemy/step.gml:120" />.

A stunned or dead Yeti left lying still on [[kalis-altar|Kali's altar]] for 20 steps is [[sacrifice|sacrificed]] to her, adding
<Stat id="yeti.favor" /> to her [[kalis-favour|favour]] if it's stunned and half that if it's dead, unless her favour is
already −8 or less <Cite gml="objects/oEnemy/step.gml:130-167" />.

## Locations

- **[[ice-caves|The Ice Caves]].** Level generation gives each block with open space above it, outside
  the shops and more than 64 pixels from the entrance, with no monster or [[spikes|Spikes]] already on it, a
  1 in 20 chance of a Yeti standing on it, once the roll for a [[spring-trap|Spring Trap]] there has failed
  <Cite gml="scripts/scrEntityGen.gml:362-371" />.
- **[[yeti-lair|The Yeti lair]].** The room that holds the [[yeti-king|Yeti King]] has four Yetis beside it
  <Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGenYeti.gml:127" /> <Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGenYeti.gml:447-450" />, and the
  lair's blocks get the rolls above too.
- **Custom levels**, as the [[level-editor|level editor]]'s tile `y`
  <Cite gml="scripts/scrCreateTileObj.gml:197-200" />.

## Tips

- In the open, touching a Yeti costs no heart until the player lands; a long fall after the throw costs
  the fall's damage instead.
- A player a Yeti throws drops anything he carries, a [[golden-idol|Golden Idol]] included.
- Only a block right above a Yeti makes it bite, so in a tunnel one block high it hurts on contact like
  most monsters.
- The Whip can't hurt a Yeti; a stomp stuns it for <Stat id="yeti.stun-time" as="seconds" />, long enough
  to carry it to Kali's altar.

## Trivia

- The Yeti has a charging state, at 3 pixels per step, but its own sight checks never set it off
  <Cite gml="objects/oYeti/step.gml:126-136" />. The only code that does is a [[piranha|Piranha]] biting a swimming Yeti
  <Cite gml="objects/oPiranha/step.gml:85-110" /> <Cite quirk="1" />.
- Its sight check records which Yeti sent it, and nothing ever reads it
  <Cite gml="objects/oYeti/step.gml:72" />.
- The Ice Caves' room script can place a Yeti on a tile `Y`, but none of its rooms has one
  <Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGen3.gml:404-407" /> <Cite quirk="1" />.
