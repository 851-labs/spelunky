The [[mothership|mothership]]'s barrier keeps the player away from the [[alien-boss|Alien Boss]], throwing him back
the way he came. It throws items too, but always to the left, the side the player comes from, whichever side the
item reaches it from. So an item from the boss's side, behind the barrier, is thrown on through it.

## What happens

Behind the Alien Boss, a bomb at the foot of a shaft the player has dug breaks the rock holding the free
[[jetpack|Jetpack]] and blows it left. It hits the Alien Boss on its way, costing it a hit point, and reaches the
barrier, which throws it on to the left, and it lands beyond the barrier, which still stands
<Cite clip="quirks/barrier-item-thrown-through" />.

<Clip id="quirks/barrier-item-thrown-through" />

## Why

The barrier's collision with an item lights a [[bomb|Bomb]], and then throws the item, whatever it is:

```gml objects/oBarrier/collision_oItem.gml:11-12
other.xVel = -rand(4,6);
other.yVel = -2;
```

`xVel` is always negative, to the left. Its collision with the player tests which side he's on, and throws him away
from it:

```gml objects/oBarrier/collision_oCharacter.gml:6-9
    if (other.x < x)
        other.xVel = -6
    else
        other.xVel = 6
```

The item's has no such test, so an item that reaches the barrier from the right, the boss's side, is thrown on in
the way it was going <Cite quirk="1" />.

## Reproducing it

Get an item behind the barrier and send it left into it, with the barrier still standing. That's the hard part:
the barrier comes down when the Alien Boss dies <Cite gml="objects/oAlienBoss/other_animation_end.gml:1-5" />, and
when its emitter is destroyed: by an explosion, a bullet, or an item faster than 2 pixels a
step <Cite gml="objects/oExplosion/collision_oBarrierEmitter.gml:1" /> <Cite gml="objects/oBarrierEmitter/collision_oBullet.gml:1" />
<Cite gml="objects/oBarrierEmitter/collision_oItem.gml:1-4" />, or once it's on screen with the block above it
gone <Cite gml="objects/oBarrierEmitter/step.gml:1-5" />. So bombs have to go off well clear of both. The replay, with
seed 63, reaches the mothership on level 10. Four bombs dig a shaft down the craft's right edge, and the last
breaks the rock holding the Jetpack and blows it left: it hits the boss, meets the barrier, which throws it on to
the left, and lands beyond it. The barrier, its emitter and the boss stand throughout
<Cite replay="fidelity/replays/ice-seed-63-barrier-through.txt" />.

## In the port

The spelunky.ai port reproduces it exactly: it's in docs/quirks.md's list of the 1.1 source's bugs
<Cite quirk="1" />, and the oracle's fingerprint of the replay pins the Jetpack's path. No option turns it off.
