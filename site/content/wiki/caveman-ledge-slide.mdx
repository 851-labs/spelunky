A [[caveman|Caveman]] walks back and forth, and stops when it reaches the edge of a ledge, as the Temple's
[[hawkman|Hawkmen]] and the Ice Caves' [[yeti|Yetis]] do. Only the Caveman's stop doesn't hold: it slides past
the edge, and the next time it heads that way it slides off, so Cavemen come down from their ledges where the
others stay put.

## What happens

A Caveman and a Hawkman walk to the edges of their platforms and stop. The Hawkman stops dead; the Caveman
keeps sliding, stops at the edge again and slides off it, dropping to the floor below
<Cite clip="quirks/caveman-ledge-slide" />.

<Clip id="quirks/caveman-ledge-slide" />

## Why

Walking, a Caveman tests for floor past its front edge, and where there's none, it stops: it goes back to
standing (`IDLE`) and sets its speed to 0. But the line that sets its walking speed comes after the test, so it
moves on in the same step:

```gml objects/oCaveman/step.gml:88-97
    if (facing == LEFT)
    {
        if (not collision_point(x-1, y+16, oSolid, -1, -1))
        {
            status = IDLE;
            counter = rand(20,50);
            xVel = 0;
        }
        xVel = -1.5;
    }
```

Its friction takes 0.1 off a step, and 0 below 0.5 <Cite gml="objects/oCaveman/step.gml:198-200" />, so it
stands with its speed running down from 1.5, and slides about 10 pixels: past the edge, but not off it. When
it sets off that way again, the test finds no floor at once, and it stops and slides again, off the edge this
time <Cite quirk="1" />.

The Hawkman and the Yeti walk with the same code, with the two lines the other way round: the speed first,
then the test, which sets it to 0 <Cite gml="objects/oHawkman/step.gml:87-106" />. So they stop dead.

## Reproducing it

Watch any Caveman on a ledge in the Mines, the Jungle or the Temple: its first stop at an edge takes it over
the edge, and its second at the same edge takes it off. The replay of the clip's staged scene, on a custom
level, has a Caveman and a Hawkman stop at their platforms' edges on the same step, the Hawkman dead and the
Caveman sliding on; the Caveman stops there again and falls, and the Hawkman never does
<Cite replay="fidelity/replays/enemy-caveman-ledge-slide.txt" />.

## In the port

The spelunky.ai port reproduces it exactly: it's in docs/quirks.md's list of the 1.1 source's bugs
<Cite quirk="1" />, and the oracle's fingerprint of the replay pins the slide. No option turns it off.
