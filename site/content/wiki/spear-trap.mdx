A Spear Trap is a pillar two blocks tall that rises from the floor, a carved face with a slot on each side.
When the player, a monster or an item comes level with it, within 64 pixels, it thrusts a set of spears out
into the block beside it on that side; they strike 20 steps later, taking 4 hearts from the player on each
step he's in them. On a [[dark-level|dark level]] its upper block is lit.

## How it works

<Clip id="spear-trap/stab" />

Each of the trap's two blocks watches its own row, both ways. Each step, unless it has thrust in the last
<Stat id="spear-trap.reload" /> (<Stat id="spear-trap.reload" as="seconds" />), it checks the player, the
nearest monster, the nearest pushable block and the nearest item: one level with it, within 4 pixels, and
within <Stat id="spear-trap.range" /> of it on one side makes it thrust its spears out on that side
<Cite gml="objects/oSpearTrapTop/step.gml:1-77" /> <Cite gml="objects/oSpearTrapTop/create.gml:3-5" />. The lower
block runs the same code, so it can thrust along the row below <Cite gml="objects/oSpearTrapBottom/step.gml:1-89" />.

The spears fill the one block beside the trap, so the trap sees farther than it reaches. They come out over
31 steps, one frame of their animation a step, and are gone at its end; they only strike on frames 20 to 23,
4 steps that start 20 steps (0.67 seconds) after the trap sees something
<Cite gml="objects/oSpearsLeft/other_animation_end.gml:1" /> <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:1632" />.
Spears whose trap has been destroyed vanish <Cite gml="objects/oSpearsLeft/step.gml:1-8" />.

The clip shows a player with eight hearts walking up to a trap: it thrusts as he comes within range, he walks
into the spears' block, and when they strike they take 4 hearts on three steps running, 8 to 4 to 0 to -4.

## Damage

| Victim | Damage | |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The player | 4 hearts on each step of the strike he's in the spears, which throw him up and away | <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:1629-1647" /> |
| Most monsters | 2 hit points on each step of the strike | <Cite gml="objects/oEnemy/step.gml:98-104" /> |
| A [[caveman\|Caveman]], [[mantrap\|Mantrap]], [[yeti\|Yeti]], [[hawkman\|Hawkman]] or [[shopkeeper\|Shopkeeper]] | 2 hit points while it has hit points left, and stuns it, throwing it up and away | <Cite gml="objects/oEnemy/step.gml:76-97" /> |

Nothing spaces out the hits: there's no invincibility after one, so the player is hit again on every step of
the strike he's still in the spears, and each hit he takes alive is counted in the end-of-game statistics'
spear trap row, not only the one that kills him <Cite quirk="1" row="The deaths table's explosion and trap rows don't count deaths." />. The monsters' check looks at the spears
nearest the monster, not necessarily the ones touching it ([[nearest-enemy]]) <Cite gml="objects/oEnemy/step.gml:76-78" />
<Cite quirk="1" row="The arrow and spear checks take the nearest one, not the one touching." />. A monster a Spear Trap kills doesn't count
as the player's kill <Cite gml="objects/oEnemy/step.gml:87" /> <Cite gml="objects/oEnemy/step.gml:101" />. The
monsters' check is in the code most monsters share, so those whose own step event replaces it, such as the
[[bat|Bat]], aren't hurt.

## Locations

[[level-generation|Level generation]] turns a floor block into a Spear Trap's lower half and puts its upper half on top. A block
qualifies if it's at the edge of a drop (open on at least one side), has two open blocks above it and ground
under it, isn't in a shop or in a column at the edge of a room, and is more than 64 pixels from the
entrance:

| Area | Chance for each such block | |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [[jungle\|The Jungle]] | 1 in 12; on a dark level, once a 1 in 20 roll for a tiki torch fails | <Cite gml="scripts/scrEntityGen.gml:226-252" /> |
| [[temple\|The Temple]] | 1 in 12, but not where the trap would block a passage of lava; on level 14, none before the [[city-of-gold\|City of Gold]]'s door is placed, as each block takes the door's roll instead | <Cite gml="scripts/scrEntityGen.gml:399-441" /> <Cite quirk="1" row="On level 14, no block visited before the gold door's becomes a Spear Trap." /> |

On a [[dark-level|dark level]] the upper half is the lit kind, which glows and lights the darkness like a
[[lamp]] <Cite gml="scripts/scrEntityGen.gml:248" /> <Cite gml="scripts/scrEntityGen.gml:436" />
<Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:125-130" />. Spear Traps

## Avoiding

- **Pass after the thrust.** Once the spears have struck, the trap can't thrust again until 50 steps after
  the last thrust, so the player can set it off from out of reach and go past when the strike is over.
- **Go over it.** The trap only sees what's level with one of its blocks, so a player standing on top of it
  doesn't set it off. The second clip shows him standing on a lit trap unhurt.
- **Set it off with something else.** A monster or a [[throwing|thrown item]] level with it uses up a thrust too.
- **Destroy it.** A [[bomb|Bomb]] destroys it like a block, as the second clip shows, and a trap with nothing
  under it falls apart, on screen <Cite gml="objects/oSpearTrapTop/step.gml:79-86" />.

## Interactions

- **Water.** Destroying a Spear Trap lets water and lava flow into the space, as destroying a block does
  <Cite gml="objects/oSpearTrapTop/destroy.gml:11" />.
- **[[push-block|Push blocks]] and ice blocks** set it off as monsters do <Cite gml="objects/oSpearTrapTop/step.gml:21-28" />.

## Trivia

- Spear Traps were meant to fire arrows: both halves have an alarm, never set, that would fire one to the
  left at 5 pixels a step <Cite gml="objects/oSpearTrapTop/alarm_0.gml:1-2" />
  <Cite gml="objects/oSpearTrapTop/create.gml:8" />.
- The spears' object is called `oSpearsLeft` whichever way they point: a trap thrusting right makes the same
  object and gives it the right-pointing sprite <Cite gml="objects/oSpearTrapTop/step.gml:41-47" />.
