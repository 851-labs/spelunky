A [[rock|Rock]] moving fast across the player costs him 2 hearts and stuns him, unless he wears the
[[pitchers-mitt|Pitcher's Mitt]] with empty hands, when he catches it. The catch is written for the object
rather than the Rock, so it catches all of them.

## What happens

Wearing a Pitcher's Mitt, the player throws a Rock at a wall and catches it on the rebound. The two Rocks
lying on the floor jump into his hands with it, and when he throws, all three fly together
<Cite clip="quirks/mitt-catch-every-rock" />.

<Clip id="quirks/mitt-catch-every-rock" />

## Why

The player's step finds the Rock nearest him among those touching him, and if it's moving faster than 4
pixels a step and isn't one he's just thrown, either hurts him or, with the Mitt, catches it
<Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:1538-1565" />:

```gml objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:1543-1548
        if (global.hasMitt and !holdItem)
        {
            holdItem = oRock;
            holdItem.held = true;
            pickupItemType = holdItem.type;
        }
```

The Rock it found is `obj`, but the catch puts `oRock` in his hands: the object, not the instance. In
GameMaker, setting a variable on an object sets it on every instance of it <Cite
quirk="2" row="obj.v op= x reads v from the first instance of obj and writes the result to all of them" />,
so `holdItem.held = true` makes every Rock that's awake held, and the shared item code puts each held item in
the player's hands <Cite gml="objects/oItem/step.gml:37-67" />. The throw then sets the speed of
`holdItem`, every Rock again, so they all leave together <Cite gml="scripts/scrUseItem.gml:595-676" /> <Cite quirk="1" />.

## Reproducing it

Wear the Mitt, with empty hands, and let a fast Rock hit the player once its few safe steps after a throw are
over: his own Rock coming back off a wall, or one an explosion throws. Any other Rock near the screen comes to
his hands with it. The replay of the clip's staged scene pins it: a Rock thrown at a pillar comes back at 7
pixels a step and is caught, and all three Rocks in the room are held until he throws them, all at the same
speed <Cite replay="fidelity/replays/item-mitt-catch-every-rock.txt" />.

## In the port

The spelunky.ai port reproduces it exactly: it's in docs/quirks.md's list of the 1.1 source's bugs
<Cite quirk="1" />, and the oracle's fingerprint of the replay pins every Rock's flight. No option turns it
off.
