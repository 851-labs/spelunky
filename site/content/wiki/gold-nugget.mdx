It's the bigger of the two loose pieces of gold, five times a [[gold-chunk|Gold Chunk]]'s worth and as much as
a [[gold-bar|Gold Bar]]. Many come out of rock: a rich gold vein, broken open by a [[bomb|Bomb]], a
[[mattock|Mattock]] or a boulder, spills three chunks and one nugget. The player collects one by touching it.

## Value

A nugget's base value is <Stat id="gold-nugget.value" />, and it's worth a quarter of that more for each area
past the Mines, the code counting [[olmecs-lair|Olmec's Lair]] as an area of its own

## Locations

- **Rich gold veins.** Each block of rock in [[mines|the Mines]], [[jungle|the Jungle]], [[ice-caves|the Ice
  Caves]] and [[temple|the Temple]] (and [[olmecs-lair|Olmec's Lair]]) is a gold vein 19 times in 100 and a
  rich one, with more gold showing, 10 times in
  100 <Cite gml="objects/oBrick/create.gml:7-9" /> <Cite gml="objects/oLush/create.gml:5-8" />
  <Cite gml="objects/oDark/create.gml:3-8" /> <Cite gml="objects/oTemple/create.gml:5-9" />. Destroyed, a rich
  vein lets out three [[gold-chunk|Gold Chunks]] and a nugget; a plain vein only the chunks
  <Cite gml="objects/oBrick/destroy.gml:9-29" />.
- **[[city-of-gold|The City of Gold]]**: its blocks are gold, and each one destroyed lets out three chunks and
  a nugget <Cite gml="objects/oTemple/destroy.gml:21-32" /> <Cite gml="objects/oBlock/destroy.gml:13-24" />.
- **[[jar|Jars]]**: a broken Jar holds one 1 in 6 times, once its 1 in 3 roll for a Gold Chunk fails
  <Cite gml="objects/oJar/destroy.gml:17-18" />.
- **Graves.** On a Jungle graveyard level ([[restless-dead|the Restless Dead]]), a grave that isn't the ash
  grave, with no treasure under it already, has a 1 in 2 chance of a nugget buried in the block beneath it
  <Cite gml="scripts/scrEntityGen.gml:187-198" />.
- **A Shopkeeper's death**: one who dies leaves one to four nuggets, except in the Stars room, a bonus room
  off the high-score room <Cite gml="objects/oShopkeeper/step.gml:487-498" />.
- **A [[monkey|Monkey]]** clinging to the player sometimes takes a nugget's worth of his money and throws it
  out as a nugget, which can't be collected for its first 20 steps <Cite gml="objects/oMonkey/step.gml:199" />
  <Cite gml="objects/oMonkey/step.gml:230-241" /> <Cite gml="objects/oGoldNugget/alarm_0.gml:1" />.

The [[level-editor|level editor]] has no tile for one, and its blocks hold no gold: it sets each block's look
back to plain rock after the block has rolled for its vein <Cite gml="scripts/scrCreateTileObj.gml:36-40" />.

## Collecting

<Clip id="gold-nugget/shopkeeper" />

The player collects a nugget by touching it while he isn't stunned or dead; the one he takes is the treasure
nearest him <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:1990-2016" />. Its value shows as "+500" under his money and,
after a short pause, is counted into it a hundred dollars a step
<Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:1953-1971" /> <Cite gml="scripts/scrDrawHUD.gml:183" />; whatever is left
is counted at once when he leaves the level <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/other_animation_end.gml:46-50" />.
Gold plays the coin sound, where a gem plays its own <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:2000-2014" />, and
the screen between levels shows each nugget collected on the level
<Cite gml="objects/oTransition/alarm_0.gml:47-52" />.

In the clip, a [[bomb|Bomb]] the player set down kills a Shopkeeper, who leaves four nuggets; the player walks
over two of them and his money counts up by $1,000 <Cite clip="gold-nugget/shopkeeper" />.

A loose nugget falls and comes to rest like other treasure, moving only while it's on the screen, and lava it
sinks into destroys it <Cite gml="objects/oTreasure/step.gml:1-79" />.
