A ware that leaves its shop by itself, thrown or knocked out, is stolen at once: the
[[shopkeeper|Shopkeeper]] is angered and the ware is free. The [[ankh|Ankh]] skips that check, so thrown out of
its shop it lies outside at its price, harmless, until the player touches it.

## What happens

In the [[black-market|Black Market]], the player throws the Ankh up through the hole in its shop's ceiling.
It lies outside with its price, and the Shopkeeper stays calm until the player climbs out and picks it up:
then it's his, and the Shopkeeper opens fire <Cite clip="quirks/ankh-thrown-out-of-shop" />.

<Clip id="quirks/ankh-thrown-out-of-shop" />

## Why

The shops' stock is made by one script, which marks each ware `forSale`
<Cite gml="scripts/scrShopItemsGen.gml:179-181" />. The Black Market makes the Ankh Shop's Ankh itself, with
the price its create event gives it, and never marks it <Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGenMarket.gml:361-364" />:

```gml scripts/scrRoomGenMarket.gml:361-364
else if (tile == "A")
{
    obj = instance_create(xpos+8, ypos+8, oAnkh);
}
```

The shared item code steals a ware outside its shop only if it's for sale
<Cite gml="objects/oItem/step.gml:18-34" />:

```gml objects/oItem/step.gml:20-25
    if (cost > 0 and forSale and not isInShop(x, y))
    {
        global.itemsStolen += 1;
        scrShopkeeperAnger(0);
        cost = 0;
    }
```

So the Ankh outside keeps its price, and nobody notices. The player's own step has the other check: a held
ware with a price, once he's outside the shop, is put on and angers the Shopkeeper. Picking the Ankh up
outside runs it at once <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:541-557" /> <Cite quirk="1" />.

## Reproducing it

In the Black Market, pick up the Ankh and throw it up through the hole in its shop's ceiling, the shop's only
way out, then climb out after it. The replay pins it: the Ankh lies outside at $50,000 with the Shopkeeper
calm, and picked up outside it's put on and he shouts "COME BACK HERE, THIEF!"
<Cite replay="fidelity/replays/item-seed-9-ankh-thrown-out.txt" />. Carried out instead, it's put on as the
player leaves the shop <Cite replay="fidelity/replays/item-seed-9-ankh.txt" />.

## In the port

The spelunky.ai port reproduces it exactly: it's in docs/quirks.md's list of the 1.1 source's bugs
<Cite quirk="1" />, and the oracle's fingerprint of the replay pins the Ankh's price and the Shopkeeper. No
option turns it off.
