Ladders stand in the rooms of [[mines|the Mines]] and [[temple|the Temple]], where they link a room's floors,
and, in [[jungle|the Jungle]], only in [[black-market|the Black Market]]. The top of one is a ladder top, a rung the player can also stand
on. The [[jungle|Jungle]]'s rooms have [[vine|Vines]] where the others have ladders, and a thrown
[[rope|Rope]] is, to the code, one more kind of ladder.

## How it works

<Clip id="ladder/climb" />

The clip shows the whole of it: the player walks to a ladder, presses up and climbs it to its top, then jumps
off toward the ledge beside it, catches the ledge's edge and pulls himself up.

### Taking hold

The player takes hold of a ladder by pressing up while he's in it, or while he's in the air with a ladder just
above him. He must be within
<Stat id="ladder.grab-range" /> of the ladder's middle, sideways, and he's put on its middle when he takes
hold, his speed stopped <Cite gml="scripts/characterStepEvent.gml:606-633" />. Pressing down on a platform with
a ladder under it, a ladder top among them, takes hold of it too, and he climbs down
<Cite gml="scripts/characterStepEvent.gml:546-566" />. Ducking over a ledge with a ladder beside its edge takes
hold of the ladder rather than hanging from the edge <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/other_animation_end.gml:6-29" />.

### Climbing

On a ladder, up and down move the player for as long as the ladder goes on above or below him: each step adds
0.6 to his speed, and climbing's friction takes 0.4 of it away again <Cite gml="scripts/characterStepEvent.gml:198-212" /> <Cite gml="scripts/characterStepEvent.gml:677-682" />
<Cite gml="scripts/characterCreateEvent.gml:70" /> <Cite gml="scripts/characterCreateEvent.gml:85-86" />. In play
that's 1 pixel a step (30 a second), a block in 16 steps <Cite clip="ladder/climb" />. Left and right only
turn him <Cite gml="scripts/characterStepEvent.gml:196-197" />. A [[cape|Cape]] closes while he climbs
<Cite gml="scripts/characterStepEvent.gml:187-190" />, and a fall doesn't count while he's climbing, so
climbing down never ends in a long drop's stun <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:254-267" />.

### Getting off

- **At the foot.** Pressing down where the ladder ends lets go, and the player falls; one who reaches the ground
  pressing down stands on it <Cite gml="scripts/characterStepEvent.gml:206-216" />.
- **Jumping.** The jump key throws the player off with an upward push, sideways at
  <Stat id="ladder.jump-off-speed" /> if left or right is held, or straight up without
  <Cite gml="scripts/characterStepEvent.gml:218-231" /> <Cite gml="scripts/characterCreateEvent.gml:72-73" />. For
  5 steps after, he can't take hold of a ladder again <Cite gml="scripts/characterStepEvent.gml:230-236" />.

## Interactions

- **[[rope|Ropes]]** are a kind of ladder to the code, as are [[vine|Vines]]: the checks for a ladder find them
  too, so the player takes hold of them and climbs them in the same way
  <Cite gml="scripts/characterStepEvent.gml:606-633" />.
- **[[monkey|Monkeys]]** leaping through the air catch hold of a ladder as they do a vine
  <Cite gml="objects/oMonkey/step.gml:102-111" />.
- **[[bomb|Bombs]]** don't destroy ladders: a blast destroys the solid blocks it touches and the Spikes it leaves with
  nothing under them, and a ladder is neither <Cite gml="objects/oExplosion/collision_oSolid.gml:1-31" />, so it
  stays standing where it was.

## Tips

- Climbing down a long ladder is safe however far it goes; dropping off it partway isn't, as the fall counts from
  where he lets go.
- Jumping off with a direction held carries the player sideways at <Stat id="ladder.jump-off-speed" />, faster
  than he walks, onto a ledge beside the ladder.
