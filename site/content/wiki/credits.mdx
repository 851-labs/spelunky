The credits are six cards of text over a scene: a camel walks in from the right with the player on its back, slows
almost to a stop in the middle while the desert scrolls past and the credits music plays, and rides off to the left
at the end. There are two versions of the scene. After a win the desert is in daylight and a caravan follows the
camel, and the credits lead to the [[high-scores-room|high scores room]]. From the [[title-screen|title screen]]'s rope it's night, the camel rides
alone, and the game quits after the credits.

## How it works

### Reaching them

- **After a win.** The final score's last press goes to the credits
  ([[endings]]) <Cite gml="objects/oEnd3/step.gml:17-22" />.
- **From the title screen.** Climbing the title screen's rope out of the top of the screen fades the title out to
  the credits <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:49-60" /> <Cite gml="objects/oTitle/step.gml:38-45" />.

### The scene

<Clip id="credits/caravan" />

The screen fades in, and 20 steps later the camel comes in from the right edge, a pixel a
step <Cite gml="objects/oCredits1/step.gml:34-42" /> <Cite gml="objects/oCredits1/alarm_0.gml:1" />. Its rider is
the player as he played: the [[spelunker|Spelunker]], the [[damsel|Damsel]] or the [[tunnel-man|Tunnel
Man]] <Cite gml="objects/oCamel/create.gml:1-4" />. In the middle of the screen it slows to a hundredth of a pixel
a step and the credits music starts <Cite gml="objects/oCamel/step.gml:1-13" />; the desert starts to scroll right,
with a shrub one time in 8 and otherwise a palm tree one time in 12, every 16 steps, and 20 steps later the first
card appears <Cite gml="objects/oCredits1/step.gml:19-32" /> <Cite gml="objects/oCredits1/alarm_1.gml:1-16" />.

After a win, a caravan follows 64 pixels behind the camel: two cavemen carrying the golden head the ending brought
out of the volcano ([[endings#the-volcano]]), with a Damsel riding on top if the player saved any, or the Spelunker
if the player is the Damsel <Cite gml="objects/oCredits2/alarm_0.gml:1-2" /> <Cite
gml="objects/oCaravan/create.gml:1-5" /> <Cite gml="objects/oCaravan/step.gml:1-16" />. In the clip, a win with no
Damsels saved, the caravan carries the head alone <Cite clip="credits/caravan" />.

From the title screen the scene is the intro's: the desert at night, its palm trees dark, with the moon and the
mountains of the intro drifting slowly while the camel crosses <Cite gml="objects/oCredits1/create.gml:5" /> <Cite
gml="objects/oDesertScroll/create.gml:5" /> <Cite gml="objects/oPalmTreeScroll/create.gml:2-3" /> <Cite
gml="objects/oMoon/step.gml:1-4" /> <Cite clip="credits/title-rope" />.

### The end

<Clip id="credits/ride-off" />

40 steps after the last card, the desert stops and the camel and the caravan ride off to the left at 2 pixels a
step <Cite gml="objects/oCredits2/alarm_8.gml:1-8" /> <Cite gml="objects/oCamel/step.gml:14-17" />. Once the last
of them is 64 pixels past the left edge, the screen fades out <Cite gml="objects/oCamel/step.gml:19" /> <Cite
gml="objects/oCaravan/step.gml:18-21" />. From the title screen the game then
quits <Cite gml="objects/oCredits1/step.gml:43-50" />. After a win the high scores are updated with the win
([[scoring#the-high-scores]]) and the game goes to the high scores
room <Cite gml="objects/oCredits2/step.gml:44-55" />.

Enter, Escape or the attack key fades the credits out early, once the camel has
appeared <Cite gml="objects/oCredits2/step.gml:1-18" />. In the clip, the attack key fades them out as the caravan
rides off <Cite clip="credits/ride-off" />.

## Quirks

- **A key during the fade-in doesn't skip it** ([[unskippable-credits-fade-in]]). The credits' keys only work once the camel has appeared, 20 steps
  after the fade-in has ended, so the line that would end the fade-in at once never
  runs <Cite gml="objects/oCredits1/step.gml:1-16" /> <Cite gml="objects/oCredits1/step.gml:34-42" /> <Cite
  gml="objects/oCredits2/step.gml:1-18" /> <Cite gml="objects/oCredits2/step.gml:35-43" /> <Cite quirk="1" row="The credits' fade-in can't be skipped." />. After
  the win, attack, Enter and Escape during the fade-in, and attack before the camel, leave it as it
  was <Cite replay="fidelity/replays/ending-seed-1.txt" />.
