[[kali|Kali]] punishes each altar destroyed in a game, and the second punishment is this: a ball chained to the
player's ankle, which stays with him on every level until the game ends. He can drag it across the ground,
but not climb away from it, so tall climbs, [[rope|ropes]] and deep drops are cut short. He can pick it up and carry
it, though, as he would a [[crate|Crate]].

## Usage

<Clip id="ball-and-chain/drag" />

Destroying one of [[kalis-altar|Kali's altars]] angers her: the first time in a game, spiders come down; the second time,
the ball and four links of chain appear at the player; the third time and after, the level goes dark and a
[[ghost|Ghost]] comes, or, if the level is dark with a Ghost already, more spiders
do <Cite gml="objects/oSacAltarLeft/destroy.gml:11-60" /> <Cite quirk="1" row="Kali's punishment for a defiled altar grows with each one." />. From the second on, every new
level chains a new ball to him as it starts, and the walking figure between levels drags one
too <Cite gml="objects/oLevel/create.gml:31-41" /> <Cite gml="objects/oTransition/create.gml:26-37" />,
until a new game starts <Cite gml="scripts/scrClearGlobals.gml:67" />. In the clip, a bomb destroys a second
altar; the ball appears, and the player drags it down to the exit; between levels, he still has it, and on
the next level, a new one <Cite clip="ball-and-chain/drag" />.

The chain holds him within <Stat id="ball-and-chain.reach" /> of the ball. Farther than that, his own
movement stops him: walking away from it, he can't; falling with the ball above him, he stops and swings
toward under it; rising with it below him, he stops <Cite gml="scripts/characterStepEvent.gml:899-925" />.
The ball, farther than that from him, follows: it's dragged along at his speed when he walks away from it
and it's level with him or lower, and otherwise it's set rolling toward
him <Cite gml="objects/oBall/step.gml:3-40" />. The four links are drawn evenly between the ball
and the player <Cite gml="objects/oChain/step.gml:1-9" />.

The ball is a heavy item: crouching over it and pressing the attack key picks it up, and while he holds it,
it's where he is and can't hold him back. Setting it down, throwing it or taking an exit leaves it on the
ground again <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:1203-1214" /> <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:815-820" /> <Cite quirk="1" row="The ball keeps the player within 24 px of it, and a pit traps him." />.

## Interactions

- **[[teleporter|Teleporter]].** A teleport takes the ball and chain with him <Cite gml="scripts/scrUseItem.gml:461-470" />.
- **The [[ankh|Ankh]].** Coming back to life with it puts the ball and chain where he
  is <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:1851-1882" />.
- **Pits.** A ball at the bottom of a pit keeps him in it, as he can't climb more than
  <Stat id="ball-and-chain.reach" /> above it, unless he picks it up and carries it out <Cite quirk="1" row="The ball keeps the player within 24 px of it, and a pit traps him." />.

## Tips

- Carrying the ball frees the player to climb, jump and use ropes as usual, for as long as he holds it.
- A third destroyed altar doesn't take the ball away; it adds Kali's next punishment.

## Trivia

- The walking figure between levels has a ball and chain of its own, which follow it
  instead <Cite gml="objects/oBall2/step.gml:1-9" /> <Cite gml="objects/oChain2/step.gml:1-10" />.
- The ball's own gravity, 1, lasts one step: the code all items share sets it back to the usual 0.6 on the
  next <Cite gml="objects/oBall/create.gml:7" /> <Cite gml="objects/oItem/step.gml:160-170" />.
