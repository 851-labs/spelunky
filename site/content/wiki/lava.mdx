Lava fills pools in [[temple|the Temple]] and the depths of [[olmecs-lair|Olmec's Lair]]. It's a liquid, the
kind of water that burns: the player dies the moment his feet touch it, and so does a monster. Some of its
surface spurts flames, and blobs of magma that become [[magma-man|Magma Men]]. On a [[dark-level|dark level]] it lights the
dark around it.

## How it works

<Clip id="lava/burn" />

Lava is a kind of water to the code: it fills a space and flows into any opening when the blocks holding it
are destroyed, as water does <Cite replay="fidelity/replays/terrain-bomb-lava.txt" />. The difference is what
it does to whatever enters it. The clip shows the player walking off a floor into a pool: he dies as his feet
touch it.

**Spurts.** When a level is made, each block of lava with nothing above it has a 1 in
<Stat id="lava.spurt-chance" bare /> chance of spurting <Cite gml="scripts/scrSetupWalls.gml:398-414" />. A
spurting block waits <Stat id="lava.spurt-time" /> (picked when it's made), then throws a flame up, or with a 1
in <Stat id="lava.magma-chance" bare /> chance a blob of magma, which turns into a [[magma-man|Magma Man]] where
it lands; it only counts down while the player is within 240 pixels <Cite gml="objects/oLava/step.gml:1-16" />
<Cite gml="objects/oLava/create.gml:3-5" />.

**Flowing.** Lava that flows away from its place is destroyed there, leaving three drips and, 1 in 6, a falling
flame <Cite gml="objects/oLava/destroy.gml:1-9" />. The second clip shows a bomb opening a Temple pool's wall:
the lava flows out and away beside the player, who isn't hurt.

## Damage

| Victim | What happens | |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The player | Loses 99 hearts, every heart he can have, as soon as a point just below his middle is in lava; he burns and sinks | <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:195-213" /> |
| Most monsters | Killed when their feet are in lava, then sink; one whose head the lava covers is removed | <Cite gml="objects/oEnemy/step.gml:63-74" /> |
| A [[damsel\|Damsel]] | Killed | <Cite gml="objects/oDamsel/step.gml:77-85" /> |
| [[olmec\|Olmec]] | Sinks and is destroyed: lava is the only thing that kills him, though not by his lair's left wall ([[olmec-lava-left-wall]]) | <Cite gml="objects/oOlmec/step.gml:35-43" /> |
| Items | Stop and sink 0.05 pixels a step, and are destroyed once lava covers them; a bomb explodes. The [[sceptre\|Sceptre]] sinks without being destroyed | <Cite gml="objects/oItem/step.gml:160-185" /> |
| Treasure | Sinks and is destroyed the same way | <Cite gml="objects/oTreasure/step.gml:69-79" /> |
| A rope | Burns from the segment in the lava up | <Cite gml="objects/oRope/step.gml:1-4" /> |

A death in lava is counted in the end-of-game statistics' lava row <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:200" />.
A monster lava kills doesn't count as the player's kill <Cite gml="objects/oEnemy/step.gml:68" />. The monsters'
check is in the code most monsters share, so those whose own step event replaces it, such as the
[[bat|Bat]] and the [[magma-man|Magma Man]], aren't hurt by it. A smash trap that slides into lava sinks and is
destroyed too ([[smash-trap#interactions|Smash Trap]]).

## Locations

- **[[temple|The Temple]]**, in its room templates' pools: each water tile of a Temple room is lava, and some
  pool edges are lava or a block, 1 in 2 each <Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGen4.gml:328-332" />
  <Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGen4.gml:497-500" />.
- **[[olmecs-lair|Olmec's Lair]]**, whose room is built over a sea of lava <Cite room="rOlmec" />.
- **The [[endings|ending]] and the Sun challenge room**, a bonus room <Cite room="rEnd" /> <Cite room="rSun" />.
- **Custom levels**, as the [[level-editor|level editor]]'s tile `l` <Cite gml="scripts/scrCreateTileObj.gml:69-72" />.

Those rooms also have solid blocks drawn as lava, which are only walls <Cite gml="objects/oLavaSolid/create.gml:1-3" />.

## Avoiding

- **Lava kills on touch**, whatever the player's hearts, so there's no crossing it: only over it, on a rope,
  a ladder or a jump.
- **Mind the spurts.** A blob of magma thrown up by a spurt hurts the player, and becomes a Magma Man where it
  lands ([[magma-man#damage|Magma Man]]).
- **Letting it out.** A bomb that breaks a pool's wall lets it flow away, as in the second clip, which can
  open the way across, or flood the floor below.

## Interactions

- **Ropes** dropped into lava burn up from the bottom, segment by segment
  <Cite replay="fidelity/replays/terrain-rope-burn-lava.txt" />.
- **Light.** Lava counts as a light source on a dark level <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:83-88" />.
- **Generation** keeps traps off lava: a Spear Trap isn't built where it would block a passage of lava, nor
  one on a block with lava on it <Cite gml="scripts/scrEntityGen.gml:410-427" />.
