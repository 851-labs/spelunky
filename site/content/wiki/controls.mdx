Each action has a key, which the [[settings|settings program]] can change, and a gamepad button; the game
reads the gamepad first and the keyboard after it, so both work at once
<Cite gml="scripts/checkJump.gml:1-4" />. Besides the twelve actions, Escape pauses, Enter and Escape move
past the screens between scenes, and a few function keys work on the title screen and the pause screen.

## How it works

### The keys

The game starts with these keys and buttons, then reads the player's own from `keys.cfg` and
`gamepad.cfg`, if the settings program has written them <Cite gml="scripts/scrInit.gml:38-59" />
<Cite gml="scripts/scrInit.gml:90-140" />.

| Action | Key | Button | What it does | |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Left, right | ← → | Stick or hat | Walk, and steer in the air | <Cite gml="scripts/characterStepEvent.gml:12-26" /> |
| Up | ↑ | Stick or hat | Look up; climb a ladder or rope; go through a door; with attack, open a [[chest\|Chest]] or [[crate\|Crate]] | <Cite gml="scripts/characterStepEvent.gml:609-610" /> <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:759-766" /> <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:561" /> |
| Down | ↓ | Stick or hat | Look down; duck and crawl; with attack, pick an item up; with jump, let go of a ledge | <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:1203-1209" /> <Cite gml="scripts/characterStepEvent.gml:494-505" /> |
| Jump | Z | 2 | Jump; swim up; free the player from a web | <Cite gml="scripts/characterStepEvent.gml:294-338" /> |
| Attack | X | 1 | Whip, or use or [[throwing\|throw]] what the player holds; held while walking, run | <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:1175-1201" /> |
| Item | C | 3 | Take out a [[bomb\|Bomb]], then a [[rope\|Rope]], then put back what the player held | <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:954-1044" /> |
| Run | Shift | 5 | Run | <Cite gml="scripts/characterStepEvent.gml:117-121" /> |
| Bomb | A | 7 | Throw a lit bomb | <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:1137-1174" /> |
| Rope | S | 8 | Throw a rope up, or with down, over the ledge in front | <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:1045-1062" /> |
| Flare | F | 4 | Nothing | <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:413" /> |
| Pay | P | 6 | In a shop, buy the item held, a bet or a kiss | <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:1359-1450" /> |
| Start | Escape | 10 | Pause; move past a screen | <Cite gml="scripts/checkStartPressed.gml:1-4" /> |

The keys do nothing while the player is stunned or dead <Cite gml="scripts/characterStepEvent.gml:71-88" />,
and the bomb and rope keys nothing once he has no hearts left
<Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:406-410" />. Up and down move the view once they've been held for 31
steps with the player standing or hanging still, 4 pixels a step, so he can look over a drop
<Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:385-397" />.

### Running

The player walks at up to <Stat id="spelunker.walk-speed" /> <Cite gml="scripts/characterStepEvent.gml:797-800" />. He runs,
at up to <Stat id="spelunker.run-speed" bare />, once a run has been held for 10 steps on the ground: the run key counts as held at once, and
the attack key, held while he isn't whipping, counts up a step at a time, so holding attack while walking
also starts a run <Cite gml="scripts/characterStepEvent.gml:117-129" />
<Cite gml="scripts/characterStepEvent.gml:685-698" />. With the settings program's run on down (on by
default), ducking while moving faster than 2 pixels per step keeps him running rather than slowing to a crawl
<Cite gml="scripts/characterStepEvent.gml:700-727" /> <Cite gml="scripts/scrInit.gml:32" />.

### Pausing

<Clip id="controls/pause" />

Escape (or the gamepad's start) pauses when the room allows it and the player is alive: the screen darkens,
the music goes quiet and everything else stops, so the clock stops too
<Cite gml="objects/oScreen/step_begin.gml:34-61" />. The clip shows it on the first level: the pause screen
lists the level, its depth in feet, the money, the kills, the time on the level and in all, and the saves,
with the keys it takes <Cite gml="objects/oScreen/step_begin.gml:85-117" /> <Cite clip="controls/pause" />.

- **Escape** returns to the game <Cite gml="objects/oScreen/step_begin.gml:62-72" />.
- **F1** kills the player, which ends the game as any death does, unless an [[ankh|Ankh]] revives him
  <Cite gml="objects/oScreen/keypress_F1.gml:1-11" />. It's only shown, and only works, in a level.
- **F10** quits Spelunky <Cite gml="objects/oScreen/keypress_F10.gml:1-2" />.

With the gamepad on, the screen names its buttons instead: start returns, bomb dies and rope quits
<Cite gml="objects/oScreen/step_begin.gml:116-122" />. The title screen, once the player stands in it, and the
high scores room can be paused too <Cite gml="objects/oTitle/create.gml:72-89" />
<Cite gml="objects/oHighscores/create.gml:1" />.

### Other screens

- **The intro and the title screen.** Enter, Escape, attack or start skip the intro and the title
  screen's opening <Cite gml="objects/oIntro/step.gml:1-4" /> <Cite gml="objects/oTitle/step.gml:1-23" />. On the title screen, F2 opens the
  [[level-editor|level editor]] and F3 the custom levels <Cite gml="objects/oTitle/keypress_F2.gml:1-3" />
  <Cite gml="objects/oTitle/keypress_F3.gml:1-8" />. Climbing its rope to the top shows the credits and
  quits <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:49-60" /> <Cite gml="objects/oCredits1/step.gml:48" />.
- **Music.** M turns the music off and on <Cite gml="objects/oGame/keypress_M.gml:1-22" />.
- **Between levels.** Attack or start goes on to the next level once the tally has finished; pressed
  earlier, it hurries the tally ([[scoring#between-levels]]) <Cite gml="objects/oTransition/step.gml:1-33" />.
  Up and down raise and lower a donation to the [[tunnel-man|Tunnel Man]]
  <Cite gml="objects/oTransition/step.gml:35-58" />.
- **Game over.** Attack or start first finishes counting the money, then goes to the high scores room
  <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:907-930" />.
- **The ending.** Enter, Escape, attack or start skip the volcano and the escape, straight to the final
  score ([[endings]]) <Cite gml="objects/oEnd/step.gml:1-14" /> <Cite gml="objects/oEnd2/step.gml:1-10" />.

### The gamepad

The gamepad works when the settings program has turned it on (`settings.cfg`'s fourth line)
<Cite gml="scripts/scrInit.gml:73-75" />. A direction is the hat's or the stick's, and a button may be
one of the first 20 or a trigger <Cite gml="objects/oGamepad/step.gml:1-57" />
<Cite gml="scripts/checkJoyButton.gml:1-23" />.

## Quirks

- **The pause screen's keys aren't only the ones it shows.** The bomb key kills the player like F1, and the
  rope key quits like F10, on the keyboard as well as the gamepad <Cite quirk="1" />
  <Cite replay="fidelity/replays/flow-pause-game-over-new-game.txt" />.
- **The flare key does nothing.** The game reads it with the others, but the line that would check it is
  commented out, and so is the code that would light a flare <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:413" />
  <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:1321-1358" />; the settings program never asks for it
  <Cite quirk="1" />. A replay presses it in the tutorial, and the oracle's run is the same as without the
  press <Cite replay="fidelity/replays/config-keys-tutorial.txt" />.
- **Playing as the [[damsel|Damsel]], the climb out by the title screen's rope shows the [[spelunker|Spelunker]].** She
  climbs the rope as herself, but the figure that climbs on out of sight at the top is set to her sprite and
  then, by an "else" that belongs to the [[tunnel-man|Tunnel Man]]'s check, to the Spelunker's
  <Cite gml="objects/oPDummy5/create.gml:6-8" /> <Cite quirk="1" />. The clip shows her change at the top
  of the rope <Cite clip="quirks/damsel-title-rope-spelunker" /> <Cite replay="fidelity/replays/title-damsel-rope.txt" />.
