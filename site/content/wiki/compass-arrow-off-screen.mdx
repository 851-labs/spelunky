The [[compass|Compass]] points to the exit with an arrow on the edge of the screen: along the bottom for an
exit below it, and on the left or right edge for one to the side, at the exit's height. Nothing stops that
height from being above the screen. Then the arrow is drawn there too, where no one can see it.

## What happens

In [[olmecs-lair|Olmec's Lair]], the player picks up a Compass, and its arrow points right, at the top of the
screen, toward the exit. He drops into the bottom left of the lair, the screen follows him down, and the
arrow goes up off its top, all but a sliver <Cite clip="quirks/compass-arrow-off-screen" />.

<Clip id="quirks/compass-arrow-off-screen" />

## Why

The HUD draws a down arrow for an exit more than a screen's height below the view's top
<Cite gml="scripts/scrDrawHUD.gml:48-65" />. For any other exit, one to the left or right gets a side arrow,
drawn at the exit's `y` less the view's:

```gml scripts/scrDrawHUD.gml:66-75
        else if (global.exitX < view_xview[0])
        {
            if (global.messageTimer > 0) draw_sprite(sCompassSmallLeft, -1, 0, global.exitY-view_yview[0]);
            else draw_sprite(sCompassLeft, -1, 0, global.exitY-view_yview[0]);
        }
        else if (global.exitX > view_xview[0]+320-16)
        {
            if (global.messageTimer > 0) draw_sprite(sCompassSmallRight, -1, 304, global.exitY-view_yview[0]);
            else draw_sprite(sCompassRight, -1, 304, global.exitY-view_yview[0]);
        }
```

For an exit above the view, that's a negative `y`, above the top of the screen; one straight above gets no
arrow at all. On a generated level the exit is in the bottom row of rooms, which the view can't go below. In
Olmec's Lair the HUD sets the exit itself, at (640, 544) <Cite gml="scripts/scrDrawHUD.gml:44" />, and the
lair goes deeper: at its bottom left, with the view's top at y 553 to 556, the right arrow is drawn at y -9
to -12 <Cite quirk="1" />.

## Reproducing it

Carry a Compass into Olmec's Lair and go down into its bottom left: the arrow that points right to the exit
goes off the top of the screen. With seed 1, the lair has a free Compass of its own, buried in a block by the
left wall that Olmec's slams dig through. The replay picks it up, with the view's top at y 536 to 539 and the
arrow at y 5 to 8, then drops into the bottom left, where the view follows him down to 556
<Cite replay="fidelity/replays/item-seed-1-compass-olmec-lair.txt" />.

## In the port

The spelunky.ai port reproduces it exactly: it's in docs/quirks.md's list of the 1.1 source's bugs
<Cite quirk="1" />, and the oracle's fingerprint of the replay pins the view's position, which places the
arrow. No option turns it off.
