The [[tunnel-man|Tunnel Man]] digs the [[tunnel-man-shortcuts|shortcuts]], and waits for the player's money in the
screen at the end of an area: after level 4 for the first. On a fresh install, the first player to get there
finds the screen empty. Each shortcut is owed $1 more than its price at first, and the screen only takes that $1
off instead of showing him.

## What happens

On a fresh install, the player takes the exit of level 4, the last of [[mines|the Mines]]. The screen between
levels tallies the loot, and nobody is there; X skips it, and the Jungle's first level loads
<Cite clip="quirks/tunnel-man-first-visit" />.

<Clip id="quirks/tunnel-man-first-visit" />

## Why

What's owed on the shortcuts is kept in the high scores table, and a fresh table stores the first two $1 above
their prices <Cite gml="scripts/scrResetHighscores.gml:32-33" />. Paying off the level 9 shortcut does the same to
the level 13 one <Cite gml="objects/oTunnelMan/step.gml:24" />. The screen between levels makes the Tunnel Man only
if what's owed is down to the price, and otherwise takes 1 off it:

```gml objects/oTransition/create.gml:47-51
if (global.tunnel1 > 0 and global.tunnel2 > 0 and isRoom("rTransition1x"))
{
    if (global.tunnel1 > global.tunnel1Max) global.tunnel1 -= 1;
    else instance_create(96+8, 176+8, oTunnelMan);
}
```

So the first visit to each of his three screens takes the $1 off and shows no one, and he first shows up on the
next <Cite gml="objects/oTransition/create.gml:52-60" /> <Cite quirk="1" />.

## Reproducing it

Start with a fresh high scores table (a new install) and reach the end of the Mines. It happens to every new
player, once for each shortcut. The replay of the clip starts from a fresh table: the screen after level 4 loads
on frame 4231, takes what the level 5 shortcut is owed from $100,001 to $100,000, and makes no Tunnel Man; level 5
loads on 4264 <Cite replay="fidelity/replays/tunnelman-seed-2-mines-over-max.txt" />. The screen after level 8
does the same for the level 9 shortcut <Cite replay="fidelity/replays/tunnelman-seed-9-jungle-over-max.txt" />, and
the screen after level 12 for the level 13 one, once the level 9 one is paid off
<Cite replay="fidelity/replays/tunnelman-seed-9-jungle-paid-ice-over-max.txt" />.

## In the port

The spelunky.ai port reproduces it exactly: it's in docs/quirks.md's list of the 1.1 source's bugs
<Cite quirk="1" />, and the oracle's fingerprints of the replays pin each empty screen. No option turns it off.
