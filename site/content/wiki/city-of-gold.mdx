The City of Gold takes the place of [[temple|the Temple]]'s last level, and is built of gold: its walls,
floors and stone are gold blocks that break into [[gold-chunk|Gold Chunks]] and
[[gold-nugget|Gold Nuggets]], its side rooms can be treasure vaults, and a statue of Lady Xoc stands in it,
studded with gems. The way in is a gold door on level
14, which takes the [[sceptre|Sceptre]] and opens only for a player wearing the [[hedjet|Hedjet]], so reaching
it means finding both ([[city-of-gold-route]] walks through it). Its exit leads to [[olmecs-lair|Olmec's Lair]], as
the Temple's level 15 does.

## Generation

Level 14 always has a gold door, on top of a block that can't be destroyed. Generation puts it on the first
block with open space above it that wins a roll of 1 in a number that starts each game at 160 and falls by
one with each block that loses, or, failing that, on the first such block in the level <Cite
gml="scripts/scrEntityGen.gml:387-409" /> <Cite gml="scripts/scrEntityGen.gml:482-495" />
<Cite gml="scripts/scrClearGlobals.gml:80" />. It can be anywhere, even sealed in rock that only a bomb opens
<Cite quirk="1" /> <Cite replay="fidelity/replays/city-of-gold-seed-9-sealed-door.txt" />. Carried into the
door, the [[sceptre|Sceptre]], which only level 13's [[tomb-lord|Tomb Lord]] leaves, opens it if the player
wears the [[hedjet|Hedjet]], and is used up; without the Hedjet the door says "THE SCEPTRE FITS... BUT NOTHING
IS HAPPENING!" and stays shut <Cite gml="objects/oTombLord/step.gml:23" />
<Cite gml="objects/oGoldDoor/collision_oSceptre.gml:1-23" />. Walking through the open door makes the next
level, 15, the City of Gold <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:880" />. The first clip shows it: the player, wearing the Hedjet,
carries the Sceptre to the door, which opens, and goes through ([[hedjet#the-gold-door|the Hedjet's gold
door]]) <Cite clip="hedjet/gold-door" />.

<Clip id="hedjet/gold-door" />

The level starts with the message "IT'S THE LEGENDARY CITY OF GOLD!" <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/alarm_0.gml:52-58" />.
It's laid out as [[temple#generation|the Temple's]] levels are, four rooms by four on a path from the top row
to the bottom, from the Temple's room script, with these differences
<Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGen4.gml:68-142" />:

| Rooms | In the City of Gold | |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Side rooms (3 in 4 of the rooms off the path) | Never Kali's altar or the [[golden-idol\|Golden Idol]]'s room. One of 14 layouts, each as likely: the Temple's eleven ordinary ones, or one of three treasure vaults | <Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGen4.gml:68-74" /> <Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGen4.gml:112-115" /> |
| The path's rooms | One of 12 layouts rather than 10: the two more each hold a [[tomb-lord\|Tomb Lord]] | <Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGen4.gml:120-141" /> <Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGen4.gml:426-429" /> |
| One room of the third row, at random | The Lady Xoc statue (below), in place of whatever the path put there | <Cite gml="scripts/scrLevelGen.gml:136" /> <Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGen4.gml:189-192" /> |

The statue can take the room the path drops through to the exit's row, and its bottom row is solid, so the
way down can then only be bombed open <Cite quirk="1" /> <Cite replay="fidelity/replays/city-of-gold-seed-9-to-olmec.txt" />.

<Clip id="city-of-gold/arrival" />

Every block of the Temple's stone is gold here, the level's walls too, and so are its stone blocks and push
blocks <Cite gml="objects/oTemple/create.gml:7" /> <Cite gml="scripts/scrInitLevel.gml:124" />
<Cite gml="objects/oBlock/create.gml:4" /> <Cite gml="objects/oPushBlock/create.gml:5" />. One block in a hundred
is still the Jungle's earth, as in the Temple <Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGen4.gml:317-322" />. In the second
clip, the City loads with its message, and the player runs down to the Lady Xoc statue
<Cite clip="city-of-gold/arrival" />.

The City can also be a [[sacrifice-pit|sacrifice pit]] <Cite gml="scripts/scrLevelGen.gml:71-87" />, and as
the statue's room is chosen after the pit's, it can take the pit's room in the third row
<Cite gml="scripts/scrLevelGen.gml:136" />. The City's
message then shows twice, 210 steps apart, before the pit's "I CAN HEAR PRAYERS TO KALI!": the timer the City's
message sets for the pit's runs the same chain of messages again, which shows the City's first and sets
another timer for the pit's <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/alarm_0.gml:52-58" />
<Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/alarm_1.gml:36-42" /> <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/alarm_4.gml:7-12" />. The roll
for a [[dark-level|dark level]] is made for the City as for the Temple's other levels
<Cite gml="scripts/scrInitLevel.gml:167-174" />, and a dark City's message is the dark level's, with the City's
210 steps later <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/alarm_0.gml:5-13" /> <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/alarm_1.gml:36-42" />.

Taking the City's exit leads to [[olmecs-lair|Olmec's Lair]], level 16, which is no longer gold: leaving the
City ends it <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/other_animation_end.gml:52-56" />
<Cite gml="objects/oTransition/step.gml:26" />. In the clip below, the player drops through a hole bombed
beside the statue, picks up an emerald, takes the exit, and the lair loads <Cite clip="city-of-gold/to-olmec" />.

## Inhabitants

Level generation makes [[temple#inhabitants|the Temple's]] monsters here, on the same terms
<Cite gml="scripts/scrEntityGen.gml:380-480" />: [[caveman|Cavemen]] and [[hawkman|Hawkmen]] on the blocks,
[[scarab|Scarabs]] if the level is dark, and a [[tomb-lord|Tomb Lord]], tried for one time in four, as on
any Temple level after 13 <Cite gml="scripts/scrEntityGen.gml:382-385" />. The two path layouts only the City
uses hold a Tomb Lord each, whatever that roll <Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGen4.gml:139-140" />
<Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGen4.gml:426-429" />. None of them leaves the [[sceptre|Sceptre]], which only
level 13's does <Cite gml="objects/oTombLord/step.gml:23" />. [[magma-man|Magma Men]] rise from its lava.

The [[ghost|Ghost]] never comes to the City of Gold: it only comes to a player without the
[[hedjet|Hedjet]], which the gold door requires <Cite gml="objects/oGame/step.gml:23-47" />.

## Hazards

The Temple's: [[arrow-trap|Arrow Traps]], [[spear-trap|Spear Traps]], [[smash-trap|Smash Traps]] and
[[lava|Lava]], made as on the Temple's other levels <Cite gml="scripts/scrEntityGen.gml:410-442" />
<Cite gml="scripts/scrEntityGen.gml:467-471" /> <Cite gml="scripts/scrEntityGen.gml:497-524" />. The City
has no idol room, so none of its [[ceiling-trap|Ceiling Traps]].

## Items and shops

### Gold

Breaking a gold block of the Temple's stone, or a gold push block, with a bomb or a [[mattock|Mattock]],
gives three [[gold-chunk|Gold Chunks]] and a [[gold-nugget|Gold Nugget]]
<Cite gml="objects/oTemple/destroy.gml:21-32" /> <Cite gml="objects/oPushBlock/destroy.gml:12-23" />. The
gold stone blocks among them, one in ten, would too, but give nothing: level generation marks every stone
block on a Temple level, the City's included, to break without a trace
<Cite gml="scripts/scrEntityGen.gml:497-499" /> <Cite gml="objects/oBlock/destroy.gml:1-25" />. The gold
blocks hide no gems or items, as the Temple's stone can: their roll for a gem is skipped in the City
<Cite gml="objects/oTemple/create.gml:5-16" />. Only the odd block of earth can hide one
<Cite gml="objects/oLush/create.gml:5-15" />, and, on a City that's a sacrifice pit, the six blocks of the
pit's floor, each of which holds a big ruby <Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGen4.gml:201-204" />
<Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGen4.gml:333-337" /> <Cite gml="objects/oTemple/destroy.gml:34-37" />.

### Treasure vaults

Two of the vault layouts are rows of shelves in the rock, holding treasure and a box; the third is six boxes
in the rock <Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGen4.gml:112-115" />. Each treasure tile rolls, each roll made once the
ones before it fail <Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGen4.gml:359-370" />:

| Tile | What, and the chance | |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Treasure | A big ruby 1 in 120, a big sapphire 1 in 80, a big emerald 1 in 60, or else [[gold-bars\|Gold Bars]] | <Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGen4.gml:364-370" /> |
| A box | A [[chest\|Chest]] or a [[crate\|Crate]], 1 in 2 each | <Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGen4.gml:359-363" /> |

### The Lady Xoc statue

The statue is 30 blocks, five wide and six tall, and six of them hold a gem: a diamond at its brow, two
sapphires, two emeralds and a ruby, each dropping two of its gem when the block breaks, as well as the gold
every block gives <Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGen4.gml:378-394" /> <Cite gml="objects/oXocBlock/destroy.gml:1-29" />.
In the clip, a bomb breaks the statue's top, and the brow's block drops two diamonds
<Cite clip="city-of-gold/xoc-statue" /> <Cite replay="fidelity/replays/city-of-gold-seed-9-xoc-gems.txt" />.

<Clip id="city-of-gold/xoc-statue" />

### Shops

Shops come as in the Temple, 2 in 15 on level 15, and none once the [[black-market|Black Market]] has been
made <Cite gml="scripts/scrLevelGen.gml:239-297" /> <Cite quirk="1" />. Treasure is scattered over the blocks
as in [[mines#treasure|the Mines]] <Cite gml="scripts/scrEntityGen.gml:475-478" />.
