No two games are the same: each level is made when the player enters it, from rolls of the game's random
number generator. The path comes first, so that the [[door|exit]] can be reached from the entrance; then each of its
sixteen rooms is picked from its area's layouts; then a pass over every block adds the monsters, traps and
treasure. A level may also be a special one, a [[dark-level|dark level]] or a [[snake-pit|snake pit]], with
odds the game sets once for all, on the levels of the areas that can have it. The areas' own rules are on
their pages: [[mines#generation|the Mines]], [[jungle#generation|the Jungle]],
[[ice-caves#generation|the Ice Caves]], [[temple#generation|the Temple]] and
[[olmecs-lair#generation|Olmec's Lair]].

## How it works

### When a level is made

A level is made when its room starts with a game in progress: the object that runs the level calls the
generator from its create event <Cite gml="objects/oGame/create.gml:27" />. The screen between levels picks
the room: the Ice Caves have a room of their own, a [[flooded-level|flooded level]] another, Olmec's Lair a
third, and every other level shares one <Cite gml="objects/oTransition/step.gml:17-27" />. The area comes from
the level's number: levels 1–4 are the Mines, 5–8 the Jungle, 9–12 the Ice Caves, 13–15 the Temple and 16
Olmec's Lair <Cite gml="scripts/scrInitLevel.gml:24-29" />. The [[tutorial|Tutorial]] and custom levels from
the [[level-editor|level editor]] are laid out by hand, and get no monsters or treasure from it
<Cite gml="scripts/scrInitLevel.gml:197-198" />.

A level is four rooms by four, each 10 tiles wide and 8 high, a tile being 16 pixels
<Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGen.gml:24-45" />. Around them runs a border of blocks that nothing destroys; the Ice
Caves' and Olmec's Lair's have no bottom, and a flooded level's is lower, to make room for its lake
<Cite gml="scripts/scrInitLevel.gml:66-128" />. Olmec's Lair is a hall laid out by hand, with only the rooms
above it made this way ([[olmecs-lair#generation]]).

### The path

The generator first lays a path through the grid <Cite gml="scripts/scrLevelGen.gml:25-33" />
<Cite gml="scripts/scrLevelGen.gml:89-133" />:

1. It starts in a random room of the top row, which will hold the entrance.
2. From each room it rolls a move. In the two middle columns, the move is left two times in five, right two
   in five and down one in five; at the left or right edge, it's away from the edge two times in three and
   down one in three.
3. A move sideways takes the path into the next room. A move toward a room the path already has goes the
   other way instead if that room is free, or down if it isn't; at an edge it does nothing, and the walk
   rolls again ([Quirks](#quirks)).
4. A move down marks the room it leaves as a drop and the one it enters as dropped into. A move down from the
   bottom row ends the path: the room it leaves holds the exit.

The path is there so that the exit can be reached, "generally no bombs required", as the code's comment
says <Cite gml="scripts/scrInitLevel.gml:57" />. The rooms it doesn't take are side rooms. Each room's place
on the path decides its layouts, which leave a way on along the path; a side room's needn't lead anywhere
<Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGen.gml:48-239" />:

| Room | What its layouts are | |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The start room | The entrance, with an open floor, or a hole in it if the path drops at once | <Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGen.gml:53-69" /> |
| The end room | The exit, open from above if the path dropped into it | <Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGen.gml:70-84" /> |
| On the path, crossed | Open to both sides | <Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGen.gml:122-151" /> |
| On the path, dropped into | Open from above | <Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGen.gml:152-172" /> |
| On the path, a drop | A gap in the floor to the room below | <Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGen.gml:219-239" /> |
| Side room | Ledges, walls, tunnels; or [[kalis-altar\|Kali's altar]], 1 in 16 from level 2, or a [[golden-idol\|Golden Idol]]'s room, once a level each | <Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGen.gml:85-121" /> |
| A shop | One of the shops, beside the path | <Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGen.gml:173-204" /> |

That table is the Mines' room script; each area has its own, with its own layouts, and the special levels
with them <Cite gml="scripts/scrInitLevel.gml:150-165" />. A layout has slots for chunks of 5 tiles by 3,
picked at random (a platform, a mound with [[spikes|Spikes]], an overhang), and the start and end rooms' slot
for the door picks one with the door in it <Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGen.gml:242-315" />. Then each tile is
made: a [[block|block]] of the area's earth, or, one time in ten in the Mines, a stone block; some tiles are a block
only half the time <Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGen.gml:317-357" />.

### Shops

From level 2 to level 15, a level rolls for a shop with a chance of 2 in its level's number: the roll always
passes on level 2, 2 times in 5 on level 5, 2 in 15 on level 15. The shop's room is a side room beside the
path, picked at random from those that qualify, and a level with none gets no shop; its kind is the room
script's roll <Cite gml="scripts/scrLevelGen.gml:239-297" />
<Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGen.gml:173-204" />. Once the game has had the [[black-market|Black Market]], no
level has a shop again ([Quirks](#quirks)).

### Special levels

Some rolls turn rooms of the grid into something else, before or after the path is laid
<Cite gml="scripts/scrLevelGen.gml:35-237" />. The graveyard's roll comes next, before the rooms are built,
and the dark level's after <Cite gml="scripts/scrInitLevel.gml:147-176" />:

| Special level | Where | Odds | |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [[snake-pit\|Snake pit]] | The Mines: a column of three side rooms from the top two rows | 1 in <Stat id="level-generation.snake-pit" bare /> for each such column, once a level | <Cite gml="scripts/scrLevelGen.gml:138-185" /> |
| [[dark-level\|Dark level]] | From level 2, outside the Ice Caves and Olmec's Lair; once an area | 1 in <Stat id="level-generation.dark-level" bare />, unless the level before it was the title screen or was finished 20 seconds or less past a whole minute | <Cite gml="scripts/scrInitLevel.gml:167-176" /> |
| [[black-market\|Black Market]] | The Jungle: the level after the player takes its hidden door | Always | <Cite gml="scripts/scrLevelGen.gml:44-69" /> |
| [[restless-dead\|Graveyard level]] | The Jungle | 1 in <Stat id="level-generation.graveyard" bare /> | <Cite gml="scripts/scrInitLevel.gml:147-148" /> |
| [[flooded-level\|Flooded level]] | The Jungle, from the screen between levels, until the Black Market's door is taken | 1 in <Stat id="level-generation.flooded" bare /> | <Cite gml="objects/oTransition/step.gml:18-24" /> |
| [[moai\|Moai]] | The Ice Caves, once a game | Below | <Cite gml="scripts/scrLevelGen.gml:212-221" /> |
| [[mothership\|Mothership]] | The Ice Caves, after the Moai's level | 1 in <Stat id="level-generation.mothership" bare /> | <Cite gml="scripts/scrLevelGen.gml:222-233" /> |
| [[yeti-lair\|Yeti lair]] | The Ice Caves, after the Moai's level | 1 in <Stat id="level-generation.yeti-lair" bare />, once the mothership's roll fails | <Cite gml="scripts/scrLevelGen.gml:234-237" /> |
| [[sacrifice-pit\|Sacrifice pit]] | The Temple | 1 in <Stat id="level-generation.sacrifice-pit" bare /> | <Cite gml="scripts/scrLevelGen.gml:71-87" /> |
| [[city-of-gold\|City of Gold]] | Level 15, after the player takes level 14's gold door | Always | <Cite gml="scripts/scrLevelGen.gml:135-136" /> |

The Black Market can be a graveyard level as well, and the City of Gold a dark level or a sacrifice pit, as
their pages say.

### Once a game

Four things are made once a game, each on one of a few levels, until it's made:

| What | Levels | |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The [[udjat-eye\|Udjat Eye]]'s [[locked-chest\|locked chest]] and its key | Level 2 one time in 3, level 3 one time in 2, otherwise level 4 | <Cite gml="scripts/scrInitLevel.gml:178-184" /> |
| The [[black-market\|Black Market]]'s hidden door | Level 5 one time in 3, level 6 one time in 2, otherwise level 7 | <Cite gml="scripts/scrInitLevel.gml:186-192" /> |
| The [[moai\|Moai]] | Level 9 one time in 4, level 10 one time in 3, level 11 one time in 2, otherwise level 12 | <Cite gml="scripts/scrLevelGen.gml:212-221" /> |
| The gold door to the [[city-of-gold\|City of Gold]] | Level 14 | <Cite gml="scripts/scrEntityGen.gml:387-388" /> |

A game started from one of the [[tunnel-man-shortcuts|Tunnel Man's shortcuts]] skips the levels before it,
and with them the chances of the first ones. [[black-market-route]] and [[city-of-gold-route]] walk through
finding and using the two doors.

### Monsters, traps and treasure

Once the rooms are built, a last pass goes over every block outside the shops, in the area's own way
<Cite gml="scripts/scrEntityGen.gml:30-525" />:

- **Monsters** hang under a block with open space below it (in the Mines, a [[bat|Bat]] 1 in 60, a
  [[spider|Spider]] 1 in 80) or stand on one with open space above (a [[snake|Snake]] 1 in 60), each roll
  made once the ones before it fail. In the Mines, none start in the start room
  <Cite gml="scripts/scrEntityGen.gml:44-71" />.
- **Treasure** goes on top of a block with nothing over it: a [[rock|Rock]] 1 in 100, or a [[jar|Jar]] 1 in
  40; failing those, in a nook one block high, a [[web|Web]], the Udjat Eye's chest, a [[crate|Crate]], a
  [[chest|Chest]], the [[damsel|Damsel]] (once a level), bones, [[gold-bar|gold]] or a big gem; in a tunnel,
  a Web, gold, bones or a big gem; on open ground, gold or bones. Nothing goes within two blocks of the
  entrance or the exit <Cite gml="scripts/scrTreasureGen.gml:24-133" />.
- **Gold in the rock** is the blocks' own: a block of the Mines' earth shows gold 29 times in 100, and one
  that doesn't may hold a big gem or, 1 time in 1,200 once those fail, an item
  <Cite gml="objects/oBrick/create.gml:3-16" />.
- **Traps**: in the Mines, one stone block in four that has open space on one side and a block on the other
  becomes an [[arrow-trap|Arrow Trap]] facing the open space, unless it's close to the entrance
  <Cite gml="scripts/scrEntityGen.gml:145-171" />.

The areas' Inhabitants sections have their monsters and odds. The pass ends with a crate of [[flare|Flares]] beside
the entrance of a [[dark-level|dark level]]; then the player is put at the entrance, a thief or murderer finds
an angry [[shopkeeper|Shopkeeper]] at the exit, and any treasure inside a block that can't be destroyed is removed <Cite
gml="scripts/scrInitLevel.gml:200-204" /> <Cite gml="scripts/scrEntityGen.gml:527-545" /> <Cite
gml="scripts/scrInitLevel.gml:245-266" />.

### The random number generator

Every roll comes from the game's one random number generator, which play draws from too: monsters, items and
even the particles each take numbers from it, so a level depends on everything that happened before it.
The game never seeds the generator; GameMaker seeds it at random when the game starts
<Cite quirk="4" row="One global stream: level generation, gameplay and cosmetic particles share it" /> <Cite quirk="4" row="At startup the runner randomizes" />. A test such as `a and rand(1, n) == 1` draws its number even when `a` is false, which
moves the generator on for rolls that can't come true <Cite quirk="2" row="and, or and xor evaluate both operands, side effects included (no short-circuit)." /> <Cite quirk="1" row="Conditions that call rand on the right of and draw from the RNG even when the left is false" />. The same
starting value and the same keys make the same levels again, which this wiki's replays rely on: the clip
below comes from one that starts the generator at 2 <Cite replay="fidelity/replays/mines-seed-2.txt" />.

## Quirks

Level generation keeps several bugs of the original's code, each a row of docs/quirks.md:

- **The path walk's moves go the other way.** Each `else` of the walk's moves belongs to the `if` nearest it,
  not to the edge check its indentation pairs it with, so a move toward a room the path already has goes the
  other way, or down, rather than being rolled again. The path runs on across a row more than it would
  otherwise <Cite quirk="1" row="The path walk's elses belong to the inner ifs, so a move toward a room already on the path goes the other way, or down." /> <Cite replay="fidelity/replays/mines-seed-1.txt" />.
- **A shop faces left only from the rightmost column.** A side room with the path only on its left is a shop
  only in the last column, so a level whose shop roll passes can still have none <Cite quirk="1" row="A shop faces left only from the rightmost column." />
  <Cite replay="fidelity/replays/mines-seed-5.txt" />.
- **No shops after the Black Market.** Once the Black Market is made, the shop roll never passes again that
  game ([[no-shops-after-black-market]]) <Cite quirk="1" row="No shop for the rest of the game once the Black Market is made." /> <Cite replay="fidelity/replays/city-of-gold-seed-9.txt" />.
- **No flooded level after the Black Market's door.** The lake is only rolled until the market's door is
  taken, so the Jungle levels after it are never flooded <Cite quirk="1" row="No flooded level after the Black Market's door." />.
- **No mothership or Yeti lair before the Moai.** Their rolls are the `else` of the Moai's, which every Ice
  Caves level takes until the Moai is made <Cite quirk="1" row="No alien craft or Yeti lair until the Moai is made" />.
- **A dark level needs the clock past 20 seconds.** It's ruled out after a level finished 20 seconds or less
  past a whole minute, and a dark roll on the Black Market's level, which stays lit, uses up the Jungle's
  dark level <Cite quirk="1" row="A dark level needs the previous level's clock" />.
- **Webs eleven times as often.** A misplaced bracket makes the web odds always take the branch meant for
  near a Giant Spider <Cite quirk="1" row="distance_to_object(oGiantSpider < 100) passes a comparison where the object goes" />.
- **Sealed in the rock.** The Udjat Eye's key ([[udjat-key-sealed]]) <Cite quirk="1" row="The locked chest's key can be sealed in rock." />
  and the gold door ([[gold-door-anywhere]]) <Cite quirk="1" row="The gold door can be sealed in rock." /> can
  each land where no way leads, which has to be broken open, and the City of Gold's Lady Xoc room can cut the
  way down to the exit ([[lady-xoc-room-in-the-way]]) <Cite quirk="1" row="The City of Gold's Lady Xoc room can seal the way to the exit." />,
  which then means breaking through its floor; either takes a bomb or the [[mattock|Mattock]].
- **Blocks under the Ice Caves.** A row of blocks lies under every Ice Caves level, out of reach, with
  treasure on it <Cite quirk="1" row="A row of Ice Caves blocks lies under the level, with treasure on it." />.
- **Places.** The Black Market can be a graveyard level that never says so
  ([[black-market-graveyard]]) <Cite quirk="1" row="A Black Market can be a graveyard level, and never says so." />,
  can have two General Stores ([[two-general-stores]]) <Cite quirk="1" row="A Black Market's fifth item shop can be a second General Store." />,
  and its door's fallback can make several doors <Cite quirk="1" row="The Black Market door's fallback puts a door in every block that qualifies." />;
  a graveyard level can have several Ash graves ([[ash-graves]]) <Cite quirk="1" row="A graveyard level can have several Ash graves, each with a free Shotgun." />;
  the City of Gold's stone blocks give no gold ([[city-of-gold-stone-blocks]]) <Cite quirk="1" row="Every stone block on a Temple level breaks without a trace, so the City of Gold's give no gold." />;
  and Olmec's Lair rolls a path and a shop that nothing uses <Cite quirk="1" row="Olmec's Lair rolls a path and a shop that its room script ignores." />.
