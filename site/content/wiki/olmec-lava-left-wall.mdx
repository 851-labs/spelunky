[[olmec|Olmec]] can only die in [[lava|lava]], when his slams have dug him down through the floor of his lair
into it ([[beating-olmec|Beating Olmec]]). By the lair's left wall, they dig him into it all the same, but
there it can't kill him: he sits on the lava's floor, out of sight, and goes on hopping out of it and landing
back in it unharmed.

## What happens

Olmec's last slam takes him down by the left wall of his lair into the lava, where he sits out of sight
without drowning. When he hops out of it and drifts right, toward the player, off the wall, he starts to sink
<Cite clip="quirks/olmec-lava-left-wall" />.

<Clip id="quirks/olmec-lava-left-wall" />

## Why

Olmec tests for lava at two points on his left edge, his `x`: under his feet, which starts him drowning, and
over his top, which kills him:

```gml objects/oOlmec/step.gml:35-43
if (collision_point(x, y+64, oLava, 0, 0)) status = DROWNING;

if (collision_point(x, y-2, oLava, 0, 0))
{
    global.enemyKills[21] += 1;
    oFinalBoss.olmecDead = true;
    global.kills += 1;
    instance_destroy();
}
```

But his body starts 2 pixels right of `x` <Cite gml="objects/oOlmec/create.gml:3" />, and his step bumps him
a pixel right while his body touches a wall on its left <Cite gml="scripts/isCollisionLeft.gml:6-10" />:

```gml objects/oOlmec/step.gml:22-27
if (isCollisionLeft(1))
{
    x += 1;
    xVel = 0;
    //if (xVel < 0) xVel = -xVel * 0.8;
}
```

So drifting into the left wall, he comes to rest at x 15, his body a pixel off the wall and his `x` in the
wall's column, x 0 to 15, where neither point is ever lava. Only a hop's drift takes him off it, and in the
air he drifts right only if the player is past his right side on the step his drift is decided
<Cite gml="objects/oOlmec/step.gml:104-111" />. With the player over him he doesn't drift, and the player
can't be left of him there <Cite quirk="1" />.

## Reproducing it

Let Olmec's slams dig him down by the left wall of his lair, and stay over him as he hops. The replay of a whole
game with seed 1 shows it: his last slam lands him in the lava at x 15, on its floor, his top 32 pixels under
its surface. His `x` stays 15 through a hop that, with the player to his right, drifts him right, and he starts
to drown 2 pixels off the wall <Cite replay="fidelity/replays/ending-seed-1.txt" />. Another run keeps him there
longer, with the player on a rope above him through two hops, until a third, with the player to his right,
drifts him off <Cite replay="fidelity/replays/olmec-door-block-crush-seed-1.txt" />.

## In the port

The spelunky.ai port reproduces it exactly: it's in docs/quirks.md's list of the 1.1 source's bugs
<Cite quirk="1" />, and the oracle's fingerprints of both replays pin his position and the step he starts to
drown. No option turns it off.
