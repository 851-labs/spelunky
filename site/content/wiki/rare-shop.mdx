Its Shopkeeper welcomes the player with "WELCOME TO HAMISH'S SPECIALTY SHOP!", his name one of 32 drawn at
random <Cite gml="objects/oShopkeeper/step.gml:130" /> <Cite gml="scripts/scrGetName.gml:24-60" />. It
sells the widest range of any shop: eleven pieces of equipment, the only shop with the
[[jetpack|Jetpack]] and the [[teleporter|Teleporter]], and [[bomb-box|Bomb Boxes]]. Like every shop it's
a side room off the way through a level, with four wares on its floor and the [[shopkeeper|Shopkeeper]]
beside them.

## Stock

The shop has four wares, and each is rolled on its own <Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGen.gml:173-191" />
<Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGen.gml:448-451" />. Each try first rolls for a Bomb Box, 1 in 20; failing
that, it picks one of the eleven pieces of equipment, each as likely, and takes it if the level hasn't
got one. A try that picks a piece the level has makes the next try's Bomb Box roll likelier, 1 in 19,
then 1 in 18 and so on, so by the twentieth try a Bomb Box is certain
<Cite gml="scripts/scrShopItemsGen.gml:102-155" />:

| Ware | Roll | |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [[bomb-box\|Bomb Box]] | 1 in 20 on the first try, 1 in 19 on the second... | <Cite gml="scripts/scrShopItemsGen.gml:108" /> |
| [[spring-shoes\|Spring Shoes]] | Otherwise 1 in 11, if the level has none | <Cite gml="scripts/scrShopItemsGen.gml:109-112" /> |
| [[compass\|Compass]] | Otherwise 1 in 11, if the level has none | <Cite gml="scripts/scrShopItemsGen.gml:113-116" /> |
| [[mattock\|Mattock]] | Otherwise 1 in 11, if the level has none | <Cite gml="scripts/scrShopItemsGen.gml:117-120" /> |
| [[spectacles\|Spectacles]] | Otherwise 1 in 11, if the level has none | <Cite gml="scripts/scrShopItemsGen.gml:121-124" /> |
| [[jetpack\|Jetpack]] | Otherwise 1 in 11, if the level has none | <Cite gml="scripts/scrShopItemsGen.gml:125-128" /> |
| [[climbing-gloves\|Climbing Gloves]] | Otherwise 1 in 11, if the level has none | <Cite gml="scripts/scrShopItemsGen.gml:129-132" /> |

| [[spike-shoes\|Spike Shoes]] | Otherwise 1 in 11, if the level has none | <Cite gml="scripts/scrShopItemsGen.gml:149-152" /> |

So a Rare Shop sells each piece once at most, and Bomb Boxes more often the more pieces the level already
has. The check is for the piece on the level, not the player's, so the shop sells a piece the player
already wears, and buying it again changes nothing ([[owned-equipment-for-sale]]) <Cite quirk="1" />. The Mattock, the Web Cannon and
the Teleporter are the player's to hold and use rather than to wear
<Cite gml="scripts/scrStealItem.gml:243-292" />.

## Shopkeeper

The Shopkeeper sells as in every shop ([[general-store#shopkeeper|the General Store]] has the details): he
names the price of a ware the player holds, and the pay key buys it
<Cite gml="objects/oShopkeeper/step.gml:72-85" /> <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:1359-1389" />. A
piece of equipment to wear goes on as it's bought, one to use stays in the player's hands, his own, and a
Bomb Box adds its bombs <Cite gml="scripts/scrStealItem.gml:80-91" />
<Cite gml="scripts/scrStealItem.gml:126-313" />. A theft, a bomb lit in the shop or a block of its walls
broken angers him, and he gives the wares away and attacks with his [[shotgun|Shotgun]]
<Cite gml="scripts/scrShopkeeperAnger.gml:7-23" /> <Cite gml="objects/oShopkeeper/step.gml:337-408" />.

## Stealing

<Clip id="rare-shop/theft" />

Carrying a ware out of the shop's room unpaid angers the Shopkeeper ("COME BACK HERE, THIEF!") and gives
it to the player as if bought. A piece to wear goes on at once only if the player leaves the room before
it, held in front of him, does; otherwise he's left holding it, free, and picking it up again puts it
on ([[stolen-ware-unequipped]]) <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:541-557" /> <Cite gml="objects/oItem/step.gml:17-34" /> <Cite
quirk="1" />. In the clip, in a Rare Shop that opens to the left, the player picks up the Spike
Shoes, priced at $5,200, and runs and jumps out with them; the Shopkeeper turns on him and fires, and the
shoes, out of the shop first, stay in his hands rather than going on <Cite clip="rare-shop/theft" />.

<Clip id="rare-shop/wanted" />

A theft marks the player a thief into the next level: its exit has a Shopkeeper waiting by it, and a
shop's Shopkeeper doesn't greet him but walks about, ready to attack, under a Wanted poster of the player
([[general-store#stealing|the General Store]] has the rules) <Cite gml="scripts/scrInitLevel.gml:245-257" />
<Cite gml="objects/oShopkeeper/step.gml:121-124" /> <Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGen2.gml:427-435" />. The
second clip shows a Rare Shop on such a level <Cite clip="rare-shop/wanted" />. Its wares are free, and each
counts as stolen in the end-of-game statistics as it comes on screen, though the player touches none
<Cite gml="objects/oItem/step.gml:8-15" /> <Cite quirk="1" />.

## Locations

A level from 2 to 15 that rolls a shop builds one of seven kinds, each as likely, so a shop is a Rare Shop
one time in seven (one in six on a Temple [[sacrifice-pit|sacrifice pit]] level, which never has a
[[kissing-parlor|Kissing Parlor]]) <Cite gml="scripts/scrLevelGen.gml:239-297" /> <Cite
gml="scripts/scrRoomGen.gml:173-204" /> <Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGenYeti.gml:149-181" />
<Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGen4.gml:155-188" />.
[[mines#shops|The Mines]] has how levels roll shops. A [[black-market|Black Market]] has five shops that
sell items, as a rule one of each kind, a Rare Shop among them ([[two-general-stores]] is the exception) <Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGenMarket.gml:117-140" /> <Cite quirk="1" />
<Cite replay="fidelity/replays/shop-seed-9-market-two-general-stores.txt" />.
