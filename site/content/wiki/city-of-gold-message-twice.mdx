A level that's two special levels at once shows two messages, one after the other: a dark
[[restless-dead|graveyard]] says "I CAN'T SEE A THING!", then "THE DEAD ARE RESTLESS!". The
[[city-of-gold|City of Gold]] can be a [[sacrifice-pit|sacrifice pit]] too, but its second message is its first
again, "IT'S THE LEGENDARY CITY OF GOLD!", and the pit's comes third.

## What happens

The City of Gold loads, and it's a sacrifice pit. "IT'S THE LEGENDARY CITY OF GOLD!" shows, then the same message
again about seven seconds later, and only seven seconds after that, "I CAN HEAR PRAYERS TO KALI!"
<Cite clip="quirks/city-message-twice" />.

<Clip id="quirks/city-message-twice" />

## Why

The player's alarm 0 shows a special level's message when it loads. For the City of Gold, it shows the City's,
and if the level is also a sacrifice pit, sets alarm 1 for the pit's message, 209 steps later
<Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/alarm_0.gml:52-58" />. But alarm 1 goes through the same list of levels in the same
order, and finds the City before the pit:

```gml objects/oPlayer1/alarm_1.gml:36-42
else if (global.cityOfGold)
{
    global.message = "IT'S THE LEGENDARY CITY OF GOLD!";
    global.message2 = "";
    global.messageTimer = 200;
    if (global.sacrificePit) alarm[4] = 210;
}
```

So it shows the City's message again, and sets alarm 4, which does check for the pit, and shows its message
another 209 steps later <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/alarm_4.gml:7-12" /> <Cite quirk="1" />. On a dark City,
alarm 0 shows the dark level's message instead, and alarm 1 the City's, so it shows once.

## Reproducing it

Reach a City of Gold that's a sacrifice pit and isn't dark. Level generation rolls the pit, 1 in 8, on every
[[temple|Temple]] level, the City's included <Cite gml="scripts/scrLevelGen.gml:71-86" />, so about one City in 8
that isn't dark has it. The replay of the clip reaches one: the City's message shows on frame 7280 and again on
7489, and Kali's on 7698 <Cite replay="fidelity/replays/city-of-gold-seed-9-sacrifice-pit.txt" />.

## In the port

The spelunky.ai port reproduces it exactly: it's in docs/quirks.md's list of the 1.1 source's bugs
<Cite quirk="1" />, and the oracle's fingerprint of the replay pins the three messages. No option turns it off.
