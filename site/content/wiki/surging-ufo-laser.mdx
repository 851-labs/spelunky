A [[ufo|UFO]] fires its laser straight down at the player, and the laser gathers speed as it falls. It should
stop gathering at 4 pixels a step, but instead it stops moving altogether and starts over, so every laser falls in
surges: a run of quickening steps, a pause, another run.

## What happens

The player throws a stunned yeti under a UFO. The UFO fires, and its laser falls in surges, speeding up and
stopping dead every seven steps, until it hits the yeti, which flies up into the UFO and blows it up
<Cite clip="quirks/ufo-laser-surges" />.

<Clip id="quirks/ufo-laser-surges" />

## Why

A laser starts with no speed and a gravity of 0.6 <Cite gml="objects/oLaser/create.gml:3-4" />. Each step, it
moves by its speed, adds the gravity, and checks the speed against 4:

```gml objects/oLaser/step.gml:1-3
y += yVel;
yVel += yAcc;
if (yVel > 4) yVel = 0;
```

The check is a cap that sets the speed to 0 instead of 4 <Cite quirk="1" />. So the laser moves 0, 0.6, 1.2,
1.8, 2.4, 3.0 and 3.6 pixels on seven steps; then its speed would reach 4.2, goes back to 0, and the run starts
over: about 12.6 pixels every 7 steps.

## Reproducing it

Every laser a UFO fires falls this way. In the replay of the clip, a laser fired on frame 1865 has a speed of 0
again on 1871 and 1878, and never above 4, before it hits the yeti on 1881
<Cite replay="fidelity/replays/enemy-ufo-stunned-yeti-seed-14.txt" />.

## In the port

The spelunky.ai port reproduces it exactly: it's in docs/quirks.md's list of the 1.1 source's bugs
<Cite quirk="1" />, and the oracle's fingerprint of the replay pins the laser's fall. No option turns it off.
