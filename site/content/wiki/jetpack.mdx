Wearing it, the player can fly: holding jump in mid-air lifts him for as long as its fuel lasts, and
landing fills it again. [[rare-shop|Rare Shops]] and the [[dice-house|Dice House]] offer it; it's rare in
[[crate|Crates]] and blocks, and one waits in the [[mothership|mothership]]. It's worn from the moment it's
picked up or paid for until the game ends, unless a [[cape|Cape]] takes its place.

## Effect

<Clip id="jetpack/fly" />

Picking up a free jetpack, paying for one or carrying one out of a shop puts it on: the message "YOU GOT A
JETPACK!" shows, the jetpack disappears, and an icon of it joins the row under the hearts
<Cite gml="scripts/scrStealItem.gml:223-232" /> <Cite gml="scripts/scrDrawHUD.gml:155-159" />. From then on
it's drawn on the player's back <Cite gml="scripts/characterDrawEvent.gml:11-19" />. It stays on until a new
game starts, which clears it <Cite gml="scripts/scrClearGlobals.gml:97" />
<Cite gml="objects/oTransition/create.gml:20-24" />.

Whenever the player stands on the ground, its fuel is set to <Stat id="jetpack.fuel" />
<Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:215-218" />. In the air, as long as the player didn't leave the ground
with the key still held (walking off a ledge counts as letting go; after a jump, it has to be let go of
once), holding jump with fuel left fires it: each step it sets him moving up at 1 pixel a step, adds a jump's
push, takes away gravity and burns one step of fuel <Cite gml="scripts/characterStepEvent.gml:257-264" />
<Cite gml="scripts/characterStepEvent.gml:284-292" /> <Cite gml="scripts/characterStepEvent.gml:325-337" />.
Every 3 steps of thrust it throws out a spark and plays its sound
<Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/alarm_10.gml:1-4" />.

In play, that's a climb of 3 pixels a step (90 per second) while jump is held, and a full tank lasts
<Stat id="jetpack.fuel" as="seconds" />: in the clip, the player leaves a shop with it, lands once
(refuelling), jumps, lets go of jump and holds it again, and the jetpack carries him over a gap and up a
shaft at 3 pixels a step to the ceiling, where its fuel runs out <Stat id="jetpack.fuel" /> later; he falls
onto a ledge, and landing fills it again <Cite clip="jetpack/fly" />. Letting go of jump stops the thrust,
and pressing it again in the air restarts it while fuel is left.

### Breaking a fall

<Clip id="jetpack/soft-landing" />

The player's step counts the steps he's been falling only while he moves down, and sets the count back to 0
on any other step in the air <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:254-257" /> <Cite
gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:290-292" />; landing after more than 16 is a [[damage#long-drops|long drop]],
which hurts <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:268-289" />. Thrust sets
him moving up, so a moment of it just before the ground starts the fall over from there. In the clip, the
player puts on a jetpack and walks off a ledge into a shaft whose drop costs two hearts. After 33 steps of
falling, 9 pixels above the floor, a tap of jump fires the jetpack for two steps. The first still counts as
falling, as his step checks before the thrust moves him; on the second the count goes back to 0, and he
lands 6 steps later, neither hurt nor stunned <Cite clip="jetpack/soft-landing" />.

## Interactions

- The Jetpack and the [[cape|Cape]] are never worn together. Putting on the Jetpack while wearing the Cape
  drops the Cape at the player's feet, free to pick up again; putting on a Cape drops the Jetpack the same
  way <Cite gml="scripts/scrStealItem.gml:214-221" /> <Cite gml="scripts/scrStealItem.gml:233-241" />.
- The fuel only refills standing, running, crouching or looking up on the ground: hanging from a ledge or
  climbing a [[rope|rope]] or ladder doesn't refill it, and the jetpack can't fire from either
  <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:215-218" /> <Cite gml="scripts/platformCharacterIs.gml:15-20" />.

## Tips

- At 3 pixels a step for <Stat id="jetpack.fuel" />, a full tank lifts the player about 150 pixels, nine
  blocks; a taller shaft needs somewhere to land and refuel on the way.
- A long drop is safe with fuel left: tap jump just above the ground, and the fall counts again from
  there <Cite clip="jetpack/soft-landing" />.

  crushed, once his hearts have fallen below -99, or once a [[mantrap|Mantrap]] or the [[ghost|Ghost]] has
  taken him <Cite gml="objects/oJetpack/step.gml:3-4" /> <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:1484-1511" />
