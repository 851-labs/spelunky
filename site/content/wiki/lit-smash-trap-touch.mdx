A [[smash-trap|Smash Trap]] hits the player for 10 hearts on every step it touches him, throws him and makes him
drop what he holds. On a [[dark-level|dark level]] the [[temple|Temple]] makes the lit kind instead, which glows
and slides as the plain one does, but isn't a Smash Trap to the one check that hits the player, so its touch
does nothing. It kills only by sliding on into him until he's crushed.

## What happens

On a dark Temple level, the player jumps into the line of a lit Smash Trap. It slides at him, and its touch
neither takes hearts nor throws him: it slides on into him, and crushes him <Cite clip="smash-trap/lit" />.

<Clip id="smash-trap/lit" />

## Why

On a dark level, `scrEntityGen` makes `oSmashTrapLit` where it would make `oSmashTrap`
<Cite gml="scripts/scrEntityGen.gml:469-470" />. The lit kind has the plain one's code
<Cite gml="objects/oSmashTrapLit/step.gml:1-137" />, but not the plain one as its parent: both are children of
`oMovingSolid`, where the lit Arrow and Spear Traps are children of their plain kinds <Cite quirk="1" />. The
player's step looks for a Smash Trap by name:

```gml objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:1649-1657
if (collision_rectangle(x-6, y-6, x+6, y+7, oSmashTrap, 0, 0))
{
    if (global.plife > 0 and isRealLevel()) global.miscDeaths[8] += 1;

    obj = instance_nearest(x, y, oSmashTrap);
    global.plife -= 10;
    if (obj.x+8 < x) xVel = -rand(4,6);
    else xVel = rand(4,6);
    yVel = -6;
```

A check for `oSmashTrap` finds its children, and the lit kind isn't one, so none of it runs: no 10 hearts, no
throw, no dropping what he holds <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:1658-1668" />, and no count in the deaths
table's `SMASH TRAP` row (`miscDeaths[8]`). The lit trap is still solid, so once it has slid into him until his
middle is inside it, the crush check any solid sets off takes 99 hearts, counted as `CRUSHED`
(`miscDeaths[2]`) <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:1492-1511" />.

## Reproducing it

Reach a dark Temple level with a Smash Trap, which one block in sixty gets once the monsters' rolls fail, and
step into its line <Cite gml="scripts/scrEntityGen.gml:466-471" />. The replay, with
seed 1, waits at level 13's exit until level 14 comes up dark, with six lit Smash Traps, and jumps the player
into one's line. It slides at him, and his 4 hearts go all at once, on the step it crushes him, with the
crush's `yVel` of -3; no step before has the hit's -6. The kill is counted as crushed, never as the smash trap
<Cite replay="fidelity/replays/trap-dark-smash.txt" />. A plain Smash Trap's touch, on a lit level, takes 10 hearts
a step before it crushes him <Cite replay="fidelity/replays/trap-smash-crush.txt" />.

## In the port

The spelunky.ai port reproduces it exactly: it's in docs/quirks.md's list of the 1.1 source's bugs
<Cite quirk="1" />, and the oracle's fingerprint of the replay pins the hearts, the player's `yVel` and the
deaths table. No option turns it off.
