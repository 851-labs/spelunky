Its Shopkeeper welcomes the player with "WELCOME TO LEMMY'S DICE HOUSE!", his name one of 32 drawn at
random, and "PRESS P TO BET $2000.", the bet's price on level 2 <Cite gml="objects/oShopkeeper/step.gml:131-139" />
<Cite gml="scripts/scrGetName.gml:24-60" />. It has no wares for sale: it has two dice on its floor, a
sign of the rules on its wall, and a prize behind two blocks of ice at the back, which only a throw of 7
wins <Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGen.gml:185" /> <Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGen.gml:385-403" />
<Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGen.gml:452-455" />. Outside the [[black-market|Black Market]], it's a side
room off the way through a level like every shop, with the [[shopkeeper|Shopkeeper]] on a ledge above the
floor.

## Stock

<Clip id="dice-house/win" />

The pay key in the shop bets: the bet comes out of the player's money ("YOU BET $2000! NOW ROLL THE
DICE!"). Pressed again while a bet is on, it says "ONE BET AT A TIME!", and without the money, "YOU NEED
$2000 TO BET!". A thief, or a killer of Shopkeepers, gets nothing <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:1392-1420" />.
The dice are picked up and thrown like any item. Each shows a number from 1 to 6, and rolls a new one on
every step it moves faster than 2 pixels a step; thrown while a bet is on, it's rolling, and it counts once
it comes to rest on the ground <Cite gml="objects/oDice/step.gml:211-231" />. When both have, the
Shopkeeper adds them up <Cite gml="objects/oShopkeeper/step.gml:154-223" />:

| Total | Shout | What the player gets | |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 2–6 | "YOU ROLLED A 5!" (the total), "I'M SORRY, BUT YOU LOSE!" | Nothing | <Cite gml="objects/oShopkeeper/step.gml:213-220" /> |
| 7 | "YOU ROLLED A SEVEN! YOU WIN A PRIZE!" | The prize, and the bet is lost | <Cite gml="objects/oShopkeeper/step.gml:173-201" /> |
| 8–12 | "YOU ROLLED A 9!" (the total), "CONGRATULATIONS! YOU WIN!" | Twice the bet, so the bet back and as much again | <Cite gml="objects/oShopkeeper/step.gml:202-212" /> |

Either way the bet is settled and both dice are ready to throw again. He settles only while he's calm
and near the screen, and only with both dice still on the level <Cite gml="objects/oShopkeeper/step.gml:3-4" />
<Cite gml="objects/oShopkeeper/step.gml:99" /> <Cite gml="objects/oShopkeeper/step.gml:156-169" />. In
the clip, the player bets $2,000 and is refused a
second bet; the dice come up 4 and 5, and his $1,100 counts up to $5,100 <Cite clip="dice-house/win" />.

A win above 7 pays twice the bet, so it gains the player one bet. A prize costs only the bet it's won
with: it keeps the price its item is made with, but nothing marks it for sale, and a 7 sets the price to

## Shopkeeper

The Shopkeeper doesn't follow the player about the shop, as he does where there are wares; he stands on
his ledge and settles the bets <Cite gml="objects/oShopkeeper/step.gml:154-235" />. What angers him in other shops angers him here ([[general-store#shopkeeper|the General
Store]] has the rules): the blocks of ice around the prize are the shop's walls, so breaking one in is
vandalism, which frees the prize with everything else <Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGen.gml:398-403" />
<Cite gml="objects/oSolid/destroy.gml:1-7" /> <Cite gml="objects/oShopkeeper/step.gml:337-348" />. Two
things are the Dice House's own, both "COME BACK HERE, THIEF!" <Cite gml="scripts/scrShopkeeperAnger.gml:7-23" />:

- **A die rolled twice.** A die that has come to rest and is thrown again before the bet is settled
  angers him as it lands. The code's comment calls it "NO CHEATING!" <Cite gml="objects/oDice/step.gml:217-224" />.
- **A die out of the shop.** A die near the screen outside a shop's room angers him at once, though it has
  no price <Cite gml="objects/oDice/step.gml:1-17" />. The check is for any shop's room
  <Cite gml="scripts/isInShop.gml:7-13" />.

<Clip id="dice-house/cheat" />

In the clip, the player bets again and throws one die, which comes up 5, then picks the same die up and
throws it again. As it lands, the Shopkeeper shouts, comes down from his ledge and shoots him dead
<Cite clip="dice-house/cheat" />.

## Stealing

The Dice House has no wares to steal, but a die carried out angers the Shopkeeper as a theft does, and
marks the player a thief for the next level, where [[general-store#stealing|the General Store's]] rules
apply <Cite gml="objects/oDice/step.gml:10-17" /> <Cite gml="scripts/scrShopkeeperAnger.gml:21-22" />. A
thief can't bet <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:1395-1398" />. A prize in its case has a price, so
on a thief's level it's freed and counted as stolen in the end-of-game statistics as it comes on screen,
as wares are <Cite gml="objects/oItem/step.gml:8-15" /> <Cite quirk="1" />.

## Locations

A level from 2 to 15 that rolls a shop builds one of seven kinds, each as likely, so a shop is a Dice
House one time in seven (one in six on a Temple [[sacrifice-pit|sacrifice pit]] level, which never has a
[[kissing-parlor|Kissing Parlor]]), in every area and on a [[yeti-lair|Yeti lair]] level too <Cite
gml="scripts/scrLevelGen.gml:239-297" /> <Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGen.gml:173-204" />
<Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGenYeti.gml:149-181" /> <Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGen4.gml:155-188" />.
[[mines#shops|The Mines]] has how levels roll shops. Every [[black-market|Black Market]] has a Dice House,
the third column's third room, where the pay key bets only in that room <Cite
gml="scripts/scrRoomGenMarket.gml:142-146" /> <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:1392-1393" />.
