Every level has two doors: the entrance, where the player starts it, and the exit, his way on to the next
level. A few levels have a third, the way to a special level: the hidden door to
[[black-market|the Black Market]] and the gold door to [[city-of-gold|the City of Gold]]. Doors are part of
the background, not blocks: nothing destroys one.

## How it works

<Clip id="door/exit" />

### The entrance

When a level is built, the player is put in its entrance's doorway <Cite gml="scripts/scrInitLevel.gml:200-204" />.
The entrance does nothing more: it's not a way back. The [[ankh|Ankh]] brings a player back to life at it, but
inside the [[moai|Moai]] or at the left end of [[olmecs-lair|Olmec's Lair]]
<Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:1856-1872" />.

### Taking the exit

Pressing up in front of the exit while standing on the ground, not dead, stunned or whipping, takes it
<Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:759-765" />. The player walks in through the door, out of reach of anything
<Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:855-878" />, and when he's through, the screen between levels comes, then the
next level <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/other_animation_end.gml:44-70" />. The clip shows it: the
player walks to the exit, presses up, and walks in <Cite clip="door/exit" />. As he goes:

- **What he holds** goes with him in his hands to the next level, but a heavy item, which he puts down; a
  [[bomb|Bomb]] or [[rope|Rope]], which goes back into his count (a lit bomb is dropped); the
  [[golden-idol|Golden Idol]], which is cashed in; and a [[damsel|Damsel]], who walks in with him and is
  rescued <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:767-853" />.
- **The money** he picked up on the level is added to his total <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:862-865" />.
- **A thief** has one level fewer to fear the [[shopkeeper|Shopkeepers]]
  <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:874" />.
- **[[monkey|Monkeys]]** holding on to him are thrown off <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:890-901" />.

Carrying the idol or a Damsel into the exit's doorway is enough, even without pressing up: the idol is cashed
in and the Damsel walks in <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:2068-2113" />.

### Special doors

- **The Black Market's door** is an exit hidden inside a block on one of [[jungle|the Jungle]]'s levels. Taken,
  it leads to [[black-market|the Black Market]] as the next level <Cite gml="objects/oXMarket/create.gml:3" />
  <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:879" />.
- **The gold door**, which level generation stands on a block that can't be destroyed on one Temple level
  <Cite gml="scripts/scrEntityGen.gml:400-406" /> <Cite gml="scripts/scrEntityGen.gml:483-490" />, opens when
  the player carries the [[sceptre|Sceptre]] into it wearing the [[hedjet|Hedjet]]: the Sceptre is used up, and the door becomes an exit to [[city-of-gold|the City of Gold]].
  Without the Hedjet, it only says "THE SCEPTRE FITS... BUT NOTHING IS HAPPENING!"
  <Cite gml="objects/oGoldDoor/collision_oSceptre.gml:1-23" /> <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:880" />.
  Where it can end up is [[gold-door-anywhere]].

## Locations

The room templates have one tile for both doors, with a block under it: in the room the level starts in, it
makes the entrance, and in any other, the room the level's path ends in, the exit, whose block can't be
destroyed <Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGen.gml:345-357" />. The [[moai|Moai]] has an exit of its own, in its chamber
<Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGen3.gml:496-497" />. The [[tutorial|Tutorial]], [[olmecs-lair|Olmec's Lair]], the
screens between levels and the bonus rooms, the [[sun-room|Sun]], [[moon-room|Moon]] and
[[stars-room|Stars Rooms]], have doors the room editor placed <Cite room="rTutorial" /> <Cite room="rOlmec" />
<Cite room="rSun" />. In custom levels they're the [[level-editor|level editor]]'s tiles `@` (the entrance)
and `X` (an exit) <Cite gml="scripts/scrCreateTileObj.gml:24-31" />, and an exit can lead to another custom
level <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:881-888" />.

## Interactions

- **[[bomb|Bombs]]** pass doors by: a door isn't solid, and the blast only breaks what is
  <Cite gml="objects/oExplosion/collision_oSolid.gml:1-31" />.
- **[[damsel|Damsels]]** running free leave by an exit by themselves, and count as rescued
  <Cite gml="objects/oDamsel/step.gml:3-24" />.
