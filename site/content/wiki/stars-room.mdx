It's the third of the three secret challenges behind the trophies of the [[high-scores-room|high scores
room]], the SHOTGUN CHALLENGE. One room, built in the room editor, of rock pillars and ledges with a
[[shotgun|Shotgun]] on the middle one. [[shopkeeper|Shopkeepers]] drop in from the ceiling, angry from the
start, and come faster as the kills mount; past the first kills, [[spider|Spiders]] drop in with them. The
score is the Shopkeepers killed before the player dies.

## Generation

### Getting there

A best of 120 kills or more puts a gold trophy third from the top of the high scores room's column of
trophies, and a door to the Stars Room under it <Cite gml="objects/oHighscores/create.gml:68-84" />.
Climbing up beside it, the board reads STARS ROOM and the goal, KILL AS MANY ANGRY SHOPKEEPS AS YOU CAN
BEFORE THEY GET YOU!, or LOCKED. without the kills <Cite gml="objects/oHighscores/draw.gml:67-92" />. The
clip of the climb to the [[sun-room|Sun Room]] passes it on the way ([[high-scores-room#the-room]]).

### The room

The room editor laid out the whole room, a screen wide: the entrance at the bottom left, rock pillars and
ledges, and the Shotgun on top of the middle pillar <Cite room="rStars" />. A carved star hangs on the back
wall. As in every bonus room, the bricks around its edge can't be broken
<Cite gml="objects/oStarsRoom/create.gml:11-17" />, the player has <Stat id="health.bonus-room" />
<Cite gml="objects/oStarsRoom/create.gml:1" />, and his bombs and ropes can't be used outside a level
([[sun-room#the-room]]).

### The challenge

<Clip id="stars-room/shotgun" />

SHOTGUN CHALLENGE BEGINS IN 3... counts down at the bottom of the screen as the room starts
<Cite gml="objects/oStarsRoom/draw.gml:9-17" />. From 100 steps in, Shopkeepers drop in from the ceiling, each
at one of four places across the top at random, on a timer that shortens as the kills mount <Cite gml="objects/oStarsRoom/create.gml:5" /> <Cite gml="objects/oStarsRoom/alarm_0.gml:1-22" />:

| Kills | A Shopkeeper every | |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0–9 | 200 steps (6.67 seconds) | <Cite gml="objects/oStarsRoom/alarm_0.gml:21" /> |
| 10–19 | 175 steps (5.83 seconds) | <Cite gml="objects/oStarsRoom/alarm_0.gml:17-20" /> |
| 20–29 | 150 steps (5 seconds) | <Cite gml="objects/oStarsRoom/alarm_0.gml:13-16" /> |
| 30–39 | 125 steps (4.17 seconds) | <Cite gml="objects/oStarsRoom/alarm_0.gml:9-12" /> |
| 40 or more | 100 steps (3.33 seconds) | <Cite gml="objects/oStarsRoom/alarm_0.gml:5-8" /> |

They come angry and without a shotgun <Cite gml="objects/oShopkeeper2/create.gml:1-5" />, chasing and
leaping at the player as an angry Shopkeeper does ([[shopkeeper#angry]]); one that runs into a loose
shotgun picks it up and fires it <Cite gml="objects/oShopkeeper/collision_oShotgun.gml:1-14" />. From 110 steps
in, every 20 steps, a roll of 1 to 100 that comes in under the kills drops a [[spider|Spider]] at one of the
same four places, so with more kills more Spiders come, and with none or one, none <Cite gml="objects/oStarsRoom/create.gml:8" /> <Cite gml="objects/oStarsRoom/alarm_11.gml:1-7" />.

A Shopkeeper killed there adds one to the room's kills, shown at the top beside a Shopkeeper's face, up to 99,
and nothing else: he leaves no [[gold-nugget|Gold Nuggets]], and counts in none of the game's kills
<Cite gml="objects/oShopkeeper/step.gml:479-502" /> <Cite gml="objects/oStarsRoom/draw.gml:7-8" />. His body
bleeds and is gone 200 steps later <Cite gml="objects/oShopkeeper2/step.gml:1-11" />.

The challenge ends when the player dies: FINISHED! comes up, then YOU SET A NEW RECORD! if the kills beat the
room's best, or BETTER LUCK NEXT TIME... <Cite gml="objects/oGame/draw.gml:99-118" />. The kills are kept
as the room's best in the high scores, which count no play, death or win for it <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:1909-1914" /> <Cite gml="scripts/scrUpdateHighscores.gml:78-117" />.
Attack (or start) goes back to the high scores room, in front of the Stars Room's door <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:907-941" /> <Cite gml="objects/oHighscores/create.gml:114-118" />.

In the clip, the first Shopkeeper drops in and knocks the player about while he climbs to the Shotgun. He
shoots one dead as more drop in; then, stunned, he drops the Shotgun, and a Shopkeeper picks it up and
shoots him with it, at 1 kill <Cite clip="stars-room/shotgun" /> <Cite replay="fidelity/replays/bonus-stars-shotgun.txt" />.

## Hazards

The room has no traps <Cite room="rStars" />: what can hurt the player is the Shopkeepers, the Spiders, and
a Shopkeeper who has picked up a shotgun.

## Items and shops

The [[shotgun|Shotgun]] on the middle pillar is the room's only item <Cite room="rStars" />.
