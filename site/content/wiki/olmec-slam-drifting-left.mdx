[[olmec|Olmec]] fights by hopping toward the player and stopping in the air over him, to slam down on him.
The check that stops him treats left and right differently: drifting right, he stops over the player at once;
drifting left at half his top speed or more, he passes over him.

## What happens

Olmec hops and drifts left over the player without stopping, and stops to slam only once his drift has slowed.
On his next hop he drifts right and stops over the player at once <Cite clip="quirks/olmec-slam-drifting-left" />.

<Clip id="quirks/olmec-slam-drifting-left" />

## Why

In the air after a hop, Olmec's drift, `xVel`, goes up by 0.25 a step toward the player to 2 and back down
again <Cite gml="objects/oOlmec/step.gml:104-119" />. On every step he tests whether to stop and slam:

```gml objects/oOlmec/step.gml:121-129
        if ((not oPlayer1.active and yVel >= 0) or
            (oPlayer1.y > y and abs(oPlayer1.x - (x+32)) < 32 and xVel > -1))
        {
            status = PREPARE;
            yVel = 0;
            xVel = 0;
            myGrav = 0;
            counter = 20;
        }
```

The player has to be below Olmec's top and within 32 pixels of his middle, and `xVel` greater than -1. A drift
to the right always passes that last test, but a drift to the left of -1 or faster, half his top drift or more,
fails it, so he goes on over the player <Cite quirk="1" />.

## Reproducing it

Stand on Olmec's left as he hops: a fast drift toward the player carries him over without stopping. The
replay of a whole game with seed 1 shows both sides: on one hop he drifts left over the player for 8 steps, his
`xVel` -2 at the fastest, and stops only once it's back to -0.75. On his next he drifts right and stops over the
player at once, with an `xVel` of 2 <Cite replay="fidelity/replays/ending-seed-1.txt" />.

## In the port

The spelunky.ai port reproduces it exactly: it's in docs/quirks.md's list of the 1.1 source's bugs
<Cite quirk="1" />, and the oracle's fingerprint of the replay pins his drift and the step he stops. No option
turns it off.
