Each pellet takes <Stat id="shotgun.damage" /> from any monster it hits, whatever that monster's
own code, so a shot that lands whole takes 24. It waits <Stat id="shotgun.cooldown" /> between shots and
knocks the player back. Angry [[shopkeeper|Shopkeepers]] carry one and fire it at the player.
[[weapon-shop|Weapon Shops]] and the [[dice-house|Dice House]] sell it; a Shopkeeper
drops his, and [[crate|Crates]], buried blocks and Ash graves give it away.

## Usage

<Clip id="shotgun/shopkeeper" />

The Shotgun is picked up like any item, crouching over it and pressing the attack key. While it's held, the
attack key fires it, on the ground, in the air, hanging or climbing; crouching, the key puts it down in front
of the player instead <Cite gml="scripts/scrUseItem.gml:529-594" />. It isn't thrown. A shot:

- fires <Stat id="shotgun.pellets" /> pellets from the barrel, each at <Stat id="shotgun.pellet-speed" />,
  never slower than 6 forward, with an up or down drift of less than 1 pixel a step, so they spread;
- shows a blast at the barrel and plays the shot's sound;
- knocks the player 3 pixels a step back and 1 up, unless he's hanging or climbing;
- starts the wait of <Stat id="shotgun.cooldown" /> before the next <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:175" />.

A pellet flies in a straight line, without falling, until it hits a block (a puff of smoke), a monster, a
damsel or a player <Cite gml="objects/oBullet/step.gml:1-2" /> <Cite gml="objects/oBullet/collision_oSolid.gml:1-3" />.
In the clip, the player fires one shot into a wall and one into a Shopkeeper beside him: all six pellets
hit, and he goes from 20 hit points to -4 in a step, drops his own shotgun and
dies <Cite clip="shotgun/shopkeeper" />.

The Shotgun goes with the player through a level's exit if he's holding it, and is in his hands on the next
level <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:842-850" /> <Cite gml="objects/oLevel/create.gml:25-30" />. He
drops it when he's stunned, or when he teeters too long at a ledge's
edge <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:329-345" /> <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:150-171" />.

## Damage

The pellets' code is the pellet's own, not the monster's, so every monster takes the same: <Stat id="shotgun.damage" />
for each pellet, with no wait and whatever it's doing, stunned or
not <Cite gml="objects/oBullet/collision_oEnemy.gml:1-36" />. Each pellet that hits is used up.

| Monster | Each pellet | |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Every monster | <Stat id="shotgun.damage" />, and knocks it up and along | <Cite gml="objects/oBullet/collision_oEnemy.gml:10-16" /> |
| [[caveman\|Caveman]], [[yeti\|Yeti]], [[hawkman\|Hawkman]], [[shopkeeper\|Shopkeeper]] | 4, and stuns it for 20 steps if it's alive | <Cite gml="objects/oBullet/collision_oEnemy.gml:17-25" /> |
| [[yeti-king\|Yeti King]], [[tomb-lord\|Tomb Lord]] | 4, with half the knock | <Cite gml="objects/oBullet/collision_oEnemy.gml:3-7" /> |
| The player | 4 hearts, and stuns him | <Cite gml="objects/oBullet/collision_oCharacter.gml:1-21" /> |
| Damsels | 4, and stuns her | <Cite gml="objects/oBullet/collision_oDamsel.gml:1-23" /> |

The [[ghost|Ghost]] isn't a monster to the game's code, and pellets go through it. Spreading, the pellets of one shot can hit two monsters: in the second clip, one shot kills two of
Kali's [[spider|Spiders]] at once <Cite clip="shotgun/spiders" />.

## Interactions

- **[[shopkeeper|Shopkeepers]].** An angry Shopkeeper with no gun picks up any Shotgun he touches, even one
  in the player's hands <Cite gml="objects/oShopkeeper/collision_oShotgun.gml:1-14" />. Shot, he's stunned
  for 20 steps and drops his gun if he's carrying
  one <Cite gml="objects/oShopkeeper/step.gml:510-521" />.
- **[[jar|Jars]] and [[skull|Skulls]].** A pellet breaks either, as a throw
  does <Cite gml="objects/oJar/collision_oBullet.gml:1" /> <Cite gml="objects/oSkull/collision_oBullet.gml:1" />.

## Tips

- From beside a Shopkeeper, as in the clip, all six pellets hit and take his 20 hit points in one shot.
- Each shot pushes the player back 3 pixels a step, which can carry him off the edge he fires from.

## Trivia

- The Shotgun, the Pistol and the Shopkeepers' guns all fire the same pellet, whose code never asks who
  fired it <Cite gml="objects/oBullet/create.gml:1-4" />.
- The pellet's 5-step grace from hurting, `alarm[0] = 5`, is commented out, so it can hurt from its first
  step <Cite gml="objects/oBullet/create.gml:4" /> <Cite quirk="1" />.
