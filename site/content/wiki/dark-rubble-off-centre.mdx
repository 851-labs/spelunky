A [[block|block]] that breaks leaves three pieces of rubble around its middle. The [[ice-caves|Ice Caves]]' dark rock, and
the [[mothership|mothership]]'s hull, leave theirs off-centre, to the right of where the block stood and below
it, because of a typo in the line that places them: `rand(0-8)`, a minus where a comma should be.

## What happens

The player breaks a brick with a [[mattock|Mattock]], and its rubble falls where the brick stood. He jumps a pit
and breaks a block of the Ice Caves' dark rock: its rubble comes out to the right of the block and below it, and
falls past the block's edge <Cite clip="quirks/dark-rubble-off-centre" />.

<Clip id="quirks/dark-rubble-off-centre" />

## Why

A brick's destroy event puts each piece of its rubble at the block's middle, plus or minus up to 8 pixels:

```gml objects/oBrick/destroy.gml:5
    instance_create(x+8+rand(0,8)-rand(0,8), y+8+rand(0,8)-rand(0,8), oRubble);
```

The dark rock's has `rand(0-8)` in place of the second `rand(0,8)`:

```gml objects/oDark/destroy.gml:5-7
    instance_create(x+8+rand(0,8)-rand(0-8), y+8+rand(0,8)-rand(0-8), oRubbleDark);
    instance_create(x+8+rand(0,8)-rand(0-8), y+8+rand(0,8)-rand(0-8), oRubbleDarkSmall);
    instance_create(x+8+rand(0,8)-rand(0-8), y+8+rand(0,8)-rand(0-8), oRubbleDarkSmall);
```

`0-8` is one argument, -8. `rand` returns a whole number from its first argument to its second
<Cite gml="scripts/rand.gml:1-2" />, and the missing second is 0, so `rand(0-8)` is -8 to 0. Subtracted, it adds 0
to 8, and the rubble lands 8 to 24 pixels right of and below the block's corner, where `rand(0,8)-rand(0,8)`'s -8
to 8 centres it on the block. Both draw the same random numbers, so only the rubble's place differs. The
mothership's hull has the same line <Cite gml="objects/oAlienShip/destroy.gml:3-5" /> <Cite quirk="1" />.

## Reproducing it

Break any block of the Ice Caves' dark rock, with a bomb or the Mattock, and watch where its rubble starts. The
replay of the clip's staged scene, a custom level, has a brick and a block of dark rock each over a pit. The
brick's rubble starts over the brick's own place; the dark rock's starts 13 to 23 pixels right of its corner
and 16 to 19 below it, and falls past its right edge
<Cite replay="fidelity/replays/terrain-dark-rubble-off-centre.txt" />. In play, a bomb that breaks the hull of
the mothership leaves the same off-centre rubble <Cite replay="fidelity/replays/ice-seed-63-barrier-through.txt" />.

## In the port

The spelunky.ai port reproduces it exactly: it's in docs/quirks.md's list of the 1.1 source's bugs
<Cite quirk="1" />, and the oracle's fingerprint of the replay pins every piece's place. No option turns it off.
