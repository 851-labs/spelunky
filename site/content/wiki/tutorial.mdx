It's one cave, built in the room editor, that walks the player through the game's moves: running, jumping,
hanging, the whip, ducking and crawling, [[ladder|ladders]], carrying and throwing, [[rope|ropes]] and
[[bomb|bombs]], each taught by a [[sign|sign]] that shows its message while he stands at it. A [[snake|Snake]], a
[[bat|Bat]] and some treasure are there to try them on. Its exit ends the lesson: the game starts over, and
the next level is [[mines|the Mines]]' first.

## Generation

### Getting there

<Clip id="tutorial/door" />

The [[title-screen|title screen]] has a door to the Tutorial under a sign of its own, to the left of the START door
<Cite room="rTitle" />. Taking it starts the Tutorial as level 0, with no bombs and 2 ropes
<Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/other_animation_end.gml:87-95" />. In the clip, the player walks into the tutorial
door, arrives with 0 bombs and 2 ropes, and the first signs greet him <Cite clip="tutorial/door" />.

On the game's first run, when its high score table holds none of the statistics it keeps there, the game
counts the player as new <Cite gml="objects/oGlobals/create.gml:57-69" />. The title screen then bricks over
the tutorial door and its sign <Cite gml="objects/oTitle/create.gml:100-105" />, and the START door leads to
the Tutorial in its place, once <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/other_animation_end.gml:72-83" />
<Cite replay="fidelity/replays/movement-tutorial-exit.txt" />. Every later game started from START begins on
level 1.

### The cave

The room editor laid out the whole cave, 42 blocks wide and 19 high, with the player's start at its top
left beside a door back to the title screen <Cite room="rTutorial" />. [[level-generation|Level generation]]
adds no monsters, treasure, traps or shops to it <Cite gml="scripts/scrInitLevel.gml:197-198" />, but its
bricks are the Mines' and roll as they do anywhere, so some show gold and a few hide gems or items, a new
draw each time <Cite gml="objects/oBrick/create.gml:1-16" />. It's never a [[dark-level|dark level]]
<Cite gml="scripts/scrInitLevel.gml:169" />.

The way runs right along the upper cave, up a ladder to a high ledge, back down into the lower cave and
left along it to the exit at the bottom left. Its seventeen signs each show their message, both lines in
white, while the player touches them, and for 100 steps after he leaves: a sign sets the message's timer to
200, but on a lit level it runs down twice as fast ([[messages-halved-on-lit-levels]]) <Cite
gml="objects/oMsgSign/collision_oCharacter.gml:1-3" /> <Cite gml="objects/oScreen/step_begin.gml:245-256" />
<Cite quirk="1" row="Messages last half as long on a lit level." />. In the order the way passes them, with the default keys:

### Leaving

<Clip id="tutorial/exit" />

The exit at the bottom left leads to the screen between levels, headed "TUTORIAL CAVE COMPLETED!", which
starts a new game: it clears everything the Tutorial gave, the money, the bombs and ropes and the kills,
puts the hearts back, and sets the level to 1 <Cite gml="objects/oTransition/create.gml:20-24" />
<Cite gml="objects/oTransition/draw.gml:76" /> <Cite gml="scripts/scrClearGlobals.gml:103-167" />. The next
level is the Mines' first <Cite gml="objects/oTransition/step.gml:17-27" />. In the clip, the player takes
the exit with 3 hearts, $4,000 and 2 bombs, and the game goes on with no money and
<Stat id="bomb.start" bare /> bombs and <Stat id="rope.start" bare /> ropes <Cite clip="tutorial/exit" />.
The door beside the start leads back to the title screen instead, which also clears the game <Cite
gml="objects/oPlayer1/other_animation_end.gml:110-131" /> <Cite replay="fidelity/replays/flow-tutorial-title.txt" />.

## Hazards

The cave has no traps, whatever its last sign says <Cite room="rTutorial" />. What can hurt the player is
the Snake and the Bat, a long fall, as the flip hang's sign warns ([[damage#long-drops]]), and his own
bombs.

## Trivia

- The pause screen calls it "TUTORIAL CAVE", and works out its depth as for any level, 174.8 feet for
  each level past the first, so for level 0 the depth comes out below zero: −147.60 feet where the player
  starts, and still −82.32 on the lower cave's floor ([[tutorial-negative-depth]]) <Cite
  gml="objects/oScreen/step_begin.gml:55" /> <Cite gml="objects/oScreen/step_begin.gml:85-91" /> <Cite
  quirk="1" row="The Tutorial's pause screen shows a negative depth." />.
- Nothing done there counts toward the statistics the [[high-scores-room|high scores room]] keeps, as the game counts only
  its own levels <Cite gml="scripts/isRealLevel.gml:1-8" /> <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:563" />.
- An explosion's code means to let it break blocks off the screen only in the Tutorial, but its test,
  `isLevel("rTutorial")`, is true on every level, so explosions break blocks off the screen everywhere
  ([[explosions-break-blocks-off-screen]]) <Cite gml="objects/oExplosion/collision_oSolid.gml:1-2" />
  <Cite quirk="1" row="An explosion breaks blocks off the screen too." />.
