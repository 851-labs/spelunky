Fire Frogs are rare: level generation puts one on a block in [[jungle|the Jungle]] a quarter as often as a
[[frog|Frog]]. A Fire Frog moves exactly as a Frog does, leaping at the player once he's within
<Stat id="fire-frog.wake-range" />, and it dies to one hit, but whatever kills it leaves its burning body
behind, which explodes <Stat id="fire-frog.fuse" as="seconds" /> later. On a [[dark-level|dark level]], a Fire
Frog lights the ground around it.

## Behaviour

A Fire Frog's step event is a Frog's line for line, with only its sprites and what happens when it dies
changed <Cite gml="objects/oFireFrog/step.gml:1-93" /> <Cite gml="objects/oFrog/step.gml:1-93" />. It sits
still until the player is within <Stat id="fire-frog.wake-range" /> of it, then croaks and jumps at him, up at
2–4 pixels per step and across at <Stat id="fire-frog.jump-speed" />
(<Stat id="fire-frog.jump-speed" as="per-second" />), and after each landing it rests for
<Stat id="fire-frog.rest" /> (<Stat id="fire-frog.rest" as="seconds" />) before it jumps again
<Cite gml="objects/oFireFrog/step.gml:38-78" />. The [[frog|Frog]]'s page describes the jumps in full.

<Clip id="fire-frog/water" />

A Fire Frog that ends up in water turns into an ordinary Frog, in the same state it was in: the code most
monsters share replaces it with one as soon as its middle is in water
<Cite gml="objects/oEnemy/step.gml:34-50" />. The clip shows a Fire Frog leaping at the player across a pool,
falling in and coming out a Frog.

On a [[dark-level|dark level]], the game lights a circle of 32 pixels around each Fire Frog, as it does around
a lit trap or a torch <Cite gml="objects/oScreen/step_begin.gml:207-210" />. A Fire Frog only acts while it's
on screen or just off it <Cite gml="objects/oFireFrog/step.gml:3-4" />.

## Damage

When the player touches a Fire Frog without stomping it, he loses <Stat id="fire-frog.damage" />, is knocked
away from it, and blinks, invincible, for 30 steps (1 second), as with most monsters
<Cite gml="objects/oEnemy/collision_oCharacter.gml:14-25" />. Touching it doesn't burn him.

Its body is what's dangerous. Whatever kills a Fire Frog leaves a burning body where it was, which falls and
bounces like a [[throwing|thrown item]] and explodes <Stat id="fire-frog.fuse" />
(<Stat id="fire-frog.fuse" as="seconds" />) later <Cite gml="objects/oFireFrog/step.gml:13-23" />
<Cite gml="objects/oFireFrogBomb/create.gml:1-9" /> <Cite gml="objects/oFireFrogBomb/alarm_1.gml:1-12" />. The
explosion is a [[bomb|Bomb]]'s: it destroys the blocks around it, hurts the monsters near it, and takes 10 of
the player's hearts if he's caught in it <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:1614-1616" />.

<Clip id="fire-frog/explode" />

The clip shows the player whipping a Fire Frog as it leaps at him and backing away from its burning body,
which explodes four seconds later and blows a hole in the floor.

## Defeating

A Fire Frog has <Stat id="fire-frog.hp" />, so any damage kills it, and every way of killing it leaves the
exploding body <Cite gml="objects/oFireFrog/step.gml:13-23" />:

| Way | Damage | |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [[whip\|Whip]] | <Stat id="whip.damage" /> | <Cite gml="objects/oEnemy/collision_oWhip.gml:1" /> |
| [[machete\|Machete]] or [[mattock\|Mattock]] | <Stat id="machete.damage" /> | <Cite gml="objects/oEnemy/collision_oWhip.gml:1" /> |
| [[stomping\|Stomp]] (landing on it), except while the player is swimming | 1, plus 1 for every 16 steps of falling; three times that with [[spike-shoes\|Spike Shoes]] | <Cite gml="objects/oEnemy/collision_oCharacter.gml:6-10" /> |
| A thrown item moving faster than 2 pixels per step, such as a [[rock\|Rock]] | 1 | <Cite gml="objects/oItem/step.gml:256-348" /> |
| A [[shotgun\|Shotgun]] or [[pistol\|Pistol]] bullet | <Stat id="pistol.damage" /> | <Cite gml="objects/oBullet/collision_oEnemy.gml:16" /> |
| A [[bomb\|Bomb]]'s explosion | <Stat id="bomb.damage" /> | <Cite gml="objects/oExplosion/collision_oEnemy.gml:14" /> |
| A [[spear-trap\|Spear Trap]]'s spears | 2 on each step they strike it, 4 at most | <Cite gml="objects/oEnemy/step.gml:76-106" /> |
| Falling onto [[spikes\|Spikes]] | Kills it | <Cite gml="objects/oEnemy/step.gml:108-128" /> |
| Standing in [[lava\|Lava]] | Kills it | <Cite gml="objects/oEnemy/step.gml:65-74" /> |
| Ending up inside solid ground | Kills it | <Cite gml="objects/oFireFrog/step.gml:11" /> |

Only two things get rid of a Fire Frog without an explosion: water, which turns it into a Frog, and lava over
its head, which removes it outright <Cite gml="objects/oEnemy/step.gml:44-49" />
<Cite gml="objects/oEnemy/step.gml:63" />.

Fire Frogs killed count toward the player's kills, and the screen between levels draws a Fire Frog icon for
each <Cite gml="objects/oFireFrog/step.gml:15-20" /> <Cite gml="objects/oTransition/alarm_0.gml:179-184" />.
One that Spikes, Lava or a Spear Trap killed doesn't count <Cite gml="objects/oEnemy/step.gml:68" />
<Cite gml="objects/oEnemy/step.gml:101" /> <Cite gml="objects/oEnemy/step.gml:120" />.

## Locations

Level generation makes Fire Frogs only in [[jungle|the Jungle]], on top of blocks with open space above them,
outside the starting room and shops, and not on a [[restless-dead|graveyard level]]
<Cite gml="scripts/scrEntityGen.gml:262-296" />:

- **On land**, a 1 in 120 chance for each such block, once the rolls for a [[mantrap|Mantrap]] and a
  [[caveman|Caveman]] have failed <Cite gml="scripts/scrEntityGen.gml:285-291" />.
- **Under water**, where the block has water above it, the same 1 in 120 chance
  <Cite gml="scripts/scrEntityGen.gml:294" />. A Fire Frog made there is in water, so it turns into an
  ordinary Frog once it's near the screen <Cite gml="objects/oEnemy/step.gml:34-50" />.

Fire Frogs also come from:

- **[[olmec|Olmec]]'s yellow balls**, which turn into a [[bat|Bat]], [[spider|Spider]], [[snake|Snake]], Frog
  or Fire Frog when they hit the ground <Cite gml="objects/oYellowBall/step.gml:4-19" />.
- **Custom levels**, as the [[level-editor|level editor]]'s tile `F`
  <Cite gml="scripts/scrCreateTileObj.gml:160-163" />.

## Tips

- A Fire Frog's body explodes <Stat id="fire-frog.fuse" as="seconds" /> after any death, so killing one with
  the Whip or a stomp leaves the player beside a bomb.
- Killing one from a distance, with a thrown item or a bullet, keeps the explosion away from the player.
- A Fire Frog lured into water becomes a Frog, which leaves no explosion when it dies.

## Trivia

- The game has a second, older fire frog's body, `oFireFrogArmed`, with the same name and sprite but no fuse,
  which nothing in the game ever creates <Cite gml="objects/oFireFrogArmed/create.gml:3" />.
- The code most monsters share checks for a Fire Frog by its name to turn it into a Frog in water; no other
  monster has a check like it <Cite gml="objects/oEnemy/step.gml:44" />.
