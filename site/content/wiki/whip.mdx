The player has it from the start of every game and never loses it: it's what the attack key does when his
hands are empty. A swing hurts what's behind him as well as what's in front, and most monsters have a single
hit point, so it kills them outright; bigger ones take it a hit point a step. A few monsters' own code
changes what it does to them (Damage, below). The [[machete|Machete]] and the [[mattock|Mattock]] swing the
same way, harder.

## Usage

<Clip id="whip/crack" />

Pressing the attack key with empty hands, standing, walking, jumping or falling but not ducking, starts a
swing <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:1175-1202" />; with anything in his hands, the key uses or throws
that instead <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:1313-1319" />. A swing has two parts:

- **The wind-up**, 16 pixels behind the player, made on the swing's first steps; it lasts 3
  steps <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:524-533" /> <Cite gml="objects/oWhipPre/create.gml:3-5" />.
- **The crack**, 16 pixels in front of him, made once the swing's animation is past its fifth frame and kept
  until the swing ends <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:415-480" /> <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:535-539" />.

Both follow the player if he moves <Cite gml="objects/oWhip/step.gml:1-14" /> <Cite gml="objects/oWhipPre/step.gml:1-14" />. In play, the wind-up comes on
the step after the key is pressed and the crack 4 steps after that, for 7 steps. A new swing can't start
until the last one's animation has ended <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:1177" /> <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/other_animation_end.gml:1-5" />. The
clip shows both parts: two [[snake|Snakes]] close in from either side, and one swing kills both, the one
behind him first <Cite clip="whip/crack" />.

## Damage

Most monsters run the code their parent object shares, which takes the
whip's <Stat id="whip.damage" /> on every step the crack or the wind-up touches them, with no pause between
hits <Cite gml="objects/oEnemy/collision_oWhip.gml:1-8" /> <Cite gml="objects/oEnemy/collision_oWhipPre.gml:1-8" />.
A crack that stays on a [[megamouth|Megamouth]] for all 7 of its steps takes 7 hit
points, one of the [[whip-every-step|whip's hits on every step]] <Cite quirk="1" />. Monsters with code of their own:

| [[caveman\|Caveman]], [[hawkman\|Hawkman]], [[vampire\|Vampire]] | 1, and stuns it; nothing while it's stunned | The same | <Cite gml="objects/oCaveman/collision_oWhip.gml:1-17" /> <Cite gml="objects/oVampire/collision_oWhipPre.gml:1-13" /> |
| [[mantrap\|Mantrap]] | 1, and stuns it; nothing while it's stunned | The same | <Cite gml="objects/oManTrap/collision_oWhip.gml:1-20" /> |
| [[giant-spider\|Giant Spider]] | 1, at most once every 10 steps | 1 a step, with no wait | <Cite gml="objects/oGiantSpider/collision_oWhip.gml:1-7" /> <Cite gml="objects/oEnemy/collision_oWhipPre.gml:1" /> <Cite quirk="1" /> |

| [[yeti\|Yeti]] | Nothing: it knocks the Yeti back | Nothing: it knocks it back | <Cite gml="objects/oYeti/collision_oWhip.gml:22-30" /> <Cite gml="objects/oYeti/collision_oWhipPre.gml:22-30" /> |

The wind-up hurts the Giant Spider, the Tomb Lord, the Yeti King and the Magma Man because none of them has
code of its own for it, so they take the shared code's, which has no wait: with his back to a Giant Spider, a
swing takes 3 of its 10 hit points in 3 steps <Cite clip="giant-spider/wind-up" /> <Cite quirk="1" />.

## Interactions

- **[[shopkeeper|Shopkeepers]].** The crack or the wind-up doesn't hurt one, but knocks him back and makes
  him attack <Cite gml="objects/oShopkeeper/collision_oWhip.gml:1-19" /> <Cite replay="fidelity/replays/shop-seed-11-whip.txt" />.
- **[[jar|Jars]].** A crack breaks one, which may let out gold, a gem, a [[spider|Spider]] or a
  Snake <Cite gml="objects/oJar/collision_oWhip.gml:1-21" />.
- **[[damsel|Damsels]].** The crack or the wind-up makes a Damsel jump without hurting her, and she comes down
  as if thrown ([[damsel-whip-hop-thrown]]); it angers the Shopkeeper if she's for sale <Cite
  gml="objects/oDamsel/collision_oWhip.gml:13-23" /> <Cite gml="objects/oDamsel/collision_oWhipPre.gml:13-23" />.

## Tips

- With his back to a monster, the player still hits it: the wind-up is the only part of the Whip that
  hurts the Yeti King or the Magma Man.
- A Caveman, a Hawkman or a Vampire takes 1 a swing and is stunned by it, so a stunned one is better finished
  some other way.

## Trivia

- As the [[tunnel-man|Tunnel Man]], the swing makes a [[mattock|Mattock]]'s hit instead
  of the crack <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:432-446" />.
- The wind-up and the crack are both of type "Whip", and only the Machete's are "Machete": the code that
  treats the Machete differently tests that name <Cite gml="objects/oWhipPre/create.gml:3" /> <Cite gml="objects/oCaveman/collision_oWhip.gml:1" />.
