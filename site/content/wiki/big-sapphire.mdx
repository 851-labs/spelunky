It's the middle one of the three big gems, worth more than a [[big-emerald|Big Emerald]] and less than a
[[big-ruby|Big Ruby]], and three times a small [[sapphire|Sapphire]]. In the rock it's the commonest of them,
as each block rolls for it first. The player collects one by touching it; a [[ghost|Ghost]] that touches one
turns it into a [[diamond|Diamond]].

A Big Sapphire's base value is <Stat id="big-sapphire.value" />, and it's worth a quarter of that more for
each area past the Mines, the code counting [[olmecs-lair|Olmec's Lair]] as an area of its own
<Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:1996" /> <Cite gml="scripts/scrInitLevel.gml:24-29" />:

## Locations

### In the rock

<Clip id="big-sapphire/bomb" />

Each block of rock that isn't a gold vein (71 in 100 aren't) and isn't on the level's edge rolls for a big gem
inside it, the Big Sapphire first:

| Area | Chance for each block | |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [[mines\|The Mines]] | 1 in 100 | <Cite gml="objects/oBrick/create.gml:7-12" /> |
| [[jungle\|The Jungle]] | 1 in 80 | <Cite gml="objects/oLush/create.gml:5-11" /> |
| [[ice-caves\|The Ice Caves]] | 1 in 40 | <Cite gml="objects/oDark/create.gml:3-11" /> |
| [[temple\|The Temple]] and [[olmecs-lair\|Olmec's Lair]] | 1 in 60 | <Cite gml="objects/oTemple/create.gml:5-12" /> |

The [[tutorial|Tutorial]]'s bricks roll as the Mines' do <Cite gml="scripts/isLevel.gml:7" />. In the Temple,
one block of rock in 100 is the Jungle's earth, which rolls at the Jungle's odds, and one in ten a stone block,
which holds no gem <Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGen4.gml:317-322" /> <Cite gml="objects/oBlock/create.gml:1-4" />.
The [[city-of-gold|City of Gold]]'s temple blocks are gold instead, and don't roll for gems
<Cite gml="objects/oTemple/create.gml:7-10" />, but its blocks of earth still do ([[city-jungle-earth]]) <Cite quirk="1" row="One block in a hundred of the City of Gold is the Jungle's earth." />. A buried gem stays where it is, hidden behind the rock, until the block around it is
destroyed, by a [[bomb|Bomb]] or a [[mattock|Mattock]] for instance; then it drops out. With the
[[spectacles|Spectacles]] or the [[udjat-eye|Udjat Eye]], treasure is drawn in front of the rock, so the buried
gems show through it <Cite gml="objects/oTreasure/step.gml:63-67" />.

In the clip, the player picks up a pair of Spectacles in a cave of bricks, and a Big Sapphire buried in the
floor ahead shows through. He sets a Bomb on the floor above it and runs back; the explosion breaks the floor,
and he drops into the hole and collects the gem <Cite clip="big-sapphire/bomb" />.

## Collecting

The player collects a Big Sapphire by touching it while he isn't stunned or dead; the one he takes is the
treasure nearest him, one a step <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:1990-2016" />. It's made uncollectable,
and its own alarm, set to <Stat id="big-sapphire.collect-delay" />
(<Stat id="big-sapphire.collect-delay" as="seconds" />) when it's made, makes it collectable
<Cite gml="objects/oSapphireBig/create.gml:6-7" /> <Cite gml="objects/oSapphireBig/alarm_0.gml:1" />, so one
fresh out of a Jar, a Chest or a monster lies a moment before the player can take it.

Its value shows under his money and is counted into it after a short pause; whatever is left is counted at
once when he leaves the level <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:1953-1971" />
<Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/other_animation_end.gml:46-50" />. A gem plays its own sound, where gold plays the
coin's <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:2000-2014" />, and the screen between levels shows each Big
Sapphire collected on the level <Cite gml="objects/oTransition/alarm_0.gml:71-76" />.

A loose one falls and comes to rest like other treasure, moving only while it's on the screen; a [[web]] it
falls into holds it, and lava it sinks into destroys it <Cite gml="objects/oTreasure/step.gml:1-79" />
<Cite gml="objects/oWeb/collision_oTreasure.gml:1-2" />.

### The Ghost

<Clip id="diamond/ghost" />

A [[ghost|Ghost]] that touches a Big Sapphire turns it into a [[diamond|Diamond]], in the same place
<Cite gml="objects/oSapphireBig/collision_oGhost.gml:1-2" />. The Ghost drifts through walls, so it does this
to gems still buried in the rock too <Cite replay="fidelity/replays/item-seed-2-diamond.txt" />
<Cite quirk="1" row="The rare items' only sources" />. In the clip, a Ghost drifting toward the player passes over a Big Sapphire lying in a
nook, and the sapphire becomes a Diamond <Cite clip="diamond/ghost" />.

## Trivia

- Only the three big gems have code for a Ghost's touch, so a Ghost passes over the small gems, gold and a
  Diamond and leaves them as they are <Cite gml="objects/oSapphireBig/collision_oGhost.gml:1-2" />.
