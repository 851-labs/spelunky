It's the plainest thing the player can carry: it does nothing in his hands, and thrown, it hurts what it
hits and keeps going, then lies where it stops, ready to be picked up again. Level generation scatters Rocks
in every area, one on a block in a hundred.

## Usage

<Clip id="snake/thrown-rock" />

Crouching over a Rock with empty hands and pressing the attack key picks it up: the item the player lifts is
the one nearest him, of any kind <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:1203-1214" /> <Cite quirk="1" />. He
carries it in front of him <Cite gml="objects/oItem/step.gml:37-61" />, and the attack key throws it, at 8
pixels a step plus his own speed, rising at 3; holding up throws it at 9 upward, and holding down tosses it
low along the floor at six tenths of the speed if he's on the ground, or throws it down at 3 if he
isn't <Cite gml="scripts/scrUseItem.gml:605-646" />. For its first 10 steps it can't hurt him
<Cite gml="scripts/scrUseItem.gml:606-607" /> <Cite gml="objects/oItem/alarm_2.gml:1" />.

In flight it falls 0.6 pixels a step faster each step, up to 8, bounces back off a wall at half its speed,
and bounces off the floor at half its speed if it lands faster than 1, losing seven tenths of its speed
across on each step it touches the floor <Cite gml="objects/oItem/step.gml:84-111" />. It moves only while
it's within 16 pixels of the screen <Cite gml="objects/oItem/step.gml:1-3" /> <Cite quirk="1" />.

While it moves faster than 2 pixels a step and touches a monster, it takes <Stat id="rock.damage" /> from
the monster nearest it and gives the monster three tenths of its speed, and flies on; a
Caveman, a Hawkman, a Mantrap, a Yeti or a Vampire is also stunned, and a few monsters have rules of their
own (each monster's page has them) <Cite gml="objects/oItem/step.gml:256-350" />. The clip shows a Rock
thrown down from a ledge killing a [[snake|Snake]] <Cite clip="snake/thrown-rock" />. A Damsel it hits
loses 1 hit point, which angers the [[shopkeeper|Shopkeeper]] if she's for
sale <Cite gml="objects/oItem/step.gml:365-398" />.

A Rock flying faster than 4 pixels a step across that hits the player, once its safe steps are over, takes
2 hearts, knocks him its way and stuns him for 20 steps <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:1538-1565" />.

## Locations

- **Level generation**, in every area: each block outside a shop with open space above it, more than 32
  pixels from the entrance and the exit and 64 from a [[golden-idol|golden idol]], has a 1 in 100 chance of a Rock on
  it <Cite gml="scripts/scrTreasureGen.gml:27-44" />.
- **The idol's boulder.** A bomb that blows up the [[boulder]] a golden idol sets rolling leaves rubble and,
  for each of three pieces, a 1 in 3 chance of a Rock <Cite gml="objects/oExplosion/collision_oBoulder.gml:1-6" />.
- **The [[tutorial|Tutorial]]**, which has one <Cite room="rTutorial" />.
- **The [[level-editor|level editor]]**'s `r` tile <Cite gml="scripts/scrCreateTileObj.gml:114-117" />.

## Interactions

- **[[pitchers-mitt|Pitcher's Mitt]].** Thrown from the hands, a Rock leaves 6 pixels a step faster across
  and nearly flat <Cite gml="scripts/scrUseItem.gml:667-676" />. A fast Rock that would hit a player
  wearing it with empty hands is caught instead, and the code's catch takes every Rock near the screen into
  his hands at once <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:1543-1548" /> <Cite quirk="1" />.
- **[[bomb|Bombs]].** An explosion adds 6 pixels a step to a Rock's speed up or down and 4 to 6 across,
  away from it <Cite gml="objects/oExplosion/collision_oItem.gml:30-36" />, which can be fast enough to hurt
  monsters, or the player.
- **[[lava|Lava]].** A Rock that touches lava stops and sinks, and is destroyed once it's under the
  surface <Cite gml="objects/oItem/step.gml:160-185" />.
- **Exits.** Carried through a level's exit, it's in the player's hands on the next
  level <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:842-850" /> <Cite gml="objects/oLevel/create.gml:25-30" />.

## Tips

- A Rock never breaks, so one Rock can be thrown at monster after monster.
- A Rock thrown at a wall comes back at half its speed: one thrown hard enough can come back fast enough to
  hurt the player once its 10 safe steps are over.
- A thrown Rock hurts the monster nearest it, which isn't always the one it touched, when two are close
  together.

## Trivia

- The Rock's own object sets only its name and size <Cite gml="objects/oRock/create.gml:3-5" />: its
  flight and its hits on monsters are the code all items share, and only the player's code singles it out,
  for the hit a fast Rock does him and the Mitt's catch <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:1538-1565" />.
