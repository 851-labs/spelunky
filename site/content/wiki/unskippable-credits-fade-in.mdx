The [[credits|credits]] can be cut short with Enter, Escape or the attack key, which fade them out. The code means
a key pressed while they're still fading in to end the fade-in at once instead. But it never does: until the
camel comes, about a second in, every key is ignored.

## What happens

After the win's tally, the credits fade in on the desert. Attack, Enter and Escape are pressed as they fade in,
and attack once more before the camel comes, and none of them does anything: the fade-in ends on its own, and the
caravan rides in <Cite clip="quirks/credits-fade-unskippable" />.

<Clip id="quirks/credits-fade-unskippable" />

## Why

The credits' step reads the three keys, but only does something with them once the camel exists. Inside that, a
press during the fade-in would end it:

```gml objects/oCredits2/step.gml:1-10
if (keyboard_check_pressed(vk_enter) or
    keyboard_check_pressed(vk_escape) or
    checkAttack())
{
    if (instance_exists(oCamel))
    {
        if (fadeIn)
        {
            fadeLevel = 0;
        }
```

The camel comes from the credits' alarm 0 <Cite gml="objects/oCredits2/alarm_0.gml:1" />, which only the end of
the fade-in sets, for 20 steps later <Cite gml="objects/oCredits2/step.gml:35-43" />. So while the credits are
fading in there's never a camel, and the line that would end the fade-in never runs <Cite quirk="1" />. The keys
do nothing for the first 30 steps or so, 11 of fading and 20 of waiting, and after that a press fades the credits
out. The night credits, from the title screen's rope, do the same
<Cite gml="objects/oCredits1/step.gml:1-16" /> <Cite gml="objects/oCredits1/step.gml:34-42" />.

## Reproducing it

Press Enter, Escape or the attack key as the credits start, after a win or from the title screen's rope. It
happens to anyone who tries to skip them straight away. In the replay of the clip, the credits start on frame
6838, and attack (6841), Enter (6844) and Escape (6847) during the fade-in, and attack (6858) after it, change
nothing: the fade-in ends on its own on 6849, the camel comes on 6869, and the credits only fade out when attack is
pressed again, on 8383 <Cite replay="fidelity/replays/ending-seed-1.txt" />.

## In the port

The spelunky.ai port reproduces it exactly: it's in docs/quirks.md's list of the 1.1 source's bugs
<Cite quirk="1" />, and the oracle's fingerprint of the replay pins the fade-in's end and the camel. No option
turns it off.
