The [[high-scores-room|high scores room]] gives a trophy for the best time: bronze, silver or gold. The
bronze one is meant for a time of 16:00 or less, but the check that makes the trophy at all asks for less than
16:00, so a best time of exactly 16:00 leaves the trophy's place empty. One second faster, and the bronze trophy is
there.

## What happens

The only win recorded took 16:00. The player walks from the [[title-screen|title screen]] into the scores door: the panel shows
TIME: 16:00, and the stand for the time's trophy has nothing on it <Cite clip="quirks/highscores-time-960" />.

<Clip id="quirks/highscores-time-960" />

## Why

When the high scores room is made, it makes the time's trophy only for a best time above 0 and under 960 seconds,
then picks its colour, starting with bronze for a time of 960 or less:

```gml objects/oHighscores/create.gml:50-56
if (tTime > 0 and tTime < 960)
{
    trophy = instance_create(64, 64, oTrophy);
    if (tTime <= 960)
    {
        trophy.sprite_index = sBronzeTrophy;
    }
```

The two tests disagree at 960 exactly: the inner one would give it the bronze trophy, but the outer one never
lets it get there <Cite quirk="1" />. The silver (720 or less) and gold (600 or less) tests after it agree with
the outer one <Cite gml="objects/oHighscores/create.gml:57-65" />. The time is stored in whole seconds: at the
end of a won game, the clock is rounded down to the second before it's compared with the best time
<Cite gml="scripts/scrUpdateHighscores.gml:54-62" />, so any win from 16:00.000 to 16:00.999 is a 960.

## Reproducing it

Win a game in 16:00, without a shortcut, as the best time recorded, and enter the high scores room. It happens in normal play, but
rarely: the best time has to land on that second. The replay of the clip starts from a high scores table with
one win at 16:00 and nothing else, and walks into the scores door: the room loads (frame 546) with a best time of
960 and makes no trophy at all <Cite replay="fidelity/replays/flow-highscores-time-960-no-trophy.txt" />. With
959, the bronze trophy is made.

## In the port

The spelunky.ai port reproduces it exactly: it's in docs/quirks.md's list of the 1.1 source's bugs
<Cite quirk="1" />, and the oracle's fingerprint of the replay pins the empty stand. No option turns it off.
