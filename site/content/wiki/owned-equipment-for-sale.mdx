Equipment in Spelunky Classic is worn for the rest of the game, so having one of each is all there is. But a
shop doesn't know what the player has: the [[general-store|General Store]], the [[clothing-shop|Clothing Shop]]
and the [[rare-shop|Rare Shop]] can all sell him a second [[compass|Compass]], pair of Spectacles or Gloves, and
paying for it spends the money on nothing.

## What happens

The player got a Compass from [[kalis-favour|Kali]] on level 9, and wears it. Level 10's General Store sells
another, and when he picks it up, the Shopkeeper prices it: "A COMPASS FOR $5400."
<Cite clip="quirks/shop-compass-restocked" />.

<Clip id="quirks/shop-compass-restocked" />

## Why

Each shop picks its wares at random, and for a piece of equipment it only checks that the level has no
instance of it yet:

```gml scripts/scrShopItemsGen.gml:169-172
        else if (rand(1,10) == 1)
        {
            if (not instance_exists(oCompass)) { obj = instance_create(xpos+8, ypos+10, oCompass); break; }
        }
```

That keeps a level to one of each, but it never looks at `global.hasCompass`, or any of the other flags that say
what the player wears <Cite quirk="1" />. The Clothing Shop's and the Rare Shop's equipment are checked the same
way <Cite gml="scripts/scrShopItemsGen.gml:68-175" />. Paying for a ware takes the money and runs the same code as
carrying it out <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:1377-1384" />, and for equipment, that only sets its flag,
which is already set <Cite gml="scripts/scrStealItem.gml:126-170" />. Kali's gift does it right: it skips the
equipment the player has <Cite gml="scripts/scrGetFavorMsg.gml:85-98" />.

## Reproducing it

Get a piece of equipment, then find a shop on a later level that stocks the same one. It happens in normal play,
on any level after the one the equipment came from. The replay of the clip gets a Compass from Kali on level 9
(frame 1111), and level 10's General Store stocks another (1961). The player picks it up, and the Shopkeeper
offers it for $5,400, though he wears one (2028); he hasn't the money to buy it
<Cite replay="fidelity/replays/shop-seed-11-compass-restocked.txt" />.

## In the port

The spelunky.ai port reproduces it exactly: it's in docs/quirks.md's list of the 1.1 source's bugs
<Cite quirk="1" />, and the oracle's fingerprint of the replay pins the second Compass. No option turns it off.
