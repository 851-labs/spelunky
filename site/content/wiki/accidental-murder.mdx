Monsters killed by traps don't count as the player's kills: [[spikes|Spikes]] and the
[[spear-trap|Spear Trap]] mark them so. The [[shopkeeper|Shopkeeper]]'s death never reads that mark. However
he dies, it adds to the player's kills and makes him a murderer, which lasts the rest of the game.

## What happens

A thief who stole a cape waits on a floor past a hole with Spikes in it. The angry Shopkeeper chasing him
jumps onto a block and walks off it into the hole. The Spikes kill him, yet the game counts him as the
player's kill and marks the player a murderer <Cite clip="quirks/shopkeeper-spikes-murderer" />.

<Clip id="quirks/shopkeeper-spikes-murderer" />

## Why

The Shopkeeper runs the code most monsters share first, where landing on Spikes takes all his hit points and
sets `countsAsKill` false, as for any monster <Cite gml="objects/oEnemy/step.gml:108-124" />. A monster's own
death reads it before it counts a kill, as the Caveman's does <Cite gml="objects/oCaveman/step.gml:176" />.
The Shopkeeper's doesn't <Cite gml="objects/oShopkeeper/step.gml:479-503" />:

```gml objects/oShopkeeper/step.gml:487-492
        else
        {
            if (isRealLevel()) global.enemyKills[19] += 1;
            global.shopkeepers += 1;
            global.kills += 1;
            global.murderer = true;
```

The player is made a murderer when a Shopkeeper is crushed or found inside a solid too, and those don't ask
what did it either
<Cite gml="objects/oShopkeeper/step.gml:24-68" />. Only the Stars bonus room's Shopkeepers are spared, where a
death counts toward the room's score instead. A murderer stays one until a new game: every Shopkeeper patrols
and shouts "YOU'LL PAY FOR YOUR CRIMES!", every ware is free, and each level's exit has a Shopkeeper waiting
by it <Cite gml="scripts/scrInitLevel.gml:245-257" /> <Cite quirk="1" />.

## Reproducing it

Anger a Shopkeeper and lead him over Spikes, or die with a lit bomb beside him. The replays pin both: a thief
luring an angry Shopkeeper into a hole with Spikes in it, where his hit points go and the kill, the
Shopkeeper tally and the murderer mark all come on that step
<Cite replay="fidelity/replays/shop-seed-35-spikes-murderer.txt" />, and a bomb the dead player left killing
the Shopkeeper <Cite replay="fidelity/replays/shop-seed-11-bomb.txt" />. A Tomb Lord the lava kills, by
contrast, isn't counted <Cite replay="fidelity/replays/temple-seed-113.txt" />.

## In the port

The spelunky.ai port reproduces it exactly: it's in docs/quirks.md's list of the 1.1 source's bugs
<Cite quirk="1" />, and the oracle's fingerprints of the replays pin the tallies. The Spear Trap's version is
read in the code and not yet in a replay. No option turns it off.
