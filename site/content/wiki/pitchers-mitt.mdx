Wearing it, the player throws harder: whatever he throws from his hands, a [[rock|Rock]], a [[jar|Jar]], a
held [[bomb|Bomb]], leaves <Stat id="pitchers-mitt.throw-boost" /> faster across and, unless he aims up or
down, almost level. With empty hands, a rock flying into him doesn't hurt him. [[clothing-shop|Clothing Shops]] and
[[rare-shop|Rare Shops]] sell it, and [[crate|Crates]], blocks and Kali give it away. It's worn from the
moment it's picked up or paid for until the game ends.

## Effect

Picking up a free mitt, paying for one or carrying one out of a shop puts it on: the message "YOU GOT A
PITCHER'S MITT!" shows, the mitt disappears, and an icon of it joins the row under the hearts
<Cite gml="scripts/scrStealItem.gml:149-159" /> <Cite gml="scripts/scrDrawHUD.gml:135-139" />. It stays on
until a new game starts, which clears it <Cite gml="scripts/scrClearGlobals.gml:99" />
<Cite gml="objects/oTransition/create.gml:20-24" />.

### Throwing

An item thrown from the hands (the attack key, holding anything that isn't a weapon) normally leaves at 8
pixels per step across, 4 if it's heavy, plus the player's own speed, and 3 pixels per step upward, 2 if
heavy; 9 upward when he holds up (4 if heavy), and 3 downward when he holds down in the air
<Cite gml="scripts/scrUseItem.gml:595-646" />. With the mitt, unless he's crouching, the item gets
<Stat id="pitchers-mitt.throw-boost" /> more across, so a light one thrown standing still leaves at 14 (420
per second). Holding neither up nor down, it starts almost level, at 0.4 pixels per step upward; holding
down in the air, it goes down at 6
<Cite gml="scripts/scrUseItem.gml:667-674" />. The throw that a low ceiling ahead turns flat, without the
mitt, isn't changed with it <Cite gml="scripts/scrUseItem.gml:647-665" />.

<Clip id="pitchers-mitt/rock" />

The mitt also sets the item's gravity to 0.1, a sixth of the usual 0.6, but the item's own step puts it back
to 0.6 on every step it isn't in [[lava|Lava]], so the lighter gravity lasts a single step
<Cite gml="scripts/scrUseItem.gml:675" /> <Cite gml="objects/oItem/step.gml:160-170" /> <Cite quirk="1" />.
Thrown straight ahead from the ground, then, an item flies fast and flat and meets the floor soon. A
[[jar|Jar]], a [[skull|Skull]] and a die have movement code of their own, which only changes their gravity in
lava, so they keep the 0.1 for good: thrown the same way, a Jar flies flat across the room, and it falls
slowly from then on <Cite gml="objects/oJar/step.gml:27-30" /> <Cite gml="objects/oJar/step.gml:81-86" />
<Cite replay="fidelity/replays/item-mitt-gravity-one-step.txt" />. The clip shows a Rock
thrown along a corridor twice: without the mitt, it leaves at 8 pixels per step, arcs, and rolls to a stop
about 100 pixels away; with it, at 14, skimming the floor, and stops about 60 pixels away
<Cite clip="pitchers-mitt/rock" />. Thrown upward, the item follows nearly the same arc as without the mitt,
6 pixels a step faster across, so it carries far; a Jar, a Skull or a die, a far flatter one.

<Clip id="pitchers-mitt/throw" />

The second clip shows a Jar thrown up and to the right with the mitt: it leaves at more than 13 pixels per
step and, falling at a sixth of the usual rate all the way, crosses the cave to break on the rock above
<Cite clip="pitchers-mitt/throw" />. Bombs thrown with
the bomb key, rather than from the hands, leave at the usual 8 pixels per step
<Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:1137-1174" />.

### Catching

A Rock moving faster than 4 pixels per step across that touches the player (and isn't one he has just
thrown) costs him 2 hearts, knocks him back and stuns him. With the mitt and nothing in his hands, none of
that happens, and the code puts the Rock object in his hands instead, not the rock that hit him
<Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:1538-1565" />. So every Rock near the screen is held: the ones lying on
the floor fly into his hands too, and his next throw throws them all together, one on top of the other
<Cite quirk="1" /> <Cite replay="fidelity/replays/item-mitt-catch-every-rock.txt" />. Only rocks are caught: a
fast arrow still hurts <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:1513-1536" />.

## Interactions

- A thrown item hurts a monster it hits by the same amount whatever its speed, so the mitt adds speed and
  reach, not damage; the monster is knocked back with 0.3 of the item's speed across, so harder
  <Cite gml="objects/oItem/step.gml:256-363" />.
- A crouching throw, which sets the item down gently, isn't changed by the mitt
  <Cite gml="scripts/scrUseItem.gml:637-644" /> <Cite gml="scripts/scrUseItem.gml:667" />.

## Tips

- To throw far with the mitt, throw upward: a level throw from the ground stops sooner than one without it.
- A level throw reaches a monster at the player's height in a few steps, before it can move far.
