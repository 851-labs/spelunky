A [[skeleton|Skeleton]] faces the player when it's made, and walks toward him. It can only do that if the player
is there yet, and in a custom level ([[level-editor|the level editor]]'s) he isn't: the level's tiles come first.
So a custom level's Skeleton faces right, wherever the player is.

## What happens

A Skeleton placed in a custom level walks right, away from the player, until it reaches the wall: it's made
before the player, so it doesn't turn to face him <Cite clip="quirks/skeleton-faces-right" />.

<Clip id="quirks/skeleton-faces-right" />

## Why

A Skeleton's create event faces it right, and turns it left only if there's a player, left of its middle:

```gml objects/oSkeleton/create.gml:25-31
LEFT = 0;
RIGHT = 1;
facing = RIGHT;
if (instance_exists(oPlayer1))
{
    if (oPlayer1.x < x+8) facing = LEFT;
}
```

Its walk turns only at a wall <Cite gml="objects/oSkeleton/step.gml:48-61" />. A custom level makes its tiles
first, the level editor's `K` among them <Cite gml="scripts/scrCreateTileObj.gml:143-146" />, and the player after
them, at its entrance <Cite gml="scripts/scrLoadLevel.gml:100" /> <Cite gml="scripts/scrLoadLevel.gml:162-173" />.
So when a tile's Skeleton is made there's no player, and it keeps facing right. In the generated levels, Skeletons
only rise from piles of bones while the player is there, and turn to him <Cite quirk="1" />.

## Reproducing it

Make a custom level with a `K` right of the player's entrance, and play it: the Skeleton walks right, away from
him. The level editor's test does the same. The replay of the clip's staged scene, on a custom level, has a
Skeleton right of the player: it starts walking right, away from him, and turns only at the wall
<Cite replay="fidelity/replays/enemy-skeleton-tile-faces-right.txt" />.

## In the port

The spelunky.ai port reproduces it exactly: it's in docs/quirks.md's list of the 1.1 source's bugs
<Cite quirk="1" />, and the oracle's fingerprint of the replay pins the Skeleton's facing. No option turns it off.
