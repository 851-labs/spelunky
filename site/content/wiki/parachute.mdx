Wearing one, the player doesn't have to do anything: on a fall long enough to hurt, it opens over him, and
he floats down slowly enough to land unhurt. It works once, and then it's gone.
[[general-store|General Stores]] sell it, and [[crate|Crates]] and buried blocks give it away. It isn't worn for good: the
player carries it from the moment it's picked up or paid for until it opens.

## Effect

<Clip id="parachute/open" />

Picking up a free parachute, paying for one or carrying one out of a shop takes it: the message "YOU GOT A
PARACHUTE! IT WILL DEPLOY AUTOMATICALLY." shows, the pack disappears, and an icon of it joins the row under
the hearts <Cite gml="scripts/scrStealItem.gml:115-125" /> <Cite gml="scripts/scrDrawHUD.gml:165-169" />. A
new game takes it away if it's still unopened <Cite gml="scripts/scrClearGlobals.gml:91" />
<Cite gml="objects/oTransition/create.gml:20-24" />.

It opens when the player has been falling for 15 steps (half a second), isn't stunned, and has nothing solid
32 pixels below him. Opening uses it up: the icon goes, and a new fall needs a new parachute
<Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:254-267" />. A fall that long would otherwise end badly: landing after
more than 16 steps of falling stuns the player and costs him 1 heart, after more than 32 steps 2, and after
more than 48 steps 10 <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:268-289" />.

While it's open, it hangs over the player and follows him <Cite gml="objects/oParachute/step.gml:1-5" />,
his falling speed is halved each step <Cite gml="scripts/characterStepEvent.gml:867-871" />, and the count
of steps he's been falling starts again from 0 on every step, so the landing never hurts
<Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:252" />. With gravity adding 1 pixel a step and the parachute halving the
total, he settles at 1 pixel a step (30 per second), against up to 10 in a free fall
<Cite gml="scripts/characterCreateEvent.gml:59-62" />. The clip shows him running off a ledge at the top of a
deep drop: after 15 steps the parachute opens, he slows from 10 pixels a step to 1, and he lands without
losing a heart <Cite clip="parachute/open" />.

The parachute comes off when the fall ends: when the player lands, grabs a ledge or a [[rope|rope]], or climbs, and
when he's stunned or killed <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:290-298" />
<Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:1738-1744" />. It drops away as a limp canopy, which falls until it
lands <Cite gml="objects/oParaUsed/step.gml:1-10" />.

## Interactions

- A second parachute adds nothing: the player has one or none, and picking up another while he has one
  only sets it again, though shops go on selling them to him <Cite gml="scripts/scrStealItem.gml:115-117" />
  <Cite quirk="1" />.
- An item touching the open parachute while it moves right or down tears it away, and the player falls
  freely again; one moving left or up doesn't <Cite gml="objects/oParachute/collision_oItem.gml:1-5" />.
- The [[cape|Cape]] slows a fall the same way, halving it each step, but it lasts and is opened by hand
  <Cite gml="scripts/characterStepEvent.gml:867-875" />.

## Tips

- A parachute only opens with 32 pixels of open air below the player, so it saves him on a deep drop, not
  on a short one onto a ledge.
- Its one use goes on the first long fall, wanted or not: dropping down a shaft spends it.
