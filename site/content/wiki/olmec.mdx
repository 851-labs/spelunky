Olmec waits at the end of the game, on level 16, [[olmecs-lair|Olmec's Lair]], above a sea of lava, with a
Hawkman and Cavemen bowing before him. He is four blocks wide and tall, and he isn't a monster as the game
counts them but a moving block of stone: nothing the player carries can hurt him, and his only attack is to
fall on the player. The way to beat him is to make him dig: every slam breaks the floor he lands on, and when
he breaks through into the lava he sinks, and the exit opens.

## Behaviour

The fight has its phases: the awakening, then hops and slams, with yellow balls between them, until he
reaches the lava.

### Awakening

<Clip id="olmec/awakening" />

When the player arrives, he can't move <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/create.gml:102" />. The view pans right
across the lair, 2 pixels a step, to Olmec <Cite gml="objects/oOlmec/step.gml:51-62" />. 100 steps after
it stops, Olmec's stone cracks: the [[hawkman|Hawkman]] bowing to him is flung away, stunned and with 1 hit
point, and the bowing [[caveman|Cavemen]] freeze <Cite gml="objects/oOlmec/alarm_1.gml:1-27" />. Two more
cracks, 50 steps apart, bare his golden face, and 50 steps after that he hops for the first time, with an
alarm <Cite gml="objects/oOlmec/alarm_2.gml:1-9" /> <Cite gml="objects/oOlmec/alarm_3.gml:1-9" />
<Cite gml="objects/oOlmec/alarm_4.gml:1-5" />. 20 steps into that hop, the Cavemen get up and charge
<Cite gml="objects/oOlmec/alarm_6.gml:1-7" />. The hop ends in a slam, and 50 steps after it, the view goes
back to the player, who can move, and the boss music starts <Cite gml="objects/oOlmec/step.gml:121-129" />
<Cite gml="objects/oOlmec/step.gml:156-176" /> <Cite gml="objects/oOlmec/alarm_5.gml:1-8" />. The clip shows
the whole awakening, 437 steps (about 15 seconds) from the player's arrival to his first move
<Cite clip="olmec/awakening" />.

### Hops and slams

<Clip id="olmec/slam" />

Olmec covers twice the speeds his code sets: the game moves every moving block by its speed each step, and
Olmec's own step then moves him by it again <Cite gml="scripts/gameStepEvent.gml:23-233" />
<Cite gml="objects/oOlmec/step.gml:8" /> <Cite quirk="1" />. He rests on the ground, 100 steps after the
awakening, 40–100 steps after a hop that lands without a slam and 60 after a slam, then hops: he leaves the
ground at 8 pixels per step (240 per second) upward, twice the <Stat id="olmec.hop-speed" /> his code sets,
and gravity brings him down <Cite gml="objects/oOlmec/alarm_5.gml:6-7" /> <Cite gml="objects/oOlmec/step.gml:68-79" />
<Cite gml="objects/oOlmec/step.gml:92-144" />. For the first 10 steps of a hop he rises straight up; then,
if the player is past either side of him, he drifts toward him, his code's speed rising by 0.25 a step to
<Stat id="olmec.drift-speed" /> and falling again, so he covers up to 4 pixels per step (120 per second)
<Cite gml="objects/oOlmec/step.gml:102-119" />. He stops at walls, and a hop that hits the ceiling breaks the
blocks above him <Cite gml="objects/oOlmec/step.gml:15-33" />.

If, while he's in the air, the player is lower than his top and within 32 pixels of his middle, Olmec stops dead for
<Stat id="olmec.hover-time" /> and then slams down at 10 pixels per step (300 per second), twice the
<Stat id="olmec.slam-speed" /> his code sets, gaining speed up to 12 (360 per second) as he falls
<Cite gml="objects/oOlmec/step.gml:121-129" /> <Cite gml="objects/oOlmec/step.gml:145-155" />. The
check skips a slam while he's drifting left at half his top speed or more, so he passes over the player, but
not right, where he stops at any speed <Cite gml="objects/oOlmec/step.gml:121-122" /> <Cite quirk="1" />. When a slam lands, it shakes the screen and breaks every
block he lands on <Cite gml="objects/oOlmec/step.gml:156-166" />
<Cite gml="objects/oOlmecSlam/collision_oSolid.gml:1-7" />, so he drops through to whatever is below. The
clip shows Olmec hopping toward the player, stopping in the air over him, and slamming down as he runs clear,
breaking the floor <Cite clip="olmec/slam" />.

A player standing on top of Olmec rides along with him <Cite gml="objects/oOlmec/step.gml:3-7" />
<Cite gml="objects/oOlmec/step.gml:48-49" />.

### Yellow balls

Once a slam has landed, Olmec has a 1 in 2 chance of spitting three yellow balls before he rests
<Cite gml="objects/oOlmec/step.gml:167-176" /> <Cite gml="objects/oOlmec/step.gml:80-91" />. Each flies off
2–5 pixels a step to either side and 4–7 upward, falling as it goes <Cite gml="objects/oYellowBall/create.gml:3-8" />
<Cite gml="objects/oYellowBall/step.gml:1-22" />. Where a ball hits a wall, floor or ceiling, it turns into a
monster: a [[bat|Bat]], [[spider|Spider]], [[snake|Snake]] or [[frog|Frog]], each a 9 in 40 chance, or a
[[fire-frog|Fire Frog]], 1 in 10 (the game rolls a 1 in 2 chance between picking from the first four and
from all five) <Cite gml="objects/oYellowBall/step.gml:4-20" />. A ball that leaves the room is gone
<Cite gml="objects/oYellowBall/other_outside.gml:1" />. The balls don't hurt the player. In the clip above,
three balls land as a Snake, a Bat and a Frog <Cite clip="olmec/slam" />.

## Damage

<Clip id="olmec/crush" />

Olmec's only attack is his weight. A player who ends up inside him, when he slams down on him, is crushed:
he dies with however many hearts he had, and the death is counted against Olmec, as any crush in his lair is
<Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:1490-1505" /> <Cite quirk="1" />. The clip shows a player who stands still under Olmec
crushed with all four hearts <Cite clip="olmec/crush" />. Touching Olmec otherwise does nothing: he's a
block the player can stand on.

Even a crush Olmec has no part in is counted against him. When he dies, a block of stone appears under the
lair's door if the floor there is gone <Cite gml="objects/oFinalBoss/step.gml:8-12" />, so a player who has
blown it out with a bomb and stands in its place is crushed by the exit's own block, and the death is still
Olmec's <Cite quirk="1" /> <Cite replay="fidelity/replays/olmec-door-block-crush-seed-1.txt" /> <Cite clip="quirks/olmec-door-block-crush" />.

The rest of the danger is what he brings: the [[caveman|Cavemen]] who charge as he wakes, which can beat a
player who stands still to death <Cite replay="fidelity/replays/olmec-death-seed-1.txt" />, and the
monsters his yellow balls make.

## Defeating

<Clip id="olmec/lava" />

Olmec can't be harmed. He's a moving block, not one of the monsters the game's weapons look for, so the
[[whip|Whip]], bombs, bullets, [[throwing|thrown item]]s and stomps do nothing to him <Cite gml="objects/oOlmec/create.gml:8" />.
A bomb can only blow up the floor around him.

Lava kills him. When his feet touch lava, he stops, shakes the screen with a roar of fire and sinks, and
once the lava closes over his top, he's gone <Cite gml="objects/oOlmec/step.gml:35-43" />
<Cite gml="objects/oOlmec/step.gml:178-185" />. That happens when his slams have dug him down through the
floor of the lair into the lava below. In the clip, his last slam breaks through, and he takes 220 steps
(7.33 seconds) to sink out of sight <Cite clip="olmec/lava" />. His death counts as a kill, and the
[[endings|exit]] opens: a door of the lair becomes its exit, with a block of stone beneath it
<Cite gml="objects/oOlmec/step.gml:39-41" /> <Cite gml="objects/oFinalBoss/step.gml:1-19" /> <Cite replay="fidelity/replays/ending-seed-1.txt" />.

At the lair's left wall, the lava can't kill him. Both of his checks for it test a point at his left edge, under
his feet and over his top, and bumping off the wall leaves that edge inside it, 2 pixels left of his body, where
there's never lava. When his slams dig him down into the lava there, he sits in it, out of sight, and hops out
and lands back in it unharmed, until a hop drifts him right, off the wall, which it does only if the player is
past his right side as the drift starts <Cite gml="objects/oOlmec/create.gml:3" /> <Cite gml="scripts/isCollisionLeft.gml:6-10" />
<Cite gml="objects/oOlmec/step.gml:22-27" /> <Cite gml="objects/oOlmec/step.gml:35-43" /> <Cite gml="objects/oOlmec/step.gml:104-111" /> <Cite quirk="1" />.
A clip shows his last slam taking him into the lava by the wall, where he sits for two seconds; he starts to
sink only when he hops out and drifts toward the player <Cite clip="quirks/olmec-lava-left-wall" />. A player
who stays over him keeps him there: in one run, from a rope above him, through two hops
<Cite replay="fidelity/replays/olmec-door-block-crush-seed-1.txt" />.

## Locations

Olmec is only in [[olmecs-lair|Olmec's Lair]], level 16, the room the game goes to after level 15
<Cite gml="objects/oTransition/step.gml:26" />. He's placed there by hand, with his worshippers, rather than
made by level generation <Cite room="rOlmec" />. The [[level-editor|level editor]] has no tile for him.

## Tips

- Slams dig: leading Olmec to slam again and again in one place takes him down through the floor toward the
  lava.
- When Olmec stops in the air, get out from under him: he slams down 20 steps later.
- Olmec dug into the lava by the left wall doesn't drown until a hop takes him off it: stay to his right
  when he hops.
- The top of Olmec is the safest place in the lair: he carries a player who stands on him.
- His yellow balls turn into monsters where they land, so a fight that drags on fills the lair with Bats,
  Snakes and Spiders.

## Trivia

- His yellow balls' collision with blocks still has the code that turns them into monsters, commented out;
  their step event does it instead <Cite gml="objects/oYellowBall/collision_oSolid.gml:1-16" />.
- His object's states include `DEAD`, which nothing sets: he's destroyed the moment the lava covers him
  <Cite gml="objects/oOlmec/create.gml:28" /> <Cite gml="objects/oOlmec/step.gml:37-43" />.
