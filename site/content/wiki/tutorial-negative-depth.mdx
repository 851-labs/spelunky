The pause screen lists the level, the player's depth in feet, his money, kills, time and saves. In the
[[tutorial|Tutorial]] it calls the level TUTORIAL CAVE, but works out the depth as it does on any level, and
there the sum comes out below zero everywhere: -147.60 feet where the player starts, and still -82.32 on the
floor of the lower cave. Nothing else uses the depth, so it changes nothing in play.

## What happens

The player enters the Tutorial and pauses: the screen gives his depth as -147.60 feet. He walks on, drops
into the lower cave and pauses again on its floor, the lowest he can stand, and the depth is still below
zero, -82.32 feet <Cite clip="quirks/tutorial-negative-depth" />.

<Clip id="quirks/tutorial-negative-depth" />

## Why

The pause screen's code writes the depth from the level's number and the player's height, `py`, his `y`
when the game paused <Cite gml="objects/oScreen/step_begin.gml:55" />:

```gml objects/oScreen/step_begin.gml:85-91
    if (isLevel())
    {
        n = 128-24;
        if (global.currLevel < 1) draw_text(40, n-24, "TUTORIAL CAVE");
        else if (isRoom("rLoadLevel")) draw_text(40, n-24, "LEVEL: " + global.customLevelName + " BY " + global.customLevelAuthor);
        else draw_text(40, n-24, "LEVEL " + string(global.currLevel));
        draw_text(40, n-16, "DEPTH: " + string(174.8*(global.currLevel-1)+(py+8)*0.34) + " FEET");
```

That's 174.8 feet for each level before this one, and 0.34 feet a pixel down the level. `isLevel()` counts
the Tutorial's room as a level <Cite gml="scripts/isLevel.gml:7" />, and both ways into the Tutorial, the
START door on a first game and the TUTORIAL door, set the level to 0
<Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/other_animation_end.gml:72-95" />. So the depth starts at `174.8 * (0 - 1)`,
-174.8 feet, and the cave, 304 pixels tall, is too shallow for the 0.34 feet a pixel to bring it back above
zero <Cite quirk="1" />. The code gives the Tutorial its own name a line above, but not its own depth.

## Reproducing it

Pause anywhere in the Tutorial. Where the player starts, `py` is 72, so the depth is
`-174.8 + 80 * 0.34`, shown as -147.60. The replay of the clip pauses there (frame 568) and again on the
lower cave's floor (980), at `py` 264, -82.32 feet <Cite replay="fidelity/replays/flow-tutorial-pause-depth.txt" />.
Another replay pauses where the player starts, on the way back to the title
<Cite replay="fidelity/replays/flow-tutorial-title.txt" />.

## In the port

The spelunky.ai port reproduces it exactly: it's in docs/quirks.md's list of the 1.1 source's bugs
<Cite quirk="1" />, and the oracle's fingerprint of the replay pins the level and the player's height the
depth is worked out from. No option turns it off.
