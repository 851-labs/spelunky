The [[black-market|Black Market]] has five item shops, one of each kind: a
[[general-store|General Store]], a [[bomb-shop|Bomb Shop]], a [[weapon-shop|Weapon Shop]], a
[[rare-shop|Rare Shop]] and a [[clothing-shop|Clothing Shop]], with the Ankh's shop beside them. The loop that
gives each its kind checks whether it has gone round once after every try, the one that succeeds included, so
the fifth shop can find the last kind and be made a General Store all the same.

## What happens

The fifth shop, with only the Rare Shop's kind left, finds it and is made a General Store all the same, so the
market has two and no Rare Shop. The player walks into Duke's Supply Shop, then down into Lemmy's
<Cite clip="quirks/market-two-general-stores" />.

<Clip id="quirks/market-two-general-stores" />

## Why

For each item shop, the market's room script picks a first kind at random, `m`, and tries it and each kind
after it in turn, General, Bomb, Weapon, Rare and Clothing, then round again, taking the first the market
hasn't made. If it comes back to `m`, it gives up with a General Store
<Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGenMarket.gml:117-141" />:

```gml scripts/scrRoomGenMarket.gml:133-139
        n += 1;
        if (n > 5) n = 1;
        if (n == m)
        {
            shopType = "General";
            break;
        }
```

The test runs after every try, once `n` has moved on, including the try that just found a kind. For the
fifth shop, with one kind left, a first pick just after that kind finds it on the fifth try, the last before
`n` is back at `m`: the script marks the kind as made, then overwrites the shop's kind with General. That's
one first pick in five, and it shows unless the kind left was the General Store's own
<Cite quirk="1" />.

## Reproducing it

It depends on the level's seed: visit Black Markets until one has two General Stores. The replay pins one:
seed 9's market, where the fifth shop finds the Rare Shop's kind and is made a General Store, and the player is
greeted in Duke's Supply Shop and in Lemmy's, with no Rare Shop on the level
<Cite replay="fidelity/replays/shop-seed-9-market-two-general-stores.txt" />. Other routes into the same
seed's market have all five kinds <Cite replay="fidelity/replays/jungle-seed-9-market.txt" />.

## In the port

The spelunky.ai port reproduces it exactly: it's in docs/quirks.md's list of the 1.1 source's bugs
<Cite quirk="1" />, and the oracle's fingerprint of the replay pins the shops. No option turns it off.
