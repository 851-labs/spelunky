Held in the player's hands, it takes the [[whip|Whip]]'s place: the attack key swings it, with a wind-up
behind him and a slash in front, each of which takes <Stat id="machete.damage" /> on every step it
touches a monster ([[whip-every-step]]), and the slash cuts through [[web|webs]]. [[weapon-shop|Weapon Shops]] and the
[[dice-house|Dice House]] sell it, and [[crate|Crates]] and buried blocks give it away.

## Usage

<Clip id="machete/caveman" />

The Machete is picked up like any item, crouching over it and pressing the attack key. While it's held, the
attack key swings it as the Whip is swung, on the ground or in the air; crouching, the same key puts it down
in front of the player instead <Cite gml="scripts/scrUseItem.gml:121-167" />. It isn't thrown. A swing makes:

- **The wind-up**, 16 pixels behind the player, for 3
  steps <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:484-496" /> <Cite gml="objects/oWhipPre/create.gml:5" />.
- **The slash**, in front of him, once the swing's animation is past its fifth frame; it goes when its own
  animation ends, and while the swing lasts a new one takes its
  place <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:415-424" /> <Cite gml="objects/oSlash/other_animation_end.gml:1" />.
  In play a swing makes two slashes of 2 steps each <Cite clip="machete/caveman" />. Like the Whip's
  crack, a slash follows the player 16 pixels in front of him <Cite gml="objects/oSlash/step.gml:5-14" />.

In the clip, a [[caveman|Caveman]] charges the player, and one swing hits it on three steps for 2 each, from
3 hit points to -3; it falls dead before it reaches him <Cite clip="machete/caveman" />.

The Machete goes with the player through a level's exit if he's holding it, and is in his hands on the next
level <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:842-850" /> <Cite gml="objects/oLevel/create.gml:25-30" />. He
drops it when he's stunned, or when he teeters too long at a ledge's
edge <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:329-345" /> <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:150-171" />.

## Interactions

- **Webs.** A slash destroys any web it touches, which the Whip
  doesn't <Cite gml="objects/oWeb/collision_oSlash.gml:1" />.
- **[[shopkeeper|Shopkeepers]].** The slash and the wind-up take 2 of a Shopkeeper's hit points, at most once
  every 10 steps, and make him attack <Cite gml="objects/oShopkeeper/collision_oWhip.gml:1-19" />.
- **[[damsel|Damsels]].** A Damsel takes 2, at most once every 10 steps, where the Whip only makes her
  jump <Cite gml="objects/oDamsel/collision_oWhip.gml:1-12" />.

## Tips

- One swing kills a charging Caveman before it reaches the player, as the clip shows: its 3 hit points go
  in the swing's first steps, stunned or not.
- Of the weapons, only the Machete cuts webs, a [[giant-spider|Giant Spider]]'s among them.

## Trivia

- The slash is a kind of whip to the game (its object's parent is the whip's), so every monster's whip code
  handles it, and code that wants the Machete tests the swing's `type` for
  "Machete" <Cite gml="objects/oSlash/create.gml:3" /> <Cite gml="objects/oCaveman/collision_oWhip.gml:1" />.
