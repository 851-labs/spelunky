The end-of-game statistics count deaths by what caused them, and a crush, the player's middle inside
something solid, has its own row. In [[olmecs-lair|Olmec's Lair]] it doesn't: every crush there is counted
against [[olmec|Olmec]], even one he has nothing to do with.

## What happens

A bomb has blown out the floor under the exit door of Olmec's Lair, and the player jumps the pit onto the
block left below it. Olmec, sinking in the lava off the screen, dies, and a new block appears under the door,
around the player's middle: he's crushed, and the game counts the death against Olmec
<Cite clip="quirks/olmec-door-block-crush" />.

<Clip id="quirks/olmec-door-block-crush" />

## Why

The player's step crushes him whenever his middle is inside a solid, and picks the row of the statistics by
the room:

```gml objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:1493-1503
if (collision_point(x, y, oSolid, 0, 0))
{
    if (global.plife > 0)
    {
        if (isRealLevel())
        {
            if (isRoom("rOlmec")) global.enemyDeaths[22] += 1;
            else global.miscDeaths[2] += 1;
        }
    }
    global.plife -= 99;
```

`enemyDeaths[22]` is the OLMEC row, `miscDeaths[2]` the CRUSHED row <Cite gml="scripts/scrWriteStats.gml:138" />
<Cite gml="scripts/scrWriteStats.gml:159" />. Olmec's slam is the usual crush in his lair, and is his. But when
he dies, the lair's door becomes its exit, with a block of stone under it if there's no floor there:

```gml objects/oFinalBoss/step.gml:8-12
    if (not collision_point(640, 560, oSolid, 0, 0))
    {
        brick = instance_create(640, 560, oTemple);
        with brick { invincible = true; }
    }
```

A player standing where it appears is crushed by it, and the death is still Olmec's <Cite quirk="1" />.

## Reproducing it

Blow out the floor under the exit door before Olmec dies, and stand in the hole, where the block under the
door goes, as he drowns. The replay, with seed 1, keeps Olmec alive in the lava by the left wall, runs across
the lair as he starts to sink, and drops a bomb by the exit door. Its blast blows out the floor under the door,
and the player jumps onto the block below, his middle at (651, 568). Olmec dies, the block under the door
crushes him, and the OLMEC row counts it, not CRUSHED
<Cite replay="fidelity/replays/olmec-door-block-crush-seed-1.txt" />. Olmec's own crush is in another replay
<Cite replay="fidelity/replays/enemy-olmec-crush-seed-1.txt" />.

## In the port

The spelunky.ai port reproduces it exactly: it's in docs/quirks.md's list of the 1.1 source's bugs
<Cite quirk="1" />, and the oracle's fingerprint of the replay pins both tallies. No option turns it off.
