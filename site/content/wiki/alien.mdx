No level generation makes Aliens: each comes out of a [[ufo|UFO]] in [[ice-caves|the Ice Caves]] that a
[[throwing|thrown item]] or a thrown monster destroys, 1 time in 3. It shoots up out of the wreck, opens a parachute and
drifts down, then walks back and forth on the ledge where it lands. An Alien doesn't chase or attack the
player; touching it costs him <Stat id="alien.damage" />, and with <Stat id="alien.hp" />, it dies to any
hit.

## Behaviour

<Clip id="alien/parachute" />

When a thrown item or a stunned monster thrown at a UFO destroys it, the UFO has a 1 in 3 chance of
ejecting an Alien <Cite gml="objects/oItem/step.gml:331-340" /> <Cite gml="objects/oEnemy/step.gml:243-251" />.
The Alien shoots straight up at 6 pixels per step, slowing by 0.5 each step and trailing flames, until it
stops or meets a ceiling <Cite gml="objects/oAlienEject/create.gml:6" />
<Cite gml="objects/oAlienEject/step.gml:7-18" />. Then it opens its parachute and drifts down at
2 pixels per step (60 per second) and 1 pixel per step to one side, which it picks at random, until a wall stops
it going sideways <Cite gml="objects/oAlienEject/create.gml:21" /> <Cite gml="objects/oAlienEject/step.gml:19-38" />.
When it reaches the ground, or a [[web]], it becomes an Alien on its feet
<Cite gml="objects/oAlienEject/step.gml:23-27" /> <Cite gml="objects/oAlienEject/collision_oWeb.gml:1-2" />;
one that drifts out of the level is gone <Cite gml="objects/oAlienEject/other_outside.gml:1" />. The clip
shows an Alien parachuting from a UFO the player has destroyed, landing and walking off.

On the ground, an Alien sets off walking in a random direction at <Stat id="alien.walk-speed" />
(<Stat id="alien.walk-speed" as="per-second" />), and turns around at walls and at the edges of its ledge,
so it never walks off one <Cite gml="objects/oAlien/step.gml:30-58" />
<Cite gml="objects/oAlien/step.gml:66-67" />. On a ledge with a wall or a drop on both sides of it, it stands
still, facing away from the wall <Cite gml="objects/oAlien/step.gml:59-65" />. It stops to stand for 20–50
steps (0.67–1.67 seconds) with a 1 in 100 chance each step, then sets off again in a random direction
<Cite gml="objects/oAlien/step.gml:69-74" /> <Cite gml="objects/oAlien/step.gml:30-38" />.

An Alien only acts while it's on screen or just off it, its parachute a little further below the screen
<Cite gml="objects/oAlien/step.gml:3-5" /> <Cite gml="objects/oAlienEject/step.gml:1-3" />.

## Damage

When the player touches an Alien without landing on it, he loses <Stat id="alien.damage" />, is knocked away
from it, and blinks, invincible, for 30 steps (1 second), as with most monsters
<Cite gml="objects/oEnemy/collision_oCharacter.gml:14-27" />. An Alien still under its parachute does the
same <Cite gml="objects/oAlienEject/collision_oPlayer1.gml:19-32" />.

The clip shows the player landing on an Alien and killing it. Under its parachute, an Alien isn't one of
the monsters weapons, items and explosions hit: only landing on it kills it, and that counts as killing an
Alien <Cite gml="objects/oAlienEject/collision_oPlayer1.gml:1-18" />. Aliens killed count toward the player's
kills, and the screen between levels draws an Alien icon for each
<Cite gml="objects/oAlien/step.gml:16-21" /> <Cite gml="objects/oTransition/alarm_0.gml:209-213" />. One that
Spikes, Lava or a Spear Trap killed doesn't count <Cite gml="objects/oEnemy/step.gml:68" />
<Cite gml="objects/oEnemy/step.gml:101" /> <Cite gml="objects/oEnemy/step.gml:120" />.

## Locations

Aliens appear only out of UFOs, and level generation makes UFOs only in [[ice-caves|the Ice Caves]]
<Cite gml="objects/oItem/step.gml:331-340" /> <Cite gml="scripts/scrEntityGen.gml:338-360" />. A UFO destroyed
any way but by a thrown item or monster ejects none <Cite gml="objects/oUFO/step.gml:6-16" />.

In custom levels, it's the [[level-editor|level editor]]'s tile `a`
<Cite gml="scripts/scrCreateTileObj.gml:205-208" />.

## Tips

- An Alien never steps off its ledge, so one on a platform stays on it.
- Under its parachute an Alien can only be stomped, so it's safer to wait until it lands than to whip at it.
- A UFO brought down by the Whip, a stomp, a bullet or a Bomb never releases an Alien.
