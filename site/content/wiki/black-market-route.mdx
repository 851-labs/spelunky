The [[black-market|Black Market]] is a level of seven shops, the [[ankh-shop|Ankh Shop]] among them, and its
only way in is a door hidden inside a block on one of [[jungle|the Jungle]]'s levels 5 to 7. The door is
made once a game, on one level, so it has to be found and opened there; the [[udjat-eye|Udjat Eye]], from
the Mines, beeps on that level. One run, with seed 9 from the level 5 [[tunnel-man-shortcuts|shortcut]], bombs the door open on
level 5 and walks through the market to its exit <Cite replay="fidelity/replays/jungle-seed-9-market.txt" />;
the clips of those steps are cut from it, and the others from replays of the Eye and the market's shops.

## Requirements

- **A game started at level 1 or from the level 5 shortcut.** The door is on level 5, 6 or 7
  <Cite gml="scripts/scrInitLevel.gml:186-192" />, so a game started from the [[tunnel-man|Tunnel Man]]'s level 9
  or 13 shortcut has no market.
- **A way through rock**: [[bomb|bombs]], or the [[mattock|Mattock]]. The door is inside a block, which has
  to be dug or bombed out <Cite gml="scripts/scrEntityGen.gml:215-223" />
  ([[black-market#the-hidden-door|the hidden door]]).
- **Optional: the [[udjat-eye|Udjat Eye]]**, which only a game started at level 1 can find: it's in a locked
  chest on level 2, 3 or 4 <Cite gml="scripts/scrInitLevel.gml:178-184" />.
- **Optional: money.** The market's wares cost what any shop's do on its level, and the Ankh
  <Stat id="ankh.price" /> ([[black-market#shops|the market's shops]]).

## Steps

1. **Get the Udjat Eye, if you want its help.** One of the Mines' levels 2 to 4 has a locked chest, and one
   of the same level's loose treasures is its key; carry the key to the chest, and it opens on the Eye
   ([[udjat-eye#obtaining|where the chest is]]) <Cite gml="objects/oLockedChest/collision_oKey.gml:1-21" />.
   In the clip, the player carries the key down to the chest and picks up the Eye that pops out
   <Cite clip="udjat-eye/chest" />.

   <Clip id="udjat-eye/chest" />

2. **On levels 5 to 7, listen for it.** On the level with the door, and only there, the Eye blinks and beeps,
   faster the nearer the player is to the door ([[udjat-eye#the-black-market|the Udjat Eye and the market]])
   <Cite gml="objects/oGame/step.gml:3-12" /> <Cite gml="objects/oGame/alarm_2.gml:1-9" />. Far from the
   door it can stay silent until he's close. In the clip, the Eye is silent as the player runs across the
   top of level 7, and blinks only once he's beside the door <Cite clip="udjat-eye/market" />.

   <Clip id="udjat-eye/market" />

3. **Find the door.** Level 5 has it one time in 3, level 6 one time in 2, and level 7 always, if neither
   did <Cite gml="scripts/scrInitLevel.gml:186-192" />. It's inside a block that isn't a tree and has
   another block under it, and [[level-generation|level generation]] makes the block under it one that can't be destroyed
   ([[black-market#the-hidden-door|where it's put]]) <Cite gml="scripts/scrEntityGen.gml:215-223" />.
4. **Open the block.** Bomb it, or dig it out with the Mattock. The door is an object of its own, not the
   block, so it's left standing in the hole, on the block under it <Cite gml="scripts/scrEntityGen.gml:220-221" />.
5. **Walk into the door**, as into an exit: up, standing on the ground in front of it
   <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:759-765" />. The next level is the Black Market
   <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:879" /> <Cite gml="scripts/scrLevelGen.gml:44-46" />, with the
   message "WELCOME TO THE BLACK MARKET!" <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/alarm_0.gml:14-20" />. In the clip, a
   bomb blows open the rock on level 5, the player walks into the door, and the market loads
   <Cite clip="black-market/entrance" />.

   <Clip id="black-market/entrance" />

6. **Shop.** The market has one of each item shop as a rule, a [[dice-house|Dice House]] and the Ankh Shop,
   laid out the same way every time ([[black-market#the-layout|the layout]]). Pick a ware up, and its
   Shopkeeper names the price; the [[controls|pay key]] buys it, with the money in hand
   ([[general-store#shopkeeper|how buying works]]) <Cite gml="objects/oShopkeeper/step.gml:72-85" />
   <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:1359-1389" />. In the clip, the player drops into the Ankh Shop and
   picks up the [[ankh|Ankh]], and the Shopkeeper names its price <Cite clip="ankh-shop/visit" />.

   <Clip id="ankh-shop/visit" />

7. **Leave by the exit, at the bottom right.** The bottom row of rooms leads from the left column to it
   ([[black-market#the-layout|the layout]]) <Cite gml="scripts/scrLevelGen.gml:44-69" />. In the clip, the
   player climbs down from Wang's Clothing Shop and walks along the bottom row to the exit
   <Cite clip="black-market/walk" />.

   <Clip id="black-market/walk" />

## Tips

- Buy what the rest of the game needs here: once the market has been made, no later level has a shop
  <Cite gml="scripts/scrLevelGen.gml:240" /> <Cite quirk="1" />.
- The market's level is the one after the door's, so a door found on level 5 makes a market on level 6, whose
  wares cost less than on level 8: prices rise 10% of the base price with each level past the second
  ([[general-store#prices|prices]]) <Cite gml="scripts/scrShopItemsGen.gml:186" />. The Ankh's never
  changes ([[ankh-shop#prices|the Ankh Shop's prices]]).
- One market in five has two General Stores and is missing another shop ([[two-general-stores]]).
- The Ankh, thrown up out of its shop, angers no one until the player picks it up outside
  ([[ankh-throw-out]]).
- The [[city-of-gold-route]] guide carries the Ankh on from here.

## Common mistakes

- **Leaving the door's level without taking it.** The door is made once a game: once it has been, no later
  level makes one, until a new game <Cite gml="scripts/scrInitLevel.gml:186-192" />
  <Cite gml="scripts/scrEntityGen.gml:222" /> <Cite gml="scripts/scrClearGlobals.gml:71" />.
- **A lit bomb in a shop.** An armed bomb in a shop's room, near a Shopkeeper, angers every Shopkeeper that's
  active, near enough the view, with "TERRORIST!" <Cite gml="objects/oBomb/step.gml:6-16" />, and the
  market's seven are close together: in the clip's run, five of them at once
  ([[black-market#hazards|the market's hazards]], [[bomb-angers-every-shopkeeper]]) <Cite quirk="1" />
  <Cite replay="fidelity/replays/shop-seed-9-market-bomb.txt" />. In the clip, the player drops a lit bomb
  in Wang's Supply Shop, and its Shopkeeper shoots him dead on the next step <Cite clip="black-market-route/bomb" />.

  <Clip id="black-market-route/bomb" />

- **Walking out with a ware unpaid.** Carried out of its shop's room, it's stolen, and the nearest Shopkeeper
  attacks <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:541-557" /> <Cite gml="scripts/scrShopkeeperAnger.gml:7-22" />.
  The theft marks the player a thief, and while he is one, the market's other Shopkeepers stop greeting him
  and walk about, attacking when he comes near ([[general-store#stealing|stealing]])
  <Cite gml="objects/oShopkeeper/step.gml:121-124" />. The City of Gold route's run steals the Ankh all
  the same, and leaves past them <Cite replay="fidelity/replays/city-of-gold-seed-9.txt" />.
