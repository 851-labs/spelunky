Every shop has one, standing by his wares; the shops' pages ([[general-store|General Store]],
[[kissing-parlor|Kissing Parlor]], [[dice-house|Dice House]] and the rest) say what each sells. He's calm
until the player steals or does damage; then he runs at the player, jumps after him, and fires a shotgun whose pellets take 4 hearts each. A theft is remembered: for a
level or more afterwards a Shopkeeper waits at each level's exit, and once one has been killed, at every exit
for the rest of the game.

## Behaviour

### Calm

Standing in his shop, the Shopkeeper turns to face the player. The first time the player is in his shop's
room, he welcomes him to the shop under a name drawn from 32 ("WELCOME TO TERRY'S SUPPLY SHOP!")
<Cite gml="objects/oShopkeeper/step.gml:99-148" /> <Cite gml="scripts/scrGetName.gml:24-58" />. While the
player holds an unpaid ware in his room, he follows the player, faster the farther away the player is, up to
3 pixels a step, and stops beside him; he goes back to standing once the ware is paid for or put down
<Cite gml="objects/oShopkeeper/step.gml:225-282" />. A ware held near him shows its price and the key to buy
it <Cite gml="objects/oShopkeeper/step.gml:72-85" />.

### Angry

<Clip id="shopkeeper/thief" />

Angry, he runs at <Stat id="shopkeeper.attack-speed" /> (<Stat id="shopkeeper.attack-speed" as="per-second"
/>), turning toward the player when they're on the same level, at most every 20 steps, and turning back at
walls; he jumps at a wall in front of him, and at a gap when the player isn't below him
<Cite gml="objects/oShopkeeper/step.gml:337-438" />. His first angry step makes every item on the level free <Cite gml="objects/oShopkeeper/step.gml:341-349" />.

He fires his shotgun at a player in front of him, nearer than <Stat id="shopkeeper.shot-range" /> and less than
32 pixels above or below his middle, every <Stat id="shopkeeper.reload" /> (<Stat id="shopkeeper.reload"
as="seconds" />) at most: <Stat id="shopkeeper.pellets" bare /> pellets, spreading a little up and down, and
the kick pushes him back <Cite gml="objects/oShopkeeper/step.gml:368-408" />. Each
pellet that hits the player takes 4 hearts and knocks him down, with no pause between hits, so one pellet
takes all four of the hearts the player starts with <Cite gml="objects/oBullet/collision_oCharacter.gml:1-21" />. His pellets don't hurt
monsters, or him <Cite gml="objects/oBullet/collision_oEnemy.gml:1" />.

Touching the player when he isn't calm, he grabs him and throws him over his head, stunned; the throw costs a
heart when the player hits a wall or lands. Under a block, with no room to throw, he hits the player for 1
heart and knocks him back instead <Cite gml="objects/oShopkeeper/collision_oCharacter.gml:22-77" /> <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:1815-1842" />.

The first clip shows a theft: the player buys a parachute, then jumps out of the shop with a compass he can't
afford. The Shopkeeper shouts "COME BACK HERE, THIEF!", chases him, and shoots him dead.

### On patrol

<Clip id="shopkeeper/patrol" />

A Shopkeeper waiting for a thief (below) is on patrol: he stands for 20 to 50 steps, then walks a step one
way or the other, at <Stat id="shopkeeper.walk-speed" />, and stands again
<Cite gml="objects/oShopkeeper/step.gml:283-336" />. His walk checks for a wall at his head's height in front
of him, not for a floor, so in open ground it stops after one step and he coasts to a halt: he shuffles rather
than walks <Cite gml="objects/oShopkeeper/step.gml:309-328" /> <Cite clip="shopkeeper/patrol" />. He turns on
the player who comes nearer than <Stat id="shopkeeper.sight-range" /> (and isn't more than 16 pixels below
his middle), or who is right above or below him <Cite gml="objects/oShopkeeper/step.gml:87-97" />. In the clip, a
patrolling Shopkeeper shuffles to and fro; when the player walks up to him, he fires, and the blast takes the
player's four hearts.

### Stunned and killed

A stunned Shopkeeper drops his shotgun, and gets up angry once he's lain on the ground (or been held) for
<Stat id="shopkeeper.stun-time" bare /> steps after a stomp or a Spear Trap, or 20 after a bullet <Cite
gml="objects/oShopkeeper/step.gml:445-478" /> <Cite gml="objects/oShopkeeper/step.gml:511-522" /> <Cite
gml="objects/oBullet/collision_oEnemy.gml:17-25" />. Without it he can't shoot, but he still grabs and
throws; running into a loose shotgun, he picks it up again <Cite gml="objects/oShopkeeper/collision_oShotgun.gml:1-14" />.

<Clip id="shopkeeper/throw" />

In the clip, the Shopkeeper follows the player while he holds an unpaid pistol, and stops once he pays. The
player shoots him with it; he falls stunned, dropping his shotgun, gets up angry, and throws the player, who
loses a heart.

Killed, he drops his shotgun and 1 to 4 [[gold-nugget|Gold Nuggets]], and counts in the end-of-game
statistics' Shopkeeper row <Cite gml="objects/oShopkeeper/step.gml:479-509" />. Crushed in a block, he
dies at once <Cite gml="objects/oShopkeeper/step.gml:24-63" />.

## Interactions

### Buying

A ware is bought by holding it in the shop and pressing the pay key with its price in hand; without it, the
player drops the ware ("YOU HAVEN'T GOT ENOUGH MONEY!") <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:1359-1390" />.
A [[golden-idol|Golden Idol]] set down on a shop's floor within 80 pixels of a calm Shopkeeper is sold for its
value ("PLEASURE DOING BUSINESS!") <Cite gml="objects/oGoldIdol/step.gml:6-27" />. A
[[crystal-skull|Crystal Skull]] sells the same way, and counts as an idol between levels <Cite quirk="1" />.

### Angering him

Each of these angers the nearest Shopkeeper, with its own shout <Cite gml="scripts/scrShopkeeperAnger.gml:7-23" />:

| Cause | Shout | |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A ware taken out of the shop unpaid, by the player or thrown | "COME BACK HERE, THIEF!" | <Cite gml="objects/oItem/step.gml:17-35" /> <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:541-553" /> |
| A die carried out of a Dice House, or rolled again before the bet is settled | "COME BACK HERE, THIEF!" | <Cite gml="objects/oDice/step.gml:10-17" /> <Cite gml="objects/oDice/step.gml:219-224" /> |
| A block of the shop's walls destroyed | "DIE, YOU VANDAL!" | <Cite gml="objects/oSolid/destroy.gml:1-7" /> |
| A lit bomb in the shop, near him | "TERRORIST!" | <Cite gml="objects/oBomb/step.gml:6-17" /> |
| The Damsel he sells hurt or killed | "HEY, ONLY I CAN DO THAT!" | <Cite gml="objects/oDamsel/collision_oWhip.gml:19-22" /> |
| Any of these, once a Shopkeeper has been killed | "YOU'LL PAY FOR YOUR CRIMES!" | <Cite gml="scripts/scrShopkeeperAnger.gml:13" /> |

Stealing equips some wares at once and not others, by which leaves the shop first <Cite quirk="1" />. Hitting
the Shopkeeper also angers him, with no shout: a crack of the [[whip|Whip]], which doesn't hurt him, a thrown
item, a bullet, a [[piranha|Piranha]]'s bite <Cite gml="objects/oShopkeeper/collision_oWhip.gml:12-18" /> <Cite gml="objects/oItem/step.gml:289-298" />.

### The thief's Shopkeeper

The shouts above also mark the player a thief: they add 2 to a count the game keeps, or 3 if it's already
above 0 <Cite gml="scripts/scrShopkeeperAnger.gml:21-22" />. Each level's exit takes 1 off it
<Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:874" />, and each new level that starts with it above 0, or after the
player has ever killed a Shopkeeper, puts an angry Shopkeeper on patrol at its exit
<Cite gml="scripts/scrInitLevel.gml:245-257" />. The shops on that level have "WANTED" posters of the player,
their Shopkeepers patrol rather than stand, every ware is free, and the Kissing Parlor and the Dice House
turn him away <Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGen.gml:404-412" /> <Cite gml="objects/oShopkeeper/step.gml:121-124" />
<Cite gml="objects/oItem/step.gml:8-15" /> <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:1392-1397" />. So one theft
puts a Shopkeeper at the next level's exit; a killing, at every exit to the end of the game. Each shop's page
covers stealing from it, as the [[general-store#stealing|General Store]]'s does.

<Clip id="general-store/waiting-shopkeeper" />

In the clip, on the level after a theft, the Shopkeeper waiting by the exit leaps at the player as he comes
down to it, and the player takes the exit unhurt <Cite replay="fidelity/replays/shop-seed-3-thief-next-level.txt" />.

Hitting a Shopkeeper doesn't make the player a thief: in the pistol clip, the angry Shopkeeper's wares are free, but
the player isn't marked <Cite replay="fidelity/replays/weapon-seed-39-pistol.txt" />.

A stomp while the player is still rising takes nothing, though it stuns him <Cite quirk="1" />. Killing a
Shopkeeper makes the player a murderer for the rest of the game <Cite gml="objects/oShopkeeper/step.gml:479-503" />.
On one of [[kali|Kali]]'s altars his body is worth half of <Stat id="shopkeeper.favor" bare /> favor dead, and
all of it stunned, if it lies still there for 20 steps before he wakes <Cite gml="objects/oEnemy/step.gml:130-166" />.
A [[mantrap|Mantrap]] can swallow him; he comes back out, angry and without his shotgun, when it dies or wakes <Cite gml="objects/oManTrap/step.gml:20-40" /> <Cite gml="objects/oManTrap/step.gml:106-120" />.
