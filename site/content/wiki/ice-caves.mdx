Where the areas before it are caves dug through solid ground, the Ice Caves are open space: platforms of
dark rock and blocks of ice float over a drop with no floor, and a fall past the bottom of the level kills
the player. The ice is slippery, and some platforms melt or fall. [[yeti|Yetis]] walk the platforms and
[[ufo|UFOs]] fly between them, and the area holds the [[moai|Moai]], the [[mothership]] and the
[[yeti-lair|Yeti lair]].

## Generation

The Ice Caves are levels 9 to 12, built in a room of their own, taller than the Mines' or the Temple's
<Cite gml="scripts/scrInitLevel.gml:27" /> <Cite gml="objects/oTransition/step.gml:25" />. Taking level 8's
exit leads to the screen between levels, where the [[tunnel-man|Tunnel Man]] may wait, and on to level 9; once two
[[tunnel-man-shortcuts|shortcuts]] are paid for, the title screen's Shortcut House has a door to level 9
<Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/other_animation_end.gml:63-65" /> <Cite
gml="objects/oPlayer1/other_animation_end.gml:144-150" />. In the clip, the player takes level 8's
exit, pays the Tunnel Man the last $500 of a shortcut, and level 9 loads, with Yetis and a UFO in view
<Cite clip="ice-caves/from-jungle" />.

<Clip id="ice-caves/from-jungle" />

The level is laid out as [[mines#generation|the Mines']] are, four rooms by four on a path from the top row
to the bottom, from the Ice Caves' own layouts <Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGen3.gml:48-188" />. But the level's
border has no floor: its blocks run down the sides and across the top only
<Cite gml="scripts/scrInitLevel.gml:84-94" />. The layouts are mostly platforms in open air, so the bottom row
of rooms opens onto nothing, and a player who falls more than 16 pixels below the bottom of the room loses
99 hearts
<Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:220-238" />.

The rock is dark blue, with one block in ten a block of ice <Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGen3.gml:295-304" />,
and some layouts add more ice, platforms that fall and thin ice <Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGen3.gml:483-518" />:

- **Ice** is slippery: standing on it, the player hardly slows down, unless he's crouching or wears
  [[spike-shoes|Spike Shoes]] <Cite gml="scripts/characterStepEvent.gml:774-778" />.
- **Falling platforms** drop after the player has stood on one for 20 steps (two-thirds of a second)
  <Cite gml="objects/oDarkFall/create.gml:9-10" /> <Cite gml="objects/oDarkFall/step.gml:3-8" />.
- **Thin ice** melts under the player in 30 steps (1 second) <Cite gml="objects/oThinIce/create.gml:3" />
  <Cite gml="objects/oThinIce/step.gml:1-13" />.

One side room in two gets a side room's layout, and one of those may be
<Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGen3.gml:68-103" />:

| Room | Chance | |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [[kalis-altar\|Kali's altar]] | 1 in 12, once a level | <Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGen3.gml:70-74" /> |
| [[golden-idol\|Golden Idol]] room: the idol on a ledge of thin ice | 1 in 9, once the altar's roll fails, once a level | <Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGen3.gml:75-83" /> <Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGen3.gml:99" /> |

A level may also hold one of the area's special places:

| Place | Chance | |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The [[moai\|Moai]] | Once a game: 1 in 4 on level 9, 1 in 3 on level 10, 1 in 2 on level 11, else on level 12 | <Cite gml="scripts/scrLevelGen.gml:212-221" /> |
| The [[mothership]] | 1 in 10 for a level after the one with the Moai | <Cite gml="scripts/scrLevelGen.gml:222-233" /> <Cite gml="scripts/scrClearGlobals.gml:59" /> |
| A [[yeti-lair\|Yeti lair]] | 1 in 6 for a level after the one with the Moai, once the mothership's roll fails | <Cite gml="scripts/scrLevelGen.gml:234-237" /> <Cite gml="scripts/scrClearGlobals.gml:58" /> |

The mothership's and the Yeti lair's rolls are the `else` branches of the Moai's, which every Ice Caves level
takes until the Moai is made, so neither comes before it <Cite quirk="1" />. Unlike the other areas, the Ice
Caves are never dark <Cite gml="scripts/scrInitLevel.gml:169" />.

## Inhabitants

[[level-generation|Level generation]] goes over every block outside the shops <Cite gml="scripts/scrEntityGen.gml:338-379" />:

| Monster | Where | Chance | |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [[ufo\|UFO]] | On a block without two open tiles below it, outside the entrance's room | 1 in 30 (1 in 90 in a Yeti lair) | <Cite gml="scripts/scrEntityGen.gml:345-359" /> |
| [[yeti\|Yeti]] | On a block more than 64 pixels from the entrance | 1 in 20, once the roll for a [[spring-trap\|Spring Trap]] fails | <Cite gml="scripts/scrEntityGen.gml:362-371" /> |

A UFO destroyed by a [[throwing|thrown item]] or monster may eject an [[alien|Alien]]. A [[yeti-lair|Yeti lair]] has
the [[yeti-king|Yeti King]], and the [[mothership]] the [[alien-boss|Alien Boss]]. The Ice Caves have no Bats, Snakes or Spiders, and no [[web|webs]]: the treasure
script's webs skip them <Cite gml="scripts/scrTreasureGen.gml:55" />
<Cite gml="scripts/scrTreasureGen.gml:100" />. The [[ghost|Ghost]] comes to a level the player stays on too
long, as in every area.

## Hazards

- **The drop.** Falling off the bottom of the level kills the player (above).
- **[[spring-trap|Spring Traps]].** One block in ten of plain rock, with four open tiles above it, more
  than 64 pixels from the entrance and the exit, gets a Spring Trap on top <Cite gml="scripts/scrEntityGen.gml:362-369" />,
  which throws the player 16 pixels up and into a jump when he steps on it
  <Cite gml="objects/oSpringTrap/collision_oPlayer1.gml:1-15" />.
- **[[spikes|Spikes]]**, under the idol's ledge <Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGen3.gml:99" />
  <Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGen3.gml:307" />.
- **Slippery ice, thin ice and falling platforms** (above).

## Items and shops

Shops come as in [[mines#shops|the Mines]]: a level's chance is 2 in its number (2 in 9 on level 9 down to 1
in 6 on level 12), in a side room beside the path, of the same seven kinds, and none once the
[[black-market|Black Market]] has been made <Cite gml="scripts/scrLevelGen.gml:239-297" />
<Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGen3.gml:122-152" /> <Cite quirk="1" />.

Treasure is scattered as in [[mines#treasure|the Mines]], without the webs. The Ice Caves' rock holds more
gems than any other area's: one block in 40 that isn't a gold vein holds a [[big-sapphire|Big Sapphire]], and rarer ones a
[[big-emerald|Big Emerald]], a [[big-ruby|Big Ruby]] or an item <Cite gml="objects/oDark/create.gml:3-15" />.

## Secrets

- **The [[moai|Moai]]**, on one of levels 9 to 12, which hides the [[hedjet|Hedjet]].
- **[[kalis-altar|Kali's altars]].**
- **The [[mothership]]**, with a free [[jetpack|Jetpack]] inside <Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGen3.gml:175-181" />
  <Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGen3.gml:472-478" />.
- **Shortcuts.** In the screen after level 12, the Tunnel Man may ask for money toward the last shortcut
  <Cite gml="objects/oTransition/create.gml:57-61" />.

  never are <Cite gml="scripts/scrEntityGen.gml:354" />.

  other, so under every Ice Caves level, out of sight and out of reach, it scatters what it would on any
