The Altar is the floor of [[mines|the Mines]]' idol room: two blocks of carved, vine-grown stone set into its
bottom row, with the [[golden-idol|Golden Idol]] lying on them and a giant tiki head high above. It's where the
idol is, nothing more: the altar has no code of its own beyond breaking.

## How it works

<Clip id="altar/idol" />

The idol room's layout puts the altar's two blocks in the middle of its floor and the idol on top of
them <Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGen.gml:117" /> <Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGen.gml:358-362" />
<Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGen.gml:374-377" />. The clip shows the room: the player walks up to the idol on its
altar, under the giant tiki head, lifts it off and drops down a shaft with it <Cite clip="altar/idol" />.
Lifting the idol is what sets off the room's trap, a [[boulder|Boulder]] from the tiki head
([[golden-idol#collecting]]).

The altar's blocks are solid, and break like any block, to a [[bomb|Bomb]], a [[mattock|Mattock]] or a rolling
Boulder, into rubble <Cite gml="objects/oAltarLeft/destroy.gml:1-9" /> <Cite gml="objects/oAltarRight/destroy.gml:1-9" />.

## Locations

Only [[mines|the Mines]] have the idol's altar: the idol room is one of the side rooms a Mines level may have, 1
time in 10 for each side room off the bottom row once the roll for [[kalis-altar|Kali's altar]] fails
<Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGen.gml:85-117" />. The idols of the other areas lie on other things: a floor of
[[trap-block|trap blocks]] in the Jungle, a ledge of [[thin-ice|thin ice]] in the Ice Caves, the floor between
two doors in the Temple ([[golden-idol#locations]]). The [[level-editor|level editor]] has no tile for it.
