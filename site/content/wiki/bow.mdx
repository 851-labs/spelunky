Holding the attack key draws it, and letting go shoots an [[arrow|Arrow]], faster the longer it was drawn;
arrows fall as they fly and hurt monsters as [[throwing|thrown item]]s do. A new Bow comes
with <Stat id="bow.arrows" /> arrows, and ones that come to rest can be picked up again. Fully drawn, it can
shoot a bomb arrow. [[weapon-shop|Weapon Shops]] sell it, and [[crate|Crates]] and buried blocks give it
away.

## Usage

<Clip id="bow/arrow" />

The Bow is picked up like any item, crouching over it and pressing the attack key; a new one
adds <Stat id="bow.arrows" /> arrows to the player's count, which shows under the hearts while he holds a
Bow <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:1216-1220" /> <Cite gml="scripts/scrDrawHUD.gml:170-178" />. While it's
held:

- **Drawing.** Pressing the attack key, with arrows left, draws it, with a
  creak <Cite gml="scripts/scrUseItem.gml:516-521" />. Holding the key draws it harder,
  by <Stat id="bow.draw-rate" /> a step up to <Stat id="bow.max-strength" />, which takes 60 steps; the Bow's
  picture shows how far <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:1463-1474" /> <Cite gml="objects/oBow/step.gml:11-14" />.
  With none left, the key shows "I'M OUT OF ARROWS!" <Cite gml="scripts/scrUseItem.gml:522-527" />.
- **Shooting.** Letting go shoots an arrow from 14 pixels in front of the player (from inside him, if a
  block is there), at 1 pixel a step plus the draw plus his own speed, at least 1, and uses up one arrow;
  the Bow can't be drawn again for 10 steps <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:1476-1479" /> <Cite gml="scripts/scrFireBow.gml:24-72" />.
- **Putting it down.** Crouching, the key puts it down in front of him <Cite gml="scripts/scrUseItem.gml:489-515" />.
  If he drops it drawn, stunned or teetering too long at a ledge's edge, it shoots as it
  falls <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:150-171" /> <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:329-345" />.

An arrow is an item, and falls as it flies. Its own code gives it a gentle fall, 0.2 pixels a step faster
each step, but the code all items share sets that to 0.6 at the end of its first step, so from its second
it falls as fast as a thrown [[rock|Rock]] ([[mitt-gravity-one-step]]) <Cite gml="objects/oArrow/create.gml:3-6" /> <Cite gml="objects/oItem/step.gml:160-170" /> <Cite quirk="1" row="The Pitcher's Mitt's light gravity lasts one step, as an arrow's does, but a jar, a skull or a die keeps it." />. In the clip, an arrow drawn for 40 steps leaves at 9 pixels
a step, drops from a ledge onto a [[snake|Snake]] and kills it. An arrow faster than 6 pixels a step
that meets a wall sticks in it; a slower one bounces off <Cite gml="objects/oItem/step.gml:87-137" />. One
that's come to rest, slower than 1 pixel a step and not stuck, is picked up by walking over it with the Bow
in hand, an arrow back each <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:1973-1988" />. In the clip, a tap of the
key drops one at the player's feet, and he takes it back <Cite clip="bow/arrow" />.

The Bow goes with the player through a level's exit if he's holding it, and is in his hands on the next
level, but doesn't count as new there <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:842-850" /> <Cite gml="scripts/scrHoldItem.gml:52-58" />. Arrows are kept from level to level, and a new game starts
with none <Cite gml="scripts/scrClearGlobals.gml:116" />.

### Bomb arrows

<Clip id="bow/bomb-arrow" />

Pressing the bomb key while the Bow is drawn, with at least one bomb, readies a bomb
arrow <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:1132-1136" />. But until the Bow is fully drawn, every step of drawing
turns it back into a plain arrow <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:1463-1474" />, so the key only works
at full draw <Cite quirk="1" row="A bomb arrow costs no bomb, and the bomb key only readies one at full draw." />. The bomb arrow explodes 80 steps after it was readied: in the air if it's been shot, or in
the player's hands if he's still holding it <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:29-40" /> <Cite gml="scripts/scrFireBow.gml:54-59" /> <Cite gml="objects/oArrow/alarm_1.gml:1-11" />. It also explodes
on hitting a monster <Cite gml="objects/oItem/step.gml:352-356" />. Readying one in a shop angers the
[[shopkeeper|Shopkeeper]] <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:42-46" />.

Nothing takes a bomb for it ([[free-bomb-arrows]]): in the clip, a bomb arrow blows up beside a [[caveman|Caveman]] and kills it,
and the player still has all four of his bombs <Cite clip="bow/bomb-arrow" /> <Cite quirk="1" row="A bomb arrow costs no bomb, and the bomb key only readies one at full draw." />.

## Damage

An arrow hurts as any thrown item does, by the code all items share, while it moves faster than 2 pixels a
step, and is used up by the hit <Cite gml="objects/oItem/step.gml:256-363" />: 1 hit point to most monsters,
and stuns a Caveman, a [[hawkman|Hawkman]], a [[mantrap|Mantrap]], a [[vampire|Vampire]] or a [[yeti|Yeti]]
that isn't stunned already. A Vampire takes 3 more, 4 in all, as arrows are stakes to the
code <Cite gml="objects/oItem/step.gml:265-266" />. The [[magma-man|Magma Man]] and monsters that can't be hurt
take nothing, and a [[ufo|UFO]] blows up. The arrow hurts the monster nearest it rather than the one it
hit ([[nearest-enemy]]) <Cite quirk="1" row="A fast thrown item hurts the nearest enemy, not the one it hit." />. A bomb arrow's blast takes <Stat id="bomb.damage" /> a step, as a Bomb's
does <Cite gml="objects/oExplosion/collision_oEnemy.gml:12-14" />.

An arrow faster than 3 pixels a step hurts the player too, 2 hearts, once its first 10 steps are
over <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:1513-1530" /> <Cite gml="objects/oArrow/alarm_2.gml:1" />.

## Interactions

- **Arrows by hand.** An arrow can be picked up and thrown like any item; it hurts like any thrown
  item <Cite gml="scripts/scrHoldItem.gml:38" />.
- **[[arrow-trap|Arrow Traps]].** A trap's arrows are the same arrows, and once they've stopped the Bow
  collects them like its own <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:1973-1988" />.

## Tips

- An arrow falls as it flies, so a monster below a ledge can be shot from the ledge, as in the clip.
- A bomb arrow is a free bomb, but it has to be shot within 80 steps of readying it.

## Trivia

- The code for buying it has a commented-out line that would add 8 arrows; the arrows come from picking up
  a new Bow instead <Cite gml="scripts/scrStealItem.gml:311" />.
- The arrow's gentler fall, which the shared item code undoes, is the [[pitchers-mitt|Pitcher's Mitt]]'s
  slow fall for thrown items over again: both set one that lasts a step.
- The shop sells it as "BOW AND ARROWS" <Cite gml="objects/oBow/create.gml:7-8" />.
