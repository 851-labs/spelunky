The [[city-of-gold|City of Gold]] takes the place of level 15, and its only way in is a gold door on level 14
that opens for a player who wears the [[hedjet|Hedjet]] and carries the [[sceptre|Sceptre]] into it. The keys
come in a fixed order, across three areas: the [[ankh|Ankh]] from the [[black-market|Black Market]] (levels 6–8),
spent on the [[moai|Moai]]'s level (9–12) to reach the Hedjet, then the Sceptre on level 13, so the Ankh has to
be carried unused from the market to the Moai. Most of the clips are cut from one run that takes the route all
the way with seed 9, from the level 5 shortcut <Cite replay="fidelity/replays/city-of-gold-seed-9.txt" />.

## Requirements

- **A game started at level 1 or from the level 5 shortcut.** The Black Market's door is on level 5, 6 or 7
  <Cite gml="scripts/scrInitLevel.gml:186-192" />, and a new game starts without an Ankh
  <Cite gml="scripts/scrClearGlobals.gml:85" />, so a game started past the market, from the
  [[tunnel-man|Tunnel Man]]'s level 9 or 13 shortcut, never has one.
- **The Ankh**, which only the Black Market's [[ankh-shop|Ankh Shop]] has, for <Stat id="ankh.price" />: it's
  the way into the Moai, which only a [[teleporter|Teleporter]] jump can also reach
  <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:1853-1861" /> <Cite quirk="1" />.
- **Bombs.** The Black Market's door is hidden inside a block, and the gold door can be sealed in rock
  <Cite gml="scripts/scrEntityGen.gml:212-225" /> <Cite quirk="1" />. The run also kills the Tomb Lord with one.
- **Optional: the [[udjat-eye|Udjat Eye]]**, which blinks and beeps faster as the player nears the Black
  Market's door ([[udjat-eye#the-black-market|the Udjat Eye and the market]]).

## Steps

1. **Find the Black Market's door.** Once a game, level 5, 6 or 7 hides it inside a block; dig or bomb it out
   and walk into it, as into an exit, and the next level is the Black Market
   ([[black-market#the-hidden-door|the hidden door]]) <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:879" />. In the clip,
   a bomb opens the rock on level 5 and the player takes the door <Cite clip="black-market/entrance" />.

   <Clip id="black-market/entrance" />

2. **Get the Ankh.** In the market's Ankh Shop, pick it up and pay with the pay key, or carry it out of the
   shop's room unpaid: either way it goes on <Cite gml="scripts/scrStealItem.gml:35-45" />. Carried out unpaid,
   it's stolen, and the Shopkeeper turns on the player ([[ankh#obtaining|taking the Ankh]],
   [[general-store#stealing|stealing]]). In the clip, the player jumps up out of the shop with the Ankh;
   it goes on, and the Shopkeeper shouts "COME BACK HERE, THIEF!" and fires <Cite clip="city-of-gold-route/ankh" />.

   <Clip id="city-of-gold-route/ankh" />

3. **Reach the Moai's level without dying.** The first death on a level while wearing the Ankh uses it up and,
   away from the Moai, puts the player back at the level's entrance <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:1847-1896" />.
   The Moai is on one of [[ice-caves|the Ice Caves]]' levels 9 to 12 ([[hedjet#obtaining|which one]])
   <Cite gml="scripts/scrLevelGen.gml:212-220" />. The run
   leaves the market past its angry Shopkeepers and crosses levels 7 to 11 without losing a heart
   <Cite replay="fidelity/replays/city-of-gold-seed-9.txt" />.
4. **Die beside the Moai.** On its level, with the Moai near the view, a death with the Ankh revives the
   player inside the Moai's sealed chamber instead, and he falls onto the Hedjet
   ([[hedjet#obtaining|the Hedjet's chamber]]) <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:1853-1861" />. In the clip,
   the player drops a bomb at his feet; the blast takes his last hearts, and the Ankh revives him in the
   chamber, where he picks up the Hedjet and leaves by the Moai's exit <Cite clip="hedjet/moai" />.

   <Clip id="hedjet/moai" />

5. **Pick up the Hedjet and take the Moai's exit.** It stays on until the game ends
   <Cite gml="scripts/scrStealItem.gml:46-56" />. The chamber has its own exit, to the next level
   <Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGen3.gml:487-501" />.

6. **Kill level 13's Tomb Lord.** Only a [[tomb-lord|Tomb Lord]] killed on level 13 leaves the Sceptre
   <Cite gml="objects/oTombLord/step.gml:23" />, and level generation tries to place one there
   ([[tomb-lord#locations|where it appears]]); its page has [[tomb-lord#defeating|what hurts it]]. In the
   clip, a bomb the player has dropped kills it, and he goes down after the Sceptre <Cite clip="city-of-gold-route/sceptre" />.

   <Clip id="city-of-gold-route/sceptre" />

7. **Carry the Sceptre out of level 13's exit.** Held as the player goes through an exit, it's in his hands on
   the next level <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:842-850" /> <Cite gml="objects/oLevel/create.gml:25-30" />.
   In the clip, level 14 loads with the Sceptre still held <Cite clip="city-of-gold-route/carry" />.

   <Clip id="city-of-gold-route/carry" />

8. **Find the gold door.** Level 14 always has one, on top of a block that can't be destroyed, wherever the
   roll that places it lands: in the open, or sealed in rock that a bomb has to open
   ([[city-of-gold#generation|the gold door]]) <Cite gml="scripts/scrEntityGen.gml:387-409" /> <Cite quirk="1" />.
9. **Carry the Sceptre into the door.** With the Hedjet on, the door takes the Sceptre and opens
   <Cite gml="objects/oGoldDoor/collision_oSceptre.gml:1-16" />.
10. **Go through it**, as through an exit: up, standing on the ground in front of it
    <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:758-764" />. The next level is the City of Gold
    <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:880" />. In the clip, the player brings the Sceptre to the door, which
    opens, and goes through it <Cite clip="hedjet/gold-door" />.

    <Clip id="hedjet/gold-door" />

The City of Gold loads with the message "IT'S THE LEGENDARY CITY OF GOLD!"
<Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/alarm_0.gml:52-58" />. In the last clip, the player runs down from the entrance
to [[city-of-gold#the-lady-xoc-statue|the Lady Xoc statue]] <Cite clip="city-of-gold/arrival" />. Its exit
leads on to [[olmecs-lair|Olmec's Lair]], as the Temple's level 15 does; [[beating-olmec]] is the guide
for that.

<Clip id="city-of-gold/arrival" />

## Tips

- From the Hedjet on, there's no hurry: it keeps the [[ghost|Ghost]] away however long the player stays on a
  level <Cite gml="objects/oGame/step.gml:23-47" />.
- The Sceptre fires psychic waves that find the nearest monster through rock, and firing it doesn't let go of
  it ([[sceptre#usage|the Sceptre's usage]]), so it's a weapon on levels 13 and 14 as well as the key.
- Thrown up out of the Ankh Shop through the hole in its ceiling, the Ankh lands outside at its price with the
  Shopkeeper still calm, until the player picks it up there ([[ankh-throw-out]]).

## Common mistakes

- **Losing the Ankh before the Moai.** A death on the way there, even one in the Black Market to the
  Shopkeeper's shotgun, spends the Ankh at the level's entrance <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:1847-1896" />.
  The Moai's chamber is filled by a block that can't be destroyed, so it can't be dug or bombed into instead
  <Cite gml="objects/oMoaiInside/create.gml:3" />. The [[ankh#effect|Ankh's own clip]] shows a theft ending
  in a death to the Shopkeeper.
- **Dying far from the Moai.** Things far outside the view are switched off, the Moai with them, and a revival
  with no active Moai goes to the entrance <Cite gml="objects/oLevel/step.gml:75-94" />
  <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:1853-1872" />.
- **Killing the wrong Tomb Lord.** Those of levels 14 and 15 leave no Sceptre
  <Cite gml="objects/oTombLord/step.gml:23" />.
- **Letting go of the Sceptre.** The player drops it when he's stunned, or when he teeters at a ledge's edge
  for 30 steps, and crouching and attacking puts it down <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:329-345" /> <Cite
  gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:150-171" /> <Cite gml="scripts/scrUseItem.gml:276-331" /> <Cite
  replay="fidelity/replays/city-of-gold-seed-2-sceptre.txt" />. Only a held item goes through an exit, so pick
  it up again before leaving <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:842-850" />.
- **Bringing the Sceptre without the Hedjet.** The door says "THE SCEPTRE FITS... BUT NOTHING IS HAPPENING!"
  and stays shut <Cite gml="objects/oGoldDoor/collision_oSceptre.gml:17-22" />.
