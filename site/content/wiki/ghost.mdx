The Ghost is the game's limit on how long the player can spend on a level. It comes once he has been on a
level for 5,000 steps (2 minutes 46.67 seconds), from the edge of the screen, and floats straight at him
through anything in its way. Nothing can hurt it, so the only way out is the level's exit.

## Behaviour

<Clip id="ghost/touch" />

A Ghost appears halfway down the edge of the screen, the right edge if the player is in the right half of
the level and the left edge otherwise, and plays its sound <Cite gml="objects/oGame/step.gml:39-46" />
<Cite gml="objects/oGhost/create.gml:29" />. It flies straight at the player at
<Stat id="ghost.speed" /> (<Stat id="ghost.speed" as="per-second" />) in whatever direction he is, turning
to face him, and nothing stops it: it has no code for walls or anything else it passes through
<Cite gml="objects/oGhost/step.gml:15-28" />. On a [[dark-level|dark level]] it lights up the cave around it
<Cite gml="objects/oScreen/step_begin.gml:231-234" />. Only one Ghost comes to a level
<Cite gml="objects/oGame/step.gml:40-45" />.

The clip shows a Ghost coming for a player who has stood still on a level of [[mines|the Mines]]: it drifts
through the rock toward him, turns a Big Sapphire it passes into a [[diamond|Diamond]], and kills him when
it reaches him <Cite clip="ghost/touch" />. Every Big Emerald, Big Ruby and Big Sapphire it touches becomes
a Diamond, even one still inside a block <Cite gml="objects/oSapphireBig/collision_oGhost.gml:1-2" />
<Cite replay="fidelity/replays/item-seed-2-diamond.txt" />.

**When it comes.** Every step the player is alive on a level, the level's clock gains 30 (the steps in a
second), from 0 when the level starts <Cite gml="objects/oGame/step.gml:14-21" />
<Cite gml="objects/oLevel/create.gml:12-14" />. At 120,000, 4,000 steps (2 minutes 13.33 seconds) in, the
game warns "A CHILL RUNS UP YOUR SPINE... LET'S GET OUT OF HERE!" and the music fades; at 150,000, 5,000
steps in, the Ghost comes <Cite gml="objects/oGame/step.gml:26-46" />. The clock stops while the game is
paused <Cite replay="fidelity/replays/flow-pause-ghost-timer.txt" />. Neither happens on the first level,
on [[olmec|Olmec]]'s level, on a custom level, while the player is going out through the exit, or while he
has the Hedjet <Cite gml="objects/oGame/step.gml:39-41" />.

## Damage

When a Ghost touches the player, unless he's invincible from a hit in the last 30 steps, he dies at once,
whatever his hearts: his body bursts into three bones and a [[skull|Skull]], and whatever he was holding
flies out of his hands <Cite gml="objects/oGhost/collision_oCharacter.gml:1-33" />. The Ghost then fades
away <Cite gml="objects/oGhost/other_animation_end.gml:9-12" />. On the game's own levels the death is
counted against the Ghost in the deaths table <Cite gml="objects/oGhost/collision_oCharacter.gml:5" />
<Cite gml="scripts/scrWriteStats.gml:139" />.

## Defeating

A Ghost can't be harmed. It isn't one of the monsters most weapons look for, so whips, bombs, bullets and
thrown items pass through it, and its own whip event is empty
<Cite gml="objects/oGhost/collision_oWhip.gml:1" />. Its step event has code to remove it when its
hit points run out, but nothing ever takes them <Cite gml="objects/oGhost/step.gml:1-6" />. The only escape
is the level's exit: the clock and the Ghost start over on every level
<Cite gml="objects/oLevel/create.gml:12-14" />.

## Locations

The clock brings a Ghost to any level of [[mines|the Mines]], [[jungle|the Jungle]],
[[ice-caves|the Ice Caves]] and [[temple|the Temple]] after the first <Cite gml="objects/oGame/step.gml:39-46" />.
Ghosts also come:

- **For defiling an altar.** The third time the player destroys one of Kali's altars, and every time after,
  the level goes dark and a Ghost comes, if there's none already
  <Cite gml="objects/oSacAltarLeft/destroy.gml:39-58" />.
- **For the golden idol in a graveyard.** Picking up the golden idol on a Jungle
  [[restless-dead|graveyard level]] sends one <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:1230-1239" />.
- **In the Sun Room**, the bonus room, once the player has 80 points there
  <Cite gml="objects/oSunRoom/alarm_0.gml:9-14" />.

## Tips

- The warning comes 1,000 steps (33.33 seconds) before the Ghost: time enough to make for the exit.
- A Ghost is slow, 1 pixel a step, but it never stops and nothing blocks it.
- Big gems the Ghost passes become Diamonds, even ones still in the rock.
