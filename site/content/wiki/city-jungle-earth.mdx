The [[city-of-gold|City of Gold]] is a [[temple|Temple]] level made of gold: its temple stone, its stone blocks and
its push blocks all turn gold there. But the Temple's room script also makes the odd block of the
[[jungle|Jungle]]'s earth, and the earth has no check for the City, so the City of Gold has a few blocks of plain
green and brown earth among its gold.

## What happens

The City of Gold loads with the player near its entrance. He walks right along its gold floor and over a block of
the Jungle's mossy earth set in it <Cite clip="quirks/city-jungle-earth" />.

<Clip id="quirks/city-jungle-earth" />

## Why

The Temple's room script, which builds the City too, makes the Jungle's earth (`oLush`) for one of its
always-solid tiles in a hundred, before it rolls a stone block or temple stone:

```gml scripts/scrRoomGen4.gml:317-322
        if (tile == "1" and not collision_point(xpos, ypos, oSolid, 0, 0))
        {
            if (rand(1,100) == 1) instance_create(xpos, ypos, oLush);
            else if (rand(1,10) == 1) instance_create(xpos, ypos, oBlock);
            else instance_create(xpos, ypos, oTemple);
        }
```

Its side room with two trees, which the City rolls among its side rooms, stands on a row of eight more <Cite
gml="scripts/scrRoomGen4.gml:70-74" /> <Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGen4.gml:104-107" /> <Cite
gml="scripts/scrRoomGen4.gml:505-508" />. Temple stone turns gold in the City
(`if (global.cityOfGold) sprite_index = sGTemple;`) <Cite gml="objects/oTemple/create.gml:7" />, and so do the
stone and push blocks, but the earth's create event has no such line: it rolls a gold vein, a gem or an item as
the Jungle's earth does <Cite gml="objects/oLush/create.gml:5-15" />. Broken, it gives rubble, and gold only from a
vein <Cite gml="objects/oLush/destroy.gml:3-33" /> <Cite quirk="1" />. It's harmless.

## Reproducing it

Reach the City of Gold and look for green blocks among the gold; most Cities have some. The replay of the City
with seed 9 loads it on frame 6745 with six earth blocks, one at (560, 96), near the entrance
<Cite replay="fidelity/replays/city-of-gold-seed-9.txt" />. The clip, cut from the run on to Olmec, walks over
that one <Cite replay="fidelity/replays/city-of-gold-seed-9-to-olmec.txt" />. [[city-of-gold-route]] has the way there.

## In the port

The spelunky.ai port reproduces it exactly: it's in docs/quirks.md's list of the 1.1 source's bugs
<Cite quirk="1" />, and the oracle's fingerprint of the replay pins the earth blocks. No option turns it off.
