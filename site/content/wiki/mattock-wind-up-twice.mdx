A swing of the [[whip|Whip]], the [[machete|Machete]] or the [[mattock|Mattock]] begins with a wind-up behind
the player, which hurts a monster there. The Mattock makes its wind-up twice, a step apart, so a monster behind
the player takes twice the Machete's wind-up damage from it.

## What happens

With his back to a [[yeti-king|Yeti King]], the player swings a Mattock. Its wind-up is made behind him twice,
and the two take 12 of the Yeti King's 30 hit points in four steps: 2, 4, 4 and 2. The hit in front breaks the
block under him, and he drops into the hole <Cite clip="quirks/mattock-wind-up-twice" />.

<Clip id="quirks/mattock-wind-up-twice" />

## Why

The player's step makes a swing's wind-up on its first two steps, once a swing: it checks that none exists
yet. The Mattock's branch is a copy of the Machete's that makes an `oMattockPre` but still checks for the
Machete's `oMachetePre`:

```gml objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:497-503
    else if (holdItem.type == "Mattock")
    {
        if ((sprite_index == sAttackLeft or sprite_index == sDamselAttackL or sprite_index == sTunnelAttackL) and facing == LEFT and image_index < 2 and instance_number(oMachetePre) == 0)
        {
            obj = instance_create(x+16, y, oMattockPre);
            obj.sprite_index = sMattockPreL;
        }
```

An `oMattockPre` isn't an `oMachetePre` (both are kinds of the Whip's `oWhipPre`), so the check always passes,
and it makes one on each of the two steps. Each lasts 3 steps <Cite gml="objects/oWhipPre/create.gml:3-5" />
and takes its 2 <Cite gml="objects/oMattockPre/create.gml:3-4" /> on every step it touches a monster, with no
wait between <Cite gml="objects/oEnemy/collision_oWhipPre.gml:1-8" />. From a monster both touch throughout,
that's 2, 4, 4 and 2. The [[tunnel-man|Tunnel Man]]'s swing with empty hands checks for an `oMattockPre`, and
makes one <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:511-523" /> <Cite quirk="1" />.

## Reproducing it

Swing a Mattock with a monster close behind the player: every such swing in play hits it twice over. The
replay of the clip's staged scene, on a custom level, makes
the two wind-ups a step apart and takes a Yeti King's hit points to 28, 24, 20 and 18
<Cite replay="fidelity/replays/weapon-mattock-wind-up-twice.txt" />.

## In the port

The spelunky.ai port reproduces it exactly: it's in docs/quirks.md's list of the 1.1 source's bugs
<Cite quirk="1" />, and the oracle's fingerprint of the replay pins every hit. No option turns it off.
