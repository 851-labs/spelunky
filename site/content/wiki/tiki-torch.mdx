Tiki torches are only made on [[dark-level|dark levels]] of [[jungle|the Jungle]], where they stand on the
ground and burn, one of the level's few lights besides the player's own. A torch can't be carried or moved,
and it goes with the block it stands on.

## How it works

<Clip id="tiki-torch/dark" />

A tiki torch flickers through its flames, half a frame a step <Cite gml="objects/oTikiTorch/create.gml:1" />. On a
dark level, it lights a circle of the cave 32 pixels around its middle, as a lit trap or a
[[fire-frog|Fire Frog]] does, where a [[flare|Flare]] or a [[lamp|Lamp]] lights 96
<Cite gml="objects/oScreen/step_begin.gml:179-206" />. It also widens the player's own light as he nears it,
counting as a light 48 pixels farther away than it is <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:107-112" />
([[dark-level#the-darkness]]). The clip shows a dark Jungle level: the cave is black but for the circle around the
player and the smaller ones around what glows, and at the right a tiki torch flickers in a circle of its own
<Cite clip="tiki-torch/dark" />.

A torch isn't solid, isn't an item and can't be picked up. When the block under it is destroyed, it's destroyed
too <Cite gml="objects/oSolid/destroy.gml:14-18" />.

## Locations

As a dark Jungle level is made, each block of the ground that a [[spear-trap|Spear Trap]] could replace (open
above, open on a side, a block under it, away from shops and the entrance) gets a tiki torch standing on it
with a 1 in 20 chance, unless there's water above it; only if it doesn't, it rolls for the trap
<Cite gml="scripts/scrEntityGen.gml:226-252" />. A trunk block of a [[tree|Tree]] never gets one. The
[[level-editor|level editor]] has no tile for it.

## Tips

- Its light is small: a Flare thrown ahead lights three times as far.
- A Bomb at a torch's foot puts its light out for good, with the block it stands on.
