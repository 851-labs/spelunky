The pit is a column of four rooms, from the top row of the level to the bottom, that the path never
crosses. A [[damsel|Damsel]] and a [[golden-idol|Golden Idol]] wait at its top on a floor of
[[trap-block|trap blocks]], and lifting the idol drops the player, the idol and the Damsel the length of
the shaft into [[lava]], with six [[big-ruby|Big Rubies]] in the blocks under it. Despite its message, "I
CAN HEAR PRAYERS TO KALI!", the pit has no [[kalis-altar|altar of Kali's]].

### The roll

Before it lays the path, [[level-generation|level generation]] makes each [[temple|Temple]] level a sacrifice pit one time in 8,
the [[city-of-gold|City of Gold]]'s too <Cite gml="scripts/scrLevelGen.gml:71-87" />
<Cite gml="scripts/scrClearGlobals.gml:60" />. The roll is made afresh for every level, so a game can have
several: with seed 43, levels 13, 14 and 15 are all three sacrifice pits
<Cite replay="fidelity/replays/temple-seed-43.txt" />. The pit takes a column other than the entrance's.
The path is laid after it and only moves sideways into rooms it hasn't been given yet, so it never enters
the pit's column, and the entrance and the exit are on the same side of it <Cite gml="scripts/scrLevelGen.gml:89-133" /> <Cite quirk="1" />.

A sacrifice pit level has its own idol and Damsel, so it gets no idol room, no other Damsel, and no
[[kissing-parlor|Kissing Parlor]], whose Damsel would be a second <Cite gml="scripts/scrLevelGen.gml:84-86" /> <Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGen4.gml:80-88" /> <Cite gml="scripts/scrTreasureGen.gml:73" /> <Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGen4.gml:155-188" />.

Ten steps after the level starts, the player says "I CAN HEAR PRAYERS TO KALI!"
<Cite gml="scripts/scrInitLevel.gml:206-219" /> <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/alarm_0.gml:59-64" />. On a
[[dark-level|dark level]], the dark level's message comes first ("I CAN'T SEE A THING!", or "THE HEDJET
SHINES BRIGHTLY." for a player wearing the [[hedjet|Hedjet]]) and Kali's 209 steps later
<Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/alarm_0.gml:5-13" /> <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/alarm_1.gml:43-48" />. In the
City of Gold, the timer meant for Kali's message runs a chain of messages that tests for the City first: it
shows the City's message, and Kali's comes 209 steps after that. So a City that isn't dark shows its own
message twice, and a dark one the dark level's, the City's and Kali's <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/alarm_1.gml:36-42" /> <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/alarm_4.gml:7-12" /> <Cite quirk="1" /> <Cite replay="fidelity/replays/city-of-gold-seed-9-sacrifice-pit.txt" />.

In the clip, a Temple level loads with the message, and the player walks left from the entrance to the
top of the pit, where the Damsel and the idol stand side by side <Cite clip="sacrifice-pit/arrival" />.

### The layout

The four rooms have layouts of their own, one each, from the Temple's room script <Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGen4.gml:193-204" />:

[[bomb|Bomb]] or a [[mattock|Mattock]].

In the City of Gold, the Lady Xoc statue's room is chosen after the pit, and one time in four takes the
pit's third room, which seals the shaft off above the lava: lifting the idol then drops the player a room,
onto the statue. In the clip below, he falls onto it, stunned, and loses a heart <Cite gml="scripts/scrLevelGen.gml:136" /> <Cite quirk="1" /> <Cite clip="quirks/xoc-statue-in-sacrifice-pit" /> <Cite replay="fidelity/replays/city-of-gold-seed-9-xoc-pit.txt" />.

The rest of the level is the Temple's, with its [[temple#inhabitants|monsters]] on the blocks of the pit's
rooms as on any other <Cite gml="scripts/scrEntityGen.gml:444-473" />. The Damsel can be carried off and
rescued, or [[sacrifice|sacrificed]] on an altar, as any other ([[damsel]]). Left at the top, she falls
with the idol and burns up in the lava.

## Hazards

- **The [[trap-block|trap blocks]].** With no [[ceiling-trap|Ceiling Trap]] on the level, lifting the idol destroys at
  once every trap block within 90 pixels of the player, all four of the pit's
  <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:1250-1279" />. Knocking the idol off them first, or destroying
  them, defuses it ([[trap-block#avoiding]]).
- **The [[lava]]** at the bottom, which kills the player as soon as he lands in it. In the clip, he lifts the idol,
  falls the length of the pit with the Damsel and dies in the lava; the Damsel burns up after him <Cite clip="trap-block/pit" /> <Cite replay="fidelity/replays/trap-sacrifice-pit.txt" />.

## Items and shops

Shops come as on [[temple#items-and-shops|any Temple level]], but never the Kissing Parlor (above). The
[[golden-idol|Golden Idol]] at the top is the level's only one. The six Big Rubies are in the blocks under
the lava, and each comes out when its block is destroyed <Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGen4.gml:333-337" />
<Cite gml="objects/oTemple/destroy.gml:34-37" />. Treasure is scattered over the pit's blocks as over the
rest of the level <Cite gml="scripts/scrEntityGen.gml:475-478" />.

## Secrets

- **[[kalis-altar|Kali's altar]]**, in a side room, as on any Temple level outside the City of Gold. The pit
  doesn't stop it: with seed 43, level 15 is a sacrifice pit with an altar <Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGen4.gml:68-79" /> <Cite replay="fidelity/replays/temple-seed-43.txt" />.

## Trivia

- The Temple's tile for a trap block rolls 1 in 1 before making one, a roll that always passes but still
  draws a random number, four times on every sacrifice pit level <Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGen4.gml:406-409" /> <Cite quirk="1" />.
- Lifting the idol plays the thump and shakes the screen once for every trap block on the level, as the
  code that does it is outside the test for their distance: four times on one step, which sounds and looks
  like once <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:1269-1278" /> <Cite quirk="1" />.
