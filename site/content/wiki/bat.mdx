Level generation hangs Bats under blocks in [[mines|the Mines]] and [[jungle|the Jungle]]. Once awake, a
Bat flies through the air toward the player, around walls rather than through them, and bites him on
contact; when he gets far enough away, it goes back to a ceiling. With <Stat id="bat.hp" />, it dies to
any damage, including a [[stomping|stomp]] from below.

## Behaviour

<Clip id="bat/swoop" />

A Bat starts out hanging under a block. It wakes when the player is within <Stat id="bat.wake-range" /> of
its middle and more than 16 pixels below its top, or when the block it hangs from is gone, unless the
player is swimming or dead <Cite gml="objects/oBat/step.gml:23-30" />. The clip shows one waking as the
player walks beneath it and swooping down to bite him.

Awake, it flies straight toward the player while he's within <Stat id="bat.chase-range" />, at
<Stat id="bat.flight-speed" /> (<Stat id="bat.flight-speed" as="per-second" />) as its code sets it
<Cite gml="objects/oBat/step.gml:34-38" /> <Cite gml="objects/oBat/step.gml:65-69" />. The game moves it in
whole pixels on each axis, so flying diagonally it covers 1 pixel across and 1 up or down each step, about
1.4 in all <Cite gml="scripts/moveTo.gml:20-31" />. A wall in the way turns it rather than stopping it:
blocked on the player's side, it flies straight up or down toward his height, and blocked above or below,
straight sideways toward him <Cite gml="objects/oBat/step.gml:39-58" />. Over water, it doesn't fly
downward <Cite gml="objects/oBat/step.gml:60-63" />.

When the player is farther away than that, or swimming, or dead, the Bat flies straight up until it
touches a ceiling and hangs from it again <Cite gml="objects/oBat/step.gml:71-80" />
<Cite gml="objects/oBat/step.gml:87-95" />. Like most monsters, it only acts while it's on screen or just
off it <Cite gml="objects/oBat/step.gml:1-3" />.

## Damage

When the player touches a Bat without stomping it, he loses <Stat id="bat.damage" />, is knocked away from
it, and blinks, invincible, for 30 steps (1 second) <Cite gml="objects/oEnemy/collision_oCharacter.gml:14-25" />.
This is the touch code the Bat inherits from the parent object most monsters share; it has no attack of its
own.

## Defeating

A Bat has <Stat id="bat.hp" />, so any damage kills it <Cite gml="objects/oBat/step.gml:8-18" />:

<Clip id="bat/jump-kill" />

The clip shows the player jumping up into a flying Bat and killing it. The touch code counts a touch as a
stomp while the player is jumping as well as falling, as long as he's higher than the monster's middle, so
a Bat can be stomped from below <Cite gml="objects/oEnemy/collision_oCharacter.gml:6" />
<Cite clip="bat/jump-kill" />.

The Bat's own step event replaces the one most monsters share, which is where [[spikes|Spikes]],
[[lava|Lava]] and [[spear-trap|Spear Traps]] hurt them, so none of those harm a Bat
<Cite gml="objects/oEnemy/step.gml:63-128" />.

Bats killed count toward the player's kills, and the screen between levels draws a Bat icon for each
<Cite gml="objects/oBat/step.gml:11-16" /> <Cite gml="objects/oTransition/alarm_0.gml:125-130" />.

## Locations

Level generation makes Bats in two areas, each hanging under a block that has two blocks of open space
below it (with no water there, and in the Mines no monster right under it), outside the starting room and
shops and above the level's bottom 64 pixels:

- **[[mines|The Mines]]**: a 1 in 60 chance for each such block, once the rolls before it have failed: a
  [[giant-spider|Giant Spider]] and, on a [[dark-level|dark level]], a lamp or a [[scarab|Scarab]]
  <Cite gml="scripts/scrEntityGen.gml:45-63" />.
- **[[jungle|The Jungle]]**: a 1 in 80 chance for each, or 1 in 60 on a
  [[restless-dead|graveyard level]], after a dark level's roll for a Scarab
  <Cite gml="scripts/scrEntityGen.gml:262-272" />.

Bats also appear wherever these are:

- **[[olmec|Olmec]]'s yellow balls**, which turn into a Bat, [[spider|Spider]], [[snake|Snake]], [[frog|Frog]]
  or [[fire-frog|Fire Frog]] when they hit the ground <Cite gml="objects/oYellowBall/step.gml:4-19" />.
- **The [[tutorial|Tutorial]]**, which has one <Cite room="rTutorial" />.
- **Custom levels**, as the [[level-editor|level editor]]'s tile `b`
  <Cite gml="scripts/scrCreateTileObj.gml:127-130" />.

## Tips

- A hanging Bat doesn't wake for a player level with it or above it, only for one more than 16 pixels
  below its top, or when its ceiling is destroyed.
- A Bat can be killed by jumping into it from below, as long as the player is higher than its middle when
  they touch.
- Leading a Bat over Spikes or Lava doesn't kill it.

## Trivia

- The Bat's code never sets its `type` string, so it keeps the parent object's `"None"`
  <Cite gml="objects/oEnemy/create.gml:4" />. The touch code checks for `"Bat"` twice, and neither check
  ever matches: a Bat that takes the player's last heart isn't counted in the deaths-by-enemy table the game
  writes to `stats.txt`, whose Bat row always reads `???` with 0 deaths, and the blood the code adds to
  a Bat's bite never appears <Cite gml="objects/oEnemy/collision_oCharacter.gml:27-29" />
  <Cite gml="objects/oEnemy/collision_oCharacter.gml:55" /> <Cite gml="scripts/scrWriteStats.gml:110-116" />.
- On the Jungle's [[restless-dead|graveyard levels]], a [[vampire|Vampire]] turns into a bat of its own to
  fly at the player. It isn't a Bat: it stays a Vampire, with the Vampire's code, health and drops
  <Cite gml="objects/oVampire/step.gml:159" />.
