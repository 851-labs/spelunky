It's the least valuable treasure in the game. About three [[block|blocks of rock]] in ten are veined with gold, and
each, destroyed, lets out three chunks. They come loose when a [[bomb|Bomb]], a [[mattock|Mattock]] or a
boulder breaks the block, and the player collects them by touching them.

## Value

A chunk's base value is <Stat id="gold-chunk.value" />, and it's worth a quarter of that more for each area
past the Mines, the code counting [[olmecs-lair|Olmec's Lair]] as an area of its own

## Locations

- **Gold veins.** Each block of rock in [[mines|the Mines]], [[jungle|the Jungle]], [[ice-caves|the Ice
  Caves]] and [[temple|the Temple]] (and [[olmecs-lair|Olmec's Lair]]) is a gold vein 19 times in 100 and a
  rich one 10 times in
  100 <Cite gml="objects/oBrick/create.gml:7-9" /> <Cite gml="objects/oLush/create.gml:5-8" />
  <Cite gml="objects/oDark/create.gml:3-8" /> <Cite gml="objects/oTemple/create.gml:5-9" />. Destroyed, either
  lets out three chunks, thrown out at up to 3 pixels a step to either side and 2–4 down, and a rich vein a
  [[gold-nugget|Gold Nugget]] as well <Cite gml="objects/oBrick/destroy.gml:9-29" />.
- **[[city-of-gold|The City of Gold]]**: its blocks are gold, and each one destroyed lets out three chunks and
  a nugget <Cite gml="objects/oTemple/destroy.gml:21-32" /> <Cite gml="objects/oBlock/destroy.gml:13-24" />.
- **[[jar|Jars]]**: a broken Jar holds one 1 in 3 times, the first of its rolls
  <Cite gml="objects/oJar/destroy.gml:17" />.

The [[level-editor|level editor]] has no tile for one, and its blocks hold no gold: it sets each block's look
back to plain rock after the block has rolled for its vein <Cite gml="scripts/scrCreateTileObj.gml:36-40" />.

## Collecting

<Clip id="gold-chunk/mattock" />

The player collects a chunk by touching it while he isn't stunned or dead, the moment it appears: a chunk is
collectable from the start <Cite gml="objects/oGoldChunk/create.gml:7-8" />
<Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:1990-2016" />. He takes one treasure a step, the one nearest him, so a
vein's three chunks come in on three steps ([[nearest-enemy]]) <Cite quirk="1" row="Collecting treasure takes the nearest treasure, and nothing while that one can't be collected." />. Its value shows as "+100" under his money and is counted into it
after a short pause <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:1953-1971" />; gold plays the coin sound
<Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:2000-2014" />, and the screen between levels shows each chunk collected
on the level <Cite gml="objects/oTransition/alarm_0.gml:23-28" />.

In the clip, the player digs along a tunnel with a Mattock; two of the blocks it breaks are gold veins, and he
collects each one's three chunks on three steps running <Cite clip="gold-chunk/mattock" />.

A loose chunk falls and comes to rest like other treasure, moving only while it's on the screen, and lava it
sinks into destroys it <Cite gml="objects/oTreasure/step.gml:1-79" />.
