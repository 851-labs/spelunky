Every new game starts here, on level 1, and the four levels down to [[jungle|the Jungle]] are brick
caves, with ladders between their ledges. Level generation fills them with [[snake|Snakes]] on the ground and
[[bat|Bats]] and [[spider|Spiders]] on the ceilings, turns stone blocks into [[arrow-trap|Arrow Traps]], and
from level 2 builds shops, Kali's altars and the [[udjat-eye|Udjat Eye]]'s locked chest. Each level can
instead be a [[snake-pit|snake pit]] or a [[dark-level|dark level]].

## Generation

The Mines are levels 1 to 4: the game's level type is the Mines' unless the level number puts it in a
later area <Cite gml="scripts/scrInitLevel.gml:24-29" />. A new game's first level is reached through the
title screen's START door; the very first game a player starts goes to the [[tutorial|Tutorial]] instead
<Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/other_animation_end.gml:72-86" />. In the first clip, the player walks into the
START door and level 1 loads <Cite clip="mines/start" />.

<Clip id="mines/start" />

A level is a grid of four rooms by four, each 10 tiles wide and 8 high (a tile is 16 pixels), inside a
border of bricks that can't be destroyed <Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGen.gml:24-45" />
<Cite gml="scripts/scrInitLevel.gml:117-126" />. First, a path is laid: it starts in a random room of the top
row, which holds the entrance, and wanders left and right, dropping a row at random, until it leaves the
bottom row, where the last room it reached holds the exit <Cite gml="scripts/scrLevelGen.gml:25-31" />
<Cite gml="scripts/scrLevelGen.gml:89-133" />. The path's rooms are built from layouts that leave a way
through: open rooms, ladders, and drops into the room below. The rest are side rooms, which needn't lead
anywhere <Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGen.gml:85-239" />. Each layout has slots that take one of a set of
5-by-3 chunks at random: a platform, a mound with [[spikes|Spikes]], an overhang
<Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGen.gml:242-315" />. Then every tile is made: a brick, or, one time in ten, a
stone block <Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGen.gml:325-340" />.

Then the side rooms get their features <Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGen.gml:85-121" />:

| Room | Chance | |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Kali's altar | 1 in 16 for each side room from level 2, once a level | <Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGen.gml:87-91" /> |
| [[golden-idol\|Golden Idol]] room, with a giant tiki head and its [[boulder\|Boulder]] | 1 in 10 for each side room off the bottom row, once the altar's roll fails, once a level | <Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGen.gml:92-101" /> |
| A shop | From level 2 (below) | <Cite gml="scripts/scrLevelGen.gml:239-297" /> |

On top of that, a level may be special. Three side rooms one above the other, from the top row or the
second, become a [[snake-pit|snake pit]], 1 in 8 for each such column, once a level; from the top row, the
pit runs down all four rows when the bottom room is a side room too
<Cite gml="scripts/scrLevelGen.gml:138-185" /> <Cite gml="scripts/scrClearGlobals.gml:55" />. From level 2, a
level may be a [[dark-level|dark level]], 1 in 12, at most once in the area, and never the first level after the title screen, nor one after a level finished 20 seconds or less past a whole minute on its clock
<Cite gml="scripts/scrInitLevel.gml:167-174" /> <Cite gml="scripts/scrClearGlobals.gml:54" /> <Cite
quirk="1" />.

Taking a level's exit leads to the screen between levels, which counts up what the player found, and on to
the next level <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/other_animation_end.gml:63-70" />. In the second clip, the player
climbs down through level 1 and takes its exit <Cite clip="mines/exit" />.

## Inhabitants

Level generation goes over every block outside the shops and below the top row, and outside the room the
entrance is in, and may hang a monster under it, if the two tiles below are open, or stand one on top of it
<Cite gml="scripts/scrEntityGen.gml:35-74" />:

| Monster | Where | Chance | |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [[giant-spider\|Giant Spider]] | Hanging, where there's room for its two blocks by two | 1 in 40 for each block, on one level in six, once a level | <Cite gml="scripts/scrEntityGen.gml:32-34" /> <Cite gml="scripts/scrEntityGen.gml:52-60" /> |
| [[scarab\|Scarab]] | Hanging, on a [[dark-level\|dark level]] only | 1 in 40, once the roll for a lamp fails | <Cite gml="scripts/scrEntityGen.gml:61-62" /> |
| [[bat\|Bat]] | Hanging | 1 in 60 | <Cite gml="scripts/scrEntityGen.gml:63" /> |
| [[spider\|Spider]] | Hanging | 1 in 80, once the Bat's fails | <Cite gml="scripts/scrEntityGen.gml:64" /> |
| [[snake\|Snake]] | On top | 1 in 60 | <Cite gml="scripts/scrEntityGen.gml:69" /> |
| [[caveman\|Caveman]] | On top | 1 in 800, once the Snake's fails | <Cite gml="scripts/scrEntityGen.gml:70" /> |

The Mines are the only area whose generation makes Snakes, Spiders and Giant Spiders. A
[[snake-pit|snake pit]] adds a shaft full of Snakes. The treasure's piles of bones (below) may be a
[[skeleton|Skeleton]] waiting to rise. From level 2, the [[ghost|Ghost]] comes to a level the player stays on
for 5,000 steps <Cite gml="objects/oGame/step.gml:39-46" />.

## Hazards

- **[[arrow-trap|Arrow Traps]].** One stone block in four becomes an Arrow Trap, if it's outside a shop,
  more than 48 pixels from the entrance (and not on the entrance's row within 144 pixels of it), and has a
  block behind it and open ground in front: two tiles on one side, three on the other
  <Cite gml="scripts/scrEntityGen.gml:145-171" />.
- **[[spikes|Spikes]]** come with the ground chunks that have a spike pit, one tile in three
  <Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGen.gml:273-284" /> <Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGen.gml:343" />.
- **Webs** hang in nooks and tunnels (below). One holds the player, who struggles free by jumping, and
  stops monsters and thrown items <Cite gml="scripts/characterStepEvent.gml:294-305" /> <Cite
  gml="scripts/characterStepEvent.gml:743-748" /> <Cite gml="objects/oWeb/collision_oEnemy.gml:1-5" /> <Cite
  gml="objects/oWeb/collision_oItem.gml:1-5" />.
- **The [[boulder|Boulder]]** of an idol room comes down when the [[golden-idol|Golden Idol]] is lifted.

## Items and shops

### Shops

From level 2, a level may have a shop: always on level 2, two times in three on level 3, one time in two on
level 4 (the code's `rand(1, level) <= 2`). It goes in a side room beside a room of the path, and only if
there is one: a side room qualifies when the room to its right is one the path crosses or leaves downward
(not one it drops into), or, in the rightmost column, when the room to its left is <Cite gml="scripts/scrLevelGen.gml:239-297" />. Its kind is one of seven, each as
likely: a [[general-store|General Store]], [[bomb-shop|Bomb Shop]], [[weapon-shop|Weapon Shop]],
[[rare-shop|Rare Shop]], [[clothing-shop|Clothing Shop]], [[dice-house|Dice House]] or
[[kissing-parlor|Kissing Parlor]] <Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGen.gml:173-204" />.

### Treasure

Level generation scatters loose things over every block outside the shops (the same in every area but
[[olmecs-lair|Olmec's Lair]]), keeping them 32 pixels from the entrance and the exit and 64 from a Golden
Idol <Cite gml="scripts/scrTreasureGen.gml:26-29" />:

| Spot | What, and the chance | |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Any block with room above | A [[rock\|Rock]] 1 in 100, else a [[jar\|Jar]] 1 in 40 | <Cite gml="scripts/scrTreasureGen.gml:42-46" /> |
| A nook (rock above and to one side) | A web 1 in 5; else a [[crate\|Crate]] 1 in 10, a [[chest\|Chest]] 1 in 15, a Damsel 1 in 8 (one a level), bones, a [[gold-bar\|Gold Bar]] 1 in 3, [[gold-bars\|Gold Bars]], or a big gem, each roll made once the ones before it fail | <Cite gml="scripts/scrTreasureGen.gml:47-94" /> |
| A tunnel (rock on both sides) | A web 1 in 10; else a Gold Bar 1 in 4, Gold Bars 1 in 8, bones, or a big gem | <Cite gml="scripts/scrTreasureGen.gml:95-115" /> |
| Open ground | A Gold Bar 1 in 40, else Gold Bars 1 in 50, else bones | <Cite gml="scripts/scrTreasureGen.gml:116-133" /> |

The webs' odds are meant to be 1 in 60 away from a Giant Spider, but a typo makes the code always take its
near-a-Giant-Spider odds <Cite quirk="1" />. The bones' odds grow with the level number, and one pile in
eight is a [[skeleton|Skeleton]] waiting to rise <Cite gml="scripts/scrTreasureGen.gml:80-88" />.

The bricks hold more. About one in five shows a vein of gold, which breaks into three
[[gold-chunk|Gold Chunks]], and one in ten a rich vein, which adds a [[gold-nugget|Gold Nugget]]. Of the
rest, 1 in 100 holds a big sapphire, and rarer ones a big emerald, a big ruby, or an item
<Cite gml="objects/oBrick/create.gml:7-16" /> <Cite gml="objects/oBrick/destroy.gml:9-29" />.

## Secrets

- **Kali's altars**, from level 2 (above).
- **The [[udjat-eye|Udjat Eye]]**: one level from 2 to 4 hides a locked chest and its key
  <Cite gml="scripts/scrInitLevel.gml:178-184" />.
- **The [[snake-pit|snake pit]]**'s bottom holds big rubies and a free [[mattock|Mattock]]
  <Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGen.gml:212-218" /> <Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGen.gml:471-481" />.
- **Shortcuts.** In the screen after level 4, the Tunnel Man may ask for money toward a shortcut, which
  opens a door to the Jungle on the title screen <Cite gml="objects/oTransition/create.gml:47-51" />.
