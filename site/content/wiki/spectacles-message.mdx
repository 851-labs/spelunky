When the player picks up a ware in a shop, the [[shopkeeper|Shopkeeper]] names it and its price. Each ware writes
that message when it's made, and past level 2 the shop writes it again with the higher price, in a form of its
own. For most wares the two read the same, but four read differently past level 2.

## What happens

In a Clothing Shop on level 5, the player picks up the Spectacles, and the Shopkeeper prices them: A
SPECTACLES FOR $10400. He runs and jumps out of the shop with them <Cite clip="clothing-shop/theft" />.

<Clip id="clothing-shop/theft" />

## Why

Each ware's create event writes its message from its base price, as the Spectacles' does: "SPECTACLES FOR
$8000." <Cite gml="objects/oSpectacles/create.gml:7-8" />. On a level past the second, the shop raises the
price by a tenth of the base price a level, and writes the message again:

```gml scripts/scrShopItemsGen.gml:182-190
    if (global.currLevel > 2)
    {
        with obj
        {
            cost += (cost/100)*10*(global.currLevel-2);
            if (shopDesc == "") buyMessage = "A " + string_upper(type) + " FOR $" + string(cost) + ".";
            else buyMessage = shopDesc + " FOR $" + string(cost) + ".";
        }
    }
```

A ware with a shop name (`shopDesc`) keeps it: the bow, the [[bomb-bag|bomb bag]] and [[bomb-box|box]], the Pitcher's Mitt, the paste, the
spring shoes and the gloves. Every other ware gets "A", its `type` in capitals and the price. Most of those
messages already read that way ("A COMPASS FOR $3000." <Cite gml="objects/oCompass/create.gml:7-8" />), but
four don't <Cite quirk="1" />:

| Ware | On levels 1 and 2 | Past level 2 | |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [[spectacles\|Spectacles]] | SPECTACLES FOR $8000. | A SPECTACLES FOR $8800. (level 3) | <Cite gml="objects/oSpectacles/create.gml:8" /> |
| [[spike-shoes\|Spike Shoes]] | SPIKE SHOES FOR $4000. | A SPIKE SHOES FOR $4400. (level 3) | <Cite gml="objects/oSpikeShoes/create.gml:8" /> |
| [[jetpack\|Jetpack]] | JETPACK FOR $20000. | A JETPACK FOR $22000. (level 3) | <Cite gml="objects/oJetpack/create.gml:8" /> |
| [[rope-pile\|Rope pile]] | EXTRA ROPE FOR $2500. | A ROPE PILE FOR $2750. (level 3) | <Cite gml="objects/oRopePile/create.gml:8" /> |

The Shopkeeper shows the message of the ware the player holds while it has a price
<Cite gml="objects/oShopkeeper/step.gml:72-84" />.

## Reproducing it

Pick up the Spectacles, the Spike Shoes, the Jetpack or a rope pile in a shop on level 3 or later. Every one
of the four for sale past level 2 shows it. The replay of the clip finds a Clothing Shop on level 5 and picks up
the Spectacles: "A SPECTACLES FOR $10400.", frame 926
<Cite replay="fidelity/replays/item-seed-77-spectacles.txt" />. A Rare Shop on level 5 prices the Spike Shoes
"A SPIKE SHOES FOR $5200." <Cite replay="fidelity/replays/item-seed-7-spike-shoes.txt" />, and on level 2 the
Jetpack keeps its own "JETPACK FOR $20000." <Cite replay="fidelity/replays/item-seed-35-jetpack.txt" />.

## In the port

The spelunky.ai port reproduces it exactly: it's in docs/quirks.md's list of the 1.1 source's bugs
<Cite quirk="1" />, and the oracle's fingerprints of the replays pin the messages. No option turns it off.
