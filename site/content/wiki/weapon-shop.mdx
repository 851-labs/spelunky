Its Shopkeeper welcomes the player with "WELCOME TO ANDY'S ARMORY!", his name one of 32 drawn at random
<Cite gml="objects/oShopkeeper/step.gml:128" /> <Cite gml="scripts/scrGetName.gml:24-60" />. It's the one
shop that sells weapons: the [[pistol|Pistol]], [[shotgun|Shotgun]], [[machete|Machete]], [[bow|Bow]] and
[[web-cannon|Web Cannon]], with [[bomb-bag|Bomb Bags]] and [[bomb-box|Bomb Boxes]] among them. Like every
shop it's a side room off the way through a level, with four wares on its floor and the
[[shopkeeper|Shopkeeper]] beside them.

## Stock

The shop has four wares, and each is rolled on its own <Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGen.gml:173-191" />
<Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGen.gml:448-451" />. The rolls go down the table until one hits. A hit on a
weapon the level already has rolls again from the top, and after twenty such tries the ware is a Bomb Bag
<Cite gml="scripts/scrShopItemsGen.gml:36-67" />:

| Ware | Roll | |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [[web-cannon\|Web Cannon]] | 1 in 12, if the level has none | <Cite gml="scripts/scrShopItemsGen.gml:43-46" /> |
| [[shotgun\|Shotgun]] | 1 in 10, if the level has none | <Cite gml="scripts/scrShopItemsGen.gml:47-50" /> |
| [[bomb-box\|Bomb Box]] | 1 in 6 | <Cite gml="scripts/scrShopItemsGen.gml:51" /> |
| [[pistol\|Pistol]] | Otherwise 1 in 4, if the level has none | <Cite gml="scripts/scrShopItemsGen.gml:52-55" /> |
| [[machete\|Machete]] | Otherwise 1 in 4, if the level has none | <Cite gml="scripts/scrShopItemsGen.gml:56-59" /> |
| [[bomb-bag\|Bomb Bag]] | Otherwise 1 in 4 | <Cite gml="scripts/scrShopItemsGen.gml:60" /> |
| [[bow\|Bow]] | Otherwise 1 in 4, if the level has none | <Cite gml="scripts/scrShopItemsGen.gml:61-64" /> |

So a Weapon Shop sells each weapon once at most, and bombs as often as its rolls give. The last four rows
share one roll, made at the start of each try <Cite gml="scripts/scrShopItemsGen.gml:41" />.

## Shopkeeper

<Clip id="weapon-shop/buy" />

The Shopkeeper sells as in every shop ([[general-store#shopkeeper|the General Store]] has the details): he
names the price of a ware the player holds, and the pay key buys it
<Cite gml="objects/oShopkeeper/step.gml:72-85" /> <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:1359-1389" />. A
weapon bought stays in the player's hands, his own <Cite gml="scripts/scrStealItem.gml:243-313" />;
a Bomb Bag or Box is used up, adding its bombs. In the clip, the player walks into the shop, picks up the
Pistol, which the Shopkeeper prices at $5,000, and pays <Cite clip="weapon-shop/buy" />.

A weapon held unpaid can be used inside the shop: only leaving the shop's room with it is theft
<Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:541-557" />. Using it on the Shopkeeper is another matter. A bullet
or a pellet takes 4 of his hit points and stuns him, and he gets up angry; the Whip, the Machete's slash
or a [[throwing|thrown item]] makes him attack at once <Cite gml="objects/oBullet/collision_oEnemy.gml:1-36" />
<Cite gml="objects/oShopkeeper/step.gml:465-477" /> <Cite gml="objects/oShopkeeper/collision_oWhip.gml:1-19" />.
None of these marks the player a thief, though the wares he gives away can then be carried out
([[hit-shopkeeper-free-wares]]; [[general-store#stealing|the General Store]] has the rules) <Cite quirk="1" row="Hitting a Shopkeeper frees the wares without marking the player a thief." />.
Angered, he gives the wares away and attacks with his own [[shotgun|Shotgun]]
<Cite gml="objects/oShopkeeper/step.gml:337-408" />.

<Clip id="shotgun/shopkeeper" />

In the second clip, the player fires the shop's unpaid Shotgun, first away from the Shopkeeper and then
into him: the six pellets take more than his hit points, and he drops his own Shotgun and dies, which
leaves the wares free <Cite clip="shotgun/shopkeeper" /> <Cite gml="objects/oItem/step.gml:8-15" />. A
dead Shopkeeper marks the player a murderer for the rest of the game, whatever killed him
([[accidental-murder]]) <Cite gml="objects/oShopkeeper/step.gml:486-499" /> <Cite quirk="1" row="Any Shopkeeper's death marks the player a murderer, whatever killed him." />.

## Stealing

Carrying a ware out of the shop's room unpaid angers the Shopkeeper ("COME BACK HERE, THIEF!") and gives
it to the player as if bought <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:541-557" />
<Cite gml="objects/oItem/step.gml:17-34" />. A theft marks the player a thief into the next level, whose
exit has a Shopkeeper waiting by it and whose shops' wares are free
([[general-store#stealing|the General Store]] has the rules) <Cite gml="scripts/scrInitLevel.gml:245-257" />.

## Locations

A level from 2 to 15 that rolls a shop builds one of seven kinds, each as likely, so a shop is a Weapon
Shop one time in seven (one in six on a Temple [[sacrifice-pit|sacrifice pit]] level, which never has a
[[kissing-parlor|Kissing Parlor]]) <Cite gml="scripts/scrLevelGen.gml:239-297" /> <Cite
gml="scripts/scrRoomGen.gml:173-204" /> <Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGenYeti.gml:149-181" />
<Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGen4.gml:155-188" />.
[[mines#shops|The Mines]] has how levels roll shops. A [[black-market|Black Market]] has five shops that
sell items, as a rule one of each kind, a Weapon Shop among them ([[two-general-stores]] is the exception) <Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGenMarket.gml:117-140" /> <Cite quirk="1" row="A Black Market's fifth item shop can be a second General Store." />.

  <Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGen.gml:182" /> <Cite gml="objects/oShopkeeper/step.gml:128" />. Its sign shows a
