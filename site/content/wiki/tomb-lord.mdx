Tomb Lords live in [[temple|the Temple]], at most one to a level, and in the
[[city-of-gold|City of Gold]]. Two blocks tall, slow and with <Stat id="tomb-lord.hp" />, a Tomb Lord is a
mini-boss: the danger is its flies, a stream of them that it spits at a player level with it, each costing
him <Stat id="tomb-lord.fly-damage" />, and they hit him again and again with no pause. On level 13, the
first of the Temple, killing it gives the [[sceptre|Sceptre]].

## Behaviour

A Tomb Lord walks at <Stat id="tomb-lord.walk-speed" /> (<Stat id="tomb-lord.walk-speed" as="per-second" />),
turning around at walls. When the player is behind it, low enough to be within 16 pixels of its feet, it
turns around to face him, at most once every 30 steps <Cite gml="objects/oTombLord/step.gml:47-91" />
<Cite gml="objects/oTombLord/other_animation_end.gml:1-10" />. It acts only while it's on screen or just
off it <Cite gml="objects/oTombLord/step.gml:3-5" />. Nothing stuns it: anything that stuns other monsters
just leaves it walking <Cite gml="objects/oTombLord/step.gml:111" />.

### The swarm

<Clip id="tomb-lord/flies" />

When the player is in front of it, at any distance, and within 32 pixels of its middle in height, a Tomb Lord
stops, opens its mouth, and spits a stream of flies at him, one every step for about a third of a second
<Cite gml="objects/oTombLord/step.gml:60-66" /> <Cite gml="objects/oTombLord/step.gml:92-110" />. The flies
fly the way it faces at <Stat id="tomb-lord.fly-speed" />, drifting a little up or down, until they hit
something: a wall or block puffs one out <Cite gml="objects/oFly/create.gml:1-2" />
<Cite gml="objects/oFly/step.gml:1-5" /> <Cite gml="objects/oFly/collision_oSolid.gml:1-3" />. After a swarm
it rests for 30 steps, and can't spit again for <Stat id="tomb-lord.attack-cooldown" /> (<Stat
id="tomb-lord.attack-cooldown" as="seconds" />) <Cite gml="objects/oTombLord/other_animation_end.gml:11-16" />
<Cite gml="objects/oTombLord/step.gml:36" />. The clip shows the player whipping a Tomb Lord from the floor
it stands on, which does it no harm, and two of its flies killing him <Cite clip="tomb-lord/flies" />.

## Defeating

<Clip id="tomb-lord/wind-up" />

A Tomb Lord has <Stat id="tomb-lord.hp" />, and nothing stuns it. The Whip only hurts it from above the
middle of its body, and at most once every <Stat id="tomb-lord.whip-cooldown" bare /> steps
<Cite gml="objects/oTombLord/collision_oWhip.gml:1-8" />. A player standing on its floor can't reach that
high: a whip swung in front of him there does nothing, as the first clip on this page shows, but from a step
one block up it takes a hit point <Cite clip="tomb-lord/whip" />. The whip's wind-up behind the player's
back is another matter: the Tomb Lord takes it through the code most monsters share, which has neither the
height limit nor the pause, so it takes a hit point every step it touches, about 3 a swing
<Cite gml="objects/oEnemy/collision_oWhipPre.gml:1-8" />. The clip shows the player with his back to a Tomb
Lord, the wind-up taking 3 <Cite clip="tomb-lord/wind-up" />.

## Locations

- **[[temple|The Temple]].** On level 13, the Temple's first, level generation always tries to place a Tomb
  Lord; on levels 14 and 15, it does with a 1 in 4 chance <Cite gml="scripts/scrEntityGen.gml:382-385" />.
  It goes on a block with two blocks of open space above it, outside the starting room: a 1 in 40 chance
  for each such block, until one is placed, so a level has one at most
  <Cite gml="scripts/scrEntityGen.gml:452-464" />.
- **[[city-of-gold|The City of Gold]].** Two of the room layouts only the City of Gold uses have a Tomb Lord
  in them <Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGen4.gml:122-140" /> <Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGen4.gml:426-429" />.
- **Custom levels**, as the [[level-editor|level editor]]'s tile `T`
  <Cite gml="scripts/scrCreateTileObj.gml:221-224" />.

## Tips

- A Tomb Lord only spits at a player level with it and in front of it: above or below it, or behind it, the
  player is safe from the flies.
- From its own floor, whip it with the player's back to it: the wind-up takes about 3 a swing, where a
  forward swing does nothing.
- One Bomb kills it. On level 13, that's the quickest way to the [[sceptre|Sceptre]].
- Its flies don't pause between hits, so at full health two flies are enough to kill the player.

      }
