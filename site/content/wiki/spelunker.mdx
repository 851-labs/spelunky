Everything in the game happens around him: he starts at a level's entrance, and the level ends when he goes
through its exit, carrying what he can <Cite gml="scripts/scrInitLevel.gml:202-203" />
<Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:756-880" />. The keys that move him are on the [[controls]] page, his
hearts on [[health|Health]]'s, and what hurts him on [[damage|Damage and stun]]'s; this page is about the
character: how he moves, what he carries, how he dies, and who can take his place.

## Behaviour

### Moving

<Clip id="spelunker/moves" />

He walks at up to <Stat id="spelunker.walk-speed" /> (<Stat id="spelunker.walk-speed" as="per-second" />) and
runs at up to <Stat id="spelunker.run-speed" /> (<Stat id="spelunker.run-speed" as="per-second" />) while
the run key is held on the ground, or once attack has been held for 10 steps of walking <Cite
gml="scripts/characterStepEvent.gml:117-129" /> <Cite gml="scripts/characterStepEvent.gml:685-698" /> <Cite
gml="scripts/characterStepEvent.gml:797-800" />. A jump from the ground goes higher the longer jump is
held, up to 10 steps, and farther from a run <Cite gml="scripts/characterStepEvent.gml:338-380" />; the
[[spring-shoes|Spring Shoes]] give it half as much push again <Cite gml="scripts/characterStepEvent.gml:357" />.
Holding up while standing still, he looks up <Cite gml="scripts/characterStepEvent.gml:382-392" />; holding
down, he ducks, and moves while ducking at a crawl <Cite gml="scripts/characterStepEvent.gml:539-546" />
<Cite gml="scripts/characterStepEvent.gml:700-727" />. Down on a ladder's top climbs down the ladder, and on
any other platform he can stand on drops through it <Cite gml="scripts/characterStepEvent.gml:546-578" />.

**Hanging.** Falling against a wall with the key toward it held, he grabs its edge and hangs, if there's no
block above the edge and none under his feet. The [[climbing-gloves|Climbing Gloves]] let him grab any wall,
and a tree's trunk or an [[arrow|Arrow]] stuck in a wall can be hung from too
<Cite gml="scripts/characterStepEvent.gml:400-469" />. Jump climbs up from a hang, jump with the key away
from the wall lets go, and down with jump drops <Cite gml="scripts/characterStepEvent.gml:494-529" />. In the
clip, he jumps at a ledge with right held, hangs from it, and climbs up with a jump <Cite clip="spelunker/moves" />.

<Clip id="spelunker/climb" />

**Climbing and crawling.** Up beside a ladder or a [[rope|Rope]] climbs onto it
<Cite gml="scripts/characterStepEvent.gml:606-632" />. Crawling toward the edge he's ducking on, he flips
over it into a hang, dropping what he holds <Cite gml="scripts/characterStepEvent.gml:803-866" />. The clip
shows both, in the tutorial: a ladder, then a crawl off a step into a flip hang, and a long fall that stuns
him <Cite clip="spelunker/climb" />.

**Water and webs.** In water he sinks slowly and can jump out from its surface. A [[web|Web]] holds him
nearly still, cutting his speed to a fifth each step, and each jump in it wears the web down <Cite gml="scripts/characterStepEvent.gml:294-319" /> <Cite gml="scripts/characterStepEvent.gml:743-771" />.
A [[ball-and-chain|ball and chain]] holds him within 24 pixels of the ball, so he can't climb away from it <Cite gml="scripts/characterStepEvent.gml:898-927" />.

### What he carries

He holds one item at a time in his hands: down and attack beside an item picks it up, and attack throws it
([[throwing|Throwing]]) <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:1203-1215" />. Equipment isn't held but worn:
picking up a free piece, or paying for one, puts it on for the rest of the game
<Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:1297" /> <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:1359-1390" />. [[bomb|Bombs]] and [[rope|Ropes]] are counts, which the
item key takes out into his hands in turn <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:954-1044" />. His hearts,
bombs and ropes stop at 99 <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:9-11" />.

Going through a level's exit, he takes a light item to the next level in his hands, puts an unlit bomb or a
rope back in his count, and leaves a heavy one behind. A [[golden-idol|Golden Idol]] is cashed in, and a living
[[damsel|Damsel]] is saved <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:767-851" />.

### Hurt, stunned and killed

<Clip id="spelunker/death" />

He starts a game with <Stat id="spelunker.hearts" />, <Stat id="spelunker.bombs" /> and
<Stat id="spelunker.ropes" /> <Cite gml="scripts/scrClearGlobals.gml:103-114" />. Monsters, traps,
explosions and long falls take hearts and often stun him; while stunned he ignores every key and drops what
he holds ([[damage|Damage and stun]], which has every source and its hearts). [[stomping|Stomping]] a monster
and the [[whip|Whip]] are his own attacks.

With no hearts left on a level, he dies, unless he wears the [[ankh|Ankh]]: his hearts are set to 0, the HUD
goes and the game's records are updated <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:1849-1921" />, and "GAME OVER"
shows <Cite gml="objects/oGame/draw.gml:12" />.
His body still takes damage, and once his hearts are below −99 it bursts into blood and vanishes
<Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:1484-1488" />. In the clip, he drops a [[bomb|Bomb]] at his feet and
stands on it <Cite clip="spelunker/death" />. Attack or start then finishes counting the money, and pressed
again goes to the [[high-scores-room|high scores room]], with everything cleared for a new game <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:907-941" />.

<Clip id="ankh/revive" />

With the Ankh, he comes back with <Stat id="ankh.revive" />, "THE ANKH SHATTERS!", invincible for
<Stat id="ankh.revive-invincibility" as="seconds" />, and the Ankh is gone: at the level's entrance, or
inside the [[moai|Moai]] while it's active, or at a set place near the left wall in
[[olmecs-lair|Olmec's Lair]] <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:1853-1896" />.

**The deaths table.** When Spelunky closes, it writes `stats.txt`, with a table of deaths by level, one by
monster and one by everything else (rock, explosion, crushed, long fall, spikes, boulder, the traps, the pit
and lava) <Cite gml="objects/oScreen/other_game_end.gml:21" /> <Cite gml="scripts/scrWriteStats.gml:83-172" />.
Each death is tallied by the code that deals it, on a real level only
<Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:1513-1703" />. Some rows don't count what they say: the explosion row
counts the blasts he survives <Cite quirk="1" row="The deaths table's explosion and trap rows don't count deaths." />, a few monsters' kills are never tallied ([[damage#quirks]])
<Cite quirk="1" row="The bat's type is never set." /> <Cite quirk="1" row="The Megamouth's kills of the player are never tallied." /> <Cite quirk="1" row="The zombie's, the vampire's and the Dead Fish's kills of the player are never tallied." /> <Cite quirk="1" row="A blob of magma's kills of the player are never tallied." />, and nothing adds to the boulder row, as a boulder kills by crushing, which is tallied as
crushed <Cite gml="scripts/scrWriteStats.gml:162" /> <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:1492-1511" />
<Cite quirk="1" row="Nothing counts a boulder's kill in the deaths table's boulder row." /> <Cite replay="fidelity/replays/trap-boulder-crush.txt" />. A death adds to its level's
row only when the player leaves the game over screen with attack or start, so closing the game on that screen
leaves it out <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:917" /> <Cite quirk="1" row="A death counts in its level's row only when the game over screen is left." />
<Cite replay="fidelity/replays/flow-pause-game-over-new-game.txt" />. Pausing on the step after a monster
takes his last heart, before his own step sees it, catches him still alive, and quitting from the pause then
leaves the whole game out of the high scores too: its play, its death and any record it set <Cite quirk="1" row="A death counts in its level's row only when the game over screen is left." /> <Cite replay="fidelity/replays/flow-pause-dying-quit-seed-1.txt" />.

### Playing as someone else

<Clip id="spelunker/change" />

Two doors change who the player is, and each changes him back the next time:

| Door | Where it is | Who | Starts with | |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| The Changing Room | The high scores room, beside the gold trophy for 8 Damsels saved in a game | The [[damsel\|Damsel]] | 4 hearts, 4 bombs, 4 ropes | <Cite gml="objects/oHighscores/create.gml:86-102" /> <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/other_animation_end.gml:173-191" /> |

([[damsel#playing-as-her|playing as her]]). The Tunnel Man swings a [[mattock|Mattock]] in place of the Whip,
only on the ground, and can't jump in the middle of a swing ([[tunnel-man#playing-as-him|playing as him]])
<Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:1181-1200" /> <Cite gml="scripts/characterStepEvent.gml:51-59" />. Who
the player is lasts until Spelunky is closed, and a new game starts each with their own hearts, bombs and
ropes <Cite gml="objects/oGlobals/create.gml:46-47" /> <Cite gml="scripts/scrClearGlobals.gml:103-114" />. In the clip, the Spelunker goes into the Changing
Room and comes out as the Damsel <Cite clip="spelunker/change" />.

## Trivia

- The player's object is `oPlayer1`, and his code sets `player = 1`, with the comment "player 2 uses
  different keyboard keys than player 1"; there's no player 2 <Cite gml="scripts/characterCreateEvent.gml:54" />.
- His create event keeps two values "for debugging": how many levels an exit moves him on, 1
  <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/create.gml:4-6" /> <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:875-876" />.
- A new game still gives him 3 flares, "not used any more" <Cite gml="scripts/scrClearGlobals.gml:115" />.
- His body is never destroyed. Eaten by a [[mantrap|Mantrap]], crushed, or burst into blood, it's hidden and
  held still where it was: "instead of destroying the player instance when dead, we occasionally make him
  disappear" <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:62-71" />.
