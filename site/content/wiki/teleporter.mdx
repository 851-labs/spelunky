Held, it moves the player a random 4 to 8 blocks the way he faces, or up with the up key, through anything
in between. Where that's inside rock, it looks a few blocks higher, and if it finds no room there, the
player is crushed. A monster where he lands is destroyed outright. It has no wait between uses.
[[rare-shop|Rare Shops]] and the [[dice-house|Dice House]] sell it, and [[crate|Crates]] and buried blocks
give it away.

## Usage

<Clip id="teleporter/snake" />

The Teleporter is picked up like any item, crouching over it and pressing the attack key. While it's held,
the attack key uses it, on the ground or in the air; crouching, the key puts it down in front of the player
instead <Cite gml="scripts/scrUseItem.gml:398-424" />. It isn't thrown. Each use:

1. **Picks the spot**: <Stat id="teleporter.distance" /> (16 pixels times a whole number from 4 to 8) the
   way the player faces, or that far above him with up held, no closer to the room's sides than 8 pixels and
   no higher than its top row <Cite gml="scripts/scrUseItem.gml:427-447" />.
2. **Looks for room**: while the spot is inside a block, it moves up a block, at most three
   times <Cite gml="scripts/scrUseItem.gml:448-453" />.
3. **Moves him there**, with three sparks where he was, and Kali's ball and chain with him if he has them;
   he's left falling <Cite gml="scripts/scrUseItem.gml:454-470" /> <Cite gml="scripts/scrUseItem.gml:482-486" />.
4. **Destroys a monster** that's where he lands: it loses 99 hit points, bleeds, and is
   gone <Cite gml="scripts/scrUseItem.gml:472-481" />.

In the clip, the player uses it facing a [[snake|Snake]] five blocks away; he reappears where it was, and
the Snake bursts into blood and is gone. The game's tally of Snakes killed doesn't
change <Cite clip="teleporter/snake" />.

With down held and the player not crouching, the key does nothing <Cite gml="scripts/scrUseItem.gml:400-425" />.
The Teleporter sets no wait of its own between uses, but a sideways use only picks a new spot once the wait
another weapon's shot starts has run out; inside it, the player is moved to the spot its last use
picked <Cite gml="scripts/scrUseItem.gml:433-447" />, or to where he last stood holding a ware he hadn't paid
for, a readied bomb arrow or pressing the pay key, as the game's check for a shop writes the same
spot <Cite gml="scripts/isInShop.gml:7-8" /> <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:543-545" />. If none of these has set it on the level, the game
stops with an error <Cite gml="scripts/scrUseItem.gml:449" /> <Cite quirk="1" />.

The Teleporter goes with the player through a level's exit if he's holding it, and is in his hands on the
next level <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:842-850" /> <Cite gml="objects/oLevel/create.gml:25-30" />.
He drops it when he's stunned, or when he teeters too long at a ledge's
edge <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:329-345" /> <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:150-171" />.

The spot is picked only by distance, and it looks for room by moving up three blocks at most, so the player
can land inside rock. In the second clip, used facing left in a shop, it puts him five blocks away, inside
the rock three blocks up from where it first tried, and he dies on the spot with all four
hearts <Cite clip="teleporter/rock" /> <Cite replay="fidelity/replays/weapon-seed-40-teleporter.txt" />.

## Interactions

- **Sealed places.** It goes through walls, so it can put the player inside places nothing else opens,
  the [[moai|Moai]]'s chamber among them <Cite quirk="1" />.
- **The ball and chain.** Kali's ball and chain come with the player wherever it takes
  him <Cite gml="scripts/scrUseItem.gml:461-470" />.

## Tips

- It looks no more than three blocks up for room, so beside thick rock it's safer used along an open
  passage, where the spot it picks is likely to be open.
- It destroys a monster outright, a [[megamouth|Megamouth]]'s 40 hit points or a [[yeti-king|Yeti King]]'s 30
  alike.

## Trivia

- Before it moves the player, it puts him no higher than 8 pixels from the room's top, a line that changes
  nothing, as the next ones move him anyway <Cite gml="scripts/scrUseItem.gml:458-460" /> <Cite quirk="1" />.
- A commented-out `// state = STANDING;` sits a few lines above the one that leaves him
  falling <Cite gml="scripts/scrUseItem.gml:471-486" />.
