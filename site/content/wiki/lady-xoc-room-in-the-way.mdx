The [[city-of-gold|City of Gold]] lays out its rooms as the [[temple|Temple]] does, on a path from the entrance to
the exit, and then writes the Lady Xoc statue's room over one room of the third row, at random. It doesn't look
at what the path needed there, and the statue's room has a solid floor, so when it takes the room the path
drops through, nothing leads down to the exit until the player breaks the floor open. On a sacrifice pit's
level it can take the pit's third room, and seal the pit's lava off from its shaft.

## What happens

The City of Gold here is a [[sacrifice-pit|sacrifice pit]] too, and the statue took the third room of the pit's
shaft. The player lifts the pit's golden idol, the floor under it gives way, and he falls a room down onto the
statue, stunned, and loses a heart, not three rooms down into the pit's lava
<Cite clip="quirks/xoc-statue-in-sacrifice-pit" />.

<Clip id="quirks/xoc-statue-in-sacrifice-pit" />

On another City, the statue took the room the path drops through to the exit's row, so the run bombs through
the floor beside it. The player drops through the hole, picks up an emerald and takes the exit
<Cite clip="city-of-gold/to-olmec" />.

<Clip id="city-of-gold/to-olmec" />

## Why

`scrLevelGen` lays the path first, a room at a time from the top row to the bottom, marking each room it drops
out of as a drop room. Then, in the City, one line overwrites a room of the third row:

```gml scripts/scrLevelGen.gml:136
if (global.cityOfGold) global.roomPath[rand(0,3), 2] = 6;
```

`rand(0,3)` is any of the row's four rooms, whatever the path made it. Room type 6 is the statue's, open at the
top and sides with a solid bottom row:

```gml scripts/scrRoomGen4.gml:189-192
else if (roomPath == 6) // Lady Xoc
{
    strTemp = "000000000000X0000000000000000000000000000000000000000000000000000000001111111111";
}
```

The `X` is the statue, 30 blocks five wide and six tall <Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGen4.gml:380-392" />, and
the last ten `1`s are the room's floor. If the path dropped through that room, the floor now stands between it
and the exit's row <Cite quirk="1" />.

A sacrifice pit is laid before the path: on 1 Temple level in 8, the City's too, `scrLevelGen` marks a whole
column as the pit, its top, two shaft rooms and its bottom, which the path never enters
<Cite gml="scripts/scrLevelGen.gml:71-87" />. The statue's line comes after, and one time in four picks the
pit's column, writing the statue's room over the pit's second shaft room. The shaft above then ends on the
statue's top, and the statue room's floor seals the pit's bottom, with its lava and its Big Ruby blocks,
under it <Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGen4.gml:193-204" /> <Cite quirk="1" />.

## Reproducing it

Reach the City of Gold and look at where the statue is. When it stands in the room above the exit's row, on the
way down, the way to the exit has to be broken open beside or through it, with a [[bomb|Bomb]] or the
[[mattock|Mattock]]; the statue's blocks, which break into gold, and six of them into gems, are as good a way down
as any <Cite gml="objects/oXocBlock/destroy.gml:1-29" />. The replay, with seed 9, finds the statue on the drop
room above the exit's row: the run bombs the statue's top for its gems, then bombs a hole through the floor
beside it and drops to the exit <Cite replay="fidelity/replays/city-of-gold-seed-9-to-olmec.txt" />.

A City that's a sacrifice pit, with the statue in the pit's column, is 1 City in 32. The replay of the first
clip reaches one with seed 9, the statue in the pit's third room: lifting the idol takes away the four trap
blocks under it, and the player falls a room onto the statue, stunned, losing a heart, and never lower
<Cite replay="fidelity/replays/city-of-gold-seed-9-xoc-pit.txt" />.

## In the port

The spelunky.ai port reproduces it exactly: it's in docs/quirks.md's list of the 1.1 source's bugs
<Cite quirk="1" />, and the oracle's fingerprints of the replays pin the rooms' layout, the statue's place and
the player's fall. No option turns it off.
