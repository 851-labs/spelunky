Zombies come only from the [[restless-dead|graveyard levels]] of [[jungle|the Jungle]], where level
generation makes them in numbers. A Zombie stands still until the player comes near, then hops at him, in
short hops and the odd long one, and bites when it lands on him. With <Stat id="zombie.hp" />, it dies to any
damage, and falls apart into bones and a [[skull|Skull]], like a [[skeleton|Skeleton]].

## Behaviour

<Clip id="zombie/hop" />

A Zombie stands where it is until the player comes within <Stat id="zombie.wake-range" /> of it, and then it
starts hopping at him, with a groan <Cite gml="objects/oZombie/step.gml:47-55" />. Each time it's on the
ground it hops toward him: 1 time in 4 a long hop, <Stat id="zombie.hop-speed" />
(<Stat id="zombie.hop-speed" as="per-second" />) across and 2–4 pixels per step up, otherwise a short one,
1 pixel per step across and 1–2 up <Cite gml="objects/oZombie/step.gml:66-103" />. It keeps that speed until
it lands, and it falls slowly, at a third of most monsters' gravity, so a long hop carries it far: about
90 pixels for a long hop 3 pixels per step up, and about 12 for a short hop 1 pixel per step up <Cite
gml="objects/oZombie/create.gml:8-9" /> <Cite gml="objects/oEnemy/create.gml:16-17" /> <Cite clip="zombie/hop" />. It bounces back off a wall and drops at once when it hits a ceiling
<Cite gml="objects/oZombie/step.gml:32-40" /> <Cite gml="objects/oZombie/step.gml:110-111" />.

When it lands, it stands for <Stat id="zombie.rest" /> (<Stat id="zombie.rest" as="seconds" />) before
hopping at a player within <Stat id="zombie.wake-range" /> again, but if he's within 48 pixels it hops
again at once <Cite gml="objects/oZombie/step.gml:50-63" />. It never hops at a swimming player
<Cite gml="objects/oZombie/step.gml:53" />. The clip shows a Zombie waking as the player walks up, closing in
with four short hops and a long one that lands on him, then resting before it hops again.

A Zombie only acts while it's on screen or just off it <Cite gml="objects/oZombie/step.gml:3-5" />.

## Damage

When the player touches a Zombie without stomping it, he loses <Stat id="zombie.damage" />, is knocked away
from it, and blinks, invincible, for 30 steps (1 second)
<Cite gml="objects/oEnemy/collision_oCharacter.gml:14-25" />. This is the touch code most monsters share;
the Zombie has no attack of its own beyond landing on him. The hop clip shows a bite taking the player from
four hearts to three.

## Locations

Level generation makes Zombies only on [[restless-dead|graveyard levels]], which 1 in 10 levels of
[[jungle|the Jungle]] is <Cite gml="scripts/scrInitLevel.gml:147-148" />
<Cite gml="scripts/scrClearGlobals.gml:56" />. On top of each block with open space above it (no block,
monster or Spikes there), outside the starting room and shops, a graveyard level makes a Zombie with a 1 in
25 chance, or failing that a [[vampire|Vampire]] with a 1 in 160 chance, in place of the Jungle's usual
[[mantrap|Mantraps]], [[caveman|Cavemen]] and [[frog|Frogs]], and under water as well as on dry ground
<Cite gml="scripts/scrEntityGen.gml:276-290" />.

Custom levels can have them, as the [[level-editor|level editor]]'s tile `z`
<Cite gml="scripts/scrCreateTileObj.gml:164-167" />.

## Tips

- A Zombie stays put until the player is within <Stat id="zombie.wake-range" />, so one can be passed at a
  distance, or above or below it.
- Within 48 pixels a Zombie hops again as soon as it lands, so standing next to one invites a bite; a long
  hop can also carry it clean over the player.
- A swimming player doesn't wake Zombies.
- Any hit kills a Zombie, so the Whip or a stomp deals with one.

## Trivia

- The Zombie's code never sets its `type` string, so it keeps the parent object's `"None"`
  <Cite gml="objects/oEnemy/create.gml:4" />. The touch code's deaths-by-enemy tally checks for `"Zombie"`,
  so a Zombie that takes the player's last heart isn't counted, and the Zombie row of the table the game
  writes to `stats.txt` always reads `???` with 0 deaths
  <Cite gml="objects/oEnemy/collision_oCharacter.gml:27-35" /> <Cite gml="scripts/scrWriteStats.gml:110-122" /> <Cite quirk="1" />.
- The code names five states, but sets only three of them: the walking (`WALK`) and drowned (`DROWNED`)
  states are never used. Its step event sends any state it doesn't handle back to standing, except the
  drowned one
  <Cite gml="objects/oZombie/create.gml:21-26" /> <Cite gml="objects/oZombie/step.gml:104-108" /> <Cite quirk="1" />.
