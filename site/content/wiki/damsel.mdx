The player carries a Damsel like any heavy item, and she can be thrown, sold, hurt and killed like one. The
game counts every Damsel saved, and the end-of-game statistics count those bought, saved and killed, and as
"grabbed" each one the first time she's knocked off her feet <Cite gml="objects/oDamsel/step.gml:237-245" />. In a [[kissing-parlor|Kissing Parlor]] the [[shopkeeper|Shopkeeper]] sells her kisses, and her; on
[[kali|Kali]]'s altar she is a sacrifice worth 8 favor.

## Behaviour

<Clip id="damsel/throw" />

Left alone, a Damsel stands still and counts down <Stat id="damsel.yell-wait" /> (<Stat id="damsel.yell-wait"
as="seconds" />), then cries out, a "HELP!" bubble rising over her head, and starts counting again
<Cite gml="objects/oDamsel/step.gml:179-213" /> <Cite gml="objects/oDamsel/other_animation_end.gml:1-5" />. She
doesn't move on her own until something knocks her over.

Thrown, or falling faster than 2 pixels a step, she lies stunned where she lands. Once she's on the ground she
counts down her stun, <Stat id="damsel.stun-time" /> (<Stat id="damsel.stun-time" as="seconds" />) after a
throw, then gets up and runs, at <Stat id="damsel.run-speed" /> (<Stat id="damsel.run-speed" as="per-second"
/>), turning back at walls but running off ledges <Cite gml="objects/oDamsel/step.gml:145-150" />
<Cite gml="objects/oDamsel/step.gml:214-308" /> <Cite gml="scripts/scrUseItem.gml:597-603" />. Caught in a web,
she counts down as on the ground.

Cracking the [[whip|Whip]] at her doesn't hurt her: she cries out and hops <Cite
gml="objects/oDamsel/collision_oWhip.gml:13-23" />. The hop's fall is fast enough to count as a throw, though,
so she lies down stunned where she lands, for whatever her cry-for-help countdown had left rather than a
throw's <Stat id="damsel.stun-time" />, and is counted as "grabbed" <Cite clip="damsel/throw" /> <Cite
quirk="1" /> <Cite replay="fidelity/replays/damsel-whip-hop-thrown.txt" /> <Cite
clip="quirks/damsel-whip-hop-thrown" />.
In the clip the player whips a Damsel, who hops and lies stunned without losing a hit point; he picks her up
and throws her, and she lies stunned for <Stat id="damsel.stun-time" /> before she gets up and runs.

In water she sinks slowly, at a third of her usual gravity <Cite gml="objects/oDamsel/step.gml:34-48" />.

## Interactions

### Rescuing her

<Clip id="damsel/rescue" />

The player picks a Damsel up with down and the action key, as any item, and carries her held in front of
him; she's heavy, so a throw sends her at half a light item's speed <Cite
gml="objects/oDamsel/create.gml:11" /> <Cite gml="scripts/scrUseItem.gml:609-635" />. Touching a level's exit
with her in his hands, or pressing up there, takes her through the door: she walks in, and the game counts a
save <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:2068-2110" /> <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:782-806" />. A
dead Damsel carried to the door is dropped there instead <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:807-812" />. A
Damsel running free who runs into an exit rescues herself <Cite gml="objects/oDamsel/step.gml:3-24" />.

In the screen between levels the saved Damsel stands beside the player and kisses him, a heart rising from
them, and she is in the level's loot <Cite gml="objects/oTransition/create.gml:42-45" />
<Cite gml="objects/oTransition/alarm_0.gml:89-95" />. Leaving that screen adds 1 heart, even when the player skips
it <Cite gml="objects/oDamselKiss/other_room_end.gml:1" />. In the clip, the player carries a Damsel to the exit; between levels she kisses him, and level 2
starts with 5 hearts.

### The Kissing Parlor

A [[kissing-parlor|Kissing Parlor]]'s Damsel is for sale, beside its [[shopkeeper|Shopkeeper]]
<Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGen.gml:456-461" />. A kiss costs <Stat id="damsel.kiss-price" at="2" /> on level 2,
and $5,000 more for each level after (<Stat id="damsel.kiss-price" at="15"
/> on level 15): with the money, the pay key beside her buys one, and the player gains a heart
("NOW AIN'T SHE SWEET!") <Cite gml="scripts/getKissValue.gml:1" />
<Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:1422-1457" />. The Damsel herself sells for three times that: carried
and paid for, she's the player's to rescue ("YOU MUST BE IN LOVE!") <Cite gml="objects/oDamsel/create.gml:12-14" />
<Cite gml="scripts/scrStealItem.gml:314-324" />. Carried out unpaid, she's stolen, as any ware. A thief or a
killer of Shopkeepers gets no kiss.

Hurting her angers the Shopkeeper ("HEY, ONLY I CAN DO THAT!"): a crack of the whip, a [[throwing|thrown item]], or her
death <Cite gml="objects/oDamsel/collision_oWhip.gml:19-22" /> <Cite gml="objects/oItem/step.gml:392-395" /> <Cite gml="objects/oDamsel/step.gml:50-54" />.

### Sacrificing her

<Clip id="damsel/sacrifice" />

A stunned or dead Damsel lying still on one of [[kali|Kali]]'s altars for 20 steps is sacrificed: she bursts
into flame, Kali accepts her ("KALI ACCEPTS YOUR SACRIFICE!") and the player's favor rises by
<Stat id="damsel.favor" bare /> (a dead Shopkeeper's gives 6); at a favor of −8 or lower, Kali devours her for
nothing <Cite gml="objects/oDamsel/step.gml:311-348" />. The favor can earn a gift, as in the clip, where the
player throws a Damsel down on an altar and Kali gives a [[compass|Compass]] <Cite gml="scripts/scrGetFavorMsg.gml:67-96" />.

### Playing as her

The high scores room keeps the most Damsels saved in one game. A best of 4 earns a bronze trophy, 6 a silver
and 8 a gold one, and with the gold a door appears beside it <Cite gml="objects/oHighscores/create.gml:86-102" />.
Going through it switches the player to the Damsel, with 4 hearts, 4 bombs and 4 ropes, and back again
<Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/other_animation_end.gml:173-191" />. The playable Damsel moves and fights as the
[[spelunker|Spelunker]] does, with her own sprites. The Tunnel Man's door needs 8 saves too
([[tunnel-man#playing-as-him|playing as him]]).

## Trivia

- When the player is the Damsel, the character to rescue is the [[spelunker|Spelunker]]: the same object,
  drawn with his sprites, and the messages say "him" ("I'LL LET YOU HAVE HIM FOR $...", "NOW AIN'T HE
  SWEET!") <Cite gml="objects/oDamsel/create.gml:13-14" /> <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:1445-1446" />.
- Her sacrifice has a branch for a stunned Damsel worth half as much again, 12, but she's never in the
  stunned state it tests for, so it never runs <Cite gml="objects/oDamsel/step.gml:331" /> <Cite quirk="1" />.
- A Damsel killed by lava or spikes counts in the end-of-game statistics, but not in the screen between
  levels' kills or the game's kill total, where one crushed, or one whose wounds kill her as she lands, counts
  in all three <Cite gml="objects/oDamsel/step.gml:77-119" /> <Cite gml="objects/oDamsel/step.gml:58-69" />
  <Cite quirk="1" />. In a staged scene, one thrown onto spikes adds to the statistics' count and to neither
  of the others <Cite replay="fidelity/replays/damsel-spikes-not-a-kill.txt" />.
- A comment explains why her death checks for a sale itself: "if damsel dies offscreen sometimes it won't
  trigger shopkeeper" <Cite gml="objects/oDamsel/step.gml:50-54" />.
