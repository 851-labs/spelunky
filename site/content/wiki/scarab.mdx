Scarabs live only on [[dark-level|dark levels]], where they glow in the dark. One is treasure that moves: the
player collects it by touching it, for more than most gems are worth, but it darts away from him, and any
weapon that touches it destroys it for nothing.

## Behaviour

A Scarab hovers in the air; nothing pulls it down. It waits 10–30 steps (0.33–1 second), then darts off at
<Stat id="scarab.dart-speed" /> (<Stat id="scarab.dart-speed" as="per-second" />), slowing by 0.5 pixels a
step until it stops, and waits again. Within <Stat id="scarab.flee-range" /> of the player, it darts
straight away from him; farther off, in a random direction. It bounces off walls, floors and ceilings
<Cite gml="objects/oScarab/create.gml:11" /> <Cite gml="objects/oScarab/step.gml:18-63" />. It only moves
while it's on screen or just off it <Cite gml="objects/oScarab/step.gml:1-3" />. On a dark level it lights up
a small circle around itself <Cite gml="objects/oScreen/step_begin.gml:227-230" />.

## Damage

A Scarab never hurts the player: its touch is a pickup (see [Defeating](#defeating)).

## Defeating

<Clip id="scarab/collect" />

When the player touches a Scarab, he collects it: its value is added to his money, which counts up on screen,
it counts toward the Scarabs he has collected, and it vanishes in a shower of sparks
<Cite gml="objects/oScarab/collision_oCharacter.gml:1-6" /> <Cite gml="objects/oScarab/destroy.gml:1-4" />.
Its value depends on the area <Cite gml="objects/oScarab/create.gml:12-14" />:

The clip shows the player dropping onto a Scarab on a dark Temple level: his money starts counting up by
<Stat id="scarab.value-temple" /> <Cite clip="scarab/collect" />.

It has <Stat id="scarab.hp" />, so anything that hurts monsters destroys it, and the player gets nothing: no
money and no kill <Cite gml="objects/oScarab/step.gml:6-16" />.

## Locations

Level generation makes Scarabs only on [[dark-level|dark levels]], hovering under blocks with two open
spaces beneath them, a 1 in 40 chance for each such block:

- **[[mines|The Mines]]**: outside the starting room, once the rolls for a [[giant-spider|Giant Spider]]
  and a lamp have failed <Cite gml="scripts/scrEntityGen.gml:45-62" />.
- **[[jungle|The Jungle]]** <Cite gml="scripts/scrEntityGen.gml:264-271" />.
- **[[temple|The Temple]]** <Cite gml="scripts/scrEntityGen.gml:445-449" />.

[[ice-caves|The Ice Caves]] have the same roll, but no Ice Caves level is made dark (defiling an altar
can darken one later, after generation), so it never comes up <Cite gml="scripts/scrEntityGen.gml:350-354" />
<Cite gml="scripts/scrInitLevel.gml:169" /> <Cite gml="objects/oSacAltarLeft/destroy.gml:48-50" />. The level
editor has no tile for a Scarab.

## Tips

- A Scarab flees a player within 64 pixels, so it's easiest to catch while it waits between darts, or from
  above, as it never falls.
- Whipping a Scarab destroys it, and its money with it.
- Only dark levels have Scarabs, and they glow, so they can be spotted in the dark.
