Every shop has a lamp hanging from its ceiling, and a [[dark-level|dark level]] in the [[mines|Mines]] hangs
more of them under its blocks. Hanging, a lamp is part of the scenery; when the block it hangs from is
destroyed, it falls as a Lamp, a heavy item the player can carry and throw. On a dark level, carried or not,
a plain Lamp lights the cave around it, as a [[flare|Flare]] does. The [[kissing-parlor|Kissing Parlor]]'s lamp is
red, and falls as a red Lamp, which, unlike the plain one, lights nothing once it's down.

## Usage

<Clip id="lamp/fall" />

### Bringing it down

A hanging lamp isn't an item: it can't be picked up, and it flickers where it hangs
<Cite gml="objects/oLamp/create.gml:1" />. When the block above it is destroyed, the block's last act is to
replace it with a Lamp, red if the hanging lamp was, which then falls
<Cite gml="objects/oSolid/destroy.gml:24-33" />. Not when that block is a stone [[block|block]], or another
block whose destroy event is its own (ice, a trap's): that doesn't run this code, so the lamp stays hanging in the
air <Cite quirk="1" row="Most blocks but an area's plain rock skip what breaking a block does." />
<Cite replay="fidelity/replays/terrain-stone-block-lamp.txt" />. A shop's lamp hangs from the shop's ceiling, out of reach of a
[[bomb|Bomb]] on the floor, and a lit bomb in a shop within 96 pixels of the [[shopkeeper|Shopkeeper]]
angers him <Cite gml="objects/oBomb/step.gml:6-17" /> <Cite quirk="1" row="The rare items' only sources" />.
In the clip, in a Weapon shop whose shopkeeper is dead, the player throws a lit bomb up toward the lamp; it
goes off under the ceiling, the block the lamp hangs from breaks, and the lamp drops to the floor
<Cite clip="lamp/fall" />.

### Carrying it

A Lamp is picked up and thrown as any item is ([[throwing]]), but it's heavy, like a
[[golden-idol|Golden Idol]]: the player carries it in front of him at his own height, and throws it at 4 pixels a
step plus his own speed, rising at 2, or at 4 upward <Cite gml="objects/oLampItem/create.gml:3-8" />
<Cite gml="objects/oItem/step.gml:44-50" /> <Cite gml="scripts/scrUseItem.gml:609-635" />. He sets it down when
he takes an exit, so it stays on its level <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:815-820" />. Thrown faster than
2 pixels a step into a monster, it takes <Stat id="lamp.damage" /> from the monster nearest it, as any thrown
item does <Cite gml="objects/oItem/step.gml:256-350" />.

### Light

On a lit level a Lamp gives no light: there's no darkness for it to cut. On a [[dark-level|dark level]]:

| Lamp | Its own light | The player's light | |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Hanging, plain or red | Within 96 pixels of it | Widens as he nears it, within 200 pixels | <Cite gml="objects/oScreen/step_begin.gml:187-190" /> <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:89-94" /> |
| A plain Lamp, carried or lying | Within <Stat id="lamp.light" /> of it | Widens as he nears it, within 200 pixels | <Cite gml="objects/oScreen/step_begin.gml:191-194" /> <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:95-100" /> |
| A red Lamp, carried or lying | None | Unchanged | <Cite gml="objects/oScreen/step_begin.gml:167-194" /> <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:73-136" /> |

While the player is within 96 pixels of a red lamp, hanging or down, every light on the screen is tinted
red <Cite gml="objects/oScreen/step_begin.gml:156-177" />. The hanging red lamp is a kind of the plain one in the
game's objects, so the code that lights the hanging lamps lights it too; the red Lamp isn't a kind of the plain
Lamp, and the code that lights a Lamp's circle and widens the player's light names only the plain one
<Cite gml="objects/oScreen/step_begin.gml:191-194" /> <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:95-100" />. So a red
Lamp carried through a dark level lights nothing but its tint.

## Interactions

- **[[bomb|Bombs]].** An explosion breaks the block a lamp hangs from, which brings it down, and throws a Lamp
  away from it without breaking it <Cite gml="objects/oExplosion/collision_oSolid.gml:1-14" />
  <Cite gml="objects/oExplosion/collision_oItem.gml:30-36" />.
- **[[lava|Lava]].** A Lamp that touches lava stops and sinks, and is destroyed once it's under the surface
  <Cite gml="objects/oItem/step.gml:160-185" />.
- **Water** does nothing to a Lamp: the check that puts a Flare out is in the Flare's own code, and the Lamp
  has none <Cite gml="objects/oFlare/step.gml:6-20" />.

## Tips

- A plain Lamp is a light that water can't put out and that never runs out, but it can't leave its level:
  on a dark level, it's for lighting the way to the exit.
- A red Lamp is no use as a light: down, it lights nothing.
