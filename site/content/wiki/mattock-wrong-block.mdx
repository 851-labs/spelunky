The [[mattock|Mattock]] digs: its swing breaks a block ahead of the player, at his height or, if there's none
there, ahead of his feet. Which block it breaks isn't always the one it tested for. Depending on where he
stands, it can break the floor under him, so he drops through, or skip the block ahead and break the next.

## What happens

Facing left, the player swings a Mattock at the floor ahead of him: it breaks the block beyond that one, and
the block in front stays. He steps right and swings again: this time it breaks the block he stands on, and he
drops into the hole <Cite clip="quirks/mattock-wrong-block" />.

## Why

The Mattock's hit sits 16 pixels ahead of the player, at his height
<Cite gml="objects/oMattockHit/step.gml:5-14" />. When its animation ends, it tests the point at its position
for a block, then the point 9 pixels below, ahead of his feet, and breaks the block `instance_place` finds
there <Cite gml="objects/oMattockHit/other_animation_end.gml:1-11" />:

```gml objects/oMattockHit/other_animation_end.gml:1-11
hit = false;
if (collision_point(x, y, oSolid, 0, 0))
{
    obj = instance_place(x, y, oSolid);
    if (not obj.invincible) hit = true;
}
else if (collision_point(x, y+9, oSolid, 0, 0))
{
    obj = instance_place(x, y+9, oSolid);
    if (not obj.invincible) hit = true;
}
```

`collision_point` tests one point, but `instance_place` tests the hit's whole sprite box there, and returns
the first block in the level's order that overlaps it. The box is 11 pixels wide, so where the player stands
it can overlap two blocks: facing right with his position at 0 to 7 pixels into a block, the block under
him as well as the one ahead; facing left at 0 to 2, the block beyond the one ahead. When that other block
comes first, it breaks instead <Cite quirk="1" />. A custom level makes its blocks row by row from the left,
so there, facing right, half of all positions dig the player's own floor <Cite quirk="1" />.

## Reproducing it

Dig with the Mattock along a floor, facing right, standing near the left edge of a block. The replay of the
clip's staged scene, on a custom level, pins both cases: facing left, the swing breaks the block beyond the one
ahead; facing right, the block under the player, and he falls in
<Cite replay="fidelity/replays/weapon-mattock-wrong-block.txt" />.

## In the port

The spelunky.ai port reproduces it exactly: it's in docs/quirks.md's list of the 1.1 source's bugs
<Cite quirk="1" />, and the oracle's fingerprint of the replay pins the blocks broken. No option turns it off.
