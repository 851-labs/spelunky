An Arrow Trap looks like a carved stone block set into a wall, with an opening facing along a corridor. It
fires an arrow the first time anything moves in front of it, and never again. The arrow takes 2 hearts from
the player, as any fast arrow does, and hurts monsters as a thrown arrow does. On a [[dark-level|dark level]]
the traps are lit, glowing so that the player can see them.

## How it works

<Clip id="arrow-trap/fire" />

A trap has a sensor: an invisible strip in front of it, made one step after the trap, that reaches from its
face to the first block in its row, but no farther than 6 blocks for a trap facing left and 5 for one facing
right <Cite gml="objects/oArrowTrapLeft/create.gml:9" /> <Cite gml="objects/oArrowTrapLeft/alarm_1.gml:1-13" />
<Cite gml="objects/oArrowTrapRight/alarm_1.gml:1-17" />. The two directions' code measures the strip
differently, which is where the extra block comes from ([[arrow-trap-sensor-left-wider]]): the left-facing
one counts up to 96 pixels from the trap, the right-facing one up to 80 from the block in front of it. So a
player midway between two traps facing each other across 11 to 13 open blocks sets off only the left-facing one
<Cite quirk="1" /> <Cite replay="fidelity/replays/trap-arrow-sensor-left-wider.txt" />.

Anything in the strip that's moving sets the trap off: the player, a monster, an item, a piece of treasure, a
pushed block or a [[boulder|Boulder]]. The player also sets it off near the end of his drop from a ledge into
a hang from it, even without moving <Cite gml="objects/oArrowTrapTest/collision_oCharacter.gml:5-8" />
<Cite gml="objects/oArrowTrapTest/collision_oItem.gml:5" /> <Cite gml="objects/oArrowTrapTest/collision_oBoulder.gml:5" />. Standing still in the strip doesn't.

When it's set off, the trap fires an arrow from its face at 8 pixels a step (240 a second), plays its sound,
and its sensor is destroyed, so it never fires again <Cite gml="objects/oArrowTrapTest/collision_oCharacter.gml:10-28" />.
The first clip shows the player standing in a trap's strip, three blocks from it, without setting it off; when
he takes a step toward it, it fires, and the arrow hits him.

An arrow falls as it flies, and on the ground it loses 70% of its speed each step, as any item does
<Cite gml="objects/oItem/step.gml:93-110" /> <Cite gml="objects/oItem/create.gml:21" />. A trap fires from near the top of its block, so its arrow
along a floor lands after about three blocks and slides on too slowly to hurt. The second clip shows a
player setting a trap off from the far end of its strip: the arrow drops onto the floor and stops short of
him.

## Damage

The trap itself hurts nothing: its arrow does, the same arrow the [[bow|Bow]] shoots, while it's moving fast.

| Victim | Damage | |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The player | 2 hearts from an arrow faster than 3 pixels a step, which throws him the way it was going, stuns him for 20 steps (0.67 seconds) and is used up | <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:1513-1536" /> |
| Most monsters | 1 hit point, as from any [[throwing\|thrown item]] faster than 2 pixels a step | <Cite gml="objects/oItem/step.gml:256-363" /> |
| A [[vampire\|Vampire]] | 4 hit points: arrows are stakes to it ([[bow#damage\|the Bow's damage]]) | <Cite gml="objects/oItem/step.gml:265-266" /> |

An arrow that kills the player is counted in the end-of-game statistics' arrow row
<Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:1520-1521" />. The check takes the arrow nearest the player, not
necessarily the one touching him <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:1515" /> <Cite quirk="1" />.

## Locations

[[level-generation|Level generation]] turns some of the plain blocks of the walls into Arrow Traps, after it has placed
everything else:

| Area | Chance for each block | |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [[mines\|The Mines]] | 1 in 4 | <Cite gml="scripts/scrEntityGen.gml:145-171" /> |
| [[temple\|The Temple]] | 1 in 3 | <Cite gml="scripts/scrEntityGen.gml:497-524" /> |

A block only qualifies if it isn't in a shop, is more than 48 pixels from the entrance and not within 144
pixels of it on the same row, and has a block behind it and two open blocks in front of it: it faces left if
the block to its right is solid and the two to its left are open, else right if the opposite holds (a
right-facing trap's check also takes in the first pixel of a third block, so a two-block gap between walls only
ever has a trap in its right-hand wall) <Cite gml="scripts/scrEntityGen.gml:155-168" /> <Cite quirk="1" />. On a
[[dark-level|dark level]], the trap made is the lit kind <Cite gml="scripts/scrEntityGen.gml:158-166" />.

Arrow Traps are also:

- **In the Sun challenge room**, a bonus room reached from the [[scoring#the-high-scores-room|high scores]], whose traps
  fire every 100 to 200 steps whatever is in front of them, while the player is alive
  <Cite room="rSun" /> <Cite gml="objects/oArrowRepeaterL/step.gml:1-9" />.
- **In custom levels**, as the [[level-editor|level editor]]'s tiles `<` (facing left) and `>` (facing right)
  <Cite gml="scripts/scrCreateTileObj.gml:229-236" />.

## Avoiding

- **Use up its arrow.** A trap fires once, at whatever moves first: a [[rock|Rock]] or any other item thrown
  through its strip, a monster walking by, or the player stepping in at the far end, where the arrow lands
  short.
- **Destroy it.** A trap is a block, and a [[bomb|Bomb]] destroys it like one. One destroyed before it has
  fired leaves its arrow lying where it was <Cite gml="objects/oArrowTrapLeft/destroy.gml:1-10" />. The third
  clip shows a lit trap firing at the player as he drops down a ladder across its line, taking two hearts, and
  a bomb laid beside it destroying it.
- **Stand still.** Standing in the strip doesn't set it off, but taking a step does.

## Interactions

- **[[boulder|Boulders]]** set off the traps they roll past, as the Boulder's clips show
  <Cite replay="fidelity/replays/trap-boulder-crush.txt" />.
- **The [[bow|Bow]]** collects a trap's arrow, once it lies still, like one of its own
  <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:1973-1988" />.
- **Light.** A lit trap counts as a light source on a dark level, as a lamp or a tiki torch
  does <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:113-124" />.

## Trivia

- The traps were first meant to watch along their row on their own: their step events still hold a motion
  check that would fire an arrow at the first moving thing in line, commented out. The sensor objects
  replaced it <Cite gml="objects/oArrowTrapLeft/step.gml:1-44" />.
- Both kinds have an alarm, never set, that would fire a slow arrow to the right at 5 pixels a step, from
  either side: a left-facing trap's would come out of its back <Cite gml="objects/oArrowTrapLeft/alarm_0.gml:1-2" />
  <Cite gml="objects/oArrowTrapLeft/create.gml:8" />.
- An unused object, `oTemp`, is an earlier arrow trap that plays a sound the game no longer has
  <Cite quirk="1" />.
