He digs three shortcuts in turn, each a door on the title screen that starts a game at the first level of an
area: the [[jungle|Jungle]]'s level 5, the [[ice-caves|Ice Caves]]' level 9 and the [[temple|Temple]]'s level
13. He asks for their price a donation at a time, in the screen between the last level of one area and the
first of the next, and remembers what he's owed from game to game. Players who have earned every gold trophy
can play as him.

## Behaviour

<Clip id="tunnel-man/donate" />

The Tunnel Man stands in the screen between levels, and stops the player who walks up to him: "HEY THERE!
I'M THE TUNNEL MAN! I DIG SHORTCUTS." <Cite gml="objects/oPDummy/step.gml:24-41" />
<Cite gml="objects/oTransition/draw.gml:3-16" />. The action key moves him on to his request: "CAN YOU LEND
ME A LITTLE MONEY? I NEED $... FOR A NEW SHORTCUT.", with the sum he still needs and the donation, which
starts at $0, or higher if the player pressed up before he asked ([Trivia](#trivia)) <Cite
gml="objects/oTunnelMan/step.gml:1-3" /> <Cite gml="objects/oTransition/draw.gml:17-32" />.
The screen can't be skipped until he's had his answer <Cite gml="objects/oTransition/step.gml:1-11" />.

Up raises the donation by $100 a press, and down lowers it. Held, either key changes it by $100 a step after
20 steps, and by $1,000 a step after 100; it never goes above the player's money or what he needs, nor below
$0 <Cite gml="objects/oTransition/step.gml:35-67" /> <Cite gml="objects/oTunnelMan/step.gml:43-83" />. The
action key gives it: the money is taken, and he answers "THANKS! YOU WON'T REGRET IT!", or, if it pays the
shortcut off, "ONE SHORTCUT, COMING UP!", or, given nothing, "I'LL NEVER GET THIS SHORTCUT BUILT!"
<Cite gml="objects/oTunnelMan/step.gml:1-41" /> <Cite gml="objects/oTransition/draw.gml:33-53" />. In the
clip, between the Mines and the Jungle, he asks for <Stat id="tunnel-man.shortcut-1" />, and the player
gives him $4,100.

He works on one shortcut at a time, and only waits at that one's screen. What's still owed on each is kept in
the high score table and saved with the scores, so donations add up over many games <Cite gml="objects/oGlobals/create.gml:34-39" /> <Cite gml="scripts/scrUpdateHighscores.gml:97-99" />.

<Clip id="tunnel-man/paid" />

Paying the level 9 shortcut off starts the level 13 one at its price <Cite gml="objects/oTunnelMan/step.gml:20-27" />.
In the clip, between the Jungle and the Ice Caves, the player gives the last $500 of the level 9 shortcut.

A paid shortcut opens on the title screen: once the first is paid, a ladder leads down to the level 5 door,
and the level 9 and 13 doors are added as their shortcuts are paid <Cite gml="objects/oTitle/create.gml:27-52" />. Going
through one starts a game at its level <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/other_animation_end.gml:137-157" />. A game
started from a shortcut doesn't set a best time <Cite gml="scripts/scrUpdateHighscores.gml:57-62" />.

A new high score table owes $1 more than each price, and the screen where the Tunnel Man would wait only takes
that $1 off, with no Tunnel Man; so he first appears the next time the screen is reached. Paying off the level
9 shortcut does the same to the level 13 one <Cite gml="scripts/scrResetHighscores.gml:32-33" /> <Cite gml="objects/oTransition/create.gml:47-61" /> <Cite quirk="1" />.

### Playing as him

When the high scores hold all four gold trophies (money, time, kills and Damsels saved), the Tunnel Man on
the title screen is replaced by a door, and going through it switches the player to him, or back <Cite gml="objects/oTitle/create.gml:54-62" /> <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/other_animation_end.gml:192-218" />.
The trophies' thresholds are $200,000 in one game, a best time of 600 seconds or less, 120 kills and 8
[[damsel|Damsels]] <Cite gml="objects/oHighscores/create.gml:32-102" />.

The Tunnel Man starts each game with 2 hearts and no bombs or ropes <Cite gml="scripts/scrClearGlobals.gml:103-114" />.
With empty hands, his attack is a swing of a [[mattock|Mattock]], which only works on the ground and holds
his jump while it lasts, and which never breaks, as a carried one can <Cite
gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:1175-1189" /> <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:432-446" /> <Cite
gml="scripts/characterStepEvent.gml:49-60" /> <Cite gml="objects/oMattockHit/other_animation_end.gml:38-39" />.
It breaks blocks as the Mattock does <Cite gml="objects/oMattockHit/other_animation_end.gml:1-23" />. A wanted poster of him has his own face <Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGen.gml:404-412" />.
