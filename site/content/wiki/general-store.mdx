Its Shopkeeper welcomes the player with "WELCOME TO SLASH'S SUPPLY SHOP!", his name one of 32 drawn at
random <Cite gml="objects/oShopkeeper/step.gml:125-148" /> <Cite gml="scripts/scrGetName.gml:24-60" />. It
sells the supplies the player uses up: [[bomb-bag|Bomb Bags]], [[rope-pile|Rope Piles]] and
[[parachute|Parachutes]], with now and then a [[mattock|Mattock]], [[climbing-gloves|Climbing Gloves]] or a
[[compass|Compass]]. Like every shop it's a side room off the way through a level, with four wares on its
floor and the [[shopkeeper|Shopkeeper]] beside them, and he turns on a player who steals from it.

## Stock

<Clip id="general-store/buy" />

The shop has four wares, and each is rolled on its own <Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGen.gml:173-191" />
<Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGen.gml:448-451" />.
The rolls go down the table until one hits; a hit on a Mattock, Gloves or Compass when the level already
has one rolls again from the top <Cite gml="scripts/scrShopItemsGen.gml:156-177" />:

| Ware | Roll | |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [[mattock\|Mattock]] | 1 in 20, if the level has none | <Cite gml="scripts/scrShopItemsGen.gml:161-164" /> |
| [[climbing-gloves\|Climbing Gloves]] | 1 in 10, if the level has none | <Cite gml="scripts/scrShopItemsGen.gml:165-168" /> |
| [[compass\|Compass]] | 1 in 10, if the level has none | <Cite gml="scripts/scrShopItemsGen.gml:169-172" /> |
| [[bomb-bag\|Bomb Bag]] | Otherwise 1 in 3 | <Cite gml="scripts/scrShopItemsGen.gml:173" /> |
| [[rope-pile\|Rope Pile]] | Otherwise 1 in 3 | <Cite gml="scripts/scrShopItemsGen.gml:174" /> |
| [[parachute\|Parachute]] | Otherwise 1 in 3 | <Cite gml="scripts/scrShopItemsGen.gml:175" /> |

So a store can sell two or more of the bags, piles and parachutes, but one Mattock, pair of Gloves and
Compass at most, and none when the level already has one: a [[black-market|Black Market]]'s other shops'
count too. The check is for the object on the level, not the player's equipment, so a store sells a
Compass or Gloves the player already wears, and buying it changes nothing <Cite quirk="1" />
<Cite replay="fidelity/replays/shop-seed-11-compass-restocked.txt" />. In the clip, the player picks up
a Parachute, which the Shopkeeper prices at $2,000, and pays for it; then he picks up the Compass but
hasn't the $3,000, and paying drops it <Cite clip="general-store/buy" />.

## Shopkeeper

The Shopkeeper stands beside the wares. When the player first comes into the shop's room, he welcomes
him, and while the player holds a ware he shows its name and price and the key that pays ("PRESS P TO
PURCHASE.") and follows him about the shop <Cite gml="objects/oShopkeeper/step.gml:72-85" />
<Cite gml="objects/oShopkeeper/step.gml:225-282" />. The pay key, in the shop's room, buys the held ware:
its price comes out of the player's money, and a bag or a pile adds its bombs or ropes, equipment goes on,
and a weapon stays in his hands, his own <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:1359-1389" />
<Cite gml="scripts/scrStealItem.gml:68-313" />. A player without the money is told "YOU HAVEN'T GOT
ENOUGH MONEY!" and drops the ware <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:1370-1379" />.

These anger him, each with its own shout <Cite gml="scripts/scrShopkeeperAnger.gml:7-23" />:

| What | Shout | |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A ware leaves the shop's room unpaid, in the player's hands or thrown | "COME BACK HERE, THIEF!" | <Cite gml="objects/oItem/step.gml:17-34" /> <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:541-557" /> |
| An armed bomb in the shop within 96 pixels of a Shopkeeper: the player's, or one in ten of those a [[monkey\|Monkey]] throws out | "TERRORIST!" | <Cite gml="objects/oBomb/step.gml:6-17" /> <Cite gml="objects/oMonkey/step.gml:258-267" /> |
| A bomb arrow readied on the [[bow\|Bow]] inside the shop | "TERRORIST!" | <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:29-46" /> |
| A block of the shop's walls or floor destroyed | "DIE, YOU VANDAL!" | <Cite gml="objects/oSolid/destroy.gml:1-7" /> <Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGen.gml:413-426" /> |
| The [[whip\|Whip]], or a thrown item hitting him | None | <Cite gml="objects/oShopkeeper/collision_oWhip.gml:1-19" /> <Cite gml="objects/oItem/step.gml:258-299" /> |

Once any Shopkeeper has died, the shout is "YOU'LL PAY FOR YOUR CRIMES!" instead. A bomb lit by a Chest's
trap or another explosion isn't armed, and doesn't anger him <Cite quirk="1" />.

Angered, he gives the wares away, their prices set to nothing, and goes for the player: he runs
at 3 pixels a step, jumps after him, and fires his [[shotgun|Shotgun]], six pellets at a time and at most
once every 30 steps, at a player ahead of him within 96 pixels and 32 above or below
<Cite gml="objects/oShopkeeper/step.gml:337-444" /> <Cite gml="objects/oShopkeeper/create.gml:47-49" />.
Each pellet that hits takes 4 hearts and stuns the player
<Cite gml="objects/oBullet/collision_oCharacter.gml:1-17" />. Except while he stands by his wares or follows the
player about the shop, his touch throws the player, who drops what he holds <Cite
gml="objects/oShopkeeper/collision_oCharacter.gml:41-76" />. The [[shopkeeper|Shopkeeper's
page]] has the rest: his health, and what he leaves when he dies.

## Stealing

<Clip id="general-store/theft" />

A ware carried or thrown out of the shop's room without being paid for is stolen: the Shopkeeper is
angered, and the ware is free. Carried out, it goes to the player as if bought; whether at once depends
on whether he or the ware leaves the room first, as the ware, held in front of him, often does <Cite
gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:541-557" /> <Cite gml="objects/oItem/step.gml:17-34" /> <Cite quirk="1" />.
In the clip, the player jumps out of the shop with the Compass, which goes on; the
Shopkeeper fires after him and misses, then leaps at him and shoots him dead <Cite clip="general-store/theft" />.

Theft marks the player a thief. Each angering adds 2 to a count, or 3 if it's already above 0, and each
exit he takes takes 1 off, so one theft lasts into the next level <Cite gml="scripts/scrShopkeeperAnger.gml:21-22" />
<Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:874" />. While it's above 0, and for the rest of the game once a
Shopkeeper has died, whatever killed him <Cite gml="objects/oShopkeeper/step.gml:24-63" />
<Cite gml="objects/oShopkeeper/step.gml:486-499" />:

- A Shopkeeper waits by each level's exit <Cite gml="scripts/scrInitLevel.gml:245-257" />.
- A shop's Shopkeeper doesn't greet him. He walks about and attacks when the player comes within 64 pixels
  of him and no more than 16 below, or passes straight above or below him at any distance
  <Cite gml="objects/oShopkeeper/step.gml:87-97" /> <Cite gml="objects/oShopkeeper/step.gml:121-124" />.
- Every ware is free <Cite gml="objects/oItem/step.gml:8-15" />, and the shop's wall has a Wanted poster of
  the player <Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGen.gml:404-411" />.

<Clip id="general-store/waiting-shopkeeper" />

In the clip, on the level after a theft, the Shopkeeper waiting by the exit leaps at the player as he
comes down to it, and he takes the exit unhurt <Cite clip="general-store/waiting-shopkeeper" />
<Cite replay="fidelity/replays/shop-seed-3-thief-next-level.txt" />. The Whip and thrown items only make
the Shopkeeper attack: they don't add to the count, but his anger frees the wares all the same, so they
can then be carried out without marking the player a thief <Cite gml="objects/oShopkeeper/collision_oWhip.gml:1-19" />
<Cite gml="objects/oShopkeeper/step.gml:341-349" />. With no Shopkeeper left on the level, every ware is
free too <Cite gml="objects/oItem/step.gml:8-15" />.

A lit bomb in a shop angers every Shopkeeper near the screen, not only the shop's, and each one not yet
angry adds to the count.
One is marked angry only by his own step, which runs only while he's on screen, so in a
[[black-market|Black Market]], with seven Shopkeepers, each one that's off screen but near enough to be
active adds 3 again on every step the bomb burns: one bomb there takes the count past 400 in under two
seconds, more levels than the
game has <Cite gml="objects/oBomb/step.gml:6-17" /> <Cite gml="scripts/scrShopkeeperAnger.gml:10-22" />
<Cite replay="fidelity/replays/shop-seed-9-market-bomb.txt" />.

## Locations

From level 2 to level 15, a level rolls for a shop: always on level 2, then less often with each level,
2 in the level's number. The shop goes in a side room beside the way through the level, if one qualifies
<Cite gml="scripts/scrLevelGen.gml:239-297" /> ([[mines#shops|the Mines]] has the details). It's a General
Store one time in seven, in every area and on a [[yeti-lair|Yeti lair]] level too <Cite
gml="scripts/scrRoomGen.gml:173-204" /> <Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGen2.gml:160-189" />
<Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGen3.gml:122-152" /> <Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGenYeti.gml:149-181" />, and one
time in six on a [[temple|Temple]] [[sacrifice-pit|sacrifice pit]] level, which has a [[damsel|Damsel]] of its own
and never a [[kissing-parlor|Kissing Parlor]] <Cite gml="scripts/scrLevelGen.gml:84-86" />
<Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGen4.gml:155-188" />. No level has a shop
once the Black Market has been made <Cite quirk="1" />, and [[olmecs-lair|Olmec's Lair]] has none.

A Black Market has five shops that sell items, and each picks a kind the market hasn't got yet, one of each
of the five; one that finds them all taken is a General Store
<Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGenMarket.gml:117-140" />.

## Trivia

- The source calls it the "General" shop, and its Shopkeeper calls it a "SUPPLY SHOP"
  <Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGen.gml:180" /> <Cite gml="objects/oShopkeeper/step.gml:134" />. Its sign shows a
  sack.
- A Black Market can have two General Stores and lack another kind. Each of its shops tries its first
  pick and then each kind after it in turn, and gives up with a General Store when it comes back round to
  the first. But it checks for that after every try, one that found a kind too, so a fifth shop whose
  first pick is the kind just after the last one left finds that kind on its last try and is made a
  General Store all the same <Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGenMarket.gml:123-140" />.
- The end-of-game statistics count stolen items as wares are freed, not as they're taken. While the
  player is marked a thief, each ware counts as it comes on screen and is freed, so on a thief's next level
  every ware of a shop he passes counts, though he takes none
  <Cite gml="objects/oItem/step.gml:8-15" /> <Cite replay="fidelity/replays/shop-seed-3-thief-next-level.txt" />.
  A ware the player carries out counts only if it leaves the shop's room before he does
  <Cite gml="objects/oItem/step.gml:17-26" /> <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:541-557" />.
