The Temple's levels are halls of stone blocks, with pools of [[lava|Lava]]. Level generation sets arrow,
spear and smash traps here, and an idol room whose ceiling comes down. [[caveman|Cavemen]] and
[[hawkman|Hawkmen]] walk its floors, level generation tries for a [[tomb-lord|Tomb Lord]] on level 13 and,
one time in four, on the levels after it, and level 14 hides the way into the [[city-of-gold|City of Gold]]. Past the Temple
lies [[olmecs-lair|Olmec's Lair]].

## Generation

The Temple is levels 13 to 15 <Cite gml="scripts/scrInitLevel.gml:28" />, with its own background
<Cite gml="scripts/scrInitLevel.gml:139-142" />. Taking level 12's exit leads to the screen between levels,
where the [[tunnel-man|Tunnel Man]] may wait, and on to level 13; once all three shortcuts are paid for, the title screen's
Shortcut House has a door to level 13 <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/other_animation_end.gml:63-65" />
<Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/other_animation_end.gml:151-157" />. In the first clip, the player takes that
door, and in the first Temple level a Tomb Lord walks off a ledge into lava and dies
<Cite clip="temple/shortcut" />.

<Clip id="temple/shortcut" />

The level is laid out as [[mines#generation|the Mines']] are, four rooms by four on a path from the top row
to the bottom, from the Temple's own layouts <Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGen4.gml:48-218" />. Its blocks are
temple stone, with one in ten a stone block and one in a hundred a block of the Jungle's earth; layouts
add [[lava|Lava]] (the tiles at a pool's edges as likely stone), ladders and push blocks
<Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGen4.gml:317-341" /> <Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGen4.gml:497-500" />.
Three side rooms in four get a side room's layout, and one of those may be
<Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGen4.gml:68-118" />:

| Room | Chance | |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Kali's altar | 1 in 12, once a level | <Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGen4.gml:75-79" /> |
| [[golden-idol\|Golden Idol]] room: the idol under a row of [[ceiling-trap\|Ceiling Traps]], between two doors | 1 in 12, once the altar's roll fails, once a level; never on a [[sacrifice-pit\|sacrifice pit]] level, which has an idol of its own | <Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGen4.gml:80-88" /> <Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGen4.gml:111" /> <Cite gml="scripts/scrLevelGen.gml:84-86" /> |
| A garden: trees on a floor of the Jungle's earth | 1 in 12, once the altar's roll fails (1 in 11 once the idol room is made) | <Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGen4.gml:103-108" /> <Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGen4.gml:505-559" /> |

The [[city-of-gold|City of Gold]]'s side rooms never get an altar or an idol room, and draw from three
more layouts: treasure vaults of [[gold-bars|Gold Bars]] and big gems, with [[chest|Chests]] and
[[crate|Crates]]
<Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGen4.gml:70-74" /> <Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGen4.gml:112-115" />.

A level may be a special level instead:

## Inhabitants

Level generation goes over every block outside the shops <Cite gml="scripts/scrEntityGen.gml:391-480" />:

| Monster | Where | Chance | |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [[scarab\|Scarab]] | Hanging under a block, on a [[dark-level\|dark level]] only | 1 in 40 | <Cite gml="scripts/scrEntityGen.gml:445-450" /> |
| [[tomb-lord\|Tomb Lord]] | On a block, with two rows of open space above it, three blocks wide | 1 in 40 for each block, on level 13 and one level in four after it, once a level | <Cite gml="scripts/scrEntityGen.gml:382-385" /> <Cite gml="scripts/scrEntityGen.gml:457-464" /> |
| [[caveman\|Caveman]] | On a block | 1 in 40, once the Tomb Lord's roll fails | <Cite gml="scripts/scrEntityGen.gml:465" /> |
| [[hawkman\|Hawkman]] | On a block | 1 in 40, once the Caveman's fails | <Cite gml="scripts/scrEntityGen.gml:466" /> |

The monsters on top of blocks keep out of the entrance's room. [[magma-man|Magma Men]] rise out of the
lava, and the [[ghost|Ghost]] comes to a level the player stays on too long, as in every area. In the
[[city-of-gold|City of Gold]], two of the path's layouts hold a Tomb Lord each
<Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGen4.gml:122-140" /> <Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGen4.gml:426-429" />.

## Hazards

- **[[arrow-trap|Arrow Traps]].** One stone block in three becomes an Arrow Trap (one in four in the
  Mines), on the same terms: outside a shop, away from the entrance, with a block behind it and open ground
  in front <Cite gml="scripts/scrEntityGen.gml:497-524" />.
- **[[spear-trap|Spear Traps]].** One block in twelve on the surface of the ground, more than 64 pixels
  from the entrance, becomes a Spear Trap, but not where it would block a passage over the lava
  <Cite gml="scripts/scrEntityGen.gml:410-442" />.
- **[[smash-trap|Smash Traps]].** One block in sixty, once the monsters' rolls fail, gets a Smash Trap on
  top <Cite gml="scripts/scrEntityGen.gml:467-471" />.
- **[[lava|Lava]]** pools.
- **The idol room's traps.** Lifting the [[golden-idol|Golden Idol]] brings every
  [[ceiling-trap|Ceiling Trap]] on the level down and drops two doors
  <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:1252-1266" />.

## Items and shops

Shops come as in [[mines#shops|the Mines]]: a level's chance is 2 in its number (2 in 13 on level 13 down to
2 in 15 on level 15), in a side room beside the path, of the same kinds, and none once the
[[black-market|Black Market]] has been made <Cite gml="scripts/scrLevelGen.gml:239-297" /> <Cite quirk="1" />. A Temple shop
is never a [[kissing-parlor|Kissing Parlor]] on a [[sacrifice-pit|sacrifice pit]] level, which has a [[damsel|Damsel]]
of its own <Cite gml="scripts/scrLevelGen.gml:84-86" /> <Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGen4.gml:155-188" />.

Treasure is scattered as in [[mines#treasure|the Mines]]. One block of stone in 60
that isn't a gold vein holds a big sapphire, and rarer ones a big emerald, a big ruby or an item
<Cite gml="objects/oTemple/create.gml:5-16" />.

## Secrets

- **The [[city-of-gold|City of Gold]]**, behind a door on level 14.
- **Kali's altars**, and the [[sacrifice-pit|sacrifice pit]].
- **The [[sceptre|Sceptre]]**, which a Tomb Lord on level 13 leaves when it dies.

## Trivia

- The room script makes [[spikes|Spikes]] for a `7` tile, one time in three, but none of the Temple's
  layouts has one, so the Temple has none <Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGen4.gml:340" /> <Cite quirk="1" />.
- The garden's comment calls it a "sun room", and its layout is chosen by a roll whose two outcomes are the
  same string <Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGen4.gml:103-108" /> <Cite quirk="1" />.
- A spear trap that would plug a passage over the lava isn't made, as the code's comment explains: "to
  keep the spear trap from plugging up lava passage" <Cite gml="scripts/scrEntityGen.gml:422-427" />.
