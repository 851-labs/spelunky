A [[shopkeeper|Shopkeeper]] waiting for a thief is on patrol: he stands, walks a little way, and stands again.
The test that ends his walk is the one an Alien turns at a ledge by, without its half that looks for a floor
ahead, so out in the open no walk lasts more than a step: he shuffles rather than walks.

## What happens

A Shopkeeper on patrol in open ground sets off walking, but his walk's check for a wall ahead sends him back to
patrolling on the very next step. He coasts about 10 pixels to a stop, waits, and shuffles again, every second or so
<Cite clip="quirks/shopkeeper-patrol-one-step" />.

<Clip id="quirks/shopkeeper-patrol-one-step" />

## Why

On patrol, a Shopkeeper counts down 20 to 50 steps on the ground, then sets off walking a random way
<Cite gml="objects/oShopkeeper/step.gml:283-298" />. His walk goes back to patrolling when there's no solid
beside his head, a pixel to his left or right:

```gml objects/oShopkeeper/step.gml:309-328
    if (facing == LEFT)
    {
        if (not collision_point(x-1, y, oSolid, -1, -1))
        {
            status = PATROL;
            counter = rand(20,50);
            xVel = 0;
        }
        xVel = -1.5;
    }
    else
    {
        if (not collision_point(x+16, y, oSolid, -1, -1))
        {
            status = PATROL;
            counter = rand(20,50);
            xVel = 0;
        }
        xVel = 1.5;
    }
```

The same lines in the [[alien|Alien]]'s and the [[mantrap|Mantrap]]'s walks also test the floor in front,
`y+16`, where they turn at a ledge <Cite gml="objects/oAlien/step.gml:49-58" />
<Cite gml="objects/oManTrap/step.gml:60-69" />. Without it, in open ground nothing is beside his head, so every
walk ends on its first step. That step still sets `xVel` to 1.5 after the test, and his friction takes 0.1 a step
off it, so he coasts to a halt over about ten steps <Cite gml="objects/oShopkeeper/step.gml:531-533" />
<Cite quirk="1" />.

## Reproducing it

Steal from a shop, or kill a Shopkeeper, and go on to the next level: the Shopkeeper at its exit shuffles in
place <Cite gml="scripts/scrInitLevel.gml:245-255" />, and so does a shop's Shopkeeper there, who patrols rather
than stands <Cite gml="objects/oShopkeeper/step.gml:121-124" />. The level editor's `!` is a Shopkeeper on patrol
<Cite gml="scripts/scrCreateTileObj.gml:151-155" />, and the replay of the clip's staged scene, on a custom level,
puts one in a flat room. In 300 steps he sets off ten times, and every walk lasts one step; after one, his speed
is 1.4, and it's gone ten steps later <Cite replay="fidelity/replays/enemy-shopkeeper-patrol-one-step.txt" />. A
thief's next level shows the same <Cite replay="fidelity/replays/shop-seed-3-thief-next-level.txt" />.

## In the port

The spelunky.ai port reproduces it exactly: it's in docs/quirks.md's list of the 1.1 source's bugs
<Cite quirk="1" />, and the oracle's fingerprint of the replay pins every walk. No option turns it off.
