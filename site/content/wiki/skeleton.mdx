Level generation scatters piles of bones through every area, and 1 in 8 of them is a Skeleton waiting to
rise. When the player comes level with one, it stands up and walks toward him; it has no attack beyond
touching him. With <Stat id="skeleton.hp" />, it dies to any damage, and falls apart into bones and a
[[skull|Skull]].

## Behaviour

<Clip id="skeleton/rise" />

A Skeleton starts out as a pile of bones on the ground, which falls if the ground under it goes
<Cite gml="objects/oFakeBones/step.gml:4-13" />. When the player is within 8 pixels of the pile's middle
in height and 64 pixels of it across, the bones rise; when the rising animation ends, the pile is a
Skeleton <Cite gml="objects/oFakeBones/step.gml:15-18" />
<Cite gml="objects/oFakeBones/other_animation_end.gml:1-5" />. The pile only does this while it's on
screen <Cite gml="objects/oFakeBones/step.gml:1-3" />. The clip shows a pile on a Jungle graveyard level
rising as the player runs at it, and the player jumping on the Skeleton as it stands up.

A new Skeleton faces the player <Cite gml="objects/oSkeleton/create.gml:27-31" />, stands for
<Stat id="skeleton.start-delay" /> (<Stat id="skeleton.start-delay" as="seconds" />), and then walks the way
it faces at <Stat id="skeleton.walk-speed" /> (<Stat id="skeleton.walk-speed" as="per-second" />), turning
around at walls <Cite gml="objects/oSkeleton/step.gml:37-61" />
<Cite gml="objects/oSkeleton/step.gml:75-76" />. It doesn't stop to rest, and it doesn't turn at the edge
of a ledge, so it walks off ([Trivia](#trivia)). It only acts while it's on screen or just off it
<Cite gml="objects/oSkeleton/step.gml:3-5" />.

## Damage

When the player touches a Skeleton without stomping it, he loses <Stat id="skeleton.damage" />, is knocked
away from it, and blinks, invincible, for 30 steps (1 second)
<Cite gml="objects/oEnemy/collision_oCharacter.gml:14-25" />. This is the touch code most monsters share;
the Skeleton has no attack of its own. A pile of bones that hasn't risen does nothing to him.

## Locations

Level generation leaves piles of bones in [[mines|the Mines]], [[jungle|the Jungle]],
[[ice-caves|the Ice Caves]] and [[temple|the Temple]], as one of the things it may put on a block with
open space above it, after the rolls for the things before it have failed. The chance depends on the spot
and on the level number <Cite gml="scripts/scrTreasureGen.gml:47-133" />:

| Spot | Chance of a pile | |
| --- | --- | --- |
| An alcove: a space one block high with a wall on at least one side | 1 in (40 − 2 × level) | <Cite gml="scripts/scrTreasureGen.gml:80" /> |
| A tunnel: a space with a wall on both sides | 1 in (80 − level) | <Cite gml="scripts/scrTreasureGen.gml:103" /> |
| Open ground | 1 in (140 − 2 × level) | <Cite gml="scripts/scrTreasureGen.gml:124" /> |

Each pile has a 1 in 8 chance of being one that rises as a Skeleton; the others are plain bones with a
[[skull|Skull]] beside them, and never rise <Cite gml="scripts/scrTreasureGen.gml:82-87" />. On a
[[restless-dead|graveyard level]] in the Jungle, each chance is 11 times higher
<Cite gml="scripts/scrEntityGen.gml:257" />, and the graveyard's rooms have piles of their own, every one of
which rises ([Trivia](#trivia)) <Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGen2.gml:490-494" />.

In custom levels, the [[level-editor|level editor]]'s tile `K` is a Skeleton, ready to walk, and its tile
`k` is plain bones with a Skull, which never rise <Cite gml="scripts/scrCreateTileObj.gml:122-126" />
<Cite gml="scripts/scrCreateTileObj.gml:143-146" />.

## Tips

- A pile that will rise has its skull in the pile. A pile that won't has no skull in it, and a separate
  Skull lies beside it.
- A pile only rises for a player at about its own height, so passing above or below it leaves it be.
- A Skeleton walks off ledges, so one on a platform doesn't stay there.

## Trivia

  a ledge and stopped it at random, the latter the same lines as the [[caveman|Caveman]]'s
  <Cite gml="objects/oSkeleton/step.gml:62-73" /> <Cite gml="objects/oSkeleton/step.gml:78-85" />
  <Cite gml="objects/oCaveman/step.gml:109-114" />.
- For each bone tile in the graveyard's rooms, the code chooses between rising bones and a [[jar|Jar]]
  with `if (rand(1,2))`, but `rand(1,2)` is 1 or 2, and both count as true, so it always makes bones
  <Cite gml="scripts/rand.gml:1-2" />:

  {

      else instance_create(xpos+8, ypos+10, oJar);
  }
