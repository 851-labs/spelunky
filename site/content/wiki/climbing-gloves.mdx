Without them, the player only catches the edge of a ledge, where there's open space above the block. With
them, he catches the side of any wall he falls past while pressing toward it, and by jumping from his grip
and catching the wall again, he climbs it. [[general-store|General Stores]], [[clothing-shop|Clothing
Shops]], [[rare-shop|Rare Shops]] and the [[dice-house|Dice House]] offer them, and [[crate|Crates]], blocks
and [[kali|Kali]] give them away. They're worn from the moment they're picked up or paid for until the game ends.

## Effect

<Clip id="climbing-gloves/climb" />

Picking up a free pair, paying for one or carrying one out of a shop puts it on: the message "YOU GOT
CLIMBING GLOVES!" shows, the pair disappears, and an icon of it joins the row under the hearts
<Cite gml="scripts/scrStealItem.gml:137-148" /> <Cite gml="scripts/scrDrawHUD.gml:130-134" />. They stay on
until a new game starts, which clears them <Cite gml="scripts/scrClearGlobals.gml:98" />
<Cite gml="objects/oTransition/create.gml:20-24" />.

In the air, pressing toward a wall he's touching, the player hangs on to it when there's a block right
beside his head. Without the gloves, he does only where there's no block above that one and none just below
his feet, at a ledge's corner <Cite gml="scripts/characterStepEvent.gml:441-460" />. With them, while he's
falling, any block beside his head will do, so he grabs a sheer wall anywhere, his height snapping to a multiple of 8 pixels
<Cite gml="scripts/characterStepEvent.gml:400-422" />. Hanging, a jump while holding toward the wall takes
him up off it, and once he falls again he grabs it again
<Cite gml="scripts/characterStepEvent.gml:507-523" />. The clip shows the player falling back from a wall
he can't grab, then, with the gloves, grabbing its bare face and climbing it to the top, a block (16
pixels) a jump <Cite clip="climbing-gloves/climb" />.

The gloves also keep him from grabbing the wall he's just left. Walking off a ledge with them, he can't
grab anything for 5 steps; letting go of a wall (down and jump), for 10 steps instead of 5
<Cite gml="scripts/characterStepEvent.gml:257-264" /> <Cite gml="scripts/characterStepEvent.gml:499-506" />.

## Interactions

- Only a fall catches a wall: rising, the player grabs only ledges, as without the gloves
  <Cite gml="scripts/characterStepEvent.gml:400-460" />.
- A grabbed wall holds him only while it's beside him: when there's nothing within 2 pixels on the side he
  faces, he falls <Cite gml="scripts/characterStepEvent.gml:525-532" />.
- [[spring-shoes|Spring Shoes]] don't raise a jump from a wall: only a jump from the ground gets their boost
  <Cite gml="scripts/characterStepEvent.gml:338-357" /> <Cite gml="scripts/characterStepEvent.gml:507-523" />.

## Tips

- A wall with nothing to stand on is no longer a dead end: falling down a shaft beside a wall, holding
  toward it stops the fall.
- Each jump off the wall climbs about a block, so a tall wall takes a jump for each block of its height.
