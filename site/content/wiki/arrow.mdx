Most arrows come from [[arrow-trap|Arrow Traps]], which shoot one along their row when something moves into
it; the [[bow|Bow]] shoots them too. Once an arrow has stopped, it's an item like any other, and can be
picked up and thrown.

## Usage

<Clip id="arrow/trap" />

An arrow trap shoots its arrow at 8 pixels a step <Cite gml="objects/oArrowTrapTest/collision_oItem.gml:5-26" />.
In the clip, the player climbs a [[ladder|ladder]] into a trap's row; it shoots, and the arrow takes his last heart and
kills him <Cite clip="arrow/trap" />. An arrow faster than 3 pixels a step across that hits the player takes
<Stat id="arrow.player-damage" />, knocks him its way and stuns him for 20 steps, and is destroyed, unless
it's one he shot or threw in the last 10 steps <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:1513-1535" />
<Cite gml="objects/oArrow/alarm_2.gml:1" />.

It moves by the code all items share: its own lighter gravity, 0.2, lasts its first step
([[mitt-gravity-one-step]]), and then it falls 0.6 pixels a step faster each step as items do <Cite gml="objects/oArrow/create.gml:6" />
<Cite gml="objects/oItem/step.gml:160-170" />. It turns to point the way it's flying <Cite gml="objects/oArrow/step.gml:3-22" />.
While it moves faster than 2 pixels a step, it takes <Stat id="arrow.damage" /> from the monster nearest
it ([[nearest-enemy]]), a [[vampire|Vampire]] that isn't stunned <Stat id="arrow.vampire-damage" bare /> more, and is
destroyed, unless that monster is invincible or a [[magma-man|Magma Man]], when it flies on; a [[damsel|Damsel]] it hits loses 1 hit point, and the arrow is destroyed
too <Cite gml="objects/oItem/step.gml:256-363" /> <Cite gml="objects/oItem/step.gml:365-398" /> <Cite quirk="1" row="A fast thrown item hurts the nearest enemy, not the one it hit." />.

### Stuck in walls

An arrow that hits a wall faster than 6 pixels a step across sticks in it, 2 pixels deep: it stops, and no
longer falls <Cite gml="objects/oItem/step.gml:82-84" /> <Cite gml="objects/oItem/step.gml:122-137" />. A
player falling past a stuck arrow, when it's 5 or 6 pixels above his middle, catches it and hangs from it,
as he does from a ledge <Cite gml="scripts/characterStepEvent.gml:462-475" />.

### By hand

At rest, an arrow is picked up and thrown as any item is, at 8 pixels a step plus the player's speed: fast
enough to hurt, and to stick in a wall <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:1203-1214" />
<Cite gml="scripts/scrUseItem.gml:605-646" />. Holding a [[bow|Bow]], the player takes back an arrow at rest
that isn't stuck in a wall by touching it, adding it to his count <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:1973-1988" />.

## Locations

[[level-generation|Level generation]] makes no arrows lying about; they come from:

- **[[arrow-trap|Arrow Traps]]**, one each: a trap shoots once, and a trap destroyed before it shoots leaves
  its arrow <Cite gml="objects/oArrowTrapLeft/step.gml:1-2" /> <Cite gml="objects/oArrowTrapTest/collision_oItem.gml:9-21" />
  <Cite gml="objects/oArrowTrapLeft/destroy.gml:1-10" />.
- **The [[bow|Bow]]**, which shoots the player's arrows <Cite gml="scripts/scrFireBow.gml:24-50" />.

## Interactions

- **[[bomb|Bombs]].** An explosion destroys an arrow <Cite gml="objects/oExplosion/collision_oItem.gml:1-5" />.
- **Exits.** Carried through a level's exit, it's in the player's hands on the next
  level <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:842-850" /> <Cite gml="scripts/scrHoldItem.gml:38" />.

## Tips

- An arrow trap shoots only once, so the arrow it leaves is the end of it.
- An arrow stuck in a wall is a handhold: a player falling past it can stop there.
