A stack of ingots, it's worth twice a single [[gold-bar|Gold Bar]] and is the most valuable gold the player
picks up by walking over it. Level generation leaves it in the open in every area, more rarely than a single
bar, and lines the [[city-of-gold|City of Gold]]'s treasure vaults with it.

## Value

Its base value is <Stat id="gold-bars.value" />, and it's worth a quarter of that more for each area past the
Mines <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:1996" />:

| Area | Value |
| --- | --- |
| [[mines\|The Mines]] | <Stat id="gold-bars.value-by-area" at="0" /> |
| [[jungle\|The Jungle]] | <Stat id="gold-bars.value-by-area" at="1" /> |
| [[ice-caves\|The Ice Caves]] | <Stat id="gold-bars.value-by-area" at="2" /> |
| [[temple\|The Temple]] | <Stat id="gold-bars.value-by-area" at="3" /> |
| [[olmecs-lair\|Olmec's Lair]] | <Stat id="gold-bars.value-by-area" at="4" /> |

The code counts [[olmecs-lair|Olmec's Lair]], the last level, as an area of its own, past the Temple
<Cite gml="scripts/scrInitLevel.gml:24-29" />.

## Locations

Level generation, in every area, gives each block outside a shop with open space above it, away from the
entrance, the exit and a [[golden-idol|Golden Idol]], a roll for what lies on it, once its rolls for a
[[rock|Rock]] and a [[jar|Jar]] fail <Cite gml="scripts/scrTreasureGen.gml:27-46" />. Gold Bars come after a
single [[gold-bar|Gold Bar]] in each chain of rolls:

| Spot | Chance | |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A nook one block high with a wall beside it | 1 in 6, once the rolls before it, up to a Gold Bar's 1 in 3, fail | <Cite gml="scripts/scrTreasureGen.gml:48-90" /> |
| A gap one block wide between two walls | 1 in 8, once the rolls for a [[web]] and a Gold Bar (1 in 4) fail | <Cite gml="scripts/scrTreasureGen.gml:95-102" /> |
| Open floor | 1 in 50, once a Gold Bar's 1 in 40 fails | <Cite gml="scripts/scrTreasureGen.gml:116-123" /> |
| A treasure tile of the [[city-of-gold\|City of Gold]]'s treasure vaults, side rooms only it has | Always, unless a big ruby (1 in 120), a big sapphire (1 in 80) or a big emerald (1 in 60) comes first | <Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGen4.gml:70-74" /> <Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGen4.gml:364-370" /> |
| A treasure tile of [[olmecs-lair\|Olmec's Lair]] | 1 in 6, once a [[chest\|Chest]]'s 1 in 15 fails | <Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGen5.gml:177-180" /> |

On a level with the Udjat Eye's locked chest, level generation then turns one treasure lying in the open,
picked at random, into the chest's key <Cite gml="scripts/scrEntityGen.gml:108-141" />. The
[[tutorial|Tutorial]] has one <Cite room="rTutorial" />, and the [[level-editor|level editor]]'s `*` tile is
one <Cite gml="scripts/scrCreateTileObj.gml:257-260" />.

## Collecting

The player collects it by touching it while he isn't stunned or dead: it's collectable from the start, and he
takes the treasure nearest him, one a step <Cite gml="objects/oGoldBars/create.gml:6-7" />
<Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:1990-2016" />. Its value shows under his money and is counted into it
after a short pause <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:1953-1971" />; gold plays the coin sound
<Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:2000-2014" />, and the screen between levels shows each stack collected
on the level <Cite gml="objects/oTransition/alarm_0.gml:59-64" />. The clip ends with the player picking up
the Tutorial's stack, after its six single bars <Cite clip="gold-bar/tutorial" />.

It falls and comes to rest like other treasure, moving only while it's on the screen; one that sinks into lava
is destroyed <Cite gml="objects/oTreasure/step.gml:1-79" />.
