The Ceiling Trap guards [[temple|the Temple]]'s [[golden-idol|Golden Idol]]. The idol's room looks like any
other until the idol is picked up: then a stone door drops into each of the room's two openings, and the
room's ceiling, a row of blocks with spikes underneath, starts to come down. It moves slowly, but nothing
stops it short of the floor, and it takes 10 hearts a step from a player it reaches.

## How it works

<Clip id="ceiling-trap/crush" />

Picking up the idol on a Temple level shakes the screen, sets every Ceiling Trap block in the level dropping,
and sets falling the doors nearest to points up and to the left and right of the player
<Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:1250-1265" />.

- **The doors** are two blocks tall, set into the room's side walls. Each falls, gaining speed up to 6 pixels
  a step, until it lands on the floor, filling the opening below it <Cite gml="objects/oDoor/step.gml:1-21" />.
  The wall where it stood was a stand-in that turns into an ordinary block once the door has left it, so the
  wall stays whole <Cite gml="objects/oTempleFake/step.gml:1-5" />.
- **The ceiling** comes down 1 pixel every 4 steps (7.5 pixels a second), showing its spikes, until it rests
  on what's below it <Cite gml="objects/oCeilingTrap/step.gml:5-21" /> <Cite gml="objects/oCeilingTrap/create.gml:13" />.

In the clip, the player walks into the idol room and picks the idol up; both doors are down 7 steps later. The
ceiling reaches his head 320 steps (10.67 seconds) after the pickup and kills him, and settles on the floor 59
steps after that.

## Damage

A Ceiling Trap block that has started to drop takes 10 hearts on every step it touches the top of the
player's head <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:1670-1680" />. Nothing spaces out the hits, and each one he
takes alive is counted in the end-of-game statistics' ceiling trap row <Cite quirk="1" row="The deaths table's explosion and trap rows don't count deaths." />. One that keeps
coming down over him also crushes him <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:1492-1511" />.

## Locations

Only in [[temple|the Temple]]'s idol room. Each side room off the path through a Temple level has a 3 in 4
chance of being one of the special side rooms; of those, the first roll is 1 in 12 for a [[kali|Kali]] [[kalis-altar|altar]] (once a
level), and then 1 in 12 for the idol room, once a level, never in the [[city-of-gold|City of Gold]] or on a
[[sacrifice-pit|sacrifice pit]] level <Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGen4.gml:68-88" />
<Cite gml="scripts/scrLevelGen.gml:84-86" />. The room has six Ceiling Trap blocks across its ceiling, a door on
each side, and the idol on the floor <Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGen4.gml:110-111" />
<Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGen4.gml:406-419" />.

## Avoiding

- **Leave the idol.** Nothing happens until it's picked up.
- **Break out.** The doors and the ceiling blocks aren't unbreakable: a [[bomb|Bomb]]'s blast destroys them as
  it does blocks, and so does the rock around the room.
- **Use the time.** The ceiling takes over 10 seconds to come down to a standing player's head.

## Interactions

- **Monsters** don't take the 10 hearts' worth of damage: that check is the player's alone.
- **The idol** only sets the trap off once <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:1281" />.
- **[[trap-block|Trap blocks]]** take the trap's place on a Temple level without one: there, lifting an idol
  destroys every trap block within 90 pixels of the player instead
  <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:1252" /> <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:1267-1279" />. The
  [[sacrifice-pit|sacrifice pit]] is always such a level. It has no idol room, so no Ceiling Trap
  <Cite gml="scripts/scrLevelGen.gml:84-86" /> <Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGen4.gml:80-87" />, and its idol
  stands beside a [[damsel|Damsel]] on four trap blocks over the pit <Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGen4.gml:193-196" />
  <Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGen4.gml:399-409" />, so lifting it drops the player into the lava at the bottom
  <Cite replay="fidelity/replays/trap-sacrifice-pit.txt" />. An idol room whose six Ceiling Trap blocks
  have all been bombed is one too, with no trap blocks to destroy: lifting its idol then sets nothing off,
  and the doors stay up.

## Trivia

- Both objects have a state for going back up, `RETURN`, that nothing ever enters: the ceiling stays down
  <Cite gml="objects/oCeilingTrap/create.gml:15-19" />.
