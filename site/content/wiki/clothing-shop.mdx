Its Shopkeeper welcomes the player with "WELCOME TO SPOONY'S CLOTHING SHOP!", his name one of 32 drawn at
random <Cite gml="objects/oShopkeeper/step.gml:129" /> <Cite gml="scripts/scrGetName.gml:24-60" />. It
sells six pieces of equipment the player wears for the rest of the game, [[spring-shoes|Spring Shoes]],
[[spectacles|Spectacles]], [[climbing-gloves|Climbing Gloves]], the [[pitchers-mitt|Pitcher's Mitt]], the
[[cape|Cape]] and [[spike-shoes|Spike Shoes]], and [[rope-pile|Rope Piles]]. Like every shop it's a side
room off the way through a level, with four wares on its floor and the [[shopkeeper|Shopkeeper]] beside
them.

## Stock

The shop has four wares, and each is rolled on its own <Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGen.gml:173-191" />
<Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGen.gml:448-451" />. Each try first rolls for a Rope Pile, 1 in 20; failing
that, it picks one of the six pieces of equipment, each as likely, and takes it if the level hasn't got
one. A try that picks a piece the level has makes the next try's Rope Pile roll likelier, 1 in 19, then 1
in 18 and so on, so by the twentieth try a Rope Pile is certain
<Cite gml="scripts/scrShopItemsGen.gml:68-101" />:

| Ware | Roll | |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [[rope-pile\|Rope Pile]] | 1 in 20 on the first try, 1 in 19 on the second... | <Cite gml="scripts/scrShopItemsGen.gml:74" /> |
| [[spring-shoes\|Spring Shoes]] | Otherwise 1 in 6, if the level has none | <Cite gml="scripts/scrShopItemsGen.gml:75-78" /> |
| [[spectacles\|Spectacles]] | Otherwise 1 in 6, if the level has none | <Cite gml="scripts/scrShopItemsGen.gml:79-82" /> |
| [[climbing-gloves\|Climbing Gloves]] | Otherwise 1 in 6, if the level has none | <Cite gml="scripts/scrShopItemsGen.gml:83-86" /> |
| [[pitchers-mitt\|Pitcher's Mitt]] | Otherwise 1 in 6, if the level has none | <Cite gml="scripts/scrShopItemsGen.gml:87-90" /> |
| [[cape\|Cape]] | Otherwise 1 in 6, if the level has none | <Cite gml="scripts/scrShopItemsGen.gml:91-94" /> |
| [[spike-shoes\|Spike Shoes]] | Otherwise 1 in 6, if the level has none | <Cite gml="scripts/scrShopItemsGen.gml:95-98" /> |

So a Clothing Shop sells each piece once at most, and Rope Piles more often the more pieces the level
already has. The check is for the piece on the level, not the player's, so the shop sells a piece the
player already wears, and buying it again changes nothing <Cite quirk="1" />.

## Shopkeeper

The Shopkeeper sells as in every shop ([[general-store#shopkeeper|the General Store]] has the details): he
names the price of a ware the player holds, and the pay key buys it
<Cite gml="objects/oShopkeeper/step.gml:72-85" /> <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:1359-1389" />. A
piece of equipment goes on as it's bought, with its message, and a Rope Pile adds its ropes <Cite
gml="scripts/scrStealItem.gml:103-114" /> <Cite gml="scripts/scrStealItem.gml:126-222" />.
A theft, a bomb lit in the shop or a block of its walls broken angers him, and he gives the wares away and
attacks with his [[shotgun|Shotgun]] <Cite gml="scripts/scrShopkeeperAnger.gml:7-23" />
<Cite gml="objects/oShopkeeper/step.gml:337-408" />.

## Stealing

<Clip id="clothing-shop/theft" />

Carrying a piece out of the shop's room unpaid angers the Shopkeeper ("COME BACK HERE, THIEF!") and puts it
on, as if bought. Whether it goes on at once depends on whether the player or the piece, held in front of
him, leaves the room first: if the piece does, the player is left holding it, free, and picking it up
again puts it on <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:541-557" /> <Cite gml="objects/oItem/step.gml:17-34" />
<Cite quirk="1" />. In the clip, the player picks up the Spectacles, which the Shopkeeper prices at
$10,400 on level 5, and runs and jumps out of the shop with them: they go on, and the Shopkeeper fires
after him <Cite clip="clothing-shop/theft" />.

A theft marks the player a thief into the next level, whose exit has a Shopkeeper waiting by it and whose
shops' wares are free ([[general-store#stealing|the General Store]] has the rules)
<Cite gml="scripts/scrInitLevel.gml:245-257" />.

## Locations

A level from 2 to 15 that rolls a shop builds one of seven kinds, each as likely, so a shop is a Clothing
Shop one time in seven (one in six on a Temple [[sacrifice-pit|sacrifice pit]] level, which never has a
[[kissing-parlor|Kissing Parlor]]) <Cite gml="scripts/scrLevelGen.gml:239-297" /> <Cite
gml="scripts/scrRoomGen.gml:173-204" /> <Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGenYeti.gml:149-181" />
<Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGen4.gml:155-188" />.
[[mines#shops|The Mines]] has how levels roll shops. A [[black-market|Black Market]] has five shops that
sell items, as a rule one of each kind, a Clothing Shop among them ([[general-store#trivia|the General
Store]] has the exception) <Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGenMarket.gml:117-140" /> <Cite quirk="1" />.
