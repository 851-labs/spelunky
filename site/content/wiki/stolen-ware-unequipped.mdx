Carrying a ware out of a shop unpaid angers the [[shopkeeper|Shopkeeper]] and makes the ware the player's, and a
piece of equipment, such as the [[spike-shoes|Spike Shoes]], goes on as if bought. But two pieces of code take
the theft, the player's and the ware's, and only the player's puts it on. Whichever leaves the shop first
decides it, so a ware carried out ahead of the player stays in his hands, unworn, until he picks it up again.

## What happens

In a [[rare-shop|Rare Shop]] on level 5 that opens to the left, the player picks up the Spike Shoes, priced at
$5,200, and heads left out of the shop with them, jumping as he leaves. The Shopkeeper turns on him and fires, but
the shoes, out of the shop first, stay in his hands rather than going on <Cite clip="rare-shop/theft" />.

<Clip id="rare-shop/theft" />

Out of the shop, he puts the shoes down and picks them up again, which puts them on, and then stomps a Caveman
with them <Cite clip="spike-shoes/stomp" />.

<Clip id="spike-shoes/stomp" />

## Why

The player's step takes a theft when he's outside the shop's room with a ware that still has a price: it runs
`scrStealItem`, which puts a piece of equipment on, and angers the Shopkeeper
<Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:541-557" />. The ware's own step takes a theft when the ware is outside
the shop, and it only angers the Shopkeeper and makes the ware free:

```gml objects/oItem/step.gml:17-25
    // stealing makes shopkeeper angry
    if (isRealLevel())
    {
        if (cost > 0 and forSale and not isInShop(x, y))
        {
            global.itemsStolen += 1;
            scrShopkeeperAnger(0);
            cost = 0;
        }
```

Once its `cost` is 0, the player's check no longer runs, so whichever is out first takes the theft. The ware
is held 4 pixels ahead of the player <Cite gml="objects/oItem/step.gml:41-42" />, but it tests where the step
before put it, while the player tests where he's just moved. Walking, at 3 pixels a step, it's out first only
when a step leaves him 1 pixel short of the room's edge; otherwise both are out on the same step, and the
player's step comes first, so the ware goes on. With the run key, faster than 4 pixels a step, the player is
always out first, and jumping out upwards, he crosses first <Cite quirk="1" />. A free ware goes on when it's picked up
<Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:1297" />.

## Reproducing it

Steal a piece of equipment by leaving the shop forwards so that it crosses the room's edge ahead of the
player. It happens in normal play. The replay of the clips jumps out of a Rare Shop to the left with the Spike
Shoes: the shoes leave the room a step before the player, so the Shopkeeper is angered (frame 1429) but the
shoes stay in his hands. He puts them down (1463) and picks them up again, and they go on (1471)
<Cite replay="fidelity/replays/item-seed-7-spike-stomp.txt" />. Running out on the ground with a Cape puts it
on at once <Cite replay="fidelity/replays/item-seed-35-cape.txt" />. The Ankh is never for sale, so it's never
left this way.

## In the port

The spelunky.ai port reproduces it exactly: it's in docs/quirks.md's list of the 1.1 source's bugs
<Cite quirk="1" />, and the oracle's fingerprint of the replay pins the theft, the unworn shoes and when they go
on. No option turns it off.
