In the [[city-of-gold|City of Gold]], every block of stone is gold, and breaking one gives three
[[gold-chunk|Gold Chunks]] and a [[gold-nugget|Gold Nugget]]. One in ten is a stone block rather than temple
stone, drawn gold all the same, and it gives nothing: level generation marks every stone block of a
[[temple|Temple]] level to break cleanly, which leaves no rubble and no gold.

## What happens

A bomb in the City of Gold breaks a stone block and the block of temple stone beside it. The temple stone gives
three chunks of gold and a nugget; the stone block, the same gold to look at, gives nothing
<Cite clip="quirks/city-stone-block-no-gold" />.

<Clip id="quirks/city-stone-block-no-gold" />

## Why

The Temple's room script makes about one block in ten of its `1` and `2` tiles a stone block (`oBlock`), and the
rest temple stone (`oTemple`) or, for a `1` tile one time in a hundred, the Jungle's earth
<Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGen4.gml:317-327" />. In the
City, a stone block's create event gives it the gold sprite <Cite gml="objects/oBlock/create.gml:3-4" />. Then
`scrEntityGen`'s Temple branch turns some stone blocks into arrow traps, and so that those go without rubble, it
sets the flag on all of them first:

```gml scripts/scrEntityGen.gml:497-500
    with oBlock
    {   
        cleanDeath = true;
        if (not isInShop(x, y))
```

Nothing sets it back on the blocks it leaves <Cite gml="scripts/scrEntityGen.gml:497-524" />. A stone block's
destroy event makes its rubble, or in the City its gold, only without the flag:

```gml objects/oBlock/destroy.gml:1-4
if (not cleanDeath)
{

    if (not global.cityOfGold)
```

So a stone block bombed or dug on a Temple level leaves nothing, and one in the City gives no gold. The temple
stone has no such flag, and gives three chunks and a nugget <Cite gml="objects/oTemple/destroy.gml:21-32" />. The
Mines' pass over stone blocks doesn't set it, so theirs leave rubble <Cite gml="scripts/scrEntityGen.gml:145-171" />
<Cite quirk="1" />.

## Reproducing it

Bomb or dig the blocks of the City of Gold: about one in ten gives no gold. On any Temple level, a stone block
leaves no rubble. The replay, with seed 9, reaches the City as a
sacrifice pit, and the player throws a bomb onto a stone block by the entrance. It breaks that block and the
temple stone beside it, and only one nugget and three chunks fly: the temple stone's
<Cite replay="fidelity/replays/city-of-gold-seed-9-sacrifice-pit.txt" />.

## In the port

The spelunky.ai port reproduces it exactly: it's in docs/quirks.md's list of the 1.1 source's bugs
<Cite quirk="1" />, and the oracle's fingerprint of the replay pins the blocks and the gold. No option turns it
off.
