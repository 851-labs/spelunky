A [[rock|Rock]] thrown out of the view doesn't land somewhere out there: it stops in mid-air, a little past the
view's edge, and waits. When the player comes back, it flies on from where it was, at the speed it had, as if it
had just been [[throwing|thrown]].

## What happens

The player throws a rock to the left from a running jump, and it flies out of the view. He walks away to the
right, and comes back much later: the rock flies in from the left at the speed it left with, bounces off the
cave's wall and lands <Cite clip="quirks/rock-freezes-off-view" />.

<Clip id="quirks/rock-freezes-off-view" />

## Why

The whole of the items' step, the flying, the bouncing and the landing, is inside one test: that the item is
within 16 pixels of the view, or is a rope:

```gml objects/oItem/step.gml:1-3
if ((x > view_xview[0]-16 and x < view_xview[0] + view_wview[0]+16 and
    y > view_yview[0]-16 and y < view_yview[0] + view_hview[0]+16) or
    type == "Rope")
```

Outside it, the step does nothing, so the item keeps its position and its speed, and stays active, until the
level deactivates everything 96 pixels past the view <Cite gml="objects/oLevel/step.gml:64-77" />. When the view
comes back within 16 pixels of it, the step runs again with the speed it kept, and it flies on <Cite quirk="1" />.

## Reproducing it

Throw a rock hard out of the view, then walk away and back. It happens whenever a thrown item leaves the view
before it lands. The replay of the clip throws the rock at 14 pixels a step (frame 978). It leaves the view and
stops in mid-air at (182, 226) on 990, still with that speed. The player walks right until the rock is
deactivated (1063), then back: it's active again on 1126, still in mid-air, and flies on once it's near the view
again (1154), bouncing off the wall and landing <Cite replay="fidelity/replays/item-seed-49-rock-off-view.txt" />.

## In the port

The spelunky.ai port reproduces it exactly: it's in docs/quirks.md's list of the 1.1 source's bugs
<Cite quirk="1" />, and the oracle's fingerprint of the replay pins where the rock stops and when it flies on. No
option turns it off.
