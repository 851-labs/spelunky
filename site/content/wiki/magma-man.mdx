Magma Men come out of lava: a lava spurt sometimes throws up a blob of magma, which forms into a Magma Man
where it lands. A Magma Man wanders the ground like a [[hawkman|Hawkman]], burning anything it touches, and
there's almost nothing to do about one but keep away: it can't be stomped or whipped to death, and a
[[bomb|Bomb]] only makes two. It doesn't last, though: within <Stat id="magma-man.hp" bare /> steps it burns
out.

## Behaviour

<Clip id="magma-man/spawn" />

**From lava.** Each block of lava with open air above it has a 1 in 4 chance, when the level is made, of
being one that spurts <Cite gml="scripts/scrSetupWalls.gml:398-414" />. While the player is within 240 pixels,
a spurting block spurts every 100–300 steps, and 1 spurt in 8 is a blob of magma rather than a flame
<Cite gml="objects/oLava/create.gml:2-5" /> <Cite gml="objects/oLava/step.gml:1-15" />. The blob flies up
and falls, leaving a fiery trail; if it lands on the ground, it forms into a Magma Man there, carrying the
blob's hit points <Cite gml="objects/oMagma/step.gml:3-9" /> <Cite gml="objects/oMagma/other_animation_end.gml:1-6" />.
A blob that falls back into the lava, leaves the screen, hits water or hasn't landed within 60 steps (2
seconds) is gone <Cite gml="objects/oDetritus/create.gml:5" /> <Cite gml="objects/oDetritus/step.gml:1-15" />
<Cite gml="objects/oMagma/collision_oWater.gml:1-2" />. The clip shows a spurt throwing a blob onto the floor
beside a pool of lava, where it forms into a Magma Man, which wanders the floor until it burns out
<Cite clip="magma-man/spawn" />.

**Walking.** A Magma Man walks at <Stat id="magma-man.walk-speed" />
(<Stat id="magma-man.walk-speed" as="per-second" />), turning around at walls. At the edge of a ledge, and
with a 1 in 100 chance each step, it stops to stand for 20–50 steps (0.67–1.67 seconds), then sets off in
a random direction <Cite gml="objects/oMagmaMan/step.gml:30-85" />. Each time it sets off, it has a 1 in 6
chance of melting back into a blob of magma instead, which keeps its hit points and forms again where it
lands <Cite gml="objects/oMagmaMan/step.gml:37-48" />. A Magma Man that isn't standing on anything, knocked
off a ledge or with the floor blown out from under it, turns into a new blob at once, which has a fresh
<Stat id="magma-man.hp" bare />, whatever it had left <Cite gml="objects/oMagmaMan/step.gml:13-19" />
<Cite gml="objects/oMagma/create.gml:9" /> <Cite quirk="1" />.

**Burning out.** It loses 1 hit point every step, so its <Stat id="magma-man.hp" /> last at most
<Stat id="magma-man.hp" bare /> steps (6.67 seconds). With none left, or if it ends up inside solid ground,
it stops and crumbles away <Cite gml="objects/oMagmaMan/step.gml:1-8" />
<Cite gml="objects/oMagmaMan/other_animation_end.gml:1-4" />.

## Damage

<Clip id="magma-man/touch" />

When the player touches a Magma Man, unless he's invincible from a hit in the last 30 steps, he loses
<Stat id="magma-man.damage" />, is knocked up and away from it, is stunned, and catches fire for
<Stat id="magma-man.burn-time" /> (<Stat id="magma-man.burn-time" as="seconds" />), which only looks the part
<Cite gml="objects/oMagmaMan/collision_oCharacter.gml:1-22" /> <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:188-193" />.
Its touch has no stomp: landing on one burns him the same. A flying blob of magma does the same, for
<Stat id="magma-man.blob-damage" /> <Cite gml="objects/oMagma/collision_oCharacter.gml:1-18" />. The clip
shows a Magma Man walking into the player, then melting into a blob that bounces into him again and kills
him <Cite clip="magma-man/touch" />.

It burns other monsters too: one it touches loses 2 hit points, catches fire, is knocked away and, but for
a [[tomb-lord|Tomb Lord]] or the [[yeti-king|Yeti King]], is stunned <Cite gml="objects/oMagmaMan/collision_oEnemy.gml:1-19" />.

## Defeating

<Clip id="magma-man/bomb" />

A Magma Man can't be killed so much as waited out. Most of what kills monsters does nothing to it:

| Way | Effect | |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [[whip\|Whip]] | Nothing: it only makes a hit sound | <Cite gml="objects/oMagmaMan/collision_oWhip.gml:1-6" /> <Cite quirk="1" /> |
| The Whip's wind-up behind the player | 1 for every step it touches it ([[whip-every-step]]) | <Cite gml="objects/oEnemy/collision_oWhipPre.gml:1" /> <Cite quirk="1" /> |
| A [[machete\|Machete]]'s or [[mattock\|Mattock]]'s swing | Their slash or hit in front, nothing; their wind-up, 2 a step (the Mattock makes two, 4 a step where they overlap) | <Cite gml="objects/oMagmaMan/collision_oWhip.gml:1-6" /> <Cite gml="objects/oEnemy/collision_oWhipPre.gml:1" /> |
| [[stomping\|Stomp]] | None: it burns the player instead | <Cite gml="objects/oMagmaMan/collision_oCharacter.gml:1-22" /> |
| A [[throwing\|thrown item]] or stunned monster | Nothing: they pass through it | <Cite gml="objects/oItem/step.gml:261" /> <Cite gml="objects/oEnemy/step.gml:177" /> |
| A thrown [[jar\|Jar]] or [[skull\|Skull]], whose own code differs | 1 off its life, and the Jar or Skull breaks | <Cite gml="objects/oJar/step.gml:131-139" /> <Cite gml="objects/oSkull/step.gml:129-137" /> |
| A [[shotgun\|Shotgun]] or [[pistol\|Pistol]] bullet | <Stat id="pistol.damage" /> | <Cite gml="objects/oBullet/collision_oEnemy.gml:16" /> |
| A [[bomb\|Bomb]]'s explosion | Splits it into two blobs of magma | <Cite gml="objects/oExplosion/collision_oEnemy.gml:1-11" /> |
| Its own heat | 1 every step | <Cite gml="objects/oMagmaMan/step.gml:1" /> |

An explosion that touches a Magma Man replaces it with two blobs of magma, each with a fresh
<Stat id="magma-man.hp" />, so a bomb makes two Magma Men where there was one
<Cite gml="objects/oExplosion/collision_oEnemy.gml:1-11" /> <Cite gml="objects/oMagma/create.gml:9" /> <Cite quirk="1" />. A bomb
it touches is lit, going off 8–12 steps later, and knocked away from it
<Cite gml="objects/oMagmaMan/collision_oBomb.gml:1-18" />. The clip shows a bomb going off by a Magma Man:
the blast kills the player, beside it, and the Magma Man bursts into two blobs, which land and form two
Magma Men <Cite clip="magma-man/bomb" />.

Its own step event replaces the one most monsters share, which is where [[spikes|Spikes]], [[lava|Lava]],
[[spear-trap|Spear Traps]] and water hurt them, so none of those harm a Magma Man
<Cite gml="objects/oEnemy/step.gml:34-128" />. Its death isn't counted as a kill.

## Locations

Magma Men aren't made with the level: they come from lava spurts, wherever there's lava with open air above
it on a level. That's [[temple|the Temple]], whose rooms can have pools of lava
<Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGen4.gml:137-138" /> <Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGen4.gml:497-500" />, and
[[olmecs-lair|Olmec's Lair]], with its sea of lava below <Cite gml="objects/oLava/step.gml:1-15" />. Custom levels with lava get them too, as the
[[level-editor|level editor]] has lava (tile `l`) but no Magma Man <Cite gml="scripts/scrCreateTileObj.gml:69-72" />.

## Tips

- A Magma Man burns out within <Stat id="magma-man.hp" bare /> steps (6.67 seconds), so waiting at a
  distance is the safe answer.
- Don't bomb one: an explosion turns it into two, each with a fresh <Stat id="magma-man.hp" bare /> steps
  to live.
- Landing on a Magma Man burns the player: it can't be stomped.
- It burns other monsters for 2 hit points, so it can thin out the Temple's Cavemen and Hawkmen.

## Trivia

- Its object has a throwing state, as the [[hawkman|Hawkman]]'s does, which nothing uses

- A Magma Man that takes the player's last heart in a game (not the tutorial or a custom level) is counted
  in the Magma Man row of the deaths-by-enemy table the game writes to `stats.txt`, but a blob of magma that
  does isn't counted anywhere <Cite gml="objects/oMagmaMan/collision_oCharacter.gml:15-19" />
  <Cite gml="scripts/isRealLevel.gml:7" />. Its touch is a copy of the Magma Man's without the tally
  <Cite gml="objects/oMagma/collision_oCharacter.gml:15" />, and the statistics' table has no row for blobs
  <Cite gml="scripts/scrWriteStats.gml:110-143" /> <Cite quirk="1" />.
