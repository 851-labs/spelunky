Each Crate holds one item, picked when it's opened, mostly a [[bomb-bag|Bomb Bag]] or a
[[rope-pile|Rope Pile]], but sometimes equipment or a weapon that shops sell for thousands. It's heavy,
so it's carried high and thrown short, and the player can open it wherever he stands on it.

## Usage

<Clip id="crate/open" />

Pressing up and the attack key while the player stands on a Crate opens it: the Crate vanishes in a puff of
smoke and its item appears in its place, free <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:612-647" />. On the
game's own levels, it adds to the lifetime count of crates opened <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:614" />.
In the clip, the player opens a Crate he's holding, which leaves his hands empty, and it holds a
[[pitchers-mitt|Pitcher's Mitt]], which he picks up and puts on <Cite clip="crate/open" />. The item comes from one roll down this list, each roll made only if the ones
before it fail <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:616-636" />:

The Crate in the [[tutorial|Tutorial]] always holds a Bomb Bag <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:616" />.
The player stands on the item when it appears, so a Bomb Bag or a Rope Pile, which are collected by touch,
are collected at once: in the second clip, a Crate's Rope Pile gives him 3 more ropes on the step he opens
it <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:2020-2023" /> <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:2052-2055" /> <Cite clip="crate/rope" />.

A Crate is heavy: the player carries it higher than other items, and throws it at 4 pixels a step plus his
own speed, rising at 2, or at 4 upward <Cite gml="objects/oItem/step.gml:44-56" />
<Cite gml="scripts/scrUseItem.gml:609-635" />. Thrown faster than 2 pixels a step, it hurts a monster as any
thrown item does <Cite gml="objects/oItem/step.gml:256-350" />.

## Locations

- **Level generation**, in every area: in a nook one block high with a wall beside it, a 1 in 10 chance,
  once the rolls for a web and, on the level that has one, the Udjat Eye's locked chest
  fail <Cite gml="scripts/scrTreasureGen.gml:47-68" />.
- **[[temple|The Temple]]**: some of its rooms have a tile that's a Crate or a [[chest|Chest]], one chance in
  two each <Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGen4.gml:359-363" />.
- **[[olmecs-lair|Olmec's Lair]]**: a 1 in 10 chance on some of its tiles, once rolls for a Chest, gold
  and big gems fail <Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGen5.gml:177-184" />.
- **The [[megamouth|Megamouth]]**: four Crates burst out of one when it dies <Cite gml="objects/oJaws/step.gml:23-28" />.
- **The [[tutorial|Tutorial]]**, which has one <Cite room="rTutorial" />.
- **The [[level-editor|level editor]]**'s `C` tile <Cite gml="scripts/scrCreateTileObj.gml:289-292" />.

## Interactions

- **Holding it.** The player can open a Crate he's holding, as he stands where it is; his hands are empty
  after <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:639-643" /> <Cite clip="crate/open" />.
- **Shops.** A Crate's item is free wherever it's opened: the code sets its price to
  nothing <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:637" />.
- **[[bomb|Bombs]].** An explosion throws a Crate away from it without breaking
  it <Cite gml="objects/oExplosion/collision_oItem.gml:30-36" />.
- **Exits.** The player sets down a heavy item, a Crate among them, when he takes an exit, so a Crate can't
  be carried to the next level <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:815-820" />.

## Tips

- A Crate is best opened where its item can't fall away: the item appears where the Crate is, and has no
  speed of its own.
- Most Crates hold a Bomb Bag or a Rope Pile: the rolls before them all fail about 69 times in 100 (the
  product of each roll's chance of failing, 499 in 500 by 199 in 200 and so on).

## Trivia

- The code that opens a Crate stores it in a variable named `chest`, the same one the code for opening a
  Chest uses <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:564" /> <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:615" />.
