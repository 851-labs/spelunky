Each shot is a ball of web that turns into a [[web]] where it lands, on a wall, on the floor or on a monster, and
a web holds every monster in it still, all but [[spider|Spiders]] and [[giant-spider|Giant Spiders]]. It does
no damage, and the webs it makes fade away. [[weapon-shop|Weapon Shops]] and [[rare-shop|Rare Shops]] sell
it, and [[crate|Crates]] and buried blocks give it away.

## Usage

<Clip id="web-cannon/caveman" />

The Web Cannon is picked up like any item, crouching over it and pressing the attack key. While it's held,
the attack key fires it, on the ground, in the air, hanging or climbing; crouching, the key puts it down in
front of the player instead <Cite gml="scripts/scrUseItem.gml:332-397" />. It isn't thrown. A shot fires one
web ball from 12 pixels in front of the player at <Stat id="web-cannon.web-ball-speed" />, never slower than
6 forward, knocks him 1 pixel a step back and 1 up unless he's hanging or climbing, and starts a wait
of <Stat id="web-cannon.cooldown" /> before the next <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:175" />.

The ball falls as it flies, 0.2 pixels a step faster each step, up to
6 <Cite gml="objects/oWebBall/step.gml:1-11" />. It stops on the first block, water, item, web or monster it
touches (but a Giant Spider), or when its life of 20 to 100 steps runs out, and spins out into a web
there <Cite gml="objects/oWebBall/collision_oSolid.gml:1-3" /> <Cite gml="objects/oWebBall/collision_oEnemy.gml:1-6" /> <Cite gml="objects/oWebBall/create.gml:6" /> <Cite gml="objects/oWebBall/other_animation_end.gml:1-6" />. A
web it makes fades out, and is gone 551 steps (about 18 seconds) after it
appears <Cite gml="objects/oWeb/create.gml:1-2" /> <Cite gml="objects/oWeb/step.gml:1-3" />.

In the clip, the player fires along the floor, and the ball makes a web a few blocks ahead. A
[[caveman|Caveman]] spots him and charges into it, and stops dead <Cite clip="web-cannon/caveman" />.

The Web Cannon goes with the player through a level's exit if he's holding it, and is in his hands on the
next level <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:842-850" /> <Cite gml="objects/oLevel/create.gml:25-30" />.
He drops it when he's stunned, or when he teeters too long at a ledge's
edge <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:329-345" /> <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:150-171" />.

## Damage

None: neither the ball nor the web hurts anything. A web holds what's in it instead, on every step:

| What's in the web | What happens | |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Any monster but a [[spider\|Spider]] or a [[giant-spider\|Giant Spider]] | It's held still | <Cite gml="objects/oWeb/collision_oEnemy.gml:1-5" /> |
| A [[magma-man\|Magma Man]] | It burns the web away | <Cite gml="objects/oWeb/collision_oEnemy.gml:7-10" /> |
| A live [[shopkeeper\|Shopkeeper]] | Held still, and set to attacking, as an angry one does | <Cite gml="objects/oWeb/collision_oEnemy.gml:12-15" /> |
| Items (not held, and not ropes) and treasure | They stop where they are | <Cite gml="objects/oWeb/collision_oItem.gml:1-5" /> <Cite gml="objects/oWeb/collision_oTreasure.gml:1-2" /> |
| The player | He moves slowly, and each jump out of it wears it down | <Cite gml="scripts/characterStepEvent.gml:294-305" /> <Cite gml="scripts/characterStepEvent.gml:743-748" /> |

Every block of rock away from the level's edges that isn't gold and holds no gem has a 1 in 1200 chance of

## Interactions

- **The [[machete|Machete]]** cuts its webs, as it does any web <Cite gml="objects/oWeb/collision_oSlash.gml:1" />.
- **Water** washes a web away, and a ball that touches water makes its web
  there <Cite gml="objects/oWeb/collision_oWater.gml:1" /> <Cite gml="objects/oWebBall/collision_oWater.gml:1-3" />.
- **Giant Spiders.** A ball goes through a Giant Spider rather than stopping on it, and webs don't hold
  either kind of spider <Cite gml="objects/oWebBall/collision_oEnemy.gml:1" />.

## Tips

- A web shot ahead along the floor, as in the clip, stops a charging Caveman before it reaches the player.
- The player's own webs hold him too, so it's better to fire them away from where he means to go.

## Trivia

- The webs a level starts with never fade; only the Web Cannon's do, as its ball marks the web it makes as
  dying <Cite gml="objects/oWebBall/other_animation_end.gml:3-4" /> <Cite gml="objects/oWeb/create.gml:2" />.
- The shot fires its single ball from a loop that runs once, `for (i = 0; i < 1; i += 1)`, and plays the
  [[shotgun|Shotgun]]'s sound <Cite gml="scripts/scrUseItem.gml:379-394" />.
