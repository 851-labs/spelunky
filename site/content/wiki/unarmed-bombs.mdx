A lit [[bomb|Bomb]] the player throws is armed, which the game checks in two places: a Bomb going through an
exit in his hands is let go and left behind, and one near a shop angers its
[[shopkeeper|Shopkeeper]]. Bombs lit by other things look and burn the same but aren't armed, so neither
check acts on them.

## What happens

The player opens a [[chest|Chest]], and out pops a lit bomb. He picks it up and takes the exit with it:
instead of being left behind, it goes back into his bag, and his bombs go from 4 to 5
<Cite clip="quirks/chest-trap-bomb-taken-out" />.

<Clip id="quirks/chest-trap-bomb-taken-out" />

## Why

One time in 12, opening a Chest lets out a trap: a Bomb with the lit sprite and a 40-step fuse, but `armed`,
which every item starts without, is never set <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:568-577" />. At a level's exit, the player's
step lets go of an armed Bomb and puts any other back in his bombs
<Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:821-831" />:

```gml objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:821-831
else if (holdItem.type == "Bomb")
{
    if (holdItem.armed)
    {
        holdItem.held = false;
    }
    else
    {
        global.bombs += 1;
        with holdItem { instance_destroy(); }
    }
```

That's what puts an unlit Bomb carried out back in the bag, and the trap bomb, lit but unarmed, goes back in
too. Olmec's exit does the same
<Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:691-702" />. A Bomb near a shop angers its Shopkeeper only if it's
armed <Cite gml="objects/oBomb/step.gml:6" />.

Only the player's throw, a [[monkey|Monkey]] and the Sun Room arm a bomb; an explosion setting one off, a
[[magma-man|Magma Man]]'s touch and the mothership's barrier light it unarmed. The player being stunned or
killed arms whatever he drops <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:1714-1720" />. Of the unarmed ones, only
the Chest's burns long enough to carry out: the others go off in 4 to 12 steps <Cite quirk="1" />.

## Reproducing it

Open Chests until one lets out a bomb, pick it up at once and walk through the exit within its 40 steps. The
replay of the clip's staged scene pins it: the trap bomb, unarmed, picked up lit and taken through the exit,
puts the bombs from 4 to 5, with no explosion
<Cite replay="fidelity/replays/item-chest-trap-bomb-unarmed.txt" />.

## In the port

The spelunky.ai port reproduces it exactly: it's in docs/quirks.md's list of the 1.1 source's bugs
<Cite quirk="1" />, and the oracle's fingerprint of the replay pins the bomb count. The Shopkeeper's side is
read in the code and not yet in a replay. No option turns it off.
