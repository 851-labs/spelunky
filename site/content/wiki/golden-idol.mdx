Unlike gold and gems, it isn't collected by touch: the player picks it up and has to carry it to the exit, and
it's worth more the deeper the area, from <Stat id="golden-idol.value" /> in [[mines|the Mines]] to
<Stat id="golden-idol.value-by-area" at="3" /> in [[temple|the Temple]]. Each area's idol room is built around
a trap that lifting it sets off, from the Mines' rolling [[boulder]] to the Temple's falling [[ceiling-trap|ceiling]]. A level
has one at most.

## Value

The idol's base value is <Stat id="golden-idol.value" />, multiplied by the area's number: twice that in the
Jungle, three times in the Ice Caves, four times in the Temple
<Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:2068-2084" />.

| Area | Value |
| --- | --- |
| [[mines\|The Mines]] | <Stat id="golden-idol.value-by-area" at="0" /> |
| [[jungle\|The Jungle]] | <Stat id="golden-idol.value-by-area" at="1" /> |
| [[ice-caves\|The Ice Caves]] | <Stat id="golden-idol.value-by-area" at="2" /> |
| [[temple\|The Temple]] | <Stat id="golden-idol.value-by-area" at="3" /> |

## Locations

Level generation builds an idol room in one of a level's side rooms, the rooms off the path from the entrance
to the exit, at most once a level:

| Area | Chance | The room | |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [[mines\|The Mines]] | 1 in 10 for each side room off the bottom row, once the roll for an altar (from level 2, 1 in 16) fails | The idol on an altar, a giant tiki head high above it | <Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGen.gml:85-117" /> |
| [[jungle\|The Jungle]] | 1 in 9 for two side rooms in three, once the altar's 1 in 12 fails | The idol on a floor of six [[trap-block\|trap blocks]] over a pool of water; on a graveyard level, the [[crystal-skull\|Crystal Skull]] instead | <Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGen2.gml:79-114" /> |
| [[ice-caves\|The Ice Caves]] | 1 in 9 for half the side rooms, once the altar's 1 in 12 fails | The idol on a ledge of thin ice | <Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGen3.gml:68-99" /> |
| [[temple\|The Temple]] | 1 in 12 for three side rooms in four, once the altar's 1 in 12 fails; never in the City of Gold | The idol under a row of ceiling traps, between two doors | <Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGen4.gml:68-111" /> |

The [[sacrifice-pit|Sacrifice Pit]], a Temple level, has its own: an idol beside a [[damsel|Damsel]], the two of them on
four trap blocks at the top of the pit, and no idol room <Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGen4.gml:193-196" />
<Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGen4.gml:399-405" /> <Cite gml="scripts/scrLevelGen.gml:84-86" />. In custom levels,
it's the [[level-editor|level editor]]'s `#` tile <Cite gml="scripts/scrCreateTileObj.gml:261-264" />.

Level generation keeps the loose Rocks, Jars and treasure it scatters 64 pixels away from an idol
<Cite gml="scripts/scrTreasureGen.gml:29" />.

## Collecting

### Lifting it

<Clip id="golden-idol/boulder" />

The player picks it up as any item, crouching over it and pressing the attack key. It's heavy: he carries it
level with himself, can't swap it for a bomb or a rope, and throws it at 4 pixels a step plus his own
speed <Cite gml="objects/oItem/step.gml:44-50" /> <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:993" />
<Cite gml="scripts/scrUseItem.gml:609-635" />. The first time it's lifted, it sets off the trap of its area
<Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:1222-1282" />, unless it has already left the ground, knocked off its
perch by an explosion, say: its own code then turns the trap off, and lifting it sets off nothing
<Cite gml="objects/oGoldIdol/step.gml:29-33" />. The traps:

| Area | What happens | |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [[mines\|The Mines]] | <Stat id="golden-idol.boulder-delay" /> (<Stat id="golden-idol.boulder-delay" as="seconds" />) later, with the screen shaking all the while, the giant tiki head above it opens and drops a [[boulder]] | <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:1225-1231" /> <Cite gml="objects/oGiantTikiHead/alarm_0.gml:1-3" /> |
| [[jungle\|The Jungle]] | The trap blocks within 90 pixels of the player crumble from the outside in: the outer two at once, the two under the idol 32 steps later. The player drops toward the water below | <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:1240-1248" /> <Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGen2.gml:477-482" /> |
| [[ice-caves\|The Ice Caves]] | Nothing | <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:1222-1282" /> |
| [[temple\|The Temple]] | Every [[ceiling-trap\|Ceiling Trap]] block on the level starts coming down, and two doors start to drop: the ones nearest the points 64 pixels above the player and 64 to his left and his right | <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:1252-1266" /> |
| The [[sacrifice-pit\|Sacrifice Pit]] | The trap blocks within 90 pixels of the player vanish, and he drops into the pit | <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:1267-1279" /> |

The boulder lands and rolls toward the player at 4.5 pixels a step, which comes to 8 to 10 in play, as the
game moves it twice each step <Cite gml="objects/oBoulder/step.gml:33-53" /> <Cite quirk="1" />. It breaks the
blocks it rolls into, slowing a little at each, and bounces back off any it can't break
<Cite gml="objects/oBoulder/collision_oSolid.gml:1-30" />. In the first clip, the player lifts the idol from
under the tiki head and the boulder comes down onto him and kills him <Cite clip="golden-idol/boulder" />
<Cite replay="fidelity/replays/trap-boulder-crush.txt" />. A [[bomb|Bomb]]'s explosion blows the boulder
apart, leaving rubble and sometimes [[rock|Rocks]]
<Cite gml="objects/oExplosion/collision_oBoulder.gml:1-13" />. Picked up in a custom level, an idol sets off
nothing <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:1222" />.

### Cashing it in

<Clip id="golden-idol/exit" />

Carried to the level's exit, the idol is taken from the player's hands and its value is added to his money,
counted in after a short pause <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:2068-2084" />
<Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:1953-1971" />, and the screen between levels shows it with the rest of
the level's loot <Cite gml="objects/oTransition/alarm_0.gml:102-107" />. In the second clip, the player grabs
the Mines' idol and drops down a shaft while the boulder rolls along the corridor above, then takes the exit
with it for <Stat id="golden-idol.value" /> <Cite clip="golden-idol/exit" />.

An idol can also be sold. Set down, not held, on a shop's floor within 80 pixels of the level's first
[[shopkeeper|Shopkeeper]] (the code checks that one, not the nearest) while he's standing idle, it's paid for at its full
value with the message "PLEASURE DOING BUSINESS!", unless the player has stolen from a shop or killed a
Shopkeeper <Cite gml="objects/oGoldIdol/step.gml:6-27" />.

## Trivia

- The code names it the "Gold Idol" <Cite gml="objects/oGoldIdol/create.gml:3" />, and the
  [[crystal-skull|Crystal Skull]] is a Gold Idol too, to the code: it follows every idol rule, with a value
  and a look of its own.
- In the Jungle and the Ice Caves, lifting the idol doesn't turn its trigger off, so the lifetime count of
  idols grabbed, which the game only writes to its statistics file, counts it on every lift until the idol's
  own code turns the trigger off, counting it once more, when the player first lets go of it: each idol
  counts at least twice <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:1222-1282" /> <Cite gml="objects/oGoldIdol/step.gml:29-33" /> <Cite quirk="1" />.
  A [[crystal-skull|Crystal Skull]] lifted and set down counts two <Cite replay="fidelity/replays/item-seed-213-crystal-skull-sold.txt" />.
