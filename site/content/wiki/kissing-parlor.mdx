Its Shopkeeper welcomes the player with "WELCOME TO DUKE'S KISSING PARLOR!", his name one of 32 drawn at
random, and "$10000 A KISS. PRESS P.", the price on level 2 <Cite gml="objects/oShopkeeper/step.gml:132" />
<Cite gml="objects/oShopkeeper/step.gml:140-144" /> <Cite gml="scripts/scrGetName.gml:24-60" />. It has no
wares on its floor: a [[damsel|Damsel]] stands near him, for sale, and the shop's lamp is red
<Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGen.gml:186" /> <Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGen.gml:427-431" /> <Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGen.gml:456-461" />. Like every
shop it's a side room off the way through a level, and its level has no other Damsel to rescue
<Cite gml="scripts/scrTreasureGen.gml:73-79" />.

## Stock

<Clip id="kissing-parlor/kiss" />

The [[controls|pay key]], in the shop and within 16 pixels of the Damsel, buys a kiss when the player has
the money and isn't holding her: the price comes out of his money and he gains
<Stat id="health.kiss-heal" /> ("NOW AIN'T SHE SWEET!"). She kisses him, a heart rises over them, and she
goes back to waiting <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:1422-1450" />
<Cite gml="objects/oDamsel/step.gml:169-178" /> <Cite gml="objects/oDamsel/other_animation_end.gml:7-10" />.
Kisses can be bought one after another, a heart each, as long as the money lasts. Without the money, the
pay key gets "YOU NEED $10000! GET OUTTA HERE, DEADBEAT!", and a thief or a killer of Shopkeepers gets
nothing <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:1425-1428" /> <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:1451-1456" />.

In the clip, on level 5, with the [[ghost|Ghost]] a [[crystal-skull|Crystal Skull]] summoned close behind,
the player runs back into Lazlo's Kissing Parlor and pays $25,000 beside the Damsel: she kisses him, and
his 1 heart becomes 2 <Cite clip="kissing-parlor/kiss" /> <Cite replay="fidelity/replays/shop-seed-213-kiss-whip.txt" />.

The Damsel herself is for sale too. Picked up, the Shopkeeper names her price, three times a kiss's
("I'LL LET YOU HAVE HER FOR $30000!"), and the pay key buys her ("YOU MUST BE IN LOVE!"): she's the
player's, to carry to the exit and rescue like any Damsel <Cite gml="objects/oDamsel/create.gml:12-14" />
<Cite gml="scripts/scrStealItem.gml:314-324" />. Without the money, he puts her down and is told "YOU NEED
$30000! GET OUTTA HERE, DEADBEAT!" <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:1370-1378" />
<Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:1451-1456" />.

## Prices

A kiss costs $10,000 on level 2 and $5,000 more for each level after it, and the Damsel three times that
<Cite gml="scripts/getKissValue.gml:1" /> <Cite gml="objects/oDamsel/create.gml:12" />:

| | Level 2 | Level 5 | Level 9 | Level 13 | Level 15 | |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| A kiss | <Stat id="damsel.kiss-price" at="2" /> | <Stat id="damsel.kiss-price" at="5" /> | <Stat id="damsel.kiss-price" at="9" /> | <Stat id="damsel.kiss-price" at="13" /> | <Stat id="damsel.kiss-price" at="15" /> | |
| The Damsel | $30,000 | $75,000 | $135,000 | $195,000 | $225,000 | <Cite gml="objects/oDamsel/create.gml:12" /> |

The price rises faster than any ware's: a ware costs 10% of its base price more a level, a kiss half its
level-2 price more <Cite gml="scripts/scrShopItemsGen.gml:179-191" />.

## Shopkeeper

The Shopkeeper sells the Damsel as a ware ([[general-store#shopkeeper|the General Store]] has the rules
every shop shares): he names her price while she's held and follows the player about the shop
<Cite gml="objects/oShopkeeper/step.gml:72-85" /> <Cite gml="objects/oShopkeeper/step.gml:225-235" />. What
is the Kissing Parlor's own is the shout for hurting her, "HEY, ONLY I CAN DO THAT!"
<Cite gml="scripts/scrShopkeeperAnger.gml:17" />:

| What | |
| --- | --- |
| A crack of the [[whip\|Whip]] at her, while she waits or kisses | <Cite gml="objects/oDamsel/collision_oWhip.gml:13-23" /> <Cite gml="objects/oDamsel/collision_oWhipPre.gml:13-23" /> |
| A [[throwing\|thrown item]] hitting her | <Cite gml="objects/oItem/step.gml:365-395" /> |
| Her death, while she has a price | <Cite gml="objects/oDamsel/step.gml:50-54" /> |

The [[machete|Machete]]'s slash hurts her without angering him, until it kills her
<Cite gml="objects/oDamsel/collision_oWhip.gml:1-12" />. The whip's crack only makes her hop, and she lies
stunned where she lands, as any Damsel does ([[damsel-whip-hop-thrown]]) <Cite quirk="1" row="A whipped Damsel's hop counts as a throw." />. Angered, he
frees her with every other item, and attacks <Cite gml="objects/oShopkeeper/step.gml:337-348" />. In the
clip above, the player cracks the whip at the Damsel while she kisses him; the Shopkeeper shouts and
shoots him dead <Cite clip="kissing-parlor/kiss" />.

## Stealing

<Clip id="kissing-parlor/theft" />

Carried out of the shop's room unpaid, the Damsel is stolen as any ware is: the Shopkeeper shouts "COME
BACK HERE, THIEF!" and attacks <Cite gml="objects/oItem/step.gml:17-34" />
<Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:541-557" />. The player is marked a thief into the next level
([[general-store#stealing|the General Store]] has the rules). When he's out of the room as soon as she is, she
goes to him as if bought, and the end-of-game statistics count her among the Damsels bought, not the items
stolen <Cite gml="scripts/scrStealItem.gml:314-318" /> <Cite quirk="1" row="A stolen Damsel counts as bought, not stolen." />
<Cite replay="fidelity/replays/shop-seed-22-kissing-theft.txt" />. When she leaves first, she's counted
stolen, and not bought <Cite gml="objects/oItem/step.gml:17-26" />.
In the clip, on level 2, the player asks for a kiss without the $10,000, picks the Damsel up, can't pay
the $30,000 for her, then picks her up again and walks out with her; the Shopkeeper shoots him dead
<Cite clip="kissing-parlor/theft" />.

## Locations

A level from 2 to 15 that rolls a shop builds one of seven kinds, each as likely, so a shop is a Kissing
Parlor one time in seven, in every area and on a [[yeti-lair|Yeti lair]] level too <Cite
gml="scripts/scrLevelGen.gml:239-297" /> <Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGen.gml:173-204" />
<Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGenYeti.gml:149-181" />. A Temple [[sacrifice-pit|sacrifice pit]] level, which has
a Damsel of its own, rolls one of the other six <Cite gml="scripts/scrLevelGen.gml:84-86" />
<Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGen4.gml:155-188" />. [[mines#shops|The Mines]] has how levels roll shops. The
[[black-market|Black Market]] has none <Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGenMarket.gml:112-146" />.
