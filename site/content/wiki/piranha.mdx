Level generation puts Piranhas in the water of [[jungle|the Jungle]], from its pools to the lakes of
[[flooded-level|flooded levels]]. A Piranha leaves a player on dry land alone, but once he's swimming near it,
it swims straight at him and bites, and a pool may hold several. With <Stat id="piranha.hp" />, it dies to any
damage, but a swimming player can't [[stomping|stomp]] it.

## Behaviour

A Piranha swims back and forth at 1 pixel per step, turning around where the water ends or a wall is in its
way <Cite gml="objects/oPiranha/step.gml:17-30" />. It starts out heading left or right at random
<Cite gml="objects/oPiranha/create.gml:12-13" />, and lets off a bubble every 31–51 steps
<Cite gml="objects/oPiranha/step.gml:123-128" />.

<Clip id="piranha/bite" />

When the player is swimming within <Stat id="piranha.attack-range" /> of it, and alive, it attacks: it swims
straight at him at <Stat id="piranha.attack-speed" /> (<Stat id="piranha.attack-speed" as="per-second" />),
its heading wobbling by a degree each step, and moves only while the way ahead is water
<Cite gml="objects/oPiranha/step.gml:32-35" /> <Cite gml="objects/oPiranha/step.gml:63-70" />. The game moves
it in whole pixels on each axis, so on a diagonal it covers 1 pixel across and 1 up or down in a step
<Cite gml="scripts/moveTo.gml:20-31" />. When he leaves the water or its range, it stops for 20–40 steps
(0.67–1.33 seconds) and then patrols again <Cite gml="objects/oPiranha/step.gml:53-62" />
<Cite gml="objects/oPiranha/step.gml:71-75" />. The clip shows the player dropping into a pool and two
Piranhas closing in on him.

A Piranha also hunts other monsters in the water. It looks at the nearest living [[caveman|Caveman]], or if
there's none, the nearest Shopkeeper, then Hawkman, then [[yeti|Yeti]], and if that one is swimming, swims at it and,
within 4 pixels of it, bites it for 1 hit point at most once every 10
steps <Cite gml="objects/oPiranha/step.gml:37-51" /> <Cite gml="objects/oPiranha/step.gml:77-121" />
<Cite gml="objects/oPiranha/alarm_0.gml:1" />.

**Out of the water.** A Piranha whose middle isn't in water turns into a [[fish-bone|Fish Bone]] at once
<Cite gml="objects/oPiranha/step.gml:136-140" />.

**Off screen.** Unlike most monsters, a Piranha has no check of its own for being on screen: it swims on
wherever the game keeps it running, which is up to 96 pixels outside the view, past which the game freezes
everything <Cite gml="objects/oPiranha/step.gml:1" /> <Cite gml="objects/oLevel/step.gml:64-77" />.

## Damage

When the player touches a Piranha without stomping it, he loses <Stat id="piranha.damage" />, is knocked away
from it, and blinks, invincible, for 30 steps (1 second), and the bite draws
blood <Cite gml="objects/oEnemy/collision_oCharacter.gml:14-25" />
<Cite gml="objects/oEnemy/collision_oCharacter.gml:55" />. This is the touch code most monsters share. The
clip shows two bites, a second apart, taking the player from four hearts to two.

## Defeating

A Piranha has <Stat id="piranha.hp" />, so any damage kills it <Cite gml="objects/oPiranha/step.gml:3-13" />:

| Way | Damage | |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [[whip\|Whip]] | <Stat id="whip.damage" /> | <Cite gml="objects/oEnemy/collision_oWhip.gml:1" /> |
| [[machete\|Machete]] or [[mattock\|Mattock]] | <Stat id="machete.damage" /> | <Cite gml="objects/oEnemy/collision_oWhip.gml:1" /> |
| [[stomping\|Stomp]] (landing on it), except while the player is swimming | 1, plus 1 for every 16 steps of falling; three times that with [[spike-shoes\|Spike Shoes]] | <Cite gml="objects/oEnemy/collision_oCharacter.gml:6-10" /> |
| A thrown item moving faster than 2 pixels per step, such as a [[rock\|Rock]] | 1 | <Cite gml="objects/oItem/step.gml:256-348" /> |
| A [[shotgun\|Shotgun]] or [[pistol\|Pistol]] bullet | <Stat id="pistol.damage" /> | <Cite gml="objects/oBullet/collision_oEnemy.gml:16" /> |
| A [[bomb\|Bomb]]'s explosion | <Stat id="bomb.damage" /> | <Cite gml="objects/oExplosion/collision_oEnemy.gml:14" /> |
| Being out of the water | Turns it into a Fish Bone | <Cite gml="objects/oPiranha/step.gml:136-140" /> |

The Piranha's own step event replaces the one most monsters share, which is where [[spikes|Spikes]],
[[lava|Lava]] and [[spear-trap|Spear Traps]] hurt them, so none of those harm a Piranha
<Cite gml="objects/oEnemy/step.gml:63-128" />.

Piranhas killed count toward the player's kills, and the screen between levels draws a Piranha icon for each
<Cite gml="objects/oPiranha/step.gml:6-11" /> <Cite gml="objects/oTransition/alarm_0.gml:149-154" />. One that
turns into a Fish Bone out of the water isn't killed, so it isn't counted.

## Locations

Level generation makes Piranhas only in [[jungle|the Jungle]]: each water block with no solid block in it has
a 1 in 30 chance of one. That includes the lakes of [[flooded-level|flooded levels]]; on a
[[restless-dead|graveyard level]], the same roll makes a [[dead-fish|Dead Fish]] instead
<Cite gml="scripts/scrEntityGen.gml:326-336" />.

Custom levels can have them, as the [[level-editor|level editor]]'s tile `p`, a water block with a Piranha in
it <Cite gml="scripts/scrCreateTileObj.gml:172-176" />.

## Tips

- A Piranha only chases a swimming player, so one crossing above the water isn't chased.
- Several Piranhas each bite as the player's invincibility runs out, so a pool with a few of them costs a
  heart a second until he gets out.
- The player can't stomp a Piranha while swimming; a thrown item or the Whip kills one.

## Trivia

- The Piranha's code names an attacking state for other monsters, Caveman, Shopkeeper, [[hawkman|Hawkman]] and Yeti, as
  well as the player, and sets a bitten one's state to 2, which for a Caveman is charging
  <Cite gml="objects/oPiranha/create.gml:22-26" /> <Cite gml="objects/oPiranha/step.gml:110" />.
- On a graveyard level, the Piranha's place is taken by the [[dead-fish|Dead Fish]], whose code is the
  Piranha's without its hunting of other monsters. A Dead Fish killed is added to the Piranha's row of the
  kills table the game writes to `stats.txt` <Cite gml="objects/oDeadFish/step.gml:10-15" />
  <Cite gml="scripts/scrWriteStats.gml:198" />.
