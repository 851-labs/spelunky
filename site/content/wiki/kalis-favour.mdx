The favour is how [[kali|Kali]], the goddess whose altars stand in side rooms of the Mines, the Jungle, the
Ice Caves and the Temple, keeps score of the player. It's the only reason to leave a body on her altar: it
decides her gifts, the [[kapala|Kapala]] among them, which nothing else gives. It carries over from level
to level, so the gifts come from sacrifices on several levels' altars. The other side of it is her anger:
every altar destroyed costs favour and brings a punishment that grows each time.

## How it works

### The count

A new game sets the favour to 0, and with it the count of gifts she has given and of altars the player has
defiled <Cite gml="scripts/scrClearGlobals.gml:65-67" />. Only two things change it:

- **A sacrifice** adds the body's worth: a Damsel's <Stat id="damsel.favor" bare />, a stunned monster's
  own value, a dead one's half of it ([[sacrifice]] has the rules and the values)
  <Cite gml="objects/oEnemy/step.gml:144-158" /> <Cite gml="objects/oDamsel/step.gml:325-339" />.
- **A defiled altar** takes 16 off it <Cite gml="objects/oSacAltarLeft/destroy.gml:18" />.

At <Stat id="kali.devour" bare /> or less, a sacrifice adds nothing: "KALI DEVOURS THE SACRIFICE!"
<Cite gml="objects/oEnemy/step.gml:144-147" />. Since only a sacrifice raises it, a favour that low stays
there for the rest of the game. Between that and 0, a sacrifice adds its worth as usual, though by a branch
of its own ([Quirks](#quirks)) <Cite gml="objects/oEnemy/step.gml:148-153" />
<Cite gml="objects/oDamsel/step.gml:329-334" /> <Cite quirk="1" />. A dead [[vampire|Vampire]] is worth half
of 1, so the favour needn't be a whole number
<Cite gml="objects/oEnemy/step.gml:154-158" />.

### Her messages

The favour is never shown. After each sacrifice, a second line under "KALI ACCEPTS THE SACRIFICE!" ("YOUR
SACRIFICE" for a Damsel, and "DEVOURS" for one that adds nothing) says where it stands, and her gifts come
with it <Cite gml="scripts/scrGetFavorMsg.gml:24-111" />:

| Favour after the sacrifice | Message | |
| --- | --- | --- |
| <Stat id="kali.devour" bare /> or less | SHE SEEMS VERY ANGRY WITH YOU! | <Cite gml="scripts/scrGetFavorMsg.gml:24" /> |
| Below 0 | SHE SEEMS ANGRY WITH YOU. | <Cite gml="scripts/scrGetFavorMsg.gml:25" /> |
| Exactly 0 | SHE HAS FORGIVEN YOU! | <Cite gml="scripts/scrGetFavorMsg.gml:26" /> |
| Above 0, below <Stat id="kali.gift-1" bare /> | SHE SEEMS PLEASED WITH YOU. | <Cite gml="scripts/scrGetFavorMsg.gml:111" /> |
| <Stat id="kali.gift-1" bare /> to below <Stat id="kali.gift-2" bare /> | SHE BESTOWS A GIFT UPON YOU! the first time, then SHE SEEMS HAPPY WITH YOU. | <Cite gml="scripts/scrGetFavorMsg.gml:67-76" /> |
| <Stat id="kali.gift-2" bare /> to below <Stat id="kali.gift-3" bare /> | SHE BESTOWS A GIFT UPON YOU! the first time, then SHE SEEMS VERY HAPPY WITH YOU! | <Cite gml="scripts/scrGetFavorMsg.gml:52-61" /> |
| <Stat id="kali.gift-3" bare /> or more | YOUR SATCHEL FEELS VERY FULL NOW! or YOU FEEL INVIGORATED! for a gift, otherwise SHE SEEMS ECSTATIC WITH YOU! | <Cite gml="scripts/scrGetFavorMsg.gml:27-50" /> |

### Her gifts

<Clip id="kalis-favour/kapala" />

A gift is given the first time a sacrifice brings the favour into its band, and appears on the altar's right
half, free for the player to take <Cite gml="scripts/scrGetFavorMsg.gml:62-64" />
<Cite gml="scripts/scrGetFavorMsg.gml:106-107" />. Which gifts have been given is kept as a count, so each is
given once a game, and one whose band a sacrifice jumps over is never given: going from 7 to 19 with a stunned
[[shopkeeper|Shopkeeper]]'s 12 gives the Kapala, and the equipment never comes
<Cite gml="scripts/scrGetFavorMsg.gml:52-76" />.

1. **At <Stat id="kali.gift-1" bare />**, a piece of equipment the player hasn't got, with two puffs of
   smoke. A roll of 1 to 8 picks the [[cape|Cape]], the [[climbing-gloves|Climbing Gloves]], the
   [[spectacles|Spectacles]], the [[pitchers-mitt|Pitcher's Mitt]], the [[spring-shoes|Spring Shoes]], the
   [[spike-shoes|Spike Shoes]], the [[paste|Paste]] or the [[compass|Compass]]; one he has passes the gift
   to the next in that list, and the Cape counts as had with a [[jetpack|Jetpack]]. With all of them, it's a
   Jetpack, or, with that too, a [[bomb-box|Bomb Box]] <Cite gml="scripts/scrGetFavorMsg.gml:77-105" />.
2. **At <Stat id="kali.gift-2" bare />**, the [[kapala|Kapala]] <Cite gml="scripts/scrGetFavorMsg.gml:58-64" />.
3. **At <Stat id="kali.gift-3" bare />**, the player's bombs are set to
   <Stat id="bombs-and-ropes.kali-bombs" bare />, if he has fewer than
   <Stat id="bombs-and-ropes.kali-bombs-below" bare />; otherwise he gets <Stat id="health.kali-heal" />
   <Cite gml="scripts/scrGetFavorMsg.gml:39-50" />.
4. **After that, at 48, 64 and every 16 more**, <Stat id="health.kali-heal" /> again
   <Cite gml="scripts/scrGetFavorMsg.gml:29-34" />.

No sacrifice is worth more than 12, so none reaches 32 from below 16 in one go.

### Her anger

Destroying either half of an altar, by anything, defiles it: a [[bomb|Bomb]]'s explosion, a [[ufo|UFO]]'s
laser, or the floor under it taken away, which makes it crumble once it's in view. "YOU DARE DEFILE MY
ALTAR?" and "I WILL PUNISH YOU!" show, the screen shakes, the favour falls by 16, and both halves go <Cite gml="objects/oSacAltarLeft/step.gml:1-5" />
<Cite gml="objects/oSacAltarLeft/destroy.gml:11-66" /> <Cite quirk="1" />. The punishment depends on how many altars the player has defiled before in the game
<Cite gml="objects/oSacAltarLeft/destroy.gml:20-60" />:

| Altar defiled | Punishment | |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The first | Six [[spider\|Spiders]] spill out of her statue's head, which is left a hole | <Cite gml="objects/oKaliHead/alarm_0.gml:1-8" /> |
| The second | A [[ball-and-chain\|ball and chain]] is locked to the player's ankle, and a new one on every level and every screen between levels after, to the end of the game | <Cite gml="objects/oSacAltarLeft/destroy.gml:27-38" /> <Cite gml="objects/oLevel/create.gml:31-42" /> <Cite gml="objects/oTransition/create.gml:26-37" /> |
| Any after | The level goes dark, and a [[ghost\|Ghost]] comes from the edge of the screen unless there's one already ([[ghost-timer]]); on a level already dark with a Ghost, six more Spiders instead | <Cite gml="objects/oSacAltarLeft/destroy.gml:39-58" /> |

Kali's page shows each punishment in a clip ([[kali#her-anger]]).

## Examples

- **A Damsel, then two Yetis.** On an Ice Caves level the player throws a [[damsel|Damsel]] down on the
  altar: the favour goes from 0 to 8 and Kali gives a Compass <Cite clip="damsel/sacrifice" />. A stunned
  [[yeti|Yeti]] on the same altar brings it to 12, and one on the next level's to 16, the Kapala. A UFO's laser
  then destroys that altar, the favour falls to 0, and six Spiders come out of the statue: the main clip
  shows the second Yeti's sacrifice and the laser <Cite clip="kalis-favour/kapala" />
  <Cite replay="fidelity/replays/item-seed-11-kapala.txt" />.
- **The punishments in turn.** A second defiled altar locks the ball and chain on the player, who carries
  it through the next levels <Cite replay="fidelity/replays/item-seed-38-ball-and-chain.txt" />; a third
  makes the level dark and sends a Ghost <Cite replay="fidelity/replays/item-seed-38-kali-third-punishment.txt" />;
  a fourth, on a level dark with a Ghost already, lets six more Spiders out
  <Cite replay="fidelity/replays/item-seed-38-kali-six-more-spiders.txt" />.

## Quirks

- **Anything that destroys an altar defiles it**, not only the player: a UFO's laser does, and the player is
  punished for it all the same <Cite quirk="1" /> <Cite replay="fidelity/replays/item-seed-11-kapala.txt" />.
- **An angry Kali's sacrifice has a branch of its own that changes nothing.** Below 0 but above
  <Stat id="kali.devour" bare />, a sacrifice adds the body's worth as above 0, then sets the body's own worth
  to 0 just before the body is destroyed, so a sacrifice is worth the same either way <Cite gml="objects/oEnemy/step.gml:148-153" /> <Cite gml="objects/oDamsel/step.gml:329-334" />
  <Cite quirk="1" />. In the replay, a defiled altar leaves the favour at −4, and a dead [[yeti|Yeti]] on the
  next level's altar adds its 2 <Cite replay="fidelity/replays/item-seed-11-kali-angry-sacrifice.txt" />.
