A graveyard level is a level of [[jungle|the Jungle]] laid out as any other, but with graves standing on
its ground, most with treasure buried under them. The dead walk there: [[zombie|Zombies]] and
[[vampire|Vampires]] take the place of the Jungle's ground monsters, [[dead-fish|Dead Fish]] swim in place
of [[piranha|Piranhas]], and piles of bones that may rise as [[skeleton|Skeletons]] are eleven times as
common. The Jungle's idol room becomes the [[crystal-skull|Crystal Skull]]'s. The level opens with "THE DEAD
ARE RESTLESS!".

## Generation

Every level of the Jungle makes the roll, 1 in 10, after its path is laid
<Cite gml="scripts/scrInitLevel.gml:144-148" /> <Cite gml="scripts/scrClearGlobals.gml:56" />. It's
separate from the other special levels' rolls, so a graveyard can be a [[flooded-level|flooded level]] or a
[[dark-level|dark level]] as well. It can be the [[black-market|Black Market]] too, one market in ten, which
never says so ([[black-market#special-levels]]) <Cite quirk="1" />.

It opens with "THE DEAD ARE RESTLESS!" <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/alarm_0.gml:27-33" />. On a dark
level, the message comes after the dark level's, and on a flooded level the lake's comes after it, as "YOU
HEAR RUSHING WATER..." if the level is dark too <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/alarm_1.gml:11-23" /> <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/alarm_4.gml:1-6" /> <Cite quirk="1" /> ([[flooded-level#the-roll]]). In the clip, level 8 loads
as a graveyard, with graves on the ground; the player whips a Zombie, kills two [[bat|Bats]], and is
bitten by a second Zombie before he jumps on it <Cite clip="restless-dead/arrival" />.

### The graves

[[level-generation|Level generation]] goes over every block of earth, and puts a grave on top of one with a 1 in 20 chance, if
nothing is there (a block, the entrance or the exit) and it isn't in one of the six columns that flank the
borders between rooms (`x` 160, 176, 320, 336, 480 and 496) <Cite gml="scripts/scrEntityGen.gml:176-187" />.
A grave is solid, one of five headstones at random, and breaks when the block under it does <Cite gml="objects/oGrave/create.gml:3-11" /> <Cite gml="objects/oSolid/destroy.gml:19-23" />.

The level's top border is earth with nothing over it inside the level, so most graveyard levels have a grave
or more above the top, out of sight, their treasure sealed in the border's rock <Cite quirk="1" /> <Cite replay="fidelity/replays/item-seed-420-ash-graves.txt" />.

### The Crystal Skull's room

The room that would hold the Jungle's [[golden-idol|Golden Idol]] (a side room's 1 in 9, once a level, once
the roll for [[kalis-altar|Kali's altar]] fails) holds the [[crystal-skull|Crystal Skull]] instead: a tomb of temple stone
with the skull inside, four piles of bones around it, which are always rising bones, and two push blocks <Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGen2.gml:79-114" /> <Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGen2.gml:490-494" />
<Cite quirk="1" />. Picking up the skull brings the [[ghost|Ghost]], if there's none on the level yet <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:1232-1239" /> <Cite clip="crystal-skull/ghost" />.

## Inhabitants

The ground's monsters are made on top of blocks with open space above, outside the entrance's room,
whether or not there's water above <Cite gml="scripts/scrEntityGen.gml:262-337" />:

| Monster | Where | Chance | |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [[zombie\|Zombie]] | On top of a block | 1 in 25 | <Cite gml="scripts/scrEntityGen.gml:280-282" /> |
| [[vampire\|Vampire]] | On top of a block | 1 in 160, once the Zombie's fails | <Cite gml="scripts/scrEntityGen.gml:283" /> |
| [[bat\|Bat]] | Hanging under a block | 1 in 60, where other Jungle levels have 1 in 80 | <Cite gml="scripts/scrEntityGen.gml:268-272" /> |
| [[dead-fish\|Dead Fish]] | In the water | 1 in 30 for each water tile | <Cite gml="scripts/scrEntityGen.gml:326-336" /> |
| [[monkey\|Monkey]] | On a vine | 1 in 15 for each vine tile | <Cite gml="scripts/scrEntityGen.gml:321-324" /> |
| [[skeleton\|Skeleton]] | Rising from a pile of bones, one pile in eight (below) | Bones eleven times as likely | <Cite gml="scripts/scrEntityGen.gml:255-259" /> <Cite gml="scripts/scrTreasureGen.gml:80-88" /> |
| [[scarab\|Scarab]] | Hanging under a block, on a [[dark-level\|dark level]] only | 1 in 40 | <Cite gml="scripts/scrEntityGen.gml:271" /> |

So level generation makes no [[mantrap|Mantraps]], [[caveman|Cavemen]], [[frog|Frogs]],
[[fire-frog|Fire Frogs]] or [[piranha|Piranhas]] on a graveyard level, and makes Zombies, Vampires and Dead
Fish nowhere else <Cite gml="scripts/scrEntityGen.gml:280-295" />. The [[ghost|Ghost]] comes to a player
who stays too long, as on every level <Cite gml="objects/oGame/step.gml:39-46" />.

## Hazards

- **[[zombie|Zombies]]** and **[[vampire|Vampires]]** on the ground.
- **[[dead-fish|Dead Fish]]** in the water.
- The Jungle's [[spear-trap|Spear Traps]] and [[spikes|Spikes]], as on its other levels.

## Items and shops

### The graves' treasure

Each grave may bury something in the block it stands on: a Shotgun for an Ash grave, or else gold or a gem,
if the block holds no treasure already <Cite gml="scripts/scrEntityGen.gml:188-202" />:

| What | Chance | |
| --- | --- | --- |
| An Ash grave, with a free [[shotgun\|Shotgun]] | 1 in 40 | <Cite gml="scripts/scrEntityGen.gml:188-195" /> |
| A [[gold-nugget\|Gold Nugget]] | 1 in 2, once the Ash grave's roll fails | <Cite gml="scripts/scrEntityGen.gml:198" /> |
| A [[big-sapphire\|Big Sapphire]] | 1 in 4, once those fail | <Cite gml="scripts/scrEntityGen.gml:199" /> |
| A [[big-emerald\|Big Emerald]] | 1 in 6, once those fail | <Cite gml="scripts/scrEntityGen.gml:200" /> |
| A [[big-ruby\|Big Ruby]] | 1 in 8, once those fail | <Cite gml="scripts/scrEntityGen.gml:201" /> |
| Nothing | Once those fail | <Cite gml="scripts/scrEntityGen.gml:196-202" /> |

The game means to make one Ash grave a level at most, but the flag it sets isn't the one it checks, so
every grave rolls its 1 in 40 afresh, and a level can have two or more ([[ash-graves]]) <Cite quirk="1" /> <Cite clip="quirks/ash-graves" />.

### Treasure and shops

Treasure is scattered as on [[jungle#items-and-shops|any Jungle level]], but piles of bones, each a
[[skeleton|Skeleton]] waiting to rise one time in eight, are eleven times as likely: their roll is passed
10, which it adds to the 1 it needs to come up <Cite gml="scripts/scrEntityGen.gml:255-259" /> <Cite gml="scripts/scrTreasureGen.gml:24" /> <Cite gml="scripts/scrTreasureGen.gml:80-88" />
<Cite gml="scripts/scrTreasureGen.gml:103-111" /> <Cite gml="scripts/scrTreasureGen.gml:124-132" />. Shops
come as on the Jungle's other levels.

## Secrets

- **The [[crystal-skull|Crystal Skull]]**, on a graveyard level with an idol room.
- **Ash graves**, each with a free [[shotgun|Shotgun]].

## Trivia

- The code spells the level's flag `cemetary` <Cite gml="scripts/scrInitLevel.gml:147-148" />.
- A grave's object has its own headstone sprite, which never shows: its create event always swaps it for one
  of five others <Cite gml="objects/oGrave/create.gml:3-11" /> <Cite quirk="1" />.
