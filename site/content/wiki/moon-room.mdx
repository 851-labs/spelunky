It's the second of the three secret challenges behind the trophies of the [[high-scores-room|high scores
room]], the ARCHERY CHALLENGE, and the reward for a fast win ([[speedrunning-basics]]). One room, built in
the room editor: ledges on the left for the player, a [[bow|Bow]] to pick up, and an open hall over a floor
of [[spikes|Spikes]], where three bat-shaped targets fly up and down. It's the only bonus room the player can
finish alive: the challenge runs on a 60-second timer.

## Generation

### Getting there

A best time of 600 seconds or less, 10:00 on the game's clock, puts a gold trophy second from the top of the
high scores room's column of trophies, and a door to the Moon Room under it
<Cite gml="objects/oHighscores/create.gml:50-66" />. Only a win from level 1, without a
[[tunnel-man-shortcuts|shortcut]], sets a time ([[scoring#the-high-scores]]). Climbing up beside it, the
board reads MOON ROOM and the goal, SHOOT THE MOVING TARGETS WITH YOUR BOW AND ARROWS!
<Cite gml="objects/oHighscores/draw.gml:41-66" />. It shows the goal for any win, though, however slow, while
the door needs the 10:00 ([[moon-room-goal-any-win]])
<Cite quirk="1" row="The Moon Room's goal shows for any winning time." />. In the clip, with one win at
11:40, the board shows the goal beside a silver trophy with no door <Cite clip="quirks/moon-room-goal-any-win" />.

<Clip id="quirks/moon-room-goal-any-win" />

### The room

The room editor laid out the whole room, a screen wide: the entrance on the highest of the ledges at the
left, the Bow beside it, Spikes along the hall's floor and a [[spring-trap|Spring Trap]]
<Cite room="rMoon" />. A carved crescent moon hangs on the back wall. As in every bonus room, the bricks
around its edge can't be broken <Cite gml="objects/oMoonRoom/create.gml:11-18" />, the player has
<Stat id="health.bonus-room" /> <Cite gml="objects/oMoonRoom/create.gml:1" />, and his bombs and ropes can't
be used outside a level ([[sun-room#the-room]]). The room gives him 100 arrows, and picking up its Bow adds
the 6 a new Bow brings <Cite gml="objects/oMoonRoom/create.gml:9" /> <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:1216-1220" />.

### The challenge

<Clip id="moon-room/archery" />

ARCHERY CHALLENGE BEGINS IN 3... counts down at the bottom of the screen, and as it ends three targets fly in
from the top <Cite gml="objects/oMoonRoom/draw.gml:10-18" /> <Cite gml="objects/oMoonRoom/alarm_3.gml:1-12" />.
They look like Bats, and each flies straight up and down, between 64 and 160 pixels from the top,
as fast as it's worth:

| Target | Points | Speed | |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Nearest the player | 1 | 1 pixel per step (30 per second) | <Cite gml="objects/oBatTarget/create.gml:5-7" /> <Cite gml="objects/oBatTarget/step.gml:1-16" /> |
| Middle | 2 | 2 pixels per step (60 per second) | <Cite gml="objects/oBatTarget/create.gml:5-7" /> |
| Furthest | 4 | 4 pixels per step (120 per second) | <Cite gml="objects/oBatTarget/create.gml:5-7" /> |

From 100 steps in, the timer at the top counts down from 60, a second every 30 steps <Cite gml="objects/oMoonRoom/create.gml:4-6" /> <Cite gml="objects/oMoonRoom/alarm_0.gml:19" />
<Cite gml="objects/oMoonRoom/alarm_11.gml:1-28" />. An arrow that hits a target scores its points, beside the
target icon at the top, and the target bleeds and is gone; a new one flies in from above in its place <Cite gml="objects/oBatTarget/collision_oArrow.gml:1-11" /> <Cite gml="objects/oMoonRoom/draw.gml:5-6" />.
Landing on one scores it too, and bounces the player up, as a stomp does; any other touch costs him a heart
and knocks him back, at most once every 30 steps <Cite gml="objects/oBatTarget/collision_oCharacter.gml:1-30" />. In the clip, the player
picks up the Bow, and from the countdown on looses an arrow every 2 seconds from the ledge: the nearest
target falls to the first that hits, for a point, and to the next, 6 seconds later <Cite clip="moon-room/archery" />.

### The last 20 seconds

<Clip id="moon-room/ufos" />

With 20 seconds or less left, each target that comes back is a UFO, worth 2 points more and flying 2
pixels a step faster, and one shot down crashes rather than bleeds <Cite gml="objects/oBatTarget/create.gml:10-14" /> <Cite gml="objects/oBatTarget/collision_oArrow.gml:9-10" />.
Every target also sways sideways as it flies below the top of its path, up to 32 pixels to the left, toward the player's ledges <Cite gml="objects/oBatTarget/step.gml:18-22" />.
A shot target's replacement still comes in at its own place, but a stomped one's comes in where the target
had swayed to: it isn't at the middle or far target's place, so it's worth what the nearest is, 3 as a UFO,
and sways from there ([[moon-room-stomped-targets]]) <Cite gml="objects/oBatTarget/collision_oArrow.gml:7" />
<Cite gml="objects/oBatTarget/collision_oCharacter.gml:13" /> <Cite gml="objects/oBatTarget/create.gml:5-14" /> <Cite quirk="1" row="A target stomped in the Moon Room's last 20 seconds comes back where it had swayed to, worth less." />.
In the clip, with 3 seconds left, the player lands on three targets in a row, and each comes back as a UFO
worth 3 above the spot where it was stomped <Cite clip="quirks/moon-room-stomped-targets" />.

<Clip id="quirks/moon-room-stomped-targets" />


When the timer runs out, the score is kept as the room's best in the high scores, up to 99; the targets
vanish in puffs of smoke, and the entrance becomes a door back to the high scores room
<Cite gml="objects/oMoonRoom/alarm_11.gml:2-26" />. FINISHED! comes up, then 30 steps later YOU SET A NEW
RECORD! if the score beat the room's best, or BETTER LUCK NEXT TIME..., and the music stops
<Cite gml="objects/oMoonRoom/alarm_10.gml:1-9" /> <Cite gml="objects/oGame/draw.gml:76-98" />. The door
leads out to the high scores room, in front of the Moon Room's door <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/other_animation_end.gml:96-109" /> <Cite gml="objects/oHighscores/create.gml:109-113" />.
A death before then ends the challenge at once, with the same score kept
<Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:1903-1908" />. In the clip, the player downs a UFO every 2 seconds, 3
points each, until the timer runs out at 35 points; he walks off the ledge onto the new door and goes back to
the high scores room <Cite clip="moon-room/ufos" /> <Cite replay="fidelity/replays/bonus-moon-archery.txt" />.
