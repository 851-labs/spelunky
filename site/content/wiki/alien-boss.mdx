The Alien Boss sits in the last room of the [[mothership|mothership]], the alien craft some levels of
[[ice-caves|the Ice Caves]] hold. It never moves; while the player is near, it sends a psychic wave that
drifts after him and knocks him down. It has <Stat id="alien-boss.hp" />, and, unlike most monsters, the
[[whip|Whip]] hurts it on every step it touches it, so it doesn't last long against one. When it dies, it
leaves <Stat id="alien-boss.gem-drops" /> big gems and brings down the craft's barriers.

## Behaviour

The Alien Boss doesn't move on its own: it only falls, if nothing is under it
<Cite gml="objects/oAlienBoss/step.gml:43-58" />.

<Clip id="alien-boss/psychic" />

When the player is within <Stat id="alien-boss.psychic-range" /> of it, and isn't dead, stunned or
invincible, it sends a psychic wave from its middle, with a sound and six sparks drawn in toward it, then
waits <Stat id="alien-boss.psychic-cooldown" /> before it can send another, so its waves come 101 steps
(about 3.4 seconds) apart at the soonest <Cite gml="objects/oAlienBoss/step.gml:63-75" />
<Cite gml="objects/oPsychicCreate/create.gml:7" />. It starts with that wait too, so the first wave comes
101 steps after it's made at the earliest <Cite gml="objects/oAlienBoss/create.gml:29" />. The wave drifts
toward the player at 2 pixels per step (60 per second), turning to follow him, until its animation ends,
about 2.3 seconds later <Cite gml="objects/oPsychicWave/step.gml:1-3" />
<Cite gml="objects/oPsychicWave/other_animation_end.gml:1" />. It passes through walls. It hurts any other
monster it touches, but not an Alien Boss, for 3 hit points <Cite gml="objects/oPsychicWave/collision_oEnemy.gml:1-7" />.
The clip shows the Alien Boss sending a wave at the player, who loses a heart, and another about three
seconds later.

The Alien Boss only acts while it's on screen or just off it <Cite gml="objects/oAlienBoss/step.gml:1-3" />.

## Defeating

<Clip id="alien-boss/whip" />

The Alien Boss isn't stunned by hits, and the Whip and the Machete hurt it through the code most monsters
share, which has no pause between hits: they take their damage on every step they touch it
([[whip-every-step]]) <Cite gml="objects/oEnemy/collision_oWhip.gml:1-8" />. The clip shows the player whipping it: each crack
took 7 hit points, and the second killed it <Cite clip="alien-boss/whip" />.

| [[machete\|Machete]] or [[mattock\|Mattock]] | <Stat id="machete.damage" /> each step the slash or the hit touches it | <Cite gml="objects/oEnemy/collision_oWhip.gml:1-8" /> |

Its step doesn't run the one most monsters share, so [[spikes|Spikes]], [[lava|Lava]],
[[spear-trap|Spear Traps]] and water don't affect it <Cite gml="objects/oEnemy/step.gml:34-128" />.

<Clip id="alien-boss/bomb" />

When its hit points run out, the Alien Boss dies where it is and flings out
<Stat id="alien-boss.gem-drops" /> big gems, each a [[big-emerald|Big Emerald]],
[[big-sapphire|Big Sapphire]] or [[big-ruby|Big Ruby]] at random <Cite gml="objects/oAlienBoss/step.gml:9-25" />.
When its dying animation ends, every barrier emitter in the level is destroyed, and the barriers with them
<Cite gml="objects/oAlienBoss/other_animation_end.gml:1-5" /> <Cite gml="objects/oBarrierEmitter/destroy.gml:8-11" />.
Its body stays, and can't be picked up <Cite gml="objects/oAlienBoss/create.gml:17" />. The clip shows the
player dropping a Bomb beside it and running out of its range before it can send a wave; the explosion
kills it. An Alien Boss killed counts toward the player's kills, and the screen between levels draws its
icon <Cite gml="objects/oAlienBoss/step.gml:26-31" /> <Cite gml="objects/oTransition/alarm_0.gml:215-219" />.

## Locations

Level generation makes the Alien Boss only in the [[mothership|mothership]]. An [[ice-caves|Ice Caves]]
level after the Moai's has a 1 in 10 chance of holding the alien craft: a row of rooms from a random one to
the level's right edge <Cite gml="scripts/scrLevelGen.gml:222-233" />
<Cite gml="scripts/scrClearGlobals.gml:59" /> <Cite quirk="1" />. The last of them, at the edge, is the only
room with the Alien Boss, beside a barrier emitter and a free [[jetpack|Jetpack]] set in a block <Cite
gml="scripts/scrRoomGen3.gml:175-180" /> <Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGen3.gml:461-464" /> <Cite
gml="scripts/scrRoomGen3.gml:472-478" />.

In custom levels, it's the [[level-editor|level editor]]'s tile `E`
<Cite gml="scripts/scrCreateTileObj.gml:213-216" />.

## Tips

- It only sends a wave while the player is within <Stat id="alien-boss.psychic-range" />, so staying
  further away is safe, and walls don't stop its waves.
- A stunned or invincible player draws no wave, and a wave can't hit a stunned one.
- Its waves are 101 steps apart at the soonest: right after one is the time to get close.
- The Whip does more against it than against most monsters: two cracks are enough.
