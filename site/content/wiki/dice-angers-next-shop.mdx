A [[dice-house|Dice House]]'s die mustn't leave the shop: carried out, it angers a [[shopkeeper|Shopkeeper]]. But
the die's check is only whether it's in a shop's room, any shop's, and the Shopkeeper it angers is the nearest one,
any shop's. On an ordinary level the two are the Dice House's. In the [[black-market|Black Market]], whose shops
stand side by side, a die can pass through the next shop unnoticed and anger that shop's Shopkeeper when it leaves.

## What happens

In the Black Market, the Dice House's die is carried left through the [[clothing-shop|Clothing Shop]], a shop's
room, so no one minds. Carried on out of it, the die angers the nearest Shopkeeper: the Clothing Shop's runs out
after the player, while the Dice House's stays where he is <Cite clip="quirks/market-die-angers-next-shop" />.

<Clip id="quirks/market-die-angers-next-shop" />

## Why

On every step a die is near the screen on a level, its step checks whether it's in a shop:

```gml objects/oDice/step.gml:10-17
    // stealing makes shopkeeper angry
    if (isLevel())
    {
        if (not isInShop(x, y))
        {
            scrShopkeeperAnger(0);
        }
    }
```

`isInShop` is true in any room of the level that's a shop's or a Dice House's, not only the die's own
<Cite gml="scripts/isInShop.gml:7-13" />. And the anger falls on the nearest Shopkeeper to the die, whoever's he
is:

```gml scripts/scrShopkeeperAnger.gml:7-12
shp = instance_nearest(x, y, oShopkeeper);
if (shp)
{
    if (not shp.dead and not shp.angered)
    {
        shp.status = 2;
```

On an ordinary level there's one shop, so the two are the Dice House's room and Shopkeeper, unless the die is
nearer the Shopkeeper who waits by the exit for a thief or a murderer. The Black Market's Dice House opens on its
left into an item shop, and that one onto the way down <Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGenMarket.gml:117-146" />. A die
carried into the item shop is still in a shop's room, and carried on out of it, it's nearest that shop's
Shopkeeper <Cite quirk="1" />.

## Reproducing it

In any Black Market, pick up the Dice House's die and walk left through the shop beside it and out of it: the
market's rooms are always laid out the same way <Cite gml="scripts/scrLevelGen.gml:44-69" />. The replay, with seed 9, does it in the market on level 6: the player picks up a die in Tarn's Dice House
and carries it left through Wang's Clothing Shop, where nothing happens. Out in the next room, "COME BACK HERE,
THIEF!": the Clothing Shop's Shopkeeper runs out after him and knocks him down, and the Dice House's is never
angered <Cite replay="fidelity/replays/shop-seed-9-market-dice-carried-out.txt" />.

## In the port

The spelunky.ai port reproduces it exactly: it's in docs/quirks.md's list of the 1.1 source's bugs
<Cite quirk="1" />, and the oracle's fingerprint of the replay pins which Shopkeeper is angered. No option turns it
off.
