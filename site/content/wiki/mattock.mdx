Held in the player's hands, it's swung like the [[whip|Whip]], but only on the ground: its hit in front of
him breaks a block of rock, and hurts monsters for <Stat id="mattock.damage" /> a step, as does its wind-up
behind him. Every block it breaks may break it. [[general-store|General Stores]], [[rare-shop|Rare Shops]]
and the [[dice-house|Dice House]] sell it, and [[crate|Crates]], buried blocks and the bottom of every
[[snake-pit|Snake Pit]] give it away.

## Usage

<Clip id="mattock/dig" />

The Mattock is picked up like any item, crouching over it and pressing the attack key. While it's held, the
attack key swings it, only while the player stands on the ground, and he can't jump for 20 steps after; crouching,
the key puts it down in front of him instead <Cite gml="scripts/scrUseItem.gml:168-215" />. It isn't thrown.
A swing makes a wind-up behind the player and a hit 16 pixels in front of him, once the swing's animation is
past its fifth frame <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:415-431" /> <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:497-509" />.

### Digging

When the hit's animation ends, it breaks the block at its middle, or if there's none, the one 9 pixels
lower: the block in front of the player's feet. A block that can't be destroyed, such as the level's border,
stops it. It also makes every treasure in the level active again, so treasure the block held up falls,
and breaks every [[spikes|Spike]] with nothing under it <Cite gml="objects/oMattockHit/other_animation_end.gml:1-36" />. The clip shows a wall two blocks
thick dug through with two swings <Cite clip="mattock/dig" />.

Which block breaks is chosen with the hit's whole shape, not only the point the code tests, and of the
blocks that shape touches it takes the first one made <Cite gml="objects/oMattockHit/other_animation_end.gml:7-11" /> <Cite quirk="1" />.
Facing right, the shape reaches 8 pixels back toward the player, so when he stands over the left half of a
block, it can be the block he stands on: in the second clip, a swing to the right breaks the floor under
him, and he drops into the hole <Cite clip="mattock/wind-up" />. Facing left, it reaches 3 pixels past the
point, so over the first 3 pixels of a block it can be the block beyond the one in front of him, which
stays <Cite replay="fidelity/replays/weapon-mattock-wrong-block.txt" />.

Each block it breaks has a 1 in <Stat id="mattock.break-chance" bare /> chance of breaking the Mattock: its
head flies off, its handle is gone from the player's hands, and the sound of it breaking plays; otherwise the
sound is a crunch <Cite gml="objects/oMattockHit/other_animation_end.gml:38-56" /> <Cite replay="fidelity/replays/item-seed-35-mattock-break.txt" />. The head is an item of its own, which can
be picked up and thrown.

## Damage

The hit and the wind-up each take <Stat id="mattock.damage" /> on every step they touch a
monster on the code most monsters share <Cite gml="objects/oMattockHit/create.gml:3-6" /> <Cite gml="objects/oMattockPre/create.gml:3-4" /> <Cite gml="objects/oEnemy/collision_oWhip.gml:1-8" />.
The hit punctures, as the [[machete|Machete]]'s slash does, but the code that treats the Machete apart
tests for its name, which the Mattock hasn't got: to a [[caveman|Caveman]], a Hawkman or a Vampire the
Mattock is a harder Whip, nothing while it's stunned.

The wind-up is made twice in a swing. The code that makes it checks that no Machete's wind-up exists rather
than a Mattock's, so on the swing's first two steps it makes one each
time <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:497-509" /> <Cite quirk="1" />. Each lasts 3 steps, and where they overlap a monster
behind the player takes 4 a step: the clip's [[yeti-king|Yeti King]] loses 12 of its 30 hit points to one
swing, 2, 4, 4 and 2 <Cite clip="mattock/wind-up" />.

## Interactions

- **[[shopkeeper|Shopkeepers]].** The hit and the wind-up don't hurt one, but make him
  attack <Cite gml="objects/oShopkeeper/collision_oWhip.gml:1-19" />.
- **Treasure.** Every block it breaks sets every treasure in the level active again, which changes
  nothing: treasure never stops, as the treasure's own code that would stop it sets `status` where the rest
  of its code reads `state` <Cite gml="objects/oMattockHit/other_animation_end.gml:25-28" />
  <Cite gml="objects/oTreasure/step.gml:41-48" />.
- **The level's edge.** The border's blocks can't be broken, and a swing at one does nothing and can't
  break the Mattock <Cite gml="objects/oMattockHit/other_animation_end.gml:2-13" />.

## Tips

- With the player's back to it, one swing took 12 of the Yeti King's hit points in the clip, where the
  Whip's wind-up takes 3: the Mattock's double wind-up is its strongest attack on the Yeti King, the Tomb
  Lord and the Magma Man.
- Facing right, a swing can break the floor under the player when he stands over the left half of a block,
  so digging sideways over a drop is risky.

## Trivia

- The Tunnel Man, one of the characters, swings a Mattock's hit with empty hands, and his never
  breaks <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:432-446" /> <Cite gml="objects/oMattockHit/other_animation_end.gml:39" />.
- The blocks it breaks leave their decorations behind, as GameMaker 8.0's `tile_layer_find` never finds
  the tiles the game looks for <Cite gml="objects/oMattockHit/other_animation_end.gml:15-20" /> <Cite quirk="5" />.
