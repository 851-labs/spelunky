Stealing from a shop, or bombing it, angers the [[shopkeeper|Shopkeeper]] through one script, which shouts and
marks the player a thief, so that a Shopkeeper waits at the next level's exit. Hitting him angers him too, but
none of the ways to hit him calls that script. And an angry Shopkeeper's first step gives every ware away, so a
player who hits him can carry the wares out free, with no theft. It may be by design.

## What happens

The player buys a [[pistol|Pistol]] and shoots the Shopkeeper beside him, who falls stunned and drops his shotgun.
He gets up angry, grabs the player and throws him against the floor, which costs a heart. The player then
collects the shop's [[bomb-bag|bomb bag]], free, and no shout calls him a thief <Cite clip="shopkeeper/throw" />.

<Clip id="shopkeeper/throw" />

## Why

A crack of the [[whip|Whip]] (or a Machete's slash) sets the Shopkeeper's `status` to `ATTACK` at once, and so do
a thrown item's hit and a Piranha's bite; a bullet stuns him, and he gets up in `ATTACK` <Cite
gml="objects/oShopkeeper/collision_oWhip.gml:1-19" /> <Cite gml="objects/oItem/step.gml:289-299" /> <Cite
gml="objects/oPiranha/step.gml:107-118" /> <Cite gml="objects/oBullet/collision_oEnemy.gml:14-25" /> <Cite
gml="objects/oShopkeeper/step.gml:465-470" />. None of them calls `scrShopkeeperAnger`, the script
that shouts and adds to the thief count <Cite gml="scripts/scrShopkeeperAnger.gml:7-22" />. But the first step of
his attack frees every ware the game is running:

```gml objects/oShopkeeper/step.gml:337-349
else if (status == ATTACK)
{
    image_speed = 1;
    
    if (not angered)
    {
        with oItem
        {
            cost = 0;
            forSale = false;
        }
        angered = true;
    }
```

A free ware isn't for sale, so carrying it out isn't a theft: the count stays at 0, so no Shopkeeper waits at the
next level's exit, and its shops greet the player as before <Cite quirk="1" />.

## Reproducing it

Hit a Shopkeeper, with the Whip, a thrown item or a shot, then take a ware. It happens in normal play. In the
replay of the clip, the player buys a Pistol and shoots the Shopkeeper (frame 1490). He gets up in his attack
(1545), which frees the bomb bag (1546), and the player collects it (1598): the thief count stays at 0 throughout
<Cite replay="fidelity/replays/weapon-seed-39-pistol.txt" />. Whipping one does the same: his attack frees the
wares on the next step, with no shout and no thief <Cite replay="fidelity/replays/shop-seed-11-whip.txt" />.

## In the port

The spelunky.ai port reproduces it exactly: it's in docs/quirks.md's list of the 1.1 source's bugs
<Cite quirk="1" />, and the oracle's fingerprint of the replay pins the free bag and the count. No option turns it
off.
