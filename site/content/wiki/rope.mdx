The player carries ropes as a count, shown at the top of the screen beside his bombs, rather than in his
hands, and the rope key uses one <Cite gml="scripts/scrDrawHUD.gml:40" />. Thrown up, a rope catches where it
stops rising and unrolls down to the ground; dropped at a ledge, it hangs over the edge. Either way he climbs
it as he would a ladder. More come in [[rope-pile|Rope Piles]], three at a time.

## Usage

### Throwing it up

<Clip id="rope/climb" />

The rope key throws a rope straight up from the player at <Stat id="rope.throw-speed" />
(<Stat id="rope.throw-speed" as="per-second" />), unless there's a ceiling right above him, when nothing
happens and no rope is spent <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:1045-1050" />
<Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:1119-1129" />. It rises as any thrown item does, slowing by 0.6 pixels a
step, so in the open it climbs about 126 pixels, nearly eight blocks (12 + 11.4 + … + 0.6)
<Cite gml="objects/oItem/step.gml:71-85" />; on the way up, a ceiling it hits turns it back
<Cite gml="objects/oItem/step.gml:153-158" />. On the step it stops rising, it catches: it moves to the middle
of the block-wide column it's in, away from a wall, puts a hook at the top, and unrolls down 8 pixels a step,
a segment each step, until it reaches the ground or has <Stat id="rope.segments" /> segments, 128 pixels
<Cite gml="objects/oRopeThrow/step.gml:1-36" />. In the clip, the player throws a rope up from the floor,
jumps onto it and climbs <Cite clip="rope/climb" />.

A rope flying up faster than 2 pixels a step that touches a monster hurts it, as any thrown item does
<Cite gml="objects/oItem/step.gml:256-350" />.

### Dropping it down a ledge

<Clip id="rope/ledge" />

Holding down, the rope key hangs a rope a block ahead of the player instead, moved half a block to the side
where it finds open air below; it catches at once and unrolls down. With no open air to hang it in, as on flat
floor or against a wall, nothing happens and no rope is spent
<Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:1053-1118" />. In the clip, the player first presses the rope key under a
low ceiling, which does nothing, then holds down at the edge of a ledge and drops a rope down it
<Cite clip="rope/ledge" /> <Cite replay="fidelity/replays/action-rope-tunnel.txt" />.

### Climbing

To the code a rope is a kind of ladder: pressing up at one, standing or in the air, grabs it, and the player
climbs up and down it as he would a ladder <Cite gml="scripts/characterStepEvent.gml:606-630" />.

### In the hands

The item key takes a [[bomb|Bomb]] into the player's hands, and pressing it again swaps the bomb for a rope; a
third press puts the rope back and returns what he held before
<Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:954-1043" />. Holding one, the attack key throws it up or, with down,
drops it down a ledge, as the rope key does; dropped where there's no room, it's tossed a little way along the
floor instead, a loose rope to pick up again. Either way, what he held before comes back to his hands
<Cite gml="scripts/scrUseItem.gml:36-119" />. A rope in his hands when he takes an exit goes back into his
count <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:835-841" />.

## Locations

- **The player's own.** A new game starts him with <Stat id="rope.start" />, the Tunnel Man with none
  <Cite gml="scripts/scrClearGlobals.gml:103-114" />, and the [[tutorial|Tutorial]] with 2
  <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/other_animation_end.gml:87-94" />. He can carry <Stat id="rope.max" /> at most
  <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:11" />.
- **[[rope-pile|Rope Piles]]**, three ropes each, picked up by touch: in shops, in [[crate|Crates]], and left
  by the [[yeti-king|Yeti King]] <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:2052-2066" />.
- **[[monkey|Monkeys]]**: one clinging to the player sometimes takes a rope and throws it out, loose, where he
  can pick it up again <Cite gml="objects/oMonkey/step.gml:244-255" />.
- **The title screen** has one hanging <Cite room="rTitle" />.
- **Custom levels** start the player with the rope count the [[level-editor|level editor]] gives them, 99 at
  most <Cite gml="scripts/scrLoadLevel.gml:146-148" />.

## Interactions

<Clip id="rope/burn" />

- **Lava.** A segment hanging in lava catches fire. The fire climbs the rope a pixel a step, and each segment
  it reaches burns away; a player climbing the rope falls when his segment goes
  <Cite gml="objects/oRope/step.gml:1-17" /> <Cite gml="objects/oRopeBurn/step.gml:1-15" />. In the clip, a
  rope dropped over a Temple lava pool catches fire at its foot, and the player hanging from it drops off as
  the fire reaches him <Cite clip="rope/burn" />.
- **[[bomb|Bombs]].** An explosion knocks away a rope that's in flight or lying loose, as it does any item,
  but not one that's unrolling, and it leaves a hung rope alone, even when the blocks it hangs from are blown
  away <Cite gml="objects/oExplosion/collision_oItem.gml:20-29" />.
- **[[web|Webs]]** don't catch a rope, where they stop other items
  <Cite gml="objects/oWeb/collision_oItem.gml:1-5" />.
- **Leaving the screen.** Unlike other items, a thrown rope doesn't freeze just past the edge of the screen
  <Cite gml="objects/oItem/step.gml:1-3" /> <Cite quirk="1" />.

## Tips

- A rope thrown up in the open climbs about 126 pixels and unrolls 128 at most, so it hangs down to about
  where it was thrown from.
- The rope key wastes nothing when it can't be used: under a low ceiling, on flat floor or against a wall, the
  rope stays in the count.
