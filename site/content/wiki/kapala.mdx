Wearing it, the player heals by fighting: the drops of blood that fly when something is hurt or killed
fill it as he touches them, and every ninth gives him a heart. Kali gives it as her second gift, at one of
her altars, and nothing else in the game makes one. It's worn from the moment it's picked up until the game
ends.

## Effect

<Clip id="kapala/heart" />

Picking it up puts it on: the message "YOU GOT THE KAPALA! IT THIRSTS FOR BLOOD..." shows, the Kapala
disappears, and an icon of it joins the row under the hearts <Cite gml="scripts/scrStealItem.gml:57-67" />.
The icon shows how full it is, in five stages from empty to nearly full
<Cite gml="scripts/scrDrawHUD.gml:116-124" />. It stays on until a new game starts, which clears it and
empties it <Cite gml="scripts/scrClearGlobals.gml:49" /> <Cite gml="scripts/scrClearGlobals.gml:87" />
<Cite gml="objects/oTransition/create.gml:20-24" />.

Each drop of blood the player touches while wearing it goes into it: the drop vanishes in a spark, the
Kapala fills by one, and the player flashes red. On the ninth drop it empties and gives him
<Stat id="kapala.heal" />, with a heart over his head and a kiss's sound
<Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/collision_oBlood.gml:1-17" /> <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:20-27" />.
There's no limit but the game's own: hearts stop at 99 <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:9" />.

A drop can only be collected once it's 5 steps old <Cite gml="objects/oBlood/create.gml:9-13" />
<Cite gml="objects/oBlood/alarm_2.gml:1" />. Blood flies when a monster or the player is hurt or killed,
unless the monster is bloodless, and only while there are fewer than 16 drops and other bits of debris
about <Cite gml="scripts/scrCreateBlood.gml:24-32" />. Any drop counts, the player's own too <Cite quirk="1" />.
The clip shows Kali's gift appearing on her altar and the player picking it up; spiders come out of her
statue when a laser breaks the altar, and as he whips them, the drops he touches fill the Kapala until the
ninth turns into a heart, taking him from 3 hearts to 4 <Cite clip="kapala/heart" />.

## Obtaining

| Source | Chance | |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Kali's second gift | Given once the player's favour with her reaches 16 at an altar, if she hasn't given it already | <Cite gml="scripts/scrGetFavorMsg.gml:52-66" /> |

Kali's altars can come in every area but [[olmecs-lair|Olmec's Lair]]. The player gains her favour by
putting a body on the altar: a Damsel's gives 8, a monster's what it's worth to her, a stunned one more than
a dead one <Cite quirk="1" />. At 8 she gives her first gift, a piece of equipment, and at 16 the Kapala,
which appears on the altar's right half, free to take <Cite gml="scripts/scrGetFavorMsg.gml:52-110" />. A
favour that jumps straight past 8 to 16 gets the Kapala and never the first gift. She gives it once a game.
No shop sells it, no [[crate|Crate]] or block holds one, and the
[[level-editor|level editor]] has no tile for it.

## Interactions

- Blood from the player's own wounds counts like anyone's, so the Kapala gives back some of what a fall onto
  [[spikes|Spikes]] or a monster's bite costs, if he touches the drops <Cite quirk="1" />.
- A drop that falls faster than 6 pixels a step vanishes, so blood that falls far is lost
  <Cite gml="objects/oBlood/step.gml:3" />.

## Tips

- Drops fly up and out from where the blood came from, then fall, so a kill close to the player is the
  easiest blood to catch <Cite gml="objects/oBlood/create.gml:4-6" />.
- Blood that falls far vanishes, so blood spilt above the player is worth catching before it drops past
  him.
