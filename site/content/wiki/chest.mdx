Opened, it spills out a handful of gems, worth from <Stat id="emerald.value" /> each on the Mines' levels,
and sometimes a big one; but now and then it's a trap that throws out a lit [[bomb|Bomb]]. It's heavy, like
a [[crate|Crate]], and stays where it is, open, once it's been opened.

## Usage

<Clip id="chest/open" />

Pressing up and the attack key while the player stands at a closed Chest opens it: its lid opens, and it
stays, open, for good <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:561-567" />. What comes out is thrown up at 2
pixels a step and up to 3 to either side <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:568-605" />:

| Contents | Chance | |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A lit [[bomb\|Bomb]], which explodes 40 steps (1.33 seconds) later, and nothing else | 1 in 12, except on the [[tutorial\|Tutorial]] | <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:568-578" /> |
| <Stat id="chest.gems" /> small gems, each an [[emerald\|Emerald]], a [[sapphire\|Sapphire]] or a [[ruby\|Ruby]], one chance in three | Otherwise | <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:580-593" /> |
| And a big gem: a [[big-emerald\|Big Emerald]], a [[big-sapphire\|Big Sapphire]] or a [[big-ruby\|Big Ruby]], one chance in three | 1 in 4, with the small gems | <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:594-605" /> |

The bomb's test is that the level number is above 0, and the Tutorial is level
0 <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/other_animation_end.gml:87-94" />. In the clip, a Chest opens into four gems,
an emerald, a sapphire and two rubies, which the player walks over and collects <Cite clip="chest/open" />.

A Chest is heavy: the player carries it higher than other items, and throws it at 4 pixels a step plus his
own speed, rising at 2, or at 4 upward <Cite gml="objects/oItem/step.gml:44-56" />
<Cite gml="scripts/scrUseItem.gml:609-635" />. Thrown faster than 2 pixels a step, it hurts a monster as any
thrown item does <Cite gml="objects/oItem/step.gml:256-350" />.

## Locations

- **Level generation**, in every area: in a nook one block high with a wall beside it, a 1 in 15 chance,
  once the rolls for a web, the Udjat Eye's locked chest (on the level that has one) and a
  [[crate|Crate]]'s 1 in 10 fail <Cite gml="scripts/scrTreasureGen.gml:47-72" />.
- **[[city-of-gold|The City of Gold]]**, a Temple level: its treasure vaults, side rooms no other level
  has, have tiles that are a Chest or a Crate, one chance in two each
  <Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGen4.gml:70-74" /> <Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGen4.gml:359-363" />.
- **[[olmecs-lair|Olmec's Lair]]**: a 1 in 15 chance on some of its tiles, the first of their
  rolls <Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGen5.gml:177-179" />.
- **The [[tutorial|Tutorial]]**, which has one <Cite room="rTutorial" />.
- **The [[level-editor|level editor]]**'s `c` tile <Cite gml="scripts/scrCreateTileObj.gml:285-288" />.

## Interactions

- **[[bomb|Bombs]].** An explosion throws a Chest away from it without breaking or opening
  it <Cite gml="objects/oExplosion/collision_oItem.gml:30-36" />.
- **Shopkeepers.** A trap's lit bomb doesn't anger a [[shopkeeper|Shopkeeper]] it's near, where one the
  player lights does: the trap lights it without marking it armed, and only an armed bomb angers him,
  though one the player is stunned or killed holding is armed as he drops
  it <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:570-577" /> <Cite gml="objects/oBomb/step.gml:6-17" /> <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:1714-1720" /> <Cite quirk="1" />.
- **Exits.** The player sets down a heavy item, a Chest among them, when he takes an exit, so a Chest can't
  be carried to the next level <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:815-820" />. A trap's lit bomb carried out of
  the exit, not being armed, goes back into his bombs, where one he lit is left
  behind <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:821-831" /> <Cite quirk="1" /> <Cite replay="fidelity/replays/item-chest-trap-bomb-unarmed.txt" />.

## Tips

- A Chest that turns out to be a trap gives 40 steps to get clear of its bomb, which is thrown up out of it.
- A Chest can be carried before it's opened, so its gems, or its bomb, come out where the player sets it
  down.

## Trivia

- The lifetime count of chests opened goes up on every press of up and the attack key at a Chest, before
  the code checks that it's closed, so an open Chest counts again each time. It's only written to the
  game's statistics file: the code that would show it is switched
  off <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:561-565" /> <Cite gml="objects/oGame/draw.gml:137-155" /> <Cite quirk="1" />.
  Pressed three times, one Chest counts three <Cite replay="fidelity/replays/item-seed-13-chest-tally.txt" />.
- A trap Chest plays the sound of a trap rather than the Chest's own <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:578-581" />.
