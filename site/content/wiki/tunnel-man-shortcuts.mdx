There are three shortcuts, and the [[tunnel-man|Tunnel Man]] digs them in turn: to the [[jungle|Jungle]]'s
level 5, the [[ice-caves|Ice Caves]]' level 9 and the [[temple|Temple]]'s level 13. He asks for each one's price
in the screen between levels at the end of the area before it, a donation at a time, and remembers what he's
owed from game to game. A paid shortcut is a door in the Shortcut House, a gallery above the title screen,
that starts a new game on its level.

## How it works

### What he's owed

The game keeps two sums the Tunnel Man is still owed, in two entries of the high score table, `TUNNEL1` and
`TUNNEL2`: it reads them when it starts, and writes them back with the scores when a game ends in a death or
a win <Cite gml="objects/oGlobals/create.gml:34-39" /> <Cite gml="scripts/scrUpdateHighscores.gml:97-99" />
<Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:1914-1915" /> <Cite gml="objects/oCredits2/step.gml:50" />. They
decide which shortcut he's digging and where he waits <Cite gml="objects/oTransition/create.gml:47-61" />:

1. **The level 5 shortcut.** A new table owes $1 more than its price, and $1 more than the level 9 one's
   too <Cite gml="scripts/scrResetHighscores.gml:32-36" />. While it's owed, he waits in the screen after
   level 4, the last of [[mines|the Mines]].
2. **The level 9 shortcut**, once the first is paid: he waits in the screen after level 8.
3. **The level 13 shortcut.** Paying off the level 9 one sets what's owed on this one to $1 more than its
   price <Cite gml="objects/oTunnelMan/step.gml:18-33" />. He waits in the screen after level 12.

Once all three are paid, he waits nowhere. In each screen, he only appears once the sum is down to the
price: the first time it's reached, the game takes the extra $1 off and shows no Tunnel Man, so he's there
from the next visit on ([[tunnel-man-skips-first-visit]]) <Cite gml="objects/oTransition/create.gml:47-61" /> <Cite quirk="1" row="The Tunnel Man skips his first visit." />.

### Donating

He stops the player who walks up to him, introduces himself and asks for what he's still owed, and the
screen can't be skipped until he's had an answer <Cite gml="objects/oTransition/step.gml:1-11" />. Each press
of up adds <Stat id="tunnel-man-shortcuts.press" /> to the donation and each press of down takes it off
<Cite gml="objects/oTransition/step.gml:35-67" />. Held for <Stat id="tunnel-man-shortcuts.hold-delay" />,
either key changes it by <Stat id="tunnel-man-shortcuts.hold-step" /> a step, and after
<Stat id="tunnel-man-shortcuts.fast-after" /> by <Stat id="tunnel-man-shortcuts.fast-step" /> a step. It never
goes above the player's money or what's owed, nor below $0 <Cite gml="objects/oTunnelMan/step.gml:43-83" />.
The action key gives it: the money is taken off the player's at once, and off what's owed
<Cite gml="objects/oTunnelMan/step.gml:1-41" />. His answers are on [[tunnel-man#behaviour|his page]].

### The Shortcut House

The Shortcut House is a gallery above the title screen's doors, which greets the player with "WELCOME TO THE
SHORTCUT HOUSE!" <Cite gml="objects/oTitle/draw.gml:17-29" />. Until the level 5 shortcut is paid, two
bricks wall it off and no ladder leads up <Cite gml="objects/oTitle/create.gml:48-52" />
<Cite replay="fidelity/replays/title-locked.txt" />. Then a ladder leads up to it, and its doors are:

| Door | There once | |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Level 5, the [[jungle\|Jungle]] | The level 5 shortcut is paid (the room editor placed it, behind the wall) | <Cite room="rTitle" /> <Cite gml="objects/oTitle/create.gml:27-35" /> |
| Level 9, the [[ice-caves\|Ice Caves]] | The level 9 shortcut is paid | <Cite gml="objects/oTitle/create.gml:37-40" /> |
| Level 13, the [[temple\|Temple]] | All three are paid | <Cite gml="objects/oTitle/create.gml:41-45" /> |

The Tunnel Man himself stands at the house's left end, until the player earns the door that switches to
him ([[tunnel-man#playing-as-him]]) <Cite gml="objects/oTitle/create.gml:54-62" />.

### The level editor

The same sums decide what the [[level-editor|level editor]] offers: it starts with the Mines' tiles, and each
paid shortcut adds its area's blocks, monsters and traps, the Jungle's with the level 5 shortcut, the Ice
Caves' with level 9's and the Temple's with level 13's <Cite
gml="objects/oLevelEditor/keypress_1.gml:5-8" /> <Cite gml="objects/oLevelEditor/keypress_2.gml:5-8" /> <Cite
gml="objects/oLevelEditor/keypress_3.gml:5-8" /> <Cite gml="objects/oLevelEditor/create.gml:42-45" />
([[level-editor#placing-tiles]]).

### A shortcut game

Going through a door starts a new game on its level <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/other_animation_end.gml:137-157" />.
It starts with what any game does, <Stat id="spelunker.hearts" />, <Stat id="spelunker.bombs" />,
<Stat id="spelunker.ropes" /> and no money (fewer as the Tunnel Man)
<Cite gml="scripts/scrClearGlobals.gml:103-118" />, and differs from one started at the START door in a few
ways:

- **No best time.** It's marked as a shortcut game, and a win so marked doesn't set the best time; its
  money, kills and saves count as any game's ([[scoring#the-high-scores]], [[speedrunning-basics]])
  <Cite gml="scripts/scrUpdateHighscores.gml:46-76" />. The START door clears the mark
  <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/other_animation_end.gml:72-74" />.
- **Its first level is never dark or flooded.** As the first level after the title screen, it's never a
  [[dark-level|dark level]] <Cite quirk="1" row="A dark level needs the previous level's clock" />, and level 5 is never a [[flooded-level|flooded level]], which
  only the screen between levels rolls for <Cite gml="objects/oTransition/step.gml:17-24" />.
- **The levels it skips make nothing.** Some things are made once a game on a few early levels
  ([[level-generation#once-a-game]]). From level 5, the game has no [[udjat-eye|Udjat Eye]], whose chest is on
  level 2, 3 or 4; from level 9, no [[black-market|Black Market]] either, whose door is on level 5, 6 or 7
  <Cite gml="scripts/scrInitLevel.gml:178-192" />. From level 13, it has no [[moai|Moai]], the only place the
  [[hedjet|Hedjet]] is <Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGen3.gml:498" />, and without the Hedjet the gold door
  doesn't open to the [[sceptre|Sceptre]]: no [[city-of-gold|City of Gold]] <Cite gml="scripts/scrLevelGen.gml:212-221" />
  <Cite gml="objects/oGoldDoor/collision_oSceptre.gml:1-22" />. [[city-of-gold-route]] has the rest.

## Quirks

- **He skips his first visit** ([[tunnel-man-skips-first-visit]]). Each shortcut is first owed $1 more than its price, which the screen where
  he'd wait takes off instead of showing him <Cite quirk="1" row="The Tunnel Man skips his first visit." />
  <Cite replay="fidelity/replays/tunnelman-seed-9-jungle-over-max.txt" />.
- **Up raises the donation before he asks.** A press of up adds to it while the player walks up to him and
  while he introduces himself, so his request can start above $0 <Cite quirk="1" row="oTransition's step adds $100 to the Tunnel Man's donate on every press of up while he exists" />.
- **No dark level to start.** A shortcut game's first level is never dark, as no game's first level is
  <Cite quirk="1" row="A dark level needs the previous level's clock" />.
