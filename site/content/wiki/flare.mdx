Level generation puts a crate of Flares beside the entrance of every [[dark-level|dark level]] it makes, and the
level's opening message, "I'D BETTER USE THESE FLARES!", points the player to it. A Flare is a light he can carry: held,
thrown or lying on the ground, it lights a circle of the cave around it, and his own small light widens
beside it. Nothing burns it out, so one carried through the level's exit is still lit on the next level.

## Usage

<Clip id="flare/crate" />

### The crate

The crate of Flares is a box with a flame on its lid. Pressing up and the attack key while the player stands
over it opens it: it vanishes in a puff of smoke and <Stat id="flare.per-crate" /> lit Flares fly out of it,
each up at 1 to 3 pixels a step and across at up to 3 either way <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:649-669" />.
In the clip, level 2 of the [[mines|Mines]] loads dark with the crate glowing beside the entrance; the player
walks over to it and opens it, and the three Flares scatter, each with its own circle of light
<Cite clip="flare/crate" />. He can open the crate while he's carrying it, and his hands are empty after
<Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:661-665" />.

Unopened, the crate is a light of its own, as wide as a Flare's, and it throws off sparks every 2 steps
<Cite gml="objects/oScreen/step_begin.gml:183-186" /> <Cite gml="objects/oFlareCrate/alarm_0.gml:1-2" />.
It's heavy, like a [[crate|Crate]]: the player carries it higher than other items and throws it at 4 pixels a
step plus his own speed, rising at 2, or at 4 upward, and he sets it down when he takes an exit, so it stays
on its level <Cite gml="objects/oFlareCrate/create.gml:9" /> <Cite gml="scripts/scrUseItem.gml:609-635" />
<Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:815-820" />.

### The Flare

A Flare is picked up and thrown as any item is ([[throwing]]): crouching over it with empty hands, the attack
key lifts it, and the attack key throws it, at 8 pixels a step plus the player's speed, rising at 3
<Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:1203-1214" /> <Cite gml="scripts/scrUseItem.gml:605-646" />. It
flickers and throws off a spark every 2 steps, which drifts up and fades <Cite gml="objects/oFlare/create.gml:2" />
<Cite gml="objects/oFlare/alarm_0.gml:1-2" /> <Cite gml="objects/oFlareSpark/step.gml:1-6" />.

On a dark level, each Flare lights the dark within <Stat id="flare.light" /> of it, wherever it is
<Cite gml="objects/oScreen/step_begin.gml:179-182" />. The darkness itself, which sets how far the player's
own light reaches, is measured from the Flare nearest him: the nearer it is, the wider his light, up to
96 pixels when he holds it ([[dark-level#the-darkness]]) <Cite gml="objects/oLevel/step.gml:110-137" />.
Nothing counts down a Flare's life: its one timer makes its sparks, so it stays lit until water or lava ends
it <Cite gml="objects/oFlare/create.gml:14" /> <Cite gml="objects/oFlare/alarm_0.gml:1-2" />.

Thrown faster than 2 pixels a step into a monster, a Flare takes <Stat id="flare.damage" /> from the monster
nearest it and flies on, as a [[rock|Rock]] does; a Caveman, a Hawkman, a Mantrap, a Yeti or a Vampire is also
stunned <Cite gml="objects/oItem/step.gml:256-350" />. It can't hurt the player, whose code checks only for
Rocks and arrows <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:1513-1565" />.

Carried through a level's exit, a Flare is in the player's hands on the next level, as a Rock is: he's drawn
holding it on the screen between levels, and while he holds it the HUD shows it in the box for the held item <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:842-850" /> <Cite gml="scripts/scrHoldItem.gml:48" />
<Cite gml="objects/oPDummy/draw.gml:35" /> <Cite gml="scripts/scrDrawHUD.gml:95" />.

## Locations

- **[[dark-level|Dark levels]]**, in [[mines|the Mines]], [[jungle|the Jungle]] and [[temple|the Temple]]:
  one crate by the entrance of each that level generation makes dark (not one a defiled altar darkens later), on its left if there's no block there, else on its right, else on it
  <Cite gml="scripts/scrEntityGen.gml:527-545" />.
- **The title screen**, which has one of its own ([Trivia](#trivia)).

[[level-generation|Level generation]] makes no Flares anywhere else, and the [[level-editor|level editor]] has
no tile for one or for the crate.

## Interactions

- **Water**, of a pool or a [[flooded-level|flooded level]]. A Flare or a crate that touches it is put out:
  it's destroyed with a splash, the crate with a puff of smoke too, and if the player is holding it, his
  hands are empty <Cite gml="objects/oFlare/step.gml:6-20" /> <Cite gml="objects/oFlareCrate/step.gml:3-18" />.
- **[[lava|Lava]].** A Flare that touches lava stops and sinks, and is destroyed once it's under the surface
  <Cite gml="objects/oItem/step.gml:160-185" />.
- **[[bomb|Bombs]].** An explosion throws a Flare or the crate away from it without putting it out
  <Cite gml="objects/oExplosion/collision_oItem.gml:30-36" />.
- **The [[hedjet|Hedjet]].** Worn, it makes the darkness 0 whatever Flares there are, so the player's own light is
  always at its widest; Flares still light their own circles <Cite gml="objects/oLevel/step.gml:116-121" />
  <Cite gml="objects/oScreen/step_begin.gml:178-182" />.

## Tips

- Thrown ahead, a Flare lights what's coming: it lights a circle around itself wherever it lands, and keeps
  lighting it.
- A crate opened beside water can lose Flares to it: they fly out to either side, and water puts them
  out at once.
- The crate itself lights the dark as a Flare does, so carried unopened, it lights the way as well as one
  Flare, though heavy and thrown short.
