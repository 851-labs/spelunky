A Fish Bone is what's left of a [[piranha|Piranha]] or a [[dead-fish|Dead Fish]] that ends up out of the
water, which happens when the water drains away around it. It can be picked up and thrown like a
[[rock|Rock]], and hurts a monster as an [[arrow|arrow]] does, breaking as it hits.

## Usage

<Clip id="fish-bone/piranha" />

A Fish Bone is picked up and thrown as any item is: crouching over it with empty hands, the attack key lifts
it, and the attack key throws it, at 8 pixels a step plus the player's speed, rising at 3
<Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:1203-1214" /> <Cite gml="scripts/scrUseItem.gml:605-646" />. It moves
by the code all items share: its own lighter gravity, 0.2, lasts its first step, and then it falls 0.6
pixels a step faster each step, as items do, bouncing off walls and floors <Cite
gml="objects/oFishBone/create.gml:6" /> <Cite gml="objects/oItem/step.gml:84-111" />
<Cite gml="objects/oItem/step.gml:160-170" />. It turns to point the way it's flying
<Cite gml="objects/oFishBone/step.gml:3-25" />.

While it moves faster than 2 pixels a step, across or down, and touches a monster, it takes
<Stat id="fish-bone.damage" /> from the monster nearest it ([[nearest-enemy]]) <Cite
quirk="1" row="A fast thrown item hurts the nearest enemy, not the one it hit." /> and is destroyed; a Caveman,
a Hawkman, a Mantrap, a Yeti or a Vampire is also stunned. It's destroyed even when the monster's own rules
spare it the hit, as a Caveman's do while it's stunned, so it breaks on the first monster it reaches. Only an
invincible monster or a [[magma-man|Magma Man]] lets it fly on. A [[damsel|Damsel]] it hits loses 1 hit
point, and the bone is destroyed too <Cite
gml="objects/oItem/step.gml:256-363" /> <Cite gml="objects/oItem/step.gml:365-398" />. Unlike a
Rock or an arrow, it can't hurt the player: his own code checks only for those two <Cite
gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:1513-1565" />.

In the clip, the player drops a bomb beside a pool and runs back. The explosion breaks the pool's side,
the water drains away, and the Piranha in it turns into a Fish Bone. He picks the bone up, jumps out of the
pool and throws it at a [[snake|Snake]], which it kills, breaking <Cite clip="fish-bone/piranha" />.

## Locations

[[level-generation|Level generation]] makes no Fish Bones, and the [[level-editor|level editor]] has no tile
for one. They come from:

- **[[piranha|Piranhas]].** One whose middle isn't in water turns into a Fish Bone at once
  <Cite gml="objects/oPiranha/step.gml:136-140" />.
- **[[dead-fish|Dead Fish]]**, the same way, while they're on the screen
  <Cite gml="objects/oDeadFish/step.gml:1-3" /> <Cite gml="objects/oDeadFish/step.gml:70-74" />.

Both live only in water, so it takes the water draining for either to leave it: a block next to water that
breaks lets the water beside it go, and the rest of the pool follows over the next few steps
<Cite gml="objects/oSolid/destroy.gml:35" /> <Cite gml="objects/oGame/step.gml:49-104" />. A Piranha that's
killed leaves only blood, and a Dead Fish three plain bones, not a Fish Bone
<Cite gml="objects/oPiranha/step.gml:3-13" /> <Cite gml="objects/oDeadFish/step.gml:5-18" />.

## Interactions

- **[[bomb|Bombs]].** An explosion destroys a Fish Bone, as it does an arrow, a Jar or a Skull
  <Cite gml="objects/oExplosion/collision_oItem.gml:1-5" />.
- **[[lava|Lava]].** A Fish Bone that touches lava stops and sinks, and is destroyed once it's under the
  surface <Cite gml="objects/oItem/step.gml:160-185" />.
- **The screen.** It moves only while it's within 16 pixels of the screen
  ([[items-freeze-off-screen]]) <Cite gml="objects/oItem/step.gml:1-3" />
  <Cite quirk="1" row="Items freeze outside the view." />.
- **Exits.** Carried through a level's exit, it's in the player's hands on the next level; while he holds
  it, the HUD shows it in the box for the held item <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:842-850" />
  <Cite gml="scripts/scrHoldItem.gml:37" /> <Cite gml="scripts/scrDrawHUD.gml:84" />.

## Tips

- A Fish Bone breaks on the first monster it hits, hurt or not, so it's good for one throw, where a Rock
  can be thrown again.
- It can't hurt the player, so one thrown at a wall beside him and bouncing back is harmless.

## Trivia

- The code that lets a thrown monster hurt the monsters it hits ends by destroying the thrown one if its
  name is "Arrow" or "Fish Bone", a line copied from the items' code; no monster has either name
  <Cite gml="objects/oEnemy/step.gml:264" />. The Jar's and the Skull's own steps keep the same line, which
  never holds for them either <Cite gml="objects/oJar/step.gml:143-144" />
<Cite gml="objects/oSkull/step.gml:141-142" /> <Cite quirk="1" row="Jar and Skull leftovers" />.
