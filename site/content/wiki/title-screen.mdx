It's a room the player walks around in rather than a menu, two screens wide. The right screen, where the
game starts, has the doors under their signs: TUTORIAL, START and SCORES, the last to the
[[high-scores-room|high scores room]]. At its right a rope hangs from the top under a QUIT sign. The left
screen is the Shortcut House, where the [[tunnel-man|Tunnel Man]] stands and his
[[tunnel-man-shortcuts|shortcuts]]' doors open once they're paid for. Nothing there can hurt the player.

## Generation

### The intro

Spelunky opens on its intro: three lines of a story, one at a time, each picked from eight at random,
"AS THE MOON BURNED BRIGHT ABOVE," "I DISMOUNTED MY CAMEL," "AND THAT'S WHEN IT ALL STARTED." among them
<Cite gml="objects/oIntro/create.gml:9-45" /> <Cite gml="objects/oIntro/draw.gml:6-32" />. The screen then
fades in on the desert at night, and the Spelunker walks to a cave, bats flying out past him, throws a rope
down and climbs down out of sight, which fades out to the title screen
<Cite gml="objects/oIntro/step.gml:14-32" /> <Cite gml="objects/oPDummy3/step.gml:1-37" />. Enter, Escape,
attack or start skips the lines to the walk, and pressed during the walk, fades out to the title screen at
once <Cite gml="objects/oIntro/step.gml:1-12" />.

### The opening

<Clip id="title-screen/opening" />

From the intro, the title screen opens dark. DEREK YU PRESENTS shows at the top for 100 steps <Cite gml="objects/oTitle/create.gml:64-71" /> <Cite gml="objects/oTitle/alarm_0.gml:1-2" />
<Cite gml="objects/oScreen/step_begin.gml:135-140" />. Seventy steps later a flare drops from the top, lighting the
cave as it falls, and 50 steps later the Spelunker climbs down the rope, drops to the floor and is the player, as the
title music starts <Cite gml="objects/oTitle/alarm_1.gml:1-2" /> <Cite gml="objects/oTitle/alarm_2.gml:1-4" /> <Cite gml="objects/oTitle/draw.gml:1-7" />
<Cite gml="objects/oTitle/alarm_3.gml:1-3" /> <Cite gml="objects/oPDummy4/step.gml:1-32" />. Enter, Escape,
attack or start during the opening starts it over with the player already at the foot of the rope and the
cave lit <Cite gml="objects/oTitle/step.gml:1-23" /> <Cite gml="objects/oTitle/create.gml:81-89" />. In the
clip, the opening plays through, and the player walks left into the SCORES door <Cite clip="title-screen/opening" />.

Coming back from somewhere else, the player is already there, lit, with the music on: at the SCORES door from
the high scores room, at the tutorial door from the Tutorial <Cite gml="objects/oTitle/create.gml:72-98" /> <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/other_animation_end.gml:110-131" />.

### The room

| Door | Under the sign | Leads to | |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| START | START | A new game, on level 1 of [[mines\|the Mines]]; the very first game, the [[tutorial\|Tutorial]] | <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/other_animation_end.gml:72-86" /> |
| Tutorial | TUTORIAL, left of START | The Tutorial, as level 0 | <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/other_animation_end.gml:87-95" /> |
| Scores | SCORES, right of START | The [[high-scores-room\|high scores room]] | <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/other_animation_end.gml:96-109" /> |

The room editor placed the doors, the signs, the rope and the level 5 shortcut's door <Cite room="rTitle" />.
A game from START, or from the tutorial door through the Tutorial's exit, is one whose winning time counts for the high scores; one from a
shortcut's door isn't ([[shortcut-win-no-time]]) <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/other_animation_end.gml:72-95" /> <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/other_animation_end.gml:137-157" />.
On the game's very first run, the tutorial door and its sign are bricked over, and START leads to the
Tutorial, once ([[tutorial#getting-there]]) <Cite gml="objects/oTitle/create.gml:100-105" />
<Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/other_animation_end.gml:76-83" />. A hand then points at START, with PRESS UP TO
ENTER! while the player stands at it, but only if the title screen started with him already there: the
opening skipped, or back from the high scores room. After the whole opening there's none
<Cite gml="objects/oTitle/create.gml:64-98" /> <Cite gml="objects/oHintHand/draw.gml:1-14" />. The view
follows the player between the two screens <Cite gml="objects/oTitle/step.gml:25-36" />.

The player's hearts are set back to the start's on every step, so nothing can kill him there
<Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:2-7" />, and his bombs and ropes can't be used outside a level
<Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:944-948" />. The room can be paused once he stands in it
([[controls#pausing]]) <Cite gml="objects/oTitle/alarm_3.gml:2" />.

With all four of the high scores' gold trophies, a gold trophy and a door take the Tunnel Man's place at the
left end. Going through the door turns the player into the Tunnel Man, and through it again back
([[tunnel-man#playing-as-him]]) <Cite gml="objects/oTitle/create.gml:54-62" /> <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/other_animation_end.gml:192-218" />.

### Leaving

- **Quitting.** Climbing the rope to the top of the screen takes the player out of sight; the title fades
  out to the [[credits]], and the game quits after them <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:49-60" /> <Cite gml="objects/oPDummy5/step.gml:1-8" /> <Cite gml="objects/oTitle/step.gml:38-45" />
  <Cite gml="objects/oCredits1/step.gml:48" />. The clip shows it <Cite clip="credits/title-rope" />.
- **The level editor.** F2 opens the [[level-editor|level editor]], and F3 the menu of custom levels <Cite gml="objects/oTitle/keypress_F2.gml:1-3" /> <Cite gml="objects/oTitle/keypress_F3.gml:1-8" />.

## Inhabitants

The [[tunnel-man|Tunnel Man]] stands at the left end of the Shortcut House until the player
can play as him <Cite gml="objects/oTitle/create.gml:59-62" />. Nothing else lives there.

## Items and shops

The rope at the right is the room's only thing to use: climbing it to the top quits ([Leaving](#leaving)) <Cite room="rTitle" />.

## Secrets

<Clip id="jungle/shortcut" />

The Shortcut House, the left screen, is walled off until the Tunnel Man's first shortcut is paid for; then a
[[ladder|ladder]] leads up to its doors, to levels 5, 9 and 13 as each is paid ([[tunnel-man-shortcuts#the-shortcut-house]])
<Cite gml="objects/oTitle/create.gml:27-52" /> <Cite gml="objects/oTitle/draw.gml:17-29" />. In the clip, the
player climbs the ladder and goes into the level 5 door <Cite clip="jungle/shortcut" />.
