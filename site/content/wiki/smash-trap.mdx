A Smash Trap is a block of stone ringed with spikes that sits on the floor of [[temple|the Temple]]. When the
player comes within <Stat id="smash-trap.range" /> of it and lines up with it, in its row or its column, it
slides at him, speeding up, until it hits a wall. Touching it takes 10 hearts a step. On a
[[dark-level|dark level]] it's lit, and the lit kind kills only by crushing.

## How it works

<Clip id="smash-trap/crush" />

While it's on screen and resting, a Smash Trap watches the player. If he's within
<Stat id="smash-trap.range" /> of it and level with its middle (within 8 pixels), or straight above or below it,
it slides toward him, unless a block is right next to it on that side: right first, then down, left and up
<Cite gml="objects/oSmashTrap/step.gml:1-36" />. It speeds up by 0.5 pixels a step each step, to
<Stat id="smash-trap.top-speed" /> (<Stat id="smash-trap.top-speed" as="per-second" />), and goes straight on
until it hits a block or a [[tomb-lord|Tomb Lord]] <Cite gml="objects/oSmashTrap/step.gml:37-105" />. Then it
stops, and once it has backed off the wall it rests for <Stat id="smash-trap.rest" /> before it can slide again
<Cite gml="objects/oSmashTrap/step.gml:107-122" />.

It moves itself rather than being moved as a platform, so it doesn't push the player: it slides into him. The
clip shows the player walking into a Smash Trap's row: it slides at him, crushes him against the wall, and
stops there.

## Damage

Each step the player touches a Smash Trap, he loses 10 hearts, is thrown toward it (and down, if it was sliding
down) and drops whatever he holds <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:1649-1668" />. Being thrown toward a
solid block, he isn't knocked clear of it <Cite quirk="1" />
<Cite replay="fidelity/replays/trap-smash-crush.txt" />. Nothing spaces out the hits, so a player who stays
against it is hit on every step, and each hit he survives is counted in the end-of-game statistics' smash trap
row as well as the one that kills him <Cite quirk="1" />. If the trap slides
on over him, so that his middle is inside it, he's crushed: every heart, as anything solid crushes him
<Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:1492-1511" />.

**The lit kind**, on a dark level, is a separate object that the player's check doesn't count as a Smash Trap.
It never takes the 10 hearts: it slides into him until he's inside it, and kills him by crushing, counted in the
crushed row rather than the smash trap row <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:1649" />
<Cite gml="objects/oSmashTrapLit/step.gml:1-137" /> <Cite quirk="1" /> <Cite replay="fidelity/replays/trap-dark-smash.txt" />.
The second clip shows one sliding at the player and crushing him.

## Locations

[[level-generation|Level generation]] puts Smash Traps only in [[temple|the Temple]], on the ground: on each block with open
space above it, outside the starting room, a 1 in 60 chance, once the rolls for a [[tomb-lord|Tomb Lord]]
(1 in 40, where one may be made), a [[caveman|Caveman]] (1 in 40) and a [[hawkman|Hawkman]] (1 in 40) have
failed <Cite gml="scripts/scrEntityGen.gml:452-472" />. On a dark level it makes the lit kind
<Cite gml="scripts/scrEntityGen.gml:469" />.

Smash Traps are also in the Sun challenge room, a bonus room <Cite room="rSun" />, and in custom levels as the
[[level-editor|level editor]]'s tile `+` <Cite gml="scripts/scrCreateTileObj.gml:249-252" />.

## Avoiding

- **Stay out of line.** A Smash Trap only slides along its row or its column, and only at a player within 90
  pixels, so crossing its line farther off, or above or below its row out of its column, is safe.
- **Let it go by.** Set off from farther along its line, it slides past a player who has stepped out of the
  line, and rests for 50 steps once it stops.
- **Block it.** It won't slide toward a block right next to it.
- **Destroy it.** A [[bomb|Bomb]]'s blast destroys it like a block.

## Interactions

- **Lava.** A Smash Trap that touches lava stops, sinks into it and is destroyed when the lava closes over it
  <Cite gml="objects/oSmashTrap/step.gml:124-137" />.
- **[[tomb-lord|Tomb Lords]]** stop a sliding Smash Trap as a wall does <Cite gml="objects/oSmashTrap/step.gml:105" />.
- **Light.** The lit kind glows and counts as a light source on a dark level
  <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:131-136" />.

## Trivia

  first slide sets one: the hit throws him down if the random direction was down, one time in four. Touching
  it without setting it off takes coming at it from outside its row and column, as by brushing its corner while
  dropping past it <Cite gml="objects/oSmashTrap/create.gml:25-29" /> <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:1658-1661" />
  <Cite quirk="1" /> <Cite replay="fidelity/replays/trap-smash-random-throw.txt" />.

  <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:1660-1665" />.
- A similar trap, `oThwompTrap`, a block that drops on a player passing under it and rises again, is in the
  game's code but never made <Cite quirk="1" />.
