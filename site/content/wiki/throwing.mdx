Anything the player can pick up he can throw: a [[rock|Rock]], a [[jar|Jar]], a [[skull|Skull]], a
[[damsel|Damsel]], the [[golden-idol|Golden Idol]], a stunned monster. It's a way to hit something out of
the [[whip|Whip]]'s reach without a gun. A thrown item flies on its own rules, bouncing and sliding, and
hurts whatever monster it meets while it's moving fast.

## How it works

### Picking up

With empty hands, holding down and pressing the attack key picks up the item at the player's feet, unless
it's stuck in a block, or, if no item is there, a monster that's stunned or dead
<Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:1203-1214" /> <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:1300-1310" />. Of
several close together, the one nearest him is taken, which isn't always the one under him
<Cite quirk="1" /> <Cite replay="fidelity/replays/action-pickup-nearest-enemy.txt" />. A held item stays in
front of him, lower when he crouches <Cite gml="objects/oItem/step.gml:37-68" />.

### Throwing

<Clip id="throwing/rock" />

Pressing the attack key with something in hand uses it: a weapon fires, a [[bomb|Bomb]] is lit (the next
press throws it), the [[rope|Rope]] is thrown up, and anything else is thrown <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:1313-1318" />
<Cite gml="scripts/scrUseItem.gml:24-35" />. A weapon is only thrown while the player crouches, low and
straight ahead <Cite gml="scripts/scrUseItem.gml:121-145" />. A throw
<Cite gml="scripts/scrUseItem.gml:595-681" />:

- sends the item forward at <Stat id="throwing.speed" />, or <Stat id="throwing.heavy-speed" /> if it's
  heavy, on top of the player's own speed, and up at <Stat id="throwing.lift" />, or 2 if it's heavy;
- with up held, throws it up at <Stat id="throwing.up-lift" /> instead (4 if heavy), a higher arc at the
  same speed across;
- with down held, on the ground, sends it skidding along the floor at 0.6 of the speed, and in the air throws
  it down at 3 pixels per step;
- against a wall at head height, throws it flat, a pixel per step faster;
- makes it safe for <Stat id="throwing.safe" />: it can't hurt the player in that time
  <Cite gml="objects/oItem/alarm_2.gml:1" />.

Heavy things include every monster <Cite gml="objects/oEnemy/create.gml:15" />, a
Damsel <Cite gml="objects/oDamsel/create.gml:11" />, the Golden Idol <Cite gml="objects/oGoldIdol/create.gml:7" />,
[[crate|Crates]] <Cite gml="objects/oCrate/create.gml:7" /> and [[chest|Chests]], the Lamp, the
[[jetpack|Jetpack]] and dice. A thrown Damsel yells and tumbles, helpless for a
time <Cite gml="scripts/scrUseItem.gml:597-603" />. The [[pitchers-mitt|Pitcher's Mitt]] throws 6 pixels
per step faster and nearly flat, with less gravity (for a step, for most items: [Quirks](#quirks))
<Cite gml="scripts/scrUseItem.gml:667-676" />.

The clip shows three throws of a Rock: ahead, which lands about seven blocks away; with up held, which flies
higher and bounces back off the wall; and with down held, which skids a short way along the floor
<Cite clip="throwing/rock" />.

### In flight

This is the code most items share (`oItem`'s step); a [[jar|Jar]], a [[skull|Skull]] and a die have steps of
their own, with their own rules, which their pages describe. A Jar, for one, breaks on whatever it hits, and
only stuns a Caveman, Mantrap, Yeti, Hawkman or Shopkeeper without taking a hit point
<Cite gml="objects/oJar/step.gml:106-147" />.

A thrown item falls with a gravity of 0.6 pixels per step, each step, to at most 8 pixels per step. It
bounces off the floor at a fraction of its speed and slows as it slides, bounces back off walls at half its
speed, and off ceilings at most of it <Cite gml="objects/oItem/step.gml:69-158" />. Each item sets how much
it bounces and slides: half its speed and 0.3 of it, unless its own create event says otherwise
<Cite gml="objects/oItem/create.gml:20-21" />. An item more than 16 pixels outside the view stops where it is,
and flies on when the view comes back to it <Cite quirk="1" />
<Cite replay="fidelity/replays/item-seed-49-rock-off-view.txt" />.

### Hitting monsters

While a thrown item moves faster than 2 pixels per step, across or up or down, a monster it touches loses
1 hit point and is pushed along at 0.3 of its speed, and the item flies on
<Cite gml="objects/oItem/step.gml:256-350" /> <Cite quirk="1" />.
Some monsters take it their own way:

- A [[caveman|Caveman]], [[mantrap|Mantrap]], [[vampire|Vampire]], [[yeti|Yeti]] or [[hawkman|Hawkman]] is
  stunned too, and isn't hurt again while it's stunned <Cite gml="objects/oItem/step.gml:270-288" />.
- A [[shopkeeper|Shopkeeper]] turns on the player, and isn't hurt again while he's stunned or dead
  <Cite gml="objects/oItem/step.gml:289-298" />.
- A [[giant-spider|Giant Spider]] can't be hurt again for 10 steps, a [[tomb-lord|Tomb Lord]] for 20, and the
  [[alien-boss|Alien Boss]] while it shows its hurt
  <Cite gml="objects/oItem/step.gml:300-330" />.
- A [[ufo|UFO]] explodes <Cite gml="objects/oItem/step.gml:331-340" />.
- A [[magma-man|Magma Man]], and a monster that's invincible, isn't hurt
  <Cite gml="objects/oItem/step.gml:261" />.

Other monsters have no pause between hits, so an item that stays inside a big one hurts it on every step it's
there: a Rock thrown at the [[yeti-king|Yeti King]] takes 9 of its 30 hit points
<Cite gml="objects/oItem/step.gml:341-348" /> <Cite clip="yeti-king/thrown-rock" />. An [[arrow|Arrow]]
breaks on the first monster it hurts, and takes 3 more from a Vampire that isn't stunned
<Cite gml="objects/oItem/step.gml:265-266" /> <Cite gml="objects/oItem/step.gml:358-361" />. A thrown item
hits a Damsel the same way, knocking her down, and angers the Shopkeeper if she's for sale
<Cite gml="objects/oItem/step.gml:365-398" />.

A stunned monster thrown, or knocked flying, hurts the monsters it touches the same way while it moves faster
than 2 pixels per step, but pushes them harder: one that isn't stunned or dead itself takes all of its speed,
not 0.3 of it, and one that is keeps its own <Cite gml="objects/oEnemy/step.gml:169-262" /> <Cite quirk="1" />.
A clip shows both: a Rock thrown at 8 pixels per step knocks a Caveman back at 2.4, and the stunned Caveman,
thrown on the run, sends a Yeti across the room at 6.7 <Cite clip="quirks/thrown-monster-full-push" />.

### Hitting the player

Of the things the player throws, only a Rock and an Arrow can hurt him, a [[bomb|Bomb]]'s explosion aside,
once they're no longer safe: a Rock
moving faster than 4 pixels per step across takes 2 hearts, and an Arrow faster than 3 takes
<Stat id="arrow.player-damage" />, each stunning him ([[damage]])
<Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:1513-1563" />. With the Pitcher's Mitt and empty hands he catches a
Rock instead.

## Quirks

- **A thrown item hurts the nearest monster, not the one it hit**: with two close together, the other can
  take the hit ([[nearest-enemy]]) <Cite quirk="1" /> <Cite replay="fidelity/replays/action-throw-rock-nearest-enemy.txt" />.
- **Picking up takes the nearest item or monster**, not the one at the player's feet <Cite quirk="1" />.
- **Items freeze outside the view**, in mid-air if that's where they are <Cite quirk="1" />.
- **A thrown monster pushes the one it hits with all of its speed**, where a thrown item pushes with 0.3 of
  its: the code that sets the item's push sets it twice, and the monster's second line is commented out
  <Cite quirk="1" /> <Cite clip="quirks/thrown-monster-full-push" />.
- **Every use of a held item moves it to the player's height**: the last line of the code that uses it only
  half belongs to its `if` <Cite gml="scripts/scrUseItem.gml:683" />. The item's own step, later in the same
  step, puts it back before it's drawn, except a [[teleporter|Teleporter]] that takes him up 8 blocks from near
  the top of the view, which is drawn level with him for that frame <Cite quirk="1" />
  <Cite replay="fidelity/replays/weapon-teleporter-held-y.txt" />.
- **The Pitcher's Mitt's light gravity lasts one step** for most items, but a Jar, a Skull or a die keeps it
  ([[mitt-gravity-one-step]]) <Cite quirk="1" />; and **its catch holds every Rock**, not only one the player threw
  ([[catching-every-rock]]) <Cite quirk="1" />.
