A custom level's info sets the hearts, bombs and ropes it starts with. Played from the menu, the game limits them,
the bombs and ropes to 0-99 and the hearts to 1-99. The [[level-editor|level editor]]'s Test button starts the level
with them as they were typed, and the player's step caps them at 99 a frame later, so all it shows is a four-digit
count on the HUD for the level's first frame.

## What happens

The player finishes the level's info, with 1111 starting bombs, and clicks Test. On the level's first frame, the
HUD shows 1111 bombs, its digits running over the rope icon. On the next, they're 99
<Cite clip="quirks/test-bombs-unclamped" />.

<Clip id="quirks/test-bombs-unclamped" />

## Why

The Test button saves the level and sets the starting values straight from what was typed:

```gml scripts/scrTestLevel.gml:93-95
    global.plife = real(lifeStart);
    global.bombs = real(bombStart);
    global.rope = real(ropeStart);
```

The test room then loads the level with `scrLoadLevel(-1)` <Cite gml="objects/oLoadLevel/create.gml:84-87" />, and
the -1 skips the rounding down and the limits that a level played from the menu gets
<Cite gml="scripts/scrLoadLevel.gml:138-148" /> <Cite quirk="1" />. The HUD draws the bombs' count after their
icon, where four digits reach past the rope icon <Cite gml="scripts/scrDrawHUD.gml:27-40" />. From the next frame,
the player's step caps the hearts, bombs and ropes at 99 <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:9-11" />.

## Reproducing it

In the level editor, click Edit, press Enter four times to reach START BOMBS, clear it with Backspace, type a
number over 99, and finish the info; then click Test. Few players type more than 99 bombs, and the count only shows
for a frame. In the clip's replay the bombs are typed as 1111, and the test level loads
on frame 548 with 1111 bombs, which are 99 on 549 <Cite replay="fidelity/replays/editor-test-hud-caps.txt" />.

## In the port

The spelunky.ai port reproduces it exactly: it's in docs/quirks.md's list of the 1.1 source's bugs
<Cite quirk="1" />, and the oracle's fingerprint of the replay pins the 1111 bombs and the cap. No option turns it
off.
