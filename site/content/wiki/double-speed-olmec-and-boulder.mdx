[[olmec|Olmec]] and the [[boulder|boulder]] that the [[golden-idol|golden idol]] lets loose are moving blocks, which
the game moves by their speed every step. Their own code then moves them by that speed again, so each covers twice
the speed its code sets. Nothing on screen says so: it shows as an Olmec who flies faster than his numbers, and a
boulder that rolls at 8 to 10 pixels a step where its code says 4.5.

## What happens

Olmec hops toward a player standing still. He leaves the ground at 8 pixels a step, where his speed is 4, and
drifts left 4 pixels a step, where his speed is never more than 2. Over the player he stops, then slams down 10
and then 12 pixels a step, where his speed is 5 and then 5.2, and crushes him
<Cite clip="quirks/olmec-double-speed" />.

<Clip id="quirks/olmec-double-speed" />

The player has taken the idol, and the tiki head drops the boulder onto him. Its speed is 4.5 pixels a step as it
rolls off, and once it's clear of him it moves 10, 8, 10 and 8 pixels a step <Cite clip="quirks/boulder-double-speed" />.

<Clip id="quirks/boulder-double-speed" />

## Why

Each step, the game first moves every moving block (`oMovingSolid`, which Olmec and the boulder are) by its speed,
pushing and carrying the player <Cite gml="scripts/gameStepEvent.gml:23-233" />. Away from the player, that's the
plain move at the end:

```gml scripts/gameStepEvent.gml:215-219
  else
  {
    x+=xVelInteger
    y+=yVelInteger
  }
```

Then each block's own step runs. Olmec's moves him by the same speed again, with `moveTo`, which stops at blocks:

```gml objects/oOlmec/step.gml:3-8
if (carryPlayer or collision_rectangle(x-1, y, x+66, y+62, oPlayer1, 0, 0))
{
    oPlayer1.x += xVel;
    oPlayer1.y += yVel;
}
moveTo(xVel,yVel);
```

The boulder's does the same <Cite gml="objects/oBoulder/step.gml:7" />. Both moves turn a fraction of a pixel into
whole pixels the same way, by the step's count <Cite gml="scripts/moveTo.gml:22-36" />, so in the open the two
moves are the same and each covers twice its speed <Cite quirk="1" />. The game's other moving blocks, such as
the [[ceiling-trap|Ceiling Trap]] and the [[smash-trap|Smash Trap]], either only set their speed and leave the moving
to the game, or move by variables of their own, so they move once.

## Reproducing it

It happens in every Olmec fight and to every boulder the idol sets off. In the first clip's replay, Olmec hops
toward the player on frame 2908: his y goes 496, 488, 480, 472 on 2909-2912, with his speed -4 and slowing; his x
goes 72, 68, 64, 60 on 2925-2928, with his sideways speed never past -2; and slamming, his y goes 416, 426, 438 on
2953-2955, with his speed 5 and then 5.2. The slam crushes the player on 2964
<Cite replay="fidelity/replays/enemy-olmec-crush-seed-1.txt" />. In the second's, the boulder's speed is 4.5 from
frame 813, and its x goes 118, 128, 136, 146, 154 on 817-821
<Cite replay="fidelity/replays/trap-boulder-crush.txt" />.

## In the port

The spelunky.ai port reproduces it exactly: it's in docs/quirks.md's list of the 1.1 source's bugs
<Cite quirk="1" />, and the oracle's fingerprints of the two replays pin the moves. No option turns it off.
