Wearing it, the player has a second life: the first time his hearts run out, the Ankh shatters and he's put
back on his feet with <Stat id="ankh.revive" />, at the level's entrance. Only the [[black-market|Black
Market]]'s [[ankh-shop|Ankh shop]] sells it. It's worn from the moment it's paid for (or stolen) until it
saves him, or until the game ends.

## Effect

<Clip id="ankh/revive" />

Paying for it or carrying it out of the shop puts it on: the message "YOU GOT THE ANKH! YOU FEEL PROTECTED."
shows, the Ankh disappears, and an icon of it joins the row under the hearts
<Cite gml="scripts/scrStealItem.gml:35-45" /> <Cite gml="scripts/scrDrawHUD.gml:106-110" />. A new game
takes it away if it's still unused <Cite gml="scripts/scrClearGlobals.gml:85" />
<Cite gml="objects/oTransition/create.gml:20-24" />.

When the player's hearts drop below 1 on a level, the Ankh takes the death's place. His hearts are set to
<Stat id="ankh.revive" />, whatever he had before, and he's moved: to the inside of the Moai if the level has
one and it's active (things far outside the view are switched off <Cite gml="objects/oLevel/step.gml:75-94" />),
to a set place near the left wall in [[olmecs-lair|Olmec's Lair]], and otherwise to the level's entrance. A [[ball-and-chain|ball and chain]], if Kali has given him one, come with him. He stops moving and blinks, invincible,
for <Stat id="ankh.revive-invincibility" as="seconds" />, while the message "THE ANKH SHATTERS! YOU HAVE BEEN
REVIVED!" shows with a teleporter's sound. The Ankh is gone <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:1847-1896" />.
However the last heart is lost, this comes before the game ends, which only happens without the Ankh
<Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:1897-1920" />.

The clip shows the player stealing the Ankh by jumping out of the Ankh shop, then dropping back in, where
the angry Shopkeeper's shots take his hearts; the Ankh shatters and he's back at the level's entrance with
<Stat id="ankh.revive" /> <Cite clip="ankh/revive" />. On a level with the [[moai|Moai]], the same death
near it puts him inside the Moai instead, a chamber nothing else opens (see [[hedjet]])
<Cite clip="hedjet/moai" />.

## Obtaining

| Source | Chance | |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The [[black-market\|Black Market]]'s [[ankh-shop\|Ankh shop]] | One in every Black Market, for <Stat id="ankh.price" /> | <Cite gml="scripts/scrLevelGen.gml:40-69" /> <Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGenMarket.gml:112-116" /> |

The Black Market comes once a game, as the level after the one in the [[jungle|Jungle]] whose hidden entrance
the player finds (the [[udjat-eye|Udjat Eye]] helps); so it's level 6, 7 or 8
<Cite gml="scripts/scrInitLevel.gml:186-192" /> <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:879" />. One of its shops
is the Ankh shop, a room with the Ankh on display and a [[shopkeeper|Shopkeeper]] who greets the player with "I
HAVE SOMETHING SPECIAL..." <Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGenMarket.gml:361-364" />
<Cite gml="objects/oShopkeeper/step.gml:133" />. The shop puts it up directly, not through the code that
stocks other shops, so it costs <Stat id="ankh.price" /> whatever the level
<Cite gml="objects/oAnkh/create.gml:7-8" />. Nothing else makes an Ankh: no other shop, Crate, block or gift,
and the [[level-editor|level editor]] has no tile for it.

Carrying it out of the shop without paying puts it on and angers the [[shopkeeper|Shopkeeper]], as with any
ware <Cite quirk="1" />. In the clip, the player jumps up out of the shop's room with it, and it goes on at
once <Cite replay="fidelity/replays/item-seed-9-ankh.txt" />.

## Interactions

- Only the first death is saved: the Ankh is used up, and the next time his hearts run out the game ends
  <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:1891" /> <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:1897-1920" />.
- The revival restores <Stat id="ankh.revive" /> even to a player who had more before, as it sets his hearts
  rather than adding to them <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:1855" />.

## Tips

- On a level with the Moai, a death with the Ankh near it is a way inside it; see [[hedjet]].
- Stealing the Ankh angers the Shopkeeper, but the death his shots may cause is the one the Ankh then saves,
  as in the clip.
