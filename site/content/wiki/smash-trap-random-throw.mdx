A [[smash-trap|Smash Trap]]'s hit takes 10 hearts and throws the player. Which way is decided by the trap: across,
toward it, and up, or down if it's sliding down. A trap that has never slid has no way it's sliding, only the one
it picked at random when it was made, so its hit throws the player down one time in four.

## What happens

The player drops into a gap beside a Smash Trap that hasn't moved yet and brushes its corner from above its row.
The hit throws him down, not up, because the trap was made facing down at random; he falls into its row, and it
slides into him <Cite clip="quirks/smash-trap-random-throw" />.

<Clip id="quirks/smash-trap-random-throw" />

## Why

When the player touches a Smash Trap, his step takes the nearest one, not always the one he touches, and throws
him by it:

```gml objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:1653-1661
    obj = instance_nearest(x, y, oSmashTrap);
    global.plife -= 10;
    if (obj.x+8 < x) xVel = -rand(4,6);
    else xVel = rand(4,6);
    yVel = -6;
    if (obj)
    {
        if (obj.dir == 1) yVel = 4;
    }
```

`xVel` is negative when the trap's middle is left of him, so it throws him toward the trap, from either side.
The trap is solid, so he stays against it, to be hit again on the next step <Cite quirk="1" />. `yVel` is -6, or
4 if the trap's `dir` is 1, down. A slide sets `dir` to the way it goes
<Cite gml="objects/oSmashTrap/step.gml:4-35" />, but before its first slide it's what the create event picked:

```gml objects/oSmashTrap/create.gml:25
dir = rand(0,3);
```

So a trap that has never slid throws him down one time in four. Touching one without setting it off takes coming
at it from outside its row and column, as inside them it slides at him first <Cite quirk="1" />.

## Reproducing it

Drop past a Smash Trap that hasn't moved, brushing its corner from above its row. The replay of the clip's staged
scene, a custom level with seed 1, has a trap whose create event makes its `dir` 1. The player drops into the gap
beside it and touches its corner from above its row, before it has moved: the hit throws him down, with a `yVel`
of 4, and toward it. Thrown down into its row, he sets it off, and it slides into him
<Cite replay="fidelity/replays/trap-smash-random-throw.txt" />. A trap that slides at the player throws him toward
it too, from his right, and goes on over him <Cite replay="fidelity/replays/trap-smash-crush.txt" />.

## In the port

The spelunky.ai port reproduces it exactly: it's in docs/quirks.md's list of the 1.1 source's bugs
<Cite quirk="1" />, and the oracle's fingerprints of both replays pin the throws. No option turns it off.
