It's thrown like a [[rock|Rock]], but breaks like a [[jar|Jar]], into one or two bones, with nothing inside.
It's left wherever bones are: beside the piles [[level-generation|level generation]] scatters, and where a
[[skeleton|Skeleton]], a [[zombie|Zombie]] or a [[vampire|Vampire]] falls apart.

## Usage

<Clip id="skull/throw" />

A Skull is picked up and thrown as any item is: crouching over it with empty hands, the attack key lifts
it, and the attack key throws it <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:1203-1214" />
<Cite gml="scripts/scrUseItem.gml:605-646" />. Its movement code is the Jar's: it falls 0.6 pixels a step
faster each step until it's falling at 6 or more, and bounces off walls and floors <Cite gml="objects/oSkull/step.gml:23-77" />. It
breaks more easily than a Jar, when it:

- hits a ceiling going up, at any speed: its test is for a speed below 2 pixels a step downward, which
  always holds for a Skull going up ([[skull-breaks-on-ceiling]]) <Cite gml="objects/oSkull/step.gml:37-41" /> <Cite quirk="1" />;
- hits a wall faster than 2 pixels a step across, or lands faster than
  3 <Cite gml="objects/oSkull/step.gml:43-51" />;
- touches a monster or a [[damsel|Damsel]] while moving faster than 2 pixels a
  step <Cite gml="objects/oSkull/step.gml:104-170" />;
- is hit by a bullet or an explosion, or sinks into
  [[lava|lava]] <Cite gml="objects/oSkull/collision_oBullet.gml:1" /> <Cite gml="objects/oExplosion/collision_oItem.gml:1-5" /> <Cite gml="objects/oSkull/step.gml:81-101" />.

Breaking, it leaves one bone, and a second with a 1 in 2 chance <Cite gml="objects/oSkull/destroy.gml:3-15" />.

A Skull does to a monster what a Jar does, by a copy of its code: it picks the monster nearest it
([[nearest-enemy]]), stuns a
[[caveman|Caveman]], a [[hawkman|Hawkman]], a [[mantrap|Mantrap]], a [[yeti|Yeti]] or a
[[shopkeeper|Shopkeeper]] without hurting it, takes <Stat id="skull.damage" /> from any other that isn't
invincible, and breaks <Cite gml="objects/oSkull/step.gml:104-146" /> <Cite quirk="1" />. In the clip, the
player drops off a ledge with a Skull and throws it at a [[snake|Snake]] on the ledge beside him: it breaks
on the Snake and kills it <Cite clip="skull/throw" />.

## Locations

- **Piles of bones.** Level generation puts piles of bones on blocks with open space above them in
  [[mines|the Mines]], [[jungle|the Jungle]], [[ice-caves|the Ice Caves]] and [[temple|the Temple]]; 7 in 8
  of them are plain bones with a Skull beside them, the rest rise as a
  Skeleton <Cite gml="scripts/scrTreasureGen.gml:80-87" /> <Cite gml="scripts/scrTreasureGen.gml:103-111" />
  <Cite gml="scripts/scrTreasureGen.gml:124-132" />. The [[skeleton#locations|Skeleton's page]] has the odds.
- **Dead monsters.** A Skeleton, a Zombie or a Vampire that dies falls apart into three bones and a Skull,
  thrown upward <Cite gml="objects/oSkeleton/step.gml:16-24" /> <Cite gml="objects/oZombie/step.gml:13-22" />
  <Cite gml="objects/oVampire/step.gml:241-249" />.
- **The player.** A [[ghost|Ghost]]'s touch kills him outright, and his body falls apart the same
  way <Cite gml="objects/oGhost/collision_oCharacter.gml:1-16" />.
- **The [[level-editor|level editor]]**'s `k` tile, a pile of bones with a Skull
  beside it <Cite gml="scripts/scrCreateTileObj.gml:122-126" />.

## Interactions

- **[[whip|Whip]].** The Skull has no code for it, so the Whip doesn't break it, unlike a Jar.
- **[[pitchers-mitt|Pitcher's Mitt]].** Like a Jar, a Skull thrown with it keeps the Mitt's lighter
  gravity for good ([[mitt-gravity-one-step]]) <Cite quirk="1" />.
- **The screen.** Most items stop once they're 16 pixels off the screen; a Skull's code has no such
  test, so it keeps moving until the level puts everything far off the screen to
  sleep <Cite gml="objects/oSkull/step.gml:1-25" /> <Cite gml="objects/oLevel/step.gml:65-77" />.
- **Exits.** Carried through a level's exit, it's in the player's hands on the next level, whole
  <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:842-850" />.

## Tips

- A Skull thrown upward under a low ceiling breaks on it, whatever its speed.
- A Skull only stuns a Caveman, a Hawkman, a Mantrap or a Yeti, where a Rock also takes a hit point.

## Trivia

- The Skull's step is the Jar's with the Jar's name changed, but its thresholds for breaking are lower, and
  its test for the ceiling, `yVel < 2` inside a test for `yVel < 0`, can't fail, where the Jar's is
  `yVel < -3` <Cite gml="objects/oSkull/step.gml:37-41" /> <Cite gml="objects/oJar/step.gml:37-41" /> <Cite quirk="1" />.
  Thrown up at a ceiling it reaches rising at 2.4 pixels a step, a Skull breaks on it, where a Jar that
  reaches it at 3 bounces off whole <Cite replay="fidelity/replays/item-skull-breaks-on-ceiling.txt" />.
- A Skull that a bullet breaks in the player's hands, as a [[shopkeeper|Shopkeeper]]'s shot can, stays on
  the HUD, as a Jar does (the [[phantom-jar|phantom Jar]]): the code that tidies up after a held Skull clears a misspelt variable, so the game
  still remembers a Skull as the item in his hands <Cite gml="objects/oSkull/destroy.gml:16-20" /> <Cite quirk="1" />.
- The number of bones is rolled anew on each pass of the loop that makes them, `i < rand(1,2)`: a first
  bone always, then a second on a 2 <Cite gml="objects/oSkull/destroy.gml:7" />.
