The player carries bombs as a count, shown at the top of the screen, and the bomb key throws one, lit
<Cite gml="scripts/scrDrawHUD.gml:36-38" />. Its explosion breaks the blocks it touches, letting out the gold
and gems inside them, kills most monsters outright and costs the player <Stat id="bomb.player-damage" /> on
every step he's in it. More come in [[bomb-bag|Bomb Bags]] and [[bomb-box|Bomb Boxes]], and [[paste|Paste]]
makes them sticky.

## Usage

### Throwing it

<Clip id="bomb/throw" />

The bomb key throws a lit bomb from the player at 8 pixels a step across plus his own speed, rising at 3;
holding up throws it at 9 upward, and holding down drops it almost at his feet if he's on the ground, or
throws it down at 3 if he isn't <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:1137-1174" />. It flies, bounces and
comes to rest as any [[throwing|thrown item]] does <Cite gml="objects/oItem/step.gml:71-111" />, and one flying faster than
2 pixels a step that touches a monster hurts it as a thrown Rock does, unless it's sticky
<Cite gml="objects/oItem/step.gml:217-350" />. In the clip, the player throws a bomb ahead of a
[[snake|Snake]] walking toward him, and the explosion kills it <Cite clip="bomb/throw" />.

A lit bomb burns for <Stat id="bomb.fuse" /> (<Stat id="bomb.fuse" as="seconds" />), then flashes for
<Stat id="bomb.fuse-flash" /> (<Stat id="bomb.fuse-flash" as="seconds" />) and
explodes <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:1143-1147" /> <Cite gml="objects/oBomb/alarm_0.gml:1-2" />
<Cite gml="objects/oBomb/alarm_1.gml:1-11" />. One that sinks into lava explodes at once
<Cite gml="objects/oItem/step.gml:172-185" />.

### In the hands

The item key takes a bomb out into the player's hands, unlit, putting away what he held; pressing it again
swaps the bomb for a rope, and a third time brings back what he had
<Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:954-1043" />. The attack key lights a bomb in his hands, starting its
fuse, without throwing it; pressed again, it throws the bomb as any item is thrown, so a
[[pitchers-mitt|Pitcher's Mitt]] throws it farther, and what he held before comes back to his hands
<Cite gml="scripts/scrUseItem.gml:24-35" /> <Cite gml="scripts/scrUseItem.gml:605-683" />. A lit bomb can't be
put away with the item key <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:959-962" />, and one still held when it goes
off explodes in his hands <Cite gml="objects/oBomb/alarm_1.gml:7-10" />. With down held on the ground, the
attack key tosses a lit one low along the floor, as it does any item.

At an exit, an unlit bomb in his hands goes back into his count; one he lit is left behind, where it explodes
<Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:821-834" />. The bomb key works whatever he's holding, so he can throw
bombs with a lit one in his hands <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:1137" />.

### The explosion

<Clip id="bomb/chain" />

The explosion lasts 12 steps (0.4 seconds) <Cite clip="bomb/chain" />, and on every step it does this to
whatever it touches:

| What it touches | What happens | |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Blocks | Destroyed, unless they're invincible (the level's border, the blocks under an entrance or an exit), when the explosion is on the screen or in the Tutorial; spikes left with no block under them break | <Cite gml="objects/oExplosion/collision_oSolid.gml:1-31" /> |
| Monsters | <Stat id="bomb.damage" />, knocked away 4–6 pixels a step and up at 6, and set burning; a [[magma-man\|Magma Man]] bursts into two blobs of magma instead, and an invincible monster takes nothing | <Cite gml="objects/oExplosion/collision_oEnemy.gml:1-19" /> |
| The player | <Stat id="bomb.player-damage" />, knocked away and up, stunned for 100 steps and set burning | <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:1614-1627" /> |
| A [[damsel\|Damsel]] | 100 hit points, knocked away and up, stunned and set burning, unless she's invincible | <Cite gml="objects/oExplosion/collision_oDamsel.gml:1-9" /> |
| Another bomb | Lit, if it wasn't, to go off 4–8 steps after the explosion last touches it, knocked away, and torn off a monster it was stuck to | <Cite gml="objects/oExplosion/collision_oItem.gml:6-19" /> |
| [[arrow\|Arrows]], fish bones, [[jar\|Jars]] and [[skull\|Skulls]] | Destroyed | <Cite gml="objects/oExplosion/collision_oItem.gml:1-5" /> |
| Other items | Thrown away from it, 6 pixels a step up or down and 4–6 across, and out of the player's hands | <Cite gml="objects/oExplosion/collision_oItem.gml:20-46" /> |
| The golden idol's [[boulder]] | Blown apart into rubble, with a 1 in 3 chance of a [[rock\|Rock]] for each of three pieces | <Cite gml="objects/oExplosion/collision_oBoulder.gml:1-13" /> |
| [[web\|Webs]], and the mothership's force-field emitters | Destroyed | <Cite gml="objects/oExplosion/collision_oWeb.gml:1" /> <Cite gml="objects/oExplosion/collision_oBarrierEmitter.gml:1" /> |

So a bomb at the player's feet takes the four hearts he starts with and more; each step in the blast costs
<Stat id="bomb.player-damage" />, and the game's tally of deaths by explosion never counts the one that kills
him <Cite quirk="1" /> <Cite replay="fidelity/replays/action-bomb-death-mines-seed-1.txt" />. In the clip, the
player drops a bomb, picks it up lit, sets a second one down beside it and walks away: the first explosion
sets off the second a few steps later, well before its own fuse would have run out <Cite clip="bomb/chain" />.

<Clip id="bomb/brick" />

A destroyed block lets out what it hides, gold and gems. In the last clip, a bomb dropped at the player's feet
blows six bricks out of the rock around it <Cite clip="bomb/brick" />.

### Sticky bombs

Wearing [[paste|Paste]], every bomb the player makes is sticky: a lit one stops dead against the first wall,
floor or ceiling it touches, and one thrown faster than 2 pixels a step into a monster sticks to it and rides
along until it goes off <Cite gml="objects/oItem/step.gml:113-121" />
<Cite gml="objects/oItem/step.gml:217-255" /> <Cite gml="objects/oBomb/step_end.gml:1-12" />.

## Locations

- **The player's own.** A new game starts him with <Stat id="bomb.start" />, the [[tunnel-man|Tunnel Man]] with none
  <Cite gml="scripts/scrClearGlobals.gml:103-114" />, and the [[tutorial|Tutorial]] with none, where a
  [[crate|Crate]] holds a bag of three <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/other_animation_end.gml:87-94" />. He can
  carry <Stat id="bomb.max" /> at most <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:10" />.
- **[[bomb-bag|Bomb Bags]] and [[bomb-box|Bomb Boxes]]**, <Stat id="bomb-bag.bombs" /> and
  <Stat id="bomb-box.bombs" />, picked up by touch once they're free: in shops and in Crates
  <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:2020-2050" />.
- **Kali.** Once the player's favour with her reaches 32 at one of her altars, her gift, if he has fewer than
  80 bombs, brings them up to 99 <Cite gml="scripts/scrGetFavorMsg.gml:27-44" />.
- **[[chest|Chests]]**: one in twelve throws out a lit bomb when it's opened, outside the Tutorial
  <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:568-578" />.
- **[[monkey|Monkeys]]**: one clinging to the player sometimes takes a bomb and throws it out, lit one time in
  ten <Cite gml="objects/oMonkey/step.gml:256-273" />. An unlit one can be picked up, and goes into his count
  when he puts it away with the item key or takes it through an exit
  <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:963-969" />.
- **The Sun Room**, a bonus room, drops lit bombs from its ceiling until the player has 50 points there
  <Cite gml="objects/oSunRoom/alarm_0.gml:29-40" />.
- **Custom levels** start the player with the bomb count the [[level-editor|level editor]] gives them, 99 at
  most <Cite gml="scripts/scrLoadLevel.gml:143-145" />.

## Interactions

- **Shopkeepers.** A bomb the player or a Monkey lit (or the Sun Room dropped), in a shop and within 96
  pixels of a [[shopkeeper|Shopkeeper]], angers him <Cite gml="objects/oBomb/step.gml:6-17" />, as does
  hurting him with the explosion. A bomb lit any other way (a [[chest|Chest]]'s trap, another explosion, a
  Magma Man) isn't marked armed, unless the player drops it when he's stunned or killed, so it doesn't, and
  at an exit it goes back into the player's count like an unlit
  one <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:570-577" /> <Cite gml="objects/oExplosion/collision_oItem.gml:6-14" /> <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:821-831" /> <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:1714-1720" /> <Cite gml="objects/oSunRoom/alarm_0.gml:31-33" /> <Cite quirk="1" />.
  Only a Chest's, with its 40 steps, burns long enough to be carried out: a free
  bomb <Cite replay="fidelity/replays/item-chest-trap-bomb-unarmed.txt" />.
- **[[magma-man|Magma Men]].** A bomb that touches one is lit, to go off 8–12 steps later, and knocked away
  <Cite gml="objects/oMagmaMan/collision_oBomb.gml:1-18" />; so are bombs that touch the
  [[mothership|mothership]]'s barriers, 4–8 steps later
  <Cite gml="objects/oBarrier/collision_oItem.gml:1-17" />.
- **[[bow|The Bow]].** With the Bow fully drawn, the bomb key readies a bomb arrow instead of throwing a bomb,
  and costs no bomb <Cite quirk="1" />.
- **[[pitchers-mitt|Pitcher's Mitt]].** A bomb thrown from the hands goes farther and flatter; one thrown with
  the bomb key doesn't <Cite gml="scripts/scrUseItem.gml:667-676" />.

## Tips

- Dropped with down held, a bomb lands almost at the player's feet: he has
  <Stat id="bomb.fuse" as="seconds" /> and more to get clear.
- An explosion sets off every bomb it touches within 4–8 steps, so bombs placed together go off almost
  together.
- An explosion off the screen breaks no blocks, so a bomb thrown far out of sight only hurts what it touches.

## Trivia

- A Chest's trap bomb and a Monkey's lit one burn for only the last 40 steps of a fuse, flashing from the
  start <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:572-577" /> <Cite gml="objects/oMonkey/step.gml:261-267" />.
- The line that took a bomb from the count when one in the hands is lit is commented out: the count goes down
  when the bomb is taken out instead <Cite gml="scripts/scrUseItem.gml:34" />
  <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:1009-1015" />.
- An explosion that breaks a block also sets every treasure on the level moving, to wake any that had come to

  of its code reads `state`, so the line changes
