Vampires are rare even on the [[restless-dead|graveyard levels]] of [[jungle|the Jungle]], the only levels
whose generation makes them. A Vampire walks and charges like a [[caveman|Caveman]], and turns into a bat to
fly up to the player. It has <Stat id="vampire.hp" />, most single hits only stun it, and the blood its bites
draw heals it. A dead Vampire leaves a [[cape|Cape]].

## Behaviour

**On foot.** A Vampire wanders like a Caveman: it walks a random way, turning around at walls, and stops to
stand for 20–50 steps (0.67–1.67 seconds) with a 1 in 100 chance each step and at the edge of a ledge
<Cite gml="objects/oVampire/step.gml:57-130" />. Its code sets its walk to <Stat id="vampire.walk-speed" />,
but friction takes 0.1 off before it moves, so in play it covers 4 pixels every 3 steps (40 per second) <Cite
gml="objects/oVampire/step.gml:356-358" /> <Cite gml="scripts/moveTo.gml:20-31" /> <Cite clip="vampire/attack" />. Standing beside a wall, it hops <Cite gml="objects/oVampire/step.gml:60-67" />.

<Clip id="vampire/attack" />

**Charging.** When the player comes within <Stat id="vampire.sight-range" />, a standing Vampire turns to
face him and charges; a walking one charges the way it's walking, whichever way that is
<Cite gml="objects/oVampire/step.gml:81-86" /> <Cite gml="objects/oVampire/step.gml:129" />. It runs at
<Stat id="vampire.charge-speed" /> (<Stat id="vampire.charge-speed" as="per-second" />). At a wall it jumps,
or turns around if there's a ceiling over it, and at the edge of a ledge it jumps, 3–6 pixels per step up
<Cite gml="objects/oVampire/step.gml:131-163" /> <Cite gml="objects/oVampire/step.gml:189-199" />. In the air
after a jump it may turn into a bat (1 in 100 each step, or at once over water); when it lands, it stands for
40–100 steps (1.33–3.33 seconds) <Cite gml="objects/oVampire/step.gml:164-188" />.

**As a bat.** A charging Vampire turns into a bat when the player is above it and less than 32 pixels away
across, unless it's in water <Cite gml="objects/oVampire/step.gml:159" />. As a bat it flies at the player
at <Stat id="vampire.flight-speed" /> (<Stat id="vampire.flight-speed" as="per-second" />) while he's
within <Stat id="vampire.chase-range" />, never down into water; farther away, or while he's swimming or
dead, it flies up to a ceiling and hangs, like a [[bat|Bat]], until he's within 90 pixels and more than 16
below it <Cite gml="objects/oVampire/step.gml:262-345" />. As soon as the bat touches the ground or water,
it's a Vampire on foot again <Cite gml="objects/oVampire/step.gml:328-331" />. The clip shows a Vampire
charging at the player on a ledge above it, turning into a bat beneath him, and flying up to bite him.

A Vampire only acts while it's on screen or just off it <Cite gml="objects/oVampire/step.gml:3-5" />.

## Damage

When the player touches a Vampire that isn't stunned, within 8 pixels of its middle across and without
stomping it, he loses <Stat id="vampire.damage" />, is knocked away from it (and
upward, if he's above it), and blinks, invincible, for 30 steps (1 second) <Cite gml="objects/oVampire/collision_oCharacter.gml:1-4" />
<Cite gml="objects/oVampire/collision_oCharacter.gml:24-41" />.

Each bite also puts a drop of blood where the player is, and any drop of blood that touches a Vampire is
absorbed and heals it 1 hit point, with no limit <Cite gml="objects/oVampire/collision_oCharacter.gml:36" />
<Cite gml="objects/oVampire/collision_oBlood.gml:1-2" />. A bite's blood usually lands on the Vampire that
bit: in the attack clip, two bites take the player's hearts from 4 to 2 and heal the Vampire from 6 to 8
<Cite clip="vampire/attack" />.

## Defeating

<Clip id="vampire/whip" />

Most hits stun a Vampire as well as hurting it, as they do a Caveman: it's knocked back and lies on the
ground for <Stat id="vampire.stun-time" /> (<Stat id="vampire.stun-time" as="seconds" />), counted down
while it's on the ground or carried, and then gets up if it has hit points left
<Cite gml="objects/oVampire/step.gml:200-240" />. A Vampire bleeds no blood of its own, so the Whip's hits
don't heal it
<Cite gml="objects/oVampire/create.gml:28" /> <Cite gml="scripts/scrCreateBlood.gml:24" />. The clip shows
the Whip stunning a charging Vampire, which gets up, charges again and bites the player, and the blood
puts back the hit point the Whip took.

A Vampire whose hit points run out dies at once, or, if it's stunned, once it comes to rest
<Cite gml="objects/oVampire/step.gml:44-47" /> <Cite gml="objects/oVampire/step.gml:347-352" />. It leaves
no body: it falls apart into three bones and a [[skull|Skull]], and leaves a [[cape|Cape]], free to pick up
<Cite gml="objects/oVampire/step.gml:241-261" />. The clip shows a Bomb landing beside a Vampire the Whip has
just stunned; the explosion flings it away, and where it comes to rest it dies and leaves its Cape. Vampires
killed count toward the player's kills, and the screen between levels draws a Vampire icon for each
<Cite gml="objects/oVampire/step.gml:250-255" /> <Cite gml="objects/oTransition/alarm_0.gml:167-172" />. One
that Spikes, Lava or a Spear Trap killed doesn't count <Cite gml="objects/oEnemy/step.gml:68" />
<Cite gml="objects/oEnemy/step.gml:101" /> <Cite gml="objects/oEnemy/step.gml:120" />.

A stunned Vampire can be picked up and thrown, and one left lying still on Kali's altar for 20 steps is
sacrificed, adding <Stat id="vampire.favor" /> to her favour, unless it's already −8 or less
<Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:1300-1303" /> <Cite gml="objects/oEnemy/step.gml:130-167" />. One that
gets up in the player's hands drops out of them <Cite gml="objects/oVampire/step.gml:227-239" />.

## Locations

Level generation makes Vampires only on [[restless-dead|graveyard levels]], which 1 in 10 levels of
[[jungle|the Jungle]] is <Cite gml="scripts/scrInitLevel.gml:147-148" />
<Cite gml="scripts/scrClearGlobals.gml:56" />. On top of each block with open space above it (no block,
monster or Spikes there), outside the starting room and shops, a graveyard level makes a [[zombie|Zombie]]
with a 1 in 25 chance, or failing that a Vampire with a 1 in 160 chance
<Cite gml="scripts/scrEntityGen.gml:276-290" />.

Vampires also appear in:

- **The Sun Room**, the minigame behind the door the high-score room shows once the best money score is
  $200,000 or more. The player scores a point a second, and every 100–200 steps a lit Bomb drops in, or,
  from 50 points, a Vampire already in bat form (from 80, the Ghost comes instead) <Cite gml="objects/oHighscores/create.gml:19" /> <Cite gml="objects/oHighscores/create.gml:32-38" /> <Cite gml="objects/oSunRoom/alarm_11.gml:1-5" />
  <Cite gml="objects/oSunRoom/alarm_0.gml:9-48" />.
- **Custom levels**, as the [[level-editor|level editor]]'s tile `A`
  <Cite gml="scripts/scrCreateTileObj.gml:168-171" />.

## Tips

- A Vampire turns into a bat when the player is above it, so a ledge doesn't keep the player out of reach.
- With the Whip alone it takes six hits, each after it gets back up, and each bite in between undoes one.
  A Bomb or two bullets kill it outright, and an arrow takes 4 hit points.
- A stunned Vampire can be carried and thrown at another monster, or sacrificed to Kali.
- Killing a Vampire is a way to get a Cape for nothing.
