Wearing it, the player can turn a fall into a slow glide: pressing jump in mid-air opens it, and he sinks a
pixel a step and lands without harm, however far he's dropped. [[clothing-shop|Clothing Shops]],
[[rare-shop|Rare Shops]] and the [[dice-house|Dice House]] offer it; [[crate|Crates]], blocks,
[[vampire|Vampires]] and Kali give it away. It's worn from the moment it's picked up or paid for until the
game ends, unless a [[jetpack|Jetpack]] takes its place.

## Effect

<Clip id="cape/glide" />

Picking up a free cape, paying for one or carrying one out of a shop puts it on: the message "YOU GOT A
CAPE!" shows, the cape disappears, and an icon of it joins the row under the hearts
<Cite gml="scripts/scrStealItem.gml:204-213" /> <Cite gml="scripts/scrDrawHUD.gml:150-154" />. From then on
the player wears it on his back <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:13" />
<Cite gml="objects/oCape/step.gml:23-47" />. It stays on until a new game starts, which clears it
<Cite gml="scripts/scrClearGlobals.gml:96" /> <Cite gml="objects/oTransition/create.gml:20-24" />.

It opens and closes with the jump key. In the air, a press of jump opens it if it's closed and closes it if
it's open <Cite gml="scripts/characterStepEvent.gml:320-324" />, as long as the player didn't leave the
ground with that press still held: walking off a ledge readies it at once, and after a jump it's ready once
the jump key is let go of <Cite gml="scripts/characterStepEvent.gml:257-264" />
<Cite gml="scripts/characterStepEvent.gml:284-292" />.

Open, it halves the player's falling speed each step <Cite gml="scripts/characterStepEvent.gml:872-875" />,
and the count of steps he's been falling starts again from 0 on every step, so the landing never hurts
<Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:15-18" />. With gravity adding 1 pixel a step, he settles at 1 pixel a
step (30 per second), against up to 10 in a free fall <Cite gml="scripts/characterCreateEvent.gml:59-62" />.
It closes again when he lands <Cite gml="scripts/characterStepEvent.gml:288-292" />, climbs
<Cite gml="scripts/characterStepEvent.gml:185-190" />, hangs from a ledge
<Cite gml="scripts/characterStepEvent.gml:494-496" /> or goes into water
<Cite gml="scripts/characterStepEvent.gml:750-752" />.
The clip shows the player running out of a shop with the cape, which puts it on, and off the edge of a gap
in the floor: one press of jump in mid-air opens it, and he sinks 1 pixel a step to the ledge below and lands
with all his hearts <Cite clip="cape/glide" />.

cape he holds out in front of him <Cite replay="fidelity/replays/item-seed-35-cape.txt" />.

## Interactions

- The Cape and the [[jetpack|Jetpack]] are never worn together. Putting on the Cape while wearing a Jetpack
  drops the Jetpack at the player's feet, free to pick up again; putting on a Jetpack drops the Cape the same
  way <Cite gml="scripts/scrStealItem.gml:214-221" /> <Cite gml="scripts/scrStealItem.gml:233-241" />.
- The [[parachute|Parachute]] slows a fall the same way, but opens by itself and only once
  <Cite gml="scripts/characterStepEvent.gml:867-875" />.
- It does nothing closed: a long fall with the Cape closed hurts as it would without it
  <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:268-289" />.

## Tips

- On any drop too deep to land from safely, a press of jump in the air opens the Cape and makes the landing
  safe; after a jump, the key has to be let go of first.
- Pressing jump again closes it, which drops the player at full speed: a way down faster when the ground is
  close.
