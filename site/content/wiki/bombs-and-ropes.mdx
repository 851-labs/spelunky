The game keeps the player's bombs and ropes as two numbers, drawn beside the hearts at the top of the screen,
rather than as items he carries. A [[bomb|Bomb]] or a [[rope|Rope]] only becomes an item when he uses one,
and goes back into its count if he takes it out unused. Both counts carry over from level to level and
start again with each game. The items' own pages say what a bomb and a rope do; this one is about how many
the player has.

## How it works

### The counts

The HUD draws the bombs beside a bomb icon (a sticky bomb's, once the player has [[paste|Paste]]) and the
ropes beside a rope icon, each as the plain number <Cite gml="scripts/scrDrawHUD.gml:36-40" />. Nothing
else keeps them: a bomb or rope the player isn't holding exists only as the count.

**Starting.** A new game sets each to <Stat id="bomb.start" />, or to
<Stat id="bombs-and-ropes.tunnel-man-bombs" bare /> for the [[tunnel-man|Tunnel Man]]
<Cite gml="scripts/scrClearGlobals.gml:103-114" />. The [[tutorial|Tutorial]] starts with no bombs and 2 ropes
<Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/other_animation_end.gml:76-94" />, and a custom level from the
[[level-editor|level editor]] with the counts its author set, each from 0 to 99
<Cite gml="scripts/scrLoadLevel.gml:143-148" />.

**The cap.** At the start of each of the player's steps, a count above <Stat id="bomb.max" bare /> is cut back
to <Stat id="bomb.max" bare /> <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:10-11" />. Nothing is lost
before that, so a bundle picked up near the cap still adds what it can.

### Using them

Each use takes one from its count:

- **The bomb key** throws a lit bomb, if the count is above 0 <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:1137-1174" />
  ([[bomb#throwing-it]]).
- **The rope key** throws a rope up, or drops it down a ledge with down held
  <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:1045-1131" /> ([[rope#usage]]).
- **The item key** takes a bomb into the player's hands, unlit; pressed again, it puts the bomb back and
  takes out a rope, and a third time puts the rope back and brings back what he held. With no bombs it
  goes straight to a rope, and with neither, or with a heavy item or a shop's ware in his hands, it does
  nothing. A lit bomb can't be put away
  <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:954-1043" /> ([[bomb#in-the-hands]]).

At an exit, an unlit bomb or a rope in the player's hands goes back into its count; a lit one is left behind
<Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:821-841" />.

### Getting more

The bundles add to the counts when they're taken: a free one when the player touches it, a shop's when he
buys it or carries it out <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:2020-2062" />
<Cite gml="scripts/scrStealItem.gml:68-111" />.

| Source | Gives | |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [[bomb-bag\|Bomb Bag]] | <Stat id="bomb-bag.bombs" /> bombs | <Cite gml="scripts/scrStealItem.gml:70" /> |
| [[bomb-box\|Bomb Box]] | <Stat id="bomb-box.bombs" /> bombs | <Cite gml="scripts/scrStealItem.gml:82" /> |
| [[rope-pile\|Rope Pile]] | <Stat id="rope-pile.ropes" /> ropes | <Cite gml="scripts/scrStealItem.gml:105" /> |
| [[kalis-favour\|Kali]]'s third gift, at a favour of <Stat id="kali.gift-3" bare /> | Bombs set to <Stat id="bombs-and-ropes.kali-bombs" bare />, if the player has fewer than <Stat id="bombs-and-ropes.kali-bombs-below" bare />; otherwise hearts | <Cite gml="scripts/scrGetFavorMsg.gml:39-50" /> |

The bundles come from:

- **Shops.** The [[general-store|General Store]] sells Bomb Bags and Rope Piles
  <Cite gml="scripts/scrShopItemsGen.gml:173-174" />, the [[bomb-shop|Bomb Shop]] and the
  [[weapon-shop|Weapon Shop]] Bomb Bags and Bomb Boxes <Cite gml="scripts/scrShopItemsGen.gml:24-67" />, the
  [[clothing-shop|Clothing Shop]] Rope Piles <Cite gml="scripts/scrShopItemsGen.gml:74" /> and the
  [[rare-shop|Rare Shop]] Bomb Boxes <Cite gml="scripts/scrShopItemsGen.gml:108" />. A Bomb Box is also the
  [[dice-house|Dice House]]'s prize when the rolls for the others fail <Cite gml="scripts/scrGenerateItem.gml:61" />.
- **[[crate|Crates]].** Mostly a Rope Pile or a Bomb Bag, at even odds, once every other item's roll fails.
  Before those, a Bomb Box's is 1 in 40, once the fourteen rolls ahead of it, from the Jetpack's to the
  Machete's, fail. A Crate in the Tutorial always holds a Bomb Bag <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:616-636" />.
- **The [[yeti-king|Yeti King]]**, which leaves four items when it dies, each a Rope Pile one time in two
  once its 1 in 12 for [[spike-shoes|Spike Shoes]] fails, and a [[big-sapphire|Big Sapphire]] otherwise
  <Cite gml="objects/oYetiKing/step.gml:23-40" />.
- **Kali.** Her first gift is a Bomb Box once the player has a [[jetpack|Jetpack]] and every other piece of
  equipment she gives <Cite gml="scripts/scrGetFavorMsg.gml:85-105" />.

### Losing them

Apart from using them, a [[monkey|Monkey]] on the player's back takes them: when it doesn't throw away some
of his money, it throws away a rope half the time, if he has one, or else a bomb, lit one time in ten
<Cite gml="objects/oMonkey/step.gml:244-274" />. The rope or bomb lands loose in the level, where he can
pick it up again ([[rope#locations]], [[bomb#locations]]).

## Quirks

- **A bomb arrow costs no bomb.** The bomb key readies one on a drawn [[bow|Bow]] while the player has a bomb,
  but takes none from the count: [[free-bomb-arrows]] <Cite quirk="1" />
  <Cite replay="fidelity/replays/weapon-bow-bomb-arrow-free.txt" />.
- **A lit bomb the player didn't light goes back into the count.** One lit by anything but the player, a
  Monkey or the Sun Room isn't marked as lit, so carried out of a level it's counted as an unlit bomb:
  [[unarmed-bombs]] <Cite quirk="1" /> <Cite replay="fidelity/replays/item-chest-trap-bomb-unarmed.txt" />.
