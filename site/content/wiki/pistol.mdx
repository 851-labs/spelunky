Its bullet is the [[shotgun|Shotgun]]'s pellet, one at a time: it takes <Stat id="pistol.damage" />
from any monster it hits, and the Pistol can fire again after <Stat id="pistol.cooldown" />, half the
Shotgun's wait, with a smaller kick. [[weapon-shop|Weapon Shops]] and the [[dice-house|Dice House]] sell
it, and [[crate|Crates]] and buried blocks give it away.

## Usage

<Clip id="pistol/shopkeeper" />

The Pistol is picked up like any item, crouching over it and pressing the attack key. While it's held, the
attack key fires it, on the ground, in the air, hanging or climbing; crouching, the key puts it down in
front of the player instead <Cite gml="scripts/scrUseItem.gml:216-275" />. It isn't thrown. A shot fires one
bullet at <Stat id="pistol.bullet-speed" />, never slower than 6 forward, straight ahead, knocks the player
1 pixel a step back and 1 up unless he's hanging or climbing, and starts the wait
of <Stat id="pistol.cooldown" /> before the next <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:175" />.

The bullet flies in a straight line, without falling, until it hits a block, a monster, a [[damsel|Damsel]] or a
player <Cite gml="objects/oBullet/step.gml:1-2" />. In the clip, the player pays for a Pistol and fires it
at the [[shopkeeper|Shopkeeper]] beside him, who loses 4 of his 20 hit points and falls stunned; turning,
he shoots a [[bat|Bat]] out of the air. The Shopkeeper gets up angry and knocks him down, and the player
drops the Pistol <Cite clip="pistol/shopkeeper" />.

The Pistol goes with the player through a level's exit if he's holding it, and is in his hands on the next
level <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:842-850" /> <Cite gml="objects/oLevel/create.gml:25-30" />. He
drops it when he's stunned, or when he teeters too long at a ledge's
edge <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:329-345" /> <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:150-171" />.

## Damage

The bullet's code is its own, so every monster takes the same <Stat id="pistol.damage" />, with no wait,
stunned or not; a [[caveman|Caveman]], [[yeti|Yeti]], [[hawkman|Hawkman]] or Shopkeeper still alive is also
stunned for 20 steps, and the [[yeti-king|Yeti King]] and the [[tomb-lord|Tomb Lord]] are knocked back half
as far <Cite gml="objects/oBullet/collision_oEnemy.gml:1-36" />. The [[shotgun#damage|Shotgun's
table]] has the rest; a Pistol's bullet is one of its pellets.

## Interactions

- **[[shopkeeper|Shopkeepers]].** A shot stuns a Shopkeeper for 20 steps; when he gets up he's angry, as in
  the clip <Cite clip="pistol/shopkeeper" />, and his wares are free, with no theft
  ([[hit-shopkeeper-free-wares]]) <Cite quirk="1" />.
- **[[jar|Jars]] and [[skull|Skulls]].** A bullet breaks either <Cite gml="objects/oJar/collision_oBullet.gml:1" />.

## Tips

- Most monsters have 4 hit points or fewer, so one bullet kills them, and the Pistol's shorter wait lets it
  fire at the next one sooner than the Shotgun can.

## Trivia
