Spikes stand on top of a block, in pits and along floors, in [[mines|the Mines]], [[jungle|the Jungle]] and
[[ice-caves|the Ice Caves]]. They
don't move and never wear out: they hurt only what falls onto them, and kill it outright, the player
included. Walking over them on level ground is safe.

## How it works

<Clip id="spikes/fall" />

Spikes do nothing on their own: the player's and the monsters' code each step checks whether they're falling
onto some. The player dies on them if, touching them, he's moving down and has been falling for more than 4
steps, or is stunned <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:1682-1693" />. The clip shows both sides of the rule:
the player walks across two Spikes on the floor unhurt, then walks off a ledge onto a row of them one block
down and dies as he lands.

A player who dies on them stays on them, sinking slowly into the points, and drips blood
<Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:1685-1691" /> <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:1810-1813" />. The
Spikes he dies on turn bloody <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:1705-1709" />.

## Damage

| Victim | What happens | |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The player | Loses 99 hearts, every heart he can have, if he has been falling for more than 4 steps or is stunned | <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:1693-1703" /> |
| Most monsters | Killed (hit points set to 0) if falling faster than 2 pixels a step | <Cite gml="objects/oEnemy/step.gml:108-128" /> |
| A [[damsel\|Damsel]] | Killed if falling faster than 2 pixels a step | <Cite gml="objects/oDamsel/step.gml:93-110" /> |

A death on Spikes is counted in the end-of-game statistics' spikes row
<Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:1697" />. A monster killed on them doesn't count as the player's kill
<Cite gml="objects/oEnemy/step.gml:120" />, except a [[shopkeeper|Shopkeeper]], who counts and makes the player a
murderer ([[accidental-murder]]) <Cite quirk="1" row="Any Shopkeeper's death marks the player a murderer, whatever killed him." /> <Cite replay="fidelity/replays/shop-seed-35-spikes-murderer.txt" />
<Cite clip="quirks/shopkeeper-spikes-murderer" />. The monsters' check is in the code most monsters share, so
those whose own step event replaces it, such as the [[bat|Bat]] and the [[giant-spider|Giant Spider]], fall onto
Spikes unharmed.

## Locations

Spikes are placed by the room templates' spike tiles, on top of a block. Most of these tiles hold Spikes with
a 1 in 3 chance; some are always Spikes:

| Area | Spikes | |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [[mines\|The Mines]] | 1 in 3 for each spike tile | <Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGen.gml:343" /> |
| [[jungle\|The Jungle]] | 1 in 3 for each spike tile, and always on the floor of one pit room, all spike tiles | <Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGen2.gml:351-352" /> |
| [[black-market\|The Black Market]] | As the Jungle | <Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGenMarket.gml:266-267" /> |
| [[ice-caves\|The Ice Caves]] | Always, on every spike tile | <Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGen3.gml:307" /> |
| [[yeti-lair\|A Yeti lair]] | 1 in 3 for each spike tile, and always on its own spike-floored tiles | <Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGenYeti.gml:317" /> <Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGenYeti.gml:423-426" /> |

They're also in the Moon challenge room, a bonus room <Cite room="rMoon" />, and in custom levels as the
[[level-editor|level editor]]'s tile `^` <Cite gml="scripts/scrCreateTileObj.gml:225-228" />.

## Avoiding

- **Walk, don't drop.** Walking across Spikes on level ground is safe. Jumping onto them, or walking off a
  ledge onto them, is a fall of more than 4 steps, which kills.
- **Don't land on them stunned.** A stunned player dies on Spikes however short his fall, so a hit that
  knocks him onto them kills him.
- **Remove the block under them.** Spikes are destroyed with the block they stand on
  <Cite gml="objects/oSolid/destroy.gml:9-13" />, and a [[bomb|Bomb]]'s blast or a [[mattock|Mattock]]
  removes any Spikes left with nothing under them <Cite gml="objects/oExplosion/collision_oSolid.gml:22-28" />
  <Cite gml="objects/oMattockHit/other_animation_end.gml:30-36" />.
- **[[spike-shoes|Spike Shoes]] don't help**: the player's check for Spikes doesn't look at them.

## Interactions

- **[[boulder|Boulders]]** destroy the Spikes on the blocks they break <Cite gml="objects/oBoulder/collision_oSolid.gml:16-23" />.
- **Treasure.** [[level-generation|Level generation]] doesn't put gold or gems on a block with Spikes on it
  <Cite gml="scripts/scrTreasureGen.gml:32-37" />, and the locked chest of the [[udjat-eye|Udjat Eye]]'s level
  avoids them too <Cite gml="scripts/scrEntityGen.gml:81-93" />.
- **A stunned monster thrown onto them** dies, if it runs the shared code, as on any fast fall onto Spikes.

## Trivia

- Spikes have no code of their own beyond being breakable: everything they do is in the code of what falls
  onto them <Cite gml="objects/oSpikes/create.gml:1-3" />.
- Level generation once lined the bottom of the Ice Caves with Spikes: the line is still there, commented out,
  beside the dark rock it lays instead <Cite gml="scripts/scrInitLevel.gml:131-137" />.
