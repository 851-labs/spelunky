Climbing the [[title-screen|title screen]]'s rope to the top is the way out of Spelunky: the credits roll and the game quits. At the
top, the player is swapped for a figure that climbs on up out of sight. Playing as the [[damsel|Damsel]], she
climbs the rope as herself, but that figure is the [[spelunker|Spelunker]]: an `else` meant for the Damsel's check
belongs to the Tunnel Man's.

## What happens

Playing as the Damsel, the player climbs the title screen's rope to quit. She climbs as the Damsel, but at the top
the figure that climbs on out of sight is the Spelunker <Cite clip="quirks/damsel-title-rope-spelunker" />.

<Clip id="quirks/damsel-title-rope-spelunker" />

## Why

When the player climbs to the top of the title screen's rope, `oPlayer1`'s step replaces him with the climbing
figure, `oPDummy5` <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:49-60" />, whose step moves it up and, once it's out of
sight, fades to the credits <Cite gml="objects/oPDummy5/step.gml:1-8" />. Its create event picks its sprite:

```gml objects/oPDummy5/create.gml:6-8
if (global.isDamsel) sprite_index = sDamselClimb2;
if (global.isTunnelMan) sprite_index = sTunnelClimb2;
else sprite_index = sClimbUp2;
```

The `else` belongs to the second `if`, the Tunnel Man's, not to the Damsel's. So for the Damsel the first line
sets her climbing sprite, and the third replaces it at once with the Spelunker's. For the
[[tunnel-man|Tunnel Man]] the `else` is skipped, and he climbs out as himself <Cite quirk="1" />.

## Reproducing it

Save 8 Damsels in one game, so that the [[high-scores-room|high scores room]] has the Changing Room's door
<Cite gml="objects/oHighscores/create.gml:86-93" />. Go through it to become the Damsel
<Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/other_animation_end.gml:173-191" />, go back to the title screen, and climb its rope
to the top. The replay starts with 8 saves: the player goes through the Changing Room's door and comes out as the
Damsel, then climbs the title screen's rope as her. At the top the figure that replaces her is the Spelunker, and
the credits follow <Cite replay="fidelity/replays/title-damsel-rope.txt" />.

## In the port

The spelunky.ai port reproduces it exactly: it's in docs/quirks.md's list of the 1.1 source's bugs
<Cite quirk="1" />, and the oracle's fingerprint of the replay pins the figure's sprite. No option turns it off.
