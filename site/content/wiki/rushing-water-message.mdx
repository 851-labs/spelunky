A [[flooded-level|flooded level]] says "I CAN HEAR RUSHING WATER...", first, or after a
[[restless-dead|graveyard]]'s or a [[dark-level|dark level]]'s message. A level that's all three shows three
messages, and the lake's, the last, comes from a timer of its own, which words it "YOU HEAR RUSHING WATER..."
instead. Nothing else is different; only the wording gives the mix of levels away.

## What happens

Level 8 of the [[jungle|Jungle]] is dark, a graveyard and flooded at once. After the dark level's message comes
the graveyard's, "THE DEAD ARE RESTLESS!", and then the lake's, as "YOU HEAR RUSHING WATER..."
<Cite clip="quirks/dark-graveyard-lake-you-hear" />.

<Clip id="quirks/dark-graveyard-lake-you-hear" />

## Why

The player's alarms show a level's messages, one after another. Alarm 0 shows the first: on a dark level the
dark level's, which sets alarm 1 for the next <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/alarm_0.gml:5-13" />, and on a flooded
level that's nothing else, "I CAN HEAR RUSHING WATER..." <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/alarm_0.gml:34-39" />.
Alarm 1 shows the second, and only its graveyard branch, which needs the level to be dark too, sets a timer for
a third:

```gml objects/oPlayer1/alarm_1.gml:11-23
else if (global.cemetary and global.darkLevel)
{
    global.message = "THE DEAD ARE RESTLESS!";
    global.message2 = "";
    global.messageTimer = 200;
    if (global.lake) alarm[4] = 210;
}
else if (global.lake)
{
    global.message = "I CAN HEAR RUSHING WATER...";
    global.message2 = "";
    global.messageTimer = 200;
}
```

That timer is alarm 4, which has the lake's message in other words:

```gml objects/oPlayer1/alarm_4.gml:1-6
if (global.lake)
{
    global.message = "YOU HEAR RUSHING WATER...";
    global.message2 = "";
    global.messageTimer = 200;
}
```

A dark flooded level without the graveyard gets the lake's message from alarm 1, and a lit flooded graveyard,
whose message alarm 0 shows, gets it from alarm 1 too <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/alarm_0.gml:27-33" />: both
say "I CAN HEAR". Only a level that's all three reaches alarm 4 <Cite quirk="1" />.

## Reproducing it

It takes a Jungle level that wins three separate rolls. The lake is 1 in 10, rolled for levels 5 to 8 entered
from the screen between levels, and never again once the player has gone through the
[[black-market|Black Market]]'s door <Cite gml="objects/oTransition/step.gml:18-24" />; the graveyard is 1 in 10, and the dark level 1 in 12, but only
while the area has had none and the level before's clock read more than 20 seconds past the minute
<Cite gml="scripts/scrInitLevel.gml:147-176" />. So at most 1 such level in 1,200 says it, and fewer for the dark
roll's conditions <Cite quirk="1" />. The replay, with seed 10, waits at level 7's exit until level 8 comes up all
three: the dark level's message shows, then the graveyard's 209 steps later, then "YOU HEAR RUSHING WATER..."
another 209 steps later, and "I CAN HEAR RUSHING WATER..." never does
<Cite replay="fidelity/replays/jungle-seed-10-dark-graveyard-lake.txt" />.

## In the port

The spelunky.ai port reproduces it exactly: it's in docs/quirks.md's list of the 1.1 source's bugs
<Cite quirk="1" />, and the oracle's fingerprint of the replay pins each message and the step it shows on. No
option turns it off.
