Ice is one of [[ice-caves|the Ice Caves]]' two kinds of rock, the other being their dark blue stone. It's
solid like any block, and breaks like one, but it's slippery: the player keeps nearly all his speed from one
step to the next on it, so he slides rather than stops. Now and then a [[caveman|Caveman]] is frozen inside a
block.

## How it works

<Clip id="ice/slide" />

When the player stands on ice, the friction that slows him each step is <Stat id="ice.friction" bare />,
the share of his speed he keeps, where on other ground it's <Stat id="ice.ground-friction" bare /> <Cite gml="scripts/characterStepEvent.gml:116" /> <Cite gml="scripts/characterStepEvent.gml:774-778" />
<Cite gml="scripts/characterCreateEvent.gml:83" />. It doesn't apply while he's ducking, or when he wears
[[spike-shoes|Spike Shoes]], and then ice is like any ground. What counts is the block just under his middle.

The clip shows the difference. The player runs on rock and lets go: he stops 4 pixels on, in 6 steps. Then he
runs onto ice and lets go: he slides on for 136 pixels, eight and a half blocks, and takes about 125 steps to
stop <Cite clip="ice/slide" />.

A stunned or dead player's body bouncing to a stop on ice keeps 0.8 of its speed at each bounce, where on
other ground it keeps 0.3 <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:362-372" />.

### Breaking it

Ice breaks like any block, to a [[bomb|Bomb]] or a [[mattock|Mattock]], but it leaves water drops rather than
rubble <Cite gml="objects/oIce/destroy.gml:1-6" />. The [[yeti-king|Yeti King]]'s roar breaks it too: each step
of the roar, each block of ice within 96 pixels of him, but not right under him, has a 1 in 60 chance of
coming loose as a falling block of ice <Cite gml="objects/oYetiKing/step.gml:113-128" />.

### Frozen Cavemen

Each block of ice the game makes has a 1 in <Stat id="ice.frozen-caveman" bare /> chance of a Caveman frozen
inside it, which does nothing <Cite gml="objects/oIce/create.gml:3" />. Breaking the block frees him: he's made
as a [[caveman|Caveman]], stunned, and can't be hurt for 20 steps <Cite gml="objects/oIce/destroy.gml:8-14" />
<Cite gml="objects/oFrozenCaveman/destroy.gml:1-7" />.

## Interactions

- **[[thin-ice|Thin Ice]]** is another thing: it isn't slippery, and it wears away under the player.
- **[[spike-shoes|Spike Shoes]]** make ice like any ground for the player who wears them.

## Tips

- Ducking stops a slide: the ice's slipperiness doesn't apply to a ducking player.
- Letting go at a walking run on ice carries the player eight blocks or more, over an edge if there's one in the way.
