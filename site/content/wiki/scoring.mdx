The game has no points. Treasure adds to the player's money and the shops take from it; the screen between
levels counts what he made on the level, and the game over and ending screens show the money as the final
score. The high scores room, reached by a door on the title screen, keeps the best of four records and three
statistics, gives a trophy for each record, and opens a bonus room for each gold one
<Cite replay="fidelity/replays/title-doors.txt" />.

## How it works

### Money

Picking up treasure adds its value, more in later areas: a quarter of it again, rounded up, for each area
after the Mines <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:1990-1997" /> (the treasure's pages give each value, such
as the [[gold-nugget|Gold Nugget]]'s and the [[gold-bar|Gold Bar]]'s). It's counted into the money shown at
the top of the screen 100 a step, after a pause of 20 steps for each piece picked up (100 at most)
<Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:1953-1971" /> <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:1997-1998" />, and what's left goes in
at once when the player takes the exit <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:862-865" />. Buying in a shop
([[general-store]]) and donating to the [[tunnel-man|Tunnel Man]] take from it. Winning adds
<Stat id="endings.bonus" /> ([[endings]]).

### The clock

The game's clock runs only in a level, while the player is alive and the game has started: it gains 30 each
step <Cite gml="objects/oGame/step.gml:14-21" />, and the screens show it in minutes and seconds of a
thousand each <Cite gml="objects/oTransition/draw.gml:91-116" />. So a second on the clock is 33⅓ steps,
about 1.11 seconds. It counts the level's time and the game's, and stops while the game is paused and between
levels, as nothing steps then ([[controls#pausing]]). A win's time on it is the game's speed record, and
[[speedrunning-basics]] walks through a fast one.

### Between levels

<Clip id="scoring/level-tally" />

The screen between levels says LEVEL N COMPLETED! and tallies the level: its time and the game's, the loot,
the kills, and the money made on the level and in all <Cite gml="objects/oTransition/draw.gml:74-126" />. The
loot is an icon for each piece of treasure picked up, then each [[damsel|Damsel]] rescued, each [[scarab|Scarab]],
[[golden-idol|idol]] and [[crystal-skull|Crystal Skull]] <Cite gml="objects/oTransition/alarm_0.gml:23-113" />; the kills an icon for each monster
killed <Cite gml="objects/oTransition/alarm_0.gml:114-275" />. Icons appear every 3 steps and the money counts
up 100 a step; attack (or start) hurries both <Cite gml="objects/oTransition/alarm_0.gml:286-291" />
<Cite gml="objects/oTransition/step.gml:69-83" />. In the clip the player has picked up a gold bar and killed
four Snakes on the first level <Cite clip="scoring/level-tally" />.

### Game over

When the player dies without an [[ankh|Ankh]], the high scores are updated at once, and the game over screen
counts his money up as the final score <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:1897-1915" />
<Cite gml="objects/oGame/draw.gml:1-24" />. Attack or start then goes to the high scores room
<Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:907-930" />.

### The high scores

The records are kept in GameMaker's high score table, with large offsets to keep them in order
<Cite gml="scripts/scrResetHighscores.gml:24-36" />. They're updated at a death, at the end of a win's
credits, and when a bonus room's game ends <Cite gml="scripts/scrUpdateHighscores.gml:28-117" />:

| Record | Kept | |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Money | The most | <Cite gml="scripts/scrUpdateHighscores.gml:47-52" /> |
| Kills | The most | <Cite gml="scripts/scrUpdateHighscores.gml:64-69" /> |
| Saves (Damsels rescued) | The most | <Cite gml="scripts/scrUpdateHighscores.gml:71-76" /> |
| Time | The least, from a win without a shortcut | <Cite gml="scripts/scrUpdateHighscores.gml:55-62" /> |
| Plays | +1 a game, not counting the bonus rooms | <Cite gml="scripts/scrUpdateHighscores.gml:78-79" /> |
| Wins, deaths | +1 a win, +1 a death | <Cite gml="scripts/scrUpdateHighscores.gml:81-95" /> |
| The Sun, Moon and Stars Rooms | Each one's best | <Cite gml="scripts/scrUpdateHighscores.gml:101-117" /> |

A record beaten in the last game has NEW beside it <Cite gml="objects/oHighscores/create.gml:120-123" />.
Custom levels keep no score <Cite gml="objects/oLoadLevel/create.gml:5" />.

### The high scores room

<Clip id="scoring/reset" />

The room lists the money, kills and saves as TOP DEFILERS, with the best time once the game has been won,
and the plays, deaths and wins as STATISTICS <Cite gml="objects/oHighscores/draw.gml:119-148" />. Left of
the records is a column of trophies, one for each record good enough ([Numbers](#numbers)), and beside each
gold trophy a door to its bonus room <Cite gml="objects/oHighscores/create.gml:32-102" />. Climbing up beside
them, the board shows SECRET CHALLENGES: the bonus rooms' best scores, and each room's goal, or LOCKED
<Cite gml="objects/oHighscores/draw.gml:1-112" />. With all four gold trophies, the title screen has a trophy
and a door in place of the [[tunnel-man|Tunnel Man]] <Cite gml="objects/oTitle/create.gml:54-62" />.

A block on the room's floor resets everything. Pushed past the middle, it brings up THIS WILL CLEAR
EVERYTHING! <Cite gml="objects/oHighscores/draw.gml:150-159" />; pushed onto the button at the end, it takes
the trophies and the doors away, and 18 steps later every record and statistic, and the Tunnel Man's
shortcuts, go back to the start, and the room shakes
<Cite gml="objects/oButtonHighscore/step.gml:1-32" /> <Cite gml="scripts/scrResetHighscores.gml:24-36" />.
The clip shows it with all four gold trophies <Cite clip="scoring/reset" />.

## Quirks

- **The Moon Room's goal shows for any winning time.** Climbing up beside the trophies, the room's
  description reads as unlocked when the best time is at most 600,000, while the time is kept in seconds, so
  any win's time unlocks it there <Cite gml="objects/oHighscores/create.gml:16" />
  <Cite gml="objects/oHighscores/draw.gml:46" />. Its door needs <Stat id="scoring.time-gold" bare /> seconds
  or less <Cite gml="objects/oHighscores/create.gml:61-64" /> <Cite quirk="1" />. In the clip, with one win
  at 11:40, the Changing Room and the Stars Room read LOCKED., but the Moon Room's goal shows beside the
  silver trophy, with no door <Cite clip="quirks/moon-room-goal-any-win" />
  <Cite replay="fidelity/replays/flow-highscores-moon-room-slow-win.txt" />.
- **A time of exactly <Stat id="scoring.time-bronze" bare /> seconds earns no trophy.** The check around the
  time's trophies is "under", the bronze one inside it "at most" <Cite gml="objects/oHighscores/create.gml:50-56" />
  <Cite quirk="1" />. A win at 16:00 leaves the trophy's place empty
  <Cite replay="fidelity/replays/flow-highscores-time-960-no-trophy.txt" />.
- Not every kill counts: a [[damsel|Damsel]] killed on spikes or in lava isn't one <Cite quirk="1" />.
