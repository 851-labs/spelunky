Level generation puts Cavemen on the ground in [[mines|the Mines]], where they're rare, and in
[[jungle|the Jungle]] and [[temple|the Temple]]. A Caveman wanders until it sees the player, then charges in
a straight line. With <Stat id="caveman.hp" />, it survives most single hits, which stun it instead: a
stunned or dead Caveman can be picked up, thrown at other monsters, or sacrificed to [[kali|Kali]].

## Behaviour

A Caveman sets off walking in a random direction as soon as it's made, turning around at walls <Cite
gml="objects/oCaveman/create.gml:24" /> <Cite gml="objects/oCaveman/step.gml:63-68" /> <Cite
gml="objects/oCaveman/step.gml:80-107" />. Its code sets its speed to <Stat id="caveman.walk-speed" />,
but it covers 4 pixels every 3 steps (40 per second): friction takes 0.1 off before it moves, and the game
moves it in whole pixels, 1 a step and 1 more every third step
<Cite gml="objects/oCaveman/step.gml:198-200" /> <Cite gml="scripts/moveTo.gml:20-31" />. It stops to stand
for 20–50 steps (0.67–1.67 seconds), and then sets off again in a random direction, with a 1 in 100 chance
each step and when it reaches the edge of a ledge <Cite gml="objects/oCaveman/step.gml:88-114" />. At a ledge, though, it keeps its walking speed
for that step and slows down over the next few, so it slides about 10 pixels on, over the edge but not
yet off it; the next time it sets off that way it stops at once and slides again, off the edge this time
<Cite gml="objects/oCaveman/step.gml:198-200" /> <Cite clip="caveman/ledge" />. Standing beside a wall, it
hops <Cite gml="objects/oCaveman/step.gml:49-56" />.

<Clip id="caveman/ledge" />

The clip shows a Caveman stopping at the end of a platform and sliding over the edge, then stopping there
again, sliding off and dropping to the floor below.

<Clip id="caveman/charge" />

Every 6 steps while it stands or walks, a Caveman sends an invisible sight check flying ahead of it at 10
pixels per step, which stops at the first block in its way <Cite gml="objects/oCaveman/step.gml:69-78" />
<Cite gml="objects/oCaveman/step.gml:116-125" /> <Cite gml="objects/oEnemySight/collision_oSolid.gml:1" />.
When one reaches the player, every Caveman within
100 pixels of him that isn't stunned starts charging, whichever Caveman sent it and whichever way it faces
<Cite gml="objects/oEnemySight/collision_oCharacter.gml:1-8" />. The clip shows a Caveman spotting the
player across the cave and charging into him.

A charging Caveman runs at <Stat id="caveman.charge-speed" /> (<Stat id="caveman.charge-speed"
as="per-second" />) the way it faces, turning only at walls: it doesn't turn toward a player behind it,
doesn't stop at ledges, and keeps charging until something stuns it
<Cite gml="objects/oCaveman/step.gml:127-137" />.

A Caveman only acts while it's on screen or just off it
<Cite gml="objects/oEnemy/step.gml:7-11" /> <Cite gml="objects/oCaveman/step.gml:3" />.

## Damage

When the player touches a Caveman that isn't stunned, without stomping it, he loses
<Stat id="caveman.damage" />, is knocked away from it (and upward, if he's above it), and blinks,
invincible, for 30 steps (1 second) <Cite gml="objects/oCaveman/collision_oCharacter.gml:24-44" />. A
stunned or dead Caveman does nothing to him.

picked up and thrown <Cite gml="objects/oCaveman/step.gml:38-41" />

<Stat id="caveman.favor" /> to her [[kalis-favour|favour]] if it's stunned and half that if it's dead, unless her favour is
already −8 or less <Cite gml="objects/oEnemy/step.gml:130-167" />.

## Locations

Level generation makes Cavemen on top of blocks with open space above them, outside the starting room and
shops:

- **[[mines|The Mines]]**: a 1 in 800 chance for each such block, once the 1 in 60 roll for a
  [[snake|Snake]] has failed <Cite gml="scripts/scrEntityGen.gml:67-71" />.
- **[[jungle|The Jungle]]**: a 1 in 60 chance for each, once the roll for a [[mantrap|Mantrap]] has failed, but not on
  water or on a [[restless-dead|graveyard level]], where the same blocks make [[zombie|Zombies]] and
  [[vampire|Vampires]] instead <Cite gml="scripts/scrEntityGen.gml:276-290" />.
- **[[temple|The Temple]]**: a 1 in 40 chance for each, once the roll for a [[tomb-lord|Tomb Lord]] has failed
  <Cite gml="scripts/scrEntityGen.gml:452-465" />.

Cavemen also come from:

- **[[ice-caves|The Ice Caves]]' frozen cavemen.** Breaking the ice around one frees a stunned Caveman
  <Cite gml="objects/oFrozenCaveman/destroy.gml:1-7" />.
- **[[olmecs-lair|Olmec's Lair]].** The Cavemen bowing to [[olmec|Olmec]] get up and charge when he starts
  to move <Cite gml="objects/oOlmec/alarm_4.gml:1-5" /> <Cite gml="objects/oOlmec/alarm_6.gml:1-7" />.
- **Custom levels**, as the [[level-editor|level editor]]'s tile `h`
  <Cite gml="scripts/scrCreateTileObj.gml:147-150" />.

## Tips

- A charging Caveman only turns at walls, so getting behind it, or over it, leaves it running the other
  way.
- One Caveman seeing the player sets off every Caveman within 100 pixels of him, even those facing away.
- The Whip can't hurt a stunned Caveman, so with the Whip alone it takes three hits, each after it gets
  back up. One bullet kills it.
- A stunned Caveman can be carried and thrown at another monster, or sacrificed for twice a dead one's
  favour.
