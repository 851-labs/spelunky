It's the cheapest of the three big gems, below the [[big-sapphire|Big Sapphire]] and the
[[big-ruby|Big Ruby]], and worth four times a small [[emerald|Emerald]]. [[level-generation|Level generation]] buries Big Emeralds in
rock and sets them in nooks in every area, and [[jar|Jars]], [[chest|Chests]] and a few monsters let them out.
The player collects one by touching it; a [[ghost|Ghost]] that touches one turns it into a
[[diamond|Diamond]].

## Value

A Big Emerald's base value is <Stat id="big-emerald.value" />, and it's worth a quarter of that more for each
area past the Mines, the code counting [[olmecs-lair|Olmec's Lair]] as an area of its own
<Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:1996" /> <Cite gml="scripts/scrInitLevel.gml:24-29" />:

| Area | Value |
| --- | --- |
| [[mines\|The Mines]] | <Stat id="big-emerald.value-by-area" at="0" /> |
| [[jungle\|The Jungle]] | <Stat id="big-emerald.value-by-area" at="1" /> |
| [[ice-caves\|The Ice Caves]] | <Stat id="big-emerald.value-by-area" at="2" /> |

## Locations

### In the rock

Each block of rock that isn't a gold vein (71 in 100 aren't) and isn't on the level's edge rolls for a big gem
inside it. The Big Emerald's roll comes once the [[big-sapphire|Big Sapphire]]'s fails:

| Area | Chance for each block | |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [[mines\|The Mines]] | 1 in 120, once the Big Sapphire's 1 in 100 fails | <Cite gml="objects/oBrick/create.gml:7-13" /> |
| [[jungle\|The Jungle]] | 1 in 100, once the Big Sapphire's 1 in 80 fails | <Cite gml="objects/oLush/create.gml:5-12" /> |
| [[ice-caves\|The Ice Caves]] | 1 in 60, once the Big Sapphire's 1 in 40 fails | <Cite gml="objects/oDark/create.gml:3-12" /> |
| [[temple\|The Temple]] and [[olmecs-lair\|Olmec's Lair]] | 1 in 80, once the Big Sapphire's 1 in 60 fails | <Cite gml="objects/oTemple/create.gml:5-13" /> |

The [[tutorial|Tutorial]]'s bricks roll as the Mines' do <Cite gml="scripts/isLevel.gml:7" />. In the Temple,
one block of rock in 100 is the Jungle's earth, which rolls at the Jungle's odds, and one in ten a stone block,
which holds no gem <Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGen4.gml:317-322" /> <Cite gml="objects/oBlock/create.gml:1-4" />.
The [[city-of-gold|City of Gold]]'s temple blocks are gold instead, and don't roll for gems
<Cite gml="objects/oTemple/create.gml:7-10" />, but its blocks of earth still do ([[city-jungle-earth]]) <Cite quirk="1" row="One block in a hundred of the City of Gold is the Jungle's earth." />. A buried gem stays where it is, hidden behind the rock, until
the block around it is destroyed, by a [[bomb|Bomb]] or a [[mattock|Mattock]] for instance; then it drops out.
With the [[spectacles|Spectacles]] or the [[udjat-eye|Udjat Eye]], treasure is drawn in front of the rock, so
the buried gems show through it <Cite gml="objects/oTreasure/step.gml:63-67" />.

### On the ground

Level generation gives each block outside a shop with open space above it, away from the entrance, the exit
and a [[golden-idol|Golden Idol]], a roll for what lies on it, in every area, once its rolls for a
[[rock|Rock]] (1 in 100) and a [[jar|Jar]] (1 in 40) fail <Cite gml="scripts/scrTreasureGen.gml:27-46" />. A
big gem comes late in it:

| Spot | Chance | |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A nook one block high with a wall beside it | 1 in 6, once the rolls for a [[web]], a [[crate\|Crate]] (1 in 10), a Chest (1 in 15), a [[damsel\|Damsel]] (1 in 8), bones, a [[gold-bar\|Gold Bar]] (1 in 3) and [[gold-bars\|Gold Bars]] (1 in 6) fail | <Cite gml="scripts/scrTreasureGen.gml:48-93" /> |
| A gap one block wide between two walls | 1 in 8, once the rolls for a web, a Gold Bar (1 in 4), Gold Bars (1 in 8) and bones fail | <Cite gml="scripts/scrTreasureGen.gml:95-114" /> |
| The [[city-of-gold\|City of Gold]]'s treasure vaults | 1 in 60, once the rolls for a Big Ruby (1 in 120) and a Big Sapphire (1 in 80) fail; otherwise Gold Bars | <Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGen4.gml:364-370" /> |
| Some of [[olmecs-lair\|Olmec's Lair]]'s tiles | 1 in 6, once the rolls for a Chest (1 in 15) and Gold Bars (1 in 6) fail | <Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGen5.gml:177-189" /> |
| A grave on a Jungle [[restless-dead\|graveyard level]], in the block under it | 1 in 6, once the rolls for a [[gold-nugget\|Gold Nugget]] (1 in 2) and a Big Sapphire (1 in 4) fail | <Cite gml="scripts/scrEntityGen.gml:196-202" /> |

On a Mines level that's still to place the Udjat Eye's [[locked-chest|locked chest]], a nook that gets no web rolls for the
chest and nothing else <Cite gml="scripts/scrTreasureGen.gml:56-64" />. On a level with the chest, level
generation then turns one treasure that isn't inside rock, picked at random, into the chest's key ([[udjat-key-sealed]])
<Cite gml="scripts/scrEntityGen.gml:108-141" /> <Cite quirk="1" row="The locked chest's key can be sealed in rock." />. The Tutorial has one lying on a high ledge
<Cite room="rTutorial" />, and the
[[level-editor|level editor]]'s `5` tile is one <Cite gml="scripts/scrCreateTileObj.gml:269-272" />.

## Collecting

<Clip id="big-emerald/tutorial" />

The player collects a Big Emerald by touching it while he isn't stunned or dead; the one he takes is the
treasure nearest him, one a step <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:1990-2016" />. It's made uncollectable,
and its own alarm, set to <Stat id="big-emerald.collect-delay" />
(<Stat id="big-emerald.collect-delay" as="seconds" />) when it's made, makes it collectable
<Cite gml="objects/oEmeraldBig/create.gml:6-7" /> <Cite gml="objects/oEmeraldBig/alarm_0.gml:1" />, so one
fresh out of a Jar, a Chest or a monster lies a moment before the player can take it. In the clip, the player
climbs to a high ledge of the Tutorial and runs along it over a Big Emerald, a Big Sapphire and a Big Ruby,
collecting each as he touches it <Cite clip="big-emerald/tutorial" />.

<Clip id="jar/whip" />

In the second clip, a whipped Jar lets out a Big Emerald and a Gold Chunk, and the player walks over them:
his money goes up by $900, the Big Emerald's <Stat id="big-emerald.value" /> and the chunk's
<Stat id="gold-chunk.value" /> <Cite clip="jar/whip" />. It lets out two treasures because a whipped Jar rolls
its contents twice ([[double-jar-roll]]).

Its value shows under his money and is counted into it after a short pause; whatever is left is counted at
once when he leaves the level <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:1953-1971" />
<Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/other_animation_end.gml:46-50" />. A gem plays its own sound, where gold plays the
coin's <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:2000-2014" />, and the screen between levels shows each Big
Emerald collected on the level <Cite gml="objects/oTransition/alarm_0.gml:65-70" />.

A loose one falls and comes to rest like other treasure, moving only while it's on the screen; a [[web]] it
falls into holds it, and lava it sinks into destroys it <Cite gml="objects/oTreasure/step.gml:1-79" />
<Cite gml="objects/oWeb/collision_oTreasure.gml:1-2" />.

**The Ghost.** A [[ghost|Ghost]] that touches a Big Emerald turns it into a [[diamond|Diamond]], in the same
place <Cite gml="objects/oEmeraldBig/collision_oGhost.gml:1-2" />. The Ghost drifts through walls, so it
does this to gems still buried in the rock too <Cite replay="fidelity/replays/item-seed-2-diamond.txt" />
<Cite quirk="1" row="The rare items' only sources" />.

## Trivia

- Only the three big gems have code for a Ghost's touch, so a Ghost passes over the small gems, gold and a
  Diamond and leaves them as they are <Cite gml="objects/oEmeraldBig/collision_oGhost.gml:1-2" />.
