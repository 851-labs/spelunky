Like a [[bomb-bag|Bomb Bag]], it isn't kept: taking one adds its <Stat id="rope-pile.ropes" bare />
[[rope|ropes]] to the count beside the rope icon at the top of the screen, and the pile is gone
([[bombs-and-ropes]] has how the count works). The [[general-store|General Store]] and the
[[clothing-shop|Clothing Shop]] sell it, and [[crate|Crates]], blocks and the [[yeti-king|Yeti King]] give it
away.

## Effect

<Clip id="bombs-and-ropes/pickups" />

A free Rope Pile is taken as soon as the player touches it, unless he's dead or stunned or the pile is
inside a block: his ropes go up by <Stat id="rope-pile.ropes" bare />, a flashing "+ROPE" rises from where the
pile was, and the message "YOU GOT 3 MORE ROPES!" shows <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:2052-2066" />.
Paying for one, or carrying one out of its shop, does the same <Cite gml="scripts/scrStealItem.gml:103-114" />.
A count above <Stat id="rope.max" bare /> is cut back to <Stat id="rope.max" bare /> at the start of his next
step <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:10-11" />. In the clip, the player walks over a Bomb Bag, a Rope
Pile and a [[bomb-box|Bomb Box]], and his ropes go from 4 to 7 at the pile
<Cite clip="bombs-and-ropes/pickups" />.

One for sale is picked up as any ware is, and stays in the player's hands until he pays: the
[[shopkeeper|Shopkeeper]] names its price <Cite gml="objects/oRopePile/create.gml:7-8" />
<Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:1359-1389" />.

<Clip id="crate/rope" />

A Crate's item appears where the player stands as he opens it, so a Rope Pile out of one is taken at once:
in the clip, a Crate gives him 3 more ropes on the step he opens it
<Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:612-616" /> <Cite clip="crate/rope" />. Every block of rock away from
the level's edges that isn't gold and holds no gem has a 1 in 1200 chance of hiding an item, one of
nineteen at random <Cite gml="objects/oBrick/create.gml:7-16" /> <Cite gml="scripts/scrGenerateItem.gml:63-89" />.
In custom levels, it's the [[level-editor|level editor]]'s tile `R`
<Cite gml="scripts/scrCreateTileObj.gml:315-320" />.

<Clip id="yeti-king/bomb" />

A Yeti King that dies leaves four items, each Spike Shoes one time in twelve, and otherwise a
[[big-sapphire|Big Sapphire]] or a Rope Pile at even odds, thrown up and out from where it fell
<Cite gml="objects/oYetiKing/step.gml:23-40" />. In the clip, a bomb kills one, and it bursts into bones, Big
Sapphires and a Rope Pile <Cite clip="yeti-king/bomb" />.

Carrying one out of a shop without paying adds its ropes and angers the Shopkeeper, as with any ware
([[general-store#stealing]]) <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:541-557" />.

## Interactions

- **Blocks.** A Rope Pile buried in a block isn't taken until the block is broken
  <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:2055" />.
- **[[bomb|Bombs]].** An explosion throws a Rope Pile aside, as it does most items, and knocks one out of the
  player's hands <Cite gml="objects/oExplosion/collision_oItem.gml:30-46" />.

## Tips

- Near the cap, a pile adds what it can: at 97 ropes, one takes the count to 99, and the third rope is lost.
