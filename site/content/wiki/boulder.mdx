The Boulder guards [[mines|the Mines]]' [[golden-idol|Golden Idol]]. The idol sits on an [[altar|altar]] under a giant
stone tiki head; 100 steps after the player picks it up, the head opens and drops a boulder two blocks across,
which rolls off toward him, smashing through blocks. It kills by crushing: one that lands on the player, or
rolls into him, takes every heart.

## How it works

<Clip id="boulder/crush" />

Picking up the idol on a Mines level shakes the screen and sets the nearest giant tiki head going: 100 steps
(3.33 seconds) later it opens and drops a boulder from its mouth <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:1222-1231" />
<Cite gml="objects/oGiantTikiHead/alarm_0.gml:1-3" />. The idol only does this once
<Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:1230" />.

The boulder falls and, the first time it's on the ground and still, sets off toward the side the player is
on at <Stat id="boulder.roll-speed" /> <Cite gml="objects/oBoulder/step.gml:33-56" />. It moves twice its
speed a step in the open ([[double-speed-olmec-and-boulder]]), 8 and 10 pixels on alternate steps at first, because both the game's pass over
moving platforms and its own step move it <Cite quirk="1" row="Olmec and the idol's boulder move twice their velocity each step." />. On the ground it loses 1% of its speed a step,
and stops once it's below 0.5 pixels a step; it turns back at the edges of the level, and edges off a block
it's balanced on <Cite gml="objects/oBoulder/step.gml:11-64" />. It's kept active wherever it is, rather than only
near the screen as most things are <Cite gml="objects/oLevel/step.gml:95-97" />.

The clip shows the player standing with the idol under the head: the boulder drops onto him and crushes him,
then rolls on to the wall.

## Damage

The boulder hurts nothing directly: it's solid, and kills what ends up inside it. A player whose middle is
inside it, because it fell or rolled onto him, is crushed, losing every heart, and is counted in the
end-of-game statistics' crushed row <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:1492-1511" />, never the boulder row,
which nothing adds to <Cite gml="scripts/scrWriteStats.gml:162" /> <Cite quirk="1" row="Nothing counts a boulder's kill in the deaths table's boulder row." /> <Cite replay="fidelity/replays/trap-boulder-crush.txt" />.

Rolling, it smashes the blocks in its way, as long as it's going at least 1 pixel a step and the block isn't
the floor under it, taking any spikes on them too, and loses 0.1 pixels a step of speed for each. An
unbreakable block, such as the level's border, or any block once it's slower than 1 pixel a step, turns it
back at half its speed instead
<Cite gml="objects/oBoulder/collision_oSolid.gml:1-30" />.

## Locations

Only in [[mines|the Mines]], in the idol room: each side room off the path through the level, except in its
bottom row of rooms, has a 1 in 10 chance of being it, once the roll for a [[kali|Kali]] [[kalis-altar|altar]] fails, and a level has
one at most <Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGen.gml:86-101" />. The room has the giant tiki head near its top and the
idol on an altar below it <Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGen.gml:115-116" /> <Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGen.gml:374-383" />.

The idol does something else in the other areas: in the Jungle the floor around it crumbles, and on a
[[restless-dead|graveyard level]] the [[ghost|Ghost]] comes; in the Temple it drops the [[ceiling-trap|Ceiling Trap]] <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:1232-1281" />.

## Avoiding

- **Leave the room's floor.** The boulder rolls along the ground toward where the player was when it landed.
  Dropping down a shaft, as in the second clip, or climbing out of its way, lets it roll past; it stops at a
  wall, or once it has slowed.
- **Don't stand under the head.** It drops straight down from the head, onto whoever is still by the altar
  after 100 steps.
- **Bomb it.** A [[bomb|Bomb]]'s blast that reaches the boulder destroys it, leaving rubble and, for each of
  three pieces, a 1 in 3 chance of a [[rock|Rock]] <Cite gml="objects/oExplosion/collision_oBoulder.gml:1-13" />.
  Nothing else breaks it: to the code that destroys blocks, it's unbreakable
  <Cite gml="objects/oBoulder/create.gml:9" />.

## Interactions

- **[[arrow-trap|Arrow Traps]]** fire at a boulder rolling past, as they do at anything that moves in front
  of them <Cite gml="objects/oArrowTrapTest/collision_oBoulder.gml:1-26" />. In both clips one fires as it goes
  by.
- **Standing on it.** Its top is a platform: a player standing on a rolling boulder is carried along
  <Cite gml="objects/oBoulder/create.gml:11" /> <Cite gml="scripts/gameStepEvent.gml:74-106" />.
