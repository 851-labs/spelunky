A dark level is an ordinary level of its area, built as usual, but drawn in the dark: the screen is black
except for the circles that lights cut out of it. The player has a dim light of his own, and a crate of
[[flare|Flares]] waits by the entrance. Generation adds [[lamp|lamps]] or [[tiki-torch|tiki torches]], makes the traps lit kinds that glow,
and hangs [[scarab|Scarabs]], which live only here.

## Generation

### The roll

Each level makes the roll, 1 in 12, after its rooms are built. A level can't be dark if it's level 1,
[[olmecs-lair|Olmec's Lair]] (level 16) or in [[ice-caves|the Ice Caves]], if its area has had a dark level
already, or if the level before it was finished too fast <Cite gml="scripts/scrInitLevel.gml:167-174" />
<Cite gml="scripts/scrClearGlobals.gml:54" />. The area's dark level is forgotten on its first level (1, 5,
9 and 13), so each of the three areas that can be dark has one at most <Cite gml="scripts/scrInitLevel.gml:31-37" />.

"Too fast" is the screen between levels' doing: it reads the finished level's time and turns dark levels
off for the next one if the seconds past the whole minute are 20 or fewer, the code's comment calling it a
speed run <Cite gml="objects/oTransition/draw.gml:89-102" />. So a level that took 1:05 rules out a dark
level as one that took 0:05 does, and one that took 0:21 doesn't. A new game starts with dark levels off,
so the first level after the [[title-screen|title screen]] is never dark, the [[tunnel-man-shortcuts|shortcuts]]' levels 5, 9 and 13 included <Cite gml="scripts/scrClearGlobals.gml:51" /> <Cite quirk="1" row="A dark level needs the previous level's clock" />.

The [[black-market|Black Market]] is never dark: it's lit again after the roll. But a roll that comes up there
still counts as the Jungle's dark level, so no later Jungle level is dark <Cite gml="scripts/scrInitLevel.gml:176" />
<Cite quirk="1" row="A dark level needs the previous level's clock" /> <Cite replay="fidelity/replays/jungle-seed-9-market-dark-roll.txt" />. Any other special level
can be dark too: a [[snake-pit|snake pit]], a [[flooded-level|flooded level]], a
[[restless-dead|graveyard level]], the [[city-of-gold|City of Gold]].

A dark level opens with "I CAN'T SEE A THING!" and "I'D BETTER USE THESE FLARES!", or, if the player wears
the [[hedjet|Hedjet]], "THE HEDJET SHINES BRIGHTLY." <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/alarm_0.gml:5-13" />. A
special level's own message comes after it <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/alarm_1.gml:1-48" />. In the clip,
level 2 of the Mines loads dark: the crate of flares glows beside the entrance, and the player climbs down
through the dark by his own small light <Cite clip="dark-level/arrival" />.

### The darkness

Each step, the screen is filled with black and then lit in circles <Cite gml="objects/oScreen/step_begin.gml:148-240" />:

| Light | Radius | |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The player | 96 pixels less 64 times the darkness (below): about 38 pixels at the darkest | <Cite gml="objects/oScreen/step_begin.gml:178" /> |
| A [[flare\|Flare]], the crate of Flares, a [[lamp\|lamp]] (hanging, or a plain one down: a red one down lights nothing, [[red-lamp-no-light]]), an explosion | 96 pixels | <Cite gml="objects/oScreen/step_begin.gml:179-194" /> <Cite quirk="1" row="A fallen red lamp lights nothing on a dark level." /> <Cite gml="objects/oScreen/step_begin.gml:219-222" /> |
| The [[ghost\|Ghost]] | 64 pixels | <Cite gml="objects/oScreen/step_begin.gml:231-234" /> |
| A lit trap, a tiki torch, a [[fire-frog\|Fire Frog]], [[lava\|Lava]] | 32 pixels | <Cite gml="objects/oScreen/step_begin.gml:195-218" /> <Cite gml="objects/oScreen/step_begin.gml:223-226" /> |
| A [[scarab\|Scarab]] | 16 pixels | <Cite gml="objects/oScreen/step_begin.gml:227-230" /> |

The darkness is the distance from the player to the nearest light (a flare, or within 200 pixels a lamp,
the crate, lava or an explosion, a lit trap or a tiki torch counting as 48 pixels farther than it is),
divided by 160, and 0.9 at most. So the player's own light grows as he nears a light, and is at its widest, 96 pixels, beside one
<Cite gml="objects/oLevel/step.gml:110-137" /> <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:73-136" />. With the
Hedjet the darkness is 0, and his light is always 96 pixels ([[hedjet#dark-levels]]).

### Made dark later

Defiling one of [[kalis-altar|Kali's altars]] a third time, and every time after, makes the level dark, with a
[[ghost|Ghost]] if there's none yet; if it's dark already and has a Ghost, [[kali|Kali]] punishes the player
another way <Cite gml="objects/oSacAltarLeft/destroy.gml:39-58" />. That can darken any level with an altar,
the Ice Caves' too, but it comes after generation, so such a level has none of the lights, lit traps or
Scarabs below.

## Inhabitants

The dark level's own monster is the [[scarab|Scarab]], made only on a dark level, in the spots where
monsters hang under a block (two open tiles below) <Cite gml="scripts/scrEntityGen.gml:47-62" /> <Cite gml="scripts/scrEntityGen.gml:264-271" /> <Cite gml="scripts/scrEntityGen.gml:445-450" />:

| Monster | Area | Chance | |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [[scarab\|Scarab]] | [[mines\|The Mines]] | 1 in 40, once the [[giant-spider\|Giant Spider]]'s and a lamp's 1 in 60 fail, before a [[bat\|Bat]]'s | <Cite gml="scripts/scrEntityGen.gml:52-63" /> |
| [[scarab\|Scarab]] | [[jungle\|The Jungle]] | 1 in 40, before a Bat's | <Cite gml="scripts/scrEntityGen.gml:271-272" /> |

The rest are the area's usual monsters. A Scarab is treasure that flits about, and glows; in the clip, on a
dark Temple level, the player drops onto one and collects it <Cite clip="scarab/collect" />.

## Hazards

The traps are the area's, but made as lit kinds that glow <Cite gml="scripts/scrEntityGen.gml:155-168" /> <Cite gml="scripts/scrEntityGen.gml:247-249" /> <Cite gml="scripts/scrEntityGen.gml:436-437" /> <Cite gml="scripts/scrEntityGen.gml:467-471" /> <Cite gml="scripts/scrEntityGen.gml:508-521" />:

- **Lit [[arrow-trap|Arrow Traps]]** in the Mines and the Temple, which fire as the plain ones do. In the
  clip, one fires into the player as he drops down a [[ladder|ladder]] through its line, and a bomb destroys it <Cite clip="arrow-trap/dark" />.

  <Clip id="arrow-trap/dark" />
- **Lit [[spear-trap|Spear Traps]]** in the Jungle and the Temple.
- **Lit [[smash-trap|Smash Traps]]** in the Temple. The lit kind isn't a smash trap to the player's code: its
  touch takes no hearts and throws him nowhere, and it kills only by sliding on into him until he's crushed
  ([[lit-smash-trap-touch]]) <Cite quirk="1" row="A lit smash trap isn't a smash trap to the player." /> <Cite clip="smash-trap/lit" />.

## Items and shops

- **A crate of [[flare|Flares]]** by the entrance, on its left if there's room, else on its right, else on it
  <Cite gml="scripts/scrEntityGen.gml:527-545" />. It's carried like a heavy item, and opened by pressing
  up and the attack key over it, which throws out three Flares <Cite gml="objects/oFlareCrate/create.gml:1-12" />
  <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:649-669" />. Flares and the crate go out in water <Cite gml="objects/oFlare/step.gml:6-20" /> <Cite gml="objects/oFlareCrate/step.gml:3-18" />.
- **[[lamp|Lamps]]**, in the Mines, hanging under a block, 1 in 60 where a monster could hang
  <Cite gml="scripts/scrEntityGen.gml:61" />. When the block above it is destroyed, a lamp falls as one the
  player can carry <Cite gml="objects/oSolid/destroy.gml:29-33" />.
- **Tiki torches**, in the Jungle, on 1 in 20 of the ground's spots for a [[spear-trap|Spear Trap]], before
  the trap's roll <Cite gml="scripts/scrEntityGen.gml:235-238" />.

Shops and treasure are the area's. Every shop has a lamp, which lights it on a dark level <Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGen.gml:173-204" /> <Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGen.gml:427-431" /> <Cite gml="objects/oScreen/step_begin.gml:187-190" />.

## Trivia

- Messages stay on screen twice as long on a dark level ([[messages-halved-on-lit-levels]]): the screen's drawing counts their time down each
  step, and the level's own drawing, which shows them only when the level is lit, counts it down again, so
  a message lasts 200 steps in the dark and 100 in the light <Cite gml="objects/oScreen/step_begin.gml:243-259" /> <Cite gml="objects/oLevel/draw.gml:11-27" /> <Cite quirk="1" row="Messages last half as long on a lit level." /> <Cite replay="fidelity/replays/mines-seed-2.txt" />.
  So a special level's second message, 209 steps after the first, follows it 9 steps after it's gone in
  the dark, where in the light the screen is clear for 109. A dark level that's a graveyard and flooded too
  has a third, the lake's, worded "YOU HEAR RUSHING WATER..." ([[rushing-water-message]]) <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/alarm_0.gml:5-13" /> <Cite replay="fidelity/replays/jungle-seed-10-dark-graveyard-lake.txt" /> <Cite replay="fidelity/replays/city-of-gold-seed-9-sacrifice-pit.txt" />.
- The light around the player is tinted blue the darker it is: its colour is full blue, with red and green
  falling as the darkness grows <Cite gml="objects/oScreen/step_begin.gml:155" />.
- The level's own drawing code once darkened the screen with a rectangle, and is commented out <Cite gml="objects/oLevel/draw.gml:1-10" />.
- Two commented-out lines sit in the roll: an older version of it with a chance of 1 in 1, and one that
  would have put a flare at the player's feet on every dark level <Cite gml="scripts/scrInitLevel.gml:168-173" />.
- [[level-generation|Level generation]] draws the lamp's and the Scarab's rolls from the random numbers on lit levels too, as
  GameMaker 8 evaluates both sides of an `and` <Cite gml="scripts/scrEntityGen.gml:61-62" /> <Cite quirk="1" row="Conditions that call rand on the right of and draw from the RNG even when the left is false" />.
