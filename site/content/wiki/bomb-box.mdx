Like a [[bomb-bag|Bomb Bag]], it isn't kept: taking one adds its <Stat id="bomb-box.bombs" bare />
[[bomb|bombs]] to the count at the top of the screen, and the box is gone ([[bombs-and-ropes]] has how the
count works). The [[bomb-shop|Bomb Shop]], the [[weapon-shop|Weapon Shop]] and the [[rare-shop|Rare Shop]]
sell it, the [[dice-house|Dice House]] puts it up as a prize, and [[crate|Crates]], blocks and
[[kalis-favour|Kali]] give it away.

## Effect

<Clip id="bombs-and-ropes/pickups" />

A free Bomb Box is taken as soon as the player touches it, unless he's dead or stunned or the box is inside
a block: his bombs go up by <Stat id="bomb-box.bombs" bare />, a flashing "+BOMB" rises from where the box was,
and the message "YOU GOT 12 MORE BOMBS!" shows <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:2036-2050" />. Paying
for one, or carrying one out of its shop, does the same <Cite gml="scripts/scrStealItem.gml:80-91" />. A
count above <Stat id="bomb.max" bare /> is cut back to <Stat id="bomb.max" bare /> at the start of his next
step <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:10-11" />. In the clip, the player walks over a Bomb Bag, a
[[rope-pile|Rope Pile]] and a Bomb Box, and his bombs go from 7 to 19 at the box
<Cite clip="bombs-and-ropes/pickups" />.

One for sale is picked up as any ware is, and stays in the player's hands until he pays: the
[[shopkeeper|Shopkeeper]] names it "A BOX OF 12 BOMBS" and its price
<Cite gml="objects/oBombBox/create.gml:6-8" /> <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:1359-1389" />.

<Clip id="dice-house/seven" />

A Dice House's prize is rolled from its own set of rare items, and a roll of seven gives it away, free, and
puts up another in its place <Cite gml="objects/oShopkeeper/step.gml:170-200" />. In the clip, a seven
gives the player the case's Spike Shoes, and a Bomb Box takes their place in the case
<Cite clip="dice-house/seven" />. Every block of rock away from the level's edges that isn't gold and holds
no gem has a 1 in 1200 chance of hiding an item, one of nineteen at random
<Cite gml="objects/oBrick/create.gml:7-16" /> <Cite gml="scripts/scrGenerateItem.gml:63-89" />. Kali's first
gift, at a favour of 8 at one of her [[kalis-altar|altars]], is one of eight pieces of equipment the player
hasn't got, the Cape only if he hasn't a Jetpack either; when he has them all, it's a Jetpack, or, if he
has one, a Bomb Box
<Cite gml="scripts/scrGetFavorMsg.gml:67-108" />. In custom levels, it's the [[level-editor|level editor]]'s
tile `:` <Cite gml="scripts/scrCreateTileObj.gml:303-308" />.

Carrying one out of a shop without paying adds its bombs and angers the Shopkeeper, as with any ware
([[general-store#stealing]]) <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:541-557" />.

## Interactions

- **Blocks.** A Bomb Box buried in a block isn't taken until the block is broken, when the player can walk
  onto it <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:2039" />.
- **[[bomb|Bombs]].** An explosion throws a Bomb Box aside, as it does most items, and knocks one out of the
  player's hands; the box's bombs don't go off <Cite gml="objects/oExplosion/collision_oItem.gml:30-46" />.

## Tips

- A Bomb Box and four Bomb Bags hold the same bombs for the same price, on any level.
- Near the cap, a box adds what it can: at 90 bombs, one takes the count to 99, and three of its bombs are
  lost.

## Trivia

- Unlike the Bomb Bag, the Bomb Box is heavy, as a [[crate|Crate]] or a [[jetpack|Jetpack]] is, so the player
  holds one for sale higher than he holds a bag <Cite gml="objects/oBombBox/create.gml:9" />
  <Cite gml="objects/oItem/step.gml:44-56" />.
