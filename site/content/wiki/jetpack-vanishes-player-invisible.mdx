A [[jetpack|Jetpack]] lying on the floor, in a shop or in the player's hands is gone the moment he turns
invisible, which only happens as he dies. Nobody sees it go: without the [[ankh|Ankh]] the game-over screen
follows, and with it, he's visible again before any Jetpack looks.

## What happens

The player walks past a Jetpack lying on the floor into a [[mantrap|Mantrap]], which swallows him. On the next
step the Jetpack behind him is gone <Cite clip="quirks/jetpack-vanishes-player-invisible" />.

<Clip id="quirks/jetpack-vanishes-player-invisible" />

## Why

The Jetpack's step has one check of its own:

```gml objects/oJetpack/step.gml:3-4
if (instance_exists(oPlayer1))
    if (not oPlayer1.visible) instance_destroy();
```

It's the worn [[cape|Cape]]'s check, word for word <Cite gml="objects/oCape/step.gml:49-50" />. The Cape is
drawn on the player's back, so it should go when he does. A worn Jetpack isn't an object at all, only a flag
(`global.hasJetpack`), so the `oJetpack` that runs the check is the pickup: the one lying in the level, for
sale, or held.

The player turns invisible when he's crushed, when his hearts fall below -99
<Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:1484-1488" />, or when a Mantrap or the [[ghost|Ghost]] takes him
<Cite gml="objects/oManTrap/collision_oCharacter.gml:41-44" /> <Cite
gml="objects/oGhost/collision_oCharacter.gml:13-16" />. After a crush or the hearts, every Jetpack goes in
the same step, as the player steps first; after a Mantrap or the Ghost, in the next, as their collisions come
after the steps. With the Ankh it never happens: the revival makes him visible again before any Jetpack's step
runs <Cite quirk="1" />.

## Reproducing it

Die out of sight with a Jetpack nearby, without the Ankh: walk into a Mantrap or let the Ghost take the
player. The game-over screen hides it, so it only shows in the step between. The replay of the clip's staged
scene, on a custom level, has a Mantrap eat the player and the Jetpack on the floor behind him destroyed on the
next step <Cite replay="fidelity/replays/item-jetpack-player-invisible.txt" />. The Ankh's case is only the
code's and the event order's: no replay has a Jetpack near an Ankh revival.

## In the port

The spelunky.ai port reproduces it exactly: it's in docs/quirks.md's list of the 1.1 source's bugs
<Cite quirk="1" />, and the oracle's fingerprint of the replay pins the step it goes. No option turns it off.
