A [[yeti|Yeti]] is written to charge, as a [[caveman|Caveman]] does: it has a charge, at 3 pixels a step, and it
sends sight checks ahead of it every 6 steps. But when a sight check reaches the player, it sets off only the
Cavemen and [[hawkman|Hawkmen]] near him, so a Yeti's sight can set off a Caveman, but never a Yeti. The only thing that
sets a Yeti charging is a [[piranha|Piranha]]'s bite, and no generated level has both.

## What happens

A Yeti's sight check reaches the player, and a Caveman walled in under him starts charging. The Yeti that saw him
never charges: it walks up and throws him <Cite clip="quirks/yeti-never-charges" />.

<Clip id="quirks/yeti-never-charges" />

## Why

A Yeti's create event names its charge, `ATTACK = 2` <Cite gml="objects/oYeti/create.gml:16" />, and its step
runs it: 3 pixels a step the way it faces, turning at walls <Cite gml="objects/oYeti/step.gml:126-136" />. While it
stands or walks, it sends a sight check, `oEnemySight`, every 6 steps
<Cite gml="objects/oYeti/step.gml:65-74" /> <Cite gml="objects/oYeti/step.gml:115-124" />. The check's collision
with the player is what sets monsters charging:

```gml objects/oEnemySight/collision_oCharacter.gml:1-8
with oCaveman
{
    if (distance_to_object(oPlayer1) < 100 and status < 98)
    {
        status = 2;
        playSound(global.sndAlert);
    }
}
```

The same follows `with oHawkman`, and there's no `with oYeti`
<Cite gml="objects/oEnemySight/collision_oCharacter.gml:10-17" />. So the Yeti's sight sets off the Cavemen and
Hawkmen within 100 pixels of the player, and never a Yeti. The only code that sets a Yeti's `status` to 2 is a
Piranha's bite: a Piranha goes for the nearest live Caveman, or with none the nearest Shopkeeper, Hawkman or Yeti,
if it's swimming, and sets what it reaches charging <Cite gml="objects/oPiranha/step.gml:77-117" />. Piranhas live
only in the Jungle and Yetis only in the Ice Caves <Cite quirk="1" />.

## Reproducing it

Let any Yeti in the [[ice-caves|Ice Caves]] see the player: it walks up to him, and never charges. The replay of the
clip's staged scene, a custom level, has a Yeti on the player's floor and a Caveman walled in under him. The Yeti's
sight check reaches the player, and the Caveman, which can't see him, charges; the Yeti walks on, sends more checks,
and throws him when it reaches him <Cite replay="fidelity/replays/enemy-yeti-sight-never-charges.txt" />. In another
custom level, with a Yeti swimming in a pool with two Piranhas and nothing else for them to go for, their bite sets
it charging, turning at the walls <Cite replay="fidelity/replays/enemy-yeti-piranha-charge.txt" />.

## In the port

The spelunky.ai port reproduces it exactly: it's in docs/quirks.md's list of the 1.1 source's bugs
<Cite quirk="1" />, and the oracle's fingerprint of both replays pins who charges. No option turns it off.
