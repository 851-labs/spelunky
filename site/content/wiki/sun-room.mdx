It's the first of the three secret challenges behind the trophies of the [[high-scores-room|high scores
room]], the DAMSEL CHALLENGE. One room, built in the room editor, over a pool of [[lava]], with
[[arrow-trap|Arrow Traps]], [[spear-trap|Spear Traps]], [[smash-trap|Smash Traps]] and
[[spring-trap|Spring Traps]] that work on their own, and a [[damsel|Damsel]] who wanders among them. The
score is the seconds the player and the Damsel last, and as it climbs the room drops in lit
[[bomb|bombs]], then [[vampire|Vampires]], then the [[ghost|Ghost]].

## Generation

### Getting there

<Clip id="high-scores-room/climb" />

A best money score of $200,000 or more puts a gold trophy at the top of the high scores room's column of
trophies, and a door to the Sun Room under it <Cite gml="objects/oHighscores/create.gml:32-39" />. Climbing
up to it, the board beside the trophies reads SUN ROOM and the goal, KEEP YOURSELF AND THE DAMSEL ALIVE FOR
AS LONG AS POSSIBLE!, or LOCKED. without the score <Cite gml="objects/oHighscores/draw.gml:14-40" />. In the
clip, with gold trophies for money, time and kills, the player climbs the column ledge by ledge, the board
naming each room, and goes through the top door <Cite clip="high-scores-room/climb" />.

### The room

The room editor laid out the whole room, a screen wide, with the player's entrance, the Damsel, the traps
and the vines <Cite room="rSun" />. A carved sun hangs on the back wall. The bricks around its edge can't
be broken, "to prevent the Tunnel Man from tearing up the whole level", as the code's comment says
<Cite gml="objects/oSunRoom/create.gml:10-17" />. The player starts with
<Stat id="health.bonus-room" />, whoever he plays as <Cite gml="objects/oSunRoom/create.gml:1" />; his bombs
and ropes can't be used outside a level <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:944-948" />
<Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:1045" /> <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:1132-1137" />. The music
is the boss music, the same in all three bonus rooms <Cite gml="scripts/startMusic.gml:75-80" />.

### The challenge

<Clip id="sun-room/challenge" />

DAMSEL CHALLENGE BEGINS IN 3... counts down at the bottom of the screen as the room starts
<Cite gml="objects/oSunRoom/alarm_1.gml:1-2" /> <Cite gml="objects/oSunRoom/draw.gml:9-17" />. From 100 steps
in, a point is counted every 30 steps, a second, shown at the top beside the Damsel's head
<Cite gml="objects/oSunRoom/alarm_11.gml:1-5" /> <Cite gml="objects/oSunRoom/draw.gml:7-8" />. The count
stops at 99, the most the high scores keep for a bonus room, two digits of one number <Cite gml="scripts/scrUpdateHighscores.gml:117" />.

After 200 to 300 steps, and then every 100 to 200, something comes in, once the top of the room above the
player is open: while a block is there, the room tries again each step <Cite gml="objects/oSunRoom/create.gml:5" /> <Cite gml="objects/oSunRoom/alarm_0.gml:1-8" /> <Cite gml="objects/oSunRoom/alarm_0.gml:48-51" />:

| Points | What comes in | |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Under 50 | A lit [[bomb]], at the top of the room above the player | <Cite gml="objects/oSunRoom/alarm_0.gml:29-51" /> |
| 50 to 79 | A [[vampire\|Vampire]], flying, at the top of the middle | <Cite gml="objects/oSunRoom/alarm_0.gml:15-28" /> |
| 80 or more | The [[ghost\|Ghost]], from the side away from the player, unless it's there already | <Cite gml="objects/oSunRoom/alarm_0.gml:9-14" /> |

The room's bombs are armed, as the player's and a [[monkey|Monkey]]'s are and no other bomb the game
lights is ([[unarmed-bombs]]) <Cite gml="objects/oSunRoom/alarm_0.gml:31-33" /> <Cite quirk="1" row="A bomb lit by anything but the player, a Monkey or the Sun Room isn't armed." />.

The challenge ends when the player dies, or when the Damsel dies or is gone: the room then takes all his
hearts <Cite gml="objects/oSunRoom/step.gml:1-14" />. FINISHED! comes up, then YOU SET A NEW RECORD! if the
points beat the room's best, or BETTER LUCK NEXT TIME... <Cite gml="objects/oGame/draw.gml:56-75" />. The
points are kept as the room's best in the high scores, which count no play, death or win for it <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:1901-1914" /> <Cite gml="scripts/scrUpdateHighscores.gml:78-117" />.
Attack (or start) goes back to the high scores room, in front of the Sun Room's door <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:907-941" /> <Cite gml="objects/oHighscores/create.gml:104-108" />.

In the clip, the player stands still through the countdown while the Damsel walks into the traps: Arrow
Traps shoot her and a Spear Trap stabs at her, and a lit bomb drops above the player and kills him at 10
points. YOU SET A NEW RECORD! follows, the room's first score <Cite clip="sun-room/challenge" /> <Cite replay="fidelity/replays/bonus-sun-damsel.txt" />.
