The Megamouth lives only in the lake at the bottom of a [[flooded-level|flooded level]] of
[[jungle|the Jungle]], one to a level. Four blocks long, it patrols the lake bed and leaves a player on dry
land alone, but as soon as he's swimming it charges straight at him. It has <Stat id="megamouth.hp" />, and
when it dies, four [[crate|Crates]] burst out of it.

## Behaviour

<Clip id="megamouth/attack" />

A Megamouth swims back and forth along the bottom of its lake at <Stat id="megamouth.swim-speed" />
(<Stat id="megamouth.swim-speed" as="per-second" />), sinking 1 pixel a step until it's just above the ground
<Cite gml="objects/oJaws/step.gml:34-69" />. Where the water ends or a wall is in its way, it turns around, if
there's room behind it: a turning animation, then a pause of 40 steps (1.33 seconds)
<Cite gml="objects/oJaws/step.gml:36-64" /> <Cite gml="objects/oJaws/other_animation_end.gml:1-15" />.

As soon as the player is swimming, it attacks, however far away he is in the water
<Cite gml="objects/oJaws/step.gml:71-74" />. It heads for him at <Stat id="megamouth.attack-speed" />
(<Stat id="megamouth.attack-speed" as="per-second" />), aiming from its head at him every step, and moves only
while the water ahead is clear <Cite gml="objects/oJaws/step.gml:86-125" />. When he gets behind it, it stops
to turn around, with the same animation and pause, before it comes at him again
<Cite gml="objects/oJaws/step.gml:93-116" />. When he leaves the water, it goes back to patrolling
<Cite gml="objects/oJaws/step.gml:127-132" />. The clip shows a Megamouth patrolling the lake bed, charging
the moment the player drops into the water, biting him, and turning around as he sinks behind it.

It lets off a bubble every 41 steps <Cite gml="objects/oJaws/step.gml:135-140" />. It acts while it's within
48 pixels of the screen, farther out than most monsters <Cite gml="objects/oJaws/step.gml:1-2" />.

**Out of the water.** A Megamouth whose head is out of water loses 1 hit point every step, so its
<Stat id="megamouth.hp" bare /> last at most 40 steps (1.33 seconds)
<Cite gml="objects/oJaws/step.gml:5-8" />. The last clip on this page shows it: a [[bomb|Bomb]] breaks the
lake bed, the water drains away, and the stranded Megamouth dies <Cite clip="megamouth/bomb" />.

## Damage

When the player touches a Megamouth without stomping it, he loses <Stat id="megamouth.damage" />, is knocked
away from it, and blinks, invincible, for 30 steps (1 second)
<Cite gml="objects/oEnemy/collision_oCharacter.gml:14-25" />. This is the touch code most monsters share. Its
body counts as well as its head: it's 64 pixels long and 32 high, and the game moves its collision box to
match the way it faces <Cite gml="objects/oJaws/step.gml:142-149" />.

## Defeating

<Clip id="megamouth/whip" />

A Megamouth has <Stat id="megamouth.hp" />. The [[whip|Whip]] and the [[machete|Machete]] hurt it through the
code most monsters share, and an explosion through its own, and both take their damage on every step they
touch it, with no pause between hits, so one swing of the Whip can take several hit points
([[whip-every-step]])
<Cite gml="objects/oEnemy/collision_oWhip.gml:1-8" /> <Cite gml="objects/oExplosion/collision_oEnemy.gml:12-14" />.
The clip shows the player whipping a charging Megamouth from the water: one swing takes 1 hit point, the next
seven, the third three <Cite clip="megamouth/whip" />.

Its own step event replaces the one most monsters share, which is where [[spikes|Spikes]], [[lava|Lava]] and
[[spear-trap|Spear Traps]] hurt them, so none of those harm a Megamouth
<Cite gml="objects/oEnemy/step.gml:63-128" />.

As it's hurt, its body looks more and more bitten: the game draws one of three bodies behind its head, by
whether it has 20 hit points or more, 10 or more, or fewer <Cite gml="objects/oJaws/draw.gml:1-13" />.

When it dies, it bursts in a spray of blood, leaving four bones, and four [[crate|Crates]] fly out of it
<Cite gml="objects/oJaws/step.gml:10-30" />. The clip shows one stranded by a drained lake dying and leaving
its Crates. Megamouths killed count toward the player's kills, and the screen between levels draws a Megamouth
icon for each <Cite gml="objects/oJaws/step.gml:12-17" />
<Cite gml="objects/oTransition/alarm_0.gml:239-244" />.

## Locations

Level generation makes a Megamouth only on [[flooded-level|flooded levels]]. Each level of
[[jungle|the Jungle]], levels 5–8, has a 1 in 10 chance of being flooded, unless the player is on his way to
the [[black-market|Black Market]] <Cite gml="objects/oTransition/step.gml:20-23" />
<Cite gml="scripts/scrClearGlobals.gml:57" />. A flooded level opens with "I CAN HEAR RUSHING WATER...", and
its two bottom rows of rooms are a lake <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/alarm_0.gml:34-36" />
<Cite gml="scripts/scrLevelGen.gml:194-210" />. One of the four rooms along the lake's bottom, never the one
in the exit room's column, is the Megamouth's, a room of water with one Megamouth in
it <Cite gml="scripts/scrLevelGen.gml:203-209" /> <Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGen2.gml:222-228" />
<Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGen2.gml:390-394" />.

Custom levels can have them, as the [[level-editor|level editor]]'s tile `{`, a Megamouth in a block of water
four tiles wide and two high <Cite gml="scripts/scrCreateTileObj.gml:177-188" />.

## Tips

- A Megamouth only attacks a swimming player, so its lake can be crossed above the water without it giving
  chase.
- It turns slowly: a player who gets behind it has its turning animation and a 40-step pause to get away.
- One swing of the Whip can take several of its hit points, as can a Machete's.
- A Megamouth out of the water dies within 40 steps, so a Bomb that breaks the lake bed and drains the lake
  kills it.

## Trivia

- Its object is named `oJaws`, its sprites `sJaws...`, and its code calls it `"Mega Mouth"`, two words, while
  the game's tables name it MEGAMOUTH <Cite gml="objects/oJaws/create.gml:3" />
  <Cite gml="scripts/scrWriteStats.gml:128" />.
- That spelling means its kills of the player are never counted. The touch code that tallies deaths by
  monster checks for `"Megamouth"`, which no monster's name matches, so the deaths-by-enemy table the game
  writes to `stats.txt` always shows ??? and 0 in the Megamouth's row
  <Cite gml="objects/oEnemy/collision_oCharacter.gml:41" /> <Cite gml="scripts/scrWriteStats.gml:110-116" />.
