Thin ice is a slab that's solid until the player has stood on it for a second. It isn't slippery, as
[[ice|Ice]] is; it melts. As it thins, it's drawn thinner, so how far gone a slab is shows, and once it's gone
it's gone for good.

## How it works

<Clip id="thin-ice/melt" />

A slab of thin ice starts <Stat id="thin-ice.thickness" bare /> thick <Cite gml="objects/oThinIce/create.gml:3" />.
Each step the player touches its top, it loses <Stat id="thin-ice.wear" bare />, and drips water now and then,
a 1 in 100 chance a step; its picture gets thinner with every 10 lost, in six stages, and at 0 it's gone
<Cite gml="objects/oThinIce/step.gml:1-13" />. Standing still or walking on it, that's 30 steps, 1 second. It
never thickens again.

The clip shows the [[moai|Moai]]'s: the [[ankh|Ankh]] has revived the player inside it, standing on the thin
ice in the statue's chest, which wears thinner under him for 30 steps and gives way, dropping him to the
[[hedjet|Hedjet]] and the exit below <Cite clip="thin-ice/melt" />.

Only the player wears it: monsters, items and the [[damsel|Damsel]] can stand on it for good. The
[[yeti-king|Yeti King]] is the exception: each step of his roar takes <Stat id="thin-ice.wear" bare /> from
every slab of thin ice on the level <Cite gml="objects/oYetiKing/step.gml:113-133" />.

## Interactions

- **[[bomb|Bombs]] and the [[mattock|Mattock]]** break it as any block.
- **The [[moai|Moai]].** Its slab is the floor of the chamber the Ankh revives the player in, so it gives way
  under him a second after he's back, and drops him to the Hedjet.

## Tips

- Thin ice is a floor for a second: a slab to cross, not to wait on. Its picture shows how much is left.
- Its wear adds up: a slab the player stood on for half a second earlier gives way after half a second the next
  time.
