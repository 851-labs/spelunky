Every level has a time limit, though the game never shows it as one. A clock runs from the moment the player
arrives; once it reaches a mark, a message and the music slowing down warn him, and at a second mark a
[[ghost|Ghost]], which nothing can hurt, comes in from the edge of the screen and drifts straight at him
through the walls. Its touch kills him whatever his hearts, so the warning means that it's time to make for
the exit.

## How it works

### The clock

The level's clock is the same one the [[scoring#the-clock|pause screen and the screen between levels]] show
as the level's time. It starts at 0 on each level <Cite gml="objects/oLevel/create.gml:11-14" />, and the
game adds 30 (the steps in a second) to it on every step that the player is alive on a level
<Cite gml="objects/oGame/step.gml:14-21" />. While the game is [[controls#pausing|paused]] it stops, as the
object that runs it is put to sleep <Cite replay="fidelity/replays/flow-pause-ghost-timer.txt" />. It counts
in thousandths of a second on its display, so it runs slow: a second on the clock is 33⅓ steps, about 1.11
seconds <Cite gml="objects/oScreen/step_begin.gml:94-114" />.

### The warning and the Ghost

On every step, once the clock has passed each mark, the game checks <Cite gml="objects/oGame/step.gml:23-47" />:

1. **Past <Stat id="ghost-timer.warning" bare />**, 4,000 steps in, the message "A CHILL RUNS UP YOUR
   SPINE..." and "LET'S GET OUT OF HERE!" shows at the foot of the screen, once, and the area's music slows
   and deepens: its playback frequency drops by 100 on each of the next 100 steps <Cite gml="objects/oGame/step.gml:26-37" />
   <Cite gml="objects/oLevel/step.gml:1-5" /> <Cite gml="scripts/scrMusicFade.gml:1-12" />.
2. **Past <Stat id="ghost-timer.ghost" bare />**, 5,000 steps in, a Ghost appears halfway down the edge of
   the view: the right edge if the player is in the right half of the level, the left otherwise. The game
   marks the level as having its ghost, so no second one comes <Cite gml="objects/oGame/step.gml:39-46" />.

Neither happens:

- on the first level, the [[tutorial|Tutorial]] or [[olmecs-lair|Olmec's Lair]];
- on a custom level from the [[level-editor|level editor]];
- while the player wears the [[hedjet|Hedjet]];
- while he's going out through the exit, as himself or as the [[damsel|Damsel]]
  <Cite gml="objects/oGame/step.gml:26-28" />.

The Hedjet only stops the clock bringing one: a Ghost already on the level stays. What the Ghost does once
it's there, and why nothing harms it, is on [[ghost|its page]].

### Other ghosts

A Ghost that comes another way marks the level as having one too, so the clock sends none after it:

| What sends it | When | |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A destroyed [[kalis-altar\|altar of Kali's]] | The third the player has defiled in the game, and each after, if the level has no Ghost yet; the level goes dark as well | <Cite gml="objects/oSacAltarLeft/destroy.gml:39-58" /> |
| The [[crystal-skull\|Crystal Skull]] | Picked up on a Jungle graveyard level ([[restless-dead]]), if the level has no Ghost yet | <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:1232-1239" /> |

Each comes from the same place as the clock's, at once. The Sun Room, a bonus room, sends its own when the
player has 80 points there <Cite gml="objects/oSunRoom/alarm_0.gml:9-14" />; it isn't a level, so its clock
doesn't run.

## Quirks

- **The warning shows for half as long on a lit level.** It's set to last 200 steps, but on a level that
  isn't dark two pieces of code each count it down, so it goes after 100, as every message does
  ([[messages-halved-on-lit-levels]]) <Cite quirk="1" row="Messages last half as long on a lit level." />.
- **The clock decides whether the next level can be dark.** The screen between levels allows a dark level
  only if the level just finished took more than 20 seconds past the minute on the clock, so a level that
  took 1:05 rules one out, as one that took 0:05 does <Cite quirk="1" row="A dark level needs the previous level's clock" />.
