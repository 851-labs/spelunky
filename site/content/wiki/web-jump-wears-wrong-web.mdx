Caught in a [[web|web]], the player jumps his way out: each jump wears the web down, and it breaks on the
eleventh. But the jump looks for the web in one place and wears down the one it finds in another. Where two
webs meet, it can wear down the one beside him, so his own holds out for longer and the other breaks first.

## What happens

The player falls into the right-hand one of three webs side by side and jumps again and again. Most of his
jumps fade the web beside him, not his own: that one breaks on the fourteenth jump, while the web holding him
is barely worn <Cite clip="quirks/web-jump-wears-wrong-web" />.

<Clip id="quirks/web-jump-wears-wrong-web" />

## Why

A web starts with a `life` of 12, fades as it goes down, and breaks at 1
<Cite gml="objects/oWeb/create.gml:1" /> <Cite gml="objects/oWeb/step.gml:1-3" />. The player's jump in one
finds it and wears it down in two different ways:

```gml scripts/characterStepEvent.gml:294-297
if (kJumpPressed and collision_point(x, y, oWeb, 0, 0))
{
    obj = instance_place(x, y, oWeb);
    obj.life -= 1;
```

`collision_point` tests for a web at one point, the player's middle. `instance_place` tests his whole body
there, and returns the first web it overlaps in the game's list of webs (in the replay below, the order
they were made), which needn't be the one he's in. His body is his sprite's frame, which changes as he
jumps. So in the later-made of two webs, near where they meet, some of his jumps wear down the other one. In
the earlier-made one, it's found first, and his jumps wear it down as they're meant to <Cite quirk="1" />.

## Reproducing it

Get caught in the right-hand web of a pair, near the left one, and jump out: it takes more than eleven jumps,
and the left one fades. The [[giant-spider|Giant Spider]] hangs above two webs side by side, the right-hand one
made second <Cite gml="objects/oGiantSpiderHang/create.gml:26-27" />, and the level's webs can fill alcoves
and tunnels next to each other. The replay of the clip's staged scene, on a custom level, drops the player into
the right-hand one of three webs; eleven of his fourteen jumps wear down the middle web, which breaks on the
last, and three his own <Cite replay="fidelity/replays/trap-web-jump-wrong-web.txt" />.

## In the port

The spelunky.ai port reproduces it exactly: it's in docs/quirks.md's list of the 1.1 source's bugs
<Cite quirk="1" />, and the oracle's fingerprint of the replay pins each jump's web. No option turns it off.
