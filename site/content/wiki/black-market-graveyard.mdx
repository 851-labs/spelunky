The [[black-market|Black Market]] is a Jungle level, and every Jungle level rolls for being a
[[restless-dead|graveyard]]. The market's is rolled too, once its rooms are laid out, so one market in ten is a
graveyard, with its graves and its dead. But the market's welcome is the level's message, and the graveyard's
comes second only on a dark level, which the market never is, so the game never says so.

## What happens

The player drops from the Black Market's entrance onto a grave below its shops. The market rolled the graveyard,
with eight graves and three Zombies, and the game welcomes him to the Black Market. THE DEAD ARE RESTLESS! never
comes <Cite clip="quirks/market-graveyard" />.

<Clip id="quirks/market-graveyard" />

## Why

`scrInitLevel` lays out the level's rooms, the market's included, and then rolls the graveyard for any Jungle level:

```gml scripts/scrInitLevel.gml:145-148
scrLevelGen();

global.cemetary = false;
if (global.levelType == 1 and rand(1,global.probCemetary) == 1) global.cemetary = true;
```

Nothing there skips the market, and `scrEntityGen`'s Jungle branch, which the market runs, makes a graveyard of
it: graves on its earth, with their treasure <Cite gml="scripts/scrEntityGen.gml:176-205" />,
[[zombie|Zombies]] and [[vampire|Vampires]] in place of the Jungle's monsters, without the market's rule that
keeps Mantraps off its shops' floors <Cite gml="scripts/scrEntityGen.gml:280-288" />, and
[[dead-fish|Dead Fish]] in place of Piranhas <Cite gml="scripts/scrEntityGen.gml:332" />.

The level's first message, from `oPlayer1`'s alarm 0, takes the market before the graveyard
<Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/alarm_0.gml:14-33" />. The second, from alarm 1, shows the graveyard's only on a dark
level:

```gml objects/oPlayer1/alarm_1.gml:11-17
else if (global.cemetary and global.darkLevel)
{
    global.message = "THE DEAD ARE RESTLESS!";
    global.message2 = "";
    global.messageTimer = 200;
    if (global.lake) alarm[4] = 210;
}
```

`scrInitLevel` clears `global.darkLevel` on the market, `if (global.blackMarket) global.darkLevel = false;`
<Cite gml="scripts/scrInitLevel.gml:176" />, so the graveyard's message never shows there <Cite quirk="1" />.

## Reproducing it

Take the Black Market's hidden door on level 5, 6 or 7, until a market comes with graves on its earth; one in ten
does. It welcomes the player as any market does, and says nothing more. The replay, with seed 9, reaches the
market on level 6 as a graveyard, with eight graves and three Zombies: "WELCOME TO THE BLACK MARKET!" shows, and
"THE DEAD ARE RESTLESS!" never does. The player drops from the entrance onto the edge of a grave, which is solid
<Cite replay="fidelity/replays/jungle-seed-9-market-graveyard.txt" />.

## In the port

The spelunky.ai port reproduces it exactly: it's in docs/quirks.md's list of the 1.1 source's bugs
<Cite quirk="1" />, and the oracle's fingerprint of the replay pins the graves, the Zombies and the messages. No
option turns it off.
