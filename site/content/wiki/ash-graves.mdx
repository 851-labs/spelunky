A [[restless-dead|graveyard level]] in the [[jungle|Jungle]] puts graves on its blocks, and a few of them are
Ash graves, with their own sprite and a free [[shotgun|Shotgun]] in the block underneath. The code keeps a flag
to make one at most, but sets a different variable from the one it checks, so a level can have two or more.

## What happens

A graveyard level opens with two Ash graves in view, each with a free Shotgun buried in the block under it
<Cite clip="quirks/ash-graves" />.

<Clip id="quirks/ash-graves" />

## Why

The level's entity script clears `global.ashGrave`, then goes over the Jungle's blocks. Each block with nothing
above it, away from the entrance, the exit and six columns, gets a grave 1 time in 20, and a grave is an Ash grave, with a Shotgun at no cost in the
block under it, 1 time in 40 if `global.ashGrave` isn't set <Cite gml="scripts/scrEntityGen.gml:175-195" />:

```gml scripts/scrEntityGen.gml:187-195
            obj = instance_create(x, y-16, oGrave);
            if (not global.ashGrave and rand(1,40) == 1)
            {
                obj.sprite_index = sGraveAsh;
                obj = instance_create(x+8, y+8, oShotgun);
                obj.cost = 0;
                obj.forSale = false;
                ashGrave = true;
            }
```

The last line sets `ashGrave` without `global.`. The code runs inside a `with` over the blocks, so it makes a
variable on the block, and `global.ashGrave` stays false: every grave rolls its 1 in 40 afresh
<Cite quirk="1" />.

## Reproducing it

It depends on the level's seed, and it's rare: of 800 Jungle seeds, 73 start on a graveyard level, 20 of those
have an Ash grave, and 3 have two <Cite quirk="1" />. The replays pin two of them: seed 420's graveyard level,
with an Ash grave and a Shotgun at two places and the flag set on the two blocks while the global stays
false <Cite replay="fidelity/replays/item-seed-420-ash-graves.txt" />, and seed 244's, whose two Ash graves are
in view from the entrance, as in the clip <Cite replay="fidelity/replays/item-seed-244-ash-graves.txt" />.

## In the port

The spelunky.ai port reproduces it exactly: it's in docs/quirks.md's list of the 1.1 source's bugs
<Cite quirk="1" />, and the oracle's fingerprints of the replays pin the graves' places. No option turns it off.
