Its Shopkeeper welcomes the player with "WELCOME TO TONY'S BOMB SHOP!", his name one of 32 drawn at random
<Cite gml="objects/oShopkeeper/step.gml:127" /> <Cite gml="scripts/scrGetName.gml:24-60" />. It sells
the player's [[bomb|bombs]], in [[bomb-bag|Bomb Bags]] and [[bomb-box|Bomb Boxes]], and the
[[paste|Paste]] that makes them sticky. Like every shop it's a side room off the way through a level, with
four wares on its floor and the [[shopkeeper|Shopkeeper]] beside them.

## Stock

The shop has four wares, and each is rolled on its own <Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGen.gml:173-191" />
<Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGen.gml:448-451" />. The rolls go down the table until one hits; a hit on the
Paste when the level already has one rolls again from the top
<Cite gml="scripts/scrShopItemsGen.gml:24-35" />:

| Ware | Roll | |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [[paste\|Paste]] | 1 in 5, if the level has none | <Cite gml="scripts/scrShopItemsGen.gml:28-31" /> |
| [[bomb-box\|Bomb Box]], <Stat id="bomb-box.bombs" /> | Otherwise 1 in 4 | <Cite gml="scripts/scrShopItemsGen.gml:32" /> |
| [[bomb-bag\|Bomb Bag]], <Stat id="bomb-bag.bombs" /> | Otherwise | <Cite gml="scripts/scrShopItemsGen.gml:33" /> |

So until the level has a Paste, each ware is a Paste one time in five, a Bomb Box one time in five (4 in
5 times 1 in 4) and a Bomb Bag three times in five; once it has one, a Box one time in four and a Bag
otherwise. The check is for a Paste on the level, not for the player's, so a Bomb Shop sells the Paste
to a player who already has it ([[owned-equipment-for-sale]]) <Cite gml="scripts/scrShopItemsGen.gml:30" /> <Cite quirk="1" row="Shops stock equipment the player already has." />.

<Cite gml="scripts/scrShopItemsGen.gml:179-191" />. Each ware's own page has its price on every level:

A box of 12 costs four times a bag of 3, so a bomb costs the same either way. A Black Market's Bomb Shop
prices its wares by the level the market is on <Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGenMarket.gml:357-360" />.

## Shopkeeper

<Clip id="bomb-shop/buy" />

The Shopkeeper sells as in every shop ([[general-store#shopkeeper|the General Store]] has the details): he
names the price of a ware the player holds, and the pay key buys it
<Cite gml="objects/oShopkeeper/step.gml:72-85" /> <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:1359-1389" />. A
bag or a box is used up as it's bought, adding its bombs to the player's, and the Paste goes on, making
every bomb he throws from then on sticky <Cite gml="scripts/scrStealItem.gml:68-102" />. In the clip, the
player picks up a bag priced at $2,500 and pays for it, and his bombs go from 4 to 7
<Cite clip="bomb-shop/buy" />.

The wares are bombs not yet lit, and nothing in them angers him. A bomb the player lights inside the shop
within 96 pixels of him does ("TERRORIST!"), and so does breaking a block of the shop's walls or floor
("DIE, YOU VANDAL!") <Cite gml="objects/oBomb/step.gml:6-17" /> <Cite gml="objects/oSolid/destroy.gml:1-7" />.
Either angers every Shopkeeper near the screen, not only the shop's, and in a [[black-market|Black Market]]
a burning bomb adds to the thief count again on every step for each one off screen
([[bomb-angers-every-shopkeeper]]; [[general-store#stealing|the General Store]] has the details) <Cite quirk="1" row="A lit bomb in a shop angers every active Shopkeeper, and those off screen again on every step." />
<Cite replay="fidelity/replays/shop-seed-9-market-bomb.txt" />.
Angered, he gives the wares away and attacks with his [[shotgun|Shotgun]]
<Cite gml="objects/oShopkeeper/step.gml:337-408" />.

## Stealing

Carrying a ware out of the shop's room unpaid angers the Shopkeeper ("COME BACK HERE, THIEF!") and gives
it to the player as if bought <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:541-557" />
<Cite gml="objects/oItem/step.gml:17-34" />. A player who runs and jumps out of an Ice Caves Bomb Shop
with the Paste has it on as he leaves <Cite
replay="fidelity/replays/item-seed-53-paste.txt" />. A theft marks the player a thief into the next
level, whose exit has a Shopkeeper waiting by it and whose shops' wares are free
([[general-store#stealing|the General Store]] has the rules) <Cite gml="scripts/scrInitLevel.gml:245-257" />.

## Locations

A level from 2 to 15 that rolls a shop builds one of seven kinds, each as likely, so a shop is a Bomb Shop
one time in seven, in every area and on a [[yeti-lair|Yeti lair]] level too (one in six on a Temple
[[sacrifice-pit|sacrifice pit]] level, which never has a [[kissing-parlor|Kissing Parlor]]) <Cite
gml="scripts/scrLevelGen.gml:239-297" /> <Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGen.gml:173-204" /> <Cite
gml="scripts/scrRoomGenYeti.gml:149-181" />
<Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGen4.gml:155-188" />.
[[mines#shops|The Mines]] has how levels roll shops. A [[black-market|Black Market]] has five shops that
sell items, as a rule one of each kind, a Bomb Shop among them ([[two-general-stores]] is the exception) <Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGenMarket.gml:117-140" /> <Cite quirk="1" row="A Black Market's fifth item shop can be a second General Store." />.
