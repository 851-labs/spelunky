The Yeti King lives in the [[yeti-lair|Yeti lair]], a special level of [[ice-caves|the Ice Caves]], in a
room with an ice ceiling and four [[yeti|Yetis]]. It is twice a Yeti's size and walks back and forth across
its room, turning to face the player when he's behind it. When he's in front of it, it stops and yells,
which brings blocks of ice down from the ceiling. It can't be stunned or picked up, and with
<Stat id="yeti-king.hp" />, a [[bomb|Bomb]] is the one thing that kills it in one go.

## Behaviour

The Yeti King walks at <Stat id="yeti-king.walk-speed" /> (<Stat id="yeti-king.walk-speed"
as="per-second" />) the way it faces, and turns around at a wall, or when the player is behind it at the
height of its feet. Turning takes a moment, during which it stands still, and after a turn it won't turn
toward the player again for 30 steps (1 second) <Cite gml="objects/oYetiKing/step.gml:63-107" />
<Cite gml="objects/oYetiKing/other_animation_end.gml:1-10" />.

<Clip id="yeti-king/yell" />

When the player is anywhere in front of it, within 32 pixels of its middle's height, it stops and yells,
at most once every <Stat id="yeti-king.yell-cooldown" /> (<Stat id="yeti-king.yell-cooldown"
as="seconds" />) <Cite gml="objects/oYetiKing/step.gml:76-82" /> <Cite gml="objects/oYetiKing/step.gml:108-112" />.
For part of the yell, each step, every block of ice within <Stat id="yeti-king.yell-range" /> of it that
isn't right above it (more than 16 pixels to either side of its middle) has a 1 in 60 chance of coming loose
as a block that falls, and every block of thin ice in the level wears thinner
<Cite gml="objects/oYetiKing/step.gml:113-133" />. After the yell it stands for 30 steps, then walks on
<Cite gml="objects/oYetiKing/other_animation_end.gml:11-16" />. The clip shows the Yeti King yelling at the
player across its room, and three blocks of the ceiling coming loose and falling.

The Yeti King only acts while it's on screen or just off it
<Cite gml="objects/oYetiKing/step.gml:3-5" />.

## Damage

<Clip id="yeti-king/swat" />

When the player touches the Yeti King without landing on it, and isn't invincible, he loses
<Stat id="yeti-king.damage" />, is knocked away from it (and upward, if he's above it), and blinks,
invincible, for 30 steps (1 second) <Cite gml="objects/oYetiKing/collision_oCharacter.gml:16-33" />. That's
on the game's own levels only: in a custom level, the touch knocks him back and costs nothing
<Cite gml="objects/oYetiKing/collision_oCharacter.gml:27-31" /> <Cite gml="scripts/isRealLevel.gml:7-8" /> <Cite quirk="1" />. So the
clip is from a real Yeti lair: the Yeti King yells at the crouching player, who then walks into it, drops from
4 hearts to 2 and is knocked away from it, blinking <Cite clip="yeti-king/swat" />.

The Whip's crack does nothing to the Yeti King, as it doesn't puncture, but the wind-up behind the player's
back hurts it through the code most monsters share, with no pause between hits ([[whip-every-step]])
<Cite gml="objects/oWhip/create.gml:5" /> <Cite gml="objects/oEnemy/collision_oWhipPre.gml:1" /> <Cite quirk="1" />. A thrown
item hurts it every step it's inside it, and pushes it along at a third of its own speed, so the two move
together: the clip shows a [[rock|Rock]] taking 9 of its hit points <Cite gml="objects/oItem/step.gml:341-350" />
<Cite clip="yeti-king/thrown-rock" />.

<Clip id="yeti-king/bomb" />

When its hit points run out, the Yeti King bursts into blood and four bones and leaves
<Stat id="yeti-king.drops" /> things, flung about, each [[spike-shoes|Spike Shoes]] with a 1 in 12 chance,
otherwise a [[big-sapphire|Big Sapphire]] or a [[rope-pile|Rope Pile]] at even odds; the Spike Shoes and
Rope Piles are free <Cite gml="objects/oYetiKing/step.gml:16-40" />. The clip shows a Bomb dropped at its
feet while it yells killing it, and the Big Sapphires and the Rope Pile it leaves. A Yeti King killed counts
toward the player's kills, and the screen between levels draws a Yeti King icon for it
<Cite gml="objects/oYetiKing/step.gml:41-46" /> <Cite gml="objects/oTransition/alarm_0.gml:245-249" />. It
leaves no body, can't be picked up, and, never stunned, can't be sacrificed to Kali
<Cite gml="objects/oYetiKing/create.gml:22" /> <Cite gml="objects/oEnemy/step.gml:130-131" />.

## Locations

The Yeti King is made only in the [[yeti-lair|Yeti lair]]. An [[ice-caves|Ice Caves]] level that is
neither the [[moai|Moai]]'s nor an alien craft has a 1 in 6 chance of being a Yeti lair, from the level after the
Moai's on <Cite gml="scripts/scrLevelGen.gml:213-237" /> <Cite gml="scripts/scrClearGlobals.gml:58" />
<Cite quirk="1" />. In a lair, until the Yeti King has been placed, each room the level's path crosses
sideways (or, 1 time in 4, a side room off it) is its room with a 1 in 10 chance; in the second row of
rooms, a 1 in 3 roll follows when that fails, in the third a 1 in 2 roll and then the 1 in 3, and in the
bottom row it's certain
<Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGenYeti.gml:80" /> <Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGenYeti.gml:101-110" />. So a lair
has at most one. Its room has a ceiling of ice, some blocks left out at random, above the Yeti King and
four Yetis
<Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGenYeti.gml:127" />.

In custom levels, it's the [[level-editor|level editor]]'s tile `Y`
<Cite gml="scripts/scrCreateTileObj.gml:201-204" />.

## Tips

- A Bomb kills the Yeti King at once; while it yells it stands still, which is the time to place one.
- Its yell is only set off by the player in front of it, so approaching from behind avoids the falling ice,
  but it turns to face a player behind it at its feet.
- The Whip hurts it only with the wind-up, behind the player's back, so it's best swung with his back to
  the Yeti King (facing it, the wind-up only reaches it from inside it).
- A thrown Rock does more than a Whip swing, as long as it stays inside the Yeti King.
