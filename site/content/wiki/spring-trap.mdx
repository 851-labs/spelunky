A Spring Trap is a spring plate lying on the floor of [[ice-caves|the Ice Caves]]. Stepping onto it throws the
player straight up at <Stat id="spring-trap.launch-speed" /> (<Stat id="spring-trap.launch-speed"
as="per-second" />), and each time he comes back down on it, it throws him up again. It never hurts anyone:
its danger is where it throws him, and it saves him from a long fall's damage.

## How it works

<Clip id="spring-trap/bounce" />

When the player is on the ground over its middle (within 6 pixels), standing, walking, ducking or looking up,
the spring lifts him 16 pixels and throws him upward at <Stat id="spring-trap.launch-speed" />. It then stays
sprung for <Stat id="spring-trap.reset" /> and until its animation ends, and can't throw anything else until
then <Cite gml="objects/oSpringTrap/collision_oPlayer1.gml:1-15" /> <Cite gml="objects/oSpringTrap/other_animation_end.gml:1-5" />
<Cite gml="objects/oSpringTrap/step.gml:4" />. The player doesn't have to press anything: landing on it is
being on the ground over it. The clip shows him landing on a spring and being thrown up three times, every 24
steps.

A monster that doesn't fly is thrown up at <Stat id="spring-trap.monster-launch-speed" /> and given 1 pixel a
step more speed the way it faces; an item that isn't held, at the same speed, from 24 pixels up
<Cite gml="objects/oSpringTrap/collision_oEnemy.gml:1-14" /> <Cite gml="objects/oSpringTrap/collision_oItem.gml:1-19" />.

## Damage

None: nothing in a Spring Trap's code hurts. Landing on one is safe however far the player falls, as the
check for a long fall's damage skips a player on a spring <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:268-276" />.

## Locations

[[level-generation|Level generation]] puts Spring Traps only in [[ice-caves|the Ice Caves]], on the dark rock, not on ice: on each
rock block with four open blocks above it, no monster or spikes on it, more than 64 pixels from the entrance
and the exit, and not in a shop, a 1 in 10 chance, before the roll for a [[yeti|Yeti]]
<Cite gml="scripts/scrEntityGen.gml:362-371" />.

They're also in the [[moon-room|Moon Room]] and the [[sun-room|Sun Room]], bonus rooms <Cite room="rMoon" /> <Cite room="rSun" />, and in
custom levels as the [[level-editor|level editor]]'s tile `_` <Cite gml="scripts/scrCreateTileObj.gml:245-248" />.

## Avoiding

- **Step around it.** It only throws what's over its middle, within 6 pixels, so a jump over it, or a step
  onto its edge, is safe.
- **Remove its floor.** A Spring Trap on screen with nothing under it disappears
  <Cite gml="objects/oSpringTrap/step.gml:1-6" />. In the second clip a [[ufo|UFO]]'s laser destroys the block
  under one, and it's gone the next step.
- **Bombs don't destroy it directly.** It isn't a block, and a [[bomb|Bomb]] that went off beside one left it
  in place <Cite replay="fidelity/replays/trap-spring.txt" />; a blast that breaks the block under it removes it.

## Interactions

- **[[spring-shoes|Spring Shoes]]** make the player's own jumps higher; a Spring Trap throws him whether he
  wears them or not.
- **Exits.** A player going through a door isn't thrown, even over a spring
  <Cite gml="objects/oSpringTrap/collision_oPlayer1.gml:3" />.

## Trivia

- The spring's code for monsters and the one for items differ: a monster is lifted 16 pixels, an item 24, and
  only a [[damsel|Damsel]] among items is nudged the way it faces <Cite gml="objects/oSpringTrap/collision_oItem.gml:7-17" />.
