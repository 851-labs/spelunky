A push block moves a pixel at a time ahead of the player, until something solid is in its way. Then it should
stop him too. But the code that moves him only stops the block, so a player who keeps pushing creeps into it at
the pushing speed, and once his middle is inside it, he's crushed. In the
[[high-scores-room|high scores room]], its block stops right past the reset button, so that's where
it shows.

## What happens

In the high scores room, the player has pushed the block onto the reset button, and it has stopped against the
sign past it. He keeps pushing and creeps into the block until it crushes him: blood, and he's gone, but no game
over follows <Cite clip="quirks/highscores-block-crush" />.

<Clip id="quirks/highscores-block-crush" />

## Why

`moveTo` moves the player a pixel at a time, and when a block that can be pushed is in his way, it moves the
block a pixel too, unless something solid is beyond it:

```gml scripts/moveTo.gml:46-55
      if object_get_parent(solidId.object_index)=oMoveableSolid and canPushMoveableSolids()
      {
        //we must move the moveable solid, unless there is another solid (moveable or non-moveable) in it's way
        with solidId
        {
          if place_meeting(x+1,y,oSolid)      //there will be a collision!
          {
            break
          }
          else
```

The `break` is meant to stop the player's loop. But in GameMaker 8 a `break` inside `with` only leaves the
`with`, so the loop goes on and steps the player into the block anyway <Cite quirk="2" />. Nothing else stops
him: the step keeps him pushing while a pushable block is beside him
<Cite gml="scripts/characterStepEvent.gml:106-107" /> <Cite gml="scripts/characterStepEvent.gml:158-168" />,
so he moves into it at the pushing speed, a pixel every other step, until his middle is inside it. Then his
step's crush check takes 99 hearts and hides him <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:1490-1511" />. In the high
scores room, the room's own guard puts his hearts back the next step <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:1-7" />,
and the code that ends the game only runs on a level, so he's left invisible, with no game over
<Cite quirk="1" />.

## Reproducing it

Push a block until it stops against something solid, and keep pushing. It's the same with any block and any
solid. In the high scores room, push the block onto the reset button and keep holding toward it: its only stop is
the sign just past the button. The replay of the clip does that: the block stops on the button (frame 835), and
the player, still pushing, is crushed ten steps later (845), invisible and with no game over, and his hearts are
back to 4 the step after <Cite replay="fidelity/replays/flow-highscores-reset-crush.txt" />.

## In the port

The spelunky.ai port reproduces it exactly: it's in docs/quirks.md's list of the 1.1 source's bugs
<Cite quirk="1" />, and the oracle's fingerprint of the replay pins the block's stop and the crush. No option
turns it off.
