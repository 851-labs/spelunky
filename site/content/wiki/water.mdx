Water fills pools in [[jungle|the Jungle]]'s rooms and [[black-market|the Black Market]]'s, and the lake of a
[[flooded-level|flooded level]]. The player swims in it: it slows his falls, ends a fall without its damage, and
lets him kick up toward the surface. Some monsters change in it, and some only hunt a player who's in it.
[[lava|Lava]] is, to the code, a kind of water too, but what it does is its own page's.

## How it works

<Clip id="water/swim" />

The clip shows a fall into a pool: the player walks off a ledge high above it, and has been falling for more
than the 16 steps that would hurt and stun him on landing, when he goes into the water unhurt. He walks along
the bottom, breathing out bubbles, then kicks up to the surface and climbs out
<Cite clip="water/swim" />.

### Going in

When the middle of the player goes into water, it splashes, and from then on he's swimming
<Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:177-186" />. A [[cape|Cape]] closes in water
<Cite gml="scripts/characterStepEvent.gml:750-752" />.

### Falling and sinking

While he's swimming, a fall doesn't count: the time he has been falling goes back to nothing each step, so a
fall into water never ends in a long drop's stun or lost hearts, however far he came
<Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:302-304" /> <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:268-289" />. Sinking, he
keeps only half his falling speed each step, where in the air he keeps all of it; otherwise, with his head
under, he keeps 0.9 of his speed up or down <Cite gml="scripts/characterStepEvent.gml:750-771" />. He walks along the bottom as on any
floor. Every <Stat id="water.bubble-interval" /> (<Stat id="water.bubble-interval" as="seconds" />) he swims he
breathes out a bubble, which rises until it leaves the water <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:305-310" />
<Cite gml="objects/oBubble/step.gml:1-6" />.

### Swimming up

The jump key kicks the player up whenever there's water just above his head, on the bottom or not: a jump's
push, as on the ground, and 3 pixels per step more speed upward besides <Cite gml="scripts/characterStepEvent.gml:308-319" /> <Cite gml="scripts/characterStepEvent.gml:338-342" />
<Cite gml="scripts/isCollisionWaterTop.gml:6-9" />. With his head out at the surface, it's an ordinary jump,
which takes him out onto a bank. Out of the water, he stops swimming
<Cite gml="scripts/characterStepEvent.gml:767-771" />.

### Draining

Water stays where it is until a block around it is destroyed. Then, each step, every block of water that has
empty space, nothing solid and no water, to its left, to its right or below it goes, dripping, until none does: a pool whose
side or floor is blown open drains out through the hole <Cite gml="objects/oSolid/destroy.gml:35" />
<Cite gml="objects/oGame/step.gml:49-104" /> <Cite gml="objects/oWaterSwim/destroy.gml:1-4" />. Water never
flows anywhere: it only goes.

## Interactions

- **Fish.** Each block of water outside a block of earth gets a [[piranha|Piranha]] with a 1 in
  <Stat id="water.fish-chance" bare /> chance, or on a graveyard level a [[dead-fish|Dead Fish]]
  <Cite gml="scripts/scrEntityGen.gml:326-335" />. Both go after a player who's swimming, and only then
  <Cite gml="objects/oPiranha/step.gml:32" /> <Cite gml="objects/oDeadFish/step.gml:52" />.
- **[[bat|Bats]]** don't wake for, or chase, a player who's swimming <Cite gml="objects/oBat/step.gml:23-36" />.
- **Stomping.** The stomp check most monsters share needs a player who isn't swimming, so landing on a monster
  in water doesn't stomp it <Cite gml="objects/oEnemy/collision_oCharacter.gml:6" />.
- **Monsters in water** splash and fall at a third of their gravity, 0.2 rather than 0.6, in the code most
  monsters share <Cite gml="objects/oEnemy/create.gml:17-18" />, and a [[fire-frog|Fire Frog]] that goes in turns into an ordinary [[frog|Frog]]
  <Cite gml="objects/oEnemy/step.gml:34-53" />.
- **Rubble** slows in water, and a drip of water ends in it <Cite gml="objects/oRubblePiece/step.gml:5-10" />.

## Tips

- A drop into water is safe from any height: a pool at the foot of a long shaft is a way down without losing
  hearts.
- Getting out takes a bank no higher than a jump from the surface; from the bottom, kicks bring the player up
  to it first.
- Swimming keeps [[bat|Bats]] off, but draws [[piranha|Piranhas]].

## Trivia

- Only water in the top 512 pixels of a level drains, except in a custom level, and none drains in
  [[olmecs-lair|Olmec's Lair]] <Cite gml="objects/oGame/step.gml:55-57" />. A safety count stops the draining
  after 100,000 blocks have been looked at <Cite gml="objects/oGame/step.gml:96-97" />.
- Every block of water is set up with the type "Water", which the draining code reads to tell it from lava's
  <Cite gml="objects/oWater/create.gml:3" /> <Cite gml="objects/oGame/step.gml:62-66" />.
