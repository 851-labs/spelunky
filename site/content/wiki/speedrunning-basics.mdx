The game keeps its own record of a fast run: the best time in the high scores, the game's clock at a win,
which earns a trophy and, at <Stat id="scoring.time-gold" bare /> seconds or less, the door to the Moon Room
([[scoring#the-high-scores|the high scores]]). The clock counts only the steps spent in levels, so what a
run is timed on is the play itself: how fast the player gets from each entrance to its exit, through all
sixteen levels, the last of them [[olmecs-lair|Olmec's Lair]]. One run, a win from the level 13 [[tunnel-man-shortcuts|shortcut]],
shows the clock stopping at the end and what a shortcut does to the record
<Cite replay="fidelity/replays/ending-seed-1.txt" />; the clips of those steps are cut from it, and the
others from replays of the moves and the high scores room.

## Requirements

- **A game started by the title screen's start door.** Only a win in such a game sets a best time: the
  [[tunnel-man|Tunnel Man]]'s [[tunnel-man-shortcuts|shortcut doors]] mark the game as a shortcut's, and its win leaves the time as
  it was <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/other_animation_end.gml:72-75" />
  <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/other_animation_end.gml:137-157" /> <Cite gml="scripts/scrUpdateHighscores.gml:54-62" />.
- **A win**, which takes beating Olmec ([[beating-olmec]] is the guide for him). Only a win records a time,
  not a death <Cite gml="scripts/scrUpdateHighscores.gml:57" />.

## Steps

1. **Start from the start door**, not a shortcut door. The clock starts at 0 with the game, and from then on
   gains 30 on each step that the player is alive in a level <Cite gml="objects/oGame/step.gml:14-21" />
   <Cite gml="scripts/scrClearGlobals.gml:119" />. A second on it is 33⅓ steps, about 1.11 real seconds
   ([[scoring#the-clock|the clock]]).
2. **Run.** Running is twice as fast as walking: <Stat id="spelunker.run-speed" /> against
   <Stat id="spelunker.walk-speed" />. The run key runs at once on the ground, and attack held while walking
   runs after 10 steps ([[controls#running|running]]) <Cite gml="scripts/characterStepEvent.gml:117-129" />
   <Cite gml="scripts/characterStepEvent.gml:685-698" />. A jump keeps the speed, as the slower limit is only
   set on the ground <Cite gml="scripts/characterStepEvent.gml:797-800" />, and goes farther from a run
   ([[spelunker#moving|the Spelunker's moves]]). In the clip, the player walks off a pillar, then runs off a
   ledge and jumps the gap at twice the speed <Cite clip="speedrunning-basics/run" />.

   <Clip id="speedrunning-basics/run" />

3. **Take each level's exit**: up, standing on the ground at its door
   <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:759-765" />. The clock only runs in a level, not in the screen between
   levels <Cite gml="scripts/isLevel.gml:7" />: in the run, it reads the same all through the screen
   between levels 13 and 14 <Cite replay="fidelity/replays/ending-seed-1.txt" />. That screen
   tallies the level's time and the game's ([[scoring#between-levels|between levels]]), as in the clip,
   after the first level <Cite clip="scoring/level-tally" />.

   <Clip id="scoring/level-tally" />

4. **Beat Olmec and take his door.** The clock stops there: the ending isn't a level
   ([[endings#the-door|the door]]) <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/other_animation_end.gml:132-136" />
   <Cite gml="scripts/isLevel.gml:7" />. In the clip, Olmec drowns, the door opens, and the player climbs out
   of the shaft and goes through it <Cite clip="olmecs-lair/exit" />; the clock reads the same from the
   ending's first scene to the credits' end <Cite replay="fidelity/replays/ending-seed-1.txt" />.

   <Clip id="olmecs-lair/exit" />

5. **Watch the ending through.** Its last scene shows the game's time after the final score
   ([[endings#the-final-score|the final score]]) <Cite gml="objects/oEnd3/draw.gml:19-36" />. In the clip,
   the time comes after the money <Cite clip="endings/tally" />.

   <Clip id="endings/tally" />

6. **Let the credits end.** The win goes into the high scores only when the credits fade out to the high
   scores room ([[endings#the-credits|the credits]]) <Cite gml="objects/oCredits2/step.gml:44-55" />. Its
   time is the clock's, cut to whole seconds, and it's kept if it's the first or beats the best
   <Cite gml="scripts/scrUpdateHighscores.gml:54-62" />.
7. **Check the trophy.** The high scores room gives the best time a bronze, silver or gold trophy, and a
   gold one, for <Stat id="scoring.time-gold" bare /> seconds or less, puts the Moon Room's door beside it
   ([[scoring#numbers|the thresholds]]) <Cite gml="objects/oHighscores/create.gml:50-66" />. The clip
   shows the room with all four gold trophies, each with its door, the Moon Room's under the time's trophy
   <Cite clip="scoring/reset" />.

   <Clip id="scoring/reset" />

## Tips

- Pausing stops the clock with everything else, so a pause costs a run nothing
  ([[controls#pausing|pausing]]) <Cite gml="objects/oScreen/step_begin.gml:34-61" />.
- Neither does the screen between levels, the [[tunnel-man|Tunnel Man]]'s requests on it included: it isn't
  a level <Cite gml="scripts/isLevel.gml:7" />. The Tunnel Man's shortcuts start a game at level 5, 9 or 13
  ([[tunnel-man-shortcuts|the shortcuts]]), a way to play the later areas without the earlier ones,
  though such a game's win sets no time.
- A level left fast keeps the next one lit. The screen between levels rules out a dark next level when the
  level just left took 20 seconds or less past the minute on its clock, which the code's comment calls
  not creating "a dark level if player is attempting a speed run" ([[ghost-timer#quirks|the clock's quirks]])
  <Cite gml="objects/oTransition/draw.gml:89-102" /> <Cite quirk="1" />. It reads only the seconds, so a
  level that took 1:05 rules one out as well as one that took 0:05 ([[dark-level#the-roll|the dark roll]]).
- The gold trophy's <Stat id="scoring.time-gold" bare /> seconds on the clock are 20,000 steps in levels, 11
  minutes 6⅔ seconds at the game's 30 steps a second <Cite gml="objects/oGame/step.gml:18-19" />.
- The [[ghost|Ghost]] comes to a player who stays 5,000 steps on a level after the first ([[ghost-timer]]),
  far longer than a fast run spends on one.
- The time's gold trophy is one of the four that let the player play as the Tunnel Man
  ([[tunnel-man#playing-as-him|playing as him]]) <Cite gml="objects/oTitle/create.gml:54-62" />.

## Common mistakes

- **Winning from a shortcut** ([[shortcut-win-no-time]]). The win counts, but the time stays as it was; with no best time before it,
  the high scores room shows TIME: 0:00 all the same, as it shows the time whenever there's a win
  ([[scoring#quirks|the scores' quirks]]) <Cite gml="scripts/scrUpdateHighscores.gml:57-62" />
  <Cite gml="objects/oHighscores/draw.gml:126-142" /> <Cite quirk="1" />. In the clip, after the run's win
  from the level 13 shortcut, the room has WINS: 1 and TIME: 0:00
  <Cite clip="speedrunning-basics/shortcut-win" /> <Cite replay="fidelity/replays/ending-seed-1.txt" />.

  <Clip id="speedrunning-basics/shortcut-win" />

- **Dying.** A death ends the game without a time <Cite gml="scripts/scrUpdateHighscores.gml:57" />. With the
  [[ankh|Ankh]], the player comes back instead, at the level's entrance as a rule, and the clock runs on
  <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:1853-1872" />.
- **Reading the Moon Room's goal as unlocked.** Climbing up beside the trophies, the panel shows the Moon
  Room's goal for any best time, though only the door means it's fast enough
  ([[moon-room-goal-any-win]]) <Cite gml="objects/oHighscores/draw.gml:46" /> <Cite quirk="1" />.
  In the clip, with a win at 11:40, the time's trophy is silver and there's no door, but the goal shows
  <Cite clip="quirks/moon-room-goal-any-win" />.

  <Clip id="quirks/moon-room-goal-any-win" />

- **A best time of exactly 16:00.** It earns no trophy, while 15:59 earns the bronze
  ([[scoring#quirks|the scores' quirks]]) <Cite gml="objects/oHighscores/create.gml:50-56" /> <Cite quirk="1" />
  <Cite replay="fidelity/replays/flow-highscores-time-960-no-trophy.txt" />.
