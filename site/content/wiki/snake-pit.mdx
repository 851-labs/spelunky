The pit is a column of rooms that [[level-generation|level generation]] sets aside from [[mines|the Mines]]' path: a room open
at the bottom, then a narrow shaft dropping one or two rooms, then a bottom room with no way out but up.
[[snake|Snakes]] wait in its walls and on its floor, beside two [[big-ruby|Big Rubies]] and a
[[mattock|Mattock]] buried in a brick. A level with a pit opens with the message "I HEAR SNAKES... I HATE
SNAKES!".

## Generation

### The roll

Once the path from the entrance to the exit is laid ([[mines#generation|the Mines' Generation]] has how),
level generation looks for a column with three side rooms (rooms the path doesn't cross) one above the
other. It checks the top row's four columns from left to right, then the second row's, and each place it
finds has a 1 in 8 chance of becoming a pit. The first that comes up is the pit, and the search stops, so a
level has one at most <Cite gml="scripts/scrLevelGen.gml:138-185" /> <Cite gml="scripts/scrClearGlobals.gml:55" />.
Every level of the Mines makes the roll, level 1 included, and no other area's does <Cite gml="scripts/scrLevelGen.gml:142" />.

A pit that starts in the top row runs down all four rows when the bottom row's room is a side room too, and
three rows otherwise; one that starts in the second row runs to the bottom
<Cite gml="scripts/scrLevelGen.gml:150-171" />. The pit's rooms stop being side rooms, so none of them can
be a shop, [[kalis-altar|Kali's altar]] or the [[golden-idol|Golden Idol]]'s room, which only side rooms become <Cite gml="scripts/scrLevelGen.gml:239-275" /> <Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGen.gml:85-121" />.

A level with a pit opens with "I HEAR SNAKES... I HATE SNAKES!"
<Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/alarm_0.gml:21-26" />. On a [[dark-level|dark level]], it comes after the dark
level's message <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/alarm_1.gml:5-10" />.

### The layout

| Room | What | |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The top | One of the drop rooms the path uses, with an opening at the bottom | <Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGen.gml:219-237" /> |
| The middle (one or two) | A shaft four tiles wide between solid walls, with blocks jutting from them here and there. Eight of its wall tiles, four a side, are each a Snake 1 in 10, or else a brick 1 in 2 | <Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGen.gml:205-211" /> <Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGen.gml:462-466" /> |
| The bottom | The shaft's foot, walled in on both sides and below: five Snakes, two Big Rubies, and the Mattock in a brick under them | <Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGen.gml:212-218" /> <Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGen.gml:467-481" /> |

The middle and bottom rooms have one layout each. In the clip, on level 4, the player drops down the shaft to the bottom room,
collects its two Big Rubies, and the Snakes around him bite him three times
<Cite clip="snake-pit/descent" />. The Mattock stays buried in its brick <Cite replay="fidelity/replays/mines-seed-2-snake-pit.txt" />.

## Inhabitants

The pit's own Snakes are made with its rooms, before the rest of the level's monsters:

| Monster | Where | Chance | |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [[snake\|Snake]] | In the walls: eight tiles of each middle room, two of the bottom room's | 1 in 10 for each tile | <Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGen.gml:209" /> <Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGen.gml:216" /> <Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGen.gml:462-466" /> |
| [[snake\|Snake]] | On the bottom room's floor | Five, always | <Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGen.gml:216" /> <Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGen.gml:467-470" /> |

Then level generation goes over the pit's blocks as over the rest of the level's, and may put the Mines'
monsters there: [[snake|Snakes]] on top of blocks, [[bat|Bats]] and [[spider|Spiders]] under them
([[mines#inhabitants|the Mines' Inhabitants]]) <Cite gml="scripts/scrEntityGen.gml:30-74" />.

## Hazards

- **The Snakes**, which bite for <Stat id="snake.damage" /> each, five of them close together on the bottom room's floor <Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGen.gml:216" /> <Cite replay="fidelity/replays/mines-seed-2-snake-pit.txt" />.

## Items and shops

The bottom room always has two [[big-ruby|Big Rubies]] and a free [[mattock|Mattock]], its price 0 and not
for sale, buried in the brick in the middle of the floor, under the rubies, where a [[bomb|Bomb]] or a
Mattock already in hand can dig it out <Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGen.gml:216" />
<Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGen.gml:471-481" />. The pit's bricks hide gems and items as any brick does, and
treasure is scattered on its ledges as on the rest of the level ([[mines#treasure|the Mines' Treasure]]).
No shop is ever in the pit (above).

## Secrets

- **The free [[mattock|Mattock]]** in the floor of every pit.

## Trivia

- The code that marks the pit's rooms has an `if (true)` around it, whose `else` would have made a pit of
  two rooms, a top and a bottom; it never runs. Inside it, a test that the third room is a side room repeats
  one the loop has already made, and two variables set before it are never read <Cite gml="scripts/scrLevelGen.gml:139-140" /> <Cite gml="scripts/scrLevelGen.gml:148-177" /> <Cite quirk="1" row="The snake pit's marking has dead code." />.
- The middle and bottom rooms pick their layout with `switch(rand(1,1))`, a roll with one outcome <Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGen.gml:207" /> <Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGen.gml:214" />.
- A commented-out line beside the roll sets its chance to 1 in 1, a pit on every level <Cite gml="scripts/scrLevelGen.gml:141" />.
