A [[caveman|Caveman]] charges when it sees the player, but it isn't its own sight that decides. When any of
them sees him, every Caveman and [[hawkman|Hawkman]] close to him charges, whichever saw him: one walled off
from him, or one facing away, which charges away from him. The one that saw him may not charge at all.

## What happens

A Caveman walking back toward the player sees him while still far off, and walks on. A Hawkman walled in under
his feet, which can't see him, starts charging. A little later the Caveman, now close, charges too
<Cite clip="quirks/caveman-sight-alerts-others" />.

<Clip id="quirks/caveman-sight-alerts-others" />

## Why

Every 6 steps, a Caveman, a Hawkman or a [[yeti|Yeti]] that's standing or walking sends a sight check
(`oEnemySight`) the way it faces, 10 pixels a step, which the first block it touches destroys
<Cite gml="objects/oCaveman/step.gml:116-125" /> <Cite gml="objects/oEnemySight/collision_oSolid.gml:1" />.
When one reaches the player, it doesn't alert the monster that sent it. It alerts all of them near him:

```gml objects/oEnemySight/collision_oCharacter.gml:1-8
with oCaveman
{
    if (distance_to_object(oPlayer1) < 100 and status < 98)
    {
        status = 2;
        playSound(global.sndAlert);
    }
}
```

The same follows `with oHawkman` <Cite gml="objects/oEnemySight/collision_oCharacter.gml:10-17" />. So every
Caveman and Hawkman within 100 pixels of the player that isn't stunned or dead charges (`status` 2), whichever
way it faces and whether or not it could see him, and a charge runs the way it faces. The one that sent the
check charges only if it's within those 100 pixels too. There's no `with oYeti`, so a Yeti's sight sets off
the others, but no Yeti <Cite quirk="1" />.

## Reproducing it

Let a Caveman or a Hawkman see the player while another is near him behind a wall, or facing away: the second
one charges. Play reaches it wherever they live, Cavemen in the Mines, the Jungle and the Temple, Hawkmen in the
Temple. The replay of the clip's staged scene, on a custom level, has a Caveman's sight reach the player while
it's still more than 100 pixels from him, and a Hawkman walled in under him charge; the Caveman charges once a
later look finds it within 100 pixels
<Cite replay="fidelity/replays/enemy-caveman-sight-alerts-hawkman.txt" />.

## In the port

The spelunky.ai port reproduces it exactly: it's in docs/quirks.md's list of the 1.1 source's bugs
<Cite quirk="1" />, and the oracle's fingerprint of the replay pins who charges when. No option turns it off.
