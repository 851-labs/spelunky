Push blocks are made in [[mines|the Mines]]' and [[temple|the Temple]]'s rooms, on tiles of their own and from
some of the [[block|stone blocks]] the levels are built with, in [[olmecs-lair|Olmec's Lair]] from stone blocks, and in
a [[restless-dead|graveyard level]]'s crypt. Unlike any other block, the player can move one: walking into it pushes it along, and pushed
over an edge it falls. Bombs and the [[mattock|Mattock]] break it like any block.

## How it works

<Clip id="push-block/push" />

The clip shows a block pushed off a ledge: the player walks into it, pushes it slowly along the floor to the
edge, and it falls to the floor below <Cite clip="push-block/push" />.

### Pushing

Walking into a push block on the ground, not ducking, the player pushes it: he presses against it, and the code
that moves him moves the block a pixel for each pixel he moves into it, unless something solid is beyond it
<Cite gml="scripts/characterStepEvent.gml:138-176" /> <Cite gml="scripts/moveTo.gml:39-93" />. In play that's
half a pixel a step (15 a second), a block's width in 32 steps, where he walks at 3 pixels a step
<Cite clip="push-block/push" />. After a moment of it, he's drawn pushing
<Cite gml="scripts/characterSprite.gml:57" />. Only the player pushes blocks: the code allows it to characters
alone, not monsters <Cite gml="scripts/canPushMoveableSolids.gml:1-6" />.

A block stuck against something solid stops, but the player pushing it doesn't: he creeps into it, and once his
middle is inside, he's crushed. That's [[push-block-crush]].

### Falling

Each step a push block has nothing solid under any part of it, it falls, gaining
<Stat id="push-block.gravity" /> of speed a step, up to <Stat id="push-block.max-fall-speed" />, and stops on
the first solid thing below it, with a thud <Cite gml="scripts/gameStepEvent.gml:235-256" />
<Cite gml="objects/oMoveableSolid/create.gml:5" />. In the clip it doesn't drop until it has been pushed wholly
past the ledge. It only falls while it's in the view; one off screen hangs where it is until the view comes to
it <Cite gml="scripts/gameStepEvent.gml:238-239" />. One that falls out of the level is gone
<Cite gml="objects/oPushBlock/other_outside.gml:1" />.

### In lava

A push block in [[lava|Lava]] sinks a twentieth of a pixel a step, taking any push blocks stacked on it down with
it <Cite gml="objects/oPushBlock/step.gml:3-9" /> <Cite gml="scripts/scrMoveableSolidRecurseDrop.gml:1-10" />.

## Interactions

- **[[spear-trap|Spear Traps]]** strike at a push block in front of them as at a monster
  <Cite gml="objects/oSpearTrapTop/step.gml:21-28" />.
- **[[arrow-trap|Arrow Traps]]** fire at a push block falling through what they watch: their sensor counts a
  block by its speed <Cite gml="objects/oArrowTrapTest/collision_oMoveableSolid.gml:5-26" />, which it has
  only while it falls, as pushing moves it a pixel at a time without one <Cite gml="scripts/moveTo.gml:46-60" />
  <Cite gml="scripts/gameStepEvent.gml:236-256" /> <Cite clip="arrow-trap/falling-block" />.
- **Breaking it.** A [[bomb|Bomb]] or the [[mattock|Mattock]] breaks a push block like any block, leaving
  rubble <Cite gml="objects/oPushBlock/destroy.gml:1-11" />, but its destroy event is its own: unlike an area's
  rock, it doesn't take what stands on it or bring down a lamp hanging under it
  <Cite quirk="1" row="Most blocks but an area's plain rock skip what breaking a block does." />.
- **[[city-of-gold|The City of Gold]].** Every push block there is gold, and breaks into three
  [[gold-chunk|Gold Chunks]] and a [[gold-nugget|Gold Nugget]] <Cite gml="objects/oPushBlock/create.gml:5" />
  <Cite gml="objects/oPushBlock/destroy.gml:12-23" />.

## Tips

- Pushing is slow, a sixth of walking pace: a block pushed across a room takes seconds.
- Stop pushing once the block stops, or be crushed ([[push-block-crush]]).
- A push block pushed into a pit or over a ledge makes a step to climb back up by.

## Trivia

- A push block is drawn with the stone block's picture, and level generation turns some stone blocks into
  push blocks once a level's walls are set up <Cite gml="scripts/scrSetupWalls.gml:457-471" />, so a stone
  block and a push block look the same until one is pushed.
- The code that lets things push blocks says it lets only the player, and that it can be edited to let monsters
  push them too; it never was <Cite gml="scripts/canPushMoveableSolids.gml:1-6" />.
