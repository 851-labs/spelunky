Wearing them, the player's [[stomping|stomps]], landing on a monster from above, take three times the hit
points they would, which kills most monsters in one stomp. They turn landing on a [[mantrap|Mantrap]] into a
stomp, and hold him still on ice. They don't make [[spikes|Spikes]] safe. [[clothing-shop|Clothing Shops]],
[[rare-shop|Rare Shops]] and the [[dice-house|Dice House]] offer them, and [[crate|Crates]], blocks, Kali
and the [[yeti-king|Yeti King]] give them away. They're worn from the moment they're picked up or paid for
until the game ends.

## Effect

Picking up a free pair, paying for one or carrying one out of a shop puts it on: the message "YOU GOT SPIKE
SHOES!" shows, the pair disappears, and an icon of it joins the row under the hearts
<Cite gml="scripts/scrStealItem.gml:182-192" /> <Cite gml="scripts/scrDrawHUD.gml:145-149" />. They stay on
until a new game starts, which clears them <Cite gml="scripts/scrClearGlobals.gml:93" />
<Cite gml="objects/oTransition/create.gml:20-24" />.

### Stomping

<Clip id="spike-shoes/stomp" />

A stomp normally takes 1 hit point, plus 1 for every 16 steps the player has been falling; in Spike Shoes,
three times that, 3 plus 3 for every 16 steps, and most monsters bleed
<Cite gml="objects/oEnemy/collision_oCharacter.gml:6-13" />. That's the rule most monsters share, and the
[[caveman|Caveman]], [[giant-spider|Giant Spider]], [[hawkman|Hawkman]], [[mantrap|Mantrap]],
[[tomb-lord|Tomb Lord]], [[alien-boss|Alien Boss]] and [[vampire|Vampire]], which have stomp code of their own, follow it
too <Cite gml="objects/oCaveman/collision_oCharacter.gml:11-12" />
<Cite gml="objects/oTombLord/collision_oCharacter.gml:9-13" />. The [[yeti|Yeti]], the
[[yeti-king|Yeti King]] and the [[shopkeeper|Shopkeeper]] count the fall a little differently, 3 for each 16
steps of falling or part of 16 <Cite gml="objects/oYeti/collision_oCharacter.gml:11-12" /> <Cite
gml="objects/oYetiKing/collision_oCharacter.gml:9-10" /> <Cite gml="objects/oShopkeeper/collision_oCharacter.gml:10-11" />.
A Caveman takes a stomp only when it isn't stunned
already, and the stomp stuns it <Cite gml="objects/oCaveman/collision_oCharacter.gml:6-15" />. The clip shows
one that has just got up from being stunned, with 2 of its 3 hit points left, killed by one stomp in Spike
Shoes: its health goes to -1 <Cite clip="spike-shoes/stomp" />.

<Clip id="spike-shoes/mantrap" />

A [[mantrap|Mantrap]] can't be stomped without them, unless it's busy eating something else: landing on it
is touching it, and it eats the player. In Spike Shoes, landing on it is a stomp like any other
<Cite gml="objects/oManTrap/collision_oCharacter.gml:5-9" />.
The clip shows the player walking off a ledge in them onto a Mantrap: the one stomp takes all its 3 hit
points, and he bounces off unhurt <Cite clip="spike-shoes/mantrap" />.

### Ice

On ice, the player normally slides, his speed across fading only slowly, unless he's crouching. In Spike
Shoes, ice holds him as any ground does <Cite gml="scripts/characterStepEvent.gml:774-778" />.

## Interactions

- [[spikes|Spikes]] kill the player who falls onto them in Spike Shoes as in any others: the code that
  kills him on them never checks for the shoes <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:1693-1703" />.
- Stomping on a monster bounces the player up and resets his fall, as without the shoes, so a long drop
  onto a monster is a stronger stomp, not a fall that hurts
  <Cite gml="objects/oEnemy/collision_oCharacter.gml:8-11" />.

## Tips

- From a short drop, a stomp in them takes 3 hit points: one stomp kills a [[caveman|Caveman]] or a
  [[mantrap|Mantrap]], which take three without them.
- Landing on a Mantrap without them gets the player eaten, unless it's already eating, so a Mantrap is only
  worth stomping in them.
- A stomp stuns a Caveman, and a stunned Caveman can't be stomped again, so in ordinary shoes a stomped
  Caveman gets up before the next stomp counts; in Spike Shoes, the first stomp kills it.
