Each thing that hurts the player takes its own number of [[health|hearts]], from 1 for most monsters to
99 for lava. Many also stun him for a time, during which he can't move, and a monster's touch knocks him
back and makes him invincible to monsters for a second. A fall hurts too, if it's long enough.

## How it works

### Monsters' touches

Most monsters' touch is the code they share, their parent object's (`oEnemy`'s). When the player
touches one without [[stomping]] it, and isn't invincible, he's knocked away from it at
<Stat id="damage.knockback" />, blinks, and is invincible for <Stat id="damage.invincibility" />; then, if
he has a heart left, he loses one <Cite gml="objects/oEnemy/collision_oCharacter.gml:14-25" />. Monsters with
a touch of their own take their own amount, usually the same way ([Numbers](#numbers)). A few do something
else:

- A [[yeti|Yeti]], a [[hawkman|Hawkman]] or an angry [[shopkeeper|Shopkeeper]] grabs the player and throws
  him over its head, stunned, unless there's a block above it; the first wall or floor he hits takes a
  heart <Cite gml="objects/oYeti/collision_oCharacter.gml:42-67" />
  <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:1815-1842" />. A long fall cancels that heart, as the landing costs
  one anyway <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:257" />.
- A [[magma-man|Magma Man]] burns him: he's stunned and set alight as well
  <Cite gml="objects/oMagmaMan/collision_oCharacter.gml:1-18" />.
- A [[ghost|Ghost]] kills him outright, as does a [[mantrap|Mantrap]], which eats
  him <Cite gml="objects/oGhost/collision_oCharacter.gml:1-16" />
  <Cite gml="objects/oManTrap/collision_oCharacter.gml:20-47" />.

### Invincibility

<Clip id="damage/invincible" />

While the player is invincible he blinks for as long, the two counts going down one a step
<Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:1929-1937" />. Only monsters' touches check it. The checks in his own
step for arrows, thrown rocks, lasers, explosions, traps, spikes, lava and falls don't, and neither do a
bullet's or a Tomb Lord's fly's, so they hurt him while he
blinks <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:1513-1710" /> <Cite gml="objects/oBullet/collision_oCharacter.gml:1-6" />
<Cite gml="objects/oFly/collision_oCharacter.gml:1-6" />. Nor do those checks space their hits
out: an explosion, a Spear Trap or a Smash Trap he stays in hits him on every step
<Cite quirk="1" row="The deaths table's explosion and trap rows don't count deaths." />. Besides a monster's touch, the [[ankh|Ankh]]'s revival makes him invincible, for
<Stat id="ankh.revive-invincibility" />, and so does going through a level's exit, for the rest of the level
<Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:1885-1886" /> <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:871-872" />.

The clip shows it. A Snake bites the player, and while he blinks he walks through a second Snake without
losing a heart. Back among them, he's bitten again, and once more exactly <Stat id="damage.invincibility" />
later, as his blinking stops <Cite clip="damage/invincible" />.

### Stun

While the player is stunned, every key is ignored <Cite gml="scripts/characterStepEvent.gml:71-88" />. He
lets go of what he holds, which is thrown up out of his hands and, if it's a Bow, fires
<Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:1714-1736" />; a whip swing stops and an open
[[parachute|Parachute]] tears away <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:1738-1753" />. In the air his speed
across isn't slowed, and he bounces off walls at half his speed and off ceilings at most of it
<Cite gml="scripts/characterStepEvent.gml:266-276" /> <Cite gml="scripts/characterStepEvent.gml:731-736" />.

Most stuns set a count of steps, which only goes down while he lies still, in the stunned pose: the stun ends
when it reaches 0 <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:243-250" />
<Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:1793-1797" />. So a stun that sends him flying lasts its steps
after he stops, and a throw by a Yeti, a Hawkman or a Shopkeeper, which sets no count, lasts until he comes
to rest <Cite gml="objects/oYeti/collision_oCharacter.gml:65-67" />.

### Long drops

<Clip id="damage/long-drop" />

The player's step counts the steps he's been falling, unless he's climbing
<Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:254-257" />. Landing after more than 16 is a long drop: he's stunned
for <Stat id="damage.long-drop-stun" /> more, bounces up, and loses 1 heart, 2 after more than 32 steps and
10 after more than 48 <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:268-289" />. Landing on a
[[spring-trap|Spring Trap]] isn't one <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:268" />, and an open
[[parachute|Parachute]] or [[cape|Cape]] keeps the count at 0 <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:15-18" />
<Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:252" />, as does a [[stomping|stomp]], which ends the fall in a bounce
<Cite gml="objects/oEnemy/collision_oCharacter.gml:11" />.

The clip shows a drop of about twenty blocks. The player lands on his 35th step of falling, loses two of his
four hearts and lies stunned for <Stat id="damage.long-drop-stun" as="seconds" />
<Cite clip="damage/long-drop" />.

## Examples

- **A Snake's bite** takes 1 heart, and touching it again within the second after does nothing
  <Cite clip="damage/invincible" />.
- **A Bomb at the player's feet** takes all 4 of a new game's hearts, and hits him again on each step he's
  in the explosion <Cite replay="fidelity/replays/action-bomb-death-mines-seed-1.txt" />.
- **A long drop** of 35 steps takes 2 hearts and stuns him for 2 seconds <Cite clip="damage/long-drop" />.

## Quirks

- **The deaths table counts some hits that don't kill.** The end-of-game statistics' explosion row counts
  every step of an explosion the player survives, and not the one that kills him; the Spear, Smash and
  Ceiling Trap rows count every hit <Cite quirk="1" row="The deaths table's explosion and trap rows don't count deaths." />
  <Cite replay="fidelity/replays/action-bomb-death-mines-seed-1.txt" />.
- **Some monsters' kills aren't counted at all.** A [[bat|Bat]], a [[zombie|Zombie]] and a
  [[dead-fish|Dead Fish]] never set the name the touch tallies by, and the [[megamouth|Megamouth]]'s is
  spelled another way; a [[vampire|Vampire]]'s own touch has no tally, and nor does a blob of magma's, a copy
  of the Magma Man's <Cite quirk="1" row="The bat's type is never set." /> <Cite quirk="1"
  row="The zombie's, the vampire's and the Dead Fish's kills of the player are never tallied." /> <Cite
  quirk="1" row="The Megamouth's kills of the player are never tallied." /> <Cite quirk="1"
  row="A blob of magma's kills of the player are never tallied." />
  <Cite replay="fidelity/replays/enemy-vampire-seed-163-death.txt" />.
- **The Yeti King's touch costs nothing in a custom level**, though it knocks the player back and its kill is
  tallied all the same ([[yeti-king-touch-custom-level]]) <Cite quirk="1" row="The Yeti King's touch costs nothing in a custom level, and its kill is tallied there all the same." /> <Cite replay="fidelity/replays/enemy-yeti-king-custom-level-touch.txt" />.
- **A Smash Trap throws the player toward it** ([[smash-trap-random-throw]]) <Cite quirk="1" row="A smash trap's hit throws the player toward it, and down if its random dir says so." />,
  and a lit one on a [[dark-level|dark level]] never takes the 10 hearts: the player's check doesn't see it
  ([[lit-smash-trap-touch]]) <Cite quirk="1" row="A lit smash trap isn't a smash trap to the player." />.
- **The arrow check takes the nearest arrow**, touching him or not, so a spent arrow lying beside him can
  spare him a fast one ([[nearest-enemy]]) <Cite quirk="1" row="The arrow and spear checks take the nearest one, not the one touching." />.
