[[olmec|Olmec]] waits on level 16, [[olmecs-lair|Olmec's Lair]], the last level of the game. Nothing the
player carries hurts him: only [[lava|Lava]] kills him, and the lava is under the lair's floor and the rock
below it <Cite gml="objects/oOlmec/create.gml:8" /> <Cite gml="objects/oOlmec/step.gml:35-43" /> <Cite room="rOlmec" />.
Each of his slams breaks the blocks he lands on, so the fight is spent drawing slams from him in one place
until he has dug himself down into it. His death opens the lair's door to the ending. The steps' clips are
cut from one run that beats him with seed 1 <Cite replay="fidelity/replays/ending-seed-1.txt" />.

## Requirements

- **[[rope|Ropes]]**, to climb back out of the shaft his slams dig: the door is up in the hall
  <Cite gml="objects/oFinalBoss/step.gml:1-19" />. The run uses all four it has
  <Cite replay="fidelity/replays/ending-seed-1.txt" />.

## Steps

1. **Wait while he wakes.** The player arrives at the hall's left door and can't move while the view pans to
   Olmec, he breaks out of his stone and makes his first hop and slam ([[olmec#awakening|Olmec's awakening]])
   <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/create.gml:102" /> <Cite gml="objects/oOlmec/alarm_5.gml:1-8" />. The clip shows the
   whole awakening <Cite clip="olmec/awakening" />.

   <Clip id="olmec/awakening" />

2. **Mind the Cavemen.** As Olmec first hops, the three [[caveman|Cavemen]] bowing before him get up and charge,
   facing right, away from the player's door <Cite gml="objects/oOlmec/alarm_6.gml:1-7" />. They're still a
   danger: in a run with the same seed as the guide's, they beat a player who never moves from the entrance to
   death <Cite replay="fidelity/replays/olmec-death-seed-1.txt" />. The Caveman's page has [[caveman#defeating|what kills them]].
3. **Wait at one end of the hall and let him come.** After the first 10 steps of each hop, Olmec drifts toward a
   player who is past either side of him <Cite gml="objects/oOlmec/step.gml:102-119" />. In the run, the player
   waits by the left door, and Olmec hops across the hall to him <Cite clip="beating-olmec/lure" />.

   <Clip id="beating-olmec/lure" />

4. **When he stops in the air over you, get out from under him.** A hop stops dead when the player is lower than
   Olmec's top and within 32 pixels of his middle; after <Stat id="olmec.hover-time" /> he slams straight down
   <Cite gml="objects/oOlmec/step.gml:121-129" /> <Cite gml="objects/oOlmec/step.gml:145-155" />. Run clear to
   either side: a player still under him is crushed, whatever his hearts
   <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:1490-1505" />. In the clip, Olmec stops over the player, who runs
   clear, and his slam breaks the floor <Cite clip="olmec/slam" />.

   <Clip id="olmec/slam" />

5. **Keep him slamming in the same place, and follow him down.** Every block a slam lands on breaks
   <Cite gml="objects/oOlmecSlam/collision_oSolid.gml:1-7" />, so slams in one place dig a shaft through the hall's
   floor and the rock under it, toward the lava ([[olmecs-lair#generation|the lair's layout]]). A wall stops his drift
   <Cite gml="objects/oOlmec/step.gml:22-33" />, so pinned against one he lands in the same place each time; the
   run keeps him at the left end. To draw the next slam, the player has to be below Olmec's
   top, so he goes down into the shaft beside him. In the clip, two more slams take Olmec a block deeper each
   <Cite clip="beating-olmec/dig" />.

   <Clip id="beating-olmec/dig" />

6. **If he reaches the lava by the left wall, stay to his right.** There, his checks for lava test a point inside
   the wall, so he sits in it unharmed until a hop drifts him off the wall, which it does only toward a player
   past his right side ([[olmec-lava-left-wall]]) <Cite quirk="1" />. Off the wall, he stops, sinks and dies
   <Cite gml="objects/oOlmec/step.gml:35-43" />. In the clip, his last slam takes him into the lava by the wall,
   where he sits for two seconds; he starts to sink only when he hops out and drifts toward the player
   <Cite clip="quirks/olmec-lava-left-wall" />.

   <Clip id="quirks/olmec-lava-left-wall" />

7. **Climb out and take the door.** When Olmec dies, the door at the right end of the hall becomes the exit,
   with a block of stone under it if the floor there is gone <Cite gml="objects/oFinalBoss/step.gml:1-19" />.
   Going through it leads to the ending rather than another level
   <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/other_animation_end.gml:132-136" />. In the clip, the player climbs out of the shaft
   on his ropes, runs along the hall and goes through the door <Cite clip="olmecs-lair/exit" />.

   <Clip id="olmecs-lair/exit" />

## Tips

- Touching Olmec does no harm: only a slam that lands on the player does. A player standing on top of him
  rides along <Cite gml="objects/oOlmec/step.gml:3-7" /> <Cite gml="objects/oOlmec/step.gml:48-49" />.
- His yellow balls don't hurt the player, but each turns into a [[bat|Bat]], [[spider|Spider]],
  [[snake|Snake]], [[frog|Frog]] or [[fire-frog|Fire Frog]] where it lands ([[olmec#yellow-balls|Olmec's yellow balls]]), so the
  shaft fills with monsters as the fight goes on.
- Drifting left at half his top speed or more, Olmec passes over the player without stopping; drifting right,
  he stops at any speed ([[olmec-slam-drifting-left]]) <Cite quirk="1" />.
- Bombs and weapons do nothing to Olmec <Cite gml="objects/oOlmec/create.gml:8" />.
- There's no time limit: the [[ghost|Ghost]] never comes to the lair <Cite gml="objects/oGame/step.gml:23-47" />.

## Common mistakes

- **Staying under him after he stops.** The slam comes <Stat id="olmec.hover-time" /> later and crushes the
  player outright. In the clip, a player who stands still is crushed with all four hearts
  <Cite clip="olmec/crush" />.

  <Clip id="olmec/crush" />

- **Standing anywhere but to his right when he's in the lava by the left wall.** His hops don't drift him off
  the wall, and he stays in the lava without drowning (step 6). In one run, a player on a rope above him keeps him there
  through two hops <Cite replay="fidelity/replays/olmec-door-block-crush-seed-1.txt" />.
- **Standing where the floor under the door was.** If a bomb has blown it out, the block of stone that appears
  there when Olmec dies crushes a player standing in its place, and counts as Olmec's ([[olmec-door-block-crush]])
  <Cite gml="objects/oFinalBoss/step.gml:8-12" /> <Cite replay="fidelity/replays/olmec-door-block-crush-seed-1.txt" />.
