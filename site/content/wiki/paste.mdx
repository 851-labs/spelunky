Wearing it, every [[bomb|Bomb]] the player throws or takes out is sticky: a lit one stops where it first
touches a wall, the floor or the ceiling, and one thrown fast into a monster sticks to the monster and goes
off on it. [[bomb-shop|Bomb Shops]] and the [[dice-house|Dice House]] offer it, every
[[giant-spider|Giant Spider]] leaves one, and [[crate|Crates]], blocks and Kali give it away. It's worn from
the moment it's picked up or paid for until the game ends.

## Effect

Picking up a free paste, paying for one or carrying one out of a shop puts it on: the message "YOU GOT
STICKY BOMBS!" shows, the paste disappears, and the bomb icon at the top of the screen becomes a sticky
bomb's <Cite gml="scripts/scrStealItem.gml:92-102" /> <Cite gml="scripts/scrDrawHUD.gml:36-37" />. It stays
on until a new game starts, which clears it <Cite gml="scripts/scrClearGlobals.gml:88" />
<Cite gml="objects/oTransition/create.gml:20-24" />.

With it, every bomb the player makes is sticky: the one the bomb key throws, and the one the item key puts
in his hands <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:1137-1140" />
<Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:1009-1012" /> <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:1028-1031" />.

### Sticking to walls

<Clip id="paste/wall" />

A lit sticky bomb that touches a wall, the floor or the ceiling stops dead, where an ordinary one bounces
off <Cite gml="objects/oItem/step.gml:113-121" /> <Cite gml="objects/oItem/step.gml:149-158" />. It then
stays there, in mid-air if that's where it stuck, until it goes off. The clip shows one thrown at the wall
beside the player sticking there in mid-air as he runs off <Cite clip="paste/wall" />.

### Sticking to monsters

<Clip id="paste/yeti" />

A sticky bomb moving faster than 2 pixels per step that touches a monster or a [[damsel|Damsel]] latches on: from then
on, it moves with it, keeping the same distance from it <Cite gml="objects/oItem/step.gml:217-255" />
<Cite gml="objects/oBomb/step_end.gml:1-12" />. The monster it latches onto is the one whose position is
nearest the bomb's, which isn't always the one it touched <Cite quirk="1" />. Latching takes the place of a thrown item's hit, so the bomb doesn't
hurt the monster until it goes off <Cite gml="objects/oItem/step.gml:217-258" />. The clip shows one thrown
into a [[yeti|Yeti]]: it sticks to the Yeti's head and rides along as it walks, then goes off and kills it;
the Yeti loses nothing when it's hit <Cite clip="paste/yeti" />
<Cite replay="fidelity/replays/item-seed-53-paste-yeti.txt" />.

## Interactions

- When the monster a bomb is stuck to dies or is otherwise gone, the bomb stops following it
  <Cite gml="objects/oBomb/step_end.gml:3-6" />.
- Another explosion knocks a stuck bomb loose and lights it, to go off 4–8 steps later
  <Cite gml="objects/oExplosion/collision_oItem.gml:6-19" />.
- A sticky bomb thrown into a Damsel latches onto her as onto a monster
  <Cite gml="objects/oItem/step.gml:237-254" />.

## Tips

- A sticky bomb thrown at a wall or ceiling stays where it stuck, so a bomb can be left exactly where a
  block should be broken.
- A sticky bomb thrown at a wall beside the player doesn't bounce away from him, so he has to get clear
  himself.
- Once stuck, a bomb goes wherever its monster goes, toward the player too.

## Trivia

- The item has three names: its `type` is "Paste", shops sell it as "BOMB PASTE", and its pickup message
  says "YOU GOT STICKY BOMBS!" <Cite gml="objects/oPaste/create.gml:3-9" /> <Cite gml="scripts/scrStealItem.gml:99" />.
- The code that latches a bomb has a commented-out rule that would let a monster carry only one sticky bomb
  at a time <Cite gml="objects/oItem/step.gml:222-232" />.
