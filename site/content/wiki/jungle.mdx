The Jungle's levels are built from earth rather than brick, with ruins of temple stone, pools of water,
vines to climb and trees. [[mantrap|Mantraps]] and [[caveman|Cavemen]] patrol the ground, and the area's traps
are [[spear-trap|Spear Traps]]. It holds more special levels than any other area: the
[[black-market|Black Market]], [[flooded-level|flooded levels]] and [[restless-dead|graveyard levels]].

## Generation

The Jungle is levels 5 to 8 <Cite gml="scripts/scrInitLevel.gml:26" />. Taking level 4's exit leads to the
screen between levels, where the Tunnel Man may wait, and from it to level 5
<Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/other_animation_end.gml:63" /> <Cite gml="objects/oTransition/step.gml:20-24" />.

loads <Cite clip="jungle/from-mines" />.

5 <Cite gml="objects/oTitle/create.gml:27-47" />
<Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/other_animation_end.gml:137-143" />. The second clip shows it: the player climbs

A level is laid out as [[mines#generation|the Mines']] are, four rooms by four on a path from the top row to
the bottom, but from the Jungle's own layouts <Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGen2.gml:52-243" />. Its blocks are
earth, with temple stone in some layouts <Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGen2.gml:327-343" />, and generation
makes no arrow traps there <Cite gml="scripts/scrEntityGen.gml:173-337" />. Layouts add pools of water (the
tiles at their edges as likely earth), vines, [[spikes|Spikes]] and trees, a trunk several
blocks tall with branches and leaves <Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGen2.gml:344-379" />
<Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGen2.gml:495-541" />. Two side rooms in three get a side room's layout, and one of
those may be <Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGen2.gml:79-117" />:

| Room | Chance | |

As in the Mines, the monsters on top of blocks keep out of the entrance's room, and the hanging ones need
two open tiles below. On a graveyard level, the ground's monsters are [[zombie|Zombies]] (1 in 25) and
[[vampire|Vampires]] (1 in 160) instead, and the water holds [[dead-fish|Dead Fish]] in place of
Piranhas <Cite gml="scripts/scrEntityGen.gml:280-284" /> <Cite gml="scripts/scrEntityGen.gml:332" />. A
flooded level's lake holds the [[megamouth|Megamouth]] <Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGen2.gml:222-228" />. The
[[ghost|Ghost]] comes to a level the player stays on too long, as in every area.

## Hazards

- **[[spear-trap|Spear Traps]].** One block in twelve on the surface of the ground (with two open tiles
  above it, open ground on at least one side and solid ground under it, more than 64 pixels from the
  entrance, outside a few fixed columns) becomes a Spear Trap, two blocks tall <Cite gml="scripts/scrEntityGen.gml:226-253" />. On a dark level, a tiki
  torch takes one block in twenty of those first <Cite gml="scripts/scrEntityGen.gml:235-238" />.
- **[[spikes|Spikes]]** line some layouts' floors, and come with others one tile in three
  <Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGen2.gml:351-352" />.
- **Water**, where [[piranha|Piranhas]] swim.
- **The idol room's trap blocks** crumble when the [[golden-idol|Golden Idol]] is lifted.

## Items and shops

Shops come as in the Mines: a level's chance is 2 in its number (2 in 5 on level 5 down to 1 in 4 on level
8), in a side room beside the path, of the same seven kinds
<Cite gml="scripts/scrLevelGen.gml:239-297" /> <Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGen2.gml:160-189" />. But once the
Black Market has been made, no level has a shop for the rest of the game: the roll for one requires that it
hasn't <Cite gml="scripts/scrLevelGen.gml:240" /> <Cite quirk="1" /> <Cite replay="fidelity/replays/jungle-seed-9-market-dark-roll.txt" />.

Treasure is scattered as in [[mines#treasure|the Mines]], with more bones on a graveyard level, whose graves
hold gold and gems <Cite gml="scripts/scrEntityGen.gml:175-205" />
<Cite gml="scripts/scrEntityGen.gml:255-259" />. The earth hides more gems than the Mines' bricks: 1 in 80
blocks holds a big sapphire, and rarer ones a big emerald, a big ruby or an item
<Cite gml="objects/oLush/create.gml:5-15" />.
