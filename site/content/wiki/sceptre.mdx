Only the [[tomb-lord|Tomb Lord]] of level 13 has one, and no shop sells it. Held, it fires psychic waves
that turn toward the nearest monster and pass through rock, hurting what they touch on every step, so a
single wave can kill a monster out of the player's sight. On level 14 it's one of the keys to the
[[city-of-gold|City of Gold]].

## Usage

<Clip id="sceptre/hawkman" />

The Sceptre is picked up like any item, crouching over it and pressing the attack key. While it's held, the
attack key fires it, on the ground, in the air, hanging or climbing, without knocking the player back;
crouching, the key puts it down in front of him instead <Cite gml="scripts/scrUseItem.gml:276-331" />. It
isn't thrown. A shot makes a psychic wave 12 pixels in front of the player, with three sparks that fall
away, and starts a wait of <Stat id="sceptre.cooldown" /> before the
next <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:175" />.

The wave goes straight ahead at 6 pixels a step for its first 5 steps. After that, every step, it turns
toward the middle of the monster nearest it, wherever it is, and moves 2 pixels that
way <Cite gml="objects/oPsychicWaveP/step.gml:1-17" />. It has no collision with blocks, so it goes through
rock, and it lasts as long as its animation, 100 steps, unless it leaves the
room <Cite gml="objects/oPsychicWaveP/create.gml:3-7" /> <Cite gml="objects/oPsychicWaveP/other_animation_end.gml:1" /> <Cite gml="objects/oPsychicWaveP/other_outside.gml:1" />. In the clip, the player fires left on level 14 and
runs after his wave. A [[hawkman|Hawkman]] leaps at him, and the wave, which has turned toward it, takes 3 of
its 4 hit points on the step it touches it and 3 more on the next, and it
dies <Cite clip="sceptre/hawkman" />.

The Sceptre goes with the player through a level's exit if he's holding it, and is in his hands on the next
level <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:842-850" /> <Cite gml="objects/oLevel/create.gml:25-30" />. He
drops it when he's stunned, or when he teeters too long at a ledge's
edge <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:329-345" /> <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:150-171" /> <Cite replay="fidelity/replays/city-of-gold-seed-2-sceptre.txt" />.

## Damage

| Monster | Each step a wave touches it | |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Every monster but the Alien Boss | <Stat id="sceptre.damage" />, and flings it up and to the left | <Cite gml="objects/oPsychicWaveP/collision_oEnemy.gml:1-7" /> |
| [[alien-boss\|Alien Boss]] | Nothing | <Cite gml="objects/oPsychicWaveP/collision_oEnemy.gml:1" /> |
| A [[damsel\|Damsel]] | 3, and the wave is used up | <Cite gml="objects/oPsychicWaveP/collision_oDamsel.gml:1-14" /> |

A wave isn't used up by a monster, so it goes on hurting it, 3 hit points a step, for as long as they
touch, and then goes on to the next nearest <Cite gml="objects/oPsychicWaveP/collision_oEnemy.gml:1-7" />.

## Obtaining

The Sceptre comes only from a Tomb Lord killed on level 13, the first level of the [[temple|Temple]], which
drops it with its bones <Cite gml="objects/oTombLord/step.gml:16-30" /> <Cite replay="fidelity/replays/city-of-gold-seed-2-sceptre.txt" />. A Tomb Lord that walks into lava drops
it into the lava <Cite quirk="1" row="The gold door can be sealed in rock." />. No shop sells it, and the [[level-editor|level editor]] has no tile for
it.

## Interactions

- **The gold door.** Held against the gold door on level 14 with the [[hedjet|Hedjet]] worn, it opens the way to the
  [[city-of-gold|City of Gold]], and the Sceptre is used up; without the Hedjet, the door shows "THE SCEPTRE
  FITS... BUT NOTHING IS HAPPENING!" <Cite gml="objects/oGoldDoor/collision_oSceptre.gml:1-23" />.
- **Lava.** It sinks slowly in lava like any item, but lava, which burns up any other item that sinks into
  it, spares the
  Sceptre <Cite gml="objects/oItem/step.gml:160-186" />.

## Tips

- A wave turns toward the nearest monster wherever it is and goes through rock, so it can reach one the
  player can't see.
- Its waves don't hurt the Alien Boss, so the Sceptre is no use against it.

## Trivia

- Its shop message is "I SHOULDN'T BE SELLING THIS!", which no shop ever shows: none stocks
  it <Cite gml="objects/oSceptre/create.gml:6-7" /> <Cite quirk="1" row="Weapons' leftovers." />.
- Its wave's code picks a random sideways push, `rand(0,2)-rand(1,2)`, and then sets it to -1 on the next
  line, so every monster it hits is flung left <Cite gml="objects/oPsychicWaveP/collision_oEnemy.gml:4-5" /> <Cite quirk="1" row="Weapons' leftovers." />.
- Hitting a Damsel, the wave sets a state meant for her on itself instead, and then is gone, so she keeps
  her own <Cite gml="objects/oPsychicWaveP/collision_oDamsel.gml:7-14" /> <Cite quirk="1" row="Weapons' leftovers." />.
