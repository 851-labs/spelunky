Signs stand in the [[tutorial|Tutorial]], where seventeen of them teach the controls along the cave, and in
custom levels, where the [[level-editor|level editor]] gives each one a message of its own. They're part of the
background, not blocks: nothing breaks one.

## How it works

<Clip id="sign/tutorial" />

While the player touches a sign, it shows its message, one or two lines, at the bottom of the screen, and sets
the message to last 200 steps, so it stays a while after he walks on <Cite gml="objects/oMsgSign/collision_oCharacter.gml:1-3" />.
A message lasts half as long on a lit level as on a dark one ([[messages-halved-on-lit-levels]]). The clip shows
the Tutorial's first signs: the welcome, then how to jump, to hang on ledges and to use the whip, each message
coming up as the player reaches its sign <Cite clip="sign/tutorial" />.

### The Tutorial's signs

A Tutorial sign's message is set by where the sign stands, which the room editor fixed: one message for each of
seventeen spots <Cite gml="objects/oMsgSign/create.gml:9-120" /> <Cite room="rTutorial" />. The ones about a key
name the player's own key for it, or his gamepad's button when he plays with one ("PRESS Z TO JUMP.")
<Cite gml="objects/oMsgSign/create.gml:15-21" />. They run from "WELCOME TO THE TUTORIAL CAVE!" by the entrance
to "THERE'S LOTS MORE AHEAD!" by the exit, with jumping, hanging, the whip, treasure, chests, ducking and
crawling, the flip hang, the ladder, picking up and throwing, ropes, running and bombs between.

### In custom levels

A sign is the [[level-editor|level editor]]'s tile `I` <Cite gml="scripts/scrCreateTileObj.gml:32-35" />. In the
editor it starts blank, the editor draws its message over it, and the message, one line, is saved with the
level and read back with it <Cite gml="objects/oMsgSign/create.gml:4-8" /> <Cite gml="objects/oMsgSign/draw.gml:1-7" />
<Cite gml="scripts/scrLoadLevel.gml:118-128" />. A level saved with neither an entrance nor an exit loses its
signs' messages ([[lost-sign-messages]]).

## Interactions

- **[[bomb|Bombs]]** pass signs by: a sign isn't solid, and a blast breaks only what is
  <Cite gml="objects/oExplosion/collision_oSolid.gml:1-15" />.
- **Other messages.** A sign's message takes the place of whatever message was showing, a pickup's or a
  shopkeeper's, and the next message replaces it in turn <Cite gml="objects/oMsgSign/collision_oCharacter.gml:1-3" />.
