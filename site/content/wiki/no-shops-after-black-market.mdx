From level 2 on, each level rolls for a [[shopkeeper|Shopkeeper]]'s shop, with a chance of 2 in its level's
number ([[level-generation#shops|Shops]]). The [[black-market|Black Market]], found through a hidden door in the
[[jungle|Jungle]], is a level of shops. But once it has been made, the roll never succeeds again: every level
after it, to the end of the game, has no shop.

## What happens

On seed 9, the player finds the Black Market on level 6. On level 8, two levels on, the shop roll passes and the
level's top-left room is beside the path, where a shop could stand. The player walks left along the top row until
that room fills the left of the view: it's an ordinary Jungle room, and the level has no
Shopkeeper <Cite clip="quirks/no-shop-after-black-market" />.

<Clip id="quirks/no-shop-after-black-market" />

## Why

The shop roll is one line of the level generator, run for every level:

```gml scripts/scrLevelGen.gml:239-241
// shop
if (rand(1,global.currLevel) <= 2 and global.currLevel > 1 and not global.madeBlackMarket)
{
```

`madeBlackMarket` is set by the Black Market's own level, which then returns before it gets to this line
<Cite gml="scripts/scrLevelGen.gml:44-69" />. Only a new game clears it <Cite gml="scripts/scrClearGlobals.gml:75" />.
So the check never applies to the market's level. Its only effect is on the levels after it, the rest of
the Jungle, the Ice Caves and the Temple: their rolls are still drawn, but they never make a shop
<Cite quirk="1" />. It may be by design, a market that's the game's last shop, but nothing in the code says so.

## Reproducing it

Find the Black Market (the hidden door on level 5, 6 or 7: [[black-market]]) and play on: no later level has a
shop. It happens in every game that finds the market. The clip's replay goes through seed 9's market on level 6
and into level 8, whose shop roll passes, and walks the top row to the room beside the path. No Shopkeeper is
made on the level <Cite replay="fidelity/replays/jungle-seed-9-market-no-shop.txt" />. Two longer runs show the
same on other levels: level 7 of seed 9's City of Gold run, whose roll is 1 and whose top-left room is beside the
path <Cite replay="fidelity/replays/city-of-gold-seed-9.txt" />, and level 8 of the run the clip's replay starts
from <Cite replay="fidelity/replays/jungle-seed-9-market-dark-roll.txt" />.

## In the port

The spelunky.ai port reproduces it exactly: it's in docs/quirks.md's list of the 1.1 source's bugs
<Cite quirk="1" />, and the oracle's fingerprint of the clip's replay pins its run, whose story checks that
level 8 makes no Shopkeeper. No option turns it off.
