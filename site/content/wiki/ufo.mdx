[[level-generation|Level generation]] scatters UFOs over [[ice-caves|the Ice Caves]]. A UFO flies to a height above the player
and patrols there until it's right over him, then fires a laser straight down, which takes
<Stat id="ufo.laser-damage" /> whether or not he's invincible. It has <Stat id="ufo.hp" />, so any hit
destroys it, but how matters: a [[throwing|thrown item]] blows it up at once and may knock an [[alien|Alien]] out of it,
and anything else brings it down as a burning wreck that explodes where it lands.

## Behaviour

A UFO flies up or down at 2 pixels per step (60 per second) to reach <Stat id="ufo.hover-height" /> above
the player, drifting back and forth sideways at <Stat id="ufo.flight-speed" /> (<Stat id="ufo.flight-speed"
as="per-second" />) as it goes <Cite gml="objects/oUFO/step.gml:81-130" />. Once it has reached that height,
it holds it and patrols, 64 pixels to each side, turning at walls
<Cite gml="objects/oUFO/step.gml:26-60" />. It goes back to following him up or down when he rises above it
or drops more than 96 pixels below it <Cite gml="objects/oUFO/step.gml:68" />. It makes a sound the first time
the player comes within 160 pixels of it <Cite gml="objects/oUFO/step.gml:18-24" />.

<Clip id="ufo/laser" />

When its middle passes within 8 pixels of the player's, a patrolling UFO stops and charges its gun with a
sound, and up to 32 steps (about a second) later, as its animation reaches the shot, it fires a laser
downward, then patrols again: if he's still under it, it charges again at once <Cite gml="objects/oUFO/step.gml:62-66" />
<Cite gml="objects/oUFO/step.gml:70-80" /> <Cite gml="objects/oUFO/other_animation_end.gml:1-5" />. The clip
shows a UFO coming down over the player and firing at him: the first shot takes three hearts, and the second,
40 steps later, kills him, and it goes on firing at his body <Cite clip="ufo/laser" />.

The laser falls, gathering speed in surges, until it hits something. It destroys the block it hits, unless the block
can't be destroyed <Cite gml="objects/oLaser/collision_oSolid.gml:1-24" />;
it hurts any other monster it hits, but not a UFO, for 3 hit points
<Cite gml="objects/oLaser/collision_oEnemy.gml:1-10" />.

A UFO only acts while it's on screen or just off it <Cite gml="objects/oUFO/step.gml:1-3" />.

## Defeating

A UFO has <Stat id="ufo.hp" />, so any of these destroys it:

| Way | What happens | |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [[whip\|Whip]], [[machete\|Machete]] or [[mattock\|Mattock]] | It becomes a falling wreck | <Cite gml="objects/oEnemy/collision_oWhip.gml:1" /> |
| [[stomping\|Stomp]] (landing on it, or rising into it from below far enough) | It becomes a falling wreck | <Cite gml="objects/oEnemy/collision_oCharacter.gml:6-13" /> |
| A thrown item moving faster than 2 pixels per step, such as a [[rock\|Rock]] | It explodes on the spot; a 1 in 3 chance of an [[alien\|Alien]] | <Cite gml="objects/oItem/step.gml:331-340" /> |
| A thrown [[jar\|Jar]] or [[skull\|Skull]], whose own code differs | It becomes a falling wreck | <Cite gml="objects/oJar/step.gml:131-139" /> <Cite gml="objects/oSkull/step.gml:129-137" /> |
| A stunned monster thrown into it | It explodes on the spot; a 1 in 3 chance of an Alien | <Cite gml="objects/oEnemy/step.gml:243-251" /> |
| A [[shotgun\|Shotgun]] or [[pistol\|Pistol]] bullet | It becomes a falling wreck | <Cite gml="objects/oBullet/collision_oEnemy.gml:16" /> |
| A [[bomb\|Bomb]]'s explosion | It becomes a falling wreck | <Cite gml="objects/oExplosion/collision_oEnemy.gml:12-19" /> |

<Clip id="ufo/crash" />

A UFO whose hit points run out turns into a wreck that falls, trailing flames, and explodes with a Bomb's
explosion when it hits the ground or a monster <Cite gml="objects/oUFO/step.gml:6-16" /> <Cite
gml="objects/oUFOCrash/step.gml:1-3" /> <Cite gml="objects/oUFOCrash/collision_oSolid.gml:1-3" /> <Cite
gml="objects/oUFOCrash/collision_oEnemy.gml:1-3" />. The clip shows the player, bounced up by a
[[spring-trap|Spring Trap]], rising into a UFO, which the game counts as landing on it; the wreck falls and blows up the
Spring Trap below.

<Clip id="ufo/thrown-rock" />

A thrown item or monster doesn't take its hit points: it blows the UFO up where it is, with a Bomb's
explosion, and 1 time in 3 an Alien ejects from it and parachutes down
<Cite gml="objects/oItem/step.gml:331-340" />. The clip shows a [[rock|Rock]] thrown up at a UFO, and the
Alien that ejects.

UFOs destroyed count toward the player's kills, and the screen between levels draws a UFO icon for each
<Cite gml="objects/oUFO/step.gml:9-14" /> <Cite gml="objects/oTransition/alarm_0.gml:203-207" />. A UFO's
step doesn't run the one most monsters share, so [[spikes|Spikes]], [[lava|Lava]], [[spear-trap|Spear
Traps]] and water don't affect it <Cite gml="objects/oEnemy/step.gml:34-128" />.

## Locations

Level generation makes UFOs only in [[ice-caves|the Ice Caves]]. Each block outside the starting room and
the shops, with open space above it and solid ground or water in one of the two blocks below it (or in the
level's bottom 64 pixels), has a 1 in 30
chance of a UFO above it, or 1 in 90 in the [[yeti-lair|Yeti lair]]
<Cite gml="scripts/scrEntityGen.gml:338-360" />.

In custom levels, it's the [[level-editor|level editor]]'s tile `U`
<Cite gml="scripts/scrCreateTileObj.gml:209-212" />.

## Tips

- A UFO fires only when it's right above the player, and its laser drops straight down, so moving sideways
  while it charges dodges the shot.
- Invincibility doesn't help against the laser, and a stunned player under a UFO is shot again 40 steps
  later.
- Bringing a UFO down any way but by throwing makes a wreck that explodes where it lands, so it's safer
  not to be under it.
- Only a thrown item or monster gets an Alien out of a UFO, and not a thrown Jar or Skull, whose own code
  takes its hit point as a bullet does.

## Trivia

- The laser speeds up as it falls, but whenever its speed passes 4 pixels per step the code sets it back to
  0, so it falls in surges <Cite gml="objects/oLaser/step.gml:1-3" /> <Cite quirk="1" />.
- A UFO destroyed by a stunned monster, thrown or knocked into it, is left out of the lifetime tally of UFOs
  killed, which a thrown item's hit adds it to <Cite gml="objects/oEnemy/step.gml:243-251" /> <Cite gml="objects/oItem/step.gml:336" /> <Cite quirk="1" />.
