Level 14 always has the [[city-of-gold|City of Gold]]'s door, and the roll that places it asks for one thing of
the block under it: open space above. It doesn't ask whether the player can reach that space, so the door can be
sealed in rock that has to be broken open, and it doesn't skip [[kalis-altar|Kali's altar]], so the door can
stand on one, and make that half of the altar as hard to break as the door's block always is.

## What happens

Level 14 loads with the gold door on the left half of Kali's altar, at the top of the level. The player walks
over and stands on the altar's other half, beside the door <Cite clip="quirks/gold-door-on-altar" />.

<Clip id="quirks/gold-door-on-altar" />

## Why

`scrEntityGen` goes over the [[temple|Temple]]'s blocks one by one, and on level 14, until the door is made, each
block outside a shop with open space above it, but for the top few rows, takes the door's roll:

```gml scripts/scrEntityGen.gml:400-409
        if (y > 32 and not collision_point(x, y-16, oSolid, 0, 0) and global.genGoldEntrance and not global.madeGoldEntrance)
        {
            if (rand(1,global.goldChance) == 1)
            {
                instance_create(x, y-16, oGoldDoor);
                invincible = true;
                global.madeGoldEntrance = true;
            }
            else global.goldChance -= 1;
        }
```

`goldChance` starts each game at 160 <Cite gml="scripts/scrClearGlobals.gml:80" />, and if no block wins, the
door goes on the first block with open space above it <Cite gml="scripts/scrEntityGen.gml:482-495" />. Either
way, one free tile is all the door needs: the space above the block can be a pocket in the rock, with no way into
it. The Spear Traps' roll, the `else` beside it, skips trees and the altar (`type != "Altar"`)
<Cite gml="scripts/scrEntityGen.gml:410-418" />; the door's doesn't, and the block under it, an altar half
included, gets `invincible = true`, which explosions and the Mattock spare
<Cite gml="objects/oExplosion/collision_oSolid.gml:14" /> <Cite gml="objects/oMattockHit/other_animation_end.gml:1-22" />. But the other half's destroy event destroys every
altar half, `with oSacAltarLeft`, which takes in its child, the right half
<Cite gml="objects/oSacAltarLeft/destroy.gml:62-66" />, and the door's half still crumbles when the floor under it
goes, once it's within the view <Cite gml="objects/oSacAltarLeft/step.gml:1-5" /> <Cite quirk="1" />.

## Reproducing it

Reach level 14 and look for the door. Most levels have it in the open; some seal it in a pocket of rock, which
a bomb or the Mattock opens from above or beside. Waiting at level 13's exit before taking it rerolls level 14, and a sweep of
3,757 level 14s rerolled that way put the door on the altar in 15, 1 in 77 of the 1,156 with an altar
<Cite quirk="1" />. The replay of the clip rerolls level 14 with seed 1: the door stands on the altar's left
half, which is `invincible`, and the player stands on the right half beside it
<Cite replay="fidelity/replays/temple-seed-1-gold-door-on-altar.txt" />. With seed 9 another level 14 puts the
door in a 20-tile pocket under the floor of the room above, and the run blows its way in with two bombs and
carries the [[sceptre|Sceptre]] into the door, which opens
<Cite replay="fidelity/replays/city-of-gold-seed-9-sealed-door.txt" />. [[city-of-gold-route]] walks the rest of
the way in.

## In the port

The spelunky.ai port reproduces it exactly: it's in docs/quirks.md's list of the 1.1 source's bugs
<Cite quirk="1" />, and the oracle's fingerprints of the replays pin the door's place, the altar's `invincible`
and the sealed door's pocket. No option turns it off.
