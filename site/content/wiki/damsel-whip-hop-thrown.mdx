Cracking the [[whip|Whip]] at a [[damsel|Damsel]] doesn't hurt her: it's meant to make her hop. But the hop
comes down as fast as a throw, and the game can't tell the two apart, so she lies stunned on the floor as if
the player had thrown her, for a time that has nothing to do with the hit.

## What happens

The player whips a Damsel. She hops, unhurt, but falls back fast enough to count as thrown, and lies stunned
where she lands for what her cry for help had left to count down, rather than a throw's
<Stat id="damsel.stun-time" />. Then she gets up and runs <Cite clip="quirks/damsel-whip-hop-thrown" />.

<Clip id="quirks/damsel-whip-hop-thrown" />

## Why

The whip's collision with a Damsel on her feet, unless it's the [[machete|Machete]]'s, which hurts her
instead, only sends her up, and doesn't hurt her:

```gml objects/oDamsel/collision_oWhip.gml:13-23
else if (status != THROWN and (status < EXIT or status == SLAVE or status == KISS) and hit == 0)
{
    yVel = -2;
    hit = 10;
    playSound(global.sndHit);
    playSound(global.sndDamsel);
    if (forSale)
    {
        scrShopkeeperAnger(3);
    }
}
```

Falling back at 0.6 a step more, she's moving down at 2.2 pixels a step as she reaches the floor, and her step
treats any Damsel falling faster than 2 out of the player's hands as thrown:

```gml objects/oDamsel/step.gml:145-150
if (not held and yVel > 2 and status != THROWN)
{
    status = THROWN;
    SS_SetSoundPan(global.sndDamsel, 0);
    playSound(global.sndDamsel);
}
```

Thrown, she counts as grabbed in the statistics if nothing had knocked her down before
<Cite gml="objects/oDamsel/step.gml:241-245" />, and lies on the floor until her `counter` runs out, then runs
<Cite gml="objects/oDamsel/step.gml:281-287" />. A throw from the hands sets `counter` to her stun time
<Cite gml="scripts/scrUseItem.gml:597-603" />; the hop doesn't, so it's whatever `counter` held: the countdown
to her next cry for help, up to 200 steps <Cite gml="objects/oDamsel/step.gml:189-205" />
<Cite gml="objects/oDamsel/other_animation_end.gml:1-5" />. The Kissing Parlor's Damsel never counts it down,
so hers is the 200 she's made with <Cite gml="objects/oDamsel/create.gml:37" /> <Cite quirk="1" />.

## Reproducing it

Crack the whip at any Damsel standing out of water (in water her gravity is a third, and the hop may not come
down fast enough). In the [[kissing-parlor|Kissing Parlor]] it angers the Shopkeeper too. The replay of the
clip's staged scene, on a custom level, whips a Damsel, who lands as thrown without losing a hit point, is
counted as grabbed, and gets up and runs 154 steps later
<Cite replay="fidelity/replays/damsel-whip-hop-thrown.txt" />.

## In the port

The spelunky.ai port reproduces it exactly: it's in docs/quirks.md's list of the 1.1 source's bugs
<Cite quirk="1" />, and the oracle's fingerprint of the replay pins her fall and her stun. No option turns it
off.
