A flooded level is a level of [[jungle|the Jungle]] with a fifth row of rooms under the usual four, and the
bottom two rows given over to a lake. The path to the exit runs as on any Jungle level until the lake, and
the exit stands at the lake's edge. In the deep water are [[piranha|Piranhas]] and, in its own room, the
[[megamouth|Megamouth]], which level generation makes nowhere else. The level opens with "I CAN HEAR RUSHING
WATER...".

## Generation

### The roll

The roll is made in the screen between levels, as the player leaves it for one of levels 5 to 8: 1 in 10,
and on a 1 the next level is built in the taller room that flooded levels use
<Cite gml="objects/oTransition/step.gml:17-24" /> <Cite gml="scripts/scrClearGlobals.gml:57" />. So a level
the player reaches another way is never flooded: the level 5 shortcut on the title screen goes straight to
level 5 <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/other_animation_end.gml:137-143" />. And taking the
[[black-market|Black Market]]'s hidden door stops the roll for the rest of the game, the market's own level
and every Jungle level after it <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:879" /> <Cite gml="objects/oTransition/step.gml:20" /> <Cite quirk="1" /> <Cite replay="fidelity/replays/jungle-seed-9-market-dark-roll.txt" />.
The rolls for a [[restless-dead|graveyard level]] and a [[dark-level|dark level]] are made separately, so a
flooded level can be either or both <Cite gml="scripts/scrInitLevel.gml:147-176" />.

A flooded level opens with "I CAN HEAR RUSHING WATER..." <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/alarm_0.gml:34-39" />.
On a graveyard or a dark level, it comes 209 steps after their message <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/alarm_0.gml:5-13" />
<Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/alarm_0.gml:27-33" /> <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/alarm_1.gml:18-23" />. A level
that's all three has three messages, and the last is "YOU HEAR RUSHING WATER..." in place of "I CAN HEAR":
only the graveyard's message on a dark level sets a timer for the lake's, and that timer's message has its
own wording <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/alarm_1.gml:11-17" /> <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/alarm_4.gml:1-6" /> <Cite quirk="1" />.
That's at most 1 flooded level in 120: the graveyard is 1 in 10, and the dark level 1 in 12 once the area
has had none and the level before took more than 20 seconds past the minute
([[dark-level#the-roll]]). In the clip below, level 8 is all three, and the graveyard's message gives way to
the lake's <Cite clip="quirks/dark-graveyard-lake-you-hear" /> <Cite replay="fidelity/replays/jungle-seed-10-dark-graveyard-lake.txt" />.

### The layout

The level is 42 tiles high instead of 34, room for a fifth row of rooms <Cite gml="scripts/scrInitLevel.gml:66-74" /> <Cite gml="scripts/scrInitLevel.gml:106-116" />. The
path is laid through the top four rows as on any level ([[mines#generation|the Mines' Generation]]). Then
the lake takes the bottom two <Cite gml="scripts/scrLevelGen.gml:192-210" />:

| Rooms | What | |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The top three rows | The Jungle's rooms, as on any of its levels | <Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGen2.gml:52-189" /> |
| The fourth row | The lake's top, whatever the path made of these rooms: open above, water below. The room the path drops into from above has an open top | <Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGen2.gml:204-221" /> |
| The exit's room, in the fourth row | The exit, on a strip of earth just above the water | <Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGen2.gml:65-77" /> |
| The fifth row | The lake's bottom: water, with earth along the bed in seven of its eight layouts | <Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGen2.gml:190-203" /> |
| One room of the fifth row, never under the exit | The Megamouth's room: water, with the Megamouth in the middle | <Cite gml="scripts/scrLevelGen.gml:204-209" /> <Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGen2.gml:222-228" /> |

In the clip, the player crosses a flooded level above the water and takes the exit at the lake's edge <Cite clip="flooded-level/crossing" />.

## Inhabitants

| Monster | Where | Chance | |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [[megamouth\|Megamouth]] | In its room along the lake's bottom | One, always | <Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGen2.gml:226" /> <Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGen2.gml:390-394" /> |
| [[piranha\|Piranha]] | In the water, the lake's and the pools' | 1 in 30 for each water tile not inside a block | <Cite gml="scripts/scrEntityGen.gml:326-336" /> |
| [[dead-fish\|Dead Fish]] | As the Piranha's, on a [[restless-dead\|graveyard level]], in their place | 1 in 30 | <Cite gml="scripts/scrEntityGen.gml:332" /> |

So a lake, with hundreds of water tiles, holds many Piranhas: eleven on seed 1's
<Cite replay="fidelity/replays/flow-lake-jungle-seed-1.txt" />. Above the water, the rooms have [[jungle#inhabitants|the Jungle's]]
monsters as on any of its levels. In the clip, the player swims down into
the lake: Piranhas bite him twice, then the Megamouth charges out of the deep water and bites him twice more,
and he dies <Cite clip="flooded-level/megamouth" />.

## Hazards

- **The [[megamouth|Megamouth]]**, which charges a player who swims.
- **[[piranha|Piranhas]]** all through the water.
- **Water** puts out flares and a crate of flares, on a dark flooded level <Cite gml="objects/oFlare/step.gml:4-17" /> <Cite gml="objects/oFlareCrate/step.gml:3-18" />.

## Items and shops

Shops and treasure are as on [[jungle#items-and-shops|any Jungle level]], in the top three rows: the lake's
rooms are never side rooms, which shops need <Cite gml="scripts/scrLevelGen.gml:239-275" />. The
Megamouth bursts into four [[crate|Crates]] when it dies <Cite gml="objects/oJaws/step.gml:23-28" />.

## Trivia

- The room script's comments name the lake's rooms "Lake Top", "Lake Bottom" and "Mega Mouth" <Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGen2.gml:190" /> <Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGen2.gml:204" /> <Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGen2.gml:222" />.
- The exit room's layout is picked with `rand(5,5)`, and the Megamouth room's with `switch(rand(1,1))`: rolls
  with one outcome <Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGen2.gml:67" /> <Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGen2.gml:224" />.
- A commented-out `global.lake = false;` sits above the lake's code in the level script <Cite gml="scripts/scrLevelGen.gml:192-194" />.
