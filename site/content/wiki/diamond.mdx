It's the most valuable gem, worth more than three [[big-ruby|Big Rubies]], and in the Mines as much as a
[[golden-idol|Golden Idol]], without having to be carried out. [[level-generation|Level generation]]'s rolls never make one:
Diamonds come from the [[ghost|Ghost]], which turns every big gem it touches into one, and from the Lady Xoc
statue in the [[city-of-gold|City of Gold]]. The player collects one by touching it.

past the Mines, the code counting [[olmecs-lair|Olmec's Lair]] as an area of its own

## Locations

A Diamond comes from three things only <Cite quirk="1" row="The rare items' only sources" />:

- **The [[ghost|Ghost]].** A [[big-emerald|Big Emerald]], [[big-sapphire|Big Sapphire]] or [[big-ruby|Big Ruby]]
  the Ghost touches is replaced, where it lies, by a Diamond <Cite gml="objects/oEmeraldBig/collision_oGhost.gml:1-2" />
  <Cite gml="objects/oSapphireBig/collision_oGhost.gml:1-2" /> <Cite gml="objects/oRubyBig/collision_oGhost.gml:1-2" />.
  The Ghost comes for a player who stays too long on a level, and drifts through walls toward him, so it
  changes the big gems it passes on the way, those still buried in the rock too: one buried gem becomes a
  buried Diamond <Cite replay="fidelity/replays/item-seed-2-diamond.txt" />.
- **The Lady Xoc statue**, which stands in the [[city-of-gold|City of Gold]]: the block at its brow
  drops two Diamonds when it's destroyed <Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGen4.gml:378-393" />
  <Cite gml="objects/oXocBlock/destroy.gml:24" />.
- **The [[level-editor|level editor]]**'s `8` tile <Cite gml="scripts/scrCreateTileObj.gml:281-284" />.

## Collecting

The player collects a Diamond by touching it while he isn't stunned or dead; the one he takes is the
treasure nearest him, one a step <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:1990-2016" />. It's made uncollectable,
and its own alarm, set to <Stat id="diamond.collect-delay" /> (<Stat id="diamond.collect-delay" as="seconds" />)
when it's made, makes it collectable <Cite gml="objects/oDiamond/create.gml:6-7" />
<Cite gml="objects/oDiamond/alarm_0.gml:1" />: a big gem the Ghost has just turned into a Diamond can't be
picked up for that long, even if the gem could have been.

Its value shows under his money and is counted into it after a short pause; whatever is left is counted at
once when he leaves the level <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:1953-1971" />
<Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/other_animation_end.gml:46-50" />. A gem plays its own sound, where gold plays the
coin's <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:2000-2014" />, and the screen between levels shows each Diamond
collected on the level <Cite gml="objects/oTransition/alarm_0.gml:83-88" />.

A loose one falls and comes to rest like other treasure, moving only while it's on the screen; a [[web]] it
falls into holds it, and lava it sinks into destroys it <Cite gml="objects/oTreasure/step.gml:1-79" />
<Cite gml="objects/oWeb/collision_oTreasure.gml:1-2" />.
