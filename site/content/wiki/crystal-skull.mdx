To the code it's a [[golden-idol|Golden Idol]] with a value of its own, three times the Golden Idol's, so the
player carries it to the exit to cash it in and a [[shopkeeper|Shopkeeper]] buys it, as he does an idol. It takes the Golden
Idol's place on the Jungle's graveyard levels, and its trap is the [[ghost|Ghost]], which comes for the player
the moment he lifts it.

<Cite gml="objects/oCrystalSkull/create.gml:3-4" /> <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:2068-2084" />. Level
generation only makes one in [[jungle|the Jungle]], where it's worth

## Locations

Each Jungle level has a 1 in 10 chance of being a graveyard level, [[restless-dead|the Restless Dead]]
<Cite gml="scripts/scrInitLevel.gml:147-148" />. On one, the Jungle's idol room (a 1 in 9 roll for two side
rooms in three, once an altar's 1 in 12 fails, and one a level at most) holds the Crystal Skull instead of the
Golden Idol <Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGen2.gml:79-114" />. The room is a tomb of temple stone with a small
chamber in it: the skull in the middle, and four piles of bones that rise as [[skeleton|Skeletons]] in the
corners, the code's `if (rand(1,2))` choosing between bones and a [[jar|Jar]] for them, which is always true
<Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGen2.gml:490-494" /> <Cite quirk="1" />. In custom levels, it's the
[[level-editor|level editor]]'s `O` tile <Cite gml="scripts/scrCreateTileObj.gml:265-268" />.

## Collecting

<Clip id="crystal-skull/ghost" />

It's heavy, carried and thrown as the Golden Idol is <Cite gml="objects/oGoldIdol/create.gml:7" />. Lifting it
on a graveyard level, when no Ghost is on the level yet, brings one: it appears just past the edge of the
screen, halfway down, on the right if the player is in the right half of the level and on the left if not
<Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:1232-1239" />. The Ghost drifts toward him through walls and kills him on
touch whatever his hearts <Cite gml="objects/oGhost/step.gml:1-28" />
<Cite gml="objects/oGhost/collision_oCharacter.gml:1-34" />. In the clip, the player lifts the skull, the
Ghost drifts in from the right, and it kills him <Cite clip="crystal-skull/ghost" />
<Cite replay="fidelity/replays/item-seed-29-crystal-skull.txt" />.

Carried to the level's exit, it's taken from his hands and its value added to his money, and the screen
between levels shows a crystal skull among the loot <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:2068-2084" />
<Cite gml="objects/oTransition/alarm_0.gml:108-113" />. Set down on a shop's floor near an idle Shopkeeper
(the level's first), it's bought at its full value, as an idol is
<Cite gml="objects/oGoldIdol/step.gml:6-27" />.
