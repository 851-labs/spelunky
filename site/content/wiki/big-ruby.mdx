It's the most valuable of the three big gems, worth more than a [[big-sapphire|Big Sapphire]] or a
[[big-emerald|Big Emerald]], and four times a small [[ruby|Ruby]]. Besides the rock and the nooks where level
generation puts every big gem, two special rooms are built with Big Rubies in them. The player collects one by
touching it; a [[ghost|Ghost]] that touches one turns it into a [[diamond|Diamond]].

A Big Ruby's base value is <Stat id="big-ruby.value" />, and it's worth a quarter of that more for each area
past the Mines, the code counting [[olmecs-lair|Olmec's Lair]] as an area of its own
<Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:1996" /> <Cite gml="scripts/scrInitLevel.gml:24-29" />:

## Locations

### In the rock

<Clip id="spectacles/reveal" />

Each block of rock that isn't a gold vein (71 in 100 aren't) and isn't on the level's edge rolls for a big gem
inside it. The Big Ruby's roll comes last, once the [[big-sapphire|Big Sapphire]]'s and the
[[big-emerald|Big Emerald]]'s fail:

| Area | Chance for each block | |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [[mines\|The Mines]] | 1 in 140, once the rolls for a Big Sapphire (1 in 100) and a Big Emerald (1 in 120) fail | <Cite gml="objects/oBrick/create.gml:7-14" /> |
| [[jungle\|The Jungle]] | 1 in 120, once the rolls for a Big Sapphire (1 in 80) and a Big Emerald (1 in 100) fail | <Cite gml="objects/oLush/create.gml:5-13" /> |
| [[ice-caves\|The Ice Caves]] | 1 in 80, once the rolls for a Big Sapphire (1 in 40) and a Big Emerald (1 in 60) fail | <Cite gml="objects/oDark/create.gml:3-13" /> |
| [[temple\|The Temple]] and [[olmecs-lair\|Olmec's Lair]] | 1 in 100, once the rolls for a Big Sapphire (1 in 60) and a Big Emerald (1 in 80) fail | <Cite gml="objects/oTemple/create.gml:5-14" /> |

The [[tutorial|Tutorial]]'s bricks roll as the Mines' do <Cite gml="scripts/isLevel.gml:7" />. In the Temple,
one block of rock in 100 is the Jungle's earth, which rolls at the Jungle's odds, and one in ten a stone block,
which holds no gem <Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGen4.gml:317-322" /> <Cite gml="objects/oBlock/create.gml:1-4" />.
The [[city-of-gold|City of Gold]]'s temple blocks are gold instead, and don't roll for gems
<Cite gml="objects/oTemple/create.gml:7-10" />, but its blocks of earth still do ([[city-jungle-earth]]) <Cite quirk="1" row="One block in a hundred of the City of Gold is the Jungle's earth." />. A buried gem stays where it is, hidden behind the rock, until the block around it is
destroyed, by a [[bomb|Bomb]] or a [[mattock|Mattock]] for instance; then it drops out. With the
[[spectacles|Spectacles]] or the [[udjat-eye|Udjat Eye]], treasure is drawn in front of the rock, so the buried
gems show through it <Cite gml="objects/oTreasure/step.gml:63-67" />. In the clip, the player picks up a pair of
Spectacles in a cave of bricks, and the big gems buried around him, two Big Rubies among them, show through
the rock <Cite clip="spectacles/reveal" />.

### In special rooms

- **A [[snake-pit|snake pit]]**, a shaft of rooms off a Mines level's main path, full of
  [[snake|Snakes]]: its bottom room holds two Big Rubies <Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGen.gml:212-218" />
  <Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGen.gml:471-474" />.
- **[[sacrifice-pit|The Sacrifice Pit]]**, a Temple level: the six blocks of the pit's floor, under its pool
  of lava, each hold one, which it lets out when it's destroyed <Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGen4.gml:201-204" />
  <Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGen4.gml:333-337" /> <Cite gml="objects/oTemple/destroy.gml:34-37" />.

[[rock|Rock]] (1 in 100) and a [[jar|Jar]] (1 in 40) fail <Cite gml="scripts/scrTreasureGen.gml:27-46" />. A
big gem comes late in it:

## Collecting

<Clip id="big-ruby/chest" />

The player collects a Big Ruby by touching it while he isn't stunned or dead; the one he takes is the
treasure nearest him, one a step <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:1990-2016" />. It's made uncollectable,
and its own alarm, set to <Stat id="big-ruby.collect-delay" /> (<Stat id="big-ruby.collect-delay" as="seconds" />)
when it's made, makes it collectable <Cite gml="objects/oRubyBig/create.gml:6-7" />
<Cite gml="objects/oRubyBig/alarm_0.gml:1" />, so one fresh out of a Jar, a Chest or a monster lies a moment
before the player can take it. In the clip, a Chest on the Tutorial's high ledge opens into a small
[[emerald|Emerald]], [[sapphire|Sapphire]] and Ruby and a Big Ruby, around the player's feet. He collects
nothing for 20 steps, then the Big Ruby, the nearest, as he walks off the other way; he picks up the small
Ruby, which fell off the ledge, below it <Cite clip="big-ruby/chest" />.

Its value shows under his money and is counted into it after a short pause; whatever is left is counted at
once when he leaves the level <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:1953-1971" />
<Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/other_animation_end.gml:46-50" />. A gem plays its own sound, where gold plays the
coin's <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:2000-2014" />, and the screen between levels shows each Big Ruby
collected on the level <Cite gml="objects/oTransition/alarm_0.gml:77-82" />.

A loose one falls and comes to rest like other treasure, moving only while it's on the screen; a [[web]] it
falls into holds it, and lava it sinks into destroys it <Cite gml="objects/oTreasure/step.gml:1-79" />
<Cite gml="objects/oWeb/collision_oTreasure.gml:1-2" />.

**The Ghost.** A [[ghost|Ghost]] that touches a Big Ruby turns it into a [[diamond|Diamond]], in the same
place <Cite gml="objects/oRubyBig/collision_oGhost.gml:1-2" />. The Ghost drifts through walls, so it does this
to gems still buried in the rock too <Cite replay="fidelity/replays/item-seed-2-diamond.txt" />
<Cite quirk="1" row="The rare items' only sources" />.

## Trivia

- Only the three big gems have code for a Ghost's touch, so a Ghost passes over the small gems, gold and a
  Diamond and leaves them as they are <Cite gml="objects/oRubyBig/collision_oGhost.gml:1-2" />.
