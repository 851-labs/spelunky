The game keeps custom levels out of its statistics with a check, "is this one of the game's own levels?", put
around each tally. Around the [[yeti-king|Yeti King]]'s touch, it went around the damage too, so in a custom
level the touch takes nothing. Around its kill, it was left out, so that kill is tallied even there.

## What happens

In a custom level the player walks into the Yeti King twice: it knocks him back and he blinks, but he keeps all
four hearts. A bomb then kills it, and the kill still goes into the lifetime tally
<Cite clip="quirks/yeti-king-touch-custom-level" />.

<Clip id="quirks/yeti-king-touch-custom-level" />

## Why

The Yeti King's touch knocks the player back and makes him invincible for 30 steps, then takes its 2 hearts
only on one of the game's own levels:

```gml objects/oYetiKing/collision_oCharacter.gml:16-31
else if (other.invincible == 0)
{
    other.blink = 30
    other.invincible = 30
    if (other.y < y)
        other.yVel = -6
    if (other.x < x)
        other.xVel = -6
    else
        other.xVel = 6
    
    if (global.plife > 0 and isRealLevel())
    {
        global.plife -= 2;
        if (global.plife <= 0) global.enemyDeaths[14] += 1;
    }
```

`isRealLevel` is, as its own comment has it, "Only used for stat-checking atm.": it's true in the game's level
rooms and Olmec's <Cite gml="scripts/isRealLevel.gml:4-8" />. Every other monster's touch takes its hearts first
and checks it only for the tally, as a Yeti's does <Cite gml="objects/oYeti/collision_oCharacter.gml:35-39" />. The
Yeti King's kill is the other way round: its step adds it to `enemyKills[14]`, the lifetime tally of the
statistics' YETI KING row, without `isRealLevel()` <Cite gml="objects/oYetiKing/step.gml:41-46" />. Every other
monster's kill but Olmec's, which only his lair has, skips that tally outside the game's own levels
<Cite quirk="1" />.

## Reproducing it

Make a custom level with the level editor's `Y`, and walk into it: it knocks the player back, and he keeps his
hearts. The level editor's test does the same. The replay of the clip's staged scene, on a custom level, walks
into the Yeti King twice, and the player keeps his 4 hearts; a bomb kills it, and `enemyKills[14]` becomes 1
<Cite replay="fidelity/replays/enemy-yeti-king-custom-level-touch.txt" />. On a real level, in a Yeti lair, the
same touch takes him from 4 hearts to 2 <Cite replay="fidelity/replays/enemy-yeti-king-swat-seed-47.txt" />.

## In the port

The spelunky.ai port reproduces it exactly: it's in docs/quirks.md's list of the 1.1 source's bugs
<Cite quirk="1" />, and the oracle's fingerprint of the replay pins the player's hearts and the tally. No option
turns it off.
