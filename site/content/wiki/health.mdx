The hearts are the first number in the row at the top of the screen. Everything that hurts the player takes
some of them, from a [[snake|Snake]]'s bite to a [[bomb|Bomb]]'s explosion ([[damage]]), and a few things
give them back: a rescued [[damsel|Damsel]], a kiss bought in the [[kissing-parlor|Kissing Parlor]], the
[[kapala|Kapala]] and [[kalis-favour|Kali]]. There's no other way to heal, and no slow recovery over time.

## How it works

The game keeps the player's hearts in one number, `global.plife`, which the HUD draws beside a heart icon,
as 0 when it's below 0 <Cite gml="scripts/scrDrawHUD.gml:32-35" />.

**Starting.** A new game sets it to <Stat id="health.start" />, or <Stat id="health.tunnel-man-start" />
for the [[tunnel-man|Tunnel Man]] <Cite gml="scripts/scrClearGlobals.gml:103-114" />. On the title screen
and the [[scoring#the-high-scores-room|high scores screen]] it's set back to that on every step, so nothing there can kill the player
<Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:2-7" />. A custom level from the [[level-editor|level editor]] starts
with the hearts its author set, from 1 to 99 <Cite gml="scripts/scrLoadLevel.gml:138-142" />, and each of
the three bonus rooms, whose doors are on the high scores screen,
with <Stat id="health.bonus-room" /> <Cite gml="objects/oHighscores/create.gml:38" />
<Cite gml="objects/oMoonRoom/create.gml:1" /> <Cite gml="objects/oStarsRoom/create.gml:1" />.

**The limit.** At the start of each of the player's steps, hearts above <Stat id="health.max" bare /> are
cut back to <Stat id="health.max" bare />, as bombs and ropes are <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:9-11" />.

**Losing hearts.** Monsters, traps, explosions and falls take hearts in their own amounts, and some stun the
player or knock him back as well: [[damage]] has them all. Most monster touches only take hearts while he
has some (`if (global.plife > 0)`), but the checks in his own step for explosions, lava, spikes and being
crushed don't, so his hearts can go below 0 <Cite gml="objects/oEnemy/collision_oCharacter.gml:23-25" />
<Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:196-212" /> <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:1614-1616" />.

**Dying.** On a level, or in a bonus room, his step checks for fewer than 1 heart while he's alive. If he
wears the [[ankh|Ankh]], it shatters and he comes back with <Stat id="ankh.revive" /> at the level's entrance
(or inside the [[moai|Moai]], on a level that has one, or at the start of [[olmecs-lair|Olmec's Lair]]), invincible for
<Stat id="ankh.revive-invincibility" />. Otherwise his hearts are set to 0, the HUD is hidden, the high
scores are updated and he's dead <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:1849-1921" />. What can still hurt a
dead body keeps taking hearts, lava 99 a step <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:196-203" />, and once
they're below −99 the body bursts into blood and disappears <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:1484-1488" />.

**Gaining hearts.** Each source adds to the count, up to the limit:

- **A rescued [[damsel|Damsel]].** Bringing one to a level's exit counts her: carried out through it
  <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:782-789" />, carried onto it, which takes her in and leaves the player
  on the level <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:2068-2092" />, or walking in by herself
  <Cite gml="objects/oDamsel/step.gml:3-11" />. On the screen between levels, a Damsel then waits in the
  player's path: when he reaches her she kisses him and a
  heart rises <Cite gml="objects/oTransition/create.gml:42-45" /> <Cite gml="objects/oPDummy/step.gml:3-21" />
  <Cite gml="objects/oDamselKiss/step.gml:3-7" />, and when that screen ends, skipped or not, he gets
  <Stat id="health.damsel-heal" /> <Cite gml="objects/oDamselKiss/other_room_end.gml:1" />. The screen makes
  one kissing Damsel however many were rescued, so two on one level give one heart; only a custom level can
  have two ([Quirks](#quirks)).
- **A kiss in the [[kissing-parlor|Kissing Parlor]].** Paying its Damsel gives <Stat id="health.kiss-heal" />
  and the message "NOW AIN'T SHE SWEET!" ("HE", playing as the Damsel) <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:1429-1449" />. A kiss costs
  <Stat id="damsel.kiss-price" at="2" /> on level 2, and $5,000 more for each level after
  <Cite gml="scripts/getKissValue.gml:1" />.
- **The [[kapala|Kapala]].** Every ninth drop of blood the player touches while wearing it gives
  <Stat id="kapala.heal" /> <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/collision_oBlood.gml:1-13" />.
- **[[kalis-favour|Kali]].** One of her gifts, for a high enough favour, is <Stat id="health.kali-heal" />
  at once, with the message "YOU FEEL INVIGORATED!" <Cite gml="scripts/scrGetFavorMsg.gml:27-50" />.

## Quirks

- **Two Damsels rescued on one level give one heart** ([[two-damsels-one-heart]]). The screen between levels
  shows both in the loot, but makes one kissing Damsel <Cite gml="objects/oTransition/create.gml:42-45" /> <Cite quirk="1" />. A level
  the game makes has one Damsel at most, so it takes a custom level with two of the
  [[level-editor|level editor]]'s; a clip shows two carried onto the exit, and one heart
  <Cite clip="quirks/two-damsels-one-heart" />.
- Not every hit that leaves the player without hearts counts in the end-of-game statistics' deaths table:
  [[damage#quirks]] has which.
