The Black Market is a level of [[jungle|the Jungle]] given over to shops: five that sell items, a
[[dice-house|Dice House]] and the [[ankh-shop|Ankh Shop]], each with its [[shopkeeper|Shopkeeper]], laid out
the same way every time. The way in is a door hidden inside a block, which the
[[udjat-eye|Udjat Eye]] helps to find. Making the market has lasting effects: no level has a shop after it,
and no Jungle level after it is flooded.

## Generation

### The hidden door

Once a game, one of the Jungle's levels 5 to 7 hides the market's door: level 5 one time in 3, level 6 one
time in 2, and otherwise level 7 <Cite gml="scripts/scrInitLevel.gml:186-192" />. Generation puts it inside the
first block, outside the shops, that has a block under it (that one made indestructible) and wins a roll of
1 in a number that starts each game at 160 and falls by one with each block that loses. The block it's in
can't be a tree or one that can't be destroyed, and the block under it can't be [[kalis-altar|an altar]]
<Cite gml="scripts/scrEntityGen.gml:212-225" /> <Cite gml="scripts/scrClearGlobals.gml:73" />. Should every
block lose, a second pass puts a door in block after block, not just one, but it never runs on a real
level: a level has hundreds of such blocks, and from the 160th on the roll can't lose
<Cite gml="scripts/scrEntityGen.gml:303-319" /> <Cite quirk="1" row="The Black Market door's fallback puts a door in every block that qualifies." />. The door is
inside the rock, so it has to be dug or bombed out. On its level, the [[udjat-eye#the-black-market|Udjat Eye]]
blinks and beeps faster the nearer the player is to it. [[black-market-route]] walks through finding it and
going in.

Walking into the door, as into an [[door|exit]], makes the next level, 6, 7 or 8, the Black Market
<Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:879" /> <Cite gml="scripts/scrLevelGen.gml:44-46" />. In the first
clip, a bomb blows open the rock hiding the door on level 5; the player walks into it, and level 6, the
Black Market, loads with the message "WELCOME TO THE BLACK MARKET!"
<Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/alarm_0.gml:14-20" /> <Cite clip="black-market/entrance" />.

### The layout

The market has no path of its own to find: its rooms are set, a grid of four by four with the entrance in
the top left and the exit in the bottom right <Cite gml="scripts/scrLevelGen.gml:44-69" />. Columns are from
the left, rows from the top:

| Rooms | What | |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The left column | The entrance's room, then rooms that drop down to the bottom row | <Cite gml="scripts/scrLevelGen.gml:51-54" /> <Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGenMarket.gml:52-64" /> <Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGenMarket.gml:147-161" /> |
| The middle two columns, top three rows | Five item shops and the [[dice-house\|Dice House]] (the third column's third room), open on both sides, so the rows of shops are walked through | <Cite gml="scripts/scrLevelGen.gml:55-62" /> <Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGenMarket.gml:117-146" /> |
| The right column | Two rooms that drop to the [[ankh-shop\|Ankh Shop]] in the third row, which opens only at its top | <Cite gml="scripts/scrLevelGen.gml:63-65" /> <Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGenMarket.gml:112-116" /> <Cite quirk="1" row="The Ankh is never marked for sale." /> |
| The bottom row | A walk from the left column to the exit, in the bottom right | <Cite gml="scripts/scrLevelGen.gml:54-66" /> <Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGenMarket.gml:65-111" /> |

The rooms around the shops are the Jungle's earth, with [[water|water]], [[vine|vines]], [[spikes|Spikes]] and [[tree|trees]] in some
of the layouts <Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGenMarket.gml:77-111" /> <Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGenMarket.gml:238-426" />.
In the second clip, the player leaves Wang's Clothing Shop, climbs down to the bottom row, and walks along
it under the Dice House to the exit <Cite clip="black-market/walk" />.

### Special levels

The market is never a [[dark-level|dark level]], though a dark roll that comes up on it uses up the Jungle's
dark level all the same <Cite gml="scripts/scrInitLevel.gml:167-176" /> <Cite quirk="1" row="A dark level needs the previous level's clock" />
<Cite replay="fidelity/replays/jungle-seed-9-market-dark-roll.txt" />. It's never a
[[flooded-level|flooded level]]: taking its door stops the roll for one, for the market's level and every
Jungle level after it <Cite gml="objects/oTransition/step.gml:20-24" /> <Cite quirk="1" row="No flooded level after the Black Market's door." />. The roll for a
[[restless-dead|graveyard level]], 1 in 10, is made for it, as for every Jungle level
<Cite gml="scripts/scrInitLevel.gml:145-157" />, so one market in ten is a graveyard: graves on its earth,
and the graveyard's monsters (below). Its message is still only the market's: "THE DEAD ARE RESTLESS!" comes
second on a graveyard only if it's dark, which the market never is ([[black-market-graveyard]])
<Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/alarm_0.gml:14-33" /> <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/alarm_1.gml:11-17" /> <Cite quirk="1" row="A Black Market can be a graveyard level, and never says so." />.
In the clip below, the player drops from the entrance onto a grave under the shops, and the market
welcomes him <Cite clip="quirks/market-graveyard" /> <Cite replay="fidelity/replays/jungle-seed-9-market-graveyard.txt" />.

## Inhabitants

Each of the seven shops has its [[shopkeeper|Shopkeeper]] <Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGenMarket.gml:342-346" />.
Outside the shops, [[level-generation|level generation]] makes [[jungle#inhabitants|the Jungle's]] monsters, as on any Jungle
level <Cite gml="scripts/scrEntityGen.gml:207-337" />:

| Monster | Where | Chance | |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [[bat\|Bat]] | Hanging under a block | 1 in 80 | <Cite gml="scripts/scrEntityGen.gml:268-272" /> |
| [[mantrap\|Mantrap]] | On a block, with no water above, but never a block of a room's bottom row | 1 in 60 | <Cite gml="scripts/scrEntityGen.gml:285-289" /> |
| [[caveman\|Caveman]] | On a block, with no water above | 1 in 60, once the Mantrap's fails | <Cite gml="scripts/scrEntityGen.gml:290" /> |
| [[fire-frog\|Fire Frog]] | On a block | 1 in 120, once those fail | <Cite gml="scripts/scrEntityGen.gml:291" /> <Cite gml="scripts/scrEntityGen.gml:294" /> |
| [[frog\|Frog]] | On a block | 1 in 30, once those fail | <Cite gml="scripts/scrEntityGen.gml:292" /> <Cite gml="scripts/scrEntityGen.gml:295" /> |
| [[monkey\|Monkey]] | On a vine | 1 in 15 for each vine tile | <Cite gml="scripts/scrEntityGen.gml:321-324" /> |
| [[piranha\|Piranha]] | In the water | 1 in 30 for each water tile | <Cite gml="scripts/scrEntityGen.gml:326-336" /> |

The Mantrap's rule is the market's own, and the code's comment gives its reason: "to prevent mantraps from
spawning near shopkeepers in black market" <Cite gml="scripts/scrEntityGen.gml:287" />. On a graveyard market, [[zombie|Zombies]] and
[[vampire|Vampires]] take the ground's place, without that rule, so they can stand by the shops, and
[[dead-fish|Dead Fish]] take the Piranhas'
<Cite gml="scripts/scrEntityGen.gml:280-284" /> <Cite gml="scripts/scrEntityGen.gml:332" /> <Cite quirk="1" row="A Black Market can be a graveyard level, and never says so." />. As it's never dark,
it has no [[scarab|Scarabs]]. The [[ghost|Ghost]] comes to a player who stays too long, as on every level
<Cite gml="objects/oGame/step.gml:23-47" />.

## Hazards

- **The Shopkeepers.** Seven, close together: a lit bomb in one shop angers every one of them, and those off
  screen again on every step it burns ([[bomb-angers-every-shopkeeper]]) <Cite quirk="1" row="A lit bomb in a shop angers every active Shopkeeper, and those off screen again on every step." /> <Cite replay="fidelity/replays/shop-seed-9-market-bomb.txt" />.
  A [[dice-house|Dice House]] die carried left into the next shop angers no one, and carried on out of it,
  angers the nearest Shopkeeper, that shop's ([[dice-angers-next-shop]]) <Cite quirk="1" row="A Dice House's die angers the nearest Shopkeeper wherever it's outside a shop's room." />
  <Cite replay="fidelity/replays/shop-seed-9-market-dice-carried-out.txt" />.
  [[general-store#stealing|The General Store]] has what else angers them.
- **[[spear-trap|Spear Traps]]**, made outside the shops as in the Jungle <Cite gml="scripts/scrEntityGen.gml:226-253" />.
- **[[spikes|Spikes]]**, along the floor of one of the bottom row's layouts <Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGenMarket.gml:87" />
  <Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGenMarket.gml:267" />.
- **Water**, where [[piranha|Piranhas]] swim.

## Items and shops

### Shops

| Shop | Where | |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [[general-store\|General Store]], [[bomb-shop\|Bomb Shop]], [[weapon-shop\|Weapon Shop]], [[rare-shop\|Rare Shop]], [[clothing-shop\|Clothing Shop]] | The five item shops: each picks a kind the market hasn't got yet, starting from one at random | <Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGenMarket.gml:117-141" /> |
| [[dice-house\|Dice House]] | The third column's third room | <Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGenMarket.gml:142-146" /> |
| [[ankh-shop\|Ankh Shop]] | The right column's third room: one [[ankh\|Ankh]], for <Stat id="ankh.price" /> | <Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGenMarket.gml:112-116" /> <Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGenMarket.gml:361-364" /> |

So a market has one shop of each kind, as a rule. One market in five, the fifth item shop finds the last
kind left and is made a General Store all the same, so the market has two General Stores and none of the
kind left (unless that's the General Store) ([[two-general-stores]]) <Cite quirk="1" row="A Black Market's fifth item shop can be a second General Store." />
<Cite replay="fidelity/replays/shop-seed-9-market-two-general-stores.txt" />. The wares are the shops' usual
ones, priced by the level the market is on, as in any shop <Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGenMarket.gml:357-360" />
([[general-store#prices|the General Store's prices]]); the
Ankh, never marked for sale, can be thrown out of its shop and picked up outside at its price
([[ankh-throw-out]]) <Cite quirk="1" row="The Ankh is never marked for sale." /> <Cite replay="fidelity/replays/item-seed-9-ankh-thrown-out.txt" />.

Once the Black Market has been made, no level has a shop for the rest of the game
([[no-shops-after-black-market]]): the roll for one, on every later level, requires that it hasn't
<Cite gml="scripts/scrLevelGen.gml:240" /> <Cite quirk="1" row="No shop for the rest of the game once the Black Market is made." />.

### Treasure

Treasure is scattered over the blocks outside the shops as in [[mines#treasure|the Mines]]
<Cite gml="scripts/scrEntityGen.gml:255-259" />, and the earth hides gems and items as the Jungle's does
<Cite gml="objects/oLush/create.gml:5-15" />.

## Secrets

- **The [[ankh|Ankh]]**, which no other shop sells.
- **The door itself**, which the [[udjat-eye|Udjat Eye]] finds.

## Trivia

- The market's level code sets its rooms and returns before the rest of the level code runs, the shop roll
  among it, which is why that roll's check for the market does nothing on the market's own level
  <Cite gml="scripts/scrLevelGen.gml:44-69" /> <Cite gml="scripts/scrLevelGen.gml:240" />.
- The door's object is a kind of exit, named "Market Exit" <Cite gml="objects/oXMarket/create.gml:1-3" />.
- The market's room script has code for an idol, trap blocks and a cemetery's bones, copied from the
  Jungle's, but none of its rooms has those tiles <Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGenMarket.gml:295-298" />
  <Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGenMarket.gml:369-377" /> <Cite quirk="1" row="The Black Market's room script makes a golden idol for an I tile and a trap block for a B tile" /> <Cite quirk="1" row="if (rand(1,2)) is always true, and so is the Temple's rand(1,1) == 1." />.
