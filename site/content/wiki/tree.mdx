Trees grow from the floor of [[jungle|the Jungle]]'s rooms, [[black-market|the Black Market]]'s and
[[temple|the Temple]]'s: a trunk a few blocks tall, branches off its sides and two leaves beside its top. The
trunk is solid; the branches and leaves are platforms. A tree stands only as long as what's under it does.

## How it works

<Clip id="tree/climb" />

### The trunk

A trunk block is solid, as rock is <Cite gml="objects/oTree/create.gml:1-3" />. A player jumping or falling past
its side, pressing toward it, catches hold of it and hangs there, at any height and without
[[climbing-gloves|Climbing Gloves]]: unlike a ledge, the trunk doesn't need open space above the spot he
catches <Cite gml="scripts/characterStepEvent.gml:423-440" /> <Cite gml="scripts/characterStepEvent.gml:441-460" />.
Each jump from the hang takes him up the trunk by a block, and he catches hold again. The clip shows it: the
player jumps at a trunk, catches it, and climbs it a block at a jump, to just under its leaves
<Cite clip="tree/climb" />.

### Branches and leaves

Branches and leaves are platforms: the player stands on them, jumps up through them from below, and drops
through them by pressing down <Cite gml="scripts/characterStepEvent.gml:546-575" />. A branch with leaves right
above it is removed as the level is set up <Cite gml="scripts/scrSetupWalls.gml:493-508" />.

### Falling

Each step a tree is in the view, a trunk block with nothing solid under it is destroyed, so breaking the block a
tree stands on, or its lowest trunk block, brings the trunk down, a block or more a step: in the second clip
four trunk blocks go in two steps. A branch with no trunk beside it goes, and so do leaves with nothing to hold
them <Cite gml="objects/oTree/step.gml:1-8" /> <Cite gml="objects/oTreeBranch/step.gml:1-8" />
<Cite gml="objects/oLeaves/step.gml:10-35" />. The second clip shows a [[bomb|Bomb]] at a tree's foot taking the
whole tree <Cite clip="tree/felled" />.

<Clip id="tree/felled" />

A trunk block breaks into rubble, and living leaves and branches into falling
leaves <Cite gml="objects/oTree/destroy.gml:3-8" /> <Cite gml="objects/oLeaves/destroy.gml:1-5" />
<Cite gml="objects/oTreeBranch/destroy.gml:1-4" />.

## Locations

A room template's tree tile grows a tree: a trunk block on the tile and up to five more above it, the first
three always, the fourth unless a 1 in 4 roll stops it and the fifth unless a 2 in 5 one does, growth stopping
under anything solid. The second to the fifth trunk blocks have a 3 in 5 chance of a branch on each side, never
on the same side as the block below's, and the top block gets leaves on both sides
<Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGen2.gml:495-541" />.

<Cite gml="scripts/scrCreateTileObj.gml:89-105" />.

## Interactions

- **No traps in trees.** As a Jungle level is made, the roll that turns a block into a
  [[spear-trap|Spear Trap]], or stands a [[tiki-torch|Tiki Torch]] on it, skips trunk blocks
  <Cite gml="scripts/scrEntityGen.gml:226-252" />.
- **[[climbing-gloves|Climbing Gloves]]** add nothing here: with them, a falling player catches any wall, a
  trunk among them, and rising he still catches a trunk by its own rule
  <Cite gml="scripts/characterStepEvent.gml:400-440" />.

## Tips

- A trunk is a way up without a rope: catch its side and jump, a block at a time.
- A Bomb at a tree's foot clears the whole tree, branches, leaves and all.

## Trivia

- A trunk block is set up with a `burning` flag that nothing reads <Cite gml="objects/oTree/create.gml:4" />.
- The level editor draws its trees with objects of their own, which do nothing but look like a tree; the
  code that would have let them fall is commented out <Cite gml="objects/oLeavesTile/step.gml:1-10" />
  <Cite gml="objects/oTreeBranchTile/step.gml:1-10" />.
