The Moai is a statue of a head, three blocks wide and four tall, standing on a ledge of rock in one room of
an [[ice-caves|Ice Caves]] level. Nothing breaks it. Inside is a sealed chamber with the
[[hedjet|Hedjet]], the crown that opens the way to the [[city-of-gold|City of Gold]], and an exit of its own.
Around it, the level is an ordinary Ice Caves level, and no message announces it.

## Generation

Every Ice Caves level rolls for the Moai until one makes it: level 9 one time in 4, level 10 one time in 3,
level 11 one time in 2, and level 12 always, so every game that reaches level 12 has had exactly one
<Cite gml="scripts/scrLevelGen.gml:212-221" />. The flag that it's made is cleared only when a new game
starts <Cite gml="scripts/scrClearGlobals.gml:77" />. A game started from the Temple's [[tunnel-man-shortcuts|shortcut]], at level 13,
never makes one. The [[mothership]] and the [[yeti-lair|Yeti lair]] are the other branches of the same roll,
so neither can share the Moai's level, and neither comes before it <Cite quirk="1" row="No alien craft or Yeti lair until the Moai is made" />.

Once the level's path is laid, a room of the second or third row, at random, becomes the Moai's, whatever
the path had put there <Cite gml="scripts/scrLevelGen.gml:220" />. It's one of two layouts, each as likely:
the statue right of the room's middle or left of it, on a ledge of rock open on both sides
<Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGen3.gml:153-160" />. The three blocks it stands on can't be destroyed
<Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGen3.gml:502-506" />. The first clip shows a Moai on level 9: the player makes his
way down to its ledge, stops beside it, and heads back left <Cite clip="moai/statue" />.

<Clip id="moai/statue" />

The statue is three solid pieces that can't be destroyed: its left and right sides, each a block wide and
four tall, and its brow between them <Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGen3.gml:487-491" /> <Cite
gml="objects/oMoai/create.gml:3" /> <Cite gml="objects/oMoai2/create.gml:3" /> <Cite
gml="objects/oMoai3/create.gml:3" />. Under the brow, a fourth block, three tall and as strong, fills
the chamber. It holds, from the top, a block of [[thin-ice|thin ice]], then the Moai's own exit with the Hedjet lying on
it, free <Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGen3.gml:492-501" /> <Cite gml="objects/oMoaiInside/create.gml:3" />.

No message announces the Moai: a level starts with one only when it's dark or a special level, and the
Moai's is neither. No Ice Caves level is dark, and the Ice Caves' special levels are the other branches of
the Moai's roll <Cite gml="scripts/scrInitLevel.gml:206-220" /> <Cite gml="scripts/scrInitLevel.gml:169" />.

## Inhabitants

[[level-generation|Level generation]] makes [[ice-caves#inhabitants|the Ice Caves']] monsters on the Moai's level as on any
other: [[yeti|Yetis]] and [[ufo|UFOs]] on the blocks <Cite gml="scripts/scrEntityGen.gml:338-379" />. Nothing
lives inside the statue.

## Items and shops

### The Hedjet

The way in is the [[ankh|Ankh]]. When a player wearing it dies on the Moai's level, the Ankh brings him back
with 4 hearts and, if the Moai is active, puts him inside it: the block filling the chamber is destroyed,
and he appears in its top, on the thin ice <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:1853-1861" />. Things far
outside the view are switched off and the code doesn't see them, so a player who dies far from the Moai
comes back at the level's entrance instead, as on any other level
<Cite gml="objects/oLevel/step.gml:75-94" /> <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:1867-1872" />. The thin ice
melts under him in 30 steps (1 second), and he drops onto the Hedjet <Cite gml="objects/oThinIce/step.gml:1-13" />.
A [[teleporter|Teleporter]] jump can land him inside too <Cite quirk="1" row="The Hedjet (oCrown) is sealed in every Ice Caves Moai" />. In the second clip, the player,
wearing the Ankh, drops a [[bomb|Bomb]] at his feet; the blast takes his last hearts, the Ankh revives him in
the chamber, the ice gives way, and he picks up the Hedjet and takes the Moai's exit
<Cite clip="hedjet/moai" />.

<Clip id="hedjet/moai" />

The Ankh is sold only in the [[black-market|Black Market]], so the player has to carry it there to the
Moai's level without dying ([[hedjet#obtaining|the Hedjet's page]] has the rest, and
[[city-of-gold-route]] walks through the whole way to the City of Gold). The Moai's exit leads to the next
level, as the level's own exit does <Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGen3.gml:496-497" />.

### Shops

Shops, [[kalis-altar|Kali's altar]] and the [[golden-idol|Golden Idol]]'s room come as on
[[ice-caves#items-and-shops|any Ice Caves level]]. A shop only takes a room the path left empty, so never
the Moai's <Cite gml="scripts/scrLevelGen.gml:239-297" />.

## Trivia

- The Moai's exit is made with the `type` "Moai Exit". It matters: a thief or a murderer finds an angry
  [[shopkeeper|Shopkeeper]] waiting at each exit whose `type` is "Exit", so none is ever made in the Moai's
  sealed chamber <Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGen3.gml:496-497" /> <Cite gml="scripts/scrInitLevel.gml:245-257" />
  <Cite quirk="1" row="The Hedjet (oCrown) is sealed in every Ice Caves Moai" />.
- The game's draw code has a commented-out line that would print "TRUE" beside the player once the Moai
  had been made <Cite gml="objects/oGame/draw.gml:168" />.
