A Bomb Bag isn't kept: taking one adds its <Stat id="bomb-bag.bombs" bare /> [[bomb|bombs]] to the count beside
the bomb icon at the top of the screen, and the bag is gone ([[bombs-and-ropes]] has how the count works).
The [[general-store|General Store]], the [[bomb-shop|Bomb Shop]] and the [[weapon-shop|Weapon Shop]] sell it,
and [[crate|Crates]] hold it more often than anything but a [[rope-pile|Rope Pile]].

## Effect

<Clip id="bombs-and-ropes/pickups" />

A free Bomb Bag is taken as soon as the player touches it, unless he's dead or stunned or the bag is inside
a block: his bombs go up by <Stat id="bomb-bag.bombs" bare />, a flashing "+BOMB" rises from where the bag was, and the
message "YOU GOT 3 MORE BOMBS!" shows <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:2020-2034" />. Paying for one, or
carrying one out of its shop, does the same <Cite gml="scripts/scrStealItem.gml:68-79" />. A count above
<Stat id="bomb.max" bare /> is cut back to <Stat id="bomb.max" bare /> at the start of his next step
<Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:10-11" />. In the clip, the player walks over a Bomb Bag, a Rope Pile and
a [[bomb-box|Bomb Box]], and his bombs go from 4 to 7 at the bag <Cite clip="bombs-and-ropes/pickups" />.

<Clip id="bomb-shop/buy" />

One for sale is picked up as any ware is, and stays in the player's hands: the
[[shopkeeper|Shopkeeper]] names it "A BAG OF 3 BOMBS" and its price, and the pay key buys it
<Cite gml="objects/oBombBag/create.gml:6-8" /> <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:1359-1389" />. In the
second clip, the player picks one up in a Bomb Shop on level 2, pays $2,500, and his bombs go from 4 to 7
<Cite clip="bomb-shop/buy" />.

A Crate's item appears where the player stands as he opens it, so a Bomb Bag out of one is taken at once
<Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:612-616" />. Bomb Bags aren't among the items buried in blocks, the
[[dice-house|Dice House]]'s prizes or [[kalis-favour|Kali]]'s gifts
<Cite gml="scripts/scrGenerateItem.gml:47-89" /> <Cite gml="scripts/scrGetFavorMsg.gml:85-105" />. In custom
levels, it's the [[level-editor|level editor]]'s tile `.` <Cite gml="scripts/scrCreateTileObj.gml:297-302" />.

Carrying one out of a shop without paying adds its bombs and angers the Shopkeeper, as with any ware
([[general-store#stealing]]) <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:541-557" />.

## Interactions

- **[[bomb|Bombs]].** An explosion throws a Bomb Bag aside, as it does most items, and knocks one out of
  the player's hands <Cite gml="objects/oExplosion/collision_oItem.gml:30-46" />. The bag's bombs don't go
  off.
- **Being stunned.** A stunned player doesn't take a bag he's lying on until he gets up
  <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:2020" />.

## Tips

- Near the cap, a bag adds what it can: at 97 bombs, one takes the count to 99, and the third bomb is lost.
