It's one of the three small gems a [[chest|Chest]] holds, with the [[emerald|Emerald]] and the
[[ruby|Ruby]]: worth twice an Emerald and as much as a Ruby. Like every gem, it can't be collected until a
moment after it appears; then the player collects it by touching it.

## Value

A Sapphire's base value is <Stat id="sapphire.value" />, and it's worth a quarter of that more for each area
past the Mines, the code counting [[olmecs-lair|Olmec's Lair]] as an area of its own
<Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:1996" /> <Cite gml="scripts/scrInitLevel.gml:24-29" />:

| [[ice-caves\|The Ice Caves]] | <Stat id="sapphire.value-by-area" at="2" /> |

## Locations

Sapphires come only out of [[chest|Chests]]. Opened, a Chest that isn't a trap spills <Stat id="chest.gems" />
small gems, each an [[emerald|Emerald]], a Sapphire or a [[ruby|Ruby]], one chance in three
<Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:580-593" />; one Chest in twelve, outside the [[tutorial|Tutorial]],
holds a lit [[bomb|Bomb]] instead <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:568-579" />. Nothing else in the game
makes one: level generation, the monsters and the [[level-editor|level editor]] make only the big gems
<Cite gml="scripts/scrCreateTileObj.gml:269-284" />.

## Collecting

<Clip id="chest/open" />

A new Sapphire can't be collected: the code all treasure shares starts it uncollectable, and the gem's own
alarm makes it collectable <Stat id="sapphire.collect-delay" />
(<Stat id="sapphire.collect-delay" as="seconds" />) after it appears <Cite gml="objects/oTreasure/create.gml:10" />
<Cite gml="objects/oSapphire/create.gml:7" /> <Cite gml="objects/oSapphire/alarm_0.gml:1" />. Gold out of the rock
has no such wait <Cite gml="objects/oGoldChunk/create.gml:8" />. After that, the player collects it by
touching it while he isn't stunned or dead <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:1990-2016" />.

He takes one treasure a step, the one nearest him, and only if that one can be collected
<Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:1990-1993" />. So a gem fresh out of a Chest that lies nearer him than
another treasure he touches holds the other back too, until its wait is over: the same nearest pick as the
[[nearest-enemy|nearest-enemy hits]] <Cite quirk="1" />.

In the clip, a Chest opens into an [[emerald|Emerald]], a Sapphire and two [[ruby|Rubies]] at the player's
feet. He stands among them, collecting nothing, for 20 steps; then he takes one a step, a Ruby, the Sapphire,
the other Ruby and last the Emerald <Cite clip="chest/open" />.

Its value shows under his money and is counted into it after a short pause; whatever is left is counted at
once when he leaves the level <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:1953-1971" />
<Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/other_animation_end.gml:46-50" />. A gem plays its own sound, where gold plays the
coin's <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:2000-2014" />, and the screen between levels shows each Sapphire
collected on the level <Cite gml="objects/oTransition/alarm_0.gml:35-40" />.

A loose Sapphire falls and comes to rest like other treasure, moving only while it's on the screen; a [[web]]
it falls into holds it, and lava it sinks into destroys it <Cite gml="objects/oTreasure/step.gml:1-79" />
<Cite gml="objects/oWeb/collision_oTreasure.gml:1-2" />.
