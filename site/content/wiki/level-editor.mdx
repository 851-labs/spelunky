A level is a grid of tiles, each a character in a text file in the game's `levels` folder: blocks, [[ladder|ladders]], [[water|water]],
monsters, traps, treasure and items, an entrance and exits, and [[sign|signs]] with messages. The editor places them with
the mouse, gives the level its starting hearts, bombs and ropes and its music, plays it to test it, and saves it. A
custom level plays like a level of the game, and its exits can lead to more custom levels; none of it counts for
the high scores ([[scoring]]) <Cite gml="objects/oLoadLevel/create.gml:5" />.

## How it works

### Opening a level

F2 on the [[title-screen|title screen]] opens the editor, which asks EDIT LEVEL: for a name. Enter opens the saved level of that
name, if there is one, and otherwise starts an empty level <Cite gml="objects/oTitle/keypress_F2.gml:1-3" /> <Cite
gml="objects/oLevelEditor/keypress_anykey.gml:1-124" />. An empty level's author is ANONYMOUS, its music CAVE, its
starting hearts, bombs and ropes 4 each and its next level
NONE <Cite gml="objects/oLevelEditor/create.gml:20-26" />. Escape at the prompt goes back to the
title <Cite gml="objects/oLevelEditor/keypress_escape.gml:12-16" />.

### Placing tiles

<Clip id="level-editor/build" />

The left mouse button places the chosen tile in the square under the cursor, and the right button erases what's
there <Cite gml="objects/oLevelEditor/step.gml:18-73" />. The keys 1 to 4 choose a group (blocks, monsters, traps
or items) and the tile shown at the cursor, and A and S or the mouse wheel step back and forward through it, going
round at its ends <Cite gml="objects/oLevelEditor/keypress_1.gml:1-11" /> <Cite
gml="objects/oLevelEditor/keypress_S.gml:1-7" /> <Cite gml="objects/oLevelEditor/mouse_wheel_up.gml:1-7" />. The
level is bigger than the screen: the view scrolls 4 pixels a step while the cursor is within 16 pixels of its
edge <Cite gml="objects/oLevelEditor/step.gml:4-12" />, and the corner shows the level's name, its author and the
cursor's position <Cite gml="objects/oLevelEditor/draw.gml:72-85" />. In the clip the player builds a floor of
bricks with an entrance and an exit, adds a snake, spikes and gold bars, erases one of them, sets the level's info
and tests it <Cite clip="level-editor/build" />.

The editor's groups start with the Mines' tiles. Paying the [[tunnel-man|Tunnel Man]]'s [[tunnel-man-shortcuts|shortcut]] to level 5 adds
the Jungle's blocks, monsters and traps, the shortcut to level 9 the Ice Caves', and the shortcut to level 13 the
Temple's; every item is there from the start <Cite gml="objects/oLevelEditor/keypress_1.gml:5-8" /> <Cite
gml="objects/oLevelEditor/keypress_2.gml:5-8" /> <Cite gml="objects/oLevelEditor/keypress_3.gml:5-8" /> <Cite
gml="objects/oLevelEditor/keypress_4.gml:5" />. The list is in [Numbers](#numbers). Only the editor checks: a level
plays with whatever tiles it has <Cite gml="scripts/scrLoadLevel.gml:96-133" />.

### Doors and signs

E over an exit or an entrance asks for its LABEL, and over a sign for its MESSAGE, up to 38 characters; Enter sets
it and Escape leaves it as it
was <Cite gml="objects/oLevelEditor/keypress_anykey.gml:125-199" /> <Cite
gml="objects/oLevelEditor/keypress_escape.gml:5-11" />. An exit's label is the level it leads to, in place of the
level's next level <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:881-888" />. An entrance's is the level the player must
come from to start there; coming from anywhere else, he starts at one of the entrances, or in the top left corner
if there's none <Cite gml="scripts/scrLoadLevel.gml:162-183" />.

### The level's info

The EDIT INFO button asks for the LEVEL NAME, the AUTHOR, the MUSIC, the START LIFE, START BOMBS and START ROPE,
and the NEXT LEVEL, each ended with Enter, which doesn't move on from an empty
field <Cite gml="objects/oEditButton/mouse_left_release.gml:1-8" /> <Cite
gml="objects/oLevelEditor/keypress_anykey.gml:201-322" /> <Cite replay="fidelity/replays/editor-build-test-save.txt" />. The music is CAVE, LUSH, ICE, TEMPLE or BOSS, the music
of the Mines, the Jungle, the Ice Caves, the Temple or Olmec's Lair; anything else plays the
Mines' <Cite gml="scripts/startMusic.gml:1-35" />. Played from the menu, the starting hearts are 1 to 99 and the
bombs and ropes 0 to 99, whatever was typed <Cite gml="scripts/scrLoadLevel.gml:138-149" />. The next level is the
one its exits lead to; with no level of that name, leaving ends the chain ([Chains of levels](#chains-of-levels)).

### Testing

The TEST button, or the attack key, saves the level to a file of its own, `test.tmp`, and plays it, with the
starting hearts, bombs and ropes as they were typed <Cite gml="objects/oTestButton/mouse_left_release.gml:1-8" /> <Cite
gml="objects/oLevelEditor/step.gml:13-17" /> <Cite gml="scripts/scrTestLevel.gml:24-95" />. Taking the exit, or
dying and pressing attack (PRESS X TO EDIT LEVEL.), goes back to the editor, which reopens the level from
`test.tmp` <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/other_animation_end.gml:58-62" /> <Cite
gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:922-926" /> <Cite gml="objects/oGame/draw.gml:28-44" /> <Cite
gml="objects/oLevelEditor/create.gml:138-283" />.

### Saving

Escape asks SAVE LEVEL? (Y/N), warning NO ENTRANCE! and NO EXIT! when the level hasn't
one <Cite gml="objects/oLevelEditor/keypress_escape.gml:1-4" /> <Cite gml="objects/oLevelEditor/draw.gml:46-71" />.
Y saves the level as its name, in lower case, with `.lvl`, and goes back to the title; N goes back without saving;
Escape goes back to editing <Cite gml="objects/oLevelEditor/keypress_Y.gml:1-75" /> <Cite
gml="objects/oLevelEditor/keypress_N.gml:1-11" /> <Cite gml="objects/oLevelEditor/keypress_escape.gml:5-11" />. The
NEW button asks the same, then starts again from the name
prompt <Cite gml="objects/oNewButton/mouse_left_release.gml:1-8" />.

The file is text: 32 lines of 40 tile characters, `0` for an empty square, then the author, the music, the starting
hearts, bombs and ropes and the next level, a line each, then how many exits and entrances are named and their
names, in the order they're in the grid, then the same for the signs'
messages <Cite gml="objects/oLevelEditor/keypress_Y.gml:8-62" />. Any text editor can write one.

### Playing custom levels

<Clip id="level-editor/load-level" />

F3 on the title screen opens LOAD LEVEL:, which lists the levels in the `levels` folder, ten to a page, but not
those whose names start with `!` <Cite gml="objects/oTitle/keypress_F3.gml:1-8" /> <Cite
gml="objects/oLoadLevel/create.gml:42-82" /> <Cite gml="objects/oLoadLevel/draw.gml:1-26" />. A click picks a
level, or its name can be typed; Enter or the LOAD button loads
it, and a name with no level shows NO SUCH LEVEL EXISTS! <Cite gml="objects/oLoadLevel/keypress_anykey.gml:1-25" /> <Cite
gml="scripts/scrLoadLevel.gml:152-160" />. Escape goes back to the title. In the clip the player pages through
twelve levels and loads ALPHA <Cite clip="level-editor/load-level" />.

A custom level plays as a level of the game does, with its own music and starting hearts, bombs and ropes. Dying
there and pressing attack goes back to the menu (PRESS X TO LOAD ANOTHER
LEVEL.) <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:927-932" /> <Cite gml="objects/oGame/draw.gml:35-39" />.

### Chains of levels

<Clip id="level-editor/chain" />

Leaving a custom level by its exit shows the screen between levels, NAME COMPLETED!, then loads the exit's level,
or the level's next level if the exit has no label, and the player keeps his hearts, bombs and
ropes <Cite gml="objects/oTransition/draw.gml:75" /> <Cite gml="objects/oTransition/step.gml:19" /> <Cite
gml="objects/oLoadLevel/create.gml:84-87" /> <Cite gml="scripts/scrLoadLevel.gml:24" />. In the clip the player
starts SECOND at the entrance named for the level he left, and its first exit takes him to THIRD in place of
SECOND's own next level <Cite clip="level-editor/chain" />. When the next level doesn't exist, the chain ends in
the custom levels' own ending ([[endings#custom-levels]]) <Cite gml="scripts/scrLoadLevel.gml:154-157" />.
