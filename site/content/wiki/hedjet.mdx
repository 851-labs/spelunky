Wearing it, the player can enter the [[city-of-gold|City of Gold]]: the gold door on level 14 takes the
[[sceptre|Sceptre]] and opens only for him. It also keeps the [[ghost|Ghost]] away and lights up dark levels
around him. It lies in a sealed chamber inside the [[moai|Moai]], a statue in one of the
[[ice-caves|Ice Caves]]' levels, which only a death with the [[ankh|Ankh]] gets into. It's worn from the
moment it's picked up until the game ends.

## Effect

<Clip id="hedjet/gold-door" />

Picking it up puts it on: the message "YOU GOT THE HEDJET! IT GLOWS A BRILLIANT WHITE." shows, the Hedjet
disappears, and an icon of it joins the row under the hearts <Cite gml="scripts/scrStealItem.gml:46-56" />
<Cite gml="scripts/scrDrawHUD.gml:111-115" />. It stays on until a new game starts, which clears it
<Cite gml="scripts/scrClearGlobals.gml:86" /> <Cite gml="objects/oTransition/create.gml:20-24" />.

### The gold door

Level 14, the [[temple|Temple]]'s second, has a gold door, on top of a block that can't be destroyed <Cite
gml="scripts/scrEntityGen.gml:388" /> <Cite gml="scripts/scrEntityGen.gml:400-408" /> <Cite
gml="scripts/scrEntityGen.gml:482-494" />. Carrying the Sceptre, which the [[tomb-lord|Tomb Lord]] of
level 13 drops, into it opens it if the player wears the Hedjet: the door takes the Sceptre and turns into a
way through to the City of Gold, the next level. Without the Hedjet, it says "THE SCEPTRE FITS... BUT NOTHING
IS HAPPENING!" and stays shut <Cite gml="objects/oGoldDoor/collision_oSceptre.gml:1-23" />
<Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:880" />. In the clip, the player, wearing the Hedjet, carries the
Sceptre to the door, which opens, and goes through to the City of Gold <Cite clip="hedjet/gold-door" />.

### The Ghost

A player who stays on a level too long is warned "A CHILL RUNS UP YOUR SPINE..." and then sent the Ghost.
Wearing the Hedjet, neither happens, however long he stays <Cite gml="objects/oGame/step.gml:23-47" />.
The other things that bring a Ghost, taking a graveyard's [[crystal-skull|Crystal Skull]] and defiling one of Kali's altars too often,
still do <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:1234-1239" /> <Cite gml="objects/oSacAltarLeft/destroy.gml:53-54" />.

### Dark levels

On a dark level, the light around the player has a radius of 96 pixels minus 64 times the level's darkness
<Cite gml="objects/oScreen/step_begin.gml:150-178" />. Wearing the Hedjet, the darkness is 0, so the light reaches 96
pixels, as far as a flare's, and at full brightness, instead of about 38 pixels of dim light at the
darkest <Cite gml="objects/oLevel/step.gml:110-137" />. The level's opening message changes too: "THE HEDJET SHINES
BRIGHTLY." instead of "I CAN'T SEE A THING! I'D BETTER USE THESE FLARES!"
<Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/alarm_0.gml:5-13" />.

## Obtaining

<Clip id="hedjet/moai" />

| Source | Chance | |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Inside the [[moai\|Moai]] | One in every Moai | <Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGen3.gml:487-501" /> |

The Moai comes once a game, on one of the Ice Caves' levels: level 9 one time in 4, level 10 one time in 3,
level 11 one time in 2, and otherwise level 12 <Cite gml="scripts/scrLevelGen.gml:212-220" />. It's a
statue in one of two rooms that hold it <Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGen3.gml:153-160" />. Inside it, its own
exit and the Hedjet, free to take, sit in a chamber filled by a solid block that can't be destroyed
<Cite gml="objects/oMoaiInside/create.gml:3" />, so it can't be dug or bombed into. The [[ankh|Ankh]] is the
way in, though a [[teleporter|Teleporter]] jump can land the player inside too <Cite quirk="1" />. When the
player dies wearing it on a level with the Moai, while the Moai is active (near the view: things far outside
it are switched off <Cite gml="objects/oLevel/step.gml:75-94" />), it revives him inside the Moai, removing
the block, and he falls onto the Hedjet <Cite quirk="1" /> <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:1853-1861" />. The Moai's exit takes him on to the next level. In the
clip, the player, wearing the Ankh, drops a [[bomb|Bomb]] at his feet; the blast takes his last hearts, the
Ankh revives him inside the Moai, and he picks up the Hedjet <Cite clip="hedjet/moai" />.

The Ankh is sold only in the [[black-market|Black Market]], levels 6 to 8, so the player has to carry it to
the Moai's level without dying on the way. Nothing else makes a Hedjet, and the
[[level-editor|level editor]] has no tile for it.

## Interactions

- The Hedjet doesn't light the whole of a dark level: it widens the light around the player only, and flares
  and lamps light their own circles as before <Cite gml="objects/oScreen/step_begin.gml:178-194" />.
- With the [[sceptre|Sceptre]], it's the City of Gold's key; either alone does nothing at the gold door
  <Cite gml="objects/oGoldDoor/collision_oSceptre.gml:1-23" />.

## Tips

- Wearing the Hedjet, there's no need to hurry: the Ghost that the level's clock sends never comes.
