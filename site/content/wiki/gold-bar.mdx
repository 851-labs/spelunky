Unlike gold in the rock, a Gold Bar lies in the open, where level generation puts it, so the player collects
it by walking over it. It's worth as much as a [[gold-nugget|Gold Nugget]], and half a stack of
[[gold-bars|Gold Bars]].

## Value

A bar's base value is <Stat id="gold-bar.value" />, and it's worth a quarter of that more for each area past
the Mines <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:1996" />:

| Area | Value |
| --- | --- |
| [[mines\|The Mines]] | <Stat id="gold-bar.value-by-area" at="0" /> |
| [[jungle\|The Jungle]] | <Stat id="gold-bar.value-by-area" at="1" /> |
| [[ice-caves\|The Ice Caves]] | <Stat id="gold-bar.value-by-area" at="2" /> |
| [[temple\|The Temple]] | <Stat id="gold-bar.value-by-area" at="3" /> |

## Locations

Level generation, in every area, gives each block outside a shop with open space above it, away from the
entrance, the exit and a [[golden-idol|Golden Idol]], a roll for what lies on it, once its rolls for a
[[rock|Rock]] (1 in 100) and a [[jar|Jar]] (1 in 40) fail <Cite gml="scripts/scrTreasureGen.gml:27-46" />. A
Gold Bar's chance depends on the spot:

| Spot | Chance | |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A nook one block high with a wall beside it | 1 in 3, once the rolls before it, for a web, the Udjat Eye's locked chest, a [[crate\|Crate]]'s 1 in 10, a [[chest\|Chest]]'s 1 in 15, a Damsel and bones, fail | <Cite gml="scripts/scrTreasureGen.gml:48-89" /> |
| A gap one block wide between two walls | 1 in 4, once the roll for a web fails | <Cite gml="scripts/scrTreasureGen.gml:95-101" /> |
| Open floor | 1 in 40, the first roll | <Cite gml="scripts/scrTreasureGen.gml:116-122" /> |

On a level with the Udjat Eye's locked chest, level generation then turns one treasure lying in the open,
picked at random, into the chest's key <Cite gml="scripts/scrEntityGen.gml:108-141" />. The
[[tutorial|Tutorial]] has six <Cite room="rTutorial" />, and the [[level-editor|level editor]]'s `$` tile is
one <Cite gml="scripts/scrCreateTileObj.gml:253-256" />.

## Collecting

<Clip id="gold-bar/tutorial" />

The player collects a bar by touching it while he isn't stunned or dead: it's collectable from the start, and
he takes the treasure nearest him, one a step <Cite gml="objects/oGoldBar/create.gml:6-7" />
<Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:1990-2016" />. Its value shows under his money and is counted into it
after a short pause <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:1953-1971" />; gold plays the coin sound
<Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:2000-2014" />, and the screen between levels shows each bar collected on
the level <Cite gml="objects/oTransition/alarm_0.gml:53-58" />.

In the clip, the Tutorial tells the player to collect the treasure, and he runs along a ledge picking up its
six Gold Bars, then a stack of [[gold-bars|Gold Bars]] <Cite clip="gold-bar/tutorial" />.

A bar falls and comes to rest like other treasure, moving only while it's on the screen; one that sinks into
lava is destroyed <Cite gml="objects/oTreasure/step.gml:1-79" />.
