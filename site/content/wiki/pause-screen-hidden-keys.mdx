The pause screen lists three keys at the bottom: ESC-RETURN, F1-DIE and F10-QUIT. With a gamepad on, it lists
the buttons instead: START-RETURN, BOMB-DIE and ROPE-QUIT. But the keyboard's bomb and rope keys work too,
whichever is shown, so a player who presses the bomb key while paused dies, and one who presses the rope key
quits the game.

## What happens

The player pauses the game on the first level. The screen shows ESC-RETURN, F1-DIE and F10-QUIT, but he presses
the bomb key, A, and that kills him: the game resumes with him flung into the air, dead, and the game over screen
follows <Cite clip="quirks/pause-bomb-key-dies" />.

<Clip id="quirks/pause-bomb-key-dies" />

## Why

The pause screen's code checks for the bomb and rope buttons with `checkBombPressed` and `checkRopePressed`:

```gml objects/oScreen/step_begin.gml:5-8
if (checkBombPressed())
{
    if (paused and global.plife > 0 and isLevel())
    {
```

Those are the same checks the player's own step uses to throw a bomb or a rope, so they take the gamepad's
button or the keyboard's key, whichever is pressed <Cite gml="scripts/checkBombPressed.gml:1-4" />
<Cite gml="scripts/checkRopePressed.gml:1-4" />. The bomb's branch then kills the player as F1 does, and the
rope's, with the game paused anywhere, ends the game <Cite gml="objects/oScreen/step_begin.gml:9-33" />
<Cite quirk="1" />. The two deaths differ only in the music: F1 sets it to full volume and stops it
<Cite gml="objects/oScreen/keypress_F1.gml:13-18" />, while the bomb's branch sets it back to the settings' music
volume and stops it only if the player has no [[ankh|Ankh]].

## Reproducing it

Pause in a level and press the bomb key, or pause anywhere and press the rope key. The replay of the clip pauses
on the first level (frame 1250) and presses A: the player dies (1280), and the game over screen follows
<Cite replay="fidelity/replays/flow-pause-game-over-new-game.txt" />. Another replay quits from the pause screen
with S <Cite replay="fidelity/replays/ending-seed-1.txt" />.

## In the port

The spelunky.ai port reproduces it exactly: it's in docs/quirks.md's list of the 1.1 source's bugs
<Cite quirk="1" />, and the oracle's fingerprints of the replays pin both keys. No option turns it off.
