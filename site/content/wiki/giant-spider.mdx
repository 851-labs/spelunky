A Giant Spider is two blocks wide, and at most one is made on a level, only in [[mines|the Mines]]. It
hangs from a ceiling above two webs until the player comes close or hurts it, then leaps at him like a
[[spider|Spider]], twice as hard. It has <Stat id="giant-spider.hp" /> and ignores a hit that comes too soon after
another, but a single [[bomb|Bomb]] kills it, and it leaves treasure: big gems and a [[paste|Paste]].

## Behaviour

<Clip id="giant-spider/drop" />

A Giant Spider starts out hanging under a block, with two webs just below it
<Cite gml="objects/oGiantSpiderHang/create.gml:26-27" />. It drops when it's hurt at all, when the block it
hangs from is gone, or when the player is below it, within 8 pixels of its middle across and within 90
pixels of it <Cite gml="objects/oGiantSpiderHang/step.gml:35-40" />. The clip shows one dropping as the
player walks beneath it, leaping onto him and taking two of his four hearts.

Once down, it moves as a Spider does, by leaping. It waits 5–20 steps (0.17–0.67 seconds), then jumps up
at 2–5 pixels per step and across at <Stat id="giant-spider.jump-speed" />, toward the player
<Cite gml="objects/oGiantSpider/step.gml:66-72" /> <Cite gml="objects/oGiantSpider/alarm_0.gml:1-18" />.
While he's within <Stat id="giant-spider.jump-range" />, it jumps at him again each time it lands, higher,
at <Stat id="giant-spider.jump-height" />, with a 1 in 4 chance each time of stopping to wait instead
<Cite gml="objects/oGiantSpider/step.gml:93-111" />. Farther away, it makes a single leap toward him after
each wait <Cite gml="objects/oGiantSpider/step.gml:113-117" />. In a tunnel too low for it to stand, where
it touches the ceiling and the floor at once, it crawls toward the player at 1 pixel per step, turning at
walls, until it has room again <Cite gml="objects/oGiantSpider/step.gml:53-60" />
<Cite gml="objects/oGiantSpider/step.gml:73-79" />.

<Clip id="giant-spider/web" />

It also spits webs. A timer of <Stat id="giant-spider.web-timer" />, counted down every step it's on screen,
lets it spit once it runs out and the Giant Spider is between leaps; then it starts again
<Cite gml="objects/oGiantSpider/step.gml:64-88" />. The ball of web flies 1–3 pixels per step to the left or
the right at random, not at the player, in an arc that starts upward
<Cite gml="objects/oWebBall/create.gml:3-6" /> <Cite gml="objects/oWebBall/step.gml:1-4" />. When it hits a
block, an item, water, another web or a monster other than a Giant Spider, or after 20–100 steps, it turns
into a web where it is <Cite gml="objects/oWebBall/step.gml:7-11" />
<Cite gml="objects/oWebBall/collision_oSolid.gml:1-3" /> <Cite gml="objects/oWebBall/other_animation_end.gml:1-6" />.
A web holds items and most monsters still and slows the player down <Cite
gml="objects/oWeb/collision_oItem.gml:1-5" /> <Cite gml="objects/oWeb/collision_oEnemy.gml:1-5" /> <Cite
gml="scripts/characterStepEvent.gml:743-748" />; one made this way fades out over about 550 steps (18
seconds) <Cite gml="objects/oWeb/step.gml:1-3" />. The clip shows a web ball arcing away from the player on
his ledge and landing on the floor as a web.

A Giant Spider only acts while it's on screen <Cite gml="objects/oGiantSpider/step.gml:1-3" />.

## Damage

When the player touches a dropped Giant Spider without stomping it, he loses
<Stat id="giant-spider.damage" />, is knocked away from it (and upward, if he's above it), and blinks,
invincible, for 30 steps (1 second) <Cite gml="objects/oGiantSpider/collision_oCharacter.gml:15-34" />. While
it still hangs, touching it costs 1 heart instead, through the touch code most monsters share
<Cite gml="objects/oEnemy/collision_oCharacter.gml:14-25" />.

## Defeating

A Giant Spider has <Stat id="giant-spider.hp" /> <Cite gml="objects/oGiantSpiderHang/create.gml:11" />, and
for <Stat id="giant-spider.whip-cooldown" /> after each hit from a whip or a thrown item, it ignores the
next one; it ignores them for its first 10 steps after dropping too
<Cite gml="objects/oGiantSpider/create.gml:16" /> <Cite gml="objects/oGiantSpider/step.gml:9" />:

| Way | Damage | |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [[whip\|Whip]], [[machete\|Machete]] or [[mattock\|Mattock]] | 1, at most once every <Stat id="giant-spider.whip-cooldown" /> | <Cite gml="objects/oGiantSpider/collision_oWhip.gml:1-7" /> |
| The wind-up behind the player, of the Whip, the Machete or the Mattock | <Stat id="whip.wind-up-damage" /> a step (the Machete's 2; the Mattock makes two, 4 a step where they overlap), with no wait | <Cite gml="objects/oEnemy/collision_oWhipPre.gml:1-8" /> <Cite clip="giant-spider/wind-up" /> |
| [[stomping\|Stomp]] (landing on it) | 1, plus 1 for every 16 steps of falling; three times that with [[spike-shoes\|Spike Shoes]] | <Cite gml="objects/oGiantSpider/collision_oCharacter.gml:6-14" /> |
| A thrown item moving faster than 2 pixels per step, such as a [[rock\|Rock]] | 1, at most once every <Stat id="giant-spider.whip-cooldown" /> | <Cite gml="objects/oItem/step.gml:300-309" /> |
| A [[shotgun\|Shotgun]] or [[pistol\|Pistol]] bullet | <Stat id="pistol.damage" /> | <Cite gml="objects/oBullet/collision_oEnemy.gml:16" /> |
| A [[bomb\|Bomb]]'s explosion | <Stat id="bomb.damage" /> | <Cite gml="objects/oExplosion/collision_oEnemy.gml:14" /> |
| Ending up inside solid ground | Kills it | <Cite gml="objects/oGiantSpider/step.gml:11-14" /> |

Its wait is in its own code for the whip, which a swing's wind-up behind the player doesn't run: having no
code of its own for the wind-up, it takes the code most monsters share, which has no wait. With the player's
back to it, a swing of the Whip takes 1 on each of the wind-up's 3 steps, inside the wait
<Cite clip="giant-spider/wind-up" />. Still hanging, it takes a whip's, a Machete's or a Mattock's full
damage, through the code most monsters share, and drops <Cite gml="objects/oEnemy/collision_oWhip.gml:1" />. Its step event doesn't run the one most monsters
share, so [[spikes|Spikes]], [[lava|Lava]], [[spear-trap|Spear Traps]] and water don't affect it
<Cite gml="objects/oEnemy/step.gml:34-128" />.

When it dies, hanging or not, it leaves <Stat id="giant-spider.gem-drops" /> big gems, each a
[[big-emerald|Big Emerald]], [[big-sapphire|Big Sapphire]] or [[big-ruby|Big Ruby]] at random, and a
[[paste|Paste]] that costs nothing <Cite gml="objects/oGiantSpider/step.gml:16-40" />
<Cite gml="objects/oGiantSpiderHang/step.gml:9-34" />. The clip shows a Bomb placed beside a hanging Giant
Spider killing it; the Paste and the Big Sapphire it leaves stick in its web
<Cite gml="objects/oWeb/collision_oItem.gml:1-5" /> <Cite gml="objects/oWeb/collision_oTreasure.gml:1-2" />. Giant Spiders
killed count toward the player's kills, and the screen between levels draws a Giant Spider icon for each
<Cite gml="objects/oTransition/alarm_0.gml:233-237" />.

## Locations

Level generation makes Giant Spiders only in [[mines|the Mines]]. Each Mines level has a 1 in 6 chance of
being allowed one <Cite gml="scripts/scrEntityGen.gml:32-34" />. On such a level, each block with open
space two blocks wide and two deep below it (with no water there and no monster right under it), outside the
starting room and shops and above the level's bottom 64 pixels, has a 1 in 40 chance of a Giant Spider
hanging from it, rolled before anything else that hangs there, until one is made
<Cite gml="scripts/scrEntityGen.gml:45-60" />.

In custom levels, it's the [[level-editor|level editor]]'s tile `S`
<Cite gml="scripts/scrCreateTileObj.gml:139-142" />.

## Tips

- A Giant Spider drops as soon as anything hurts it, so a whip hit or a thrown item brings it down.
- Whipping it faster than once every <Stat id="giant-spider.whip-cooldown" /> does nothing; a Bomb does
  three times its health in one explosion.
- Its webs aren't aimed: a ball goes left or right at random.
