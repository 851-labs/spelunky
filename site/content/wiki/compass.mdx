Wearing it, the player sees which way the [[door|exit]] is: while the exit is off the screen, below it or to one
side, a red arrow at that edge of the screen points toward it. [[general-store|General Stores]],
[[rare-shop|Rare Shops]] and the [[dice-house|Dice House]] offer it, and [[crate|Crates]], blocks and [[kali|Kali]]
give it away. It's worn from the moment
it's picked up or paid for until the game ends.

## Effect

<Clip id="compass/arrow" />

Picking up a free compass, paying for one or carrying one out of a shop puts it on: the message "YOU GOT A
COMPASS!" shows, the compass disappears, and an icon of it joins the row under the hearts
<Cite gml="scripts/scrStealItem.gml:160-170" /> <Cite gml="scripts/scrDrawHUD.gml:160-164" />. It stays on
until a new game starts, which clears it <Cite gml="scripts/scrClearGlobals.gml:90" />
<Cite gml="objects/oTransition/create.gml:20-24" />.

[[level-generation|Level generation]] notes where it puts the exit <Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGen.gml:353-354" />, and with the
Compass, the screen's overlay compares that with the view each frame
<Cite gml="scripts/scrDrawHUD.gml:46-76" />:

| Where the exit is | The arrow |
| --- | --- |
| Below the screen and left of it | In the bottom left corner, pointing down and left |
| Below the screen and right of it (or under its rightmost 16 pixels) | In the bottom right corner, pointing down and right |
| Below the screen, within its width | On the bottom edge, under the exit, pointing down |
| Left of the screen | On the left edge, level with the exit, pointing left |
| Right of the screen, or in its rightmost 16 pixels | On the right edge, level with the exit, pointing right |
| Above the screen | None; to one side, the arrow is drawn level with the exit, off the top of the screen |
| On the screen, but for its rightmost 16 pixels | None |

While a message shows at the bottom of the screen, the arrow is drawn smaller
<Cite gml="scripts/scrDrawHUD.gml:52-53" />. In [[olmecs-lair|Olmec's Lair]], the overlay sets the exit's
place itself, 640 pixels across and 544 down <Cite gml="scripts/scrDrawHUD.gml:44" />. The lair goes deeper
than that, so near its bottom left the exit is above the screen and to its right, and the arrow is drawn off
the top of the screen, all but a sliver ([[compass-arrow-off-screen]])
<Cite quirk="1" row="The Compass's arrow for an exit above the screen is off it." /> <Cite replay="fidelity/replays/item-seed-1-compass-olmec-lair.txt" /> <Cite clip="quirks/compass-arrow-off-screen" />. The clip at the top of this section shows a compass carried out of a
[[general-store|General Store]] without paying: as it goes on, an arrow appears on the bottom edge of the
screen, pointing down to the exit, and stays as the player runs <Cite clip="compass/arrow" />.

## Tips

- An arrow in a bottom corner means the exit is both lower and farther across; one on the bottom edge, that
  it's straight below.
- No arrow means the exit is on the screen, or above it.
- The arrow shows the way, not a path: it points to the exit whatever lies between.
