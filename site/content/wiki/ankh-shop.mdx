Its Shopkeeper greets the player with "I HAVE SOMETHING SPECIAL..." and nothing more
<Cite gml="objects/oShopkeeper/step.gml:133" /> <Cite gml="objects/oShopkeeper/step.gml:145" />. The
shop is a ledge at the top of one of the [[black-market|Black Market]]'s rooms, with the [[ankh|Ankh]] on
it beside the [[shopkeeper|Shopkeeper]] and a lamp, and no sign; it's open only above, where two rooms
drop to it <Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGenMarket.gml:112-116" /> <Cite quirk="1" />. The source names it
the "Ankh" shop <Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGenMarket.gml:115" />.

## Stock

<Clip id="ankh-shop/visit" />

One ware: the Ankh, which the market's room code puts on its ledge itself rather than rolling it as other
shops' wares are, so every Ankh Shop has one <Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGenMarket.gml:361-364" />. Nothing
else in the game makes an Ankh ([[ankh#obtaining|the Ankh's page]] has its sources). In the clip, the
player drops into the shop, the Shopkeeper greets him, and he picks the Ankh up: "AN ANKH FOR $50000.
PRESS P TO PURCHASE." <Cite clip="ankh-shop/visit" />

## Prices

The Ankh costs <Stat id="ankh.price" />, the price it's made with <Cite gml="objects/oAnkh/create.gml:7-8" />.
Other wares cost 10% of their base price more for each level past the second, the market's included, but
that's done by the code that rolls them, which the Ankh skips, so its price is the same on levels 6, 7 and
8, wherever the market is <Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGenMarket.gml:357-364" />.

## Shopkeeper

He sells the Ankh as any Shopkeeper sells a ware ([[general-store#shopkeeper|the General Store]] has the
rules): he names its price while it's held, and the [[controls|pay key]] with <Stat id="ankh.price" /> in hand
buys it, which puts it on ("YOU GOT THE ANKH! YOU FEEL PROTECTED.") <Cite gml="objects/oShopkeeper/step.gml:72-85" />
<Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:1359-1389" /> <Cite gml="scripts/scrStealItem.gml:35-45" />. What
angers him angers every Shopkeeper, and the market has seven close together: a bomb lit in one shop angers
them all ([[black-market#hazards|the Black Market]]) <Cite quirk="1" />.

## Stealing

<Clip id="ankh/revive" />

Carried out of the shop's room unpaid, the Ankh goes on at once and the Shopkeeper attacks ("COME BACK
HERE, THIEF!"); his shots can take the player's last heart, which the Ankh then gives back
<Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:541-557" /> <Cite gml="scripts/scrStealItem.gml:35-45" />. In the
clip, the player jumps up out of the shop with it, drops back in, and is shot until his hearts run out:
the Ankh shatters and he's back at the level's entrance <Cite clip="ankh/revive" />.

<Clip id="quirks/ankh-thrown-out-of-shop" />

The Ankh is never marked for sale, so the check that angers the Shopkeeper when a ware itself leaves the
shop passes it by. Thrown up out of the shop, it lies outside with its price and nobody minds, until the
player picks it up there: then it's his, and the Shopkeeper attacks ([[ankh-throw-out]]) <Cite
gml="objects/oItem/step.gml:17-34" /> <Cite quirk="1" /> <Cite replay="fidelity/replays/item-seed-9-ankh-thrown-out.txt" />.
The theft marks the player a thief for
the next level, as any does ([[general-store#stealing|the General Store]] has the rules), though no shop
comes after the Black Market <Cite quirk="1" />.

## Locations

Only in the [[black-market|Black Market]], one in every market: the room in its right-hand column, third
from the top <Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGenMarket.gml:112-116" /> <Cite gml="scripts/scrLevelGen.gml:63-65" />.
The market is once a game, on level 6, 7 or 8 <Cite gml="scripts/scrLevelGen.gml:44-46" />.

## Trivia
