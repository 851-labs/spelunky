The mothership lies across a row of rooms, from one of the level's first three columns to its right edge: a
hull of grey metal plates over a metal floor, closed all round. In its last room, at the level's edge, sits
the [[alien-boss|Alien Boss]], behind a crackling pink barrier, with a free [[jetpack|Jetpack]] beside it. The
level starts with the message "THERE'S A PSYCHIC PRESENCE HERE!" The code calls it the alien craft
<Cite gml="scripts/scrLevelGen.gml:222" />.

## Generation

On an [[ice-caves|Ice Caves]] level after the [[moai|Moai]]'s, the mothership comes one time in 10
<Cite gml="scripts/scrLevelGen.gml:222-233" /> <Cite gml="scripts/scrClearGlobals.gml:59" />. Its roll is the
other branch of the Moai's, which every Ice Caves level takes until the Moai is made, so a level before the
Moai's, or the Moai's own, never has it, and the mothership only comes on levels 10, 11 and 12
<Cite quirk="1" row="No alien craft or Yeti lair until the Moai is made" />. A level with it can't also be a
[[yeti-lair|Yeti lair]], whose roll is only made when this one fails
<Cite gml="scripts/scrLevelGen.gml:234-237" />. It starts with "THERE'S A PSYCHIC PRESENCE HERE!"
<Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/alarm_0.gml:46-51" /> <Cite gml="scripts/scrInitLevel.gml:206-220" />.

<Clip id="mothership/psychic-presence" />

In the clip, the mothership's level loads with its message. The craft lies under the player's ledge, and
the Alien Boss, in its last room, sends a psychic wave up through the hull, which knocks the player down and
takes a heart <Cite clip="mothership/psychic-presence" />.

Once the level's path is laid, the craft takes a row of rooms, the second or third, chosen at random, from a
random room that isn't the last in the row to the right edge, whatever the path had put there. So it's two,
three or four rooms long <Cite gml="scripts/scrLevelGen.gml:224-231" />:

| Room | What's in it | |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The first | The craft's nose and front: its front plates, and blocks of ice that close it above them | <Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGen3.gml:161-167" /> |
| Each between | Hull above, floor below, nothing between | <Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGen3.gml:168-174" /> |
| The last, at the right edge | The [[alien-boss\|Alien Boss]]; the barrier's emitter in the hull in front of it; behind it, a free [[jetpack\|Jetpack]] in a block of rock; rock along the room's right side | <Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGen3.gml:175-181" /> |

The hull and the floor are blocks, which a bomb breaks as it does the rock, into dark rubble <Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGen3.gml:408-418" /> <Cite gml="objects/oAlienShip/destroy.gml:1-5" />
<Cite gml="objects/oExplosion/collision_oSolid.gml:1-15" />. Like [[ice-caves#trivia|the rock's]], it comes out off-centre, to the
right of the block and below it ([[dark-rubble-off-centre]]): the line that places it draws a number from -8 to 0 where it means 0 to 8 <Cite quirk="1" row="Dark rock's rubble lands off-centre." />
<Cite clip="quirks/dark-rubble-off-centre" /> <Cite replay="fidelity/replays/ice-seed-63-barrier-through.txt" />. And its big piece
falls straight through the hull, rock and ice below, as the rock's does ([[dark-rubble-falls-through]]), since it stops only on a brick or a stone block <Cite gml="objects/oRubbleDark/step.gml:1-8" />
<Cite quirk="1" row="The big piece of dark rubble falls through the rock." /> <Cite clip="quirks/dark-rubble-falls-through" /> <Cite replay="fidelity/replays/ice-seed-63-barrier-through.txt" />. Seven of the last room's ten floor blocks are
each, one time in 2, a block of the area's rock or ice instead <Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGen3.gml:419-432" />. The windows and
the machinery inside are drawn behind, and stop nothing <Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGen3.gml:451-460" />.

## Inhabitants

| Monster | Where | Chance | |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [[alien-boss\|Alien Boss]] | The craft's last room | One in every mothership | <Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGen3.gml:461-464" /> |

The Alien Boss doesn't move on its own, and sends psychic waves after a player who comes near, which pass through walls
([[alien-boss#behaviour|its page]] has how it fights). When it dies, the barrier comes down
<Cite gml="objects/oAlienBoss/other_animation_end.gml:1-5" />.

[[level-generation|Level generation]] goes over the craft's blocks as over the rock, so [[ice-caves#inhabitants|the Ice Caves']]
[[yeti|Yetis]] can stand on its floor and its hull, and [[ufo|UFOs]] hover over it
<Cite gml="scripts/scrEntityGen.gml:338-379" />.

## Hazards

- **The barrier.** The emitter in the last room's hull makes one barrier, a beam three blocks tall down to
  the floor, between the front of the room and the Alien Boss
  <Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGen3.gml:479-482" /> <Cite gml="objects/oBarrierEmitter/create.gml:1-5" />. A
  player who touches it loses a heart, and is thrown back away from it and made invincible for 30 steps (1
  second) <Cite gml="objects/oBarrier/collision_oCharacter.gml:1-13" />. Items that touch it are thrown to
  the left whichever side they come from, and out of the player's hands; a [[bomb|Bomb]] is lit, set to go off
  in 4 to 8 steps <Cite gml="objects/oBarrier/collision_oItem.gml:1-17" />. So an item that reaches it from the
  Alien Boss's side goes on through it (below, and [[mothership-barrier-throws-left]]) <Cite quirk="1" row="The mothership's barrier throws every item left." />. Bullets and the [[web-cannon|Web Cannon]]'s webs
  stop at it <Cite gml="objects/oBarrier/collision_oBullet.gml:1-6" />
  <Cite gml="objects/oBarrier/collision_oWebBall.gml:1-4" />.
- **Bringing the barrier down.** Destroying the emitter destroys the barrier
  <Cite gml="objects/oBarrierEmitter/destroy.gml:1-11" />. An explosion destroys it, as do a bullet and an
  item that hits it moving faster than 2 pixels a step <Cite gml="objects/oExplosion/collision_oBarrierEmitter.gml:1" />
  <Cite gml="objects/oBarrierEmitter/collision_oBullet.gml:1" /> <Cite gml="objects/oBarrierEmitter/collision_oItem.gml:1-4" />.
  It also breaks once it's on screen with the block above it gone, and when the Alien Boss dies
  <Cite gml="objects/oBarrierEmitter/step.gml:1-5" /> <Cite gml="objects/oAlienBoss/other_animation_end.gml:1-5" />.
- **The Ice Caves' own**: the drop below the level, ice, and [[spring-trap|Spring Traps]]
  ([[ice-caves#hazards|the Ice Caves' hazards]]).

## Items and shops

The Jetpack in the last room is free: it's made with no price and not for sale, set in a block of rock
behind the Alien Boss <Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGen3.gml:472-478" />. The Alien Boss leaves <Stat
id="alien-boss.gem-drops" /> big gems when it dies, each a [[big-emerald|Big Emerald]], a
[[big-sapphire|Big Sapphire]] or a [[big-ruby|Big Ruby]] <Cite gml="objects/oAlienBoss/step.gml:9-25" />.

The rest of the level is as [[ice-caves#items-and-shops|any Ice Caves level]]: its shops, its treasure, and
[[kalis-altar|Kali's altar]] and the [[golden-idol|Golden Idol]]'s room in its side rooms. A shop only takes
a room the path left empty, and the craft's rooms are its own by then, so a shop is never in the craft
<Cite gml="scripts/scrLevelGen.gml:239-297" />.
