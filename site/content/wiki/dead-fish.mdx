On a [[restless-dead|graveyard level]] of [[jungle|the Jungle]], the water holds Dead Fish where other
levels have [[piranha|Piranhas]]. A Dead Fish leaves a player on dry land alone, but once he's swimming near
it, it swims straight at him and bites. Its code is a copy of the Piranha's, less its hunting of other
monsters and its bubbles, plus a check for being on screen. With
<Stat id="dead-fish.hp" />, it dies to any damage, and falls apart into three bones.

## Behaviour

A Dead Fish swims back and forth at 1 pixel per step, turning around where the water ends or a wall is in
its way <Cite gml="objects/oDeadFish/step.gml:21-35" />. It always starts out heading right
<Cite gml="objects/oDeadFish/create.gml:11" />.

<Clip id="dead-fish/bite" />

When the player is swimming within <Stat id="dead-fish.attack-range" /> of it, it attacks: it swims straight
at him at <Stat id="dead-fish.attack-speed" /> (<Stat id="dead-fish.attack-speed" as="per-second" />), its
heading wobbling by a degree each step, and moves only while the way ahead is water
<Cite gml="objects/oDeadFish/step.gml:36-39" /> <Cite gml="objects/oDeadFish/step.gml:50-57" />. The game
moves it in whole pixels on each axis, so on a diagonal it covers 1 pixel across and 1 up or down in a step
<Cite gml="scripts/moveTo.gml:20-31" />. When he leaves the water or its range, it stops for 20–40 steps
(0.67–1.33 seconds) and then patrols again, heading left or right at random
<Cite gml="objects/oDeadFish/step.gml:41-49" /> <Cite gml="objects/oDeadFish/step.gml:58-62" />. The clip
shows a Dead Fish swimming at the player in a graveyard level's pool, biting him, and the Whip breaking it
up.

Unlike a Piranha, a Dead Fish doesn't hunt other monsters in the water, and lets off no bubbles
<Cite gml="objects/oPiranha/step.gml:37-51" /> <Cite gml="objects/oPiranha/step.gml:123-128" />.

**Out of the water.** A Dead Fish whose middle isn't in water turns into a [[fish-bone|Fish Bone]] at once
<Cite gml="objects/oDeadFish/step.gml:70-74" />.

**Off screen.** A Dead Fish does nothing at all unless it's on screen, or less than 16 pixels off its left
or top edge: it doesn't move, and even one killed or out of the water there only falls apart or turns into
a Fish Bone once it's back on screen <Cite gml="objects/oDeadFish/step.gml:1-3" />. A Piranha has no such
check, and swims on out of sight.

## Damage

When the player touches a Dead Fish without stomping it, he loses <Stat id="dead-fish.damage" />, is knocked
away from it, and blinks, invincible, for 30 steps (1 second)
<Cite gml="objects/oEnemy/collision_oCharacter.gml:14-25" />. This is the touch code most monsters share.
Unlike a Piranha's, its bite draws no blood <Cite gml="objects/oEnemy/collision_oCharacter.gml:55" />
<Cite gml="objects/oDeadFish/create.gml:27" />. The bite clip shows a bite taking the player from four
hearts to three.

## Defeating

A Dead Fish has <Stat id="dead-fish.hp" />, so any damage kills it <Cite gml="objects/oDeadFish/step.gml:4-17" />:

| Way | Damage | |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [[whip\|Whip]] | <Stat id="whip.damage" /> | <Cite gml="objects/oEnemy/collision_oWhip.gml:1" /> |
| [[machete\|Machete]] or [[mattock\|Mattock]] | <Stat id="machete.damage" /> | <Cite gml="objects/oEnemy/collision_oWhip.gml:1" /> |
| [[stomping\|Stomp]] (landing on it), except while the player is swimming | 1, plus 1 for every 16 steps of falling; three times that with [[spike-shoes\|Spike Shoes]] | <Cite gml="objects/oEnemy/collision_oCharacter.gml:6-13" /> |
| A [[throwing\|thrown item]] moving faster than 2 pixels per step, such as a [[rock\|Rock]] | 1 | <Cite gml="objects/oItem/step.gml:256-348" /> |
| A stunned monster thrown into it | 1 | <Cite gml="objects/oEnemy/step.gml:169-180" /> <Cite gml="objects/oEnemy/step.gml:252-259" /> |
| A [[shotgun\|Shotgun]] or [[pistol\|Pistol]] bullet | <Stat id="pistol.damage" /> | <Cite gml="objects/oBullet/collision_oEnemy.gml:16" /> |
| A [[bomb\|Bomb]]'s explosion | <Stat id="bomb.damage" /> | <Cite gml="objects/oExplosion/collision_oEnemy.gml:14" /> |
| Being out of the water | Turns it into a Fish Bone | <Cite gml="objects/oDeadFish/step.gml:70-74" /> |

The Dead Fish's own step event replaces the one most monsters share, which is where [[spikes|Spikes]],
[[lava|Lava]] and [[spear-trap|Spear Traps]] hurt them, so none of those harm a Dead Fish
<Cite gml="objects/oEnemy/step.gml:63-128" />.

<Clip id="dead-fish/stomp" />

A swimming player can't stomp it, but one dropping into the water lands on it before he's swimming, and
that is a stomp. The clip shows the player dropping into a pool onto a Dead Fish, which falls apart.

A Dead Fish that dies falls apart into three bones <Cite gml="objects/oDeadFish/step.gml:6-9" />. Dead Fish
killed count toward the player's kills, and the screen between levels draws a Dead Fish icon for each; in
the kills table the game writes to `stats.txt`, which has no row for them, they're added to the Piranha's
row <Cite gml="objects/oDeadFish/step.gml:10-15" />
<Cite gml="objects/oTransition/alarm_0.gml:143-148" /> <Cite gml="scripts/scrWriteStats.gml:181-198" />. One
that turns into a Fish Bone out of the water isn't killed, so it isn't counted.

## Locations

[[level-generation|Level generation]] makes Dead Fish only on [[restless-dead|graveyard levels]], which 1 in 10 levels of
[[jungle|the Jungle]] is <Cite gml="scripts/scrInitLevel.gml:147-148" />
<Cite gml="scripts/scrClearGlobals.gml:56" />. Each water block with no solid block in it has a 1 in 30
chance of a monster, which on a graveyard level is a Dead Fish and on any other Jungle level a Piranha
<Cite gml="scripts/scrEntityGen.gml:326-336" />.

The [[level-editor|level editor]] has no tile for a Dead Fish, so custom levels can't have them.

## Tips

- A Dead Fish only chases a swimming player, so one crossing above the water isn't chased.
- Dropping into the water right onto a Dead Fish stomps it.
- A Dead Fish freezes as soon as it's off screen, so one left behind stays where it was, and one killed
  off screen falls apart only when it's seen again.

## Trivia

- The Dead Fish's code never sets its `type` string, so it keeps the parent object's `"None"`
  <Cite gml="objects/oEnemy/create.gml:4" />. A Dead Fish that takes the player's last heart isn't counted
  in the deaths-by-enemy table the game writes to `stats.txt`, under the Piranha or anywhere else
  <Cite gml="objects/oEnemy/collision_oCharacter.gml:27-51" /> <Cite quirk="1" row="The zombie's, the vampire's and the Dead Fish's kills of the player are never tallied." />.
- Unlike the Piranha's, its code starts an attack without checking that the player is alive, so near a
  dead swimming player it keeps starting attacks and giving them up the next step <Cite
  gml="objects/oDeadFish/step.gml:36-39" /> <Cite gml="objects/oDeadFish/step.gml:52-62" /> <Cite
  gml="objects/oPiranha/step.gml:32" />.
