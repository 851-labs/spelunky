Kali never moves. Her statue stands over an altar in a side room of some levels: a stone body
behind the altar and a head above it, one of three faces drawn at random <Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGen.gml:363-369" />
<Cite gml="objects/oKaliHead/create.gml:1-6" />. She speaks in the messages at the foot of the screen,
after each sacrifice and when an altar is destroyed. The altar itself, where it's made and what it's made
of, is [[kalis-altar|Kali's altar]]'s page; this one is about the goddess, and [[kalis-favour|Kali's favour]]
and [[sacrifice|Sacrifice]] have the rules of her favour and of sacrificing in full.

## Behaviour

### Favour

Kali keeps a favour for the player, a count the game never shows, which a new game sets to 0
<Cite gml="scripts/scrClearGlobals.gml:65-67" />. Sacrifices raise it ([Sacrificing](#sacrificing)), a
destroyed altar takes 16 off it, and nothing else changes it <Cite gml="objects/oSacAltarLeft/destroy.gml:18" />.
After each sacrifice her message says how she feels, from "SHE SEEMS VERY ANGRY WITH YOU!" at
<Stat id="kali.devour" bare /> or less to "SHE SEEMS ECSTATIC WITH YOU!" at <Stat id="kali.gift-3" bare /> or more
<Cite gml="scripts/scrGetFavorMsg.gml:24-111" />. [[kalis-favour|Kali's favour]] has the count, every message and
the marks.

### Her gifts

<Clip id="damsel/sacrifice" />

The first time a sacrifice brings the favour to each of three marks, she gives a gift, which appears on the
altar's right half, free to take <Cite gml="scripts/scrGetFavorMsg.gml:27-110" />:

- **At <Stat id="kali.gift-1" bare />**, a piece of equipment the player hasn't got, with two puffs of smoke.
- **At <Stat id="kali.gift-2" bare />**, the [[kapala|Kapala]], the only way to get one <Cite quirk="1" row="The kapala's only source is Kali's gift at favor 16." />.
- **At <Stat id="kali.gift-3" bare />**, 99 bombs, or <Stat id="health.kali-heal" /> for a player with 80 or more,
  and hearts again for every 16 more after it.

Each is given once a game, and a mark a sacrifice jumps past is skipped for good. Which equipment, and the
rest, is on [[kalis-favour#her-gifts|Kali's favour]]. In the clip, a Damsel thrown down on the altar brings
the favour from 0 to 8, and Kali gives a [[compass|Compass]] <Cite clip="damsel/sacrifice" />.

### Her anger

Destroying her altar angers her. Either half of it destroyed, by anything (a [[bomb|Bomb]]'s blast, a
[[ufo|UFO]]'s laser, or the floor under it taken away, which makes it crumble once it's on screen), defiles it:
"YOU DARE DEFILE MY ALTAR?" and "I WILL PUNISH YOU!", the screen shakes, the favour falls by 16, and both
halves go <Cite gml="objects/oSacAltarLeft/step.gml:1-5" /> <Cite gml="objects/oSacAltarLeft/destroy.gml:11-67" />
<Cite quirk="1" row="Anything that destroys an altar defiles it" /> <Cite quirk="1" row="Kali's punishment for a defiled altar grows with each one." />. What she does depends on how many altars the player has defiled in the game <Cite gml="objects/oSacAltarLeft/destroy.gml:20-60" />:

| The first | <Stat id="kali.spiders" bare /> [[spider\|Spiders]] spill out of her head, which is left a hole | <Cite gml="objects/oKaliHead/alarm_0.gml:1-8" /> |
| The second | A heavy [[ball-and-chain\|ball and chain]] on the player's ankle, and a new one on every level after, to the end of the game | <Cite gml="objects/oSacAltarLeft/destroy.gml:27-38" /> <Cite gml="objects/oLevel/create.gml:31-42" /> |

## Interactions

### Sacrificing

A stunned or dead monster, or a thrown or dead [[damsel|Damsel]], left lying still on either half of an altar
for <Stat id="sacrifice.wait" /> is sacrificed: it bursts into flame and is gone, and Kali's message is "KALI
ACCEPTS THE SACRIFICE!" ("YOUR SACRIFICE" for a Damsel) <Cite gml="objects/oEnemy/step.gml:130-166" />
<Cite gml="objects/oDamsel/step.gml:311-348" />. A Damsel is worth <Stat id="damsel.favor" bare />; of the
monsters, a stunned [[shopkeeper|Shopkeeper]] is worth the most, <Stat id="shopkeeper.favor" bare />, and a
dead one half what it's worth stunned. At a favour of <Stat id="kali.devour" bare /> or less, "KALI DEVOURS THE
SACRIFICE!", and it adds nothing. The player's own body is never a sacrifice. [[sacrifice|Sacrifice]] has the
rite step by step, what can be sacrificed and what each is worth.

### Defiling her altar

The [[kalis-altar|altar]]'s page has the ways an altar is destroyed. A defiled altar is gone for the level,
and a level has one altar at most <Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGen.gml:87-91" />. A favour below 0 recovers
a sacrifice at a time, as long as it's above <Stat id="kali.devour" bare />. At
<Stat id="kali.devour" bare /> or below it never does: only a sacrifice raises it, and she devours every
one <Cite gml="objects/oEnemy/step.gml:144-147" />. So a single defiled altar shuts her off for the rest of
the game unless the favour was above 8 before it.

  then sets that value to 0. The body is destroyed a moment later, so the line changes nothing, and an angry
  Kali takes a sacrifice as gladly as a pleased one <Cite gml="objects/oEnemy/step.gml:148-153" /> <Cite gml="objects/oDamsel/step.gml:329-334" />
  <Cite quirk="1" row="An angry Kali's sacrifice zeroes the body's value after adding it." />. In the replay, a defiled altar leaves her favour at −4, and a dead [[yeti|Yeti]] on the
  next level's altar raises it by 2, to −2 <Cite replay="fidelity/replays/item-seed-11-kali-angry-sacrifice.txt" />:

```gml objects/oEnemy/step.gml:148-153
            else if (global.favor < 0)
            {
                if (status == 98) global.favor += favor;
                else global.favor += favor/2;
                if (favor > 0) favor = 0;
            }
```

- A Damsel's sacrifice has a branch that would make a stunned one worth half as much again, 12, but she's
  never in the state it tests for, so she's worth the same alive or dead <Cite gml="objects/oDamsel/step.gml:331" /> <Cite quirk="1" row="A Damsel's sacrifice never takes its stunned branch." />.
- A dead Vampire is worth half a favour, so the favour needn't be a whole number, and "SHE HAS FORGIVEN
  YOU!", which needs it to be exactly 0, can be missed <Cite gml="objects/oEnemy/step.gml:154-158" />
  <Cite gml="scripts/scrGetFavorMsg.gml:26" />.
- With two altars or more defiled, the ending's walking figure drags a ball and chain too
  <Cite gml="objects/oEnd/create.gml:6-17" />.
