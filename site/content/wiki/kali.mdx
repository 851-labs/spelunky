Kali never moves. Her statue stands over an altar in a side room of some levels: a stone body
behind the altar and a head above it, one of three faces drawn at random <Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGen.gml:363-369" />
<Cite gml="objects/oKaliHead/create.gml:1-6" />. She speaks in the messages at the foot of the screen,
after each sacrifice and when an altar is destroyed. The altar itself, where it's made and what it's made
of, is [[kalis-altar|Kali's Altar]]'s page; this one is about the goddess: her favour, her gifts and her
anger.

## Behaviour

### Favour

The game keeps one count of Kali's favour, which a new game sets to 0, along with the count of her gifts and
of her punishments <Cite gml="scripts/scrClearGlobals.gml:65-67" />. Sacrifices raise it
([Sacrificing](#sacrificing)), a destroyed altar takes 16 off it, and nothing else changes it
<Cite gml="objects/oSacAltarLeft/destroy.gml:18" />. The game never shows the number. After each sacrifice,
Kali's message says where it stands, and at three marks, the first time it reaches them, she gives a gift <Cite gml="scripts/scrGetFavorMsg.gml:24-111" />:

| Favour after the sacrifice | Message | Gift | |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| <Stat id="kali.devour" bare /> or less | "SHE SEEMS VERY ANGRY WITH YOU!" | None | <Cite gml="scripts/scrGetFavorMsg.gml:24" /> |
| Above <Stat id="kali.devour" bare />, below 0 | "SHE SEEMS ANGRY WITH YOU." | None | <Cite gml="scripts/scrGetFavorMsg.gml:25" /> |
| 0 | "SHE HAS FORGIVEN YOU!" | None | <Cite gml="scripts/scrGetFavorMsg.gml:26" /> |
| Above 0, below <Stat id="kali.gift-1" bare /> | "SHE SEEMS PLEASED WITH YOU." | None | <Cite gml="scripts/scrGetFavorMsg.gml:111" /> |
| <Stat id="kali.gift-1" bare /> or more, below <Stat id="kali.gift-2" bare /> | "SHE BESTOWS A GIFT UPON YOU!", then "SHE SEEMS HAPPY WITH YOU." | A piece of equipment | <Cite gml="scripts/scrGetFavorMsg.gml:67-109" /> |
| <Stat id="kali.gift-2" bare /> or more, below <Stat id="kali.gift-3" bare /> | "SHE BESTOWS A GIFT UPON YOU!", then "SHE SEEMS VERY HAPPY WITH YOU." | The [[kapala\|Kapala]] | <Cite gml="scripts/scrGetFavorMsg.gml:52-65" /> |
| <Stat id="kali.gift-3" bare /> or more | "YOUR SATCHEL FEELS VERY FULL NOW!", then "SHE SEEMS ECSTATIC WITH YOU!" | 99 bombs | <Cite gml="scripts/scrGetFavorMsg.gml:27-50" /> |

The first two gifts are given once a game each, and a sacrifice that takes the favour past a mark skips
that mark's gift: from 7 to 19 with a stunned Shopkeeper, the player gets the Kapala and never the equipment
<Cite gml="scripts/scrGetFavorMsg.gml:52-76" />. No sacrifice is worth more than 12, so none goes from below
16 to 32 at once.

### Her gifts

<Clip id="damsel/sacrifice" />

Kali's gifts appear on the altar's right half, free, as the player's to pick up <Cite gml="scripts/scrGetFavorMsg.gml:62-64" /> <Cite gml="scripts/scrGetFavorMsg.gml:106-107" />.

- **At <Stat id="kali.gift-1" bare />**, with two puffs of smoke, a piece of equipment the player hasn't got:
  a roll of 1 to 8 picks the [[cape|Cape]] (not if he has a [[jetpack|Jetpack]]), the
  [[climbing-gloves|Climbing Gloves]], the [[spectacles|Spectacles]], the [[pitchers-mitt|Pitcher's Mitt]], the
  [[spring-shoes|Spring Shoes]], the [[spike-shoes|Spike Shoes]], the [[paste|Paste]] or the
  [[compass|Compass]], and one he has already passes to the next in that order. If none is left, it's a
  Jetpack, or, if he has one, a [[bomb-box|Bomb Box]] <Cite gml="scripts/scrGetFavorMsg.gml:77-105" />. In the clip, a
  Damsel thrown down on the altar brings the favour from 0 to 8, and Kali gives a Compass
  <Cite clip="damsel/sacrifice" />.
- **At <Stat id="kali.gift-2" bare />**, the [[kapala|Kapala]], the only way to get one
  <Cite gml="scripts/scrGetFavorMsg.gml:52-65" /> <Cite quirk="1" />.
- **At <Stat id="kali.gift-3" bare />**, 99 bombs. A player with 80 or more gets
  <Stat id="health.kali-heal" /> instead: "YOU FEEL INVIGORATED!". After it, every 16 favour more (48, 64
  and so on) gives the hearts again, and a sacrifice that falls short of the next 16 has her "ECSTATIC"
  <Cite gml="scripts/scrGetFavorMsg.gml:27-50" />.

### Her anger

Destroying her altar angers her. Either half of it destroyed, by anything (a [[bomb|Bomb]]'s blast, a
[[ufo|UFO]]'s laser, or the floor under it taken away, which makes it crumble once it's on screen), defiles it:
"YOU DARE DEFILE MY ALTAR?" and "I WILL PUNISH YOU!", the screen shakes, the favour falls by 16, and both
halves go <Cite gml="objects/oSacAltarLeft/step.gml:1-5" /> <Cite gml="objects/oSacAltarLeft/destroy.gml:11-67" />
<Cite quirk="1" />. What she does depends on how many altars the player has defiled in the game <Cite gml="objects/oSacAltarLeft/destroy.gml:20-60" />:

| Altar defiled | Punishment | |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The first | <Stat id="kali.spiders" bare /> [[spider\|Spiders]] spill out of her head, which is left a hole | <Cite gml="objects/oKaliHead/alarm_0.gml:1-8" /> |
| The second | A heavy [[ball-and-chain\|ball and chain]] on the player's ankle, and a new one on every level after, to the end of the game | <Cite gml="objects/oSacAltarLeft/destroy.gml:27-38" /> <Cite gml="objects/oLevel/create.gml:31-42" /> |
| Any after | The level goes dark, and a [[ghost\|Ghost]] comes in from the edge of the screen unless there's one already; on a level already dark with a ghost, six more Spiders | <Cite gml="objects/oSacAltarLeft/destroy.gml:39-58" /> |

## Interactions

### Sacrificing

A stunned or dead monster, or a thrown or dead [[damsel|Damsel]], that lies still on either half of an
altar for 20 steps, not held, is sacrificed: it bursts into flame and is gone, and Kali's message is "KALI
ACCEPTS THE SACRIFICE!" ("YOUR SACRIFICE" for a Damsel), then where her favour stands
<Cite gml="objects/oEnemy/step.gml:130-166" /> <Cite gml="objects/oDamsel/step.gml:311-348" />. A monster's
sacrifice only counts on screen, as its step only runs there <Cite gml="objects/oEnemy/step.gml:7-8" />.
At a favour of <Stat id="kali.devour" bare /> or less, "KALI DEVOURS THE SACRIFICE!", and it adds nothing.
The player's own body is never a sacrifice: nothing in his step looks for the altar.

A Damsel thrown onto the altar lies there stunned, so she's worth the same alive or dead; the half-again
branch for a stunned Damsel never runs ([Trivia](#trivia)). The monsters' pages say how to stun each one, and
[[sacrifice]] the whole of it.

### Defiling her altar

The [[kalis-altar|altar]]'s page has the ways an altar is destroyed. A defiled altar is gone for the level,
and a level has one altar at most <Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGen.gml:87-91" />. A favour below 0 recovers
a sacrifice at a time, as long as it's above <Stat id="kali.devour" bare />. At
<Stat id="kali.devour" bare /> or below it never does: only a sacrifice raises it, and she devours every
one <Cite gml="objects/oEnemy/step.gml:144-147" />. So a single defiled altar shuts her off for the rest of
the game unless the favour was above 8 before it.

## Trivia

- A sacrifice at a favour below 0 has a branch of its own, which adds the body's value as the other does and
  then sets that value to 0. The body is destroyed a moment later, so the line changes nothing, and an angry
  Kali takes a sacrifice as gladly as a pleased one <Cite gml="objects/oEnemy/step.gml:148-153" /> <Cite gml="objects/oDamsel/step.gml:329-334" />:

```gml objects/oEnemy/step.gml:148-153
            else if (global.favor < 0)
            {
                if (status == 98) global.favor += favor;
                else global.favor += favor/2;
                if (favor > 0) favor = 0;
            }
```

- A Damsel's sacrifice has a branch that would make a stunned one worth half as much again, 12, but she's
  never in the state it tests for <Cite gml="objects/oDamsel/step.gml:331" /> <Cite quirk="1" />.
- A dead Vampire is worth half a favour, so the favour needn't be a whole number, and "SHE HAS FORGIVEN
  YOU!", which needs it to be exactly 0, can be missed <Cite gml="objects/oEnemy/step.gml:154-158" />
  <Cite gml="scripts/scrGetFavorMsg.gml:26" />.
- With two altars or more defiled, the ending's walking figure drags a ball and chain too
  <Cite gml="objects/oEnd/create.gml:6-17" />.
