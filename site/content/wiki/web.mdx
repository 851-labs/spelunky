A web is a grey mesh the size of a block, stretched across a gap in the rock. It hurts nothing. Anything
that moves into it, a monster, a [[throwing|thrown item]] or a piece of treasure, stops dead and hangs there, and the
player in one moves at a crawl until he jumps his way out. Level generation leaves webs in the nooks of
the Mines, the Jungle and the Temple, and a [[giant-spider|Giant Spider]] and the
[[web-cannon|Web Cannon]] make more.

## How it works

<Clip id="web/fall" />

A web has a strength of <Stat id="web.life" />, and it's drawn fainter as the strength goes down: its
opacity is its strength over 12 <Cite gml="objects/oWeb/create.gml:1-2" /> <Cite gml="objects/oWeb/step.gml:1-3" />.
Once the strength is 1 or less, the web is gone.

While the player's centre is in a web, unless he's climbing a rope or a ladder, he keeps only a fifth of his
speed each step, across and up or down, and his fall is forgotten: the count of steps he has been falling,
which decides what a landing costs him, goes back to 0 <Cite gml="scripts/characterStepEvent.gml:677-681" />
<Cite gml="scripts/characterStepEvent.gml:743-749" /> <Cite gml="scripts/characterStepEvent.gml:895-896" />.
On ice he slides as he would without it, and under an open [[parachute|Parachute]] or [[cape|Cape]] he keeps
half his speed up or down <Cite gml="scripts/characterStepEvent.gml:774-778" /> <Cite
gml="scripts/characterStepEvent.gml:868-875" />.
So a long fall that ends in a web does him no harm. A fall of more than 32 steps would cost him 2 hearts on
landing, and one of more than 48 steps 10 <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:268-289" />.

The jump key gets him out. Pressed in a web, it jumps him even in mid-air, and takes 1 from the strength of
the web <Cite gml="scripts/characterStepEvent.gml:294-307" />. In the web, the jump only lifts him a little,
and he sinks back, so it takes eleven jumps to break a web from full strength, and can take more where
another web meets it ([Trivia](#trivia)).

The clip shows the player walking off a ledge into a shaft and falling 43 steps, enough for a 2-heart
landing, into a web at its foot, which catches him unhurt. He jumps again and again, the web fading with
each jump, until the eleventh breaks it, and the next jump is a full one <Cite clip="web/fall" />.

A web the Giant Spider spits or the Web Cannon fires also fades by itself, losing <Stat id="web.fade" /> of
strength each step it's near the view <Cite gml="objects/oLevel/step.gml:75-94" />, so one in view is gone 551
steps (about 18 seconds) after it appears: the subtraction's rounding leaves the strength a hair over 1 after
the 550th <Cite gml="objects/oWebBall/other_animation_end.gml:1-6" /> <Cite gml="objects/oWeb/step.gml:1-3" />.
The webs level generation makes, and the Giant Spider's two under its perch, never fade.

## Damage

None. A web never hurts the player or a monster, and holding a monster still doesn't hurt it either. It can
save the player from a fall, as above, and it holds a monster where the player can reach it: in the second
clip, a [[snake|Snake]] slithers into a web and stays there, and the player walks up and whips it dead
<Cite clip="web/snake" />.

## Locations

Level generation tries for a web in the open space on top of each block outside the shops, after the rolls
for a loose [[rock|Rock]] (1 in 100) and a [[jar|Jar]] (1 in 40) there fail, and before the rolls for
anything else in the space <Cite gml="scripts/scrEntityGen.gml:35-41" /> <Cite gml="scripts/scrEntityGen.gml:255-259" />
<Cite gml="scripts/scrEntityGen.gml:475-478" /> <Cite gml="scripts/scrTreasureGen.gml:42-55" />:

| Space | Chance | |
| --- | --- | --- |
| An alcove: a block over the space and one beside it | 1 in 5 | <Cite gml="scripts/scrTreasureGen.gml:48-55" /> |
| A tunnel: blocks on both sides of the space, none over it | 1 in 10 | <Cite gml="scripts/scrTreasureGen.gml:95-100" /> |

That's in [[mines|the Mines]], [[jungle|the Jungle]] and [[temple|the Temple]]; the
[[ice-caves|Ice Caves]] get no webs this way <Cite gml="scripts/scrTreasureGen.gml:55" />. The space must
be empty (no treasure, chest, spikes, entrance or exit in it) and at least 32 pixels from the entrance and
the exit and 64 from an idol <Cite gml="scripts/scrTreasureGen.gml:27-40" />. The odds were meant to be
1 in 60, rising to these only within 100 pixels of a Giant Spider; a mistake in the test makes every space
count as near one ([Trivia](#trivia)).

Webs are also made by:

- **[[giant-spider|Giant Spiders]]**, which start out hanging with two webs side by side under them
  <Cite gml="objects/oGiantSpiderHang/create.gml:26-27" />, and spit balls of web that stick where they
  land <Cite gml="objects/oGiantSpider/step.gml:80-87" />.
- **The [[web-cannon|Web Cannon]]**, whose shots stick as webs where they stop
  <Cite gml="scripts/scrUseItem.gml:332-397" />.
- **The [[level-editor|level editor]]**, as its tile `&` <Cite gml="scripts/scrCreateTileObj.gml:110-113" />.

## Avoiding

- **Cut it.** A [[machete|Machete]]'s slash destroys a web it touches; the Whip's crack passes through
  <Cite gml="objects/oWeb/collision_oSlash.gml:1" />.
- **Blow it up.** A [[bomb|Bomb]]'s explosion destroys the webs it reaches
  <Cite gml="objects/oExplosion/collision_oWeb.gml:1" />.
- **Jump out.** Eleven jumps break a web the player is caught in, or more where another web meets it (above).
- **Fall into it.** A web at the foot of a drop is a safe landing.

## Trivia

- The webs' odds in level generation pass a comparison where the object should go:

  ```gml scripts/scrTreasureGen.gml:52-55
      n = 60;
      if (distance_to_object(oGiantSpider < 100)) n = 5;

      if (global.levelType != 2 and rand(1,n) == 1) instance_create(x, y-16, oWeb);
  ```

  `oGiantSpider < 100` is false, so the call measures the distance to an object that doesn't exist, and
  gets a large number, which counts as true. So every alcove gets the 1 in 5 meant for one near a Giant
  Spider, and every tunnel the 1 in 10, and webs are about 11 times as common as the code means
  <Cite quirk="1" />.
- A jump checks for a web at the player's centre, but wears down the first web his body overlaps
  ([[web-jump-wears-wrong-web]]) <Cite gml="scripts/characterStepEvent.gml:294-297" />: where two webs meet, a jump can wear down the
  other one. With the player in the later-made of the two, near where they meet, most of his jumps can go to
  the web beside him, which breaks first, while the one holding him takes more than eleven jumps
  <Cite quirk="1" />. In a staged scene with three webs side by side, eleven of his fourteen jumps wear down
  the middle web, which breaks, and only three the web he's in
  <Cite replay="fidelity/replays/trap-web-jump-wrong-web.txt" />. The [[giant-spider|Giant Spider]]'s two
  webs are such a pair.
