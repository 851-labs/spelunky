A stomp is the player's attack without a weapon, and one of the few that scales: a monster with several hit
points, such as a [[caveman|Caveman]], survives a hop onto it but not a fall from high above. It also ends
the fall, so the drop that would have stunned the player costs him nothing. Most monsters share the code
for it, and a few have their own, with other rules.

## How it works

The stomp is part of a monster's touch: the code most monsters share (`oEnemy`'s collision with the
player) decides between a stomp and a bite <Cite gml="objects/oEnemy/collision_oCharacter.gml:1-14" />.

1. **Close enough.** Nothing happens unless the player's middle is within 12 pixels, across, of the
   monster's <Cite gml="objects/oEnemy/collision_oCharacter.gml:2-5" />.
2. **From above.** It's a stomp if the player is alive, not swimming, jumping or falling, and his middle is
   above a point 8 pixels below the monster's top. Otherwise the monster bites, if he isn't invincible
   ([[damage]]) <Cite gml="objects/oEnemy/collision_oCharacter.gml:6" />
   <Cite gml="objects/oEnemy/collision_oCharacter.gml:14" />.
3. **The hit.** The monster loses 1 hit point, plus 1 for every whole 16 steps the player has been falling,
   and three times that if he wears [[spike-shoes|Spike Shoes]], which draw blood as well
   <Cite gml="objects/oEnemy/collision_oCharacter.gml:9-10" />. His step counts the falling steps, adding 1
   on every step he moves down (`fallTimer`) <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:254-257" />.
4. **The bounce.** He's sent up at <Stat id="stomping.bounce" />, plus a fifth of his falling speed, and his
   count of falling steps starts again, so the landing that ends the bounce isn't a long
   drop <Cite gml="objects/oEnemy/collision_oCharacter.gml:8" />
   <Cite gml="objects/oEnemy/collision_oCharacter.gml:11" />.

The monster's own step kills it once its hit points are gone; the stomp only takes them
<Cite gml="objects/oSnake/step.gml:16-26" />. A stomp while the
player is still rising counts too, since jumping is enough, so jumping up into a [[bat|Bat]] from just
below it kills it <Cite quirk="1" row="A stomp doesn't need the player to be falling" /> <Cite replay="fidelity/replays/enemy-mines-seed-1-kills.txt" />.

page; in short:

monster's top it must be above:

## Numbers

Damage of a stomp by how many steps the player fell, for most monsters and for those that round up (the
Yeti, the Yeti King and the Shopkeeper) <Cite gml="objects/oEnemy/collision_oCharacter.gml:9-10" />
<Cite gml="objects/oYeti/collision_oCharacter.gml:11-12" />:

| Steps of falling | Most monsters | With Spike Shoes | Yeti, Yeti King, Shopkeeper | With Spike Shoes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0 (rising) | 1 | 3 | 0 | 0 |
| 1–15 | 1 | 3 | 1 | 3 |
| 16 | 2 | 6 | 1 | 3 |
| 17–31 | 2 | 6 | 2 | 6 |
| 32 | 3 | 9 | 2 | 6 |
| 33–47 | 3 | 9 | 3 | 9 |

The player falls 1 pixel a step faster each step, up to 10 pixels a step
<Cite gml="scripts/characterCreateEvent.gml:58-62" />, so from a standing start 16 steps of falling are about
seven blocks, and 32 about seventeen.

## Examples

<Clip id="stomping/caveman" />

- **From a height.** The player walks off a high ledge and lands on a Caveman after 33 steps of falling:
  the stomp takes 3 hit points, all of its 3, and he bounces off it with his 4 hearts, where the same drop to
  the floor would have cost him 2 <Cite clip="stomping/caveman" />.
- **On the 16th step.** Landing on a Yeti on the 16th step of falling takes 1 of its hit points, where the
  same drop onto a Caveman takes 2 <Cite replay="fidelity/replays/enemy-yeti-stomp-sixteen-steps.txt" />.
- **With Spike Shoes.** A stomp from a short drop takes 3 hit points, three times a plain one's 1, so a
  Caveman with 2 left dies <Cite replay="fidelity/replays/item-seed-7-spike-stomp.txt" />.

## Quirks

- **A stomp doesn't need the player to be falling** ([[stomping-while-jumping]]). Jumping is enough, so a monster he jumps up into from
  just below is stomped, and he bounces off its underside <Cite quirk="1" row="A stomp doesn't need the player to be falling" />
  <Cite replay="fidelity/replays/enemy-mines-seed-1-kills.txt" />.
- **Stomping a Yeti, the Yeti King or a Shopkeeper on the way up takes nothing**, as their stomps round the
  steps of falling up where the others' add 1: a Yeti is stunned all the same
  ([[yeti-stomp-rising]]) <Cite quirk="1" row="Stomping a Yeti, the Yeti King or a Shopkeeper on the way up takes nothing." /> <Cite replay="fidelity/replays/enemy-yeti-stomp-rising.txt" />.
