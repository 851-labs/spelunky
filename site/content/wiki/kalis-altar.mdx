The altar is two blocks of stone under a statue of [[kali|Kali]], in a side room of the level. A body left
on it is a [[sacrifice]], which raises [[kalis-favour|Kali's favour]], and her gifts, the
[[kapala|Kapala]] among them, appear on it. Destroying it, by anything, defiles it: the favour falls and
Kali punishes the player. A level has one at most, and many have none.

## Generation

<Clip id="kalis-altar/defile" />

Each area's room script rolls for the altar first when it lays out a side room, before the idol room or
any other layout, and stops rolling once the level has one <Cite gml="objects/oGame/create.gml:9-11" />:

| Area | Side rooms that roll | Chance | |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [[mines\|The Mines]], from level 2 | Every side room | 1 in 16 | <Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGen.gml:85-91" /> |
| [[jungle\|The Jungle]] | Two in three | 1 in 12 | <Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGen2.gml:79-85" /> |
| [[ice-caves\|The Ice Caves]] | One in two | 1 in 12 | <Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGen3.gml:68-74" /> |
| [[temple\|The Temple]] | Three in four | 1 in 12 | <Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGen4.gml:68-79" /> |

The [[city-of-gold|City of Gold]]'s side rooms draw from other layouts and never get an altar
<Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGen4.gml:70-74" />. The [[black-market|Black Market]] and the
[[yeti-lair|Yeti lair]] are built by room scripts of their own, which have no altar, and
[[olmecs-lair|Olmec's Lair]] is a room of its own <Cite gml="scripts/scrInitLevel.gml:150-165" />. The other
special levels are built by their area's script and keep its side rooms, so they can have one: a
[[sacrifice-pit|sacrifice pit]] has no altar in the pit, but with seed 43, level 15 is a sacrifice pit with
an altar in a side room <Cite replay="fidelity/replays/temple-seed-43.txt" />.

### The altar room

The altar room is open, with the altar on a low step of blocks in the middle of its floor, and in the
Mines, the Jungle and the Temple, but not the Ice Caves, blocks in its top corners, each one time in 2 <Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGen.gml:118-119" /> <Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGen2.gml:115-116" /> <Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGen3.gml:100-101" />
<Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGen4.gml:116-117" />. The altar is two solid blocks, a left half and a right half,
which the player can stand on <Cite gml="objects/oSacAltarLeft/create.gml:1-4" />. Behind it the room
draws Kali's stone body, and above it puts her head, one of three faces at random
<Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGen.gml:363-369" /> <Cite gml="objects/oKaliHead/create.gml:1-6" />. Level
generation puts no treasure on the altar, and in the Temple no [[spear-trap|Spear Trap]] in it <Cite gml="scripts/scrEntityGen.gml:39-42" /> <Cite gml="scripts/scrEntityGen.gml:255-258" /> <Cite gml="scripts/scrEntityGen.gml:373-376" /> <Cite gml="scripts/scrEntityGen.gml:475-478" /> <Cite gml="scripts/scrEntityGen.gml:410" />.

The main clip shows a Mines altar room: the player drops a [[bomb|Bomb]] beside the altar and jumps clear,
and the blast destroys it ([Hazards](#hazards)) <Cite clip="kalis-altar/defile" />.

## Hazards

The altar's halves are solid blocks, none of them made indestructible, so what breaks blocks breaks
them <Cite gml="objects/oSacAltarLeft/create.gml:1-4" /> <Cite quirk="1" />:

- **An explosion**, a Bomb's or any other, within 16 pixels of the view <Cite gml="objects/oExplosion/collision_oSolid.gml:1-15" />.
- **A [[mattock|Mattock]]'s swing** <Cite gml="objects/oMattockHit/other_animation_end.gml:1-23" />.
- **A [[ufo|UFO]]'s laser** <Cite gml="objects/oLaser/collision_oSolid.gml:1-16" />.
- **A [[boulder|Boulder]]** rolling into it at speed <Cite gml="objects/oBoulder/collision_oSolid.gml:1-25" />.
- **The floor under it taken away**: a half with no block under it crumbles as soon as it's within 20
  pixels of the view's left and top and 4 of its right and bottom <Cite gml="objects/oSacAltarLeft/step.gml:1-5" />.

Destroying either half destroys both, and defiles the altar, whatever destroyed it: "YOU DARE DEFILE MY ALTAR?"
and "I WILL PUNISH YOU!" show, the screen shakes, and the favour falls by 16
<Cite gml="objects/oSacAltarLeft/destroy.gml:11-66" />. Kali's punishment grows with each altar the player
has defiled in the game <Cite gml="objects/oSacAltarLeft/destroy.gml:20-60" />:

| Altar defiled | Punishment | |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The first | <Stat id="kali.spiders" bare /> [[spider\|Spiders]] spill out of her head | <Cite gml="objects/oKaliHead/alarm_0.gml:1-8" /> |
| The second | A [[ball-and-chain\|ball and chain]] on the player's ankle, to the end of the game | <Cite gml="objects/oSacAltarLeft/destroy.gml:27-38" /> |
| Any after | The level goes dark and a [[ghost\|Ghost]] comes, or, on a level dark with a Ghost already, six more Spiders | <Cite gml="objects/oSacAltarLeft/destroy.gml:39-58" /> |

[[kalis-favour#her-anger]] has the punishments in full, and [[kali#her-anger]] a clip of each. In the main
clip, the first altar the player defiles lets the Spiders out <Cite clip="kalis-altar/defile" />. Below,
a Bomb takes the floor from under a fourth altar, which crumbles, and six more Spiders come out <Cite clip="kali/spiders" />.

## Items and shops

A stunned or dead monster, or a thrown or dead [[damsel|Damsel]], left lying still on either half for
<Stat id="sacrifice.wait" /> is a sacrifice: it bursts into flame, and its worth is added to the favour <Cite gml="objects/oEnemy/step.gml:130-166" /> <Cite gml="objects/oDamsel/step.gml:311-348" />.
[[sacrifice|Sacrifice]] has the rules and what each body is worth. The first time a sacrifice brings the
favour to each of three marks, Kali gives a gift, which appears on the altar's right half, free to take <Cite gml="scripts/scrGetFavorMsg.gml:27-110" />:

| Favour | Gift | |
| --- | --- | --- |
| <Stat id="kali.gift-1" bare /> | A piece of equipment the player hasn't got, with two puffs of smoke: the [[cape\|Cape]], [[climbing-gloves\|Climbing Gloves]], [[spectacles\|Spectacles]], [[pitchers-mitt\|Pitcher's Mitt]], [[spring-shoes\|Spring Shoes]], [[spike-shoes\|Spike Shoes]], [[paste\|Paste]] or [[compass\|Compass]]; with all of them, a [[jetpack\|Jetpack]], then a [[bomb-box\|Bomb Box]] | <Cite gml="scripts/scrGetFavorMsg.gml:67-105" /> |
| <Stat id="kali.gift-2" bare /> | The [[kapala\|Kapala]] | <Cite gml="scripts/scrGetFavorMsg.gml:52-64" /> |
| <Stat id="kali.gift-3" bare /> | <Stat id="bombs-and-ropes.kali-bombs" bare /> bombs, or <Stat id="health.kali-heal" /> for a player with <Stat id="bombs-and-ropes.kali-bombs-below" bare /> or more; then hearts again every 16 more | <Cite gml="scripts/scrGetFavorMsg.gml:27-50" /> |

Each is given once a game, and one a sacrifice jumps past is never given ([[kalis-favour#her-gifts]]). In
the clip, a Damsel thrown down on an Ice Caves altar burns, and Kali gives a Compass <Cite clip="damsel/sacrifice" />.
