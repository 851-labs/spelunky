The game has one ending, reached by beating [[olmec|Olmec]] at the bottom of the Temple. It plays as three
short scenes: the volcano, the escape and the final score. Then come the credits and the high scores room.
The custom levels have an ending of their own, the last scene only. A game that ends in a death has no
ending: its final score is shown over the level ([[scoring#game-over]]).

## How it works

### The door

When Olmec has drowned, the door at the right end of [[olmecs-lair|Olmec's Lair]]'s hall (the player came in
by the left one) becomes the way out
<Cite gml="objects/oFinalBoss/step.gml:1-12" />; going through it, with up, starts the ending
<Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:741" /> <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/other_animation_end.gml:132-136" />.
The game's clock stops there, as it runs only in a level <Cite gml="objects/oGame/step.gml:14-21" />.

### The volcano

<Clip id="endings/volcano" />

The player walks right along a cave above a lake of lava, the view following, and stops beside a big chest
<Cite gml="objects/oPDummy/step.gml:57-68" /> <Cite gml="objects/oEnd/step.gml:33" />. The chest opens and a
giant golden head pops out beside him <Cite gml="objects/oPDummy/alarm_1.gml:1-6" />. The lava then rises in
a column under the stone platform they stand on and lifts it, him and the treasure, while the screen shakes
<Cite gml="objects/oPDummy/alarm_3.gml:1-3" /> <Cite gml="objects/oLavaSpray/step.gml:1-18" />. Outside, the
volcano erupts, and the player and the treasure are thrown out of it, small against the sky <Cite
gml="objects/oLavaSpray/alarm_0.gml:1" /> <Cite gml="objects/oEnd2/alarm_0.gml:1-4" /> <Cite
gml="objects/oEnd2/alarm_1.gml:1" />. The clip shows both scenes <Cite clip="endings/volcano" />.

The player in the ending is whoever he played as, the [[damsel|Damsel]] or the [[tunnel-man|Tunnel Man]]
included, and carries what he held, his [[jetpack|Jetpack]], and Kali's [[ball-and-chain|ball and chain]] if
he has one <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:683-722" /> <Cite gml="objects/oPDummy/create.gml:14-16" />
<Cite gml="objects/oPDummy/draw.gml:1-38" /> <Cite gml="objects/oEnd/create.gml:6-17" />.

### The final score

<Clip id="endings/tally" />

Once the treasure falls out of sight, the last scene starts
<Cite gml="objects/oTreasureSil/step.gml:13-14" />. The player falls into a desert and lies stunned; the
treasure lands beside him, and he gets up <Cite gml="objects/oPDummy2/step.gml:1-62" />. Then, with the
victory music, YOU MADE IT! <Cite gml="objects/oPDummy2/alarm_2.gml:1-6" />, the final score, counting up to
the player's money 1,000 and then 100 a step <Cite gml="objects/oEnd3/step.gml:25-40" />, and 50 steps after
it has, the treasure's <Stat id="endings.bonus" />, with a thump
<Cite gml="objects/oEnd3/alarm_11.gml:1-5" />. The game's time, the kills and the saves follow
<Cite gml="objects/oEnd3/draw.gml:19-52" />.

Once the saves are shown, attack (or Enter, Escape or start) fades the screen out to YOU SHALL BE REMEMBERED
AS A HERO., and once more goes on to the credits; before then it does nothing, so the count can't be
hurried
<Cite gml="objects/oEnd3/step.gml:1-23" /> <Cite gml="objects/oEnd3/draw.gml:62-70" />. In the clip the
player brought out $21,125, and the final score is $71,125 <Cite clip="endings/tally" />. The first two
scenes can be skipped to this one: the volcano's after 50 steps, the escape's at once
<Cite gml="objects/oEnd/step.gml:1-14" /> <Cite gml="objects/oEnd2/step.gml:1-10" />.

### The credits

The [[credits]] roll over a camel caravan crossing the desert, and attack fades them out
<Cite gml="objects/oCredits2/step.gml:1-43" />. Then the win is recorded in the high scores, with the
game's time if the player took no shortcut ([[scoring#the-high-scores]]), and the game goes to the high
scores room <Cite gml="objects/oCredits2/step.gml:44-55" />.

### Custom levels

A custom level whose next level doesn't exist ends the chain with a scene of its own, the final score
without the treasure: YOU MADE IT!, the money counted up, the same <Stat id="endings.bonus" /> added, the
time, kills and saves, and YOU SHALL BE REMEMBERED AS A HERO. <Cite gml="scripts/scrLoadLevel.gml:152-157" />
<Cite gml="objects/oEndCustom/alarm_11.gml:1-5" />. It goes to the high scores room without changing them,
as custom levels keep no score <Cite gml="objects/oEndCustom/step.gml:17-23" />.
