Vines hang in [[jungle|the Jungle]]'s rooms, whose room script makes a vine from each tile that would be a
[[ladder|Ladder]] in the Mines', and in [[black-market|the Black Market]]. To the code a vine is a kind of ladder, so
everything about climbing one is the same, but its top: a ladder's top is a rung to stand on, and a vine's
isn't. [[monkey|Monkeys]] start out on them.

## How it works

<Clip id="vine/climb" />

The player climbs a vine exactly as he does a [[ladder#how-it-works|ladder]]: up in front of it, standing or in
the air, takes hold, up and down climb it at 1 pixel a step, and the jump key throws him off
<Cite gml="scripts/characterStepEvent.gml:185-236" /> <Cite gml="scripts/characterStepEvent.gml:606-633" />. A
fall doesn't count while he climbs <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:254-267" />. The
clip shows it in the Jungle's green rock: the player climbs a vine hanging past a ledge, jumps off toward the
ledge at its top, catches its edge and pulls himself up.

A vine's top is a kind of ladder, not of ladder top, so it's climbed like the rest of the vine and isn't a
platform: nothing stands on it <Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGen2.gml:349-350" />. How a vine looks is set when the
level is built: its top block is drawn as the vine's source and its bottom block as its end
<Cite gml="scripts/scrSetupWalls.gml:437-455" />.

## Interactions

- **[[monkey|Monkeys]].** Level generation puts a Monkey on each block of vine with a 1 in 15 chance
  <Cite gml="scripts/scrEntityGen.gml:321-324" />. It climbs up and down its vine, turning where the vine ends,
  and leaps off at a player below it <Cite gml="objects/oMonkey/step.gml:146-185" />.
- **[[bomb|Bombs]]** don't destroy vines, which aren't solid, as they don't destroy ladders
  <Cite gml="objects/oExplosion/collision_oSolid.gml:1-31" />.

## Tips

- A vine is safe to climb down however long it is, as a ladder is: a fall doesn't count while the player
  climbs.
- With no top to stand on, a vine that ends below a ledge is left by jumping off toward the ledge, as the clip
  does.
