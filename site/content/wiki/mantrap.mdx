Level generation plants Mantraps on the ground in [[jungle|the Jungle]], where they creep about slowly. A
Mantrap has no bite: whatever walks into it, the player included, it swallows whole, and the player dies on
the spot whatever his hearts. Landing on one is no defence without [[spike-shoes|Spike Shoes]]. With
<Stat id="mantrap.hp" />, it survives most single hits, which stun it, but one [[machete|Machete]] cut kills
it.

## Behaviour

A Mantrap sets off in a random direction as soon as it's made
<Cite gml="objects/oManTrap/step.gml:44-52" />. It turns around at walls and at the edges of ledges, and
stands still where it has a wall or a drop on both sides <Cite gml="objects/oManTrap/step.gml:53-78" />. Its
code sets its speed to <Stat id="mantrap.walk-speed" />, but friction then cuts that to 0.3 every step, and
the game moves it in whole pixels, so it covers 1 pixel every 3 steps (10 per second)
<Cite gml="objects/oManTrap/step.gml:144-149" /> <Cite gml="scripts/moveTo.gml:20-31" />. It has a 1 in 100 chance each step of stopping for 20–50 steps (0.67–1.67 seconds), and then sets off
in a random direction again <Cite gml="objects/oManTrap/step.gml:80-85" />.

It never looks for the player: it eats what it walks into, or what walks into it.

<Clip id="mantrap/eat" />

**Eating.** When the player touches a Mantrap that isn't stunned or already eating, within 8 pixels of its
middle across, the Mantrap swallows him: he vanishes, his hearts are set to −99, and he dies. Whatever he was
holding is thrown out <Cite gml="objects/oManTrap/collision_oCharacter.gml:1-4" />
<Cite gml="objects/oManTrap/collision_oCharacter.gml:20-62" />. The clip shows the player dropping onto a
Mantrap from a ledge and being eaten. It eats the same way a [[caveman|Caveman]] (alive, stunned or dead), the
Damsel (even out of the player's hands) or a [[shopkeeper|Shopkeeper]] that touches it; a Shopkeeper's
[[shotgun|Shotgun]] flies out as he's swallowed <Cite gml="objects/oManTrap/collision_oCaveman.gml:1-10" />
<Cite gml="objects/oManTrap/collision_oDamsel.gml:1-18" /> <Cite
gml="objects/oManTrap/collision_oShopkeeper.gml:1-24" />.

When its eating animation ends, it falls asleep, spitting out the odd bone, and when its sleeping animation
ends it lies stunned for 400 steps (13.33 seconds), twice as long as a hit stuns it, before it wakes and walks
again <Cite gml="objects/oManTrap/other_animation_end.gml:1-11" />
<Cite gml="objects/oManTrap/step.gml:158-170" />.

Like most monsters, it only acts while it's on screen or just off it
<Cite gml="objects/oManTrap/step.gml:3-4" />.

## Damage

A Mantrap kills the player outright, with no knockback and no invincibility to save him: it sets his hearts to
−99 <Cite gml="objects/oManTrap/collision_oCharacter.gml:41-46" />. Its touch event replaces the one most
monsters share, so it never takes a single heart. It can't eat him while it's stunned or already eating, nor
while he's invincible after a hit <Cite gml="objects/oManTrap/collision_oCharacter.gml:20-22" />.

Landing on a Mantrap is only a stomp with [[spike-shoes|Spike Shoes]], or while it's eating something else;
otherwise it counts as touching it, and it eats the player, as in the clip
<Cite gml="objects/oManTrap/collision_oCharacter.gml:5" /> <Cite clip="mantrap/eat" />.

## Defeating

<Clip id="mantrap/whip" />

Most hits stun a Mantrap as well as hurting it: it's knocked back and lies on the ground for
<Stat id="mantrap.stun-time" /> (<Stat id="mantrap.stun-time" as="seconds" />), counted down while it's on the
ground or carried, and then gets up if it has hit points left <Cite gml="objects/oManTrap/step.gml:87-137" />.
A stunned Mantrap can't eat, and the Whip, a stomp and thrown items can't hurt it again until it's up. The
clip shows the Whip stunning a Mantrap, which gets up about seven seconds later and walks off.

A Mantrap that ends up inside solid ground is pushed up out of it rather than killed
<Cite gml="objects/oManTrap/step.gml:151" />.

When its hit points run out, a Mantrap bursts into leaves and is gone, leaving no body
<Cite gml="objects/oManTrap/step.gml:20-40" />. If it had swallowed a living [[shopkeeper|Shopkeeper]], he
bursts back out, without his Shotgun, when it dies, or the next time it gets up from being stunned, which destroys it
<Cite gml="objects/oManTrap/step.gml:30-38" /> <Cite gml="objects/oManTrap/step.gml:111-120" />. Mantraps
killed count toward the player's kills, and the screen between levels draws a Mantrap icon for each
<Cite gml="objects/oManTrap/step.gml:22-27" /> <Cite gml="objects/oTransition/alarm_0.gml:191-196" />. One
that Spikes, Lava or a Spear Trap killed doesn't count <Cite gml="objects/oEnemy/step.gml:68" />
<Cite gml="objects/oEnemy/step.gml:87" /> <Cite gml="objects/oEnemy/step.gml:120" />.

A stunned Mantrap can be carried and thrown like a stunned [[caveman|Caveman]], and one left lying still on
Kali's altar for 20 steps is sacrificed to her, adding <Stat id="mantrap.favor" /> to her favour, unless her
favour is already −8 or less <Cite gml="objects/oEnemy/step.gml:130-167" />. As it leaves no body, a dead one
can't be.

## Locations

Level generation makes Mantraps only in [[jungle|the Jungle]], on top of blocks with open space above them,
outside the starting room and shops: each such block has a 1 in 60 chance, but not under water or on a
[[restless-dead|graveyard level]] <Cite gml="scripts/scrEntityGen.gml:276-289" />. In the
[[black-market|Black Market]], blocks at a height that's a multiple of 128 pixels (every eighth row) get none,
which the code's comment says is to keep Mantraps from growing near the shopkeepers there
<Cite gml="scripts/scrEntityGen.gml:287-289" />.

Custom levels can have them, as the [[level-editor|level editor]]'s tile `M`
<Cite gml="scripts/scrCreateTileObj.gml:189-192" />.

## Tips

- A Mantrap can't be stomped without Spike Shoes: landing on one is touching it.
- It moves 1 pixel every 3 steps, so it's easy to outwalk, and it never turns toward the player.
- One Machete cut kills it, as does a bullet or a Bomb; with the Whip, it takes three hits, each after it
  gets back up.
- A stunned Mantrap can't eat anything, so it's safe to carry until it wakes.
- After eating, it sleeps and then lies stunned for over 13 seconds.
