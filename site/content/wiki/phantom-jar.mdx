The player's hands and the item he's holding are two things to the game: the instance in his hands, and the
kind of item it remembers he's holding, which the HUD shows and the item key gives back. A bullet that breaks
a held [[jar|Jar]] or [[skull|Skull]] clears the first and, through a typo, not the second, so a new one
comes back.

## What happens

An angry [[shopkeeper|Shopkeeper]]'s shot breaks the Jar the player is holding. His hands are empty, but the
Jar stays on the HUD, and switching items gives him a [[bomb|Bomb]], a [[rope|Rope]], and then a brand-new
Jar <Cite clip="quirks/jar-shot-in-hands" />.

<Clip id="quirks/jar-shot-in-hands" />

## Why

A bullet's collision with a Jar only destroys it <Cite gml="objects/oJar/collision_oBullet.gml:1" />. The
Jar's destroy event then breaks it, and, if the player held it, empties his hands
<Cite gml="objects/oJar/destroy.gml:30-34" />:

```gml objects/oJar/destroy.gml:30-34
    if (held)
    {
        oPlayer1.holdItem = 0;
        oPlayer1.pickupItem = "";
    }
```

The variable the player's code reads for the kind of item he holds is `pickupItemType`; `pickupItem` is a
new variable that nothing reads. The Jar's whip code, which breaks a held Jar its own way, clears the right
one <Cite gml="objects/oJar/collision_oWhip.gml:16-20" />. The Skull's destroy event has the same typo
<Cite gml="objects/oSkull/destroy.gml:16-20" />.

So after the shot, `pickupItemType` is still "Jar": the HUD draws a Jar in the held item's box
<Cite gml="scripts/scrDrawHUD.gml:79-83" />, and the code that gives back the remembered item makes a new
one. The item key does, after a bomb and a rope, as it puts the rope away
<Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:983-991" />, and so does losing a bomb or a rope from his hands, by
being stunned, killed or knocked <Cite quirk="1" />.

## Reproducing it

Carry a Jar or a Skull where an angry Shopkeeper's [[shotgun|Shotgun]] can hit it. Once a pellet breaks it, press the item key until a Jar comes round. The
replay of the clip's staged scene pins it: the shot breaks the Jar, `pickupItemType` stays "Jar", and the item
key gives a bomb, a rope and a new Jar <Cite replay="fidelity/replays/item-held-jar-shot.txt" />.

## In the port

The spelunky.ai port reproduces it exactly: it's in docs/quirks.md's list of the 1.1 source's bugs
<Cite quirk="1" />, and the oracle's fingerprint of the replay pins the Jar's return. The Skull's version is
read in the code and not yet in a replay. No option turns it off.
