It's carried and thrown like a [[rock|Rock]], but it breaks on whatever it hits hard, and a broken Jar
sometimes has something in it: gold, a big gem, or a monster. [[level-generation|Level generation]] leaves Jars lying in every
area, and they have movement code of their own, which makes them fall and bounce a little differently from
other items.

## Usage

<Clip id="jar/throw" />

A Jar is picked up and thrown as any item is: crouching over it with empty hands and pressing the attack key
lifts it, and the attack key throws it, at 8 pixels a step plus the player's speed, or up, or low along the
floor <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:1203-1214" /> <Cite gml="scripts/scrUseItem.gml:605-646" />. Its
own code moves it: it falls 0.6 pixels a step faster each step until it's falling at 6 or more, and
bounces off walls and floors as a Rock does <Cite gml="objects/oJar/step.gml:23-79" />. It breaks when it:

- hits a ceiling going up faster than 3 pixels a step (a Skull breaks on any:
  [[skull-breaks-on-ceiling]]), hits a wall faster than 3 across, or lands faster than 3
  <Cite gml="objects/oJar/step.gml:37-53" />;
- ends up inside a block <Cite gml="objects/oJar/step.gml:49" />;
- touches a monster or a [[damsel|Damsel]] while moving faster than 2 pixels a
  step <Cite gml="objects/oJar/step.gml:106-172" />;
- is hit by the [[whip|Whip]]'s crack (or a [[machete|Machete]]'s slash or a [[mattock|Mattock]]'s hit,
  which the game counts as kinds of whip), a bullet or an explosion, or sinks into
  [[lava|lava]] <Cite gml="objects/oJar/collision_oWhip.gml:1-21" /> <Cite gml="objects/oJar/collision_oBullet.gml:1" /> <Cite gml="objects/oExplosion/collision_oItem.gml:1-5" /> <Cite gml="objects/oJar/step.gml:83-103" />.

Breaking, it shatters into three pieces and rolls once for what's inside, each roll made only if the ones
before it fail <Cite gml="objects/oJar/destroy.gml:3-28" />:

| Contents | Chance | |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A [[gold-chunk\|Gold Chunk]] | 1 in 3 | <Cite gml="objects/oJar/destroy.gml:17" /> |
| A [[gold-nugget\|Gold Nugget]] | 1 in 6 | <Cite gml="objects/oJar/destroy.gml:18" /> |
| A [[big-emerald\|Big Emerald]] | 1 in 12 | <Cite gml="objects/oJar/destroy.gml:19" /> |
| A [[big-sapphire\|Big Sapphire]] | 1 in 12 | <Cite gml="objects/oJar/destroy.gml:20" /> |
| A [[big-ruby\|Big Ruby]] | 1 in 12 | <Cite gml="objects/oJar/destroy.gml:21" /> |
| A [[spider\|Spider]] | 1 in 6 | <Cite gml="objects/oJar/destroy.gml:22" /> |
| A [[snake\|Snake]] | 1 in 12 | <Cite gml="objects/oJar/destroy.gml:23-28" /> |
| Nothing | Otherwise | |

In the clip, the player throws a Jar at a Snake: the Jar shatters on it and the Snake
dies <Cite clip="jar/throw" />.

### Thrown at monsters

The Jar's own code decides what it does to a monster, and it isn't the code other [[throwing|thrown item]]s
share <Cite gml="objects/oJar/step.gml:106-148" />. It picks the monster nearest it, as they
do ([[nearest-enemy]]) <Cite quirk="1" row="A fast thrown item hurts the nearest enemy, not the one it hit." />, and unless that one is invincible:

| Monster | What it does | |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [[caveman\|Caveman]], [[hawkman\|Hawkman]], [[mantrap\|Mantrap]], [[yeti\|Yeti]], [[shopkeeper\|Shopkeeper]] | Stuns it, taking nothing; nothing while it's stunned | <Cite gml="objects/oJar/step.gml:114-130" /> |
| Any other, the [[vampire\|Vampire]], [[magma-man\|Magma Man]] and [[ufo\|UFO]] included | <Stat id="jar.damage" />, without stunning it | <Cite gml="objects/oJar/step.gml:131-139" /> |

Either way, the monster takes three tenths of the Jar's speed, and the Jar breaks, on an invincible monster
too <Cite gml="objects/oJar/step.gml:139-147" />. A UFO that loses its hit point becomes a falling wreck, as
from a bullet, rather than blowing up as it does for a [[rock|Rock]] <Cite gml="objects/oUFO/step.gml:6-16" />.

## Locations

- **Level generation**, in every area: on each block that could have a Rock (outside shops, with open space
  above, away from the entrance, the exit and a [[golden-idol|golden idol]]), a 1 in 40 chance, once the Rock's 1 in 100
  fails <Cite gml="scripts/scrTreasureGen.gml:44-45" />.
- **The [[level-editor|level editor]]**'s `j` tile <Cite gml="scripts/scrCreateTileObj.gml:118-121" />.

The Jungle's [[restless-dead|graveyard]] room with the [[crystal-skull|Crystal Skull]] has four tiles that the code means to make rising bones
or a Jar, but its test is always true, so they're always bones <Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGen2.gml:490-494" /> <Cite quirk="1" row="if (rand(1,2)) is always true" />.

## Interactions

<Clip id="jar/whip" />

- **[[whip|Whip]].** The crack breaks a Jar, and its code rolls for the contents, then destroys the Jar,
  which rolls again: a whipped Jar gets two rolls, and can let out two things (the
  [[double-jar-roll|double Jar roll]]), as does one a
  [[machete|Machete]] or a [[mattock|Mattock]] breaks <Cite gml="objects/oJar/collision_oWhip.gml:9-21" /> <Cite gml="objects/oJar/destroy.gml:3-28" /> <Cite quirk="1" row="A whipped Jar rolls its contents twice." />.
  In the clip, one Jar lets out a big emerald and a gold chunk, with two puffs of smoke and six
  pieces <Cite clip="jar/whip" /> <Cite replay="fidelity/replays/item-jar-whip-rolls-twice.txt" />. The wind-up
  behind the player doesn't break one.
- **[[pitchers-mitt|Pitcher's Mitt]].** A Jar thrown with it keeps the Mitt's lighter gravity for good, as
  its own code only changes gravity in lava, so it flies far and flat ([[mitt-gravity-one-step]]) <Cite
  quirk="1" row="The Pitcher's Mitt's light gravity lasts one step" />.
- **The screen.** Most items stop once they're 16 pixels off the screen; a Jar's code has no such
  test, so it keeps moving until the level puts everything far off the screen to
  sleep <Cite gml="objects/oJar/step.gml:1-25" /> <Cite gml="objects/oLevel/step.gml:65-77" />.
- **Exits.** Carried through a level's exit, it's in the player's hands on the next level, whole: the old
  one is removed without breaking <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:842-850" />.

## Tips

- A Jar breaks on the first monster it touches, so each one is a single shot; a Rock can be thrown again.
- A Jar only stuns a Caveman, a Hawkman, a Mantrap or a Yeti, where a Rock also takes a hit point.
- Whipping a Jar gives two chances at what's inside, where throwing it gives one.

## Trivia

- The Jar's movement code is a copy of the shared item code's, and keeps lines that only matter for other
  items: a [[bomb|Bomb]] exploding in lava, and an [[arrow|arrow]] being destroyed when it hits a
  monster <Cite gml="objects/oJar/step.gml:92-101" /> <Cite gml="objects/oJar/step.gml:143-144" /> <Cite quirk="1" row="Jar and Skull leftovers" />.
- A Jar that a bullet breaks in the player's hands, as a [[shopkeeper|Shopkeeper]]'s shot can, stays on the
  HUD, the [[phantom-jar|phantom Jar]]: the code that tidies up after a held Jar clears a misspelt variable, so the game still remembers a Jar
  as the item in his hands, and switching items gives him a new one after the bomb and the
  rope <Cite gml="objects/oJar/destroy.gml:30-34" /> <Cite quirk="1" row="A held Jar or Skull broken by a bullet leaves its type in the player's hands." /> <Cite replay="fidelity/replays/item-held-jar-shot.txt" />.
