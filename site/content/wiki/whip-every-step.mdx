A [[whip|Whip]] swing is two hits: the wind-up behind the player, then the crack in front of him. The code most monsters share takes a hit from each on every step it touches them, with
no pause between hits. It matters for the few monsters with more than one hit point, and most for those whose
own code limits the whip, which forgets the wind-up.

## What happens

The player whips a [[megamouth|Megamouth]] three times as it charges. The second swing's crack stays on the
fish for seven steps and takes a hit point on each, seven in all <Cite clip="quirks/whip-hits-every-step" />.

<Clip id="quirks/whip-hits-every-step" />

### The wind-up behind the player

A [[giant-spider|Giant Spider]] waits <Stat id="giant-spider.whip-cooldown" /> between whip hits, by its own
code. With his back to one, the player swings: the wind-up behind him takes 1 of its
<Stat id="giant-spider.hp" bare /> hit points on each of three steps in a row, while that wait is still
running <Cite clip="quirks/giant-spider-whip-wind-up" />.

<Clip id="quirks/giant-spider-whip-wind-up" />

The [[tomb-lord|Tomb Lord]] can only be cracked level with its top 12 pixels, so a player on its own floor
can't hurt it facing it. With his back to it, the wind-up takes three of its hit points a swing
<Cite clip="quirks/tomb-lord-whip-wind-up" />.

<Clip id="quirks/tomb-lord-whip-wind-up" />

The [[yeti-king|Yeti King]]'s own code makes the Whip's crack harmless. The wind-up takes three of its hit
points each swing all the same, so the Whip kills it in 10 swings with the player's back to it
<Cite clip="quirks/yeti-king-whip-wind-up" />.

<Clip id="quirks/yeti-king-whip-wind-up" />

## Why

The collision of most monsters (`oEnemy`) with the whip takes the swing's `damage` and nothing else: no wait,
no stun, no check of where the hit is. A collision event runs on every step two things overlap, so it hits
again on every step <Cite gml="objects/oEnemy/collision_oWhip.gml:1-8" />:

```gml objects/oEnemy/collision_oWhip.gml:1-8
hp -= other.damage;
countsAsKill = true;
if (bloodLeft > 0)
{
    scrCreateBlood(x+sprite_width/2, y+sprite_height/2, 1);
    if (hp < 0) bloodLeft -= 1;
}
playSound(global.sndHit);
```

Its collision with the wind-up (`oWhipPre`, which lasts 3 steps) is the same code
<Cite gml="objects/oEnemy/collision_oWhipPre.gml:1-8" /> <Cite gml="objects/oWhipPre/create.gml:3-5" />. A
Megamouth (<Stat id="megamouth.hp" />) and the [[alien-boss|Alien Boss]] (<Stat id="alien-boss.hp" />) have
no whip code of their own, so they take every step's hit <Cite quirk="1" />.

The monsters that limit the whip do it in their own collision with `oWhip`, the crack: the Giant Spider with
a wait (`whipped`) <Cite gml="objects/oGiantSpider/collision_oWhip.gml:1-7" />, the Tomb Lord with the wait
and a height test <Cite gml="objects/oTombLord/collision_oWhip.gml:1-8" />, the Yeti King with a test the
Whip never passes (only a hit that punctures hurts it, and the Whip's doesn't)
<Cite gml="objects/oYetiKing/collision_oWhip.gml:1-16" />, and the [[magma-man|Magma Man]] by only playing a
sound <Cite gml="objects/oMagmaMan/collision_oWhip.gml:1-6" />. None of them has a collision with `oWhipPre`,
so the wind-up runs the shared code instead, with no wait and no test. A Magma Man's hit points are its life,
which runs out by itself, so the wind-up takes 3 steps off it <Cite quirk="1" />.

The [[machete|Machete]] and the [[mattock|Mattock]] swing kinds of whip (children of `oWhip` and `oWhipPre`),
so their slashes and wind-ups do the same, 2 a step; a Mattock swing makes its wind-up twice
<Cite quirk="1" />.

## Reproducing it

Whip a Megamouth, or turn the player's back on a Giant Spider, a Tomb Lord, the Yeti King or a Magma Man and
swing. The replays of the clips' staged scenes pin each case: three swings at a Megamouth taking 1, 7 and 3 of
its hit points <Cite replay="fidelity/replays/enemy-megamouth-whip-every-step.txt" />, a wind-up taking a
Giant Spider from 10 to 7 inside its wait <Cite replay="fidelity/replays/enemy-giant-spider-whip-wind-up.txt" />,
a Tomb Lord from 20 to 17 from its own floor <Cite replay="fidelity/replays/enemy-tomb-lord-whip-wind-up.txt" />,
and the Yeti King from 30 to 21 in three swings while the crack takes nothing
<Cite replay="fidelity/replays/enemy-yeti-king-whip-wind-up.txt" />.

## In the port

The spelunky.ai port reproduces all of it exactly: the four rows are in docs/quirks.md's list of the 1.1
source's bugs <Cite quirk="1" />, and the oracle's fingerprints of the replays pin every hit. No option turns
it off.
