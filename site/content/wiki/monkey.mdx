[[level-generation|Level generation]] puts Monkeys on the vines of [[jungle|the Jungle]]. A Monkey climbs up and down its vine
until the player passes below, then leaps down at him and jumps about until it lands on him. It doesn't bite:
it clings to his back for a second or two, then trips him or throws away some of his money, a [[rope|rope]] or a
[[bomb|Bomb]]. With <Stat id="monkey.hp" />, it dies to any damage.

## Behaviour

**On a vine.** A Monkey starts out hanging from a vine. It climbs up it at 1 pixel per step to the vine's top,
hangs there for 10–40 steps (0.33–1.33 seconds), climbs down to the bottom, hangs again, and so on
<Cite gml="objects/oMonkey/create.gml:29" /> <Cite gml="objects/oMonkey/step.gml:135-169" />. While it climbs,
if the player is below it and within <Stat id="monkey.jump-range" /> of it, counting from the edges of the
two, it leaps off toward him, across at 3 pixels per step and up at 2–4, and can't catch hold of a vine again
for 30 steps <Cite gml="objects/oMonkey/step.gml:170-185" />. In the air, it catches the first vine or ladder
it meets, once those 30 steps are up, or lands <Cite gml="objects/oMonkey/step.gml:93-112" />.

**On the ground.** When it lands, it stands for 10–40 steps, then walks at <Stat id="monkey.walk-speed" />
(<Stat id="monkey.walk-speed" as="per-second" />), turning at walls and stopping for 20–50 steps with a 1 in
100 chance each step <Cite gml="objects/oMonkey/step.gml:61-92" />. Whenever it's standing still and the
player is within <Stat id="monkey.jump-range" />, it jumps at him, up at 4–5 pixels per step and across at 2,
and chatters as it leaves the ground <Cite gml="objects/oMonkey/step.gml:61-68" />
<Cite gml="objects/oMonkey/step.gml:113-134" />. Unlike a climbing Monkey, it jumps at a player above it too.

<Clip id="monkey/steal" />

**Clinging.** When a Monkey touches the player, within 4 pixels of its middle across, and he isn't invincible,
it grabs him and holds on, moving with him, for <Stat id="monkey.grab-time" />
(<Stat id="monkey.grab-time" as="seconds" />) <Cite gml="objects/oMonkey/collision_oCharacter.gml:13-23" />
<Cite gml="objects/oMonkey/step.gml:187-197" />. Then it does one thing
<Cite gml="objects/oMonkey/step.gml:199-275" />:

| Chance | What it does | |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 in 4 | Trips him: he's knocked over and stunned for 40 steps, and drops what he's holding | <Cite gml="objects/oMonkey/step.gml:200-229" /> |
| Otherwise, if he has the money, 8 in 10 | Takes $500 plus $125 for each area after the Mines ($625 in the Jungle) and throws it out as a [[gold-nugget\|Gold Nugget]], which can be picked up after 20 steps | <Cite gml="objects/oMonkey/step.gml:199" /> <Cite gml="objects/oMonkey/step.gml:230-243" /> |
| Otherwise, if he has a rope, 1 in 2 | Takes a rope and throws it out | <Cite gml="objects/oMonkey/step.gml:244-255" /> |
| Otherwise, if he has a bomb | Takes a [[bomb\|Bomb]] and throws it out; 1 time in 10 the bomb is lit, with a 40-step fuse | <Cite gml="objects/oMonkey/step.gml:256-275" /> |

If none of those applies, it takes nothing. Either way it then leaps off away from him and can't grab him
again for 60 steps (2 seconds) <Cite gml="objects/oMonkey/step.gml:276-289" />. The clip shows a Monkey
leaping off its vine, jumping onto the player and throwing away one of his bombs, then jumping on him again
and throwing away a rope.

The money it throws out is worth what it took: a Gold Nugget is worth $500, plus a quarter of that for each
area after the Mines, when it's picked up, the same sum the Monkey takes
<Cite gml="objects/oGoldNugget/create.gml:6" /> <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:1996" />.

The player can shake a Monkey off by flipping down from a ledge to hang from it, and leaving the level through
its exit throws one off too <Cite gml="scripts/characterStepEvent.gml:803-828" />
<Cite gml="scripts/characterStepEvent.gml:829-852" /> <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:890-900" />.

**Items.** A Monkey knocks away any item that touches it while it isn't clinging to the player, at most once
every 60 steps (2 seconds): the item flies off the way the Monkey faces at 5 pixels per step, and up
<Cite gml="objects/oMonkey/collision_oItem.gml:1-26" />.

Like most monsters, a Monkey only acts while it's on screen or just off it
<Cite gml="objects/oMonkey/step.gml:3-4" />.

## Damage

A Monkey never takes the player's hearts. Its touch code replaces the one most monsters share and has no
damage in it: touching a Monkey either stomps it or lets it grab him
<Cite gml="objects/oMonkey/collision_oCharacter.gml:1-23" />. What it can cost him is what it takes (above), the
fall after a trip, and a lit bomb it throws away, whose explosion hurts him like any [[bomb|Bomb]]'s.

## Locations

Level generation makes Monkeys only in [[jungle|the Jungle]], on its vines: each vine block has a 1 in 15
chance of one <Cite gml="scripts/scrEntityGen.gml:321-324" />.

Custom levels can have them, as the [[level-editor|level editor]]'s tile `m`
<Cite gml="scripts/scrCreateTileObj.gml:193-196" />.

## Tips

- A Monkey on a vine only leaps at a player below it, so passing above or level with it is safe until it
  comes down.
- A Monkey takes nothing a player doesn't have: with no money to spare, no ropes and no bombs, its grab only
  costs time, or a trip.
- Flipping down from a ledge to hang from it throws off a Monkey that's holding on.
- A clinging Monkey can't be stomped, and while it's on the player's back, it moves with him.

## Trivia

- The Monkey's touch code is a copy of the one most monsters share, first line included, a comment that names
  other monsters:

  ```gml objects/oMonkey/collision_oCharacter.gml:1
  // jumped on - oCaveman, oManTrap replaces this script with its own
  ```

- Monkeys have a kill count of their own, which a comment says "is for stats", rather than a slot in the
  monsters' kill counts: the kills table the game writes to `stats.txt` adds it as a row after the Frog's
  <Cite gml="objects/oMonkey/step.gml:35" /> <Cite gml="scripts/scrWriteStats.gml:214-224" />.
- Its code names a drowned state that nothing ever puts it in, and has a commented-out line, copied from the
  Frog's, that would have played the Frog's croak when it jumps <Cite gml="objects/oMonkey/create.gml:25" />
  <Cite gml="objects/oMonkey/step.gml:67" />.
