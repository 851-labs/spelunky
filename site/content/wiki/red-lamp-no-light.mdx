On a [[dark-level|dark level]], a [[lamp|Lamp]] carried or lying on the ground lights the cave around it, and
the player's own light widens as he nears it. The [[kissing-parlor|Kissing Parlor]]'s lamp is red, and while it
hangs it lights the shop like any other. Once its block is broken and it falls, though, the red Lamp lights
nothing: in the dark, carrying it is the same as carrying nothing, but for a red tint.

## What happens

On a dark level, the player throws a lit bomb up under the Kissing Parlor's ceiling, and the red lamp falls. He
picks it up and carries it out of the shop into the dark: it casts no light of its own, the dark closes in round
him as if his hands were empty, and only the red tint shows <Cite clip="quirks/red-lamp-no-light" />.

<Clip id="quirks/red-lamp-no-light" />

## Why

The red hanging lamp, `oLampRed`, is a kind of the plain one, `oLamp`, in the game's objects. The red Lamp it
falls as, `oLampRedItem`, is an item like any other, not a kind of the plain Lamp, `oLampItem`
<Cite gml="objects/oSolid/destroy.gml:24-33" />. On a dark level the screen draws a circle of light round every
hanging lamp, which takes in the red one, and round every fallen Lamp, which names the plain one only:

```gml objects/oScreen/step_begin.gml:187-194
            with oLamp
            {
                draw_circle((x+8)-view_xview[0], (y+8)-view_yview[0], 96, false);
            }
            with oLampItem
            {
                draw_circle(x-view_xview[0], (y-4)-view_yview[0], 96, false);
            }
```

The player's step, which finds the nearest light to set how dark it is round him, looks for the same two
<Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/step.gml:89-100" /> <Cite gml="objects/oLevel/step.gml:110-136" />. The red Lamp
appears in the lighting code only where it tints the lights red while the player is within 96 pixels of it
<Cite gml="objects/oScreen/step_begin.gml:167-177" /> <Cite quirk="1" />.

## Reproducing it

Find a Kissing Parlor on a dark level: the Mines' levels 2 to 4, the Jungle or the Temple. Its lamp hangs from
the shop's ceiling, out of reach of a bomb on the floor, and a lit bomb near the Shopkeeper in his shop angers
him, so deal with him first, then throw a lit bomb up under the lamp as its fuse runs out. Pick the red Lamp up
and walk into the dark. The replay of the clip does so on a dark level 2: carrying the red Lamp, the nearest light
the player has is never closer than 20 pixels, and the darkness reaches its deepest
<Cite replay="fidelity/replays/item-seed-2-red-lamp-dark.txt" />. Another replay brings a dark level's plain
lamp down and carries it, and the darkness is gone while he does
<Cite replay="fidelity/replays/item-seed-2-lamp-dark.txt" />.

## In the port

The spelunky.ai port reproduces it exactly: it's in docs/quirks.md's list of the 1.1 source's bugs
<Cite quirk="1" />, and the oracle's fingerprints of the two replays pin the light round the player with each
Lamp. No option turns it off.
