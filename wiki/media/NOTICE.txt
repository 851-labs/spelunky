spelunky.ai wiki media

Sprites, animation strips, gameplay clips and the GML source of Spelunky Classic 1.1 for spelunky.ai's
unofficial fan wiki. The sprites are exported from the game's assets (scripts/wiki-sprites); the clips are
recorded from the spelunky.ai port, an unofficial, fan-made port (a mod) of the game, not the original
(scripts/wiki-clips); gml/ is spelunky.gmk's code as spelunky.ai's extractor writes it, one file per script
or object event, the "// ====" lines naming each action of an event, and rooms/ its rooms, each one's
settings and the instances the room editor placed in it (scripts/extract). Some scripts are
others' work that Derek Yu credits in the source's readme.txt: Martin Piecyk's platform engine, ChevyRay's
view scaling scripts and tsg1zzn's Super Sound System.
Source: https://github.com/851-labs/spelunky (docs/wiki-media.md).
