Level generation can put a Snake on top of almost any block in [[mines|the Mines]], and a [[snake-pit]]
holds several more; elsewhere, Snakes come out of [[jar|Jars]]. A Snake doesn't chase the player: it
patrols the ground it's on and bites him on contact. With <Stat id="snake.hp" />, it dies to any damage,
whether from the [[whip|Whip]], a [[stomping|stomp]] or a thrown [[rock|Rock]].

## Behaviour

A Snake walks along the ground at <Stat id="snake.walk-speed" /> (<Stat id="snake.walk-speed"
as="per-second" />). It turns around when it walks into a wall or reaches the edge of a ledge, so it never
walks off one <Cite gml="objects/oSnake/step.gml:41-54" />. Where it has a wall or a drop on both sides, as
on a platform one block wide, it stands still <Cite gml="objects/oSnake/step.gml:56-62" />.

While walking, it has a 1 in <Stat id="snake.pause-chance" bare /> chance each step of stopping for
<Stat id="snake.pause" /> (<Stat id="snake.pause" as="seconds" />). When the pause ends, it sets off in a
random direction <Cite gml="objects/oSnake/step.gml:30-37" />, as a new Snake does on its first step
<Cite gml="objects/oSnake/create.gml:18-26" />. Its animation runs at half speed while it stands still
<Cite gml="objects/oSnake/step.gml:81-87" />.

A Snake only acts while it's on screen or less than 4 pixels off it: its whole step event is inside that
check <Cite gml="objects/oSnake/step.gml:3-4" />.

## Damage

<Clip id="snake/bite" />

When the player touches a Snake without stomping it, he loses <Stat id="snake.damage" />, is knocked away
from it, and blinks, invincible, for 30 steps (1 second), so the Snake can't bite again straight away
<Cite gml="objects/oEnemy/collision_oCharacter.gml:14-25" />. This is the touch code the Snake inherits from
the parent object most monsters share; the Snake has no attack of its own ([Trivia](#trivia)). The clip
shows a bite taking the player from four hearts to three.

## Locations

Level generation makes Snakes only in [[mines|the Mines]], levels 1–4
<Cite gml="scripts/scrInitLevel.gml:24-29" />:

- **On the ground.** Each solid block with open space above it has a 1 in 60 chance of a Snake on top,
  except in the starting room and in shops <Cite gml="scripts/scrEntityGen.gml:30-45" />
  <Cite gml="scripts/scrEntityGen.gml:67-69" />.
- **In a [[snake-pit]].** A Mines level may have one snake pit, a shaft of rooms off the main path, with a
  1 in 8 chance for each place one fits <Cite gml="scripts/scrLevelGen.gml:138-185" />
  <Cite gml="scripts/scrClearGlobals.gml:55" />. Each snake tile in its middle rooms holds a Snake with a 1
  in 10 chance, and its bottom room is built with five Snakes and two [[big-ruby|Big Rubies]]
  <Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGen.gml:205-216" /> <Cite gml="scripts/scrRoomGen.gml:462-473" />. Such a
  level opens with the message "I HEAR SNAKES... I HATE SNAKES!", after "I CAN'T SEE A THING!" on a dark
  level <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/alarm_0.gml:5-26" /> <Cite gml="objects/oPlayer1/alarm_1.gml:5-10" />.

Snakes also appear wherever these are:

- **[[jar|Jars]].** A broken Jar makes a Snake with a 1 in 12 chance once its rolls for gold, gems and a
  Spider have all failed <Cite gml="objects/oJar/destroy.gml:17-28" />
  <Cite gml="objects/oJar/collision_oWhip.gml:9-15" />.
- **[[olmec|Olmec]]'s yellow balls**, which turn into a Bat, Spider, Snake, Frog or Fire Frog when they
  hit the ground <Cite gml="objects/oYellowBall/step.gml:4-19" />.
- **The [[tutorial|Tutorial]]**, which has one <Cite room="rTutorial" />.
- **Custom levels**, as the [[level-editor|level editor]]'s tile `n`
  <Cite gml="scripts/scrCreateTileObj.gml:131-134" />.

## Tips

- A Snake never walks off a ledge, so one on a platform stays on it, and one on a platform one block wide
  never moves.
- After a bite, the player is invincible for 1 second, during which touching the Snake again costs
  nothing.
- A Snake off screen doesn't move, so one is still where it was when it left the screen.

## Trivia

- The Snake's code defines an attack state, but nothing in it ever enters that state, and its branch holds
  only a question mark:

  ```gml objects/oSnake/step.gml:73-76
  else if (status == ATTACK)
  {
      // ?
  }
  ```

- A commented-out block at the end of its step event would switch to a right-facing sprite,
  `sSnakeWalkR`, which the game doesn't have. The Snake uses its left-facing sprite both ways, mirrored
  when it faces right <Cite gml="objects/oSnake/step.gml:88-96" />
  <Cite gml="objects/oEnemy/draw.gml:1-2" />.
